Basics

  • Entity: Starii Holdings (listed as a subsidiary in Meitu's 2025 interim report); Starii Tech Pty Ltd, Google Play address Suite 901, 77 Pacific Highway, North Sydney; the privacy policy lists a Haymarket, Sydney office.
  • Product matrix (after the starii.com redesign on 2026-09-25): ZAWA (brand/AI design, likely formerly X-Design), VMAKE LABS (video Agent, UGC product videos), MVLAND (music to music video), ARTFLO (creative workflows), KOOZEE (AI models/try-on for apparel e-commerce). Also apps such as Winkit, Starii AI Beauty Editor (com.starii.starii), and the discontinued Gemix.
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2017-08-02 (eName); Meitu had parked this name long ago. starii.com also serves as the H5 terms pages, the s.starii.com short link, and the account and payment center.
  • Parent company scale: Meitu's H1 2026 revenue was RMB 2.21 billion, global MAU 282 million (100M+ overseas), 18.44 million paying subscribers.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid influencer promotion: a large number of "AI tool recommendation accounts" post Vmake template videos at per-post rates (e.g. $330 per post), tagged "Paid partnership", with links all going through s.starii.com short links (for per-creator attribution), so a big chunk of starii.com traffic is "click short link → redirect to app/web".
  • Trend-jacking templates: social memes like the Netflix billboard stunt and "Apple keynote-style product video" are turned into one-click templates within days, then pushed by influencers.
  • Launch platform: X-Design launched on Product Hunt, took #1 of the day, and issued a press release (endorsement only, not the main volume).
  • In-product redirects: when users upgrade inside Vmake/MVLAND etc., they are sent to starii.com account/payment pages (Referrals is the #1 channel in our data).
  • Meitu open-sourced vmake-skills on GitHub, riding the AI Agent ecosystem | https://github.com/meitu/vmake-skills

Speculation:

  • Likely also App Store/Google Play paid acquisition and ASO (Meitu's standard overseas playbook), plus SEO tool pages; no direct data.
  • Free-tier output videos may carry a watermark, a weak form of branded output.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Vmake ARR: about $3M at end of 2025 → about $5M in 2026-06; MVLAND $680K ARR (2026-10).
  • Semrush: about 693K monthly visits in 2026-08 (US); our checkout visits averaged 108K over the past 4–6 months → 203K two months ago → 185K last month The two months ago jump coincides with the Billboard Guy template + dense influencer campaigns and the Koozee launch (correlation, not proof of causation).

Confidence and gaps

  • The Meitu affiliation comes from Meitu's interim report (secondhand) + app package names + the privacy policy, high confidence.
  • Second follow-up check: growth engine changed from "Paid ads (speculation)" to evidence-backed "paid influencers + s.starii.com tracking short links"; added facts such as X-Design's 2025-08-01 Product Hunt #1 of the day, the Koozee launch on 2026-08-18, the Billboard Guy trend template, and a single influencer's 6 paid posts and rate card.
  • Still missing: total influencer budget and share of paid acquisition; whether the Grace_AI_ X post was paid (402, could not open); starii.com's first Wayback snapshot.
