Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Existing audience migration: the two founders have about 13K X followers combined, the launch post got 130K views, and our data shows organic social traffic is 100% from X with search being brand keywords only.
  • Content as the entry point: the founders keep posting AI ad breakdowns and use "link in the comments" to route traffic to lacuisine.app; YouTube video descriptions carry the membership link.
  • Community retention: Discord, weekly livestreams, monthly guests, weekly AI news, with continuous updates sustaining the monthly subscription.

Speculation:

  • The first members came almost entirely from the two founders' X followers and reposts within the French e-commerce/ads scene; the French-speaking world lacks comparable communities (positioned as "the only one"), so even a small following converts.
  • Ads that members produce with the course methods carry no branding when run, so product self-propagation is weak and the growth ceiling depends on the founders' content output.
  • Collecting payments through Whop may bring a little discovery traffic inside the Whop platform (unverified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • €199.90/month; member count not public. The launch post got 130K views; at a 0.1%-0.3% conversion that would be roughly 130-400 members and about €26-80K in monthly revenue (pure speculation).
  • Our data: France 100%; direct 46.6%, X 40.1%, referral 8%.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: no earlier report in the notes. Of the four search batches, "launch", "review" and "tiktok" were almost all irrelevant (weather and nutrition apps); the useful evidence comes from X posts (original post data read via the fxtwitter API), the site and RDAP.
  • Launch date (domain 09-13, launch post 09-17) and founder identities are first-hand evidence, credible.
  • Gaps: member count and revenue; ownership and view counts of the YouTube channel (page could not be read); the 6-month €600 tier is no longer on the site, so pricing may have changed.
