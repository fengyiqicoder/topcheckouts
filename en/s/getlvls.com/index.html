Basics

  • Product: LVLS publishes supply and demand zones for NQ, ES, SPY, QQQ and 30+ stocks/ETFs before the open every day, with TradingView / thinkorswim indicators that import in one click; the members area has the day's Game Plan, morning watch videos, "Top Watch" opportunities and briefings on 30+ tickers. NQ is free to preview, and a free account unlocks ES/SPY/QQQ.
  • Founder: Vincent Desiano ("Desi"), founder and host of The Insiders, claims 12 years of trading experience and "seven-figure verified P&L" (not verified).
  • Pricing: free tier; LVLS $49.99/month; The Insiders $199.99/month (includes LVLS, 30% off with annual billing). Whop checkout.
  • Social proof: getlvls.com claims 16,000+ members and Whop 4.8 rating / 700+ reviews; options-insider.com claims 4,000+ members and 500+ reviews (the two are counted differently, the former may include free accounts).
  • Domain: getlvls.com registered 2026-06-19 (RDAP), no Wayback snapshot; options-insider.com registered 2021-04-30, first Wayback snapshot 2021-12-20.
  • Traffic: United States 98%; social 43% all from YouTube, direct 43%, referral 8.5%; search term "lvls vincent whop". 26 visits three months ago → 3,358 last month

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Daily YouTube pre-market live → LVLS link in the description → free NQ levels / free signup → $49.99 upgrade → $199.99 Insiders community (value ladder).
  • @getlvls posts charts annotated with levels and a "6 of today's 33 charts" teaser on X every day, and the charts themselves carry the LVLS brand.
  • Whop review count (700+) serves as social proof.

Speculation:

  • The "free tier + free signup to see more" is meant to turn YouTube viewers into an email/account list, then convert them by reviewing how the levels held during the live.
  • Level charts carry the brand when traders repost them, giving some self-spread, but the audience is highly concentrated in the US index trading circle.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Priced at $49.99 / $199.99 per month; claims 16,000+ members (including free) and 4,000+ Insiders members. If all 4,000 Insiders paid monthly, that would be about $800K/month, a ceiling estimate only, not verified.
  • X self-reports 1,000 DAU (snippet only).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The founder, prices, domain registration time, X account registration time and YouTube live traffic routing all have original evidence.
  • Gaps: after the Google search several groups of results were all unrelated traffic-boosting sites; the YouTube subscriber count was inconsistent across fetches (a "3.15K" figure turned up, which doesn't match 10–20K views per session, so it was not used); the 1,000 DAU tweet only has a search snippet; the X timeline was not read via WebFetch.
