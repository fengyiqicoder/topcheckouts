Listary (listary.net): Windows desktop tool for fast file search and app launching with a free core version and Pro upgrade. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 3,015 visits went from Listary to a checkout payment page — #605 of the 607 sites we track. That is 55% up from the April–June monthly average of 1,946.
