Basics

  • Product: online stamp design (templates / AI-generated company seals, rubber stamps, wax seals, foil seals), stamping on PDF/Word/photos, PDF editing, e-signatures (send signing requests, audit trail), etc.
  • Who built it: the site has no company name and no About page (/about returns 404); contact is service@stampdy.com and a UK WhatsApp number +44 7719 569000; the footer reads "Copyright © 2011-2026" and "Reproduction without a link is prohibited", boilerplate common on Russian/Eastern European sites. 2011 does not match a domain registered in 2025, so this is probably a reused template or the team has older sites (Speculation). Team Not found.
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2025-03-10; Wayback first captured it 2025-07-13 (301), and on 2025-07-19 captured the homepage, /pdf-editor, /blog and 30+ language subdirectories (ko, ar, ja, ru, pl, es, de, ur, etc.).
  • Pricing (/pricing, 2026-10): single PNG $2.5, all formats $3.5 (the profile record says $2.9); 30-day unlimited $15.9; annual $119 (about $9.9/month); lifetime $299; single purchase needs no sign-up, paid via Stripe.
  • Scale: sitemap has about 890 generic pages (languages × feature pages) + 251 blog posts + 51 template pages.
  • Traffic (profile record): India 17.6%, United States 7.9%, Indonesia 4.6%; organic search 67.5%, with terms stamp maker, online stamp maker, stamp generator and the Indonesian munculkan gambar stempel; direct 24.4%, referral 4.9%, no social. The probedex summary shows about 101.2K visits/month.

Where the first users came from: there is no launch post, Product Hunt or community record. From the first capture the site was already a finished SEO site in 30+ languages, so the first users were most likely people searching "stamp maker" and the equivalent in other languages (Speculation, based on 67.5% of traffic coming from organic search and no social source).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Multilingual programmatic SEO: 30+ languages × feature pages, plus 251 blog posts; non-brand feature keywords make up most of the search traffic.
  • Free design + paid download (from $2.5 per download), lowering the barrier to the first purchase.
  • A small number of YouTube tutorials.

Speculation:

  • Demand for "company seals / electronic stamps" is strong in India, Indonesia and similar markets (government and bank documents often need a stamp); competitors are mostly old-fashioned sites, and the new site takes rankings with a more modern editor and multilingual coverage.
  • Stamped documents carry no Stampdy branding, so there is no product self-propagation.

Revenue and scale clues

  • About 100K visits per month (probedex summary).
  • Revenue Not found; with $2.5–3.5 per download plus subscriptions, monthly revenue is probably in the low thousands of dollars (Speculation, no supporting figures).

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site written from scratch. The 5 search groups returned almost no third-party information (the "review" group was all irrelevant RTX 5090 results); only the official homepage/pricing page, sitemap, Wayback CDX and RDAP were used.
  • Team, country and revenue are all Not found; the origin of "© 2011" is unknown.
