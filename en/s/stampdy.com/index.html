Overview

Stampdy (stampdy.com): Browser-based custom stamp designer with free templates and exports, paid high-res downloads. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Stampdy to a checkout payment page — #465 of the 607 sites we track. That is 48% down from the April–June monthly average of 16,567.

Total website visits that month were about 38,588 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 22.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 68%, direct 24%, referrals/email/paid 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 14%, with queries like “munculkan gambar stempel”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: $2.5 single-use PNG; $2.9 single-use all formats; $15.9/month 30 Days Unlimited (was $19.9); $119/year (meta description) etc..
