A small local drum studio in Dubai (original campus in Barsha Heights) built its site during the 2020 pandemic as a "content SEO" website from day one: a drum learning blog, student performance videos, a "4-lesson beginner trial pack" with WhatsApp price inquiries. In 2025-04 it launched a free English-Russian online metronome and drumless backing track pages; these tool pages captured a large amount of Russian "метроном онлайн" search, which pushed the checkout page to 20K+ visits in 2026-09, but this traffic (mainly Russia and Ukraine) basically does not overlap with the Dubai customer base for paid drum lessons. Basics

  • Product: drum lessons (children/adults, beginner trial packs, Trinity Rock & Pop exams), online or in store; campuses: Barsha Heights (original), Dubai Silicon Oasis IT Plaza (newly opened 2026-03); sister sites theguitarcamp.co (guitar) and thedrumshop.ae (drum shop).
  • Free tool pages: /metronome-online/, /ru/metronome-online/ (snapshots since 2025-04/06), /drumless-tracks-for-practice/; blog Drum Diaries.
  • Pricing: AED 640 / 690 / 1,680 / 3,180 / 5,999 lesson packs, all buy.stripe.com links; DSO opening price 4 lessons AED 390.
  • Who is behind it: the site does not name a founder; in 2021 the site footer read "Powered by Elan Marketing Consultancy" (the outsourced site-building/marketing provider). Reviews mention teachers Mandar, Don Santos, Stuart Francis, Anshul and Sandip/Sandeep. KHDA certified, claims 2025 "Best Drumming Academy". No funding.
  • Launch date: WHOIS 2020-06-20; first Wayback snapshot 2021-02-20 (already with 6 blog posts, student videos and a beginner offer).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Content SEO (blog, student videos) and local signals (Google 4.9 rating from 123 reviews, KHDA, two campus addresses) since the site was built.
  • Social media is mainly student performance videos (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram 641 followers), at a very small scale.
  • Free metronome + Russian pages → a lot of tool traffic from Russian-speaking regions (unrelated to the paid lessons).
  • Speculation:
  • Paying students mainly come from local Google search "drum classes dubai", parents' word of mouth and the new campus opening offer.
  • The last month checkout page surge is the rising Russian ranking of the metronome page being counted (or crawlers), not paid growth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Lesson packs AED 640–5,999; two campuses; 5+ teachers (per reviews); 123 Google reviews. Revenue or student count Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • This is an in-person training business, so the "how it got started" framing for internet products does not apply; the takeoff year is counted as 2026, when checkout page traffic emerged, while the actual business began in 2020.
  • The founder's identity was Not found; the reason for the Russian traffic surge is still speculation.
  • Second follow-up check: confirmed Silicon Oasis is a second campus newly opened in 2026-03 (the old report wrongly listed it alongside the original campus), the metronome/Russian pages launched in 2025-04, and that the 2021 site was already content-SEO style and built by an outsourced marketing company; takeoff year changed from 2020 to 2026 (the year traffic emerged), with 2020 noted as the opening and site-building year.
