Overview

Thedrumcamp (thedrumcamp.com): Dubai drum school offering in-studio and online drum lessons for all ages. Category on TopCheckouts: Courses & content.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 21,708 visits went from Thedrumcamp to a checkout payment page — #118 of the 607 sites we track. That is 13218% up from the April–June monthly average of 163.

Total website visits that month were about 10,534 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 46%, search 38%, referrals/email/paid 9%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 30%, with queries like “метроном онлайн”, “metronomo online drums”, “метроном громкий”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
