Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral rebates: users get a dedicated referral link, earn commission on every order from referred users, and can withdraw once a threshold is reached (official site); NodeSeek review posts carry #AFF links.
  • Hosting review site sponsored article chain: multiple promo articles (with tracking parameters) on vpsquery, gwvpsceping, vps55 etc.
  • Forum giveaway posts for exposure (NodeSeek).
  • Brand migration: wholesale migration of existing customers + domain 301 redirect is the direct cause of the last month traffic surge.

Speculation:

  • The YouTube/X social sources may be Chinese "VPN/VPS recommendation" YouTubers' videos with AFF links (no specific video found).
  • The announcement mentions "impersonation sites", suggesting the brand already had some recognition, possibly one motive for the rename.
  • Core user need: cross-border e-commerce/TikTok livestreaming needs US residential IPs + direct connectivity to China; the new CN2 GIA product extends into the larger "VPN/website hosting" market.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Prices: CN2 GIA Lite 1C2G20G 1TB @1Gbps $9.99/month ($7.49 equivalent on biennial billing), other plans $15.99–$189.99/month; residential IP servers $35.99/$55.99/$99.99/month; proxies $34.98/month, dedicated tunnels $59.98/month.
  • Scale: promo articles claim 400+ residential lines; the rename announcement has 40k+ reads. Revenue and user numbers not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Rename date, domain dates and prices are highly credible (official announcement + WHOIS + Wayback).
  • Company background (started 2022, self-owned apartment server rooms) comes only from sponsored promo articles, not independently verified; founder identity not found.
  • Risk note: the product is strongly tied to VPN/proxy use, and the V2EX thread already had people questioning the vendor's reputation. Low value as an "organic spread sample"; it is typical VPS-circle AFF + forum marketing.
