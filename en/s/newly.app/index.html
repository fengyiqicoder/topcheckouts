Overview

Newly (newly.app): No-code AI platform that builds native iOS/Android apps from a text prompt with App Store / Play Store deployment. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Newly to a checkout payment page — #443 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 67,443 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 12.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 45%, search 41%, referrals/email/paid 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, public profiles / leaderboards, referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 62%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $0/mo (Agent plan, 200 credits/mo); Newly Apex: 'Coming Soon', no price.
