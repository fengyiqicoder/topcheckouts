Growth loop

Evidence:

  • UGC short-video matrix: multiple "girl who makes things" persona accounts pushing "build apps for passive income" with the same script; commenters recognized them as ads (2026-07~09).
  • Affiliate/referral: tolt.io referrals 3.2%;?ref= referral links; Referral Program on the site.

Speculation:

  • The rename to Newly may be meant to shift from a "developer tool" to the mass "side-hustle app building" audience.
