Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • New customers come almost entirely from local searches for the shop name (brand keyword search) and direct return visits; the 10.7% referral presumably comes from the MyMealsOnline platform itself.
  • The 5 given search sets returned nothing about this shop (only a Leek local nostalgia Facebook group "This is Leek Staffordshire", not directly related to the shop).
  • The site has Facebook and Twitter links.
  • Speculation:
  • The first online users were regulars who used to order by phone and were told "you can order online now"; local Facebook groups and takeaway menu flyers may be supplements.
  • The SEO/SMS marketing offered by Meals Online may be helping with local search and repeat-order reminders.

Revenue and scale clues

  • A small-town takeaway; website revenue is just part of the takeaway's revenue; no public figures.
  • The traffic visible in our data suggests monthly visits in the low thousands (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Shop details, the builder and the domain registration date can all be verified directly; the growth section has only traffic structure. This is a local business, so there is no "rise to success" story.
