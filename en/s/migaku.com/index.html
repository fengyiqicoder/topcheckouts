Overview

Migaku (migaku.com): Language-learning SaaS turning Netflix, YouTube and web content into flashcards via Chrome extension and mobile apps. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 14,888 visits went from Migaku to a checkout payment page — #207 of the 607 sites we track. That is 81% up from the April–June monthly average of 8,237.

Total website visits that month were about 222,946 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 6.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 50%, search 43%, social 7%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 17%, with queries like “住所 英語 変換”, “th sonido en ingles nombre”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: USD 10/month; USD 15/month (Early Access); USD 499 one-time (lifetime).
