Basics

  • Product: TradingView indicator suite. 15 free public indicators (Opening Gap Tracker, Session VWAP, Smart Order Blocks, Fair Value Gap Finder, Liquidity Levels, etc.) + 5 premium toolkits; Premium includes GapAlgo Signals on-chart entry/exit markers and SPY/QQQ 5-minute candle signal alerts in Discord. Also free calculators (position size, R multiple, ATR stop, options expected move) and SPY/QQQ stats pages (gap fill rate, daily range, etc., "recalculated daily").
  • Pricing (profile): 15 indicators free; Premium $39/month (was $49, "launch price"), $299/year; payments via Whop.
  • Team: the site is unsigned, calling itself only "an independent product". TradingView publishing account grahamtrading (registered 2022-11-09, Premium member, 608 followers, 20 scripts, sole profile link gapalgo.com). An individual or small team; real identity not found.
  • Domain: registered 2026-07-06 (RDAP); no Wayback archive; all sitemap lastmod 2026-09-25.
  • Traffic (profile): US 85.7%, UK 14.3%; direct 49.1%, search 34.5% (brand keywords 83.6%: gapalgo, gapalgo trading), referral 9.8%.

Where the first users came from: TradingView's community script library. All 15 free indicators are published with the "GapAlgo-" prefix, the author profile links only to gapalgo.com, and every indicator page on the site links back to the TradingView script; SMC (order blocks/FVG) is one of the most searched and saved themes on TradingView, and popular scripts enter the recommendation feed via boosts. The Whop free tier ("GapAlgo Free Access", 5 indicators) turns these people into a member list. No trace of Reddit/YouTube/X promotion was found, and no press coverage in search results.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free indicators public on TradingView with the brand in the name → users add them to charts and see "GapAlgo" every day → brand-keyword search (83.6% of search is brand) → site → Whop free/paid membership.
  • Site indicator doc pages + calculators + stats pages (launched all at once on 2026-09-25) target long-tail SEO and send traffic back to the TradingView scripts.
  • Premium signals pushed in Discord create daily retention.

Speculation:

  • The high count on the flagship Smart Order Blocks script may come from TradingView's "popular scripts / editors' picks" slots, which would be the largest single traffic source (specific placement unverified).
  • The "AI-engineered" positioning implies the indicators are AI-assisted and mass-produced at low cost.
  • The 49% direct traffic may be Discord/Whop members returning plus clicks from inside TradingView (in-app jumps are often recorded as direct).

Revenue and scale clues

  • 3,722 Whop members (most presumably on the free tier); site claims 3,600+ traders and 50,000+ setups.
  • Premium $39/month; at a 2–5% paid rate that is roughly 75–190 people and about $3k–7k/month (pure estimate, unverified).
  • Only 6 Whop reviews, so the paid base may still be small.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Domain registration, TradingView account and script dates, and Whop member count are all first-hand page data, so credibility is medium-high; the meaning of TradingView's count fields is unlabeled.
  • Search results are almost all irrelevant generic articles; no interviews, Reddit threads or Product Hunt launch posts.
  • Gaps: founder identity; whether it made TradingView editors' picks; paid-member count; Discord size.
