A music production blog that an amateur computer-music hobbyist named Ronnie began running alone in 2005. It started as a roundup of free VST plugins, sounds and samples (there were free plugin entries like DtBlkFx as early as 2006), and for 20 years he has posted new plugins, deals, free resources and giveaways almost every day, building up tens of thousands of posts titled with specific product names. Basics

  • Product: a music production news blog with sections for latest news, a deals roundup ("Deals, deals, deals", updated daily), reviews, free downloads (loops/samples, free sound libraries, licensed by their authors) and giveaways.
  • Operator: Ronnie (About page: "It's me, Ronnie!", calls it a "one-man-show", running it for 20+ years; social bio "I love chicken vindaloo & all things glitch"). Location not stated. There are also 12 regular contributors of posts/resources (Medway Studios, Tonedeff, ModeAudio, etc.).
  • Monetization: affiliate links (Plugin Boutique and AudioDeluxe are explicitly disclosed as affiliate partners, ADSR links carry a_aid=rekkerd), vendor display ads (about 9 on the homepage: Sonuscore, Audiomodern, Plugin Boutique, Tracktion, etc.), a "From our partners" sound library placement, and PayPal/Ko-fi donations.
  • Domain: rekkerd.org registered 2003-10-31, registrar transferred 2007-06 (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2005-11-18, with snapshots every year after.
  • Social: Facebook, Instagram/Threads @rekkerdblog, Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube @rekkerd, X.
  • Traffic: United States 12%, India 10%, France 7%; non-brand search 61% (plugin product names, even "rapidcomposer 6 torrent"), direct 20%; referrals emastered.com 3%, bsky.app 2%; ChatGPT 0.25%. rekkerd accounts for 6.2% of synthanatomy.com's referral traffic, which shows it is a traffic distribution node in the same circle.

Where the first users came from: no direct evidence. From memory, unverified: in the mid-2000s the free VST scene was concentrated in communities like the KVR Audio forum, so rekkerd most likely got going early through those forums and long-tail searches like "free vst".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Posting a large volume of new product / deal / free resource posts titled with product names every day → long-tail search traffic makes up 61%.
  • Affiliate links + vendor ads + giveaways: vendors buy ads for exposure and supply giveaway prizes, and giveaways in turn draw readers to follow and subscribe.
  • Similar music tech sites send traffic to each other (rekkerd accounts for 6.2% of synthanatomy's referral sources).

Speculation:

  • Free resources and the deals page bring many repeat visits (direct visits 20%), and vendors submitting news on their own keeps the content supply cost very low.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue not disclosed; monetization is through affiliate commissions, display ads and donations.
  • No absolute traffic figures; a third-party site compares it with 46 similar sites (xranks), which shows it has some scale in this niche.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The founding year, operator and monetization come from the original text of the site's About page and deals page, so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: most Google results were unrelated (CVEs, health); Wayback page snapshot fetches were rate-limited (429), so the site's original look in 2005 could not be seen; the early promotion channels (forums like KVR) are only speculation; Ronnie's full name and country were not found; the WordPress API returned 403, so the total number of posts could not be counted.
