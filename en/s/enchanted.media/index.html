Basics

  • Product: After Effects / Premiere Pro templates, overlays, motion graphics, green-screen footage; plenty of free items + paid items at $10–$20 each, plus bundle discounts (https://www.enchanted.media/).
  • Operator: Enchanted Studios Ltd (UK company number 15654699), address in Martlesham Heath, Suffolk; the About page lists Simon Brough as Creative Director, working in graphic/screen design, animation and 3D in advertising since the mid-1990s (https://www.enchanted.media/about-us/).
  • Predecessor: Envato author EnchantedStudios, joined in July 2010, 12 items (1 VideoHive + 11 GraphicRiver), 14,725 sales in total, 621 followers (https://videohive.net/user/enchantedstudios).
  • Monetization: single-item sales + affiliates (Adobe Stock, Uppbeat, Redbubble links carry commissions, and the site has a "Become an Affiliate" page).
  • Domain: registered 2015-04-02 per RDAP; first Wayback capture 2015-09-05.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,327 visits in September; US 24%, India 11%, UK 4%; social 44.6% (mainly YouTube, then Reddit and WhatsApp); search 40.5%, almost all non-brand keywords (free template after effect, vfx free download, raining overlay), brand keywords 8.9%.

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: the founder already had an Envato author identity (since 2010, 14k+ sales), which shows he had a body of work among AE template buyers; after the independent site launched he posted free-asset threads on Reddit (r/eFreebies and AE-related subreddits).
  • Speculation: the first traffic mainly came from free-asset posts plus template showcase videos on his own YouTube channel (EnchantedStudiosHD); there is no evidence of directly sending customers over from Envato (Envato rules usually forbid external links, not verified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free items bring in traffic: the home page has long put the "Free" menu and "Most Popular Free Files" in the most prominent spot; most Trustpilot reviews are about free items.
  • Non-brand long-tail search: brand keywords are only 8.9%, the rest are long-tail terms like "free template after effect / raining overlay".
  • YouTube showcase videos: social traffic is mainly YouTube, and the website links to the YouTube channel.

Speculation:

  • Free items → users register accounts → pushed paid items, bundle discounts and affiliate products; seasonal content (Christmas, Black Friday templates) brings a wave of search each year.

Revenue and scale clues

  • About 4k visits a month, paid items at $10–20, so monthly revenue is probably in the hundreds to one or two thousand dollars (speculation, not verified).
  • Envato's 14,725 cumulative sales is the only hard number for the founder's historical scale.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration/first-capture dates, operator, Envato history and Trustpilot scale have primary page evidence, so credibility is medium to high.
  • Gaps: search results were mostly unrelated generic pages; the original Reddit posts would not open, so only summaries were used; YouTube subscriber and view counts were not obtained (neither YouTube nor SocialBlade returned content); no interview or "takeoff event" was found, and this site looks like long, slow accumulation with no obvious takeoff point.
