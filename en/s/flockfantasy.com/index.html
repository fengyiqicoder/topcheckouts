Basics

  • Product: Dynasty / Redraft fantasy football tools: analyst rankings, rookie scouting, a trade calculator and trade values, custom draft boards, ADP, syncing Sleeper/ESPN/Yahoo leagues, Best Ball tools, paid creator team reviews; a paid Discord group.
  • Company: Flock Network LLC (App Store seller), US.
  • Team: the hosts of the YouTube channel @Flockfantasy; Mason Dodd is visible in video descriptions (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hXKatjfCsu8, summary only); the X account @FlockFantasy's bio reads 'Best ball, fantasy football and DFS livestreams, from Davis Mattek', streaming Monday, Thursday, and Friday (summary only, https://x.com/FlockFantasy). The full list of founders is unverified.
  • App: iOS 'Flock Fantasy' (id 6799588005), listed 2026-08-28, 194 ratings, 4.76 stars (2026-10).
  • Pricing: Flocker $6.95/month, Mother Flocker $8.30/month (annual-billing discounted); some features are free. Videos use the promo code FLOCK to drive traffic. The 2022 guide bundle was $89.
  • Video sponsorships: YouTube descriptions carry Polymarket US and trading-type ads ('Trading is risky'), showing the channel itself takes sponsorships (summary only).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: Flock's own YouTube/TikTok audience ('the flock' is what the fan base is called), who first paid for the draft guide on Shopify and then migrated to the self-built platform.
  • Where new users come from: the content accounts keep sending traffic. Several YouTube livestreams a week + 65K TikTok followers + a free Discord group -> people search the brand name and come in (profile: direct 75.6%, search 18.3%, brand keywords 93.9% of search). There is also ESPN referral at 1.45%.
  • 2026 Flock League: big streamers (Jynxzi and others) play in the league, cut into short videos and distributed across multiple TikTok accounts, bringing gaming/streaming fans into fantasy football.
  • Cross-channel collaborations (Domain Fantasy Football, Fantasy Stock Exchange) send traffic to each other.
  • Speculation: the August draft season each year is a fixed peak; the August 2026 peak stacked draft season + the App launch + the Flock League. The tool itself (a draft board that invites league members) has a bit of an invite feature, but it is not the main source.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 65K TikTok followers; the App has 194 ratings in two months.
  • Subscriptions $6.95-8.30/month + video sponsorships (Polymarket and others) + draft guides.
  • Checkout visits 40K in August. MRR not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass check: confirmed that Flock Fantasy was originally a fantasy football YouTube/TikTok content account with the website as its monetization tool, so the growth engine changed from 'Creator supply' to 'Creators or community' and Pure product-led spread changed to 'No'; added the Mason Dodd / Davis Mattek leads, the Reddit word-of-mouth posts, and the Flock League standalone site and clip accounts.
  • Gaps: YouTube subscriber count, channel creation date, and the division of roles among founders were not obtained (YouTube/TikTok/Socialblade pages could not be fetched, Reddit was blocked, all summaries). The respective roles of Davis Mattek and Mason Dodd are unverified.
