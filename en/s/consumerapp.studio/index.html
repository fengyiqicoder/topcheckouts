Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Price experiments: $97 → $29 → $9 → $29 → $97, combined with a countdown and strikethrough price.
  • Email funnel: free chapter (2026-02) → quiz for a discount code (2026-10).
  • AI agent selling point: in the second half of 2026 it added slash commands for "hand the cards to an AI agent", riding the AI coding tools trend.
  • The landing page was featured (with backlinks) by several web-design inspiration sites.

Speculation:

  • 100% US + social source Facebook + a quiz/discount-code landing page matches the typical structure of Meta paid ads; whether the GTM container holds a Meta pixel could not be confirmed (the page source shows only GA4 and GTM).
  • A high share of brand-keyword search suggests people saw it elsewhere (ads/posts) and then searched "product design playbook".

Revenue and scale clues

  • The home page claims "Bought by 1.8k+" (2026-10). With transaction prices between $9 and $97, cumulative sales would be roughly $30k–$150k (speculation; the buyer count is self-reported).
  • The "$100K MRR" in the testimonial is a client company's figure, not this product's revenue.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration time, price changes and changes in the self-reported buyer count have Wayback evidence; the author's LinkedIn post has the original text.
  • Gaps: the channel behind the mid-2026 scale-up (ads, TikTok, newsletter partnerships or Product Hunt) has no evidence, so launch method and growth engine are both written as "Not found"; the X page would not open; "1.8k+" is self-reported and unverified.
