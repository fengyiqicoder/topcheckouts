Basics

  • Product: Prepaid eSIM with an Estonian +372 number, usable in 154+ countries, supports SMS and calls. Number €2.99 (€3.66 in 2025); data pay-as-you-go from €0.88/GB, €9 = 10GB across 42 countries; balance never expires, one use within 365 days keeps the number; free SMS reception; you can pick your own number; first 9 device transfers are free. Sister brand eSIM.TF (shared FAQ).
  • Who is behind it: The site only says "eSIM.GG", with no company entity or founder; hosted on Cloudflare; has /zh and /zh-CN pages; supports Alipay (a real address is required). The underlying carrier was not found. No funding seen.
  • Launch date:.gg domain registered 2024-08-06; first Wayback record 2024-12-03; by 2025-02-13 the homepage was already selling "Estonian Numbers from €3"; in 2026-07 the homepage claimed "16K+ travelers connected".

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Cold start: first post on the Chinese number-retention card players' forum (Naixi), and the nickname "Turtle Telecom" became the memorable hook; personal blogs wrote activation tutorials.
  • Coupon-code spread: forum-specific code (coupon=naixi), users' personal codes (Honrine), codes circulating online (furina). Users post coupon codes for each other in threads, with a rebate/referral flavor (whether referrers actually get a rebate is unverified).
  • Alipay payment lowers the barrier for Chinese users.
  • From 2026 the pitch widened to "use ChatGPT/TikTok directly", with referrals from AI/tech communities such as linux.do and V2EX.
  • Speculation:
  • Telegram and QQ groups (qq.com referrals) where people show off "tested and working" drive secondary sharing.
  • Existing users return to top up data, so direct visits make up 70%.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The 2026-07 homepage claims 16K+ users. Ticket size is €2.99 activation plus data top-ups; from 21,000 checkout visits last month, monthly revenue is estimated at tens of thousands of euros (pure speculation).
  • Data anomaly warning: in our table esim.gg has exactly the same two months ago/last month numbers as several other sites (4,919 / 21,490), likely tiered estimates from the data source; also it was already selling in 2025 while the table shows 0 for April to July, so do not treat it as exact traffic.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch date and first Chinese-forum post are backed by Wayback and the original post, so credibility is high; the operating entity, underlying carrier, and whether coupon codes carry a rebate are still not found; the original linux.do / V2EX posts could not be opened.
  • Second-pass check: launch date changed from "speculated to start selling 2026-08" to "launched 2024-12, already selling by 2025-02"; added the first Naixi forum post (2025-02-24), the origin of the "Turtle Telecom" nickname, coupon-code spread, the 2026-07 homepage adding ChatGPT/TikTok as a selling point and 16K users; takeoff year changed from 2026 to 2025.
