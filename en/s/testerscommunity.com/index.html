Overview

Testerscommunity (testerscommunity.com): Managed service plus free community app supplying real Android testers to meet Google Play's 14-day closed testing requi. Category on TopCheckouts: Developer tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Testerscommunity to a checkout payment page — #300 of the 607 sites we track. That is 18% down from the April–June monthly average of 15,733.

Total website visits that month were about 49,821 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 25.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 64%, search 27%, referrals/email/paid 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 91%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: NT$399 per app one-time (Starter); NT$699 per app one-time (Pro).
