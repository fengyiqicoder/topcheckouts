Overview

Bibguru (bibguru.com): Free citation and bibliography generator for APA, MLA, Harvard, Chicago. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 6,030 visits went from Bibguru to a checkout payment page — #513 of the 607 sites we track. That is 33% down from the April–June monthly average of 9,035.

Total website visits that month were about 545,940 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 1.1 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 66%, direct 26%, social 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 53%, with queries like “formato apa”, “apa”, “citar en apa”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
