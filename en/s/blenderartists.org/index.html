Blenderartists (blenderartists.org): Blender Artists Community forum for Blender 3D artwork, support, resources and jobs. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,524 visits went from Blenderartists to a checkout payment page — #545 of the 607 sites we track. That is 41% down from the April–June monthly average of 7,629.
