Basics

  • Product: a 24/7 non-stop AI-generated video TV station. Viewers submit / vote in chat to decide what plays next, split into channels such as banana / cat / space / news / game show; likes give the author karma, and the leaderboard wins cash prizes ($8,500 total); the "buy ad" button in the top right goes to a Stripe payment link (buy.stripe.com/…) to buy an ad break.
  • Maker: the page is signed "Infinite Slop by @levelsio + fal.ai", and the fal logo links to fal.ai/minimax-h3-max (presumably a co-branded promotion for MiniMax's new video model). levelsio is a Dutch indie developer (Nomad List, Photo AI, fly.pieter.com, etc.) with hundreds of thousands of X followers; fal.ai is a US generative-media inference platform (funded, common industry knowledge, not separately verified here).
  • Status: the homepage banner reads "Infinite Slop has ended… Live AI TV continues at FAL.live" and asks winners to leave an email to claim prizes.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: viewers submit → get aired → earn karma from likes → win cash prizes, which motivates users to share "my video is playing right now" to canvass votes; "buy ad" lets brands or individuals pay for an ad break, producing revenue.
  • Speculation: when user A's submission airs, they screenshot or screen-record it and post to X / TikTok to get friends to like it (the vote-canvassing mechanic naturally drives spread); "AI slop" is itself a hot meme of 2025–26 and comes with built-in talkability.
  • Evidence (second-pass check): levelsio's own X account (hundreds of thousands of followers) and email newsletter (185K readers) were the only ignition source; milestone posts like "concurrent viewers" and "people watched" kept pushing traffic back.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue source: selling ad slots via a Stripe payment link; compute costs sponsored by fal.ai (speculation).
  • 37K checkout visits last month, which is considerable for an event site that lived only about 3 weeks.
  • Prize pool $8,500 (stated on the page).
  • Ad slot unit price and actual revenue not found.
