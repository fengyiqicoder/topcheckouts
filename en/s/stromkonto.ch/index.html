Basics

  • Product: a "virtual storage account" for Swiss solar owners: surplus power is credited to the account, used in winter/at night or for EV charging, and the balance can be sold back (8 Rp./kWh + 2 Rp. for proof of origin) or carried forward, with year-end shortfalls topped up at 15 Rp./kWh; data is collected by a whatwatt Go device plugged into the smart meter's customer interface; has an app (https://www.stromkonto.ch/faq).
  • Pricing (profile file): LIGHT CHF 5.90/month, Basic CHF 14.90/month (plus a CHF 90 device fee); Business CHF 1.00 per kWp per month.
  • Operator: in 2024–2025 the Impressum was Siima Solution GmbH (Bargen BE, CHE-448.407.000), managing director Dominik Hanisch, with "Stromkonto" as its registered trademark (Wayback 2024-10 Impressum); in 2026 the FAQ names the operator as NRGY One AG (Aarberg), and the soaktuell advertorial also says it is driven by NRGY One AG, speculation: the business moved to a new company or was recapitalized/restructured (not verified).
  • Partner page: solar/electrical firms such as Enicon AG, Dauwalder Elektro, Roost + Aeberli, Staehelin Solar, Helios Solar, MR Elektro, who "advise customers on site and offer Stromkonto directly" (https://www.stromkonto.ch/partner).
  • Domain: first Wayback capture 2024-05-08 (no RDAP registration date for.ch domains).
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,221 visits in September (newly appeared), Switzerland 100%, German; search 47.2% (only the brand keyword stromkonto), direct 37%, referrals 9.4%.

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: the wemakeit crowdfunding pre-sale plus the individual supporters and solar companies (Profisolar GmbH and others) listed on the on-site thank-you page show that the first users were the local solar scene and the founder's contacts.
  • Speculation: most crowdfunding backers were probably solar owners around Bern/Solothurn (partner entric is also in this area), i.e. starting from a personal and local industry circle.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Installer channel: solar/electrical companies recommend Stromkonto directly at installation (Partner page).
  • Regulatory-tailwind narrative: rising power prices, falling feed-in tariffs, the new 2026 virtual ZEV/LEG rules, backed by paid local-media advertorials.

Speculation:

  • User word of mouth + community ("wachsende Prosumer-Community"); a future feature to "sell power to neighbors / gift it" would bring an invite mechanism if launched, but it is not live yet.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No user count. Monthly fee CHF 5.90–14.90 + CHF 90 device fee + the spread on power trading (buy at 8–10 Rp., sell at 15 Rp.).
  • Hiring a part-time CTO and relying on a crowdfunding pre-sale suggests that both the team and revenue are very small (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Launch time, the change of operator, the crowdfunding, partners and advertorials all have evidence; credibility is medium.
  • Gaps: the wemakeit and startup.ch pages both returned 403, so the crowdfunding amount and number of backers were not obtained; the relationship between NRGY One AG and Siima was not clarified; no user-count data.
