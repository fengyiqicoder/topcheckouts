Basics

  • Product: a medical cannabis telehealth consultation + pharmacy delivery platform. Consultation from €14.99, medication from €4.99 plus shipping; private medical service, not covered by public insurance, no data reported to insurers; the questionnaire shows partner pharmacies' live stock; live delivery tracking; Intercom online support. Advertised indications: chronic pain, sleep disorders, loss of appetite, nausea, anxiety.
  • Same-city express cities: Berlin, Hamburg, München, Köln, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Dresden.
  • Company: Rapid Health GmbH, Zossen (Brandenburg), HRB 39679 P (Amtsgericht Potsdam), managing director Richard Wiesner, VAT ID DE370323516 (site Impressum). Founded 2024.
  • Founder: Richard Wiesner, formerly at BCG's healthcare group and a founding team member of the German instant medicine delivery platform MAYD (https://rapid-health.de/en/). Funding not disclosed; hiring covers engineering, operations and medical roles.
  • Scale: the company site says it has served 50,000+ patients; Trustpilot has 1,199 reviews at 4.8, 567 in the last 12 months.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The site's titles and body copy are packed with high-intent search terms like "Cannabis Rezept", "Lieferung in Minuten" and "Berlin, Hamburg & München", with Organic + Paid Search leading the channel mix.
  • The differentiating selling point "same-city 60–90 minute delivery + live stock + live tracking", aimed at repeat patients (who need a new prescription when theirs runs out).
  • Trustpilot reviews almost all praise "fast delivery", showing express delivery is the main word-of-mouth point; the page shows the company has "not actively invited reviews recently", but the 4.8 rating is already enough to support trust in search results.

Speculation:

  • First users: local Berlin plus Google searches like "Cannabis Rezept online"; the founder's delivery and pharmacy partnership experience from MAYD meant 40-minute delivery in Berlin was possible from the start.
  • Medical cannabis falls under drug advertising regulation, so social ads are restricted, and it relies mainly on Google search (including paid bidding), comparison/review sites, and community word of mouth such as Reddit r/germantrees.
  • Repeat purchase is the main revenue: the same patient renews the prescription and buys medication monthly, and Direct being the top channel suggests a large share of returning users.
  • There may be referral/promo code rewards (common among peers), which were not found this time.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Consultation from €14.99 + medication margin / delivery fees; 38–41K checkout page visits per month. If 10–20K of those are paid consultations, monthly consultation fee revenue is on the order of €150–300K (pure speculation, excluding medication).
  • 50,000+ patients (self-reported on the site); if each repeats 3–4 times a year, annual consultation + medication GMV may be in the millions of euros (pure speculation). Funding not disclosed.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch time and company entity are backed by WHOIS + Impressum evidence, so credible.
  • Second follow-up check: found the founder's background (BCG healthcare group, MAYD founding team), 50,000+ patients, and the earliest Trustpilot review 2024-09-26 (40-minute delivery in Berlin). Search results are drowned out by same-name "quick green" terms (lawns, loans), no media coverage or founder interview was found, and the specific source of the first users is still speculation.
  • Funding not found.
  • The specific progress of the regulatory tightening is not verified.
