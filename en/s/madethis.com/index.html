Basics

  • Product: an autonomous business-running platform. It generates the website, payments, customer management and marketing tasks from an idea, covering online stores, courses, digital products, services, subscription boxes, booking platforms and more; available on web and mobile; "first 3 tasks free, no credit card needed".
  • Pricing: the channel profile records self-serve plans at $49 / $79 / $199 per month (can start free); a 2026-10 search snippet shows the pricing page changed to "AI employees... starting at $5,000/mo, with a free AI audit first", split into Starter / Growth / Scale and Enterprise.
  • Team (YC page): Jacob Wright (CEO, formerly founding engineer and FDE manager at RunPod, which reached $100M revenue), Cambree Bernkopf (design; at 19 led product design for Remi YC W22, runs Bernie Studios), Santiago Gomez Paz (formerly founder of Zaymo YC W24, MIT CSAIL). 3 people, San Francisco. Previously launched Castari ("Vercel for AI Agents") on YC.
  • Domain: registered 2003-10-17 (RDAP); a 301 redirect through 2024-2025, the 2025-09 snapshot reads "MadeThis.com is For Sale | BrandBucket"; became the new product from 2026-05-03.

The first users: mainly non-technical American side-hustlers brought in by Facebook/Instagram ads (one person each on Trustpilot and the self-run review site said they came from scrolling Facebook and seeing an Instagram ad), plus referrals from acquaintances (one reviewer was brought in by their sister). No evidence of B2B users from the YC circle.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid social ads: UGC-style ads on Instagram, and "comment to get a 50% off code" Reels (2026-08).
  • Trust assets: the self-run madethisreviews.com (explicitly says "operated by MadeThis", 48 reviews, rated 5.0), plus a synced Testimonial.to Wall of Love, used to intercept searches like "madethis reviews / is it legit".
  • Founder-led support: reviews mention several times that Jacob and Santi fixed problems directly.

Speculation:

  • 87% of search is brand keywords, showing people see the ads first and then search. The old domain's authority helps SEO only a little.
  • The 2026-10 shift to a $5,000/month enterprise tier may reflect weak unit economics from consumer ad acquisition, or pressure from refund disputes.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The homepage "N+ businesses launched" counter shows 0 in the snapshot (client-side rendered); the actual number was not obtained.
  • Self-serve plans $49-199/month; with 13 Trustpilot reviews plus 45 ratings on its own site, paying users are estimated at a few hundred to one or two thousand (speculation).
  • Standard YC funding $500K (from memory, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch (no earlier report). The evidence for the domain change of hands, the new site launch and the ad channel is fairly solid (RDAP, Wayback, the YC page, Trustpilot).
  • Gaps: the exact date of the Castari to MadeThis pivot, the date and numbers of the YC Launch post, user count and revenue were not found; the "polsia.ai clone" allegation is unverified.
