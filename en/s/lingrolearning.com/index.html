Basics

  • Product: LingroLearning (LingroHub platform), digital foreign-language courseware for colleges/high schools: Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Chinese. Flagship Contraseña (video lessons, student eportfolios, branching story lessons), plus former Pearson programs such as Conexiones (authors Zayas-Bazán, Bacon, García); Project Echo free teacher program, Language Incubator, Lingro Ambassadors, LinguaMeeting native-speaker conversation; it also released a gamified app, LingroToGo, in 2017.
  • Team: four co-founders from Pearson / McGraw-Hill (CEO Steve Debow in 2018; co-founder Samantha Alducin, formerly in online courses at a publisher). Julie Sykes became Chief Innovation Officer in 2023-08 and was announced as CEO in 2024-11 (effective 2025-01-01). The company is in New York; funding not found.
  • Student price: about $75 per term (2018 report; in 2022 UCF students complained about the "$75 fee" on Reddit), also bundled through campus bookstores / BibliU inclusive access.
  • Note: unrelated to the Lingro online dictionary by Artur Janc from 2008.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: schools where the authors teach + 40+ fellow teachers trying it, with 5 colleges adopting it for free in fall 2018.
  • Institutional channels: Norton exclusive sales (from 2019), acquiring Pearson-line textbooks to inherit their adopting institutions (2023), BibliU / campus bookstore bundles.
  • One teacher adopting = the whole class forced to pay; students enter via LMS redirects (referrals from csuci.edu, illinoisstate.edu, edu-apps.org).
  • Teacher-side operations: Project Echo, Ambassadors, a Concierge support team (teachers on the Wall of Love praise support rep Alejandro).
  • Speculation: language-teaching conferences and teacher word of mouth bring new institutions; there is no spread among students.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 5 colleges in 2018; about $75 per student per term. Later numbers of institutions and revenue not found; after acquiring the Pearson line the scale should be noticeably larger (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • The 2018 Inside Higher Ed and PR Newswire headline list are highly credible; for the 2023 acquisition, the specific titles and amount are known only for one case, Conexiones.
  • In our data, the 1–3 months ago numbers of 4 sites (focustown / lingro / ink-revolution / snapgen) are exactly identical, possibly a data-bucket artifact, so be cautious.
  • Second-pass check: changed the old report's "nature of the Norton relationship not found" to "Norton became exclusive seller in 2019-01"; added the 2017 CASLS partnership, LingroToGo, the 2023-10 acquisition of former Pearson foreign-language textbooks, the exact date Sykes became CEO in 2024-11, and the BibliU channel.
