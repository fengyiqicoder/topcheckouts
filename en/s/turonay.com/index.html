Basics

  • Product: SMS verification platform - one-time SMS verification numbers and numbers rented by the day/week/month, 180+ countries, 200+ services; if no code arrives the money is automatically returned to the balance; provides an SMS API (Python/PHP/Node.js/Webhook docs) and a reseller interface.
  • Who makes it: the About page names the legal entity as Akolya Limited (UK Companies House 17028511, incorporated 2026-02-12, at a registered agent address in East Ham, London, SIC codes online retail + software development); footer "© 2026 Turonay Digital". The About page also says it "started in 2023 under the slogan 'privacy for everyone'", so it may have operated under another brand before (not verified). In 2026-03 the homepage notice bar promoted the SMM panel radosmedya.com, presumed to be the same operator or a partner (speculation).
  • Pricing: pay per number, from 5 ₺; 10% bonus on a first top-up of 1,000 ₺; the inviter gets 5% of every top-up by the invitee (for life), and the invitee gets 5 ₺ after their first top-up.
  • Domain: turonay.com registered 2026-02-07 (RDAP); earliest blog sitemap lastmod 2026-02-01; first Wayback snapshot 2026-03-01.
  • Traffic (profile file): Turkey 100%; search 55.6% (non-brand about 44.5%: "sms onay", "sanal numara", "sanal numara satın al", "sanal numara whatsapp"), direct 33.3%, social 6.7%.

What can be confirmed is that it began posting blogs a week before registering the domain and quickly rolled out landing pages after launch; search now makes up 55.6% of traffic, of which non-brand terms are 44.5%, so the first users most likely came from Turkish long-tail search (evidence: sitemap dates + traffic structure). In 2026-03 the homepage advertised the SMM panel radosmedya.com, so early on it probably also exchanged traffic with the Turkish SMM/follower-buying scene (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: one landing page for each platform/country/use (whatsapp-onay, fake-numara-alma, ucretsiz-sanal-numara, amerika-abd-sanal-numara-alma, chatgpt-claude-sanal-numara-alma, etc.), in three language versions.
  • Referral commission: the inviter gets a lifetime 5% top-up commission and the invitee gets 5 ₺.
  • First top-up bonus and "refund if no code arrives" lower the cost of trying.
  • Developer API + reseller (Bayilik): lets SMM panels and other SMS verification sites resell.

Speculation:

  • The Turkish SMM/engagement-boosting scene (Telegram groups, forums) is probably the main part of the 6.7% social source.
  • Figures on the About page such as "3.5 million verifications" and "started in 2023" may reuse data from an upstream supplier or an old brand, with some exaggeration.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Homepage counters: about 35K registered users, 168K orders (2026-10). At a unit price of 5-50 ₺, cumulative turnover is roughly in the hundreds of thousands to a million lira range (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The search results were all unrelated pages; all evidence comes from the site, the sitemap, RDAP and Wayback.
  • Gaps: the real operator's identity, the predecessor brand that "started in 2023", the relationship with RadosMEDYA, and the specific promotion channels were not found.
