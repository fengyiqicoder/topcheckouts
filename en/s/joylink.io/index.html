Basics

  • Product: JoyLink, "The Operating System for Affiliate Creators". Tracked deeplinks that open retailer apps (Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Sam's Club, Home Depot, Lowe's, connected through Impact.com), AI social post generation, a real-time Amazon deals/coupon-code library, a Chrome extension, Telegram auto-posting, shoppable Link in Bio (35+ themes), Instagram comment-triggered DMs, invitation-only Boosting (JoyLink pays for ads on creators' past affiliate posts and earns the money back through commission share), Creator Connections auto-apply, and JoyLink's own commissions (up to 30%).
  • Pricing: Free; Pro $20/month + billed per click (first 10K clicks included); Enterprise from $99/month. It says the average creator earns 8–15 cents per click.
  • Team/company: not found (the entity name was not captured on the site/terms page; support runs on US Eastern time).
  • Users: top users BrainyBargains (500K), CuponesGratis (667K, Spanish), Austins Deals (355K), Deals For Kids (550K Facebook group members), KickAssDeals, LeaveItOnThePorch, OrlandoQponQueen (Telegram) and others, 22 million followers combined; the FAQ says "thousands of creators" and the Link in Bio page says "120+ creators".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Every deal post carries a joylink.io/xxx short link (the site demo shows joylink.io/stanley-40oz), and the bottom of the link page says "Powered by JoyLink ♥": branded output.
  • The "Wall of love" uses top deal accounts as endorsement (Heather Rhodes 642K, favoritefinds_ 478K, brainybargains 497K) to recruit new people from the same circle.
  • Boosting: JoyLink pays for FB/IG ads on creators' affiliate posts itself and earns the money back via commission; currently open to Walmart Creator Program members (invitation only). This is both bait for recruiting top creators and explains why Paid Social is a top-three channel (site /boosting).
  • Pricing moved from a $13/month flat fee (2025-06) to $20 + per-click billing, so revenue grows with creators' traffic.
  • The referral program affiliate.joylink.io + an official FB group for community.
  • Support that "replies in minutes" and a team that helps creators expand to Walmart (user reviews): heavy ops service for top accounts.

Speculation:

  • It started by pulling in members one by one through the founding team's private relationships with the US deal-reposting scene (FB group owners, Telegram channel owners), one big account bringing others in the same circle ("Existing audience").
  • Per-click billing + its own commission share means revenue scales with top deal accounts' traffic.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pro $20/month + usage; Enterprise from $99/month; plus JoyLink commission share and Boost commission share.
  • "tens of millions of clicks monthly" (site structured copy); a user pool with 22 million followers combined.
  • No public MRR or funding.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch time (2024-07) is credible; the product's evolution has Wayback evidence.
  • Team, company and funding were not found (this session's WebSearch quota ran out).
  • Second follow-up check: added the old 2025-06 pricing ($13 flat monthly), the Boosting mechanism (JoyLink pays ad costs, tied to the Walmart Creator Program), the FB group and referral program; for the core question "where did the first users come from" the search results are almost entirely same-name noise (Joyline, JoyLinkABC, joy.link), no founder interview was found, and the team is still not found (/about and /terms both return 404).
  • Speculation (unverified): the two months ago surge may relate to Boost ad spend around Walmart's back-to-school season.
  • The source mechanism of the 269K checkout page visits two months ago is unclear; they may be a third-party checkout page referenced by deal posts rather than JoyLink's own subscription revenue, and need manual review.
