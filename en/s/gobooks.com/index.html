Overview

Gobooks (gobooks.com): Online store for Go (board game) books as EPUB/GoBook downloads, tied to the SmartGo One app. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Gobooks to a checkout payment page — #228 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 7,376 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 47%, search 38%, referrals 9%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 47%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.
