Overview

Chutes (chutes.ai): Serverless open-source decentralized platform for running open-source AI models via API. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Chutes to a checkout payment page — #188 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 15,733.

Total website visits that month were about 74,602 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 23.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 78%, search 18%, social 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 78%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: $10/mo Plus; $20/mo Pro; Enterprise custom; pay-as-you-go per M tokens e.g. D.
