Basics

  • Product: serverless open-source model inference API (OpenAI-compatible), supports deploying any "chute", now leading with TEE (trusted execution environment) private inference; subscriptions Plus $10/month (2,000 calls/day), Pro $20/month (5,000 calls/day), plus usage-based pricing (e.g. DeepSeek V4 Flash $0.44/$1.32 per million tokens). Also does decentralized training (in 2026-09 it trained an 8B model for $6,500 on 240 consumer GPUs across 13 countries).
  • Company: Chutes Global Corp (site footer); team of about 8, core backend by Jon Durbin (author of the open-source model Airoboros), most others use handles. Originally out of Rayon Labs (a Bittensor subnet team; GitHub org created 2024-09). Has a dedicated growth lead, Burhan Khan (LinkedIn title "Head of GTM and Growth"). The Reddit community is run by Mikesoft (@thestreamcode).
  • Funding: no traditional VC funding found; revenue and subsidies depend on Bittensor subnet emissions (after dTAO, subnets have their own alpha tokens).
  • Country: mainly United States (our data shows the US at 55%).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Upstream distribution: OpenRouter routes to Chutes as a provider; coding-agent tools like Kilo list it as a partner backend; taostats.io (Bittensor ecosystem) is the main referral source.
  • Free quota for acquisition: about 200 free DeepSeek calls per day, spread by word of mouth in communities like SillyTavern and the Cursor forum ("Chutes offers deepseek for free").
  • Community operations: the official Reddit community has a dedicated manager, and community members build their own plugins wiring Chutes into coding tools; the Bittensor/crypto crowd is the base (official wording).
  • Reddit ads from 2026-05, i.e. paid ads.
  • Speculation: token subsidies form a flywheel of "low prices → more traffic → more subnet emissions → more subsidy"; roleplay-community Discord and TikTok tutorials ("get free DeepSeek with a Chutes API key") spread it, and traffic from apps like JanitorAI is from memory, unverified. The two months ago checkout spike may correspond to new model launches like DeepSeek V4 (official V4 Flash pricing announced 2026-08-25).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: Plus $10, Pro $20/month, plus usage-based billing.
  • Scale: over 3 trillion tokens/month in 2025-06; 34 trillion cumulative by 2026-09, daily peak 120 billion.
  • 2026-03 official claim: revenue per token +37.7% after cutting volume (absolute revenue not disclosed).

Confidence and gaps
