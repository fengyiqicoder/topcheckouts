  • Pricing: 150 free credits on signup; Spark $9/month (600 credits), Creator $19/month (1,500), Pro $49/month (4,000, marked "most popular"), Studio $129/month (12,000); annual billing saves 23%. Every tier says "ByteDance Seedance 2.0 & 2.5 included" and "24/7 engineer support, reply within 5 minutes". Source: https://www.lunostudio.ai/pricing
  • Launch: RDAP shows lunostudio.ai was registered on 2026-03-15; earliest Wayback snapshot is 2026-04-12 (200); Academy articles in the sitemap are dated 2026-04-04 to 04-10. Inferred launch in early 2026-04.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Upstream model window: every subscription tier sells Seedance 2.0/2.5, a third-party comparison article lists it as one of the overseas entry points for Seedance 2.5, and brand searches include "seedance 2.5 luno studio".
  • Community: social traffic is 22.3%, mostly from Reddit; the site's Trust page links a Discord invite (discord.gg/fwqC53zcUM), promising a real person replying within 5 minutes.
  • Content: Academy masterclasses and a 75-prompt Seedance 2 library, with original authors credited and people invited to tag @lunostudioai to add more, which makes it easy to get reshared on X/Reddit.
  • Direct traffic is 70.3%, mostly returning users; search is only 4.5%, of which 73.9% is brand keywords.

Speculation:

  • The first users most likely came from posts and replies in Reddit's AI video communities (such as Seedance and AI video subreddits), with Discord to hold them; but Reddit pages would not open, so no specific posts were obtained (unconfirmed).
  • The entity name "AI Simply Explained" hints the team may have previously run AI explainer content or accounts with an existing audience (unconfirmed; the YouTube channel SimplyExplained AI turned up no link to Luno).
  • No evidence of affiliate payouts or referral rewards, and none that the generated videos carry a brand watermark, so this does not count as "branded output".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing runs $9-$129/month; cards are charged through Stripe (Trust page).
  • Our data covers only channel shares; no public revenue or user numbers. About six months since launch; no scale clues found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The site is front-end rendered and only the title was captured from the homepage; product and pricing information comes from server-side text on the pricing, terms, trust and academy pages and from the sitemap.
  • Google results were almost all irrelevant (SQL, CVEs, generic AI video rankings), and Reddit and Wayback pages could not be opened with WebFetch, so the specific first-user posts, the founder's identity and the takeoff date were not found.
  • The "New-platform window" call rests on: launch timing right after the Seedance 2 surge, every subscription tier headlining Seedance, the combined search terms, and third-party channel articles; it is an inference backed by indirect evidence.
