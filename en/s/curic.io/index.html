Curic ("curic4su" = Curic for SketchUp) is a small SketchUp plugin studio that since at least 2016 has shipped a steady stream of small tools that patch gaps in native SketchUp (Face Array, Section, DIO line drawing, Offset/Intersect, Extend, Stretch, Hidden, MiniMap, Outer Shell, etc.). Each release gets a demo video posted to the official SketchUp forum, X, YouTube and Facebook; users arrive via the SketchUp ecosystem's forums and plugin-name searches, payments run through Gumroad, free plugins bring people in, and paid plugins plus the Curic Studio bundle monetize. In 2023 it moved from curic4su.com to curic.io; after the old domain lapsed it was hijacked to serve malicious PowerShell commands (exposed on the forum in 2026-09), and the new site's last month traffic jumped +914% month over month, presumably as existing users were forced over to the new domain.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: SketchUp extensions: Curic Section (section drawings), DIO 2 (draw lines on objects), OI (offset + intersect), Extend, Stretch, Section View, Hidden, MiniMap (pick camera positions on a 2D plan), Cursor, Publish, Teleport Tool, Measure++, Face Array and more; also a free-extensions section and the bundled Curic Studio product; takes custom plugin development work.
  • Pricing: sold on Gumroad; prices not captured.
  • Who built it: official forum account curic4su, posts signed "Hai"; country and team size not found ("Hai" may be a Vietnamese name, Speculation).
  • Launch date: X @curic4su posted "New Extension for @SketchUp - Curic Face Array" on 2016-09-14 (search summary); curic4su.com registered 2019-12-03, first Wayback capture 2020-07; curic.io first Wayback capture 2023-10-31 (RDAP has no data for.io).
  • Traffic (profile): search 45.5% (plugin name + brand: curic outer shell, curic extend to; brand keywords 84.9%), direct 39.4%, referrals 9.1%; Brazil 48%, Japan 17%, India 15%; last month +914% month over month.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Every new plugin gets its own thread on the official SketchUp forum (the Related topics list shows a string of Curic release threads), with a demo video.
  • The Free Extensions section draws people in; paid plugins and Curic Studio are sold on Gumroad, with holiday discounts.
  • Plugin-name search: brand keywords 85%, with most queries being "curic + plugin name."

Speculation:

  • Brazil, Japan and India have many architecture/interior-design SketchUp users, and YouTube tutorial creators spread the word on their own (Speculation).
  • The last month spike is most likely the old domain lapsing and in-plugin links plus existing users moving to curic.io, not genuine new growth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No revenue or user numbers found. A small plugin studio; annual revenue presumably in the tens of thousands to low six figures in USD (Speculation, no evidence).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Useful search results were two SketchUp forum threads, X summaries and Gumroad titles; YouTube, Facebook and the Gumroad store page came back empty, and Wayback was unavailable part of the time.
  • The developer's real identity, country, prices and sales all not found; the 2016 takeoff year is based on the earliest plugin post on X.
