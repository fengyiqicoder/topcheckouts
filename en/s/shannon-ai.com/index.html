A small US team, Shannon Lab LLC (TrustMRR founder Felix Devon @future_dev_), registered the domain in 2025-10 and launched in 2025-11. It is wrapped as safety research on "red-teaming LLMs", but the actual selling point is "uncensored AI" (Refusal layers: 0): it says it distilled 15,000 Claude Opus / GPT Pro samples into uncensored open-source Kimi and Mistral models, offering free chat plus a cheap API. Acquisition relies almost entirely on search: the main terms are "uncensored ai / unfiltered ai / uncensored ai tools", then it spreads research landing pages such as "AI hacker", "pentest AI", "uncensored security AI" and "uncensored image generator". In 2026-03 a same-named open-source project, KeygraphHQ/Shannon (AI penetration testing, 10,000+ GitHub stars), went viral, and brand searches for "shannon ai" were very likely lifted along with it (Speculation). Revenue doubled to $5–8k/month from 2026-06, while the founder has kept the business listed for sale on TrustMRR.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: uncensored LLM chat + API (Shannon 2 / Shannon 3.1 / Shannon Pro 1.6), with memory, web search, skills and visible reasoning, plus uncensored image generation/editing; publicly shared conversation pages (/share/…). Positioning copy: "frontier red-team lab for LLM safety".
  • Team: Felix Devon (TrustMRR lists him as founder, about 7k followers on X; the HUNT0 page calls him CTO), Axel Minh (CFO), HUNT0 publisher Lucius McArcher. Real identities are hard to verify.
  • Pricing: free version available; API Shannon 3.1 $3.35 per million tokens (same price for input and output), Shannon 2 cheaper; also subscriptions (TrustMRR shows about 600 active subscriptions). The founder says revenue from direct crypto payments is 3 times Stripe's.
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2025-10-01; earliest Wayback 2025-12-19 (copy at the time: "Break your AI before attackers do… NIST & ISO compliant"); in 2026-03 the title changed to "Expert-Level Uncensored AI Assistant".
  • Traffic: search 54.6%, direct 34%, social 6.8% (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram); US, India, Venezuela rank highest; search terms uncensored ai, shannon ai, uncensored ai tools, unsensored ai, unfiltered ai.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: pitches the high-intent terms "uncensored ai / unfiltered ai" and spreads topic landing pages such as ai-hacker, pentest-ai, uncensored-security-ai and uncensored image.
  • User-generated public share pages (/share/…) are indexed, bringing long-tail traffic.
  • A free tier + cheap API attracts developers; monetizes via subscriptions + crypto payments.

Speculation:

  • After the same-named open-source project KeygraphHQ/Shannon went viral in 2026-03, searches for "shannon ai" and "AI pentest" were partly intercepted (the site specifically built pentest-ai and ai-hacker pages); the timing of revenue doubling in 2026-06/07 is close but does not match exactly, and causation is unconfirmed.
  • Tightening censorship in mainstream models pushes "uncensored" demand outward; sites like this generally grow on SEO + community word of mouth.
