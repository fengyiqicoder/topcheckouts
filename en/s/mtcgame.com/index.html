Basics

  • Product: game top-up cards, gift cards (Steam, Xbox, PSN, iTunes, Google Play, Netflix, Spotify, Razer Gold, Valorant, LoL, TikTok/Douyin diamond cards, etc.), mobile game direct top-ups (PUBG Mobile UC, Free Fire, MLBB, Genshin Impact, Bigo Live, Uplive, Yalla Ludo, Jawaker), and low-price-region Xbox game purchasing. Has iOS/Android/Huawei apps, wholesale (wholesale@), KYC/AML policies; claims "PayPal, credit cards + 500+ local payment methods" (r/MTCGAME description, summary only).
  • Entity: started by a Turkish team (the 2010 site was entirely in Turkish). Now: MTCGAME CO., LIMITED (Hong Kong). Trustpilot registered address is 25/F Bank of America Tower, Central, Hong Kong, with a UK +44 phone; LinkedIn lists headquarters in Wong Chuk Hang, Hong Kong, 51–200 employees; Tracxn lists 44 employees, no funding, registered address Sepapaja tn 6 (a common e-residency agent address in Tallinn, Estonia), and "founded 1997" (possibly a data error, inconsistent with the 2008 domain registration). Employee Sinan Bağ (a Turkish name) handled international business development at MTCGAME CO., LIMITED from 2018-07 to 2022-12 (LinkedIn, summary only). Founder Not found.
  • Self-reported: 5M+ active users (Trustpilot company profile).
  • Localization: Next.js site with about 130 locales, including ar-DZ, ar-MA, ar-TN, fr-CI, etc.
  • Funding: none (Tracxn).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Origins: the Turkish online gaming scene (Facebook page, game top-up cards); imitators existed by 2010, showing it was well known in that circle.
  • Current traffic structure: direct 65% (mostly repeat purchases by existing users), search 25% (of which 22% is paid search, mainly brand keywords), indicating a mature repeat-purchase business rather than growth from new channels.
  • Multilingual long-tail product pages + local payment coverage across Africa/Middle East/South Asia win players in markets with low credit card penetration; cited in top-up guides by mobile gaming media like Sportskeeda.
  • Official social accounts (Facebook @MtcGamee, Instagram, r/MTCGAME, X) constantly post new game/new card listings.
  • Large volumes of Trustpilot reviews as trust signal, but suspected review manipulation (the platform has removed the score).

Speculation:

  • Algeria becoming the top market: international credit cards are hard to use locally while Free Fire/PUBG are popular, so top-up resellers and internet cafe card vendors buy wholesale from or send traffic to MTCGAME (the Referrals channel supports this).
  • Cross-region price differences (Turkey and other low-price regions) create arbitrage demand.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 5M+ active users; 44 employees (Tracxn) or 51–200 (LinkedIn); no funding.
  • About 1.52M total site visits last month, about 168K to checkout pages (topcheckouts #7/607). Revenue Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Turkish origin, 2009 start and early product categories are backed by first-hand Wayback pages, credible. The Hong Kong entity and Trustpilot data are directly verifiable.
  • Second follow-up check: added the Hong Kong entity MTCGAME CO., LIMITED, the Estonian registered address, headcount, the self-reported 5M users, the 48K Trustpilot reviews with the score removed, the Reddit fake-review accusations, and the traffic structure (direct 65%, Algeria/South Africa/Nigeria); the earlier report's "Trustpilot review count undetermined" is now 48,347; the growth engine now includes official social account operations.
  • Still missing: founder; the specific source of Algerian traffic (distributors?); Tracxn's "founded 1997" conflicts with the domain year and was not adopted.
