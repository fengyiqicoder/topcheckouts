Overview

Photofeeler (photofeeler.com): Photo-testing tool giving business, social and dating photos trait scores (smart, trustworthy, attractive) from real peo. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Photofeeler to a checkout payment page — #276 of the 607 sites we track. That is 73% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,450.

Total website visits that month were about 164,365 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 7.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 81%, search 18%, AI assistants 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 7%, with queries like “websites where women rate men”, “youtube profile picture size”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
