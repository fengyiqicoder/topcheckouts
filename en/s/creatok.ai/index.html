Basics

  • Product: an AI video workspace for TikTok Shop sellers: break down viral product video scripts, remake them with your own product, generate images/voiceover, and schedule posts through TikTok's official API; with small tools for watermark removal, subtitle removal, background removal, upscaling, translation and more; a Chrome extension (TikTok script/product analysis); in the second half of 2026 it released an Open API, a CLI and skills for Claude Code/Codex/OpenClaw.
  • Who built it: the site's About page just says "CreatOK", with no company entity; a Miraheze wiki says "two Spanish entrepreneurs in Barcelona, launched 2025-10" (source unknown, possibly a self-written entry, not verified); the Chrome extension developer email is dev@echosell.com (echosell.com was registered in 2022-07, content not captured), the extension supports English and Simplified Chinese, and China leads traffic at 27%, so the team's background is in doubt.
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2025-07-22; Wayback first captured it on 2025-10-26 (homepage + login page); /pricing, /sora-video-cloning and /sora-video-generation appeared on 2025-11-10.
  • Site's self-reported figures: 500K+ users, 10M+ videos, 20+ countries; "TikTok Official API Gold Service Partner".
  • Pricing: free to start, with subscription tiers such as Pro (profile file; specific prices not found).
  • Traffic (profile file): China 27%, Thailand 14%, Indonesia 8%; direct 49.8%; brand keywords 57.4%, with about 30% of new customers from non-brand search (bg remover, remove subtitles from video online free, subtitle remover); paid search 7.5%; social is only YouTube.

Where the first users came from: no direct evidence. The visible clues: (1) within the first month after launch, all landing pages revolved around "Sora video cloning/generation", riding the search buzz of Sora 2's release; (2) from 2025-12 it built free tool pages such as Sora watermark removal and video subtitle removal, and the non-brand search terms in the profile are exactly these tool terms; (3) Chinese sellers are the largest share, so early promotion was probably in Chinese cross-border e-commerce circles (seller groups, WeChat official accounts) (speculation, no evidence).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free tool pages earn search: subtitle removal, watermark removal, background removal and similar pages are localized (en/zh/zh-TW/ja), with about 30% non-brand search (profile file + Wayback).
  • Chasing hot models: Sora → Seedance 2 → Kling/Veo, each time with review/comparison blog posts and matching landing pages.
  • Listed in AI tool directories (aitools.fyi, aitoolly, startupaitools, saaspirate, etc.).
  • Paid search 7.5%.

Speculation:

  • Videos sellers make with CreatOK carry no obvious branding when posted to TikTok, so the product itself is hard to self-spread.
  • Most of the "500K+ users" are probably people using the free small tools; the share of paying sellers is unknown.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site self-reports 500K+ users, 10M+ generated videos, 20+ countries (2026-10).
  • The Chrome extension has only about 1,000 users.
  • Revenue and funding not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The "launch", "review" and "tiktok" search groups were mostly unrelated generic articles; the main evidence comes from the site homepage, the first-capture times of pages in Wayback CDX, RDAP, the Chrome Web Store page and the Miraheze wiki.
  • The team's origin is contradictory: the wiki says a Spanish team in Barcelona, but the Chinese version, China being the top traffic source, and the echosell.com email all point to a Chinese team; could not be verified.
  • User numbers are self-reported by the site.
