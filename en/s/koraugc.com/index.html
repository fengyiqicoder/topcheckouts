Basics

  • Product: a UGC creator back office + brand job board. Creator side: analytics across 4 platforms, deal tracking, payments, contract review, invoice generation, Pitch (send proposals to brands not on Kora, 30 credits a week), a Learn course library, Kora Verified certification, free live coaching. Brand side: post UGC / ad / affiliate commission tasks, integrates with Meta, Shopify, AppLovin and TikTok Shop, runs Partnership Ads, sample shipping, brand wallet.
  • Pricing: free creator tier gets 3 applications a day; Pro is billed monthly through Stripe (price not shown on the page). Kora charges nothing when brands pay creators themselves, and a 1.5% platform fee when paying through Kora.
  • Team: footer says "Made with care in California," phone (214) 810-4478 (a Dallas area code), founder not found.
  • Domain: koraugc.com registered 2026-06-28 (GoDaddy); Wayback first captured it 2026-09-26; ScamAdviser had listed it by 2026-08-24.
  • Scale: the homepage says 31,800+ creators; example gigs pay $150-200 per video.
  • Our data: United States 90.15%, United Arab Emirates 3.86%, United Kingdom 2.15%; direct 45%, search 40% (brand keywords 77%, such as kora ugc), social 9% (Instagram, TikTok).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Teaching creators recommend it: "sites for UGC gigs" style Reels on IG rank Kora first; the 9% social in our data comes from Instagram and TikTok, and 77% of the 40% search is brand keywords (people watch a video and then search "kora ugc").
  • Brands bring in creators: every campaign has a join link, and creators who aren't logged in must sign up first after clicking; it can be set to invite-only, with brands using Kora to manage their own private creator lists. This is the invite mechanism of a two-sided platform.
  • Free quota hook: 3 free applications a day -> upgrade to Pro to apply more.
  • Content SEO: guide pages (how to find brand gigs, how to price UGC, best UGC platforms) hold search positions, though the non-brand search share is not high yet.

Speculation:

  • Accumulating 31,800 creators in 4 months is very fast; speculated that on top of creator recommendations there is paid advertising or partnership with UGC training courses / communities, with no evidence.
  • "UGC creator" is itself a popular side-hustle topic on TikTok/IG, with lots of beginners looking for gig channels, and the platform has caught this wave of side-hustle teaching traffic.
  • The UAE's 3.86% may come from a local creator community or a particular brand campaign.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 31,800+ creators (self-claimed); Pro subscription price not public; 1.5% fee on the brand side.
  • Revenue and number of brands not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The registration date, product mechanics, fee structure and the creator-recommendation Reels have primary sources; the 31,800 is a claim on the official site.
  • Most of the search results provided were irrelevant (same-name results like Kora Kurage and Corgi); Reddit queries returned nothing.
  • Gaps: the founder, the exact launch date, where the first creators came from (whether there is paid advertising or KOL partnerships), the Pro price.
