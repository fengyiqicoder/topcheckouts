Overview

Flux-context (flux-context.org): Web platform bundling 29+ third-party AI models for image editing, video and music, credit-based. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Flux-context to a checkout payment page — #448 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 96,253 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 8.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 52%, search 48%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 29%, with queries like “flux ai”, “wan 2.5”, “seedream”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: AI Music 10 credits/generation.
