Basics

  • Product: originally a free AI coding agent connecting to GitHub/GitLab/Jira/Linear/Slack, with a choice of GPT-5 Codex, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Pro and a multi-model "Alloyed" mode; also a CLI, GitHub App, MCP server (as a background agent for Cursor) and Live Preview. The homepage positioning now is "Build businesses with AI. Free forever.": chat so an AI team builds a website or app, connects Stripe to take payments and send invoices, custom domains, business email, runs ads on ChatGPT; there is also a marketplace of AI team templates.
  • Company: Engine Labs (London, UK), began building cto.new in 2025. The funding blog post is signed by Simon Spurrier (CEO), and the account sdspurrier replied under the HN launch post; the people who posted on Product Hunt are Jamie Barton and Michael Ludden.
  • Funding: $5.7M in pre-seed + seed, led by Kindred Ventures and PROfounders, with Wonder Ventures, Entrepreneurs First and Script participating.
  • Launch: official blog launch post dated 2025-10-14; earliest Wayback snapshot 2025-10-15.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: "completely free access to frontier models" is itself the strongest spreading point, picked up on their own by many "free AI freebie" blogs, tool directories and multilingual sites. TAAFT is still the largest referral source.
  • Evidence: the funding PR plus EF/VC backing created concentrated exposure on launch day.
  • Evidence: a steady engineering blog cadence (about one post per week from 2025-10 to 2026-03), cto bench and user case studies keep it top of mind for developers.
  • Evidence: Discord community, X account @ctodotnew, TikTok account @cto.new; product entry points include a GitHub App and MCP (github.com referrals are 3.3%).
  • Evidence: referrals include the Indian coding training institute masaischool.com (1.58%), which may use it in courses, a small-scale institutional distribution.
  • Speculation: after moving to the founder market, it may be acquiring customers through YouTube and TikTok creators posting about "AI side hustles" (social sources include TikTok, Facebook and YouTube), but no specific creators were found.
  • Speculation: sites built by users carry a cto badge, forming "branded output"; unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The homepage claims 250K+ founders and 1M+ businesses (self-reported).
  • Official figures as of 2025-12-31: 300B+ tokens, 10K+ codebases.
  • Pricing: free (with ads, daily limits); Premium from $20; ChatGPT ads require topping up an ad budget.
  • MRR: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: verified the launch date (2025-10-14), the official figures at the end of 2025 and the July 2025 Terminal Bench warm-up using the official blog list; added the lead investors and TAAFT as the main referral source. Takeoff method changed from "Institutional distribution" to "Creator or press spark", because the first users mainly came from the funding PR plus reposts and spread by AI tool directories and blogs; institutional distribution has only one small clue, masaischool.
  • "6,000 developers" appears only in secondhand sources, not in the official blog original.
  • The exact date of the pivot to "starting a company", the reason for the 2026-07 traffic peak, and which creator or video brought the first thousand users are still Not found. Reddit would not open this round, and TikTok account data was not captured.
