ecomflow is a "direct from China" 3PL founded in 2023 and registered in Hong Kong: for DTC brands that manufacture in China, it stores goods in two China warehouses near the factories, packs them under the brand and ships direct to 100+ markets, replacing overseas local warehouses. The site claims 300+ brands served and 10M+ orders in total, with client cases concentrated in small DTC brands in the Netherlands/Europe (ICON Amsterdam, JOGA, Ekster, Clipstic, Dore & Rose, Fluff Co, etc.). It gets new customers through a recurring-commission partner program for e-commerce agencies/consultants (Partner Dashboard v2 with automatic monthly PayPal payouts launched in 2025-04), and retains customers with companion offerings such as its own packaging factory Paking Duck, Postflows and Stockflows. There is no public account of where the first customers came from.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: in-China 3PL fulfillment + a customer dashboard (dashboard.ecomflow.com: inventory, orders, inbound, invoices), plus sourcing, custom packaging (Paking Duck, a co-owned packaging factory), Postflows (branded shipping notifications, free) and Stockflows (AI replenishment forecasting, beta).
  • Eligibility: serves only DTC brands that "manufacture in China and hold inventory"; no inventory-less dropshipping (wording from the FAQ/support pages).
  • Pricing: quoted by product, package size and weight, destination and order volume; no public price (profile file).
  • Who runs it: the About page says only "founded in 2023 by operators with a decade of experience working with China and building enterprise-scale brands" and gives no founder names; the LinkedIn company page ecomflowhq has its HQ in Bonham Strand, Sheung Wan, Hong Kong, 3,852 followers, 39 listed employees; remote hiring in Hong Kong, the Philippines, London and Argentina.
  • Launch timing: domain registered 2019-01-09 (RDAP; Wayback snapshots from 2013 belong to an earlier holder, not checked one by one); the About page timeline starts in 2023; the site's earliest blog post is 2024-01-07; customer dashboard v1 released 2024-12-05; earliest Trustpilot review 2024-10 (experience date 2024-09).
  • Traffic (profile file): about 4,000 visits a month; direct 46.3%, search 40.3% (brand keywords 86.6%), paid social 8.1%; US 19%, Netherlands 12.7%, Indonesia 12.6%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Partner program: aimed at e-commerce agencies, consultants, software teams and logistics partners, who get referral links and recurring commissions on brands they bring in, paid automatically each month via PayPal since 2025-04.
  • Ecosystem bundling: Paking Duck packaging factory (co-owned) and Instant Commerce (a Shopify platform) give ecomflow customers discounts; Postflows is given to customers free.
  • Tariff/policy content: blog posts and LinkedIn posts on US tariffs 2025, Section 321, the EU ending the €150 duty exemption, etc.
  • Paid social is 8.1% of traffic (profile file), showing that it runs ads.

Speculation:

  • Customers are mainly Dutch/European brands and may spread by word of mouth through the Dutch e-commerce scene (agencies, founder communities); no specific person or community was found.
  • A high share of brand-keyword search suggests new customers mostly search the name directly after being referred.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 300+ brands and 10M+ cumulative orders. If average fulfillment revenue per order is a few dollars, annual revenue could be in the tens of millions of dollars (pure extrapolation, not verified).
  • 39 listed on LinkedIn, stated size 51–200; Trustpilot has only 9 reviews (3.4 rating).
  • Funding was not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Google search results were mostly unrelated (EcoFlow power banks, CVEs, speedtest); evidence comes from the site's About/Partners/Changelog/blog metadata, the sitemap, Trustpilot, the LinkedIn company page, and RDAP/Wayback CDX.
  • The founders' names, the source of the first customers and the takeoff event were not found, so the launch method is filled in as "Not found".
  • The domain was registered in 2019, earlier than the 2023 founding, so it may be an old domain that was bought; not verified.
