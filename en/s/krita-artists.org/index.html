Overview

Krita-artists (krita-artists.org): Community forum for artists and developers of the Krita painting software. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 41,307 visits went from Krita-artists to a checkout payment page — #53 of the 607 sites we track. That is 5% down from the April–June monthly average of 43,547.

Total website visits that month were about 207,401 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 19.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 62%, direct 32%, referrals/email/paid 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 94%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
