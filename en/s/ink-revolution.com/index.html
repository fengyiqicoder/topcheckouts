Basics

  • Product: compatible and remanufactured ink cartridges, toner and label tape, covering Epson, Canon, Brother, HP, Ricoh and others, with new models added continuously (Brother LC511/514/516 in 2025-12, Epson KZR in 2026-02). Prices about ¥3,380–10,800; shipping ¥275, free over ¥4,000.
  • Company: 株式会社シー・コネクト (C-Connect), corporate number 6010001127257, 1-16-7 Higashikanda, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo (moved in from Yushima, Bunkyo-ku in 2020-10), logistics center in Edogawa-ku; ISO9001 / ISO14001. A small-to-mid-sized e-commerce company with no funding information.
  • Self-reported figures: 160K customers, 400K transactions, "one cartridge sold every 10 seconds", defect rate 0.1% (https://ink-revolution.com/pages/new-customer).
  • After-sales: 1-year warranty, toll-free phone 0120-761-109, LINE support (¥500 coupon for adding as a friend); cash on delivery, PayPay and convenience-store payment; the site is built on Shopify.
