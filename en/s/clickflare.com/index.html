Basics

  • Product: ad tracking and attribution: multi-step funnel tracking, cost sync, Conversion API (server-side postbacks to Meta/Google/TikTok), search-arbitrage revenue postbacks (System1, Sedo, Tonic, Domain Active, etc.), GTM-style single-script events, multi-user workspaces, managing ads directly inside the tracker, AI copilot, MCP (the LanderLab blog has a ClickFlare MCP article), mobile app.
  • Pricing: 14-day trial; Starter $89/month ($69 annual), Pro $219, Master $429, Agency $699, Enterprise $1,199, Corporate $2,199.
  • Who built it: founders not disclosed on the site; the About page cites a team of about 30 "from around the world"; LinkedIn lists 11–50 employees and 495 followers, with Kai Xiong visible among staff. From memory (unverified): the team has roots in Romania's TheOptimizer; supporting evidence is that TheOptimizer appears among the partner logos and the 2022 site already had a TheOptimizer integration graphic.
  • Launch date: About page says "99.99% uptime since January 2021" and "events tracked since 2021"; the original domain was clickflare.io, first Wayback capture 2022-03-22 (Beta recruitment page); moved to clickflare.com around 2024-09 (that domain was registered in 2007, listed for sale 2019–2023, RDAP change 2024-09-06).
  • Traffic (profile): direct 97.6%, brand keywords 98.9% (clickflare, clickflare pricing), social 1.1% from Facebook; United States 44%, India 28%, China 12% — almost all existing users logging in.

Where the first users came from: the 2022 Beta offer of "6 months free + unlimited events," clearly aimed at search-arbitrage and affiliate media buyers (the System1/Sedo/Tonic/Domain Active/Crossroads integrations are the arbitrage scene's standard feeds). Which forum/community they were recruited from not found; Speculation: affiliate forums like affLIFT and STM, and the TheOptimizer user base.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral commissions: lifetime 15%.
  • Scene endorsements: homepage testimonials from A4D founder Jason Akatiff, the5.co, JC Lead Gen, Boring Media and other well-known affiliate operators.
  • Ecosystem integrations as a traffic source: 250+ integrations, with partner logos including TheOptimizer, LanderLab, Adplexity, Taboola, Media.net, System1, Sedo and ClickDealer; LanderLab published a ClickFlare MCP tutorial.
  • Free Beta to recruit the first cohort → 14-day trial at official release + Black Friday switching offers ("switchers get up to…") aimed squarely at poaching competitors' users.

Speculation:

  • Tracker migration costs are high, so customers rarely churn once integrated; the 98% direct traffic is login return visits.
  • The high share of Indian and Chinese users likely comes from word of mouth among local affiliate/arbitrage teams.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 3,500+ media buyers (site); at an average of about $150–200/month per customer, annual revenue would be roughly $6–8M (our estimate, unverified).
  • $578M+ ad spend and 2.3 billion events tracked in 2025 (site).
  • Team of about 30 (About page).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Most search results were irrelevant; evidence comes from the site's About/homepage/referral pages, LinkedIn, Wayback (clickflare.io 2022-03, 2023-03) and RDAP. G2 (29 reviews) returned 403 and was not read.
  • Founders and location not found; the TheOptimizer connection is from memory, unverified.
