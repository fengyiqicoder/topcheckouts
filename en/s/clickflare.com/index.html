Overview

Clickflare (clickflare.com): Ad tracking and attribution platform with server-side conversion capture for media buyers. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 3,241 visits went from Clickflare to a checkout payment page — #595 of the 607 sites we track. That is 15% down from the April–June monthly average of 3,808.

Total website visits that month were about 49,745 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 6.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 98%, search 1%, social 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 99%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Starter $89/mo ($69/mo yearly); Pro $219/mo ($169 yearly); Master $429/mo ($32.
