Overview

Iggm (iggm.com): Marketplace selling in-game currency, items, boosting, accounts, gift cards and top-ups for many games. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 55,874 visits went from Iggm to a checkout payment page — #34 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 228,010 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 24.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 57%, search 41%, social 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, and direct plus brand search is about 74%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
