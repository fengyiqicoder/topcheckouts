Basics

  • Product: a done-for-you service for e-commerce sellers to launch, boost rankings and build reviews, covering Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopify, Walmart, Target and Chewy. Real US buyers order with their own money at the pace the seller specifies, Sellico refunds them within 24–48 hours, and they are invited (not required) to leave a review after about 7 days.
  • Pricing: no monthly fee, no account opening fee, a service fee per order: $5–12 per order in 2023; TikTok Launchpad $7.60–15 per order in 2026, plus the product's retail price and sales tax (about 75% of the product payment can be recovered through TikTok).
  • Team: Daniel Del Carlo (co-founder/CEO; his LinkedIn says business covers the US and Canada), Ian Page (co-founder); the 2023 team page also lists BD (Josh Friedman and others), full-stack developer Nikolay Tkachuk, and a dozen or so operations/data analysts with Philippine names. The blog has an author account "semtitans", which looks like outsourcing to the SEO agency SEMTitans (Speculation).
  • Domain: sellico.com registered 2014-08-25 (RDAP, an old domain); listed for sale at $3,595 in 2019-01, listed on BrandBucket in 2021-12, and the first official Sellico site appeared in 2022-08. A Brazilian company with the same name, SELLICO LTDA (2014), is unrelated to this site.
  • Traffic profile: United States 83%, Brazil 8%, Indonesia 5%; direct 44%, search 42% (brand keywords 86%, non-brand almost 0), no social source.

The first customers were probably from the founders' existing relationships in the Amazon seller/review service circle and BD outreach (Speculation); no founder interview was found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Buyer network size → can take bigger orders → more sellers: the FAQ says the buyer pool grows 4–6% a month.
  • Sales-driven: a BD team + strategy calls + a self-serve ordering dashboard from 2024.
  • Content: case study pages, an annual buyer survey, and TikTok Shop guide articles in 2026.
  • Following the platform to a new track: from Amazon ranking boosts to getting TikTok Shop through probation.

Speculation:

  • Brand keywords make up 86% of search with no non-brand terms, which suggests new customers mostly hear about it in seller communities (Facebook groups, seller forums, agencies) and then search the brand, i.e. in-circle word of mouth + agency/affiliate referrals.
  • The traffic from Brazil and Indonesia may be buyer network members or outsourced operations staff visiting the dashboard.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 9,850+ launches in total (about-us, 2026); a team of about 20 in 2023 (mostly Philippines-based operations).
  • Revenue model: a $5–15 service fee per order; at 9,850 launches and tens to hundreds of orders each, cumulative service fees could be in the millions of dollars (pure Speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site written from scratch. The launch time (2022-08), team, service evolution and pricing have Wayback and official site evidence.
  • Gaps: the founders' background (LinkedIn returns 999 and would not open), the source of the first customers, real revenue. The search results were almost all irrelevant. A "real-buyer refunded orders to boost rankings" service carries compliance risk under Amazon's terms; the site claims to be compliant, unverified.
