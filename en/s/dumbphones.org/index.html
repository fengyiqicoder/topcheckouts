Overview

Dumbphones (dumbphones.org): Creator-run Dumbphone Finder: guides to low-distraction phones via affiliate links, YouTube, book and course. Category on TopCheckouts: Media.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 7,538 visits went from Dumbphones to a checkout payment page — #490 of the 607 sites we track. That is 770% up from the April–June monthly average of 866.

Total website visits that month were about 63,986 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 11.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 64%, social 22%, direct 14%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 78%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
