Basics

  • Product: a browser-based AI image and video generation platform with a large number of community fine-tuned models and LoRAs. A free tier plus Gems credits (run any model on demand) plus a paid membership (unlimited generation, faster GPUs, private generation and more). Images generated on the free tier are public by default (genfindr review).
  • Team: Gregory Hunkins (former Lifion AI developer, CTO type) and Roi Lee (CEO, previously ran an eco-fashion e-commerce business). They met in New York in 2019 and the company is in New York. Still a "two-person team" in 2023 (404 Media, GFR Fund).
  • Funding: GFR Fund (a Japanese VC) announced an investment on 2024-08-26, amount undisclosed. Crunchbase shows private, 1-10 people.
  • Domain: mage.space's current registration date is 2022-09-16 (GoDaddy). The 2017 snapshots in Wayback belong to a previous holder.

Growth loop

Evidence

  • Free, unlimited and low-filter: in the window when SD had just come out and people had no GPUs, it drew people with "no deployment, no limits", and users spread it by word of mouth (HN, GFR).
  • Creator revenue sharing: model and LoRA authors who upload their work to Mage get a share of revenue, and authors actively promote their models on Mage on Civitai, Reddit and Discord (Model Creators Program launched 2023-03).
  • Discord community: feedback, bugs and requests all live here, with updates shipped weekly (GFR).
  • New models are put up free right away (Flux 2024-08), borrowing external hype for traffic.
  • Public work feed: free-tier generations are public by default, forming a browsable gallery whose prompts can be reused and which may be indexed by search engines ("public by default" per genfindr is evidenced, the SEO effect is Speculation).

Speculation

  • share_result: share links to generated results spread on Reddit and WhatsApp (this site's social channels are Reddit, YouTube, WhatsApp).
  • NSFW demand is an important driver of paid conversion (404 Media confirms NSFW needs a subscription), but it was tightened from 2023, so the effect on growth is uncertain.

Revenue and scale clues

  • MAU: 300K+ at the end of 2022, 1 million in 2023-05, recently claiming 1.4 million, +30% month over month, and profitable (self-reported).
  • Pricing: AlternativeTo shows $4-15/month, plus Gems credits. Exact tiers are per the official site, not verified this time.
  • Our data: last month checkout page visits about 73K versus an 4–6 months ago average of about 43K, a second round of growth for an old site (Speculation: related to video generation and the Gems revamp, unconfirmed).
  • Revenue amount: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The takeoff time (2022-09 HN) has double evidence from the original HN post and the investor's blog, high credibility.
  • "1.4M MAU, 30% MoM" comes only from a search summary; the original post was not opened.
  • The Reddit launch post and details of early Twitter spread are missing.
  • The specific cause of this 2026 wave of growth (video models? a particular YouTube video?) is Not found.
