Overview

Mage (mage.space): Browser-based AI image and video generator with free tier, Gems credits and paid memberships. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 73,066 visits went from Mage to a checkout payment page — #25 of the 607 sites we track. That is 70% up from the April–June monthly average of 43,033.

Total website visits that month were about 886,799 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 8.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 84%, search 13%, social 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 97%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
