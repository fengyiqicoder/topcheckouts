Basics

  • Product: a crowdsourced bank of real tech/finance (including quant) interview questions, organized by company, role and stage (OA, phone, coding, system design, low-level design, onsite), with the time they were last asked; credit system: submit questions to earn credits, spend credits to unlock; claims review by senior engineers. About 7,500 questions (2026-10, per competitor PracHub's count), covering 210+ companies (summary of the site's get-started page).
  • Price: there is a free tier; monthly subscription $49; one-year pass $299; there are also passes bought per company (the unlock-all page says "Choose a company first, then pick the pass length"). All purchases are non-refundable (ToS 2026-04-17).
  • Team: HN account rascal_rabbit ("we built"), X account @KyomotoFujino associated on Microlaunch; the site has an "Our Founder Story" page (2025-01, saying "writing code in a small apartment, building the resource we wanted ourselves", summary only, the original page returns 403). The founder's name is Not found.
  • Distinction: unrelated to interviewdb.com (AI/ML interviews) and InterviewBit.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: the North American Chinese job-hunting circle — Blind discussions (2024-12/2025-01), search terms containing 1point3acres in the channel profile, and referrals from uscardforum.com; about 5,000 users before the HN launch.
  • Content supply: credits as "give to get" (HN original post).
  • Search ranking: one "XX Interview Questions (Updated Oct 2026)" page per company, with the date in the title refreshed every month, and it ranks on Google for "company name interview questions" (the Reddit/Stripe/TikTok pages all appeared in this round of search results).
  • Channel profile: direct 63.8%, search 27.1% (brand keywords 85.5%).
  • Speculation: classmates/colleagues sharing by word of mouth during interview season (high direct traffic); reposting/driving traffic on forums such as 1point3acres (consistent with the "cheaper 1point3acres" positioning, but no direct evidence).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claimed about 5,000 users in 2025-03; about 7,500 questions in 2026-10.
  • Price $49/month, $299/year; last month checkout visits were 17K. At a 1–2% conversion rate, that is roughly several hundred paid orders a month, and monthly revenue may be on the order of $10–30K (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Medium. The HN launch, prices and question count have evidence; the founder's identity and the original founder story (403) were not read; early Blind discussion comes only from competitor relays.
  • Second follow-up check: changed the takeoff method from "Launch platform" to "Existing audience" (it already had 5,000 users before HN, and early discussion was in the Blind/1point3acres circles); added Search ranking to the growth engine; added the $49/month and $299/year prices, the 7,500-question count and the no-refund clause.
