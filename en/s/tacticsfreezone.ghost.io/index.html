Basics

  • Product: a personal soccer analysis newsletter / website, TacticsFreeZone™ ("The place for high-level MLS and USMNT analysis. And scotch."). Schedule: Sunday match recaps (paid), Monday/Tuesday Power Rankings 1-30 (free), Wednesday features (Tiers, transfer grades, team needs, etc., paid), Thursday weekly roundup (free); plus a Tip Jar for tips. Three paid tiers: basic paid reads all articles; the middle tier adds the monthly "Brown Liquor Specials" (shows with friends from the MLS world); the top tier adds a monthly live AMA. Prices are not shown on the page.
  • Author: Matthew (Matt) Doyle, X @MattDoyle76, mainly active on BlueSky; 15 years at MLSsoccer.com (about 2011-2026), formerly part of the host lineup of MLS's official podcast Extratime; also joined Kickback Soccer Media (Soccerwise podcast) in 2026.
  • Platform: hosted on Ghost (a ghost.io subdomain); the About page says "independent publication launched in January 2026"; plans to switch to TacticsFreeZone.com later.
  • Our data: United States 93.87%, Mexico 2.62%, Australia 1.85%; direct 44.2%, social 41.9% (Reddit, X), search 8.4% (mls power rankings, best usmnt prospects).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Existing readers migrate: the author built up 15 years of readers at MLSsoccer, the 2024-12 first post relied on BlueSky/Twitter followers to subscribe; the Substack list moved wholesale to Ghost in 2026-02.
  • Social distribution: social is 41.9% in our data (Reddit, X); the author says Twitter is used only for links but still brings "quite a bit of traffic"; on X he can be seen posting short article links (t.co pointing to site articles).
  • Free hook: the Power Rankings and weekly roundup are always free and are what gets spread on Reddit/X; the search term "mls power rankings" accounts for part of search.
  • Podcast cross-promotion: the Soccerwise (Kickback Soccer Media) show, and earlier Extratime listeners.

Speculation:

  • The weekly Power Rankings discussion thread on r/MLS is the main entry point for new readers (our data shows Reddit is one of the social sources; the specific threads were not found, Reddit would not open).
  • The news of the MLS layoff itself drew sympathy and shares in the fan community, and the week of 2026-02 was the peak of paid conversion.
  • Direct visits of 44.2% are email subscribers clicking links to come back.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Three paid tiers + tips, prices not public.
  • Early Substack articles typically get 20-80 likes, suggesting free readers in 2025 were roughly in the thousands to ten thousand range (speculation).
  • Paying subscriber count and revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The start date, the reason for changing platforms, the layoff timing and the paid plans all come from the author's own posts, high credibility.
  • Only a few of the search results provided were relevant; Reddit threads were not found.
  • Gaps: paid prices, subscriber count, the specific Reddit referral threads.
