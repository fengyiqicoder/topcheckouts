  • Business: formal/informal class photos, individual photos, ID photos, staff photos, collages, CCA (co-curricular activities) group photos, graduation/award ceremonies, Founder's Day and other school events; quoted per school with a tailored plan, with multi-tier staging and studio lights set up on site.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • School side: B2B contracts, with the selling point "take the administration off your teachers' hands", using a list of well-known school clients as endorsement (Our Clients page, portfolio images).
  • Parent side: access only with a password issued by the school/photographer; school website PDFs and notices directly post the shop address + password, which is the only source of parent traffic (channel profile: direct 49%, brand search 38%).
  • Multiple shoots per school: one school has several albums a year — class photos, CCA photos, staff photos, ceremony photos — raising orders per school.
  • Speculation: new schools come through school-to-school word of mouth and procurement/renewals within the Ministry of Education system; the two months ago peak is speculated to correspond to concentrated photo ordering after the mid-year CCA / school anniversary / National Day season shoots in Singapore.
