This is not an internet product but the online ticketing page for a Russian/Slavic bathhouse (banya) on Dubai's Bluewaters Island, hosted on the UK spa-booking SaaS Trybe (try.be). Its starting point was a category gap: when it opened in 2024 it billed itself as 'Dubai's first authentic banya', with steam rooms, venik beating (Parenie), a snow room, an ice pool and a Slavic restaurant. Searches like 'banya dubai' and 'banya price dubai' have practically no other match, so they land on it by default. From there, Instagram (25k followers, 472 posts) and TikTok experience Reels, customer UGC, a beehiiv newsletter and 1,000+ 4.9-star Google reviews keep steering people to the try.be page to book. The booking page itself has no viral mechanism; its traffic is downstream of the venue's word of mouth and social content.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: Banya Forrest's booking/ticketing page (hosted on Trybe), selling timed entry (AED 300–450 / 3 hours, including sauna, ice pool, snow room, locker, restaurant seating and the communal steam ritual), treatment packages (AED 600–1,300), a 'first-timer' intro package, 'Reset Thursday', memberships and gift vouchers; private buyout for 10+ people AED 8,000. The page has English and Russian descriptions.
  • Venue: Banya Forrest, Bluewaters, Dubai (website https://www.banyaforrest.com/), a mixed-gender social banya plus a licensed Slavic restaurant. Contact emails info@ / marketing@banyaforrest.com; website built by Celementa. No founder named on the site.
  • Platform: try.be is Trybe's booking domain. Trybe is UK spa/leisure-venue management software (its case studies include UK hotel spas such as Shrigley Hall).
  • Domain: banyaforrest.com registered 2023-05-17 (RDAP), earliest Wayback 2024-05; earliest snapshot of banyaforrest.try.be is 2025-06, and the earliest product page date in search results is 2024-10-07 ('TUESDAY TO FRIDAY ENTRY').

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Category-term search: the channel profile shows search at 63.2%, with terms being the venue name plus 'banya dubai' and 'banya price dubai'.
  • Social content: Instagram 25k followers, 472 posts, mostly experience Reels and customers on camera ('Eva shares her Banya experience'); TikTok has a #banyaforrest tag and the official account @banya.forrest, which pushes bookings via a phone number.
  • Word of mouth: 1,000+ Google reviews, 4.9 stars.
  • Repeat spend: memberships, gift vouchers, the 'bring a friend AED 500' deal, 10+ person buyouts and corporate wellness.

Speculation:

  • Gift vouchers and the 'bring a friend' price get existing customers to bring new ones — an offline acquisition loop.
  • Influencer visits (Dubai creators trying the sauna/ice bath) may be the main social source; no specific viral video was found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ticket prices AED 300–1,300, buyouts AED 8,000. Going by 1,000+ Google reviews and typical review rates, cumulative visits are in the tens of thousands (speculation).
  • The channel profile shows Switzerland at 76.3% of traffic, clearly inconsistent with a Dubai venue — most likely a data error.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written fresh from scratch (no earlier report). Evidence for the business type, prices and opening year is fairly hard (website + Wayback + RDAP).
  • Gaps: founder, exact opening date and opening coverage not found; search results contain no Reddit, press or Product Hunt content. This is a ticketing page for a physical venue, so the 'takeoff' label applies an online taxonomy and is only approximate.
