Overview

Gotya (gotya.com): Pay-per-pack site where buyers open graded trading cards, with instant buyback and a 0% fee marketplace. Category on TopCheckouts: Collectible cards.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 60,172 visits went from Gotya to a checkout payment page — #30 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 361,386 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 16.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 66%, search 25%, social 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 92%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $15 one-time (Pokemon Rookie Pack); $25 one-time (Trainer Packs); $30 one-time.
