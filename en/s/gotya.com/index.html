Basics

  • Product: pay to open digital card packs, each pack corresponding to one physical graded card (PSA, CGC), covering Pokémon, One Piece, football (American), baseball and soccer. Packs range from $15 to $500. Pulled cards are kept in the platform vault and can be bought back instantly at 90% of fair value (third-party reviews say ungraded cards at 100%), or traded and auctioned on the marketplace with a 0% fee, or shipped home by paying shipping (requires a top-up and identity verification first). 18+ only.
  • Operator: the page is signed Gotya Collect LLC, with copyright held by VBR Group LLP (UK Companies House OC447607, founded 2023-06-01, London, designated members Vasco Rouw and Tamara Weeda, who by their names look Dutch). A compulsory strike-off notice was filed in 2026-09, and the annual confirmation statement is overdue (RipsWorld, Companies House summary). Funding Not found.
  • Domain: gotya.com is an old domain registered in 2004 (GoDaddy), used several times: in 2024 a yoga-pants Shopify store, in 2025-08 a small Dutch and Belgian cards and collectibles Shopify shop "GotYa – Cards. Collectibles. Culture." (priced in euros), and in 2026 turned into a US pack-opening site. Also registered gotyacards.com on 2026-07-11 as a secondary or campaign domain.
  • Speculation: the Dutch card-shop team pivoted to the US pack-opening market (the timeline and names fit, but there is no direct evidence).

Growth loop

Evidence

  • Invite link for a free pack: signing up through an invite link gets a free $10 pack (perk.hk). Affiliate review sites earn from referral rewards, so they actively promote it, forming a chain of "affiliate site recommends → new user signs up → gets a referral link → keeps recommending".
  • Giveaway tied to payment: you must open a paid pack to enter the big prize draw, with an extra chance for each additional pack, and 16,243 people took part (the site). This move raises both the paying rate and the buzz.
  • Public live feeds: "Just Pulled", "Biggest Hits" and leaderboards, where people who pull big cards are shown publicly, tempting onlookers to open too (the site). This corresponds to the public_profile_leaderboard mechanism.
  • First-payment guarantee: if the first paid pack pulls a card worth less than the pack price, it is refunded (RipsWorld), lowering the barrier to a first payment.

Speculation

  • Users post screenshots or screen recordings of big cards they pull to IG, TikTok and WhatsApp (share_result).
  • Some of the Direct and Organic Social in this site's data may actually come from Meta paid ads and influencer partnerships (the social channels are mainly FB and IG, which does not look like purely organic spread).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Packs $15 to $500, 90% buyback, 0% seller fee on the marketplace (auctions charge the buyer a commission), minimum withdrawal $25.
  • Self-reports 445,884 collectors, 51K cards in the vault, vault total value about $2.01M (unaudited). The giveaway had 16,243 entrants.
  • Our data: last month checkout page visits about 60K, with 0 over the past 4–6 months, matching the four months ago launch and the last month ramp-up.
  • Revenue: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch date (2026-06) has only one source, RipsWorld, but it fits both Wayback (still a Dutch shop in 2025-08) and the registration time of gotyacards.com.
  • The operating entity carries clear risk: the LLP is subject to a strike-off application, the terms state user funds are not segregated, and the vault has no dedicated insurance. From TopCheckouts' point of view, this looks more like a sample of "buying traffic with subsidies and giveaways" than a sample of the product spreading itself.
  • The founders' background, the scale of ad spending, and exactly which influencer or video drove the volume are all Not found.
