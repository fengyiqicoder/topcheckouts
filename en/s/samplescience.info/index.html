Pierre Parenteau, a hobbyist musician in Montreal, spent more than ten years on KVR (the forum for audio plugin developers and users). In 2012 he built samplescience.ca on Blogger to sell the sound libraries and small plugins he sampled himself. Lots of free and pay-what-you-want "retro hardware samples" (Game Boy, Sega, old drum machines, old pianos) got reposted by KVR's product database and free plugin news sites like Bedroom Producers Blog, and he held search positions with long-tail terms like "specific machine name + VST/plugin", slowly building a personal shop of 130-plus products. In 2020-12 he moved to the.info domain and kept going.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: virtual instrument plugins (VST/VST3/AU), Kontakt / Decent Sampler / Reason ReFill sounds, sample packs, collections; about 43 free and 5 donation-based (KVR product page count).
  • Price: free, Pay-What-You-Want (via Gumroad) and paid collections coexist.
  • Author: Pierre Parenteau. 2016 About page: "Based in Montreal (Canada), SampleScience is the sound design and music project of Pierre Parenteau. For the last 12 years, Pierre has been an active member of the KVR forum." (https://web.archive.org/web/2016/http://www.samplescience.ca/p/about.html). The 2026 new release Cozy Piano says it was recorded in Thuir, France in 2025-03 (he may have moved to France, unverified).
  • Tech: always hosted on Blogger (images served from blogger.googleusercontent.com).
  • Domain: old site samplescience.ca, Wayback has an about post URL from 2012/08, homepage snapshots from 2015-08 to 2018; 301 redirect from 2019, 404 from 2021. The new domain samplescience.info was registered 2020-12-03 per RDAP, with the first Wayback crawl the same day. The new site's Blogger feed has 152 posts, the earliest batch being product posts migrated over between 2019-11 and 2020-12.
  • Traffic profile (profile): US 25%, Germany 7%, Indonesia 5%; search 57.5%, non-brand about 34% (gorilla bass vst, handpan plugin); social from YouTube and Reddit; referrals include computermusic.jp.

Where the first users came from: the evidence points to the author's standing as a KVR forum member of more than ten years (the About page states it outright as a credibility endorsement), and to the free plugins being listed in the KVR product database and in partnership with Bedroom Producers Blog; the number of first users and the launch post were not found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free products as a draw: about 43 free and 5 donation-based, and the same sound is released in multiple formats (plugin/Kontakt/Decent Sampler/Reason), each format providing its own download entry.
  • Third-party reposting: long listed in the KVR product database; partners with Bedroom Producers Blog to release a free plugin; a referral source is computermusic.jp (Japanese DTM news).
  • Long-tail search: non-brand terms are about 34%, and the terms are specific instrument/machine names + vst/plugin.
  • Collection discounts: has sold the big "Complete" collection since 2015.

Speculation:

  • Free plugin news sites (BPB, KVR, Japanese DTM sites) post about a new release → download → email subscription → paid collection; this is a common funnel for independent sound authors, and this site fits the structure but there is no specific conversion data.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue; a one-person project with low unit prices, relying on collections and PWYW. Small scale: last month visits of 4,524 per the profile.

Confidence and gaps

  • The site is written from scratch. The Google search results were all unrelated (health, CVE, SQL articles); this report relies on the old site's Wayback, the Blogger feed, RDAP and the KVR developer page. The author's identity and old-site history have original page evidence, so it is fairly credible.
  • Gaps: the first traffic sources during the 2012–2015 start-up stage can only be inferred from his KVR standing; the Bedroom Producers Blog tag page 404s and the original partnership article was not read; sales and revenue Not found.
