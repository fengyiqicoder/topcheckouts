Basics

  • Product: creator payments platform supporting one-time tips ($5 per coffee), monthly memberships, a digital goods shop (Extras), posts/emails. The platform takes 5%, plus Stripe/PayPal fees.
  • Founders: Jijo Sunny (CEO), Joseph Sunny, Aleesha (Jijo's wife). From Kozhikode, Kerala, India; HQ is still in Kozhikode. At 15 Jijo ran a blog ad network, AdIndigo (2010, later shut down); BMC is their third product for creators.
  • Funding: YC W19 ($150K); early investment from Stripe; angel investment from Box CEO Aaron Levie (per Onmanorama). Largely bootstrapped overall; no large follow-on rounds found.
  • Team: about 26–30 people in 2024.

A third-party article claims "the launch brought thousands of signups", but there is no primary data.

Growth loop

Evidence

  • Badge/button virality: every creator embeds the buymeacoffee.com/username link or button on their website, YouTube bio and social profiles (official buttons and widgets provided; stated in the Launch HN). GitHub's default FUNDING.yml template has a built-in `buy_me_a_coffee` field, so the Sponsor button on open-source repos links straight to BMC.
  • Supporters can pay without signing up and leave messages that form a "wall of love"; every payment is a brand impression, and some fans are creators themselves who convert into new accounts.
  • External event amplification: COVID lockdown (10x), Ukraine donations, and the media attention when Laura Loomer switched to BMC after being banned by PayPal/Venmo.
  • An article mentions a well-known creator's tweet going viral and bringing hundreds of creator signups within days (no date or tweet link given).

Speculation

  • Supporters sign up themselves after seeing "Start my page" or the branding at the bottom of the payment page (a typical "Powered by" loop).
  • Checkout visits in September 2026 (about 27k) fell sharply from the April–June average (about 82k), possibly related to the shrinking payout country coverage, competitors (Ko-fi, Patreon, self-built Stripe links) taking share, or the site changing its payment flow paths. No evidence found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 5% platform fee; third-party estimate of about $5M ARR in 2024.
  • 1M+ creators (2023); about 3 million users and about ₹600 crore in annual transaction volume (Indian media, 2026-08). At 5% that implies platform revenue of about $3.5M, which is in the same ballpark as the $5M ARR estimate (rough calculation).
  • Team of about 26–30, YC plus a small amount of angel investment.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch dates (Wayback and PH), YC, the 5% fee and the 10x COVID growth (founder's own account) are all credible.
  • "$1,600 for the domain" comes from a single secondhand article, which also gives the year as 2019, conflicting with Wayback; the year should be taken as late 2017.
  • Gaps: Reddit was blocked, so no early Reddit promotion posts were found; no official ARR; the reason for the 2026 traffic decline is unverified.
