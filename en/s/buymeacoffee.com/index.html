Overview

Buymeacoffee (buymeacoffee.com): Creator-support platform for one-time tips, monthly memberships, digital shop and posts. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 26,942 visits went from Buymeacoffee to a checkout payment page — #97 of the 607 sites we track. That is 67% down from the April–June monthly average of 82,053.

Total website visits that month were about 1,876,895 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 65%, social 23%, search 9%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from social platforms: organic social is about 23%, led by YouTube › X (Twitter) › Instagram.
