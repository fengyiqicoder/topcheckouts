A new VPS brand under "FAST NETWORK LIMITED" in Hong Kong. In the second half of 2025 it bought qqg.net, a three-letter old domain that had been hoarded since 2000, and sells Los Angeles VPS with CN2 GIA/9929/all-three-carrier direct routes back to China. Basics

  • Product: Los Angeles cloud VPS (from $4.5/month, 32T of traffic, direct China connection, AS9929, CN2 GIA, up to 10G port), "Black Gold" servers, dedicated physical servers (from $79/month), AMD multi-IP site-cluster servers, and "CF Cloud Shield" Cloudflare-protected servers (from $149/month); with ICP filing, tickets and documentation.
  • Who built it: review sites describe "QQG.NET is a cloud computing and network service brand under FAST NETWORK LIMITED, Hong Kong" (logovps 2026-08-18); the official site footer says only QQG.NET. The owner's identity was not found; a LINUX DO user said "this company's owner has a dark history, opened many companies before that ran off, then changed the name and kept selling" (no evidence, forum user claim only).
  • Promotion mechanics: the homepage says "Referral program - 50% commission", "50% commission on referred customers, on every order!", discount code QQGNET66z for 60% off sitewide; the 2025-11 version of the homepage also said "CPS promotion: up to 30% high commissions on multiple products, up to 50,000 (5W) in commission" and "sign-up gift worth 2000 yuan".
  • Domain history: Verisign RDAP registration date 2000-02-28 (renewed without a gap). Wayback: 2003-2010 a US domain parking page "qqg.net is available for lease or sale"; 2016 a 403; 2025-04 an eName "domain expired" notice page; 2025-07 "premium domain for sale for a limited time"; 2025-11-14 first appearance of "QQG.NET-GLOBAL CLOUD, focused on high-quality US servers". So the current operator bought it between three months ago and November 2025. The registrar is now NameSilo.
  • Traffic (profile data): China 79.5%, Hong Kong 6.9%, US 6.8%; direct 67.4%, social 24.5% (X, YouTube); referral includes gwvpsceping.com (a VPS review site); search terms such as "三网直连vps".

Where the first users came from: Chinese VPS review blogs and forums (evidence: at least 6 review sites posted in a concentrated burst from three months ago to last month, and the review sites themselves earn from merchants' commission links; on LINUX DO someone read a review and went to buy a ¥19 plan). The high 50% commission is the direct motive for these review sites to write (speculation, but consistent with the commission terms on the official site).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • 50% referral commission + a 60%-off code, with review sites and promoters driving traffic via links; referrals include a VPS review site.
  • Promotion on X (spotted by forum users); social is 24.5%.
  • Ultra-low annual prices ($10-12.99) create buzz and spark forum discussion.

Speculation:

  • The loop is "review sites post for commission -> users buy cheap annual plans -> users post/ask on forums -> more reviews", but word of mouth is negative (unreachable, MTU problems, suspicion of running off), so positive reputation is hard to build.
  • It uses a three-letter old domain to raise credibility and mask the new brand's identity.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No revenue data. The mainstay is low-priced $10-13 annual plans, plus dedicated servers and DDoS protection. China is 80% of traffic. Forum feedback suggests a very small scale ("a fly-by-night cloud, never heard of it").

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Two NodeSeek threads returned 403 and could not be opened, so only summaries were used; two LINUX DO threads were read in full via the JSON API.
  • FAST NETWORK LIMITED's registration details, the owner's identity and the "changes names and runs off" history are all unverified, forum user claims only.
  • The profile's "new user source: viral" is more accurately described as review-site promotion driven by commissions.
