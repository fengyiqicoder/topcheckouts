The human reader is Carolina Wikstrom (per the site: Colombian, lives in London, 1/3 Sacral Generator, ten-year background in business finance).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The free chart is the conversion entry point; every term page funnels to "Get Your Human Design Chart / Get a Human Design Reading" at the bottom.
  • 528 programmatic/AI-assisted term pages, a textbook case of search ranking.
  • Active backlink building in the early days: press release, Padlet/LeetCode profile pages, social pages.
  • Paid search is in the channel mix (9.7%).
  • The brand name "a human design" almost overlaps with the generic search term, so the "brand keyword" share is high but it is really capturing generic queries.

Speculation:

  • After getting their free report, users post their type in Instagram/TikTok Human Design circles, and some come back to search; but the report carries no strong branding, so spread is weak.
  • Revenue comes mainly from one-time purchases of the paid long report and human readings.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public user count or revenue; a third-party estimate of about 390K monthly visits in 2023-11; our data shows a monthly average of 20K checkout visits in April–June.
  • Competitor comparison: Human Design iOS apps have ratings in the thousands (Align 1,532, HD Birth Chart 3,990), showing stable search demand in this niche.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass recheck: found the 2023-08 press release (Lake Elmo, MN), the Padlet/LeetCode backlink profile pages, the social account creation dates, and parsers.vc's 2023-11 traffic estimate; the "Long-tail search" call is now firmer, and the operator may be in Minnesota, US (previously "Not found").
