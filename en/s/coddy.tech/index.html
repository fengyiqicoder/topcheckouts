Three childhood friends (COO Barak Glanz, ex-Meta; another co-founder ex-Intel) built a Duolingo-style coding site around early 2022 with an in-browser compiler and short, level-based lessons. The first users were friends and family in a private beta, then Google search ads brought in free users from India — a few thousand, but none paying. After a failed attempt to sell bootcamps (B2B), they added payments around 2022-12 and pivoted to B2C at $10/month, with about 5% of users paying. The real takeoff came from paid TikTok micro-influencer videos: the first one cost about $30 with a creator of roughly 20K followers and brought 200–300K views and 20–30 paying users. They went on to work with about 100 influencers, with per-video prices rising from $50 to $3,000, then reused influencer videos as Facebook ad creative (CAC about $60 against LTV about $70). By 2023-09 they had 500+ paying users, new users growing about 20% per month, running on their own revenue. Later they layered on SEO assets (30+ language playgrounds, developer mini-tools, even non-coding keywords like kaomoji and Morse code) and an app (500K downloads) to reach 5M+ users; they did not do a proper Product Hunt launch until 2026-10.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: short lessons in 20+ programming languages (Python/JS/C++/SQL/Rust/Go…) + online compiler, Daily Challenge, Journey, certificates, hint-only AI tutor Bugsy; 13 interface languages, 100+ courses; later expanded to Chess/Math Spaces and "Coddy Build" (AI site builder); iOS/Android app (4.9 stars, 500K downloads).
  • Team: Nethanel (Nathan) Bar (co-founder & CEO), Barak Glanz (co-founder & COO, ex-Meta, runs growth and influencer spend), Kevin Spektor (co-founder & CTO), Ohad Yaniv (VP Business). The three co-founders are childhood friends; went through the Signal Apps accelerator (Sorona Partners). Speculation from the names and the Hebrew interface on the TikTok page ("Coddy ב-TikTok") that it is an Israeli team (unconfirmed).
  • Funding: self-sustaining as of 2023-09 (spend roughly equal to revenue); whether it raised afterwards not found.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Paid influencer videos → creative reused as Facebook/Instagram ads → keep scaling spend as long as CAC is below LTV (founder's own account on the podcast). The influencer program still exists; in 2026 they were still hiring an influencer partnerships manager; TikTok and Instagram have many creator videos framing it as "Duolingo but for coding".
  • Early on, Google search ads bought free users from India to build user volume.
  • SEO assets: online playgrounds, developer tools like JSON/SQL formatters, algorithm visualizers, comparison articles, embeddable editor; the profile shows about 25% of new users come from non-brand search, including non-coding tool keywords like kaomoji and Morse code.
  • App (500K downloads) + teacher edition + affiliate commissions + shareable certificates; streaks/leaderboards/daily challenges drove monthly retention to about 86% (2023).
  • The official line is "word of mouth + occasional influencer collaborations", which conflicts with the 2023 podcast's "about 100 influencers, focus on Facebook ads"; the podcast's details are treated as authoritative.
  • Speculation: the large free user base in Bangladesh, India and Russia comes from search and tool pages, while paying US users come mainly from influencers and Meta ads.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2023-09: 500+ paying users, Premium $10/month, about 5% conversion, new users growing about 20% per month, self-sustaining.
  • 2026-10: 5M+ learners, about 5.8M monthly visits (ReviewBolt); Plus/Pro membership tiers (33% off annual), prices not individually verified.
  • Our data as above; the 2–3 months ago dip is presumably the summer low season.
