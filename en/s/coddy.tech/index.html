Overview

Coddy (coddy.tech): Learn to code, 5.84M learners, affiliate program + GitHub syllabus. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Coddy to a checkout payment page — #191 of the 607 sites we track. That is 14% down from the April–June monthly average of 20,083.

Total website visits that month were about 2,798,343 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 58%, search 41%, referrals/email/paid 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 25%, with queries like “kaomoji”, “morse code translator”, “c”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
