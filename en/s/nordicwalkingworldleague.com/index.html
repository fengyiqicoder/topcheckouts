This is not a startup product but the registration and points site for Poland's Nordic Walking event system. Nordic Walking Poland of Poznań (a coach training and event organization since 2016, sharing accounts with the koronapolskinw.pl "Crown of Poland" race series and the nordicwalkingworldcup.com World Cup site) registered this domain in 2022-10. In 2023 it renamed the existing "Crown of Poland" series the "Polish Nordic Walking League" and placed it under the ONWF "World League", unifying the points rules (three levels of points: national Poland, western/central/eastern/northern regions, and world). The participants were already in this system, and website traffic comes from them registering, checking points and rankings, plus place-name searches for local events and Facebook promotion.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: ONWF (Original Nordic Walking Federation) official site / World League site: event list, online registration, 5/10/21 km individual/team/country rankings, points rules (national cup winner 1,000 points, online races 200 points), charity fundraising counter (EUR 15,262 in 2023-01, EUR 69,814 in 2026-10), awards gala. Supports in-person and "online participation" (upload a phone distance-tracking screenshot to score points).
  • Operator: the pages don't name a specific organization; it shares login accounts with Nordic Walking Poland (Poznań), Marsz Po Zdrowie, Akademia Polska and koronapolskinw.pl; Nordic Walking Poland's coach courses are listed under ONWF and "Nordic Walking founder" Marko Kantaneva. Partner sites cover Slovakia, Czechia, Ukraine, Kazakhstan, Italy, Uruguay and more.
  • Pricing: some events are free (such as the Montevideo stage), entry fees for other events not found; sponsors include Polar, Gabel, Aquayo and others.
  • Domain: registered 2022-10-19 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2023-01-27. Related domains: nordicwalkingpoland.pl (2016-05), koronapolskinw.pl (2016-05), nordicwalkingworldcup.com (2018-10).
  • Traffic: Poland 100%; direct 45%, search 41% (place name + event terms like "oborniki onwf", "liga nordic walking", "nordic walking championship belchatow"), referral 8.3%; social is only Facebook.

Where the first users came from: the existing participants and coach network in the Nordic Walking Poland system (the site lists about 1,028 certified coaches across 16 provinces). When the new domain launched it directly took over registration and points for the former "Crown of Poland" series, so users migrated over as a group (evidence: the 2023-02 renaming announcement, shared login accounts).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Points league format: at least 3 events with best results counted, which encourages participants to register multiple times and return to check rankings.
  • Team and family awards, charity fundraising and an awards gala strengthen community cohesion.
  • Online participation (upload a distance-tracking screenshot) lowers the barrier.
  • Coach network: Nordic Walking Poland trains coaches who bring their members to compete (the effect is speculation, but the coach count has evidence).
  • Local governments and sports centres co-host events, with Facebook promotion.

Speculation:

  • Local searches for event place name + "nordic walking" bring new participants to the site; participants are mostly middle-aged and older, and word of mouth spreads among local walking clubs.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Income comes from entry fees, sponsorship (Polar, Gabel, etc.) and awards gala fees; amounts not found.
  • The country ranking table lists 10 countries, with Poland at about 10.27 million points, far ahead of the others.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The migration relationship is supported by RDAP, Wayback and the official announcement article; the legal entity and founder of the operating organization were not found.
  • Search results were mostly unrelated pages (stackoverflow, Omegle alternatives, etc.), with only a few official site links useful.
  • Gaps: participant numbers, entry fees, growth data by season.
