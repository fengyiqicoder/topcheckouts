Overview

Openpathcollective (openpathcollective.org): Nonprofit connecting people who can't afford market rates with vetted therapists for reduced-fee sessions. Category on TopCheckouts: Consumer services.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Openpathcollective to a checkout payment page — #442 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 73,947 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 11.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 53%, search 42%, referrals/email/paid 5%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 53%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: $65/$89 CAD one-time membership; $40-70; $50-90 CAD per individual session; $.
