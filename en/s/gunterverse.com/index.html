Overview

Gunterverse (gunterverse.com): Roblox community hub tracking events, event rewards, badges and puzzle games with user lists, reviews and leaderboards. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Gunterverse to a checkout payment page — #222 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 130,073 (estimated from global rank). Roughly 9.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 66%, social 25%, search 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 70%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Supporter $4.99/mo or 1,799 Robux; Supporter $9.99/quarter or 3,599 Robux etc..
