Basics

  • Product: upload product images (automatic background removal) -> generate product scene clips with Sora 2 and other models (presets like bathroom counter, kitchen counter, office desk) -> combine them with your own footage and captions in bulk into dozens of 9:16 videos; later added Overlay Studio, TikTok Shop showcase import, Formulas (viral templates), Remake (pick a video already selling on TikTok Shop and remake it with your AI avatar, voice and product), AI editing, photo carousels and ads.
  • Who built it: Grant Werlin, whose LinkedIn says "Founder & CEO at BatchBot", with a post summary "A week ago I launched BatchBot.io to help bottom-of-funnel UGC creators make TikTok Shop videos" (summary only, date unknown). The official site only says "© 2026 BatchBot", with no company entity information.
  • Pricing: 7-day free trial; Start $19/month, Scale $49/month, Max $99/month (credit-based); at the 2026-04 launch the tiers were Start/Scale/Max at 1,500/4,500/11,000 credits, and a 3-second video cost 100 credits.
  • Domain:.io registered 2026-02-11 (RDAP), registrar transfer 2026-06-21; an old snapshot from 2021-11 (previous holder); this product's first snapshot is 2026-04-15.
  • Traffic (profile data): US 99.1%; direct 51.4%, search 36.5% (brand keywords 87.8%), social 7.3%: users hear about it elsewhere and then search the name.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral link rewards: /r/ invite codes, invitees get 500 credits in the first month; an Affiliates entry existed from launch.
  • "Side-hustle test" content: team members post 7-day earnings tests from personal channels, with their affiliation disclosed.
  • Community screenshot wall: Discord #wins earnings screenshots are moved straight onto the official homepage as social proof (TurnerFinance himself also appears in the #wins list).
  • The Facebook page posts aimed at TikTok Shop affiliates.

Speculation:

  • AI-generated product videos that users post on TikTok carry no branding, so spread relies mainly on creator recommendations of the "learn to use AI for TikTok Shop" kind (commission-driven), rather than branded output.
  • Brand search is 88%, meaning most people searched the name after seeing a video or the community.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 2,000+ creators; at $19-$99/month subscriptions, if half pay at an average of about $40, monthly revenue would be roughly in the tens of thousands of dollars (speculation).
  • A promo video gets about 22k views each, so the scale is still small.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Only the official site, LinkedIn, YouTube and Facebook returned useful results; all other queries came back empty. Evidence comes from the official site, Wayback, RDAP and YouTube video metadata. LinkedIn returned 999 and could not be opened, so the launch date and first-week data rest on summaries only.
  • Company entity, team size, affiliate commission rate (the /affiliates page returns 404) and real revenue were not found.
