Basics

  • Product: a tool account-sharing membership. The portal masterkeyvip.com is just a Discord OAuth login page (scopes include identify, guilds, guilds.members.read, guilds.join); after verifying the VIP role in Discord, members enter the tool library; login problems are directed to discord.gg/spycom.
  • Promoter co-branded page (masterkeyvip.com/amijay/): titled "AmiJay x Master Key - one login for every premium tool", reads "Stop paying $4,000 a month for tools you barely log into"; lists PipiAds, ChatGPT, Veo 3, Claude, ForePlay, ElevenLabs, Canva, HeyGen and others; prices $29.99/month, $69.99 quarterly, $359.99 lifetime; claims "1,200+ Active members"; the buy button goes to whop.com/masterkey/master-key-access/...&a=<promoter ID> (Whop affiliate parameter).
  • Who made it: Whop store "Created by Cole" (@c0ldc0le), store rated 5.0 with 3 reviews; no company name or real name.
  • Launch date: the Discord server "Spycom - Master Key" (ID 1076723026172837908) was created on 2023-02-19 per snowflake ID conversion; the Discord OAuth app used by the portal (client 1365652436995543111) was created 2025-04-26; the current domain was registered 2025-08-10 (RDAP). Wayback snapshots from 2010-2022 belong to a previous domain holder (301 redirects) and are unrelated to this product.
  • Traffic (profile file): direct 67.2%, social 24.6% all from Discord, search 4.9% (brand keywords 72%: masterkeyvip, spycom master key); Slovenia 63%, Australia 14%, New Zealand 7.5% (the country mix looks skewed by a few heavy users or the operators themselves).
  • Note: this kind of "account sharing" means many people share a single paid account, which usually violates the terms of service of PipiAds, Kalodata, ChatGPT and other tools; no clearly illegal content such as child sexual material was found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Promoter affiliates: each promoter gets a masterkeyvip.com/<name>/ co-branded landing page + a Whop affiliate parameter; promoters put the link in YouTube video descriptions (the amijay case).
  • Discord is the only member entry and support channel; 100% of social traffic comes from Discord, and return visits are direct.

Speculation:

  • Like luxuryextras.online and other "tool account sharing" sites, it spreads on the "$4,000 in tools for $29.99" price anchor and affiliate payouts to small YouTubers/TikTokers; other promoter pages were not enumerated.
  • Slovenia's 63% traffic share may indicate the operators or core members are based there, unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claimed 1,200+ active members x $29.99/month is on the order of $36,000 in monthly billings (calculated from self-reported figures, unverified; promoter payouts and tool account costs still need to be deducted).
  • Discord about 8,880 members. The Whop store has only 3 reviews.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Google results were almost entirely irrelevant (SQL, CVE, VPN articles); evidence comes mainly from the portal homepage, the promoter co-branded page source, the Discord invite API, YouTube oembed/video descriptions, the Whop store page and RDAP.
  • Wayback was temporarily down at the time, so portal snapshots after 2025 could not be checked; the operators' real identity, the full tool list and other promoters were not found.
  • The takeoff method is classed as Existing audience based on "Discord community first, portal later", which is an inference.
