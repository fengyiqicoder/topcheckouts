In 2011, Mobile Interaction (Mobilbyrån), a mobile development company in Stockholm, built a small side tool: it generates a short link or QR code for an app that automatically sends visitors to the App Store, Google Play, BlackBerry or the Windows store depending on their device. It was free and needed no sign-up to generate, which hit the pain point of "one app is on several stores but a poster can only print one QR code". Every link and QR code users generate carries the onelink.to domain, printed on packaging, posters, events and social media; each scan by an end user is an impression, and developers and marketers who see it come to generate their own. This loop took it to 1M+ registered users and 200M+ clicks a month; after 10 years of being free, around 2022 it switched to a free tier plus subscriptions.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: every link is onelink.to/xxxx, and the QR code image is also hosted on the onelink.to domain (the 2012 embed code was http://onelink.to/2dax2t.png). The vast majority of the 200M monthly clicks are end users, and our referral sources are all events and app sites that embed onelink links, showing the traffic itself comes from links users produced.
  • Zero-barrier free: completely free from 2012 to 2021, and links can be generated without logging in, lowering the cost of trying it.
  • Asking for spread: from 2015 the homepage carries a "if you like it, help us spread the word" share button.
  • Brand-keyword search: brand keywords are 63.5%; developers who have seen a link search the name directly to find it.

Speculation:

  • First users: Mobile Interaction's own clients and the Swedish developer circle, plus people searching long-tail terms like "one QR code for app store and google play" (the domain and title are exactly that need); no launch post or press coverage was found.
  • Free first to build up a stock of links and charging 10 years later works because the migration cost of existing links is high: once users print the QR code on packaging it can't be swapped out, so they can only pay for analytics and advanced features.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 1M+ registered users, 200M+ clicks per month (self-reported).
  • Subscriptions $10-$80/month; if 1% of registered users paid at an average of $15/month, annual revenue would be about $1.8M (pure speculation).
  • A tiny team (LinkedIn lists only 1 person), a side product of the parent company, so margins should be very high (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there was no old report in the notes. Search results were mostly unrelated products with the same name (AppsFlyer OneLink, Onelink payments, Amazon Associates OneLink, the ThinkPad OneLink interface) and generic short-link roundups; the work relied mainly on the official What is page, Wayback snapshots from 2012-2022, LinkedIn and RDAP.
  • The timeline and "free for 10 years, then freemium" have first-hand snapshot evidence, credible.
  • Gaps: founder interviews, the launch channel, paying-user count and revenue all Not found; the size of Mobile Interaction is unknown.
