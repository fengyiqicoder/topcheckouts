Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: the founders' own TikTok/Instagram content accounts (apprenticeship applications, interview POVs, "how I got my apprenticeship"), with the Instagram bio link going straight to the site; site reviews mention "your TikTok videos" and @ApprenticeGuide.
  • Content tightly bound to product: interview-rant videos lead directly to the "practice video interview" feature.
  • Application-season rhythm: the account started in 2025-09 in sync with launch, and checkout visits exploded in 2026-09.
  • Speculation:
  • The TikTok account may predate the product (personal-story YouTube videos), i.e. followers first, then product, a "content account monetization" pattern; but no videos before 2025-08 were found, so this cannot be confirmed.
  • Brand keywords are 85.7% of search, meaning most people see a video and then search the name.
  • The product has no sharing or branded-output mechanism; complaints about charges after cancellation may hurt word of mouth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site claims 4.9/5; an older homepage read "2,500+ Success Stories" and "92% Success Rate" (no source, reviews look templated).
  • 17K checkout visits last month; rough estimate of a few hundred to one or two thousand paying users via the 3-day trial conversion (speculation); lifetime £59.99, monthly £9.99.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second pass: found the main accounts, TikTok @theapprenticeguide (S+R Apprentice, about 39K followers, started 2025-09) and Instagram @theapprenticeguide_ (47K followers); takeoff method changed from "Not found" to "Creator or press spark" (the founders' own content account sparked it), growth engine changed to "Creators or community"; added the Trustpilot 2.9 score and billing complaints.
