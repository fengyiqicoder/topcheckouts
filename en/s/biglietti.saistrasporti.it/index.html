Basics

  • Product: SAIS Trasporti's official online ticket purchase portal ("ACQUISTO ON-LINE"), where you can register an account (username + password + SMS verification code on first login) or buy without registering, with a 20-minute payment window; there is also an eticket.saistrasporti.it subsystem that handles changes (cambio corsa) and multi-ride tickets (carnet). An old-style ASP system. Main site notice: the Agrigento–Catania line can be bought online, other regional bus (TPL) lines require contacting a ticket office; changing the date of an inter-regional ticket requires contacting a point of sale (2026-06).
  • Company: SAIS Trasporti S.p.A., Via Portello 32/A, Palermo, VAT no. 04417970821. Its predecessor I.A.S. was founded in Enna in 1926 by engineer Antonio Scelfo, and through acquisitions in the late 1970s it became one of Sicily's largest private road passenger transport companies (Wikipedia). Instagram @saistrasporti has 13K followers.
  • Related brand: SAIS Autolinee SpA (saisautolinee.it, which likewise claims descent from Antonio Scelfo and 1926), 190+ vehicles, 3.7M passengers a year, 10M km driven a year, with its own app, e-tickets and contactless payment on board. Whether the two are the same group or have separated was not clarified this time (not verified).
  • User reputation: Trustpilot has 15 reviews with a 1.9 rating, complaining about checkout freezing, no official app and a dated website (2025-09 ~ 2026-09).

Growth loop

  • Evidence: no user-brings-user mechanism. Channel profile: referrals 43% (redirects from the main SAIS site), search 42.7% ("sais", "coupon sais"), direct 8.5%. The main site puts the ticket purchase link in a prominent spot on its homepage.
  • Speculation: growth is driven entirely by offline passenger volume and the online adoption rate — passengers shifting from station counters/tobacconists to online; tourists click in after checking routes on Google Maps/Rome2Rio/TripAdvisor.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The related brand SAIS Autolinee self-reports 3.7M passengers a year and 190+ vehicles (unclear whether it is the same company as SAIS Trasporti). The 17K checkout visits last month correspond to ticket transactions.
