Basics

  • Product: low-cost API relay/gateway; one key to call GPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, plus 14 image and 28 video models; compatible with OpenAI Chat/Responses and Anthropic Messages; routing by model group, sequential fallback within the same model (switches route only, not model); per-request latency, token and cost logs; plugins for VS Code, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, MCP, n8n, Zapier, Dify; sub-product Castaly (AI video/image studio); 12 interface languages.
  • Company: Havenbyte LLC (site footer); V2EX poster WorkerMatt says "US company, team in the US and Singapore, members with big-tech experience".
  • Launch: domain registered 2026-06-16 (RDAP, Cloudflare); first Wayback capture 2026-07-17; X @modelflare_dev already posting on 2026-06-27; the V2EX post claims "operating for over a year, running an anniversary event", which does not match the domain date; it may previously have run the enterprise business under a different domain (unverified).
  • Pricing: usage-based, about 200 models; different rate multipliers per group (stable/award/0.1x groups); the 0.1x "openai-first-topup" group requires a single $20 top-up to unlock; Claude-specific pricing requires a single $50 top-up (profile).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Cold start: the team itself posted twice in V2EX's promotion board, using small credits + redemption codes + low rate multipliers to attract Chinese developers; the comment sections were effectively credit-claiming queues.
  • Referral rewards: inviter and invitee each get 8% of first top-up; cash commissions for promotion partners; "Project Plaza / project sponsorship" gives partner projects a link placement.
  • Referral is 25%, from veridrop.org, z2u.com (a game/account trading site; presumably people resell credits or keys there) and v2ex.
  • Relay checker/price-comparison sites (BazaarLink, TokenAPI Scan, HowToken, llmtest) list it automatically, creating passive exposure.
  • Speculation:
  • "linux.do login" (mentioned in V2EX replies) suggests it also acquires users in the linux.do community.
  • Users are price-sensitive freebie-hunting developers who switch between relays; retention depends on rate multipliers, growth on commissions.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No user or revenue figures; the two V2EX posts had about 1,700 views each, a small scale.
  • Revenue model: prepaid usage-based billing, group rate multipliers, minimum top-up thresholds unlock the cheaper groups.

Confidence and gaps

  • The two V2EX posts open directly; the team's self-description and perk terms are credible. "Operating for over a year / serving enterprise clients" is self-reported, contradicts the 2026-06 domain registration, and is unverified.
  • What veridrop.org is and its relationship to Modelflare not found.
  • Third-party checkers have questioned the authenticity of the relay's models (failed a test), which is a business risk.
