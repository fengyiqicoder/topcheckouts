Webdot.club, a software studio in Melbourne, built an "Instagram story reply/comment keyword auto-DM" tool right after Meta opened the Instagram DM interface (Messenger API for Instagram) to third parties in 2021. It bought linkdm.com, which was listed for sale on HugeDomains, launched in July 2021 and became a certified Meta Business Partner in 2021. It rode the wave of the "comment LINK and I'll DM you the link" selling style that became popular on Instagram, and its users are mainly creators doing LTK, Mavely and Amazon affiliate sales. The free plan gives 1,000 DMs a month with the LinkDM brand in each DM (removing the branding is a paid feature), so every automatic DM advertises it, and fans who see it search the name. It has grown to 60,000+ users and 1.5 million DMs a day.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: automatic DMs triggered by Instagram/Facebook comments, Reels comments, story replies and story @mentions; later added inbox openers, click stats, flow automation, Rewind (sends DMs for old comments), lead capture forms, auto DMs for ad comments, Broadcasts, and integrations with Kit/Flodesk/Mailchimp/Google Sheets.
  • Pricing: Free $0 (1 account, 1,000 DMs per month); Pro $19/month (3 accounts, 25,000); Platinum+ $99/month (10 accounts, 300,000); plus one-time DM packs from $5/1,000 DMs (chatimize review). In the feature table, "White Label: Remove LinkDM branding from DMs sent" is a paid feature, which means free-plan DMs carry the LinkDM brand. Has a Referral Program (profile data records it as a 30% first-year share).
  • Who built it: the official terms page footer says "Copyright © 2026 Webdot.club"; webdot.club describes itself as "A creative software development agency based in Melbourne (Australia)". Founder name not found.
  • Scale: the site says "Used by 60,000+ creators, brands and agencies", "1.5 Million+ DMs sent daily", "35 Million+ Link clicks monthly", "25 Million+ Comments sent monthly"; "certified Meta Business Partner since 2021". The user wall includes food/parenting/home creators like @eatingbirdfood and @yummytoddlerfood, and brands such as @shoptoday, @nbcselect, @food52, @enews, @delta, @dillards and @allure; it lists use cases "Mavely Creators", "Amazon Creators", "LTK Creators".
  • Domain history: Wayback has records since 2004 (a redirect from a previous owner); 2017-04 a HugeDomains "LinkDm.com is for sale" page; Verisign RDAP currently shows registration 2016-10-05 (GoDaddy). On 2021-07-17 the product page first appears: "LinkDM – Instagram Link Automation… Instagram Approved story automation tool". The official Facebook/Instagram accounts use the name linkdm.io.
  • Traffic (profile data): India 31.9%, US 15.0%, Canada 10.0%; direct 54.6%, search 34.0% (brand keywords 75%, non-brand such as "auto dm instagram free"), social 6.8%.

Clues: at launch (2021-07) Meta had just opened the Instagram DM interface, and the first version stressed "Instagram Approved"; the user wall is concentrated on affiliate-selling creators (LTK/Amazon/Mavely). Presumably the first users were Instagram creators doing affiliate sales, spreading it among themselves around the pain point that "link in bio doesn't work well" (a creator sees another creator's post saying "comment LINK to get the link", the DM they receive has the LinkDM name at the bottom, and they go sign up) (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: free-plan DMs carry the LinkDM brand and removing it is a paid feature; the recipients are potential users (other creators/fans).
  • A generous free allowance (1,000 a month) lowers the barrier to trying it.
  • Referral Program.
  • Search: free ManyChat alternative and competitor comparison pages (Beacons, HighLevel, ManyChat, MobileMonkey, Stan).

Speculation:

  • The loop is "a creator posts a 'comment a keyword to get the link' post -> hundreds or thousands of followers receive DMs bearing the LinkDM name -> the creators among them sign up -> they post the same kind of post". That 75% of search in the profile is brand keywords is consistent with "see the name, then search it".
  • Meta Business Partner status became a trust signal when Instagram tightened its rules on automation.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 60,000+ users; Pro $19/month, Platinum+ $99/month. At a 5-10% paid rate, monthly revenue would be about $60,000-120,000 (speculation, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results included the official site, reviews and Reddit summaries; the rest were irrelevant (stress management, Stack Overflow).
  • The founder's name, Webdot.club's team size, the exact source of the first users and real revenue were not found; "Instagram DM interface opened in 2021" is from memory, with the exact date unverified.
