Overview

Linkdm (linkdm.com): SaaS that auto-sends Instagram and Facebook DMs in response to comments, story replies and mentions. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Linkdm to a checkout payment page — #430 of the 607 sites we track. That is 1676% up from the April–June monthly average of 484.

Total website visits that month were about 52,229 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 16.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 55%, search 34%, social 7%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows branded free output, referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 75%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $0 (Free, 1,000 DMs/mo); $19 USD/mo (Pro).
