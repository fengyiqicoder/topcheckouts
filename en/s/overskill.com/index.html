Basics

  • Product: describe an idea in natural language and get a web app with login, database, payments, email, push notifications and mobile install; 1,634 integrations; an Agent interface for AI assistants (SKILL.md, llms-full.txt). 500 credits on signup; Starter $25/month (2,500 credits), Studio $99/month. Was invite-only.
  • Company: Overskill, LLC, a Delaware LLC founded in 2025, based in Canton, Georgia. The site says "built by the team behind ClickFunnels" but it is a separate company from ClickFunnels.
  • Team: Todd Dickerson (founder and CEO, ClickFunnels technical co-founder), Russell Brunson (co-founder, ClickFunnels co-founder, author of DotCom Secrets and other books), Jason Sanzone (co-founder, head of design), Ashley Dickerson (co-founder, culture, partnerships and evangelism); Dante Torelli is head coach (also runs ClickFunnels' One Funnel Away Challenge).
  • Domain: Wayback shows someone else's old site from 2004-2016; the 2025-07-12 snapshot redirects to an atom.com for-sale page "OverSkill"; from 2026-04 it redirects to the official www.overskill.com site.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Parent-circle traffic: the founding team is the ClickFunnels core; the ClickFunnels YouTube channel runs Overskill training; the head coach comes from ClickFunnels' challenge program; referrals include onetomanysecrets.com (a course site in the Brunson ecosystem); email traffic 7.3%.
  • Affiliate commissions: 20% recurring for the first year, 60-day attribution, no application needed, one-click?ref= links; during the invite-only phase affiliates could also hand out "get in now" invite codes. Both ordinary users and review bloggers post content with referral codes.
  • Scarcity funnel: invite-only, waitlist counts, "only X founding seats left", "2 joined this hour", the ClickFunnels-style launch playbook.
  • Events and livestreams: Overskill Live streams, OverSkill Games hackathon.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 users most likely came from Brunson/ClickFunnels email lists and community launches (like his previous product launches), not Product Hunt or HN (neither appeared in search results).
  • The 65% drop last month visits suggests that once the launch-phase list traffic was spent, organic retention and word of mouth had not yet taken over, with affiliates topping up volume.
  • If apps users build sit on an Overskill subdomain or carry its badge, there would be a weak branded-output effect; unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Waitlist of 726+ in 2026-05; user count and revenue not public. Pricing $25-$99/month.
  • Our data: United States 61.9%, United Kingdom 9.3%, India 6.3%; direct 51.3%, referral 36.5%.

Confidence and gaps
