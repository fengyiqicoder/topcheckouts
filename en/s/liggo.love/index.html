Basics

  • Product: AI dating chat assistant app (iOS, Android, web, Chrome extension); upload a chat or Instagram story screenshot and it produces replies in styles such as Gracioso / Coquetear / Provocativo / Enamorar / Salvada épica, supporting Tinder, WhatsApp and Instagram.
  • Company: VIRAL APPS SL (a Spanish limited company, the developer name on the App Store and Google Play); Android package com.cupidoai.liggo (the early internal name may have been CupidoAI). Founder not found.
  • Pricing: free trial; US App Store in-app weekly subscription $3.99–$12.99, monthly $17.99, yearly $34.99.
  • Scale: the website claims 200K+ users and 5M+ generated replies; Google Play 1M+ downloads; US App Store 48 ratings, 4.4.
  • Domain: liggo.love registered 2025-08-22, first Wayback capture 2026-03-21; ligo.chat has the same page.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator short-video seeding: several Spanish-language creators post chat skits tagged @liggo.love on Reels/TikTok, including series-style "episode 2" formats; the official accounts have few followers (a few thousand), which shows it relies mainly on other people's accounts.
  • Own persona account: the "Dr. Liggo" account posts rizz skits in bulk.
  • Search capture: our data shows search at 79.3% but brand keywords at 94.8% (liggo, ligo, liggo ia), people searching the name after being seeded; ligo.chat catches misspelled traffic.
  • Long-tail SEO: Spanish/English reply guide pages (how to reply on Tinder, WhatsApp, Instagram) and an "Alternativa a Bombiny" competitor comparison page.

Speculation:

  • The company is literally named VIRAL APPS, so it is most likely a studio playbook of mass-producing "creator short video + subscription app" products, with creator deals paid or revenue-shared (deal terms not found).
  • Screenshot replies are easy to turn into video: screen recording plus a twist in the dialogue suits the skit format of short-video platforms.
  • Many Latin American users come from dating and Instagram DM scenarios, and the English competitors (Rizz, Plug AI and others, from memory, unverified) have not localized into Spanish, leaving a gap.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Google Play 1M+ downloads; the website claims 200K+ users and 5M+ replies (self-reported).
  • Subscription prices: weekly $3.99–12.99, monthly $17.99, yearly $34.99.
  • Our data: Peru 32%, Colombia 16%, Argentina 12%.
  • Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The company, listing dates, download count and prices have first-hand sources; creator videos were seen only as search summaries (TikTok and Instagram bodies could not be fetched).
  • The launch and tiktok search groups were basically irrelevant.
  • Gaps: the founder, whether creator deals are paid, which video was the first hit, revenue.
