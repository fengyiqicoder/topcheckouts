Basics

  • Product: a recovery service for Twitter/X deleted tweets and banned-account content. The data source is only the public Wayback Machine archive; entering a username shows the recoverable count for free, and nothing is charged if none is found; after payment it delivers JSON/text/image-video folders within a few hours (<1,000 tweets) to 24–48 hours (large accounts); the 2026-09 version upgraded to an online browsable masonry gallery (filter "still online/deleted", filter by date/media, reply and quote context). There is also a "download Twitter video" feature page.
  • Pricing: the page only says "small fee"; the amount Not found (the profile record also Not found).
  • Operator: not public; the footer reads "Twitter Archive Recovery Service", with only a contact email.
  • Languages: en/es/pt-BR/zh-CN/ja/fr/ar (sitemap, updated 2026-08-09).
  • Traffic (profile record): search 81.0%, direct 14.3%, AI assistants 2.9%; US 82.6%, Australia 4.8%, India 4.7%; search terms "deleted tweet finder", "see deleted tweets", "view deleted tweets".
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2025-11-18; first Wayback snapshot 2026-01-26 (already a complete product page).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Content SEO: at least 6 tutorial blogs around demand terms, plus 7-language landing pages (sitemap, blog pages).
  • Free count → paid delivery: first show "N tweets can be recovered" to create certainty, then charge (homepage/FAQ).

Speculation:

  • News-event-driven search spikes: after celebrities/politicians delete tweets or accounts get banned, searches for "see deleted tweets of X" surge, and the site catches that sudden traffic.
  • No spread mechanism: the deliverable is a private download package with no branded outbound links; pure product spread is weak.
  • Competitors (throwbackmachine.org, xarchive.net, postrewind.com, etc.) emerged at the same time, showing this is an SEO keyword niche that many people discovered simultaneously.

Revenue and scale clues

  • last month checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate). At a $5–20 unit price and 20–30% payment conversion (people reaching checkout already have strong intent), monthly revenue would be about $10,000–50,000 (Speculation, a very wide range).
  • No self-reported user or order counts.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The launch timing (2026-01) and the SEO route are well evidenced (WHOIS + Wayback + blog dates + sitemap).
  • Gaps: price and operator identity unknown; no external coverage or community launch post; which specific news events brought the spikes Not found.
