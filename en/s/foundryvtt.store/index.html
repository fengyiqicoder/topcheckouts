This is not a site built from zero but the officially licensed "app store" of Foundry VTT. Since 2020 Foundry has built up a large base of GM users with its one-time-purchase, self-hosted tabletop software, and has gone on to win official content licenses from Cubicle 7, Paizo, Wizards of the Coast (D&D) and others. Users used to have to go to each creator's site, Patreon or itch to buy modules and then enter activation codes, and the community kept asking for a central store. In February 2025 Foundry and its partner MetaMorphic Digital Studio opened this marketplace, where purchases go straight into your Foundry account with no activation codes. Traffic is sent over from the Foundry website, inside the software, the official X account and creators' own sites; creators and publishers keep listing new content, and returning users come back to buy again.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: the official content marketplace for Foundry Virtual Tabletop, selling adventure modules, rulebooks (including the official D&D 2024 Player's Handbook), map packs, token packs and bundles, covering systems such as D&D 5e, Pathfinder 2e, Call of Cthulhu, Daggerheart and Savage Worlds. About $14.99-$30 per item.
  • Operator: MetaMorphic Digital Studio (MDS), "in partnership with Foundry Virtual Tabletop". The launch announcement is signed by Amy (President), with Chris Valentine as lead project manager and architect. Tech stack: React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Tailwind.
  • Upstream: Foundry VTT is developed by Andrew Clayton (handle Atropos); Foundry Gaming LLC was registered in Spokane, Washington on 2018-10-09; in 2023 the software team had 9 people and the content team 12 (Wikipedia).
  • Publishers listed (visible on the homepage): Wizards of the Coast, Paizo, Chaosium, Darrington Press, Ulisses Spiele, Kobold Press, MCDM, Magpie, Cubicle 7, Mage Hand Press and others, plus many independent map and module authors.
  • Domain: registered 2024-05-10 (RDAP), first Wayback capture 2024-05-27.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Piggybacking on upstream: Foundry's official documentation makes the marketplace the default purchase channel (no activation codes), the official X account tweets marketplace links, and users recommend the marketplace to each other on package pages.
  • Creator supply: big publishers and independent authors keep joining, and each arrival gets a news post and a publisher-specific promotion; the creator dashboard shows sales, giving creators a reason to send their own fans over. In our channel data referral is 41.2%, coming from creators' own sites (foundryborne.online, skydawngames.com, jamesrpgart.com).
  • Holiday promotions: regular promotions such as the 15% opening sale, summer, Halloween and Apocalypse 20% bring returning users back.

Speculation:

  • The first users were GMs who had already bought a Foundry license; the marketplace does not need to acquire users itself, and the hard part is pulling in purchases that used to be scattered across Patreon/itch/creators' sites.
  • Official D&D and Pathfinder content is the biggest draw (D&D has the largest player base).
  • From memory, unverified: in early 2023 WotC's OGL controversy and Roll20's subscription model pushed many GMs toward the one-time-purchase Foundry, expanding the upstream user pool.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The marketplace itself has no public sales, creator count or revenue share.
  • Our data: direct visits 45%, referral 41.2%, search only 8.2% ("foundry marketplace", "foundry vtt"), US 34%, Brazil 7.8%, UK 5.1%.
  • Upstream scale: Foundry supports about 350 game systems (2025-05, Wikipedia); TikTok #foundryvtt has about 4,400 posts.
  • $15-30 per item, plus official rulebooks and bundles, so the average order value is not low.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch date, operator, partner publishers and official traffic routing all have first-hand sources, so they are credible.
  • Most search results were irrelevant (the first group was all Macau weather), and Reddit could not be opened.
  • Gaps: revenue share, GMV and user count; the equity relationship between MetaMorphic Digital Studio and Foundry; on which day the official WotC D&D content started selling in the marketplace.
