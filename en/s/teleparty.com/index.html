Overview

Teleparty (teleparty.com): Browser extension letting friends watch streaming shows together in sync with group chat. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 13,143 visits went from Teleparty to a checkout payment page — #213 of the 607 sites we track. That is 35% down from the April–June monthly average of 20,083.

Total website visits that month were about 1,459,567 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 84%, search 12%, referrals/email/paid 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, multiplayer rooms, and direct plus brand search is about 96%, plus shares via Messenger. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
