Basics

  • Product: an authentication + payment action for the GPT Store. GPT authors install it for free and can set a monthly paywall (optional 14-day trial), collect emails, set allowlists (free access for course/consulting clients) and see user chat topics and regions. End users log in on gpt-tools.co, pay with Stripe and manage their subscriptions.
  • Pricing: author side Free $0 / Pro $10/month / Business $90/month; the platform takes 30% of GPT subscription revenue (20% on Pro and Business); minimum payout $10 via PayPal or Wise (https://toproad.ai/faq).
  • Company: footer reads "re-mint pty ltd. trading as GPT Tools" (an Australian private company); parent brand Top Road (toproad.ai, YC W22, Sydney, 2 people; the YC page says "Founded 2024", probably counted again after a pivot) (https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/top-road).
  • Founders: Jeevika Makani (CEO, formerly at Fat Llama and Lendable, economics at the University of Melbourne, INSEAD MBA); Celilsemi Sam Erkiner (full-stack engineer, 50+ apps, Ionic open-source contributor).
  • Domain: the registration date of the.co could not be found via RDAP; Wayback first captured it in 2024-08. toproad.ai was registered 2024-03-05; gpt.builders was registered 2024-07-16.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Piggybacks on the GPT Store: all end users come from redirects out of GPTs inside ChatGPT (our data: Gen AI 91.6%, almost no referral or social). Every GPT with GPT Tools installed sends its users to log in at gpt-tools.co, exposing the domain and brand.
  • Author side uses case-study marketing: the toproad.ai blog tells author earnings stories (AI Gen making $10K+ a month, an African developer's first $1,000), and the site links to author GPTs and YouTube tutorials.
  • One big author = a lot of end users: AI Gen alone has thousands of GPTs and tens of thousands of conversations a day.

Speculation:

  • The first authors came from the creator community right after the GPT Store opened (posts on X, Reddit and Discord about "how to monetize a GPT"); the launch post was not found.
  • Japan is 32% of users, probably because one or two big Japanese GPT authors installed it.
  • Growth room is limited after the GPT Store's popularity faded; from 2025 the brand focus moved to toproad.ai.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Creators' cumulative subscription revenue is over $400K (official FAQ); at a 20-30% cut, the platform's cumulative take is about $80-120K (estimate).
  • Author-side subscriptions are $10 / $90 per month; number of authors not public.
  • Team of 2.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The company, founders, pricing and cut come from the YC page and the official site text, credible; the search results were all generic OpenAI articles and went unused.
  • Gaps: the exact launch date of gpt-tools.co; what Top Road did between 2022 and 2024 (YC W22 but "founded 2024"); number of authors and active GPTs.
