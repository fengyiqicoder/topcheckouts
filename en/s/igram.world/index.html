Basics

  • Product: paste a public Instagram link to download Reels, videos, photos, carousels, Stories, Highlights, profile pictures and IGTV, plus Threads video download, an API and an MCP interface; no login, unlimited use; Premium 1/6/12-month plans offer HD/4K and original audio track (prices not public). The pages carry Google ads and also push an Android downloader APK (downloadhelper.app).
  • Operator: the terms page only says "Company means iGram," with jurisdiction "Alaska, United States" (boilerplate terms), and no real company or person name.
  • Traffic (our data): direct 80.6%, search 16.4% (brand keywords 83.8% is igram, non-brand about 13.1%: instagram story download, instagram story downloader), social 3.1% (YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp). India 19.8%, South Korea 11.6%, Indonesia 10.8%.
  • Domain: registered 2023-02-10 (RDAP, Tucows); Wayback has a roughly 700-byte placeholder page from 2021-12 that belonged to the previous holder (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Multilingual long tail: 30 language subdirectories from launch; today's top three traffic countries are India, South Korea and Indonesia, and non-brand keywords are function terms like "instagram story download."
  • Cross-promotion in a site network: the pages point TikTok/Facebook/YouTube links to ssstik.live, instasupersave.com and ssyoutube.com.
  • Bookmark and return: direct visits 80.6%, brand keywords 83.8%; downloader tools are used frequently and repeatedly.
  • Advertorials and imitation sites: third-party "intro articles" provide backlinks; imitation sites like igramworld.in show that the term "igram world" has search volume.

Speculation:

  • The first users came from Google confusing the "igram" brand keyword (old iGram users searching igram may have clicked on it), plus function terms in each language; no sign of any community launch.
  • WhatsApp and YouTube among the social sources are mostly users sending each other links or "how to download Reels" tutorial videos.
  • Sites like this play cat-and-mouse with Meta's API blocking and Google's demotions for a long time; the /blocked redirects and multiple page versions (/en1, /en2) may both relate to this.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue comes from AdSense ads + Premium subscriptions + pushing the APK (possibly with a revenue share, speculation); amounts not public.
  • Third parties say it is one of the mainstream IG downloaders, but there is no reliable visit figure (the total in our traffic profile is not here).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there is no earlier report in notes. Search results were overwhelmingly generic articles about Instagram history and "Instagram alternatives"; evidence came mainly from RDAP, Wayback CDX and snapshots, and the current site's pages and terms.
  • Launch timing (registered 2023-02, live 2023-03) is credible; the site network relationship is only inferred from cross-linking, with no common operator found.
  • Gaps: the real operator, Premium prices and user count, revenue; the relationship to older "iGram" sites like igram.io.
