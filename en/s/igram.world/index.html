Overview

Igram (igram.world): iGram, free web tool that downloads public Instagram Reels, videos, photos, carousels and Stories from a link. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,744 visits went from Igram to a checkout payment page — #339 of the 607 sites we track. That is 4454% up from the April–June monthly average of 192.

Total website visits that month were about 3,178,805 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 81%, search 16%, social 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 13%, with queries like “instagram story download”, “instagram story downloader”, “instagram story”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
