Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder with his own audience: Viktor's YouTube channel dates back to 2015 with tens of thousands of subscribers; the site homepage links directly to his YouTube and X.
  • Time-limited free prompts: the founder posts time-limited free links on social media to draw traffic (competitor article).
  • Affiliate program: 40% commission, 30-day attribution, organic promotion only; in our channel data tolt.io referrals are 5.15%, matching the affiliate back end.
  • Brand search: brand keywords are 89% of the 32.5% search share, meaning users see it on a social platform and then search the name; social 6.5% comes from YouTube, X and Facebook.
  • Secondary spread: Korean and Chinese coverage (feishu.cn referrals, a Chinese site at motionsites.dev) and GitHub reposts show it being passed around AI site-building circles in several countries.

Speculation:

  • The output is itself a demo video: the motion pages each prompt generates are easy to screenshot and screen-record, and users and affiliate creators post "make a 3D website in one sentence" videos that carry the MotionSites name.
  • It rode the boom in vibe-coding tools like Lovable/Bolt/Cursor, sitting right on the upstream tool users' pain point of "it works but doesn't look good".

Revenue and scale clues

  • The founder claims 1 million monthly visits (2026-09, unverified).
  • Pricing $129–$399; the affiliate dashboard example numbers (41 orders, $2,940 paid) are only UI placeholders and should not be taken as real.
  • Our data: India 18.9%, US 13.5%, China 8.2%; direct visits 56.7%.
  • Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The operator, domain dates and product pivot path have first-hand evidence; the "1 million monthly visits" claim comes only from the founder's own promo video prompt.
  • Search results contained a lot of irrelevant content (the Motion calendar app), Reddit and X could not be opened, and the launch post was not found.
  • Gaps: Viktor's X follower count and first viral post, Product Hunt launch date, actual revenue.
