Overview

Viblo (viblo.ai): AI short-form video editor that auto-clips footage, generates voiceovers and captions, exports ready-to-post videos in b. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 107,450 visits went from Viblo to a checkout payment page — #13 of the 607 sites we track. That is 54% up from the April–June monthly average of 69,967.

Total website visits that month were about 221,969 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 48.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 46%, search 41%, social 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 16%, with queries like “viewmax”, “viewmax io”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
