Basics

  • Product: browser AI short-video editor (app.viblo.ai): Text Story, Video Story, Video Commentary, Split Screen, Video Ranking, Auto Clipping, AI voiceover, transcription, AI image/video generation, one-click export to TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
  • Company: the site and about page say Viblo AI LLC, Wyoming, USA; yet the Trustpilot business profile says "based in Australia", claimed 2026-01. Speculation: an Australian team collecting payments through a Wyoming LLC. Founder identity Not found, and the about page lists no team. WHOIS uses privacy protection (NameCheap).
  • Note: do not confuse it with viblo.asia, the Japanese Framgia tech community.
  • Pricing: Starter $25/month (annual 150), Creator $49/month (annual 294), Business $99/month (annual 594); 7-day trial, automatically charged when it ends.
  • Self-claims on the site: 100K+ users, 100K+ videos generated, 12K active creators; the homepage testimonials have first names only and the carousel repeats, low credibility.
  • Tech/growth stack: PostHog, GrowthBook (A/B testing), VariantNow, FirstPromoter, Tolt (affiliate/referral commission tracking), GTM.
  • Funding: no sign of any.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: lots of SEO landing pages (Crayo Alternative, VidIQ Alternative, Viewmax.io Alternative, Clipchamp Alternative, AI TikTok Video Generator, etc.). Our channel data: search is 40.8%, non-brand search about 16%, mainly the competitor terms "viewmax" and "viewmax io", showing it really does capture competitors' traffic.
  • Short-video exposure: a Trustpilot user wrote "saw this website on a reel"; social is 8.2% of our channel data (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok).
  • Community: Discord is the only social entry on the site, and reviews mention Discord.
  • Referrals: trustpilot.com 4.8%, toolify.ai 4.3% (an AI tool directory).
  • The site loads two affiliate tracking scripts, FirstPromoter and Tolt, but there is no public Affiliate page (/affiliate and /affiliates both 404), and no affiliate link on the homepage. The commission program may recruit privately, or may have stopped.

Speculation:

  • The first users probably came from "faceless TikTok account money-making" creators and Discord monetization groups (the same way the similar product Crayo took off), but no direct evidence was found this time.
  • The 7-day trial with automatic charging is the main conversion tactic, and it also brings many refund/chargeback complaints.
  • Videos users make (Reddit stories + gameplay split-screen) carry no brand watermark, so the product spreads itself weakly.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing $25-99/month; the site claims 100K+ users (unverified).
  • Trustpilot has about 92 reviews, mostly from 2026; the corresponding paying user base is probably in the thousands (Speculation).
  • MRR: Not found.
  • Our data: monthly checkout visits of about 50K–130K, very volatile.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch date, entity, pricing, SEO landing pages and Trustpilot reviews are all first-hand evidence.
  • Second follow-up check: the search results provided this time were almost all unrelated CVEs and adult sites, and only the official site pages were useful. Newly found: Trustpilot lists the company as in Australia, the first reviews appeared in 2026-02, a user "saw it on a Reel", the competitor term "viewmax" is the main driver of non-brand search, and the site has no public affiliate page. The growth engine changed from "Referral rewards or affiliates + Search ranking" to "Search ranking": commissions have only tracking scripts and no public program, so the evidence is not enough.
  • Gaps: founders, the specific source of the first 1,000 users, the reasons for the three months ago and last month jumps.
