Basics

  • Product: an AI video ad platform. Marketing Studio (preset templates produce multiple ad versions), Hooks Studio (pick the opening hook first), Avatar Studio (AI talking presenters), Recast Studio (copies a reference ad's format and camera moves and swaps in your own product), 60+ Viral Presets (Mirrorverse, Bullet Time, Museum Heist, etc.), Soldy Agent. Underlying models: Seedance 2.0/2.5, Kling 2.6, Minimax, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, Fish Audio.
  • Company: the About page says "developed and operated by CHATAIL, with a team in Shanghai and Singapore"; the domain registrant is Shanghai Chatail Information Technology Co., LTD. Founder not found.
  • Pricing: Starter $39/month ($27.30/month billed yearly, 2,000 credits); Growth $149/month ($104.30/month billed yearly, 8,000 credits); Custom contact sales (includes MCP); no free tier (compiled by the third-party withai.top).
  • Domain: soldy.ai registered 2025-10-23 (Dominet HK registrar); Wayback first captured it 2026-01-19.
  • Our data: United States 78.52%, Indonesia 21.48%; search 45.5% (brand keywords 66.6%, non-brand about 18% with terms hooks studio and the competitor name higgsfield), direct 39.3%, referral 9.1%; social has Facebook.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Credits for a new model to attract users: the X bio uses "early Seedance free credits" to guide people into Discord (summary only).
  • Search ranking: search is 45.5% in our data; non-brand terms include the competitor name higgsfield and the feature name hooks studio; the site has many "vs competitor," "competitor alternatives" and "best xxx 2026" articles aimed squarely at competitor terms.
  • Creator supply: pays overseas creators to make viral templates, with a revenue share by usage; the template library (60+ presets) is the core selling point in outward promotion (Instagram Reel: "60+ viral presets").
  • Official social accounts: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X and Discord all exist, but follower counts are small (IG 745).
  • Directory listings: SourceForge, Slashdot, moge.ai (Chinese), withai.top and others.

Speculation:

  • Indonesia at 21% is odd; speculated to be Indonesia-targeted advertising or an agency partnership, with no evidence.
  • Some US users may come from Meta ad campaigns (a company making an ad tool most likely runs ads itself), with no direct evidence.
  • The first users were most likely AI video hobbyists in Discord who came for the Seedance credits, not brands, and only later shifted to e-commerce / DTC merchants through SEO.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscriptions of $39-149/month, no free tier, so a relatively high price per customer.
  • User count and revenue not found; small social following suggests the scale is still small.

Confidence and gaps

  • The company entity, registration date, pricing, underlying models and the creator recruiting model all have primary sources.
  • The search results provided were mostly irrelevant (Sol AI, military AI, etc.); X would not open, and the Seedance credits item is a snippet only.
  • Gaps: the exact launch date and first launch channel (no PH/HN post found), the source of the Indonesian traffic, whether there is paid advertising, revenue.
