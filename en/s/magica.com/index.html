Overview

Magica (magica.com): AI workspace with an 'AI worker': 'Work at the speed of thought'. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Magica to a checkout payment page — #438 of the 607 sites we track. That is 117% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,959.

Total website visits that month were about 114,181 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 7.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 65%, search 26%, social 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)
