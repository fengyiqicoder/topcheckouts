Basics

  • Product: Magica (formerly Galaxy.ai), a general-purpose AI Agent workbench: connects multiple LLMs, your apps and a cloud computer, and completes tasks you assign; also keeps 5,000+ tools (image, video, audio, LLM etc.), projects, scheduled tasks, API/MCP.
  • Company: GalaxyAI Inc. (8 The Green STE A, Dover, DE), which also runs the AI Snacks daily AI newsletter and GalaxyAI for Business. Sources: https://galaxy.ai/; https://ai-alternative.com/tool/magica
  • Founders: not stated on the site or third-party pages; not found.
  • Pricing: Hobby $14.99/month ($12.50/month billed yearly), Pro $100/month, Max $200/month, Teams $15/user/month; in the Galaxy.ai era $15/month or $99.99/year (list price $30 shown at 50% off).
  • Domain: magica.com registered 2023-03-23 per RDAP (2023-03 snapshot was a 301 redirect), Magica on Wayback from 2026-05-13; galaxy.ai was NanoTechGalaxy before 2021 (liquidated as insolvent in 2021-06), the Galaxy.ai bundle site from 2024-11.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Programmatic SEO: magica.com/youtube-summarizer about 200K pages, blog about 53K pages, 1,270 feature pages, 524 mini-tools, 3,436 news items (sitemap count, 2026-10-25); search is 26.5%, non-brand about 16% with very scattered terms, matching these pages.
  • Affiliate commissions: 30% commission, 12 months, $60 cap per sale, 90-day cookie; new users who redeem a referral code get bonus credits. Source: https://magica.com/refer
  • Creator referral codes: small YouTube creators put referral codes (e.g. GWKKP26) in video descriptions.
  • Referral sources: galaxy.ai (1.9%, old-domain redirect), stackbalo.com, workona.com; social 5.3% (LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook).
  • The parent company's own newsletter AI Snacks can cross-promote (exists, but traffic volume not found).

Speculation:

  • Galaxy.ai's first users came from the "all models for one price" comparison pitch + affiliate creators + long-tail search pages, not a community launch (no PH/Reddit launch post found).
  • Direct 64.6% is mainly existing subscribers returning.
  • The parent's "acquire and scale AI products" positioning suggests it may have bought other tool sites for traffic (unverified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Trustpilot 1,530 reviews (4.3 rating); extrapolating from typical review rates, paying users are likely in the tens of thousands (speculation).
  • Pricing $14.99–$200/month; revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New entry, written from scratch. Rename, company, pricing and commission come from the Magica/Galaxy sites, Wayback and third-party reviews, credible; programmatic SEO scale comes from an actual sitemap count.
  • Gaps: founders, exact Galaxy.ai launch date, user count and revenue, and the reason for the rename were not found. The "launch" search set returned nothing.
