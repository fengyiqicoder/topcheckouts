Overview

Wiki-masters (wiki-masters.com): French collectible card game where each card is generated from a Wikipedia article, with duels, guilds and auctions. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 197,708 visits went from Wiki-masters to a checkout payment page — #3 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 13,550,150 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 1.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 60%, search 30%, social 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, multiplayer rooms, public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 90%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
