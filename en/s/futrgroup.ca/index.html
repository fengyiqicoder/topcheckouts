Basics

  • Product: FutrGroup Education. Three things: the FutrGroup Lite membership (sold on Whop at $1,500 one-time for life, the site claims "350+ members worldwide"), the FutrGroup AI Course ($47, covers making AI UGC videos in 55 minutes), and a free YouTube channel. All check out through Whop (whop.com/futrgroup-access).
  • Operator: Franky Shaw (YouTube bio: "10-year e-commerce operator, focused on dropshipping and consumer psychology"). Nationality / real identity not found; the.ca domain hints at Canada.
  • Domain: futrgroup.ca registered 2023-10-19 (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2024-01-09.
  • YouTube: @francoshaw, channel ID UC0pjftumJvH_5uEYM2WuFFQ, about 12K subscribers, 32 videos; the channel's sameAs points to www.futrgroup.ca.
  • Traffic: United States 47%, India 30%, Indonesia 23%; direct 46%, search 39% (mainly a person's name and course names such as "futr course and ecom talent", "franky shaw coaching"), referral 9%. April–June average about 9,881, September 3,451.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Long YouTube tutorial videos → course link in the description → futrgroup.ca → Whop checkout.
  • A low-priced $47 AI UGC micro course as the front-end product and the $1,500 Lite as the back end (the site lists both tiers side by side).
  • 224 Whop reviews at 4.9 serve as social proof.

Speculation:

  • India and Indonesia make up more than half of the traffic, possibly from dropshipping beginners in those regions watching the English tutorials; or from Whop affiliate promotion (Whop has a built-in affiliate mechanism), not verified.
  • Traffic rises and falls with the YouTube update schedule: no new videos appear in the channel RSS in 2026, which matches the September decline.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site claims 350+ Lite members; at $1,500 each that is on the order of $500K (estimate, not verified, there may be discounted prices).
  • 224 Whop reviews; 12K YouTube subscribers, 1K–29K views per video.
  • Video titles claim "$3.6 million in 28 days from one image ad" and "an extra $295,000 in 90 days", which are marketing lines and not verified.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The Google results were all unrelated (CVEs, flights, Fruugo); this report's evidence comes from the site, Whop, the YouTube channel page and RSS, RDAP and Wayback CDX.
  • Gaps: Wayback page snapshot fetches were rate-limited (429), so what the site first sold in early 2024 could not be seen; the publish dates of early videos are unknown; the founder's real identity and when FutrGroup 1.0 went on sale were not found.
