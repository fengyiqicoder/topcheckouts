Basics

  • Product: real-time AI assistant for interviews, sales calls, meetings and exams: listens to the question → gives the answer, reads shared code, summarizes calls; desktop / browser / mobile web; claims to be "undetectable". Comes with mock interviews, resumes, AI ID photos and a real-question bank.
  • Company: ParakeetAI d.o.o., Ljubljana, Slovenia; the same team also makes Kismo (kismo.app) and Tarko AI (tarko-ai.com). No outside funding.
  • Founders: Jure Sotošek (X @JureSotosek, formerly founder of the WrumerSound ecommerce business); co-founder Urban Peklar handles video production (note.com citing an IndieHackers interview). The person who posted ParakeetAI 2.0 on Product Hunt in 2026-10 is Domen Perko (team member). Started with 2 people, about 10 in 2026, 3 of them on video.
  • Launch: domain registered 2024-08-18; development began in 2024-09, selling started after about 3 weeks of development, and the first order came in the first week after launch.
  • Tech: Next.js + Stripe + Speechmatics (transcription) + ChatGPT; the landing page was outsourced on Webflow for about €1,000.
  • Pricing: free 10 minutes; per-use credits $38 each (30 minutes is 0.5 credit), 3 for $59 … 15 for $177; Unlimited $149.90/month, $599.90/year, $78/week.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Cold start: the founder brought the short-video craft from the previous ecommerce project (not users), posting 2 short videos a day on his own accounts → hits → visitors arrive and pay (Starter Story founder's own account).
  • Amplification: hand the format to hundreds of zero-follower new account creators paid $5–10 per video, thousands of videos a day across TikTok/IG/YT/Facebook; the founder estimates the cost at about 1/100 of getting the same views from influencers or Meta ads (IH interview retold by note.com).
  • Controversy-driven: the cheating topic sparks fights in the comments and the algorithm keeps pushing it (passionbits, LinkedIn posts).
  • Conversion: video → brand keyword Google search (92% of search traffic is brand keywords) → website → one-time credit purchase; Google Ads on brand keywords intercepts traffic.
  • Reddit posts contributed little; HN did not work.
  • Speculation:
  • Many third-party "Parakeet review / alternatives" articles (written by competitors such as Final Round, Interview Coder and MeetAssist) in fact heat up brand keyword search.
  • Word of mouth in Indian job-seeker groups.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2025-01: $16K/month (Starter Story, founder's own account).
  • Around the end of 2025: a third party estimates $60K/month (passionbits, unverified).
  • 2026-09: IndieHackers interview says $1M+ monthly revenue (retold by note.com; a LinkedIn post also says $1M/month in 18 months).
  • Users: 530K+ in 2026-02 (third party), 1.5M+ on the official site in 2026-10.
  • Videos: 3B+ cumulative views, 1B+ in the last 30 days (self-reported).

Confidence and gaps

  • The early playbook and the $16K figure come from the founder's own account (Starter Story), high credibility; the $1M/month figure is a secondhand retelling of the IH interview, and the original was not opened.
  • Missing the specific first hit video and its date; the time of the breakout in Taiwan was still Not found.
