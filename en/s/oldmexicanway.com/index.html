  • Product: the PDF ebook "The Old Mexican Way", 632 pages and 41 chapters (cleaning, folk remedies, food storage, organizing), $39.99 (list price $59.99 shown, countdown discount); 3-book bundle $74.99, pick-your-own combos; 7-day refund without returning the book; payments via Stripe.
  • Persona: Bertha Ramírez, who says she is a cleaner's daughter who learned these methods from her mother in Guadalajara. The website has no company name or address, only a contact form.
  • Scale signal: the page says "first two books rated 5.0 by 82 verified buyers", which suggests the same operator had or has other books/sites (which ones was not found).
  • Domain: registered 2026-09-01 (Name.com, RDAP); earliest Wayback capture 2026-09-15.
  • Channel: YouTube @HackTheMexicanWay, "Joined Aug 18, 2026", 128K subscribers, 57 videos, 11,521,889 total views (captured 2026-10-25).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Content → description link → ebook: the first line of every video description fixedly links to the book site.
  • High posting frequency: 57 videos in two months (nearly daily), with titles following a "nostalgia + saving money + Mexican mom vs. Americans" template aimed at middle-aged and older Americans and Latino families.
  • Page conversion design: struck-through list price, countdown, bundle price, "82 verified buyers, 5.0 rating".

Speculation:

  • The persona and content are very likely an AI-assisted, mass-produced "faceless channel" (57 long videos in two months from a new account, and no real entity on the website), unverified.
  • The same operator is probably copying the "old-ways persona + ebook" template (the "first two books" wording), and there may be versions for other countries or ethnic groups.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Price $39.99 / bundle $74.99. With 11.52 million total views, at a typical 0.05%-0.2% purchase conversion that is roughly 6,000-23,000 orders and $240K-$900K (speculation, very wide range, unverified). There is also YouTube ad revenue share.
  • The page only discloses "82 verified buyers" (for the first two books).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch (no earlier report). The channel launch date, domain registration date, video traffic routing and channel scale all have first-hand data.
  • Gaps: whether Bertha is a real person, the operator's identity, which videos are the hits, and actual sales were not found; the scam-detection page could not be opened.
