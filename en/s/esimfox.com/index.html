A small company in Essen, Germany (eSIM FOX GmbH) opened a Shopify eSIM resale store in 2023, reselling wholesale data from upstream carriers. With prices from €1.99, country landing-page SEO, and affiliate commissions, it got itself listed on the major eSIM comparison/review sites (esimchart, mybestsim, and others carry commissioned links as a 'verified partner'), then topped up volume with social media in small languages such as Arabic and Russian. In 2026 it was rebuilt as a 20-language Next.js site and put an App on the store, and in September checkout visits surged. No founder story or 'takeoff event' was found; growth is channel spread, not word of mouth.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: prepaid eSIM data plans for international travel, 200+ destinations (esimcomparator counts 987 plans across 168 destinations), installed by scanning a QR code, top-up supported, WhatsApp support.
  • Entity: the official site's structured data gives the legal name eSIM FOX GmbH, address Gerlingplatz 1, 45127 Essen, Germany, foundingDate 2023 (https://esimfox.com/en). The App Store seller shows ESIMFOX LTD (App id 6759492009, listed 2026-05-04). esimchart calls it 'a German-language supplier focused on the DACH market, priced in euros' (https://www.esimchart.com/en/anbieter/esimfox). Founder/team not found.
  • Funding: not found.
  • Pricing: country plans from €1.99, US from €2.99, Dubai from €5.99.
  • Self-reported figures are inconsistent: the homepage says '300,000+ travelers' while the reviews page says '50K+ travelers connected' and claims 28,347 reviews at 4.7 (https://esimfox.com/en/reviews); Trustpilot actually has only about 96-98.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Where the first users came from: no founder account was found. The earliest external trace visible is a question post in the Reddit eSIM forum a month after launch, plus the Shopify store's discount code, suggesting the early approach was 'low price + exposure on travel forums / comparison tables' (summary only).
  • Where new users come from: (1) affiliate distribution via comparison/review sites: at least 6 eSIM comparison sites list it, 3 of which clearly carry commissioned links or exclusive discount codes; (2) its own affiliate program, up to 20% commission, with assets for creators to put in videos/bios; (3) SEO: country landing pages + 'best eSIM for X' blog, replicated across 20 languages; the profile data shows search at 50.8% and brand keywords at 45%; (4) small-language social media: Arabic TikTok @esimfox_ar has 12.8K likes while the main account @esimfox has only 451 likes (https://www.tiktok.com/@esimfox_ar); Russia accounts for about 8% of user countries.
  • Speculation: the September surge is more likely affiliate/comparison-site volume stacked on summer travel, plus Direct traffic from in-app repurchases; the eSIM product itself has no mechanism for bringing in other people. The self-reported 28K reviews versus under 100 on Trustpilot is a big gap; it may count in-app ratings or aggregator platforms in full.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported 300K travelers (homepage) / 50K+ (reviews page); the two contradict each other, so taking the lower bound is safer.
  • September checkout visits 31K; at an eSIM order value of €5-15, monthly revenue could be in the tens of thousands to over a hundred thousand euros (speculation).
  • Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass check: the entity was corrected from 'LTD / Essen / Dutch registration number conflict' to eSIM FOX GmbH (Essen, founded 2023) from the official site's structured data; added evidence of affiliate distribution via comparison sites and the Arabic TikTok; found the contradiction between 300K and 50K self-reported users.
