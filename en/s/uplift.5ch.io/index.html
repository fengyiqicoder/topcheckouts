UPLIFT is not a new startup but the official paid membership of the operator of Japan's largest anonymous forum, 5ch (formerly 2ch). In 2023-08 the dedicated-browser company Jane broke with 5ch and the old paid account "Ronin (浪人)" was cut off, so the 5ch operators launched UPLIFT on 2023-08-30 and automatically migrated the Ronin subscribers over. After that, the forum used on-site means such as the "Donguri (どんぐり)" posting level/restriction system, VPN and overseas carrier posting limits, ads, and charging for past logs to push heavy users toward the $4/month membership. Nearly 70% of its traffic is internal forum referral, so it depends entirely on 5ch's own upstream traffic; in 2026 it moved to 5ch.io along with the forum and showed up in Stripe checkout data in September.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: 5ch's official paid membership UPLIFT (formerly "Premium Ronin"). Perks: no ads on 5ch.io, access to past logs (via the dedicated browser), treatment at the "Hunter" level in the "Donguri (どんぐり)" system so posting/thread-creation limits are relaxed, and lifting of some regional and carrier posting restrictions. New in 2026-10: the "5ch Council (5ch 評議会, council.5ch.io)", where members get one vote each on topics the operators open up.
  • Pricing: 1 month $4, 6 months $20, 12 months $36, auto-renewing (profile record; the 5ch wiki in 2026-05 also lists $4/month), mainly by credit card (the forum has a thread "Victims' club for those scammed because prepaid virtual cards can't buy UPLIFT").
  • Operations: the 5ch operators (from memory: 5ch is run by Loki Technology, unverified).
  • Traffic (profile record): referral 69.8% (in-forum links from 5ch.net, bbspink.com, etc.), direct 22.6%, search 4.5% ("5ch uplift", "uplift とは"); Japan 72%, US 22.8%, South Korea 3.4% (overseas users are mostly VPN users / Japanese living abroad, Speculation).
  • Domain: uplift.5ch.net has Wayback snapshots from 2023-08-29, unstable after 2025-09 and a 301 redirect from 2026-06; the first snapshot of uplift.5ch.io is 2026-03-06.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • On-site guidance: referral 69.8% comes from 5ch's own domains, and the membership entry is embedded in forum pages and restriction prompts (profile record + wiki perk description).
  • Restriction → payment: the Donguri system and posting limits block ordinary users from posting, and UPLIFT members get the limits relaxed (wiki).
  • 2026-10 Council: gives paying members "voting rights", upgrading membership from "ad removal" to "participating in governance" and strengthening the reason to renew (council page).

Speculation:

  • The Council thresholds (low-impact topics 100 votes = 5%, medium 200 = 10%, high 300 = 15%, critical 500 = 25%) all correspond to a base of about 2,000 members across the four tiers, hinting at an active paying member count on the order of 2,000 (Speculation, back-calculated from the ratios).
  • Much of the 22.8% US traffic is VPN users / Japanese living abroad, who have to buy membership because overseas carriers are restricted from posting.
  • Trolls/spammers (荒らし) also buy UPLIFT to bypass restrictions (the forum has a thread "Troll report on UPLIFT users ★34"), so a considerable share of paying members are heavy/spamming users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Back-calculating from the Council thresholds gives about 2,000 active members × ~$3/month (including annual discounts) ≈ $6,000/month (Speculation, possibly an underestimate because the thresholds may be designed around "active voters").
  • September Stripe checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate).
  • The 2023 announcement says 5ch revenue fell sharply after the break with Jane, and UPLIFT is the direct means of making up the revenue.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The 2023-08 origin (Ronin → UPLIFT) has the original announcement and Wayback evidence, credible.
  • Gaps: member count and revenue not public; the Donguri launch time and the operating company name are from memory; the reason and exact date of the 5ch.net → 5ch.io migration, with no original official announcement found (only an X repost summary); the announcement page's encoding was garbled, so some content may be missing.
