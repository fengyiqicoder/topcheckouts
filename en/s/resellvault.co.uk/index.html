Basics

  • Product: Vinted new-listing monitor (claims 0.14ms), AutoBuy/AutoCop automatic ordering, retail drop alerts (public ledger), a discount group, wholesale sourcing, AI valuation/listing tools, a mobile app, 45+ guides; free to join on Discord.
  • Pricing: Pro £18 for the first month (code UNLOCKNOW), then £25+VAT/month; Elite £50+VAT/month; 1:1 mentor £100; 30-day refund.
  • Who is behind it: Tom (technical) and Louie (teaching, hosts the community voice classes), full names not public. Company Resell Vault LLP (OC456967, England and Wales, registered 2025-06-03, Thatcham, Berkshire). No funding seen.
  • Launch date: Discord 2025-02-18; domain registered 2025-02-28.
  • Note on same-name entities: the "Resell Vault" on Whop (Wavey, reselling since 2020), the 2022 TikTok @resellvault_ (sneakers), the "resell vault that resells Warhammer books" criticized on Reddit r/Blacklibrary in 2024, and resellvault.com / theresellvault.com are not this site, and this site's official page specifically clarifies this.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Cold start: the founders came from the existing UK reselling group circle, 500 members in 12 days (the first batch were most likely dissatisfied members of other cook groups, inferred from the about page saying they were "members of other groups"; the exact way they recruited was not written).
  • Free Discord + paywall (free alerts are slow, AutoCop only on higher tiers) → conversion.
  • Listing in Discord server directories; group links in reselling YouTubers' descriptions; official TikTok (@resellvaul7, plus @_resellvault with 10K followers and "£3m+ profit", ownership unverified).
  • Social proof: results page, public drop ledger, Missed Flips ledger, Trustpilot management.
  • 2026-09 takeoff: rewritten bot + first-month discount code.
  • Speculation:
  • Members post Vinted buy-low-sell-high screenshots on TikTok and bring people into the group; there may be an invite reward (not mentioned on the site).
  • The US accounts for 66% of last month visits (Similarweb), which does not fit the UK positioning, possibly ad/bot traffic or a data error.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 500+ paid members × about £25–50 → estimated about £12K–20K/month (calculated).
  • Discord 32,000+; bots have placed 147,000 cumulative orders (official site).

Confidence and gaps

  • The founding timeline comes from the official about page's own account (with very specific dates), cross-checked against the Discord ID, WHOIS and the Companies House number, fairly high credibility.
  • Where the 500 members in the first two weeks came from and the viral TikTok videos were Not found; the direct cause of the last month surge is still speculation.
  • Second follow-up check: added the full official milestones (500 in 12 days, 10,000 in 4 months, charging started 2025-04), the LLP registration info, the founders' origin story of "leaving other reselling groups", and the YouTuber description traffic evidence, and flagged that several same-name "Resell Vault" entities are not this site.
