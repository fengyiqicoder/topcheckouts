This is not a startup but a unified "official airport car parks" booking site run by Flughafen Parken GmbH (registered at Munich Airport), a joint venture initiated by the German Airports Association (ADV) and several German-speaking airports. It took the park.aero domain in early 2018 and launched with the airports' own car parks at 7 airports (Frankfurt, Hamburg, Hanover, Cologne/Bonn, Munich, Stuttgart, Vienna), then added airports year by year (11 in 2022, 13 in 2024, 15 now). New customers come from the "book parking" entry points on each airport's official site, affiliate traffic from parking comparison and travel deal sites, and "airport + parking booking" searches; returning customers come back directly to book for their next trip.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: online booking of official car parks at German and Austrian airports, with discounts for booking in advance and free cancellation; includes a business traveler option; the interface is in German, English, Dutch, Czech and Slovak. It now covers 15 airports: Berlin BER, Bremen, Dortmund, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Hanover, Cologne/Bonn, Memmingen, Munich, Nuremberg, Paderborn, Stuttgart, Salzburg and Vienna. https://www.park.aero/
  • Operator: Flughafen Parken GmbH, "a joint venture of multiple German-speaking airports", "an initiative of the German Airports Association (ADV)"; address Terminalstraße Mitte 18, 85356 München-Flughafen; CEO Ludwig Ascher; Munich district court HRB 239034; there is also a partner portal (Partner-Portal). https://www.park.aero/en/imprint
  • Pricing: pay per booking by airport, car park and number of days, with prices that vary over time.
  • Traffic mix (profile record): direct 83.8%, search 9.8%, referral 6.4%, brand keywords 91.7%; referrals from parkmundo.de 5.5%, travel-dealz.de 4.7% and parkmundo.at 3.8% (parking comparison and travel deal sites); Germany 68%, Austria 12%, Netherlands 4%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Institutional backing and supply: the shareholders are the airports themselves, who own the inventory of the airports' own car parks; the ADV led more airports to join, taking the airport count from 7 to 15.
  • Traffic from affiliates and comparison sites: parkmundo.de/.at and travel-dealz.de together account for about 14% of referral traffic (relative to total referrals), indicating affiliate/partner distribution.
  • Search: non-brand terms such as "parken flughafen nürnberg buchen".
  • Repeat purchase: direct visits are 84% and brand keywords 92%, mostly returning customers or people who click in from airport official sites.

Speculation:

  • The parking booking button on each airport's official site probably redirects to the matching park.aero page, which may be counted as direct or referral in traffic stats; this is the main acquisition entry point.
  • The Dutch, Czech and Slovak interfaces target travelers who drive across borders to German and Austrian airports.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue is the parking fee (which goes to the airport) plus a platform commission or service fee, not public. The 15 airports together have a large annual passenger volume, but the online booking share is unknown.

Confidence and gaps

  • The shareholding of the joint venture partners, the exact founding date and booking volume were not found; "takeoff year 2018" is filled in from the site's launch time.
  • Why the Zurich partnership ended was not found.
  • This is a new site, written from scratch this time; the search results were almost entirely irrelevant (Macau water show, rocket launches), and the evidence comes from the official imprint page (Impressum), yearly Wayback archives (read with curl), RDAP and the profile record.
