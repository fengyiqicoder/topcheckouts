Overview

Nintendoproject (nintendoproject.com): Spanish-language catalog linking downloadable Nintendo game ROMs, torrents and Mediafire folders with VIP access. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,185 visits went from Nintendoproject to a checkout payment page — #200 of the 607 sites we track. That is 12% down from the April–June monthly average of 19,524.

Total website visits that month were about 32,529 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 52.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 41%, referrals/email/paid 33%, search 26%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 67%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
