Basics

  • Product: game ROM downloads for 25 platforms including Switch, Switch 2, 3DS, Wii, PS1 to PS5, Xbox and PC, headlined by "all games are multi-language including Spanish". The paywalls are "CATÁLOGO COMPLETO TORRENT VIP" and "CARPETA VIP JUEGOS MEDIAFIRE". There is also a MIG Switch (Switch flashcart) section, emulator recommendations, and it has started adding movie resources (a "spider-man-brand-new-day-pelicula-online" post in 2026-08).
  • Operator: anonymous. The only WordPress author is "Nintendo Project" (slug iykdl). The DMCA page states it "operates under Spanish and EU law as a service provider within the European region", so it is probably in Spain (speculation). Team, scale and funding were not found (sites like this do not raise funding).
  • Tech: WordPress 7.1 plus Elementor, with a lot of self-written gamified JS on the front end (Mii avatars, levels, the "Poké Recreo" pet).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Member referral program: a Trustpilot review says the user signed up after being "invited by a friend through the referral program" (evidence; the reward content was not found).
  • Official TikTok account driving traffic: posts console collections, MIG Switch and meme videos, 25K followers (evidence; how much traffic a single video brings was not found).
  • One article per game, plus "complete collection" landing pages by console and by series (Mario, Pokémon, Zelda), is a typical SEO long-tail matrix (in our data the top channel is also Organic Search).
  • Free content paired with a VIP paywall (complete torrent catalogs, Mediafire folders).
  • Member system: registered accounts, Mii avatars, levels, "Mi Pokémon" raising. The members-only chat room Plaza Mii advertises "real-time chat with 19,500+ members" and "opens slots gradually", creating a sense of scarcity.
  • The homepage carries a Trustpilot link and "VER +6.000 OPINIONES" (6,000+ reviews), and also a Google business page g.page link, used to build trust and raise conversion.
  • Speculation:
  • In Similarweb, 66.6% of new users are classed as viral and referrals are 33.4%, which fits a referral program + WhatsApp private-chat forwarding.
  • Members ask each other for links and help each other in Plaza Mii, and also recommend the site to friends who play with MIG Switch or modded consoles; YouTube and TikTok tutorial videos in the Switch modding community may cite this site (this is the source of the third channel, Referrals).
  • The three months ago peak corresponds to summer vacation and the release period of big titles.

Revenue and scale clues

  • There is a paid VIP product (complete torrent catalogs, Mediafire folders); the specific prices were not visible in the homepage HTML.
  • Claims 19,500+ community members; Trustpilot 6,647 reviews, rating 4.9. No revenue data.

Confidence and gaps

  • The domain dates, the scale of WordPress content and on-site features all have direct evidence.
  • The old domain or old site before the migration, the true earliest takeoff time and the operator's identity were not found: this time the WebSearch quota ran out, Trustpilot was blocked and Wayback was rate-limited (429), so the trail could not be followed further. Pirated ROM sites often change domains because of DMCA, so "took off in 2024" is probably not the earliest takeoff.
  • Second-pass check: added the official TikTok account (videos since 2024-11, 25K followers), the Trustpilot 2024-08 claim and review pacing, and the evidence of the member referral program; the growth engine was changed from "Search ranking" to "Referral rewards or affiliates" (Similarweb new users are mainly viral/referral, and there is evidence of a referral program, but the referral program's share is unverified); the first users are still speculated to come from Spanish ROM long-tail search with no direct evidence; the old site/old domain was still not found.
