In 2026-07 it became an "AI-native personal CRM" (footer: "Made by HubSpot Labs"): connect LinkedIn to import your network in a minute, then hook up Gmail, calendar, HubSpot and X, and it automatically reminds you who to contact and drafts messages. New users come mainly from Dharmesh himself posting constantly on LinkedIn (1M+ followers) and resharing user praise; social is 48.5% of traffic, with LinkedIn first. Basics

  • Product: AI CRM for individuals (independent consultants, founders, investors, salespeople); reads email, calendar, LinkedIn, HubSpot and X, automatically builds person/company profiles; a "second brain" that can be carried into other AI apps via MCP; nightly digest with follow-up reminders; drafted messages require user confirmation before sending; one-click export. Comes with mini-tools MetaPrompt (prompt optimization) and HubCode (build HubSpot apps with AI).
  • Team: About page is a letter signed by Dharmesh Shah (2026-07); footer "Made with ❤ by HubSpot Labs" (2026-08 Wayback); FAQ does not state the legal relationship with HubSpot the company.
  • Domain: registered 2006-01-01 (RDAP, GoDaddy); other content from 2004 onward; in 2024-04 it was Dharmesh's "solopreneur tools" page (NodeRank, required Twitter login); in 2024-12 it was you.ai ("build your own bot… Built by @dharmesh with GPT-4"); from 2026-08 it is YouSpot Solo CRM.
  • Pricing: free $0 (50 credits/month, no card); Pro $10/month (1,000 credits).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Founder's personal audience: Dharmesh has 1M+ LinkedIn followers (he says 1.2M), posts about YouSpot frequently and reshares user testimonials; social is the number one traffic source.
  • Testimonial re-amplification: early users write about their experience on LinkedIn, and the founder reshares to amplify.
  • HubSpot ecosystem: hubspot.com brings referrals, HubCode targets HubSpot developers, and the product connects to HubSpot.
  • Sign-up connects LinkedIn directly, lowering the onboarding barrier.
  • Speculation:
  • The first users were Dharmesh's LinkedIn/X followers and independent consultants and founders in the HubSpot community; the high share of Indian users is tied to the makeup of his LinkedIn following.
  • The product's "draft messages to your contacts" output does not expose the brand externally, so output is unbranded and spread depends mainly on the founder.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No user or revenue figures; priced at $10/month, scale depends on free-to-paid conversion.
  • Facebook 8.9K followers; LinkedIn posts get dozens to low hundreds of interactions, not a viral level of buzz.

Confidence and gaps

  • Domain history, Dharmesh's signature and the HubSpot Labs footer all come from Wayback and the site's own text; credible.
  • YouSpot's formal public launch date (estimated 2026-07~08) and whether it launched on Product Hunt not found; the relationship between HubSpot Labs and HubSpot the company not found.
  • The Samuel Ebanja LinkedIn result in the given searches is unrelated to this site.
