Basics

  • Product: two lines. (1) Tippie Trinkgeld: collect tips via QR code/link, €0.19 + 6.5% fee (the site says 92%–96% of customers cover the fee themselves), free for merchants, no contract; (2) TippiePay: Android/iOS phone Tap to Pay and QR payments, 1.3% per transaction for businesses, 3.9% for non-profits, no monthly fee, no hardware, payout in 3 business days. The nav also lists a ticketing app and donation collection.
  • Target customers: hair and beauty, hotels, restaurants and bars, events and festivals, associations/NGOs, tradespeople, vets. German-speaking markets only.
  • Company: the site's Impressum now lists GSI Verwaltung GmbH (Am Hag 10, 83627 Warngau, Bavaria; managing director Christian Schuller; HRB 268854 Munich) — https://tippie.de/impressum/. LinkedIn says founded 2021, headquartered in Leipzig, 3 employees (Volodymyr Letiahin, Annika Tippie, Jens Krause), 177 followers — https://www.linkedin.com/company/tippie/. Gastgewerbe-Magazin also calls it 'a Leipzig company'. The Leipzig vs. Warngau discrepancy may reflect a change of operating entity; not verified.
  • Domain: tippie.de belonged to someone else in earlier years (2013 'under construction', 2016–2018 a sweepstakes site 'Tippie.de - Gewinnspiele'); the earliest snapshot of the current product is 2021-06 (just the title 'Tippie'), and the 2023-02 snapshot is already a full digital-tipping landing page..de RDAP does not disclose registration dates.
  • Reputation: the site displays Google 4.8 stars / 186 reviews (2026-01). SoftPOS24's review scores it 2.8/5, ranked 53rd of 122.

From the available evidence, the earliest merchants came from direct sales to restaurants/hair salons (the site has always pushed 'free in-store materials: QR stands customized to your shop's branding', plus WhatsApp and Zoom consultations), supplemented by placements in a hairdressing-industry newsletter (speculation, based on the utm parameters).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded checkout page: every customer who scans the tippie QR code at a merchant's counter opens a tippie page (app2.tippie.de). This matches the channel profile's 'referral 39.1%, direct 47.8%' — most traffic is the payment action itself, not people looking for the product.
  • Paid acquisition: sign-up links carry Facebook Ads utm parameters (including Reels test creatives).
  • Industry channels: Friseur.digital newsletter, Gastgewerbe-Magazin advertorial, Google Business profile.
  • SEO: a dozen-plus 'phone as card reader / card payments for [industry]' landing pages added after 2025; non-brand keywords in the profile include 'trinkgeld app zum verteilen' and 'tap to pay'.

Speculation:

  • Tipping merchants → TippiePay cross-sell: the 500+ merchants already using it for tips were the first SoftPOS customers.
  • Employee word of mouth: staff who earn more tips recommend it at their next workplace — no evidence for this.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 5,000+ employees and 500+ merchants using the tipping feature (Gastgewerbe-Magazin, date unknown).
  • Revenue model: tips at 6.5% + €0.19 (mostly paid by the customer); TippiePay takes 1.3%. Assuming 500 merchants, revenue should be within a few hundred thousand euros a year (speculation).
  • LinkedIn shows only 3 employees and 177 followers, indicating a very small company. 186 Google reviews.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written fresh from scratch (no earlier report). Evidence for the product, pricing and pivot timing is fairly hard (site plus multiple Wayback snapshots).
  • Gaps: who the founders are, how the Leipzig and Warngau entities relate, and where the first merchants came from — no primary accounts found; search results are almost all unrelated 'Tippi' namesakes, with no Reddit, Product Hunt or press coverage. 'Takeoff = Paid ads' rests solely on the FB Ads utm parameters, which is weak evidence.
