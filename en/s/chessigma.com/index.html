Basics

  • Product: free, unlimited chess game analysis (local Stockfish, no signup, just enter a Chess.com/Lichess username); later expanded to daily training, a Chess DNA report, the AI coach Supercoach, Woodpecker tactics, Blunder Shield, opening courses, bot practice, a leaderboard, and more.
  • Team: founder Mehdi (software engineer, X @mehdibhaddou, author of the official launch blog post); the site also links @eliot_hennebo, speculated to be a cofounder. Started with the ShipFast template (a Next.js indie stack).
  • Funding: not found, speculated to be bootstrapped.
  • Pricing: Free; monthly $14-19; annual $97-140; lifetime $490 ('Founding Class #2').
  • Community: Discord of about 1,580 people; a Chess.com club; YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: the Reddit launch post (before it was deleted) -> Sadistic Tushi's short video amplified it a second time -> reposted in TikTok/IG/FB chess groups/WhatsApp groups. The first few thousand users came mainly from this video, not SEO.
  • Where new users come from: (1) SEO, with the founder claiming 0 to 5M impressions and lots of tool pages (Elo calculator, analysis board, FEN, chess clock); the profile data shows search at 41.6% and direct at 58.4%; (2) return visits: strong retention, pulled back by daily streaks / training plans; (3) Discord + Chess.com club + an Instagram content account.
  • The selling point is 'the game review Chess.com wants a membership for is free here', which naturally gets recommended in chess communities.
  • Speculation: shareable review reports and the leaderboard both help word of mouth; AI referrals (Claude/Gemini) are starting to appear but are very small.

Revenue and scale clues

  • TrustMRR (2026-10): MRR $7.6K, $18.3K over the last 30 days (including lifetime orders), cumulative $72.8K, 592 active subscriptions, about 900K monthly visitors.
  • 100K+ unique visitors in the first week (2025-03/04); Medium claims 5M search impressions.
  • Checkout visits 30K in September.
