onboard.windsor.ai is the sign-up and onboarding backend of Windsor.ai. Windsor.ai was founded in Zurich in 2017 by Niklas Kolster, a former Accenture and eBay data scientist, working alone. It began as marketing attribution software sold to large Swiss companies (Betty Bossi, Schroders, lastminute.com, AMAG) through booked demos. In 2020 it became a self-serve "marketing data connector": free trial, public pricing, a Google Data Studio template gallery, positioned as a "cheaper Supermetrics". The onboard subdomain for self-serve onboarding launched in 2021, followed by mass-generated programmatic pages like "How to connect X to Power BI / Looker Studio / Google Sheets" to capture long-tail search. At the end of 2025 it launched Windsor MCP, generated more pages on "how to connect X to Claude / ChatGPT" and got into the Claude connector directory, which pushed AI referrals to 11% of this subdomain's traffic. It reports 19,000+ customers and was acquired by Belgium's team.blue in 2026-01.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: marketing/analytics/e-commerce data connectors, 350+ data sources (Facebook, Google Ads, Reddit Ads, Shopify, HubSpot, etc.) synced to Google Sheets, Looker Studio, Power BI, BigQuery and Snowflake, and, via MCP, to AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot and Manus; also multi-touch attribution. onboard.windsor.ai is the entry point for signing up, choosing data sources, authorizing and getting an API key.
  • Company: Windsor.ai, Zurich, founded in 2017; founder and CEO Niklas Kolster (ran Scandinavia's first VoIP company in 2003, then spent over ten years in data science at Accenture and eBay); CTO Octavian R. Corlade, COO Matthias Kraaz (https://windsor.ai/about-us/). Acquired by team.blue on 2026-01-20, price undisclosed (https://ai.miraheze.org/wiki/WindsorAI, third-party wiki).
  • Pricing: free $0/month; from $19/month ($228 billed annually); several tiers including $99/month; 30-day free trial (https://windsor.ai/pricing/).
  • Launch: windsor.ai domain registered 2017-12-16 (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot of the onboard subdomain 2020-09-30; "Data onboarding" appeared in the main site navigation in 2021-06.
  • Traffic (profile record): Brazil 17%, United States 9%, India 9%; direct 55%, referral 27%, AI referrals 11%; brand keywords 55%, non-brand search 0 (the subdomain does not carry SEO; SEO lives on the main site).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Programmatic SEO: one page per "data source × destination" combination (how to connect X to Power BI/Sheets/Looker Studio/Claude), started in 2021 and now about 2,900 pages; the subdomain itself has no non-brand search, and traffic flows in from these main-site pages and brand searches.
  • AI channel: Windsor MCP is in the Claude connector directory and the ChatGPT apps, with about 590 "connect to AI assistants" pages; 11% of the onboard subdomain's traffic comes from AI referrals.
  • A shift from enterprise demo sales (2018–2019) to a free tier + self-serve subscriptions from $19 (2020), competing on low price against Supermetrics (a portermetrics review is titled "self-described cheaper Supermetrics alternative", summary only).
  • Open-source SDKs (GitHub windsor-ai/RedditAdsWindsorApi, windsoraiR, a PyPI package) send developers to onboard to get an API key.

Speculation:

  • The partner connector gallery and template gallery for Looker Studio (formerly Google Data Studio) were probably the most important upstream traffic in 2020–2023 (many users in Brazil and India, matching the Looker Studio user profile), but this time the Google connector gallery page was not directly found.
  • The hubspot.com and shopify.com referrals may come from app marketplace listings.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 19,000+ customers, 350+ data sources and 500 million data points processed per day; 82 Trustpilot reviews (summary only).
  • Acquired by team.blue in 2026-01, price undisclosed; no public revenue figures.

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site written from scratch. The founder and 19,000 customers come from the official About page; the product pivot timeline comes from original Wayback snapshots, so credible; the acquisition comes only from a third-party wiki, and no official press release was found.
  • Gaps: where the first self-serve users came from (Product Hunt / AppSumo, etc.) was not found; the contribution of the Looker Studio connector gallery is not quantified; revenue unknown.
