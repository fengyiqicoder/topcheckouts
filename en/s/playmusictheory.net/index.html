Basics

  • Product: play_music_theory, a visual instrument. The browser version at playmusictheory.net/play is free to play; the iOS app costs $1.99, with an in-app purchase "the instrument" at $4.99; the Android version is on Google Play (APKPure shows paid at $1.99).
  • Creator: Tala Rae Schlossberg, an independent artist; the app developer name is also her; her work has been shown in Paris and Beijing and projected on the Manhattan Bridge (self-described on her author page).
  • Scale: App Store 4.3 rating, 67 ratings, #2 on the US paid music chart; Instagram @play_music_theory has 84.5K followers (a search snippet in mid-last month still said 73K).
  • Traffic (our data): direct 46.3%, social 38.3% (X, Facebook, YouTube), search 9.3% (brand keywords 55.6%); referrals from sheezy.art (an illustrator community) 1.05%, vk.ru 1.02%, threads.com 0.91%. United States 43.9%, Brazil 6%, South Korea 4.6%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The work is the spread: users screen-record the "draw -> press play -> sound" process and post it on Reels, Bilibili and X, with the URL shown right in the video (the Bilibili title is just the URL); referrals include the illustrator community sheezy.art, vk.ru and threads.com.
  • The official Instagram encourages users to share their work tagging @play_music_theory, and the account reposts users' work.
  • Social is 38% of sources and search is mostly brand keywords, meaning people watched a video and then searched the name or clicked a link.

Speculation:

  • The initial spark probably came from the creator's own art circle and Instagram (she has some name recognition), then was passed on by the anime / illustrator community; the creator's own launch post was not seen.
  • The free web version handles spreading and the paid app ($1.99 + $4.99) handles monetization; people who get hooked on the web version go download the app.
  • Imitation and guide sites show search demand was large during the burst and may have diverted some search traffic.

Revenue and scale clues

  • App is #2 on the US paid music chart with 67 ratings; if it sold 20K-50K copies over a bit more than a month at $1.99, plus some $4.99 in-app purchases, revenue would be about $50K-$150K (pure speculation).
  • Instagram grew from 73K to 84.5K followers within a month.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there is no earlier report in notes. Search results were flooded with same-name music theory sites like musictheory.net; evidence came mainly from the official site, the creator's page, App Store version history, RDAP, Wayback, Bilibili and Instagram pages.
  • Launch timing (app 8/20, domain 8/23) and burst timing (9/12-9/14) are credible.
  • Gaps: who posted the first viral video and how many views it got; the X trending page and the Naver blog would not open, so only snippets were seen; no data on downloads and revenue.
