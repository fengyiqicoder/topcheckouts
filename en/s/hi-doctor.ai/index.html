Basics

  • Product: European online consultations + EU e-prescriptions (does not sell medication; the prescription is filled at a pharmacy of the user's choice). Categories: weight loss, sexual health, hair loss, skin. 7 languages (EN/ES/FR/DE/IT/PT/NL), covering the EU-27.
  • Company: HI DOCTOR AI SL (tax ID B23893027); Trustpilot address Calle Lope De Rueda 275, 28009 Madrid; the About page says founded in 2025. Named doctor Dr. Alejandro Infante (registered with the Barcelona medical college). Founders not public.
  • Credentials: LegitScript certified; Stripe payments (including PayPal, Apple Pay, SEPA).
  • Pricing: weight loss €25 per 28 days (follow-up subscription) or €35 one-off; hair loss €15/month (€45 for 90 days) or €35 one-off; sexual health €35; skin €40. Full refund if no prescription is issued.
  • Domain: WHOIS registration 2025-09-22; earliest Wayback snapshot 2026-05-09 (301), first 200 on 2026-08-18.
  • Traffic: search 50.2%, direct 37.3%, email 7.5%; non-brand search about 30%, all multilingual drug-name/prescription long-tail; countries Spain 17%, France 16%, United States 10%; YouTube is the only social source seen.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Programmatic SEO: about 250,000 URLs of multilingual drug-name/symptom/country pages; search is half of traffic and the non-brand keywords are all long-tail.
  • Trust layer: Trustpilot (paid subscription, 4.9 rating), LegitScript, a named doctor, all lowering hesitation about "getting a prescription online."
  • Repeat purchases: weight-loss/hair-loss prescriptions renew on 28-day/90-day subscriptions; "free prescription extension" is mentioned repeatedly in reviews; the 7.5% email channel corresponds to renewal reminders.
  • Low-risk promise: full refund if no prescription is issued.

Speculation:

  • EU mutual recognition of prescriptions (cross-border e-prescriptions) is the core arbitrage: prescribed in country A, filled at a pharmacy in country B, serving people who struggle to get a GLP-1 prescription at home; SEO keywords like "tirzepatide sweden" and "xanax in belgium" confirm this cross-border demand.
  • Rising interest in GLP-1 weight-loss drugs in Europe in 2026 is a tailwind.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Stripe checkout visits: 0 in 2026-07, about 4.7k in 08, about 12.9k in 09 (bucketed estimates).
  • Self-reported "50,000+ patients served" (2026-05); Trustpilot 688 reviews.
  • Order value €15–€40, weight-loss subscription €25 per 28 days. Global rank about 318k. Revenue undisclosed.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results contain almost no coverage of the site; the "Long-tail search" call rests mainly on sitemap size plus traffic mix; medium confidence.
  • Gaps: founders, funding and whether paid ads were bought early all not found; "50,000+ patients" has no third-party corroboration; no Wayback snapshots before 2026-05, so the actual launch can only be inferred as 2025-10~11 (combining domain registration and the Trustpilot claim).
