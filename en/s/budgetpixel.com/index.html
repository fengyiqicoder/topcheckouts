Overview

Budgetpixel (budgetpixel.com): AI image, video and music generator with design studio, one account and one credit balance. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Budgetpixel to a checkout payment page — #468 of the 607 sites we track. That is 65% down from the April–June monthly average of 24,433.

Total website visits that month were about 175,924 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 4.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 60%, search 30%, referrals/email/paid 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 72%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $0 (Free, 300 credits/mo); $8.33/mo or $100/yr (Basic, 11,000 credits/mo); $16.
