In November 2021 someone started ungrandjour.fr as a customizable online cash-gift / gift-list page (weddings, births, birthdays, retirements), and registered the company Un Grand Jour SAS in May 2022. The focus later narrowed to weddings: wedding website + gift list + cash-gift fund + RSVP, all free, with only a 3% + €1 fee on cash gifts. Every couple sends their branded wedding page to a hundred or more guests; guests discover the brand when they pay on the page, and use it for their own wedding next time. Add long-tail blog content such as wedding/birth congratulation templates and a high Trustpilot score earned by actively inviting reviews (4.8, 4,000+ reviews in the last 12 months), and it took off in the French wedding cash-gift niche in 2025–2026. In 2026 the founder used the same approach to build a general-purpose cash-gift platform, Memorable.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: mainly French (with English, German, Spanish and Italian interfaces) all-in-one wedding platform: wedding website, gift list, cash-gift fund (cagnotte), RSVP form, QR code generator; also supports birth, birthday and retirement events.
  • Fees: "On se finance avec une commission de 3% + 1€ sur la cagnotte, tout le reste est inclus 100% gratuitement" (https://www.ungrandjour.com/fr); the English page says cash-gift funds are protected up to €100k (https://www.ungrandjour.com/en/wedding-cash-fund). Payments run through Stripe Connect, and couples must complete identity verification with Stripe (CGV).
  • Company: Un Grand Jour SAS, share capital €1,000, the CGV gives an address in Bondues (near Lille); the French company registry API shows it was incorporated 2022-05-03, current registered address in Paris 8th arrondissement, NAF 62.01Z (software development), registered managing director Georges Vernier (https://help.ungrandjour.com/legal/cgv; recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr, SIREN 913113460).
  • Related: Memorable (memorable.me) "créée en 2026 par le fondateur de ungrandjour.com", operated by Memorable Day SAS.
  • Domain: ungrandjour.com registered 2021-11-05 per RDAP, ungrandjour.fr registered the same day;.fr went live first (snapshots 2021-12 to 2022-04), and from 2023.fr 301-redirects to.com.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): France 80%, Belgium 6%, Germany 4%; direct 72.8%, search 27.2% (brand keywords 89%); social from Facebook Messenger; content search terms such as birth congratulation messages; clearly seasonal (410 two months ago → 4,500 last month).

Where the first users came from: no direct evidence. The early site was a general cash-gift / gift-list tool, so the first users were probably wedding/birth events in the founder's personal circle (speculation). What can be confirmed is that over 80% of Trustpilot reviews fall in the last 12 months, which shows real growth only happened in 2025–2026.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: every wedding page, cash-gift page and RSVP link sits on the ungrandjour.com domain and is sent by couples to guests (the profile shows social source Facebook Messenger and direct visits at 72.8%).
  • Free + commission only on cash gifts: everything except the 3% + €1 is free, lowering the barrier for couples to try it.
  • Review operations: actively invites reviews, Trustpilot 4.8 with 4,800+ reviews, Trustpilot widget on the home page.
  • Long-tail content: blog with wedding/birth/birthday guides and congratulation templates ("modèle texte"); the profile shows content terms such as birth congratulation messages bringing in search traffic.
  • Wedding-blog partnership: La Mariée en Colère introduction (probably sponsored).

Speculation:

  • The core loop is guest → future couple conversion: wedding guests include many peers who are about to get married themselves.
  • Multilingual interfaces (German, Spanish, Italian) suggest it is testing other European markets, but 80% of traffic is currently in France.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue = cash-gift volume × 3% + €1 per transaction. No public volume data; the company registry API returned no financial data.
  • Trustpilot has 4,000+ reviews in the last 12 months; at a review rate of 10%–30%, that implies a few thousand to over ten thousand events per year (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Domain/company founding dates, fee model, Trustpilot data and the Memorable relationship have primary evidence.
  • Gaps: founder identity (whether the registered managing director Georges Vernier is the founder is unverified; the president is a corporate shareholder); the source of the first users and the takeoff event were not found; "Long-tail search" is a launch method inferred from the blog and the profile, with weak evidence. The Pappers page returned 403, so no financial data was obtained. The "launch / reddit / review" search groups returned almost entirely unrelated dictionary pages.
