Overview

Ungrandjour (ungrandjour.com): French platform for wedding websites, gift lists, cash funds and RSVP, plus births/birthdays. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,500 visits went from Ungrandjour to a checkout payment page — #549 of the 607 sites we track. That is 49% down from the April–June monthly average of 8,754.

Total website visits that month were about 24,012 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 18.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 73%, search 27%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 89%, plus shares via Messenger. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: 3% + €1 commission on cagnotte.
