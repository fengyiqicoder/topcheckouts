UAB Madcom, a 17-person company in Kaunas, Lithuania, registered frameandtail.com in May 2026 to run a US gift e-commerce store built on "upload a pet photo → instant AI-style portrait free preview → buy the digital version or a framed print shipped to you", packaged to the outside world as a brand based in "Laguna Beach, California". It took off entirely on Facebook/Instagram ads (paid social is 36%, and most of the rest is the 56% direct return visits from people who saw an ad); its Facebook page grew to about 19K followers and Instagram to 16K in two or three months. To keep feeding the ads with creative, it launched an "unboxing/reaction video for 50 credits" program in which buyers authorize the videos for use in ads - using existing customers' touching reaction videos to buy new customers.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Where the first users came from: Facebook/Instagram paid ads (paid social 36%, and the brand page's "talking about this" is unusually high, consistent with ad engagement). There is no trace of a Product Hunt, Reddit or press launch.

Where new users come from: still Meta ads; ad creative increasingly comes from buyers' reaction videos (50 credits in exchange for a license); repeat purchases as holiday gifts (direct visits 56%).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid acquisition: paid social is the only significant external source.
  • UGC creative loop: buyer → films a delivery reaction video → gets 50 credits (to regenerate/rebuy) → the video goes into ads → new buyers.
  • Free preview with no signup lowers the conversion barrier on the ad landing page; "65% OFF" is a permanent promotion.

Speculation:

  • Madcom is a team that builds ad-driven e-commerce stores/apps and frameandtail is one of its brands; the US gift market was chosen because of its high order value and Meta ad conversion.
  • The Q4 holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas) are the volume peak.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No revenue data. reviews.io has 891 product reviews; at a 1-3% review rate that implies tens of thousands of orders sold (speculation, unverified).
  • The operator has 17 people overall (not only on this one project).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The entity, launch date and the UGC-for-credits program all have original-text evidence, so confidence is high; "took off through Facebook ads" comes from the traffic profile + page engagement data, medium-high.
  • Search found no founder interviews or press coverage; "Tails & Frames" on PH is another company's product.
  • Gaps: ad spend and ROAS, actual average order value, Madcom's other brands, and whether the Laguna Beach address hosts a real business.
