Overview

Ticketshop (ticketshop.eu): Online ticket seller for concerts, theater, festivals and standup shows, mostly in the Baltics and wider Europe. Category on TopCheckouts: Consumer services.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Ticketshop to a checkout payment page — #426 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 42,372 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 20.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 52%, search 36%, referrals/email/paid 7%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 29%, with queries like “ready fest”, “ready fest warsaw”, “little big bratislava”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: EUR 20.00-69.00 one-time per ticket; PLN 129.00 one-time (Naviband); CHF 50.00.
