Growth loop

Evidence:

  • App store search: first listed on the Play Store with keyword titles such as "adult blocker / porn blocker / quit porn" (in 2019 the app title was "BlockerX – adult blocker / porn blocker for Android"), 50K installs by 2019-10, now 5 million+.
  • Content SEO: since 2020 the website has been 30+ languages plus blog posts on quitting porn; it now has about two to three thousand articles, and the non-brand search terms in the profile record include long-tail Japanese ones such as "ポルノ依存症 診断".
  • Product matrix cross-promotion: NoPo, NoPo Gold, BlockerX and BlockerX Pro come from the same company and share the nopomail.com mailbox.
  • Accountability partner feature: users bring in a friend as an accountability partner, a weak form of "Invite to use".

Speculation:

  • Direct visits make up seventy percent of the website, which suggests most visits come from in-app redirects to the web for payment and login (web payment avoids the app store commission).
  • Search results and review sites are mostly "BlockerX review / alternatives" articles written by competitors, which suggests the main acquisition battleground in this category is search and the app stores, not social sharing.
  • Quitting porn is a topic users naturally do not want to share, so word of mouth is weak, hence pure product spread is judged "Mixed".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Downloads: Play 5M+; claims 6 million+ downloads across all products; iOS 8.3K ratings.
  • Prices are on the high side (annual $110–140, lifetime $150–200). Assuming 1% of downloads convert to annual users, annual revenue would be in the millions of dollars (Speculation, not disclosed).
  • The website's marketing line: "92% of BlockerX premium subscribers noted a reduction in porn consumption within 3 months".

Confidence and gaps

  • Founder's name, team size and funding were not found; when it moved from India's FunSwitch to Dubai's Atmana Tech FZCO was not found.
  • Takeoff method is judged "Launch platform" (Google Play), because the Play Store already had 50K installs before the website; the specific takeoff event (a particular recommendation or hit) was not found.
  • This is a new site, written from scratch this time. The search results were almost all irrelevant blocker and proxy reviews; the main evidence came from Wayback archives (funswitch.io, the 2019 Play Store archive), RDAP, sitemaps and App Store pages. The about-us page returns 404.
