An AI tool directory with its domain registered 2023-02 and launched 2023-03 (it claims to have "started in 2022"), starting with only 400+ tools and jumping to 5,000+ in 2023-09. Its core loop is "backlinks for listings": a free submission requires placing a crawlable dang.ai link on your site, or you pay $49 one-time or $30 + $19/month. Thousands of AI tool developers added backlinks to get listed, pushing its Ahrefs DR to 83, so it ranks near the top for a huge number of AI tool names (especially NSFW tools other directories refuse to list, such as dreemy ai, n8ked, undressme ai); non-brand search is about 55%. Basics

  • Product: Dang.ai, an AI tool directory, browsable by category (e.g. 896 text-to-image, 776 video), with paid featured slots, a newsletter, the AI Graveyard and an NSFW section.
  • Pricing: free submission (requires a dang.ai backlink), Basic $49 one-time, Pro $30 setup + $19/month (includes newsletter exposure). Sources: https://dang.ai/pricing; https://launchdirectories.com/directory/dang-ai
  • Team: not stated on the About page; not found. X account @dangdotai registered 2023-02-23, bio "the largest, fastest-growing AI tools directory on the old-school web".
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2023-02-16 (the 2021-11 Wayback snapshot was a previous owner's blank page); Wayback first captured the directory on 2023-03-21.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Backlinks for listings: free submission requires a crawlable dang.ai link on your own site; referral traffic comes from listed tools (falconvid.ai 2.2%, vidclean.net 1.2%, astrocarto.net).
  • High authority: Ahrefs DR 83.
  • Search 55.4%, brand keywords only 33.4%, non-brand mostly NSFW AI tool names.
  • NSFW listings: third-party reviews note that Dang.ai lists both mainstream and NSFW products.
  • Citable content: the AI Graveyard comes with a "citation template" for journalists and researchers.
  • Newsletter 9,100+ subscribers; the Pro paid tier includes newsletter exposure.
  • Many Chinese and English AI directories and "submission guide" sites repost it, creating second-tier backlinks.

Speculation:

  • The first traffic came from "AI tool directory" keywords during the 2023 ChatGPT boom and developers submitting proactively; no PH/Reddit launch found.
  • The 2023-09 jump to 5,000+ may have been a bulk import from other directories or an acquisition (unverified).
  • Revenue comes mainly from the $49 listing fee and $19/month featured slots.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 7,581 tools tracked; if one tenth paid $49, roughly $37K in one-time revenue (rough speculation, no evidence).
  • Newsletter 9,100+ subscribers; X 69 followers.
  • Third-party estimate of about 2K monthly traffic (launchdirectories, clearly too low, low credibility).

Confidence and gaps

  • New entry, written from scratch. Google results were mostly unrelated people and game characters with the same name; relied mainly on the site's About/Pricing/AI Graveyard, Wayback homepage history and X account data.
  • Gaps: owner identity, reason for the 5,000+ jump, and revenue not found. "Started in 2022" conflicts with the 2023-02 domain registration; it may have started on another domain (not found).
