Basics

  • Product: online consultation + compounded peptide drugs. The user picks a "goal" → a licensed physician at the telehealth partner evaluates and prescribes → a 503A compounding pharmacy (Precision Pharma Corp in Jupiter, FL, or the partner network) fills it, shipped cold-chain as a 28-day supply on a monthly subscription. The site is bilingual (English / Spanish) and supports HSA / FSA.
  • Company: Avellum Health, LLC (a Florida LLC), legal address 2750 Taylor Ave, Suite A-84, Orlando, FL 32806. The terms state that the payee for the drugs is not Avellum but the telehealth partner / pharmacy ("more than one company collects money in this flow").
  • Sister product: Valdura (valdura.health, registered 2026-06-11), an AI health assistant that reads blood-test reports plus a "Longevity Panel" blood test kit; the FAQ calls it "the same family of companies", with content reviewed by "a physician with 30 years of practice" (unnamed).
  • Channel partnership: the FAQ explicitly lists an "Amway Partner Store Discount": enter via amway.com/en_US/avellum-health and link an Amway account, and prices are set by Amway account tier; the footer states "Amway is not a sponsor, endorser or co-provider".
  • Founders, team, funding: not found.

Growth loop

  • Evidence (second-pass check): "amway avellum peptides" shows up among brand search queries (our channel data), showing people from the Amway circle really do arrive searching that term; Instagram has only about 658 followers and there are no creators or press, so a social-media takeoff can be ruled out.
  • Evidence: Amway entry point + account-tier pricing (member price varies with Amway status); Avellum and Valdura give each other $10 discounts; live lectures inside the app to bring in new users.
  • Speculation: Amway distributors (IBOs) who recommend external goods through the Partner Store usually earn commissions or points, so distributors actively pitch "peptide anti-aging" to their downlines and acquaintances; this is classic MLM-style person-to-person spread. Peptides (GLP-1 class, BPC-157, etc.) are a hot topic in 2025–26, providing a tailwind.

Revenue and scale clues
