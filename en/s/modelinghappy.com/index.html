MODELING HAPPY is the personal blog of a Japanese 3DCG learner: in 2014 the owner (pen name photsohop777), while studying Maya at a CG school, wrote up how to use each tool as study notes (constraints, deformers, UV unwrapping, linear workflow…). Japanese long-tail queries of the "Maya ○○ 使い方" (how to use X) type had almost no competition, so Google search traffic built up slowly. The owner later quit a day job at 27 to become a CG designer and turned the blog into a "daily-updated 3DCG news site," posting frequent write-ups of Blender/Maya add-ons and free assets (2,300+ posts in the Blender category, roughly 5,000–6,000 across the site), distributed via X (@happy_modeling) and Hatena Bookmark; revenue comes from Google AdSense and paid articles on note.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: Japanese-language 3DCG news blog (WordPress): Blender/Maya add-on write-ups, free assets, software how-to tutorials, hardware recommendations; a "有料記事" (paid articles) category plus paid articles/magazine on note ("modelinghappy マガジン", 28 articles for ¥3,000; single articles ¥100–¥999).
  • Who built it: a solo blogger, signed photsohop777 in 2014; a note article describes quitting a day job at 27 ("脱サラ") to attend CG school, then receiving offers from 4 CG companies and switching careers to CG designer (the article is paid; only the title was seen). Real name not found.
  • Launch date: domain registered 2014-09-07 (Japan's Onamae.com); first Wayback capture 2014-12-26, already publishing daily Maya tutorial notes; footer reads "2014–2026."
  • Scale: homepage paginates to 270 pages; 6 post sitemaps (the first with 1,000 entries), an estimated 5,000–6,000 posts; Blender category 2,317 posts.
  • Monetization: Google AdSense on the pages (ca-pub-5283129756952551); paid articles on note.
  • Traffic (profile): direct 47.4%, non-brand search 29.5% (proximity snap, blender editpoly, ノーマルマップ, blender アドオン), social 9.5% (X, YouTube, Hatena Bookmark), referrals raindrop.io 1.2%; Japan 79%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: a large volume of "tool/add-on name + 使い方" posts; non-brand search is about 30% (add-on names like proximity snap and blender editpoly).
  • High-frequency updates + X distribution: daily add-on and asset news; X, Hatena Bookmark and YouTube bring about 10% social traffic; 47% direct suggests a large base of daily returning readers.
  • Monetization: AdSense + paid articles on note.

Speculation:

  • Japanese Blender users grew rapidly after Blender 2.8 in 2019, and the owner shifted focus from Maya to Blender to ride that wave (Speculation; timing of the shift unverified).
  • Japanese-language write-ups of overseas add-ons (Blender Market/Gumroad) fill a language gap, creating a steady pipeline of topics: "new overseas add-on → first Japanese write-up."

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue data. AdSense + paid articles on note (single articles ¥100–¥999, magazine ¥3,000); monthly revenue presumably in the tens to hundreds of thousands of yen (Speculation).
  • About 5,000–6,000 posts (estimated from sitemaps).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Search results had almost no third-party coverage; evidence comes from the site itself, its sitemaps, page source (AdSense), the note profile, the Wayback snapshot of 2014-12 and RDAP.
  • The owner's identity, the year of the career change, when traffic took off and revenue all not found; the 2015 takeoff year is a Speculation.
