Basics

  • Product: TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels viral trends, outlier video and account discovery (library of 14.7M+ outlier videos, 500K+ hashtags), Meta ad competitor tracking, content brief generation; pay-per-call API (dev.virlo.ai, $0.05–1.50 per call), MCP Server, Agent plugin (GitHub Virlo-AI/Virlo-Agent-Plugin).
  • Team: co-founders Nicolas Mauro (growth and GTM, HN account nmauro1) and Deniz Sancar (data and engineering, built the scraping pipeline). Legal entity Red Lab, LLC, headquartered in the US (city not stated). Describes itself as a "small, founder-led team".
  • Funding: not mentioned on the site, not found.
  • The early site linked to the marketing agency trailblazermktg.com (relationship unconfirmed).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Founder-owned audience: co-founder Deniz Sancar's YouTube channel appears in the early testimonials; the cold-start audience was the faceless-shorts money-making crowd.
  • Affiliate commissions: the Tolt affiliate program (virlo.tolt.io) gets YouTube tutorial and review creators to put ref links in descriptions; "Virlo AI Tutorial" videos have appeared continuously since 2025-09.
  • Community conversion: "join Discord for a discount" moves visitors into Discord, which offers 24-hour support.
  • Directories and content SEO: AI directories like toolify and nextaitool (main referral sources in the profile) + the Medium publication + feature landing pages (/tiktok-analytics, /features/outliers); non-brand keywords include "tiktok creative center" and "whop".
  • Agent distribution: in 2026 the API is recommended by Agents like OpenClaw (per the founder), and it shipped an MCP server and Agent plugin.
  • Display ads are about 11% of traffic (profile), so there is paid acquisition.
  • Speculation: money-making communities such as Whop clipping bounties recommend Virlo as a tool; the near-zero August checkout visits may correspond to the B2B redesign and a checkout page switch.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2025-05: "6,500+ creators" (including free users); 2026-10: "1,700+ teams" (metric changed after the B2B pivot).
  • Current pricing from $49/$199/$499 per month, plus prepaid pay-per-call API.
  • Revenue not disclosed.

Confidence and gaps

  • Founder names, roles and legal entity come from the site's About page, credible; "Deniz Sancar's YouTube was the cold-start audience" rests on the testimonial account sharing the co-founder's name, and the YouTube channel's subscriber count and content were not captured, so it is a strong speculation.
