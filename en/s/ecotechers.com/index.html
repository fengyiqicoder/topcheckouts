Basics

  • Product: a Spanish-language startup ecosystem news newsletter (Substack), with a free weekly edition + Pro paid in-depth pieces (at least two exclusive/in-depth pieces a week, special editions for breaking news, plus a podcast and Substack chat).
  • Pricing: free; Pro €5/month or €52/year (since 2025-04), with a two-week free trial and 30% off for group subscriptions; the free edition is supported by sponsors.
  • Founder: Jesús Martínez González, a Madrid journalist with nearly 20 years in the field, writing on startups and VC since 2013; has written for El Español, Expansión, Forbes (freelance) and La Información (Henneo group, until 2025); known for exclusive reports such as the Glovo, Job&Talent and Wallapop funding rounds and the Holded acquisition. Source: https://www.ecotechers.com/about
  • Domain: ecotechers.com registered 2020-03-06; in 2022-01 Wayback still shows a Hostalia parking page, and from 2024-01 it points to Substack. The earliest issue in the Substack archive is "Ecotechers #51" (2020-06-14), with the URL slug 7990982_ecotechers-51, which looks like an import from Revue (Speculation).
  • Traffic profile: Spain about 97%; direct 42%, organic search 43% (brand keywords 68%); non-brand terms are funding queries such as "cala ai cuánto ha levantado" and "valoración vicio ronda vc"; social from X and LinkedIn.

Where the first 1,000 users came from: not stated directly. What the evidence shows is that the founder had spent the previous ten years covering startups at mainstream business media, both his X (@jesusmargon) and LinkedIn bios link to the Ecotechers subscription, and fellow Spanish startup journalists (such as El Español reporter Fernando Pastor on LinkedIn) have reshared his announcements. So the first readers were basically his own journalist contacts and social followers (Speculation, no figures).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Exclusive news → reshared inside the industry / cited by peers → new subscriptions: Pro pieces focus on undisclosed funding, valuation and acquisition details, found by people searching "how much did company X raise" (the non-brand search terms are exactly this kind).
  • Tiering of free weekly + paid in-depth: the free edition keeps reach and sells sponsorships, the paid edition monetizes.
  • The founder's social accounts: his X bio reads "Fundador y editor de @ecotechers… Suscríbete", and every news post drives traffic.

Speculation:

  • Substack's in-app recommendations (Recommendations/Notes) contribute part of the new subscriptions; in his review the founder says Notes "fall short" but he still uses them.
  • Paid conversion is about 2.6%, and growth still mainly depends on the founder's personal credibility and the density of exclusive news.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2025-05: 130+ paid (if 60% are annual, about €6–7k annualized), plus sponsorship income (slots booked solid); the author himself says "unit economics are challenging".
  • 2026-01: 5,000+ subscribers; 2026-10: 6,000+ subscribers.

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site written from scratch. The Pro launch date and first-month data come from the author's own posts, so credible; the weekly's start time is counted back from issue numbers, and #1–#50 are not in the Substack archive and may be on Revue.
  • Gaps: Revue-era data, the founder's X follower count, the latest number of paid subscribers. Most search results were irrelevant, and the work relied mainly on the Substack archive API, the About page and the author's posts.
