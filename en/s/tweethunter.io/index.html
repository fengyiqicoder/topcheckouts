In 2021 the "build in public" and thread-writing creator culture on Twitter was hot, and a few founders who were themselves indie developers on Twitter with hundreds to tens of thousands of followers built a tool for "if you don't know what to post, come here for viral tweet inspiration". They first pulled people in through an invite-only beta inside their own Twitter circle, took #2 of the day on Product Hunt in 2021-07, and quickly added AI tweet writing, scheduling, auto retweets and analytics, turning it into an "all-in-one Twitter growth kit". Growth came from the founders demonstrating follower growth on Twitter themselves, a large set of free mini tools (an AI tweet generator, the TweetPik tweet screenshot tool, "how much is my X account worth", a shadowban checker and more) that capture long-tail search and spread the brand, and the content users post with it spreading on Twitter. It was later acquired by lempire, the parent company of lemlist, and is sold alongside Taplio, the LinkedIn version.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: X (Twitter) growth tool: a library of 3 million viral tweets, AI tweet writing/rewriting/threads, scheduled posting, auto retweets and auto DMs, analytics, CRM, a browser sidebar extension, plus a pile of free tools. Pricing: 7-day free trial, $49–$99/month.
  • Company: now owned by lempire (parent of lemlist, a bootstrapped company led by Guillaume Moubeche, about 90 people); the WHOIS registrant organization is lempire, and lempire's website product list includes Tweet Hunter and Taplio.
  • Founders: the site FAQ says the founders have "anywhere from 100 to 50K followers"; an HN hiring post links to a tweet by @tibo_maker. From memory, unverified: the founders are Tibo Louis-Lucas and Thomas Bustos, who also built Taplio.
  • Scale: the site claims "5,644+ X creators trust it" (2026), and said "2,634+ Twitter pros" in 2022-09.
  • Domain: registered 2021-05-12, first Wayback capture 2021-05-15.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Launch inside the circle: invite-only beta, then Reddit SideProject, then #2 of the day on Product Hunt, all in indie developer circles.
  • Free mini tools capturing the long tail: tweet generator, TweetPik, shadowban checker, X video downloader, "how much is my account worth" and others; in our data the non-brand keywords include "fake tweet maker", "twitter analytics" and "twitter stats", with new-user SEO around 20%.
  • Content marketing: pieces that ride hot topics, such as the Twitter algorithm analysis, tweet prediction and the Maker Grant, were reposted to HN repeatedly.
  • Users can publicly share their stats page (our channel profile classifies this as viral).
  • There are Google ads records (Ads Transparency shows several advertiser accounts pointing to this domain).

Speculation:

  • The founders themselves demonstrating "grow followers with the tool" on Twitter, with their followers as potential users, is probably the main acquisition method (the FAQ mentions the founders all have their own audiences, but no specific posts were seen).
  • After the lempire acquisition, it can cross-sell through lemlist's email lists and Taplio.
  • The user base later widened to crypto project teams and OnlyFans promoters (channel profile); the 8.9% of traffic from Uganda may come from this kind of outsourced account operation.

Revenue and scale clues

  • A public goal of "getting to $10M ARR" in 2023-06 suggests ARR was then in the millions of dollars (speculation).
  • "Sold for $1.4 million" rests on a single newsletter headline, unverified.
  • Site user count: 2,634+ (2022-09) to 5,644+ (2026).
  • Price $49–$99/month.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch date, Product Hunt launch, product evolution and lempire ownership have evidence.
  • The founders' names, acquisition date and price have only indirect evidence or are from memory; X, IndieHackers and Reddit could not be opened.
  • Gaps: acquisition date and price, actual ARR figures, the specific posts behind the first 1,000 users.
