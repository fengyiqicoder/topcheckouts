Overview

Tweethunter (tweethunter.io): AI X/Twitter growth tool: content ideas, scheduling, auto-engagement, analytics, outreach. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Tweethunter to a checkout payment page — #277 of the 607 sites we track. That is 64% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 83,668 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 15.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 51%, direct 37%, social 7%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 67%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
