Basics

  • Product: OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible LLM router/relay; one key calls Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, aimed at Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, DeepSeek Harness; claims >99% prompt cache hit rate, no charge for failed requests, new models listed "within hours". Also a Chinese site teamorouter.cn and a "Teamo client for one-click Codex/CC setup".
  • Who built it: Teamo HK Limited (site footer © 2026); WHOIS registrant country CN, registered via Tencent Cloud. X account @teamo_agent lists its location as Delaware, USA. Founder not found. GitHub user sophiaashi maintained a teamorouter-resources repo (1 star, containing Medium article drafts, looks like SEO/backlink material made by a team member).
  • Pricing: prepaid credits (from $5, tiers $10/$20/$50/$200), billed per token, discounts vary by model and time "as low as 90% off"; limited-time price subsidies; new users "get some trial credit by default" (a Russian review from 2026-10 says signup balance was $0; doubtful, pending the Russian review).
  • Domain: teamorouter.com registered 2026-05-15 (Verisign RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2026-07-28.
  • Traffic (profile data): China 45.1%, Singapore 9.6%, United States 7.3%; direct 51%, search 36.7%, paid 7.3%; brand keywords 73.1%, non-brand terms include "openrouter" and new model names; referrals from floatai.cn 5.9%, askmanyai.cn 5.8%, mirror domain teamorouter.org 5.8%; social from X, YouTube, Telegram.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Invite cashback: the site has an "invite cashback" entry, and Wayback captured multiple?i= invite-code links, showing users post invite links elsewhere.
  • Price hooks: 50 free DeepSeek V4 Flash calls a day, limited-time 99.9% off on DeepSeek/GLM, relayed by the Russian roundup and X users.
  • Directory/review listings: gapis.money, ccnavx, veridrop; referral traffic from floatai.cn and askmanyai.cn about 6% each.
  • Paid search: Google Ads on the "relay station" keyword (landing link on 2026-10-07); profile paid traffic 7.3%.
  • New-model content: blog posts tracking GPT-6 Astra, DeepSeek V4 and other hot releases to capture long-tail searches for new model names.

Speculation:

  • The real backdrop for takeoff is Claude/OpenAI being unavailable in China plus the 2026 surge in Claude Code and Codex usage; the whole relay category is feeding on that demand (external event tailwind). TeamoRouter's own actions alone cannot explain the "100K+ teams" claim (a self-reported figure, unverified).
  • Mirror domains teamorouter.org and.cn handle domestic access and blocking.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site claims 100K+ teams and 139B+ tokens routed per day (unverified). At relay prices of 10-20% of list, 139 billion tokens a day even at an average $0.3 per million is roughly $40K/day in volume (rough estimate, speculation).
  • Revenue model: margin on prepaid credits; X has only 253 followers, a very small community.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Most search results were irrelevant router/launch-tracking pages; useful evidence from the site, the GitHub issue, Wayback CDX (invite codes and Google Ads landing links), RDAP, fxtwitter.
  • Founder, team size, and real revenue not found; "100K+" is self-reported. No direct evidence of the launch channel; only inferred from directory listing dates.
