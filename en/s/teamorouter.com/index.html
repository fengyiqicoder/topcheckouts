Overview

Teamorouter (teamorouter.com): Credit-based LLM router exposing one OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible endpoint for Claude, GPT, Gemini at discounted token prices. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Teamorouter to a checkout payment page — #412 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 70,066 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 12.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 51%, search 37%, referrals/email/paid 7%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 73%, plus shares via Telegram. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $10/$20/$50/$200 credit top-ups (min $5); $0.53 input; $3.18 output per 1M tok.
