Basics

  • Product: CherryBox C6 Plus Android TV box, 4K, dual-band Wi-Fi, claims 1,000+ free channels (the on-site channel directory lists 1,576 channels in 25 categories), "$0/month CherryBox fee", with a calculator claiming $1,491 saved in year one (vs. a $150/month TV bill). Legality of the channel sources is questionable (copyright grey area).
  • Pricing: $289 (homepage, 2026-10); $309 in the 2026-05/06 videos; list price $399; free US shipping, 60-day refund, 365-day hardware warranty, Stripe checkout.
  • Seller: no company name or address on the site. The first demo short video on 2026-05-26 was posted on a channel called "Legends of the Orient"; a "CherryboxTV" channel was created afterwards. Likely a white-label direct seller from an Asian supply chain (speculation).
  • Domain: RDAP shows a registration date of 2026-05-18 (.ai), but the same site's /news/feed contains smart-ring articles from 2025-01 to 2025-04, and Google indexed a "Nova Smart Ring" guide page from 2025-06 — so the domain or site existed in 2025 as an AI smart-ring content site and was repurposed around 2026-05 (possibly an expired re-registration or a sale; unverified). No Wayback snapshots.
  • Traffic profile: United States 100%; direct 61.5%, search 28.9%, paid search 5.8%; brand keywords 73% (cherrybox, cherry tv); the roughly 17% non-brand keywords are old-content terms like "smartring monthly fee study", "oura ring alternatives", "chatbox". 2026-08 at 0 → 2026-09 about 6,633.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid search 5.8% (traffic profile).
  • The landing page has a "savings calculator", a searchable channel directory, and a comparison table against monthly-fee TV — a typical direct-response conversion page.
  • Self-made YouTube demo/setup videos, used as ad creative and to ease buyer doubts.

Speculation:

  • The main driver is Meta/YouTube feed ads with brand-keyword search catching the demand (brand keywords at 73% suggests users see the ad elsewhere and then search "cherrybox").
  • No referral or sharing mechanism; a pure paid-traffic single-product store. The copyright grey area may limit the lifespan of ad accounts.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Unit price $289; at roughly 6,600 monthly visits and a typical e-commerce conversion rate of 1–2%, a rough estimate is a few dozen to a hundred-plus units per month, around $20–40K monthly revenue (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Product, pricing and video dates have direct evidence; the contradiction between the domain's 2025 smart-ring content and the RDAP registration date of 2026-05 remains unresolved.
  • Gaps: the seller entity, ad creatives, real sales volume. Most of the given search results were generic AI-tool articles; the technical report PDF failed to download, so only the summary was used.
