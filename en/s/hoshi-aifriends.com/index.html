HoShi (also called Hoshi-Bond / Bondmate internally) is an AI companion chat app from the Hong Kong company Huayun Innovation & Idea Technology Limited. The domain was registered in September 2025, and the app pitches role-play chat with characters that "remember you", aimed at US Android users. The website is just a landing page and web version that catches brand searches, while new users mainly come from Google Play; it monetizes with in-app ads through ad networks such as Bigo, plus a Premium subscription that removes ads, lengthens replies and strengthens memory. An invite-link subdomain went live in April 2026, showing it is working on invite-based acquisition. No direct evidence was found for the specific acquisition channel (paid ads or organic app store traffic); from "a China-background team going overseas + ad monetization + almost no social traffic", it is inferred to rely mainly on app store promotion and store search.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI character chat where you can discover characters, create characters, set your own persona (Persona), and see community content; the web version is a PWA written in Nuxt (www.hoshi-aifriends.com/home) supporting 9 languages (German, English, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese). Premium has longer replies, better memory and no ads (our records cite user reviews), price Not found.
  • Company: Huayun Innovation & Idea Technology Limited, address Room 509, 5/F The Cloud, 111 Tung Chau Street, Tai Kok Tsui, Kowloon, Hong Kong; the contact phone is a Mexican number (+52 81).
  • Monetization: app-ads.txt is mainly a direct account with bigo.sg (Bigo Ads, Singapore) plus a dozen or so ad resellers, showing programmatic ads inside the app.
  • Community: the web code contains 3 Discord invites; of these, the "Bondmate AI" server was created on 2026-07-27 and currently has only 7 members; discord.gg/hoshi is a French server with the same name (339 members) and may be unrelated to this product.
  • Traffic (our records): United States 97.3%; direct 72.8%, search 20.4%, brand keywords 93.2%, with search terms mostly existing users looking for the Discord and error fixes (hoshi ai friends discord, hoshi app not working); social is only YouTube.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Invite links: since 2026-04 there is a separate invite landing page subdomain; users share the link and the recipient is redirected to download after opening it.
  • Ad monetization: app-ads.txt shows integration with Bigo Ads and others; free users watch ads and heavy users pay for Premium.
  • Catching brand search: 93.2% of the site's traffic is brand keywords, with existing users looking for the Discord and error fixes.

Speculation:

  • The main acquisition is likely search and recommendations inside Google Play ("AI friend", "AI companion" keywords) plus paid ads on Bigo, Meta, TikTok and similar; this is a common playbook for China-background overseas AI companion apps, with no direct evidence on this site.
  • A web version in 9 languages suggests plans for multiple countries, but 97% of current traffic is in the US.
  • First users: possibly from paid-ad tests in the early days after the store listing, no evidence.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No download or revenue data. The Google Play page was not found (all package names tried returned 404).
  • The Discord community is tiny (7 members), showing community operations are not a growth method.
