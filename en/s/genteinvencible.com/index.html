Basics

  • Product: a Spanish-language paid email subscription, "Gente Invencible" — one "good idea" email per day; the first months break down books (sales and persuasion, Atomic Habits, How to Win Friends and Influence People, The Millionaire Fastlane, Cialdini's Pre-suasión, creativity), with a weekly audio summary; also a members area for "extras, livestreams and talks" (2026 Wayback captured /products/extras-directos-y-charlas).
  • Pricing: the 2021-07 sales letter says "€120 for 6 months (tax included), renew voluntarily when it ends", "no Black Friday"; the 2025 Kajabi checkout page shows €180 (tax included); 27 days free first (2024 /about-dinero).
  • Who is behind it: Álvaro Sánchez, from Grado, Asturias; 37 in 2021, father of twins (born 2019-11-22); describes his main business as "local SEO", and also does a passive house (Casa ESE), helps others monetize Instagram audiences, and membership products; earlier projects include La Bella Solera Co. (/about, /about-dinero, 2019 homepage).
  • Launch: domain registered 2016-01-19 (RDAP); first Wayback capture 2016-10, when it was a personal growth blog positioned as "Excelencia personal / Vida consciente", offering a mini-course for email sign-ups.
  • Platform: 2016–2020 WordPress (Genesis theme) → 2021-04 moved to Kajabi (/blog, /offers/…/checkout) → 2026 front end became a single-page app (site fetch returns only the title).
  • Traffic (profile data): Costa Rica 66.7%, Spain 33.3% (small sample, proportions may be distorted); direct 44.5%, search 41.6% (non-brand about 42%: sr lobo pulp fiction, señor lobo, como ser millonario, como me hago millonario).

Where the first users came from: readers and the email list built by the blog in 2016–2020 (mini-course for email), plus visitors from long-tail search posts; when the paid email launched in 2021, those readers were the conversion target (the sales letter states that "the first piece of content is the very email sequence I use to sell this subscription").

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Long-tail search to old blog posts (señor lobo, como ser millonario) → homepage/about → 27-day free trial → paid (/about-dinero, profile keywords).
  • The emails themselves are the sales example: the "sales and persuasion" module teaches readers the email sequence he uses to sell the subscription.
  • Stylized copy (self-deprecating, "majadero") forms the personal brand.

Speculation:

  • Paying readers are mainly from Spain's entrepreneurship/marketing scene; word-of-mouth referrals ("if someone who loves you recommended this" — implied by homepage copy) are the second source.
  • Since he runs a local SEO business, the content site's search ranking is deliberate.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscription price €120 per half-year (2021) → €180 (2025).
  • Says "the tax office is now very happy with this joke", indicating real income; subscriber count not disclosed.
  • Site traffic is very small (no scale figure in the profile data; country distribution shows only two countries, so monthly visits are presumably very low).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results were almost all unrelated; relied mainly on Wayback archives (multiple versions from 2016, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2024, 2025 of the homepage, About, sales letter and checkout page) and RDAP. The current site is a single-page app, so a direct fetch returns only the title.
  • Gaps: paid subscriber count, revenue, the reason for the unusually high Costa Rica traffic share.
