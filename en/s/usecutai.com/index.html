Before the official launch (2026-06) it got Whitelist Wealth, a YouTube channel that teaches people to make money with UGC, to publish a full tutorial. The website displays endorsements from million-follower TikTok/IG creators, and the team hands out?ref= tracking links and personal discount codes (such as LAURENH for a free first month) to many UGC creators, who then post #ad videos. Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Creator affiliate program:?ref=creator-name tracking links plus personal discount codes; creators post #ad videos to promote it.
  • A YouTube "make money with UGC" teaching channel produced full tutorials, starting before launch.
  • The website showcases 3 creators with roughly 1.2M combined followers to drive conversion.
  • Paid social: 50.5% of channel traffic is paid social (Instagram ads); the website has a TikTok Pixel installed.
  • Organic search is almost entirely brand keywords (cutai, usecutai), catching people who search again after being seeded by ads and creators.
  • Speculation:
  • The target users are themselves UGC creators, so "creators recommending to creators" closes the loop. Affiliate payouts mean every paying user can become a promoter.
  • Exports are watermark-free, so the product's output carries no brand exposure of its own.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 1,341 App Store ratings (2 months after launch).
  • 21,490 checkout visits last month Roughly estimated at $19.99–89.99/mo, monthly revenue could be in the tens of thousands of dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Founder and funding still not found. No records on Reddit, PH or HN; the Reddit and TikTok search results were all noise.
  • For the YouTube channel and Instagram posts we only got titles and summaries; view counts, follower counts and whether sponsorship was disclosed are not visible.
  • Second-pass recheck: growth engine changed from "Creators or community" to "Referral rewards or affiliates", and pure product spread from "Mixed" to "No". Basis: the?ref= creator affiliate links, personal discount codes, #ad posts, and the YouTube tutorials 2 months before launch (which explain the checkout traffic already present three months ago).
