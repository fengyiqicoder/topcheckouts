Basics

  • Product: a one-page website builder (personal homepages, landing pages, forms, bio link pages).
  • Founder: AJ (@ajlkn, author of HTML5 UP / Pixelarity), a solo developer for a long time and later about 2 people; US (the Nashville-area claim is unverified, the about page does not say).
  • Funding: bootstrapped for the first 5 years; took a small round in early 2021 (AJ says 'mainly for the connections and experience'; the IH AMA title is 'after 2.5M sites, 1M ARR and a funding round'); investors/amount not found.
  • Pricing: free (up to 3 sites, with the Carrd badge,.carrd.co subdomain); Pro billed annually at $9 / $19 / $49 ($19/year early on).

First 1,000 users: Evidence: the Twitter followers AJ built up with HTML5 UP + the Product Hunt launch.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The 'Made with Carrd' footer on free sites + the branded.carrd.co subdomain: visitors see it -> click -> start building right away with no signup (starterstory and superframeworks both list it as a core viral element).
  • Trying it without signing up lowers the barrier to a first try -> word of mouth (AJ's own account).
  • Fandom usage: fans use carrd as a bio/info page (which fandom they are in, social links, DNI, commissions/portfolio), placed in Twitter/Tumblr bios (Fanlore) -> others in the same circle see it and follow suit by making 'their own carrd'.
  • Templating of public events: the 2020 BLM resource carrds were reposted en masse.

Speculation:

  • The social sources X/Instagram/YouTube correspond to bio links + 'how to make a carrd' aesthetic tutorial videos; fandom aesthetic templates get copied around -> chain spread among teenagers.
  • The roughly 2.7x jump in checkout visits last month may involve a price increase / new feature / some external event, not verified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2019: ~$30K MRR, 20K new users and 40K sites a month.
  • Early 2021: $1M ARR, 2.5-3M sites; later self-reported $2M ARR.
  • Profitable throughout; team of 1-2 people (starterstory estimates 10, unreliable).
  • Other third-party figures (starterstory's 800K users, $30.4K/month) contradict one another and are not used.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch and early path have strong evidence (AJ's own interviews + PH).
  • Gaps: the investor/amount; the specific starting date of the fandom breakout; Kim Kardashian's repost could not be confirmed a second time; the reason for the 2026 traffic surge; the HN launch post could not be pulled (the algolia request failed).
