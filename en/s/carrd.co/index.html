Overview

Carrd (carrd.co): One-page website builder. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 30,730 visits went from Carrd to a checkout payment page — #82 of the 607 sites we track. That is 167% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,501.

Total website visits that month were about 3,842,943 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 0.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 58%, social 32%, search 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows embed badge, and direct plus brand search is about 62%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $19/yr.
