Basics

  • Product: UgenticAI, an "agentic personal brand platform" that builds four kinds of AI clones for experts/coaches — Brain, Image, Voice, Video (the BIVV Method) — to mass-produce content across 16 social platforms. Also an in-person Clone Studio ("4 hours in the studio, clone delivered in 5 business days", go.ugenticai.com/clone-studio), an Academy members area (academy.ugenticai.com), and the annual UgenticAI Summit. In 2026 the public pitch became "the world's largest Agentic AI incubator, acquiring 100+ revenue-generating AI tools and plugging them into its growth engine" (ugenticai.com/home-new, invest.ugenticai.com).
  • Who is behind it: Anik Singal (CEO & Founder); the site claims 20+ years in business, $150M+ cumulative digital product sales, 1.7M followers; the leadership team claims "$500M+ in past exits, 50+ acquisitions". Anik Singal earlier founded Lurn and other make-money-online / info-product training companies (from memory, unverified). Legal entity: UgenticAI, Inc.
  • Launch: domain registered 2025-02-08 (RDAP, Namecheap); earliest Wayback capture 2025-05-14; the 2025-06-16 capture includes the homepage plus /founders, /investors, /terms etc., so an "investors" page existed from the start; /summit-gala captured 2025-08; /opportunity captured 2026-06.
  • Current form (profile data): no product prices on the site; the main button is Invest Now, linking to the crowdfunding page; visitors United States 71%, Canada 25%.
  • Funding: Reg CF crowdfunding via Jumpstart Micro, Inc. d.b.a. Issuance Express (go.ugenticai.com/investment); KingsCrowd covered "UgenticAI on Issuance Express 2026" on 2026-05-27 (summary only).

Where the first users came from: the evidence points to Anik's own existing audience — the site repeatedly cites "Proven across 90,000+ entrepreneurs", 1.7M followers, Facebook posts and the Challenge livestream (32K sign-ups), i.e. existing info-product students and email/social followers were funneled into the new brand. Visitors to this particular domain, however, are prospective investors brought in by crowdfunding ads.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder-IP funnel: Challenge / summit / Facebook posts → sales of cloning services, Academy and summit tickets (site's own account).
  • The product's output is AI-clone videos of the founder and clients, published at volume on social platforms (self-reported 500M+ views), and that content in turn promotes UgenticAI.
  • 2026 site traffic: display ads 44.7%, direct 40.4%, social 8.9%, zero non-brand search (profile data).

Speculation:

  • A classic make-money-online / info-product funnel: free challenge → high-ticket close on a livestream → summit → then convert students/followers into crowdfunding investors; the September 2026 traffic surge matches the crowdfunding campaign period.
  • "Make My Clone" customers are themselves creators, and the clone videos they publish may generate word of mouth, but there is no direct evidence.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site's own claims: an ecosystem of 90K+ entrepreneurs, 500M+ views on clone content, "millions" in Challenge sales within 60 days; founder's cumulative digital sales $150M+ (all self-reported, unverified).
  • LinkedIn company page 1,388 followers (search snippet).
  • Crowdfunding target, amount raised and valuation are not shown on the site's investment pages; crowdonomics and KingsCrowd would not open (429/403).
  • Monthly visits 6,436 (2026-09, profile data) — very small, mostly paid traffic.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Evidence comes mainly from the site's own claims (figures not verified by third parties; some stat placeholders display as 0), RDAP, Wayback CDX and the investment landing pages.
  • Search results were mostly unrelated UGC-tool lists; no genuine Reddit discussion; review sites like scam-detector have pages but were not opened.
  • Gaps: crowdfunding amount and investor count, actual paying customer count, third-party coverage of the Make My Clone Challenge, verification of Anik Singal's past companies.
