Overview

Openseo (openseo.so): Open-source SEO tool. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 47,278 visits went from Openseo to a checkout payment page — #43 of the 607 sites we track. That is 1425% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,100.

Total website visits that month were about 195,191 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 24.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 43%, direct 43%, referrals/email/paid 9%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 43%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: $10/mo (Base Plan, incl. $10 usage credits); ~$0.05 per keyword search; ~$0.08 per backlink domain overview etc..
