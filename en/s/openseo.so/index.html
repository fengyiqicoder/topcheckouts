Basics

  • Product: an open-source SEO platform with keyword research, rank tracking, domain overview, backlinks, site audits and AI search visibility (Prompt Explorer), plus the OpenSEO MCP, a Search Console MCP and a set of SEO skills for Claude/Codex (/seo-audit, /keyword-research, /competitor-analysis). Data comes from DataForSEO (the site carries a DataForSEO affiliate link aff=255379).
  • Team: Ben Senescu (Founder/CEO, former Klaviyo Senior SWE), team of 2, Boston; YC Fall 2026, lead partner Charlie Warren. See https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/openseo
  • Repo: github.com/every-app/open-seo (created 2026-02-27; as of 2026-10-25, 24,799 stars, 3,378 forks, 858 commits, 30 releases). Parent organization every-app ("open-source personal software platform", created 2025-10).
  • Pricing: self-hosting is free (bring your own DataForSEO key); hosted version $10/month with $10 of usage included, extra top-ups never expire; trial comes with $0.50 credit.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Open source plus the "open-source alternative to X" positioning: the GitHub star count is itself social proof, and the homepage shows the star count directly and calls on "people who want to build their own SEO tool, build on OpenSEO".
  • Designed for AI agent scenarios: MCP plus skills let users install it in Claude/Codex with a single sentence, and a "copy setup prompt" makes it easy to forward in developer communities.
  • Hosted version at $10/month vs Ahrefs Lite at $129/month; the price comparison is the main conversion pitch.
  • Two Product Hunt launches (#1 of the day three months ago; submitted by fmerian this month, #2 with 384 votes).
  • Third-party content spreads on its own: marketing newsletters, YouTube/blog self-hosting tutorials and TikTok marketing creators are all talking about a "free alternative to Ahrefs"; the official Instagram has only 158 followers, so spread does not mainly rely on owned accounts.
  • Star curve: 1.6k (4-27) → 4.0k (7-04) → 24.8k (10-25). In the three months before the PH launch it gained only 2.4k, and in the three months after it gained over 20k, so the takeoff point is the three months ago PH launch.
  • Referrals: stripe.com, saashub.com, ycombinator.com (profile data).

Speculation:

  • After taking #1 on PH it very likely hit GitHub Trending, stacked on reposts in the AI agent circle on X and inclusion in "awesome MCP" style lists; the specific breakout post was not found (X cannot be scraped).
  • First users: the 1.6k stars before six months ago presumably came from the founder's X account @bensenescu and developer circles (Show HN got only 2 points, so HN was not the source); unconfirmed.
  • Self-hosting users who hit DataForSEO billing and deployment hassle move to the hosted version.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 3,000+ users (self-reported on the site); hosted from $10/month plus usage top-ups, with about a 28% markup on resold DataForSEO (estimate from a third-party article).
  • last month checkout visits were 47,278.

Confidence and gaps

  • The star curve has three data points; there is indirect evidence of acceleration after the three months ago PH launch, but no day-by-day data.
  • Second follow-up check: added the 4,001-star baseline on 7-04, confirming the PH launch as the takeoff point; added the this month second-launch data (submitted by fmerian, 384 votes, #2) and the 1–3 months ago spontaneous spread through third-party newsletters, YouTube and TikTok. The growth engine was changed from "Creator supply" to "Creators or community": OpenSEO is not a UGC platform, so the old label was wrong, and the actual spread comes from the open-source community and third-party creators repeating it on their own.
  • The Reddit archive API was rate-limited this round, so the launch situation on Reddit is not verified.
