At the end of 2017 France closed the prefecture vehicle registration counters (the PPNG reform), so all registration certificate (carte grise) business could only be done online at ANTS, a process that is hard and easily rejected, and a large crop of "paid agents authorized by the Ministry of the Interior" appeared. SAS MGH of Grenoble (founded 2020, owner Mehdi Ghaoui) used an exact-match keyword domain, servicecartegrise.com, plus Google search ads (the top channel is Paid Search) to catch high-intent searches like "carte grise ownership transfer", charging a per-order service fee (€89 for a transfer, €9 for the cheapest Crit'Air sticker). Basics

  • Product: French registration certificate handling: ownership transfer (changement de propriétaire), replacement, address change, imported-vehicle registration, sale declaration, no-lien certificate, Crit'Air sticker, vehicle identification sheet. The user fills in a form online and uploads photos of documents → AI pre-check + human review → an authorized professional partner submits remotely to the SIV system → the national printing office mails the document to your home. Service fees (excluding government taxes and fees): transfer €89, replacement €79, imported vehicle €79, address change €49, sale declaration €29, no-lien certificate €19, vehicle info sheet €15, Crit'Air sticker €9; license-plate coupons are bundled in.
  • Company: SAS MGH (SERVICE CARTE GRISE), SIREN 885 367 532, founded 2020-04-09, NAF 45.11Z, president Mehdi Ghaoui (born 1978), headquartered in Saint-Martin-d'Hères (Isère, Grenoble), website hosted in Belgium at My Cyber Hosting. Prefecture authorization number n° 228147 (shown on the site). No funding info; looks like a self-funded local small business.
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2017-05-21, earlier than the company, which suggests someone grabbed it before the PPNG reform and may later have transferred it to MGH (the hand-off was Not found).
  • Compliance: the homepage states "a paid service run by a private company independent of the government, which you can do yourself for free on service-public.fr" (French regulation requires such sites to carry this notice).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Acquisition relies mainly on Paid Search and Display (our channel data). Administrative-service needs are one-off, with almost no organic spread.
  • SEO blog: categories such as Démarches, Prix/taxes and Problèmes et litiges hold dozens of posts, used to catch long-tail questions.
  • Trustpilot operation: since 2025-04 it has run a paid subscription and replied to every negative review, using 1,700+ reviews to offset the "looks like a scam" impression (the biggest conversion barrier in this category).
  • Channel data (ours): paid search 46.6%, direct 47.1%, search terms are generic government terms like ants, carte grise, changement adresse carte grise, with no referral or social sources.
  • Trust signals: claims "50,000+ files handled in the past 12 months", the Ministry of the Interior authorization number, and a carousel of user reviews.
  • Speculation:
  • Bundling plate coupons, Crit'Air stickers and the like raises order value, used to support high CPC bids.
  • The surge from July may be related to the summer used-car peak season, plus competitors dropping out because of "tightened authorization conditions" while it expanded spending.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Fiscal 2020: revenue about €410K, net profit about €83K.
  • Self-reports 50,000+ files in the past 12 months; the main business, transfers, charges €89, and with a blended order value of roughly €50–70, annual service fee revenue is about €2.5–3.5M (estimate, unverified).
  • Trustpilot has 1,028 reviews in the last 12 months, which roughly fits the scale of "50,000 files a year" (review rate about 2%).
