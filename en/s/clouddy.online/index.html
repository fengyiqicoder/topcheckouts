Overview

Clouddy (clouddy.online): Subscription cloud media player app that plays user-supplied playlists on smart TVs and other devices. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 25,788 visits went from Clouddy to a checkout payment page — #107 of the 607 sites we track. That is 64% up from the April–June monthly average of 15,733.

Total website visits that month were about 39,870 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 90%, search 10%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 90%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: $1/mo (3 devices); $2/yr (1 device); $6/yr (3 devices); 7-day free trial.
