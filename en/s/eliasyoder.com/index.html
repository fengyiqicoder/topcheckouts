Basics

  • Product: the ebook "The Amish Home Savings Manual" second edition, 700+ pages, 93 methods, $47 (labeled "launch price", regular price $77), 7-day refund, payments through Gumroad. Author byline "Elias & Esther Yoder".
  • The truth: compiled from RichTactic's 2026-10-09 investigation: the channel @EliasYoderAmish is an AI-generated persona; three positive-review comments in the page source read "PLACEHOLDER TESTIMONIALS…NOT real", and the purchase pop-ups are fake "illustrative" data. It has nothing to do with the real former-Amish influencer Eli Yoder (1.8M TikTok followers).
  • Operator: Ranga (@GOATiology), who claimed it on X on 2026-07-02: "Elias made us over $100k in his first month"; says he started mass-producing AI host channels in 2025-12. Unverified rumors say he is 30 and Zimbabwean. No company entity.
  • Users: older American homeowners (US traffic 64%); in the operator's interview he says "many older people don't understand computers well and think they are buying a paper book".

Where the first users came from: YouTube recommendation traffic - the very first video blew up to the million level, and the "$X old way to fix XX" titles plus the old Amish man persona hit older viewers precisely; the videos pitch the ebook in voiceover, with a link in the description to eliasyoder.com. It did not start from PH/Reddit/communities.
Where new users come from: still YouTube (profile: 41% social, all from YouTube, direct 45%, search mainly the person's name and the book title); sister channels such as "Esther Yoder" and "Amish Survival" were also opened to drive traffic to the same book. TikTok has a small account @eliasyoderlife (15.5K followers).

Growth loop

  • Evidence: AI mass-produces long videos (1.3 per day) → YouTube recommendation → voiceover and description-box traffic → $47 ebook; multiple related channels cross-promote; the operator suppresses bad reviews with generous refunds.
  • Speculation: after the exposé videos (September-October) and fading novelty, median views dropped, and the model depends heavily on the early window; the operator has turned to selling AI host tools and coaching courses, and "exposing how to make money" has itself become new acquisition content.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Operator's claims: Elias made "over $100K" in the first month (ads + ebook); the whole matrix earned $273,832 in 2026-06; a Gumroad screenshot shows ebook sales of about $160K (as of 2026-07-02). All self-reported and not independently verified, and the operator sells courses, so has a motive to exaggerate.

Confidence and gaps

  • The timeline comes from RichTactic's 2026-10-09 investigation (citing YouTube, X and RDAP raw records), credibility medium-high; the X posts and interview video were not opened directly, only relayed. All revenue is self-reported by the operator.
  • The homepage's "280K subscribers" and "used by tens of thousands of families" are marketing talk, and the page source itself admits the reviews are placeholders.
  • New site, written from scratch.
