Basics

  • Product: one-stop AI short-video generation (script, images, video, voiceover, subtitles) for TikTok/Reels/Shorts; video models Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Grok Imagine, Gemini Omni, Wan 3.0; image models Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2.5; callable inside Claude via MCP. English/French bilingual.
  • Pricing: not public on the current site (/pricing 404, profile); FAQ says "no commitment, cancel anytime". 2025-10 snapshot: CLIPPER €19/month (50 videos), EXPERT €29/month (100 videos), -15% for annual.
  • Team: unsigned on the site; Trustpilot company address shows the UK; users and content are mainly French.
  • Domain: RDAP query returned nothing (.io); first Wayback 2025-05-24; app.shortfy.io once showed a waitlist page ("Launching in 3 weeks. Join the waitlist").
  • Reputation: Trustpilot 28 reviews, 2.6 score (53% one star); complaints cluster around credits deducted with no video, generation stuck at 90%, charges after cancelling, and unreachable support; Trustpilot claimed in 2026-09. Several French site-trust checkers give low trust scores (scamdoc 15%, franceverif "reliability questionable").
  • Traffic (profile): 4,664 visits in 2026-09; France 81.2%, Brazil 3.4%, Belgium 3.4%; search 55.7% (brand keywords 88.9%), direct 33.2%, referral 6.6%.

Where the first users came from: early on (2025) it targeted faceless-account operators making Reddit story / brainrot videos on TikTok, a tool for the "faceless video income" crowd; 2026 growth comes from templated promo videos on Instagram Reels and brand-suffixed French UGC accounts, with viewers following the in-video instruction to search "Shortfy.io". Whether these accounts are paid UGC/affiliates or organic is not stated (the uniform naming suggests an organized paid or in-house network).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Instagram Reels "earn a doctor's salary in 67 seconds" template → voiceover/captions say "search up Shortfy.io" → brand-keyword search (88.9%).
  • A network of branded UGC accounts (xxx_shortfy, xxx.shortfy) keeps posting videos with shortfy.io in the bio.
  • The product itself is a content tool for faceless creators, and the videos users produce may keep promoting it with the same "make money" narrative.

Speculation:

  • The account network is a paid UGC/affiliate program (clipper model; the 2025 plan was literally named "CLIPPER"), a form of paid acquisition.
  • High churn: Trustpilot complaints centre on charges and cancellation, suggesting new users are replenished by continuous paid acquisition rather than word of mouth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No credible revenue data. The site claims 250k+ creators and "thousands of videos a day"; in 2025-10 the total was only 4,352 videos, so growth happened mainly in 2026.
  • 2025 pricing €19–29/month; about 4,700 monthly visits (profile), 28 Trustpilot reviews; the actual paying base may be a few hundred to one or two thousand (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The product pivot (Wayback snapshot text) and the Instagram promotion pattern (search snippets + shortcode-decoded dates + account metadata) have medium credibility; the Reels themselves would not open, summaries only.
  • Search results were heavily polluted by the URL shortener short.io and CVE pages (same-name noise).
  • Gaps: team identity and company registration (Trustpilot says UK); whether the UGC accounts are paid; current prices; scale of promotion on TikTok.
