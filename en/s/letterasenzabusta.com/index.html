In Italy, sending a registered letter (raccomandata) means queuing at the post office. Since 2016, LetteraSenzaBusta has turned "write a letter online → partners SailPost / Poste Italiane print and deliver it with legal effect" into a pay-per-use web service, acquiring customers through high-intent long-tail searches such as "raccomandata online" and "PEC gratuita" plus paid search. After that, every year or two it launched a new "free to open, pay per use" entry point: free digital signature cheFirma! in 2020-12, SPID authentication support in 2021-04, SuperPEC in 2022-05, and the WhatsApp registered letter NotificaTo in 2026-07. These new entry points both capture new search terms and get referrals from sites like the Italian social security agency inps.it as SPID and digital government services spread. Registered users have accumulated to 650–700K, and returning users in lawyer, property management, debt collection and cancellation scenarios come back repeatedly.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: online sending of domestic Italian registered letters, ordinary letters, telegrams and cancellation letters; the PEC certified mailbox SuperPEC (opened free with SPID or a digital signature, paid per message); the digital signature cheFirma! (opened free, paid per signing); NotificaTo (electronic registered mail sent via WhatsApp, SMS and email, eIDAS compliant, with up to 8 kinds of delivery and read receipts); the trusted timestamp Legal Date. There is an iOS app (ID 1246657115, listed around 2017). All free to register, no subscription, pay per use.
  • Operator: Innovatio Online Ltd (registered in England and Wales, company number 09301098, VAT GB206342932, Tallis House, London). LinkedIn says founded in 2016, 11–50 employees. Partners include SailPost, Poste Italiane, Mobysign Ltd and Land S.r.l.
  • Founders: not named on the official site, LinkedIn or in media coverage; Not found. Funding: Not found (looks like a self-run small company).
  • Launch: WHOIS registration 2013-06-18, earliest Wayback snapshot 2013-08-17; the official "about us" says the first services officially launched in 2016-01. The two are inconsistent, and it is speculated that 2013–2015 was an early version or warm-up phase.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: search ranking. Search is 46.7%, with non-brand keywords including pec gratuita and superpec; business media mention it when writing about "how to send a registered letter online".
  • Evidence: a product matrix of "free to open, pay per use" (signature, PEC, WhatsApp registered mail), where each new product is a new free search entry point, and users who come in then cross-buy registered letters.
  • Evidence: referrals come from the Italian social security agency inps.it (5.77%), its own chefirma.it (3.9%) and the portal libero.it (3.64%). Users are directed over when they need a PEC or signature for government procedures like social security (which page on inps.it links to it is unverified).
  • Evidence: paid search is about 8%; it ran TV ads in 2019; it makes short educational videos on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.
  • Evidence: trust building, including the Codacons trusted website certification and about 3,600 Trustpilot reviews.
  • Speculation: the brand name itself is a category description ("letter without an envelope"), which helps it hold SEO positions.
