Overview

Adspower (adspower.com): Antidetect browser giving each account an isolated fingerprinted profile for multi-account management. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Adspower to a checkout payment page — #384 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 176,779 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 4.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 55%, search 43%, referrals/email/paid 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 81%, plus shares via Telegram. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
