Basics

  • Product: antidetect browser. One isolated browser environment per account (fingerprint, proxy IP, cookie isolation), with team permissions, RPA automation, local API, window synchronizer, and MCP added in 2025. Windows/macOS/Linux clients.
  • Company: the site's About page says founded in Hong Kong in 2019; in 2020 the Chinese site's copyright holder was "Guangzhou Sanbuqu Information Technology Co., Ltd." (ICP license 粤ICP备20046039号); the English site's copyright holder is now SUNFLOWER TECH PTE. LTD. (Singapore). Founders not public.
  • Pricing: free plan with 2 profiles forever; Professional/Business subscriptions billed by number of profiles, quarterly -15%, annual -40%; 7-day trial.
  • Domain: adspower.com was registered 1995-12-20, belonged to someone else's site in 1996–2007, and content only reappears in Wayback in 2021-12, so it is an old domain bought in 2021. The Chinese site adspower.net was registered 2020-08-14; the Russian site adspower-ru.com was registered 2025-06.
  • Traffic: United States 18%, Brazil 6.7%, Vietnam 5.7%; direct 55%, search 43% (brand keywords 81%); referrals from coupon site simplycodes.com, techjury.net, github.com.

Where the first users came from: Chinese cross-border e-commerce sellers (anti-association for multiple Amazon/Shopee stores, etc.). Evidence: the 2020 Chinese site's positioning as "e-commerce anti-association", the ICP license in Guangzhou, and partner links that were all cross-border e-commerce service and proxy providers.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral commissions: referring new users earns up to 30% of their subscription fees, settled within 24 hours of payment, no cap on referrals, paid via PayPal/WebMoney/Capitalist/Alipay.
  • Free plan: 2 profiles free forever; install the client first, upgrade later.
  • Cross-promotion with proxy providers: homepage integrates mainstream proxy providers (with Luminati affiliate links in 2020); proxy provider tutorials and coupon sites drive traffic (simplycodes is 6% of referrals).
  • Multilingual local sites: Chinese.net, Russian site, Vietnamese, matching the big markets for traffic arbitrage and airdrop farming.
  • Paid UGC: in 2026 it offered bounties for users to post on Reddit mentioning AdsPower.

Speculation:

  • Multi-account operators are mostly teams and studios; once a team buys, every member has to install the client, so team expansion brings organic growth.
  • Many "best antidetect browser" comparison articles (including ones written by competitors) keep it showing up in non-brand search.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Official self-reported user counts: 100k+ in 2020, 800k+ in 2021, 3 million+ in 2023, 5 million+ in 2024, 9 million+ in 2025 (About page); the Trustpilot bio says 9M+.
  • Subscriptions billed by number of profiles, many team customers; revenue not public.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Origins, domain dates, early positioning, and referral terms have evidence from the site and Wayback; user counts are official self-reports.
  • Search results were mostly comparison articles, coupon sites, and irrelevant pages, not used.
  • Gaps: founder identity; no primary sources on which cross-border seller communities / WeChat accounts it was promoted in early on; the specific events behind the English-market takeoff (which creators/proxy providers) not found.
