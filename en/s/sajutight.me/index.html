Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Facebook ads: a Taiwanese Dcard user said outright they came from an FB ad (2026-03); the ad format looks like UGC-style "someone sharing this site".
  • Threads interactive posts: "enter your birthday and theirs → get a compatibility reading → come back and comment your result" (2025-03).
  • The site embeds Facebook Pixel and GTM; the third channel in our data is Display, indicating paid display/social ads.
  • 199 celebrity saju pages × 2 languages, plus free SEO content such as day-pillar readings, shensha and a perpetual calendar.
  • robots.txt contains a /referral/ path, indicating a referral/invite mechanism exists (specific rewards not found).
  • Runs Instagram @tightsaju and a Naver blog, tightsaju.

Speculation:

  • Compatibility and "match with a celebrity" readings naturally require entering someone else's birthday, and the result pages are well suited to screenshots shared to Instagram Stories and KakaoTalk, bringing friends in to test each other.
  • Expansion to Taiwan: Taiwan has strong interest in Korean fortune-telling plus K-pop stars; the same AI reports are reused, and Stripe payments come mainly from these overseas users (which explains how the Stripe checkout page made the top 200).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue or user numbers found; pricing is not shown on the homepage.
  • Our data: 60,172 checkout visits last month (4–6 months ago average 10,550), roughly 6x growth.
