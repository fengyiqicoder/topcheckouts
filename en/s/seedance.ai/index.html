Basics

  • Product: multi-model AI image/video/voice generation aggregator. Images: FLUX 3, GPT Image 2/2.5, Nano Banana 2/Pro, Z Image Turbo; video: Kling 4.0, Google Veo, Minimax H3, Wan. Community features: public gallery, official daily challenges with voting, chat room. Basic Stable Diffusion is free and unlimited.
  • Pricing (annual billing, monthly equivalent): Starter $19.90/month (list price $29.90), Premium $34.90/month, Advanced $62.90/month; credit packs of 5,000 credits for $50 and 14,000 credits for $100.
  • Self-declaration: both the homepage and pricing page FAQ say "an independent Seedance (SeedDance) AI community, with no relationship to ByteDance, and does not offer the Seedance model".
  • Operator: the footer only says "Copyright seedance.ai" with no company name; the address registered on Trustpilot is Anguilla (the home of.ai domains, so this may just be domain registration info).
  • Our data: US 12.1%, India 9.2%, Brazil 5.8% (fragmented); search 79.1% (brand keywords 94.8%: seedance, seedance ai, seedance 2.0), direct 15.7%, referral 3.1% (ventmagazines.co.uk).
  • Reputation: 9 reviews on Trustpilot, 2.6 rating; complaints about hard-to-get refunds, unresponsive support, credits deducted for no clear reason, and low resolution; profile unclaimed.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Model-name interception: 79% of traffic is search, 95% of it brand keywords, all of them ByteDance's model name; every ByteDance version release (1.0 → 2.0 → 2.5) brings a wave of searches.
  • Community retention design: daily challenges, gallery, chat room and non-expiring credits encourage return visits (direct 15.7%).

Speculation:

  • The first users were people who searched the model name after Seedance 1.0 launched in 2025-06; real growth came during the Seedance 2.0 viral period in 2026-02, because ByteDance's overseas entry points were limited then (via Jimeng/CapCut/Volcano etc.), so users who couldn't find the official entrance landed on this site.
  • The community template and outdated FAQ text such as "Seedance proprietary dance video models" suggest a reused AI-art-site template, and the operator may be the same as for several similar sites (not verified).
  • Referrals come from advertorial sites like ventmagazines.co.uk, possibly with a few paid backlinks.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The homepage claims "millions of people use it every month", with no supporting evidence. Subscriptions at $19.9-62.9/month plus credit packs; no public revenue data.
  • Risk: it depends on ByteDance's trademarked name and could face a trademark complaint or search engine demotion at any time.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Domain registration, Wayback, the site's own declarations and Trustpilot are all first-hand evidence; the takeoff points are inferred from ByteDance's release dates, with no traffic curve from this site to back them (Wayback page content fetches were rate-limited with 429).
  • Search results are almost entirely articles about ByteDance's Seedance model and unrelated to this site's operations.
  • Gaps: operator identity, real user count and revenue, and how this site's traffic changed around 2026-02.
