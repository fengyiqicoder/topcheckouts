Basics

  • Product: two 24/7 gyms (Rovato main location, Passirano branch), memberships, personal training, a powerlifting area; the all-inclusive card works at both; signup and purchase go through the "Palestre" app (Google Play package com.shaggyowl.app, a white-label of a third-party gym-management SaaS).
  • Pricing: €34.90 per month; autumn promotion 14 months for €399 all-inclusive; monthly family cards for 2–4 people; 10%–50% discount by verified number of visits.
  • Operator: brand FIT CLUB PALESTRE, review replies are signed "Staff fit Club", and one review mentions "Giampi", possibly the owner (unconfirmed). The legal entity was not found (not the focus).
  • Current traffic (profile): Italy 80.6%; direct 49%, search 34.5% (brand keywords 83.6%, non-brand palestra nuova apertura brescia), paid 9.8%. The profile notes "traffic rose around the new opening two months ago".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Offline conversion: a free booked trial workout, no-lock-in monthly card, family card and visit-based discounts lower the barrier to joining and reward retention.
  • Local word of mouth: 109 Google reviews at 4.9, with the owner replying to almost every one.
  • Social: Instagram @fit_club_rovato is the only social entry on the site.
  • Paid: 9.8% paid traffic, bidding on "new gym opening" type keywords at the new opening.

Speculation:

  • The first members came from Rovato's local circle of acquaintances and foot traffic from the neighboring padel center; when the second location opened, the all-inclusive cross-location card was used to bring existing members over.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Monthly card €34.90; membership count Not found; size of the two locations and revenue: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The exact opening dates of the locations, the owner's identity, and whether the Passirano location is the "new opening" are all unconfirmed.
  • The search results had only one third-party directory page, everything else was unrelated; this report mainly relies on the official site, the directory page and the profile.
