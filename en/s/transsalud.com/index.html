Basics

  • Product: website of a trans healthcare network: a doctor directory (pick a doctor, see wait times, book hormone therapy), with about 30 services including psychology, nutrition, surgery, endocrinology, voice therapy and legal aid, plus calculators, psychology mini-games, a job board, continuing education courses and research statistics ("survey responses from 4,000+ trans people").
  • Founder: Dra. Daniela Muñoz-Jiménez (site bio: founder/CEO of TranSSalud and MASS Clinik, founder of ATLASST, the Latin American association of trans health practitioners, co-chair of the WPATH 2026 Mexico City conference, advisor to the INER trans clinic). Site technology powered by +Clinik (imclinik.com).
  • Pricing: first consultation 1,250 Mexican pesos, follow-up 1,000 pesos; solidarity price from 350 pesos, payable online or at Oxxo convenience stores (site). A 2023 Reddit user said Dr. Daniela charged 300 pesos (summary only).
  • Locations: Roma Sur, Mexico City (Tuxpan 54); Máss Clinik in León, Guanajuato.
  • Launch date: domain registered 2021-05-08 (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2021-12-26; the 2022-01 snapshot is a small Google Sites page with only 2 doctors.
  • Traffic (profile): Mexico 96%; search 46% (brand keywords 77%), direct 40%, referrals 8%, no social sources (people search the brand name after community word of mouth).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The founder's existing standing and exposure in the trans community (column, association, WPATH conference) brought trust and the first patients; the site began as simply moving her practice to online booking.
  • Community word of mouth: users in the Mexican trans subreddit recommend prices and booking methods to each other; 77% of search is brand keywords, meaning most people search after hearing about it.
  • Solidarity pricing (from 350 pesos) + Oxxo cash payment lower the barrier for low-income people, helping spread within the community.
  • Content tools (dosage calculator, statistical reports, psychology mini-games, language guides) and a job board add stickiness to the site.

Speculation:

  • Expansion relies on "platformization": adding more community-aligned doctors and psychologists to the directory; more supply → shorter waits → more patients.
  • Non-brand search (about 9%) comes from long-tail questions like "where to find a psychologist who can do an HRT evaluation".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Founder says 4,800+ trans patients; the research page says 4,000+ trans survey responses collected.
  • Per-consultation 350–1,250 pesos (about $20–$70), two physical clinics + online consultations; no public revenue data.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Founder identity from the site's bio page; launch date from RDAP and raw Wayback snapshots; credible.
  • Gaps: when Daniela started practicing, clinic founding year and the Informe 2025 figures could not be read; the original Reddit posts would not open, only search summaries were used; search results were mostly irrelevant TRT clinics and celebrity lists.
