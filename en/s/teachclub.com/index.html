Basics

  • Product: AI-generated online school (courses, sales page, student login, payments), with an AI tutor teaching students one by one. Pricing: Teacher $27/month (1 school, 100 students), Club $69/month (10 schools, custom domain, add-ons, ad pixels), Pro by quote (done-for-you). Students pay nothing to the platform; teachers set their own prices and the platform takes a cut. Positions against Skool, Kajabi, Thinkific, Circle.
  • Company: site footer reads "© 2026 TeachClub by Deal.ai Inc."; the help center help.famouslabs.com serves Famous.ai, SuperCool.com, TeachClub.com and Deal.ai, with support email support@deal.ai; Trustpilot reviewers say the software comes from "Famous Labs".
  • Founder: the site gives no name, only that "the founder has taught over 300,000 students through his own online schools". A Trustpilot review mentions attending "a seminar hosted by the software's author himself". Who exactly: not found.
  • Domain: registered 2007-12-02 (GoDaddy, RDAP), last changed 2026-06-14 (very likely acquired or transferred at that point). Wayback shows someone else's old site from 2005-2019, a Chinese parking page ("ICP备案查询官网") in the 2025-07 snapshot, and the current TeachClub page only appears in 2026-10.
  • Parent company scale: Deal.ai's site claims 10,000+ active users and 75+ AI tools, with white-label plans ($69-$997/month), an affiliate program and certifications.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Riding the parent company: the brand belongs to Deal.ai Inc. and shares the Famous Labs help center and support, so it is a new product line from the same team.
  • Paid social acquisition: our data shows paid social at 32.6% (Facebook), and search is almost entirely brand keywords.
  • Sales funnel: join.teachclub.com is a standalone funnel page; negative Trustpilot reviews mention being pushed a pricier "today only" package right after paying $27, plus weekly Zoom Q&A; positive reviews mention seminars, live demos and a free 60-minute call. This is the classic "ads → seminar/call → low-price entry → upsell" play.
  • Teachers' public pages: every school has a public page under teachclub.com/@name, several already show up in search results (medical-IT career change, AI course bundles, etc.), and the Explore directory showcases them.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 users very likely came from Deal.ai / Famous.ai's existing customer lists, emails and white-label agencies (the parent claims 10,000+ users), layered with Facebook ads. No direct numeric evidence.
  • Teachers drive their own traffic to their school pages to sell courses, and students see the TeachClub brand along the way, forming a weak "branded output" loop; student counters currently show anomalies (0+), so scale is still small.
  • The "Is TeachClub Legit" article and free human calls suggest ad-sourced users have many doubts, with conversion propped up by manual sales.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing $27-$69/month plus transaction cuts; the funnel has higher-priced upsell packages (a negative review mentions $100).
  • Trustpilot: 41 reviews (since 2026-06); Explore directory on the order of a few dozen schools.
  • Parent Deal.ai claims 10,000+ active users. Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Company ownership, the launch window (after 2025-07, around 2026-06) and the funnel play have first-hand evidence; credibility medium-high.
  • Written from scratch: no earlier report in the notes. The "launch", "review" and "tiktok" search batches were almost entirely unrelated generic articles; evidence comes mainly from the site, RDAP, Wayback CDX, Trustpilot and the help center.
  • Gaps: names of the Deal.ai / Famous Labs founders; exact product launch date (no Wayback snapshots between 2025-07 and 2026-10); user count and revenue.
