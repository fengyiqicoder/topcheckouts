Basics

  • Product: AI influencer generation: AI images, AI vertical videos (connected to models such as Seedance), face consistency, "Learn Academy" tutorials (e.g. "how to create viral game trends"), plus an API and an MCP interface for Claude/ChatGPT (https://alexya.ai/, /api-docs, /mcp).
  • Pricing: Starter $9/month (1,900 credits), Pro $29/month, Power User $99/month, billed monthly/annually (https://alexya.ai/; profile file).
  • Self-reported scale: the home page claims "112,000+ creators", "nearly 1 million pieces of content", "+10 famous content creators trust it" (not verified).
  • Entity: the terms say only "AlexyaAI", governed by UAE law, effective 2026-03-09, last updated 2026-09-08 (https://alexya.ai/terms); founders not disclosed.
  • Community: Discord "AlexyaAI" with 1,421 members (58 online), server created 2025-09-04 (decoded from the server ID); TikTok @alexya.ai about 59.8k followers (search snippet, French UI "Rejoins 59.8K followers"); Skool account @alexya-ai-9771 joined 2025-12-05, active in French AI influencer/OFM course communities such as "ALPHA OFM IA" (12.8k members), "Ineffable AI Lite" (21.2k), "Free AI OFM Course" (4.9k), "Incubateur OFM IA" ($47/month) and "AI VAULT" ($97) (https://www.skool.com/@alexya-ai-9771); there is also a Whop page (empty).
  • Content note: OFM (OnlyFans management) is an adult-oriented gray business; this time only the site, terms, Skool profile page and Discord invite metadata were viewed, and no generated content was opened; the home page itself has no NSFW positioning; no illegal-content leads that would require stopping the investigation were found.
  • Domain: registered 2025-07-08 per RDAP; first Wayback capture 2026-01-23 (307 redirect).
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,198 visits in September, France 95.4%; steady at 4–5k for a long time; direct 54.1%, search 34.4% (brand keywords 88.5%: alexya, alexya ai, alexya ia); social 6.9% from YouTube.

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: French AI influencer/OFM course communities (several groups of over ten thousand on Skool) + its own French TikTok + Discord. Search terms include the French "ia" (alexya ia).
  • Speculation: partnerships with course owners (the "famous content creators"), who recommend Alexya as a generation tool in their courses (home page "+10 famous content creators"), with students signing up in batches; this is an existing-audience migration riding the "make money with AI influencers" tutorial boom.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Teaching drives traffic: Learn Academy teaches "making viral trend videos" → users generate with Alexya → post to TikTok/Instagram.
  • Community operations: Discord with 1,421 people, ongoing posting in Skool course communities.
  • Its own TikTok showcases results (about 60k followers).

Speculation:

  • Recommendations from course owners/influencers (possibly with commissions; no affiliate program found); generated content itself carries no watermark branding (paid tiers), so outward product-driven spread is weak.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 4–5k monthly visits and 1.4k Discord members; at $9–99/month subscriptions, monthly revenue is probably in the thousands to over ten thousand dollars (speculation, not verified).
  • The home page's "112,000+ creators" does not match the traffic scale well and may be cumulative signups or exaggerated.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration time, Discord size and creation date, Skool community links, pricing and terms have evidence; credibility is medium.
  • Gaps: founders and company entity were not found (Crunchbase returned 403); Wayback was offline at the time, so early pages were not seen; the TikTok follower count comes only from a search snippet; which course owners it works with and whether there are commissions are unconfirmed.
