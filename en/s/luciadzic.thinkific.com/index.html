luciadzic.thinkific.com is the paid-course backend of Croatian podcaster and business mentor Lucia Adžić, home to her membership program for women entrepreneurs, "Biznis Klub" (an e-learning platform plus 6 or 12 months of group coaching and regular Zoom Q&As). Her audience comes from the podcast "Neka Nova Priča" (211 episodes, started in spring 2020, which she calls a leading podcast in the region) and Instagram @luciadzic (about 18K followers). She built the personal brand and listeners first, then converted listeners into high-priced coaching students; the Thinkific subdomain is only where students log in to watch the lessons, and the traffic is almost all existing students visiting directly or searching "thinkific lucia adzic" and "biznis klub lucia".One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: the Biznis Klub course platform, in Croatian, with modules "Foundation (the entrepreneur + setup) → Structure (business model, 1:1/group/passive products, free lead magnets) → Visibility (personal brand, content, positioning) → Invitation (launching and selling)", plus Zoom Q&A and live mentoring recordings from 2024 on (earliest Q&A 2024-03-26), with new live coaching still added in 2025-08 and 2026-03.
  • Price: "All Products" on the store homepage is empty (the course is not sold publicly, students are enrolled directly), price not found. The official site says Biznis Klub helps "women entrepreneurs grow their online business to six or seven figures".
  • Who runs it: Lucia Adžić, Croatia, describes herself as "mother, wife, entrepreneur"; podcast "Neka Nova Priča" (originally "Lucie's little show" on Apple Podcasts, 211 episodes, latest 2025-05-28), Facebook page lucieslittleloves, Instagram @luciadzic with about 18K followers.
  • Launch timing: podcast started in spring 2020 (the official site's own words); luciadzic.com registered 2022-12-18, built on Squarespace; earliest recording inside the Biznis Klub course 2024-03-26; Thinkific subdomain traffic only appears in the data in 2026-09 (possibly a course migrated from elsewhere or a newly opened subdomain, not verified).
  • Traffic (profile data): direct 55.1%, search 33.7% (brand keywords 72%), paid search 6.7%.

From the structure of the official site, students come from podcast listeners and Instagram followers ("if you stay in my world, you'll know what this means"), and the homepage puts the podcast and Biznis Klub side by side to send people over; this is inference.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Podcast (211 episodes, since 2020) + Instagram build personal trust → the official site's "RADI SA MNOM (work with me)" → Biznis Klub.
  • New live coaching keeps being added to the course, which helps retention during the 6/12-month membership.
  • A small amount of paid search (6.7%).

Speculation:

  • The Croatian-language market is small with little competition, and the trust from the podcast's top regional ranking is the main moat.
  • Successful students are probably invited onto the podcast as guests, forming a word-of-mouth loop (not verified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Neither price nor student count was found; the Thinkific subdomain has low monthly traffic (new last month in the profile data), so the student base is estimated at dozens to a few hundred (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The Google results were all unrelated (Thinkific official docs, slot machine videos, Lucidchart); the evidence comes from the Thinkific store and course catalog, luciadzic.com, the Apple Podcasts API, Instagram and RDAP.
  • Wayback returns 403 for the 2024 snapshots of luciadzic.com, so early pricing can't be seen; course price and student count are missing.
  • The takeoff year is set to 2020 when the podcast started; the course itself only opened in 2024.
