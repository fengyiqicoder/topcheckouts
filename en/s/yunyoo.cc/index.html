Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: TG channel + official group, drawing people in with "join the group for a server giveaway" and holiday promo codes (2023-09~12), reaching "1,000 people aware" in two months.
  • Commissions: AFF launched in 2023-11 with a high rate of 30% on the first order / 25% on renewals, spawning third-party review posts, review sites (digvps, vpsclub, vps.fund) and a GitHub promo code repo, all carrying AFF links; in the channel profile, referrals come from idcjk.com, vpstarter.com and linux.do.
  • Forums: the company posts vendor/promo posts on the Dalao forum and the Naixi forum; third-party "test only, no rating" review posts keep appearing on NodeSeek.
  • Supply-side expansion: keeps opening new regions (every new region triggers a round of promo codes + reviews).
  • Speculation: "原烟火云" may be a predecessor brand, with some of the first users migrating from the old brand (only one snippet of evidence); the mid-2026 doubling may be related to exposure in the linux.do community or a new region launch, not confirmed.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site says "trusted by 9,000+ users" and "10,000 users" in 2026-01; the main TG channel has about 4,500 subscribers.
  • 17K checkout visits a month × a low order value (¥15–60/month), monthly turnover is guessed at tens of thousands to a hundred-odd thousand RMB (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Medium-high. The site's history + WHOIS + RIPE + early TG channel posts corroborate each other; the original NodeSeek posts return 403 so only snippets were used; the "原烟火云" predecessor is not verified; the founder is Not found (not the focus).
