Overview

Keepa (keepa.com): Amazon price-tracking extension, 6M users, affiliate program. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 9,972 visits went from Keepa to a checkout payment page — #326 of the 607 sites we track. That is 23% up from the April–June monthly average of 8,075.

Total website visits that month were about 1,162,462 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 0.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 77%, search 17%, referrals/email/paid 4%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 14%, with queries like “amazon price tracker”, “price comparison”, “price history”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
