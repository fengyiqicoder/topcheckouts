Overview

Gregmat (gregmat.com): Subscription GRE test-prep platform with study plans, live classes, practice tools and free practice tests. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Gregmat to a checkout payment page — #297 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 563,873 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 2.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 86%, search 13%, social 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 5%, with queries like “gre calculator”, “prepswift”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: $11.99/mo (GregMat Plus); $14.99/mo (Plus + PrepSwift).
