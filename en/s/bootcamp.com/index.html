Bootcamp (bootcamp.com): Online test-prep platform (question banks, videos, study plans) for dental, medical, nursing, optometry and anatomy exam. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 34,384 visits went from Bootcamp to a checkout payment page — #73 of the 607 sites we track. That is 3% down from the April–June monthly average of 35,400.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 77%, search 17%, social 4%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)
