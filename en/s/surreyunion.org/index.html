This is the official website of the students' union at the University of Surrey (Guildford, UK), not a startup project. Every enrolled student at Surrey is automatically a union member, and the union site is the only entry point for finding events, buying Freshers' Week tickets, joining clubs and societies and getting academic advice. It used to use ussu.co.uk (in use before 2001); in June 2023 it registered surreyunion.org and switched over before the August freshers season, with ussu.co.uk 301-redirected as a whole, carrying 20+ years of links and search equity over to the new domain. The traffic structure is "university distribution + brand search + freshers-season peak": brand keywords are 87.7%, and seasonal searches like "university of surrey freshers week 2026" are the source of the peaks.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: students' union website. Events (What's On) and ticketing (some events sell tickets through Tixtu, such as Freshers' Week and FRAT parties; workshops like pumpkin painting at £2.50), clubs and societies A-Z, a community area, academic and welfare support, student reps and elections (Surrey Decides), the careers workshop Destination Success, union policy and priorities.
  • Organization: Surrey Students' Union (formerly University of Surrey Students' Union, USSU), with 5 full-time student-elected Sabbatical Officers (a President plus four Vice Presidents for Activities / Community / Support / Voice). The SSL certificate holder is the University of Surrey.
  • Users: all current University of Surrey students (student body roughly 15K-20K, from memory, not verified); the Facebook page has 28.8K followers.
  • Traffic (our data): direct 50.9%, search 36.8% (brand keywords 87.7%: surrey union, surrey su, university of surrey freshers week 2026), referral 7.4%. United Kingdom 93.3%, United States 3.2%, India 1.3%.
  • Domain: surreyunion.org registered 2023-06-08 (RDAP, Network Solutions).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Institutional distribution: all enrolled students are automatically members, election voting links are sent to university email; the SSL certificate is issued to the university.
  • Freshers-season peak: the homepage launched as a Freshers' Week ticket countdown; search terms include "freshers week 2026."
  • Old domain 301: 20+ years of backlinks and bookmarks on ussu.co.uk redirect to the new domain.
  • Social accounts (Facebook 28.8K, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) post events and elections and bring people back to the site to buy tickets.

Speculation:

  • New users come in as a batch every last month with the new student intake, getting the link in the university's admissions materials, emails and app before arrival; they drop off at graduation, so traffic is an annual sawtooth.
  • Ticketing (Tixtu) and club membership fees are the site's only direct revenue, while the union's main funding comes from a university grant (from memory, not verified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site itself barely charges: most events are free, some events and workshops cost a few pounds; club membership fees, Freshers' Week tickets and students' union bar income go to the union.
  • The scale ceiling is the number of current University of Surrey students plus parents and applicants.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there is no earlier report in notes. Search results were mixed with lots of unrelated same-name results such as Surrey city (Canada) and credit unions; evidence came mainly from RDAP, Wayback (ussu.co.uk 2001-2024, surreyunion.org 2023-2026) and the current site.
  • The timing of the domain change (June-September 2023) is reliably evidenced; the idea of "takeoff" means little for a students' union website, and the classification was applied as "institutional distribution + freshers-season operations."
  • Gaps: the union's annual funding, number of enrolled students, number of tickets sold on the site.
