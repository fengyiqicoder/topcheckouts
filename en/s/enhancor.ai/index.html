In spring 2025 the AI influencer / AI adult model industry (Fanvue and others) exploded, and the "plastic skin" of AI-generated images was a widely acknowledged pain point. Enhancor solves only one problem: "fix AI skin to look like a real person". Founder Sirio Berati is himself an influential creator in the AI community who builds the product in public. Around the 2025-05 launch there was a YouTube review and a Product Hunt launch (#9 of the day), after which AI influencer tutorials, newsletters and directory sites repeatedly wrote it into workflows ("last step: drop it into Enhancor to add pores"). Within 3 months it grew from a single-point tool into a suite, and a third party claims MRR above $100K (unverified). From 2025-11 it was also plugged in as a model on upstream creation platforms such as FLORA.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Founder's personal influence: techpilot says the founder is "well known in the AI community and builds in public", and the Medium article says he appeared on the Forbes 30 Under 30 stage. The cold start most likely relied on the founder's own social audience.
  • Tutorial and review spread: YouTube reviews (since 2025-05), AI influencer workflow newsletters and directory sites keep recommending it and writing it in as a fixed step, forming a loop of "tutorial → new users → new users write more tutorials". The profile shows social sources are YouTube, Instagram and X.
  • Upstream platform distribution: plugged in as a model by creation platforms such as FLORA (2025-11), and AI tool sites like lastframe.ai and pippit.ai send it traffic.
  • Category search terms: the non-brand terms "ai plastic skin fix" and "kora ai" show it has taken a position on the category term it defined for itself.
  • Speculation: sellers of AI influencer courses promote it through affiliate commissions; output images carry no watermark (paid tier), so it does not spread through branded output. Word of mouth spreads among Venezuelan AI model studios.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing from $9-12/month, up to $299/month, billed in credits.
  • techpilot.ai says MRR is above $100K (2025-08, source unclear, may come from figures the founder shared publicly, unverified); an aggregator site says about 250K visits in 2025-07.
  • Our data: 17,192 checkout visits in September, average 11,800 in April-June, steady with a slight rise.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch timing (domain, Wayback, the 2025-05 YouTube review, PH) is credible. The founder's name has two independent sources (techpilot, the Medium title), but his background, nationality and the specific "Forbes 30 Under 30" list are unverified.
  • Second-pass check: added the main thread of founder Sirio Berati and his build-in-public personal influence, the 2025-05 PH launch (#9 of the day, small scale, not the takeoff event), the earliest YouTube review, the 2025-11 FLORA integration, and the third-party claim of MRR above $100K. The takeoff method is unchanged.
  • The founder's social accounts and follower counts, the first launch post and real revenue were not found; there is no relevant discussion on Reddit.
