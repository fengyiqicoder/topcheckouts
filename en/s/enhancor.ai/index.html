Overview

Enhancor (enhancor.ai): AI skin retouching, 50K creators. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Enhancor to a checkout payment page — #186 of the 607 sites we track. That is 46% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 25,882 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).
