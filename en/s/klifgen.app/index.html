Basics

  • Product: AI video/image generation aggregator reselling models such as Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Grok Imagine, Seedance and Wan; credit packs $10/$20/$50, no expiry, no subscription. Homepage selling point "Veo 3.1 Fast $0.55 per clip", claims to be 77%–95% cheaper than Fal.ai / Replicate / Pollo AI.
  • Who is behind it: the official site only says "© 2026 KLIFGEN" with no company entity; third-party articles also call the developer "relatively anonymous". HN account easyfree posted the Show HN, and that account released a string of tool sites from March to August 2025 including instavidai.app, articleaigenerator.com, gfastscraper.com and circuitai.online → an independent developer who keeps making small tools (nationality Not found). No funding seen.
  • Launch date: earliest Wayback snapshot 2025-08-27, the same day as the Show HN.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Launch: HN did not work; a YouTube "use Veo 3 cheaply, no subscription" tutorial video with a link appeared 2 days after launch.
  • The main source of new customers is social (YouTube > Reddit > X); search is small and mostly brand keywords — the old report's "search ranking" was overturned by the data.
  • Every new upstream model gets a dedicated landing page (/create-seedance-2-5, etc.) and comparison content, catching discussions of "where is the new model cheapest".
  • Users on Reddit discuss it in model communities such as r/grok (mainly usage and account deletion questions).
  • The homepage has an "Earn More" entry (whether it pays affiliate commission is unverified).
  • Speculation:
  • Some new customers come from NSFW generation demand (search term "seedance nsfw"), spreading by word of mouth in niche Reddit communities.
  • Non-expiring credits + cheap → high repeat purchase from existing users, hence direct visits at 70%.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: Veo 3.1 Fast $0.55 per clip; Sora 2 $0.23; Seedance $0.14; Grok Imagine images $0.05.
  • MRR, user count and founder statements Not found. With about 25K checkout visits in September, monthly revenue might be in the tens of thousands of dollars based on conversion (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Evidence for the launch date is strong (HN + Wayback on the same day); the date of the first YouTube promo video comes from a search snippet.
  • The author's identity, the relationship between the Busy-tech channel and the author, and the exact source of the first 1,000 users are still Not found; the original Reddit posts could not be fetched.
  • Second follow-up check: added the 2025-08-29 YouTube promo video (first external traffic), official IG/FB content, and the Similarweb channel structure; growth engine changed from "Search ranking" to "Creators or community" (search is only 4.6% and mostly brand keywords, with new customers mainly coming from YouTube/Reddit tutorials and discussions).
