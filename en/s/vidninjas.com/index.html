Official YouTube channel @Vidninjas (opened 2023-06-10, 1.31K subscribers).
  • Pricing (profile data): Free $0; Basic $9.99/mo; Pro $29.99/mo.

Where the first users came from: Romayroh's YouTube viewers and paid Skool community students (Evidence: he has linked VidNinjas in every video since 2026-02 and demoed it on screen since four months ago). The Chrome extension has only about 1,000 users, so organic store traffic is small.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator's own content sells the product: every "faceless channel monetization" tutorial puts VidNinjas in the first line of the description and demos it on screen, at 60K-140K views each.
  • Community conversion: Skool community of 1,551; the workflow taught in the community is the product workflow.
  • Search comparison pages: /vs/vidiq and /vs/tubebuddy intercept competitor keywords (non-brand search is currently 0, so the effect is still small).
  • Early referral code (?ref=VONEIG542BEA) indicates an affiliate/referral mechanism.

Speculation:

  • "Monetization screenshots" students produce with the tool flow back as material for the creator's new videos, forming a "tutorial -> tool -> student results -> tutorial" loop.
  • The high Australia/Canada share may relate to where the creator or team is based or the audience makeup (unverified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue. Chrome extension about 1,000 users; official channel 1.31K subscribers.
  • Main audience pool: Romayroh's 183K subscribers and 1,551 Skool members. At $9.99-29.99/mo, a few hundred to a thousand paying users would mean monthly revenue in the thousands to tens of thousands of dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The YouTube links in search results were valuable (descriptions and view counts read one by one); the rest were irrelevant generic articles. Main evidence from YouTube page data, Chrome Web Store/ExtWise, terms of service, sitemap, RDAP, Wayback.
  • VidNinjas belonging to Romayroh is inferred from the "VFI Starter INC" / "Views For Income" name match and the vidIQ swap in his toolbar; no explicit statement seen.
  • Gaps: number of paying users, why AU/CA traffic dominates.
