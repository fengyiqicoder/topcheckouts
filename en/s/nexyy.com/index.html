Basics

  • Product: a search directory of avatars, assets and worlds for VRChat and other VR platforms. Users click into an item's detail page and are sent to the original shop to buy; nothing is sold on the site. Has accounts, favorites lists, browsing history, and creators claiming their shops (connect Gumroad to import items).
  • Source platforms: the official help page (updated 2026-03-15) says it aggregates Gumroad, Sellfy, Payhip, Shopify, Booth (the main VRChat avatar marketplace in Japan, owned by pixiv), plus VCC Packages.
  • Version: v1.17.0-alpha; the footer says only "© 2026 Nexyy" with no company info; the help page does not state the operator, launch date or revenue model; the three social icons in the footer all link to "#", so there is no public Discord or social account.
  • Operator: most likely the VRChat avatar author Nexyy (VRChat name NeXuS, Gumroad shop lunanexus.gumroad.com, works include Vee and Miyuki, plus the public world "Nexyy's Avatar World"). An individual or small-team project; country Not found (English ToS, the "US and other countries" wording is template language). Funding: Not found, looks self-funded.
  • Domain: WHOIS created 2023-06-21 (the earlier domain of the same name had other snapshots in 2018–2021, expired and re-registered). The earliest changelog entry, v1.2.0-alpha, is 2024-06-01, so it launched around the first half of 2024.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • The site has a large number of indexable pages: /browse?q=..., /browse?tag=..., and /<shop>/<item> SEO landing pages (pages like "Browse 'vrchat' in Avatars..." can be seen indexed in search results).
  • Users can create and share item lists (the ToS says "create lists of products and share them with others"); v1.3.0 fixed the og image when sharing a profile on Discord, showing users paste links into Discord.
  • Creators can claim shops and connect Gumroad (a supply-side driver: authors join on their own for exposure).
  • Speculation:
  • New avatar authors share their nexyy item page as a "second showcase", forming a loop of creator supply → exposure → buyers → more authors joining.
  • Main growth is still Google long-tail and word of mouth among VRChat players (our top three channels are Direct/Organic Search/Referrals).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No paid tier or ad info seen; revenue model Not found (possibly affiliate or promoted placements, unverified).
  • Our data: about 60–110K visits per month sent to Gumroad and other checkout pages.
  • User count and item count: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: the 5 sets of searches provided this time returned almost nothing relevant (Reddit's u/NexYY is a 2014 Notes user unrelated to this site; the "viral" search found nexy_ybzz, an unrelated Instagram account). The only new findings: the help page confirms Shopify and Booth were added as source platforms, the site has no public social accounts, and the changelog filled in v1.4–v1.8. Where the first users came from and the takeoff event are still Not found; the takeoff method "Long-tail search" is judged from channel data (search 52.9%, non-brand about 21%, such as "vrchat nsfw avatars" and "emo vrchat avatar"), with no first-hand account.
  • The operator's identity is inferred from the avatar author of the same name, with no about page to confirm it.
  • The Reddit API is blocked, and no launch posts were found on X or Discord.
  • The launch and feature timeline comes from the official changelog, high credibility; the growth channels rest mainly on Speculation.
