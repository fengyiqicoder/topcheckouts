Overview

Nexyy (nexyy.com): Search directory for VRChat avatars, worlds and assets sold on Gumroad, Payhip, Sellfy. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 91,662 visits went from Nexyy to a checkout payment page — #16 of the 607 sites we track. That is 60% up from the April–June monthly average of 57,462.

Total website visits that month were about 88,560 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: search 53%, direct 47%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 21%, with queries like “vrchat nsfw avatars”, “emo vrchat avatar”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: one VRChat model ~ US$29 (set by third-party creators).
