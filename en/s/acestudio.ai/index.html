Basics

  • Product: desktop AI music production software: 140+ AI singers, 8 languages, AI instruments, voice cloning, voice conversion, stem separation, video scoring, DAW plugin integration; the vocals are trained on recordings of human singers paid for licensing (the site stresses "artists get revenue share").
  • Company: TimeDomain. The title of the official about page reads "AI Music Technology by TimeDomain", with a team of 60+ and creators in 100+ countries; partners include EastWest Sounds (instrument sampling) and AMD (AI infrastructure); professional audio media such as Sound On Sound and Mixdown have reviewed it. Founder Joe Guo: the Product Hunt launch comment is signed "Joe, founder of ACE Studio", LinkedIn account jingguoai, who posted on 2025-12-08 asking for votes; on vi-control (a professional composing and sound library forum) there is a post "Hi, this is Joe, the builder of acestudio.ai", showing he personally cold-started in composer forums early on (the post returned 403 and did not open, date unknown). The founder, country of registration and funding could not be verified this time (WebSearch quota ran out, Baidu Baike 403). Based on the team's Chinese background (e.g. the partner trumpeter Li Xiaochuan), it is speculated to be a Chinese team going overseas.
  • Launch: domain acestudio.ai registered 2022-10-19; earliest Wayback snapshot 2022-10-24 (301), a normal homepage from 2023-09.
  • Pricing: Artist $16.58/month (annual), Artist Pro $22/month (annual), a "two-year rent-to-own" model; 7-day full refund; team/education editions available.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • The open-source model ACE-Step brings the brand exposure among developers and the AI scene.
  • SEO content matrix (music production explainers) + paid search.
  • Human instrumentalists/singers as "voice library providers" for personal-story marketing.
  • YouTube producer tutorials and reviews are the early overseas acquisition channel (2023-09 official tutorial, 2024-02 a Reddit user says they learned of it from YouTube).
  • Free mini tools occupy search terms: vocal remover, bpm finder, plus the competitor term suno; vocalremover.org referrals are 2.69%.
  • Traditional music software distribution (Music Marketing reseller network, since 2025-11) and the Product Hunt 2.0 launch.
  • Chinese media referral: sina.com.cn is 3.03%.
  • Speculation:
  • Users (musicians, cover-song creators) label their releases "vocal by ACE Studio", creating branded-output spread on YouTube/Bilibili/TikTok; YouTube producer reviews/tutorial videos were the main early channel (common for similar vocal-tuning software, not directly verified).
  • Early users migrated from the Chinese virtual singer / vocal tuning scene.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue or user count; team of 60+.
  • Pricing $16-22/month (annual), aimed at professional/semi-professional producers.
