Overview

Acestudio (acestudio.ai): All-in-one AI music production studio with AI vocals, instruments, voice cloning and stem splitting. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 25,788 visits went from Acestudio to a checkout payment page — #110 of the 607 sites we track. That is 19% down from the April–June monthly average of 31,883.

Total website visits that month were about 477,624 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 5.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 51%, direct 44%, social 4%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 30%, with queries like “vocal remover”, “suno”, “bpm finder”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
