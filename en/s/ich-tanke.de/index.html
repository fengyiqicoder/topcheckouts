In 2013 Germany's Federal Cartel Office (MTS-K) required gas stations to report fuel prices within 5 minutes and opened the data. In November 2014 NOLIS GmbH, a company in municipal digitalization, used that window to launch ich-tanke.de, grabbing the "Benzinpreise + place name" long tail with programmatic pages for districts / counties / cities / motorways / federal roads, and made it a commonly used fuel price site in the German-speaking region. In August 2025 it was bought by 1337 UGC GmbH (30M+ monthly users), which builds a matrix by acquiring existing traffic sites; the playbook since the takeover is ad monetization plus technology and content upgrades. The checkout traffic that appeared from three months ago is a new monetization attempt after the acquisition (speculation), not a renewed takeoff.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: German fuel price comparison; find the cheapest nearby E5 / E10 / diesel by postcode or place name, with a map, statistics, a no-signup watchlist (Merkliste), an FAQ, and Android / iOS apps (iOS app id6811966302, developer 1337 UGC GmbH, 67 ratings, average 4.6). Data comes from the Cartel Office's Markttransparenzstelle für Kraftstoffe.
  • Founder: NOLIS GmbH, whose main business is municipal digitalization, serving e-government for more than 400 German towns; ich-tanke is its side product (launched 2014-11).
  • Current operator: 1337 UGC GmbH, Schwanstetten (near Nuremberg), HRB 28738; managing directors Dominik Jaworski and Christoph Kilz. Its sites include Woxikon, Wort-Suchen, Einfach-Sparsam, Kreuzworträtsel, MeinTurnierplan and others, and it claims 30M+ monthly users and 200M+ monthly page views; the group also has a fuel price site, Tankpreise (launched 2024).
  • Monetization: the footer has "Mediadaten/Werbung" (advertising) and "Widerruf" (withdrawal) links; no paid plan seen on the homepage (verified via WebFetch this time).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Programmatic SEO pages (regional, autobahnen, tankstellen pages by coordinates); non-brand search terms benzinpreise, benzinpreise in der nähe, diesel preis.
  • Direct visits 63.5%: returning users come back through bookmarks / Merkliste / the app.
  • Referrals from tankstellenpreise.de, wetter.com and focus.de (our channel data), i.e. backlinks from media and portal sites.
  • Acquired by a traffic-matrix company in 2025; it now relies on the group's SEO and ad operations capabilities.
  • Speculation:
  • The first users came from the search long tail after the fuel price data opened in 2014 (few competitors then) + backlinks when media reported on fuel prices; NOLIS has 400+ municipal clients, but whether it drove traffic from town portals is Not found.
  • Every fuel price rise in the news (the 2022 energy crisis, prices breaking €2 in 2026) causes search volume to jump. The product has no mechanism for acquiring new users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The parent company has 30M+ monthly users, 200M+ monthly page views and about 30 sites; ich-tanke's standalone traffic and the purchase price are not disclosed.
  • Monetization is mainly display ads; in our data checkout traffic appears from 2026-07, and what is being sold is Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • High (launch date, founder and acquisition are all backed by dpa-AFX/OTS press releases).
  • Gaps: how it was promoted during the NOLIS era in 2014–2025, the source of the first 1,000 users, and what the checkout page sells.
  • Second follow-up check: (1) the old report said "whether it was acquired or self-built: Not found", now confirmed: created by NOLIS GmbH in 2014-11, acquired by 1337 UGC on 2025-08-08; (2) the launch time was narrowed from "2014 (official statement)" to 2014-11; (3) added Trustpilot reviews (poor servers before the acquisition, inaccurate prices in 2026); (4) updated the parent company's scale to 30M+ monthly users (press release figure).
