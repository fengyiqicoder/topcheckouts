Basics

  • Product: Wron ("Build apps with AI"), generates native mobile apps and websites from chat, with phone preview, code editing, RevenueCat/Supabase integration and publishing to the App Store / Google Play; bundled with Niche Finder (app download and revenue tracking, rankings) and a Winning Ads Library (a library of high-performing ad formats).
  • Pricing: a third-party report from 2026-06 recorded "Indie 100" from $25/month (20% off annual), up to 3,200 credits/month; the current site shows annual "discounted" prices of Starter $4.99, Builder $6.99, Studio $29.99/month (struck-out original prices $24.99/$34.99/$149.99), plus a Lifetime option.
  • Team/company: the site and terms only say "Wron", contact support@wron.ai, terms governed by the law of Georgia, USA; the Trustpilot profile is unclaimed and lists the location as Anguilla (probably just inferred from the.ai domain); founders, legal entity and funding all undisclosed, no record on Crunchbase/AngelList (CGEN report, 2026-06).
  • Launch: domain WHOIS registered 2026-04-15; privacy policy updated 2026-06-17, terms 2026-09-22. Wayback was inaccessible this time.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid ads and influencers: Trustpilot users explicitly mention TikTok ads, Facebook videos and influencer recommendations; the timing matches the three months ago traffic jump. The product ships with a "winning ads library", showing the team itself knows the paid-media playbook.
  • Pricing conversion: permanent deep struck-out discounts with annual lock-in, plus a Lifetime option.
  • The homepage's "See what people are building with Wron" wall of user projects, used as social proof.
  • Profile: social sources include X, YouTube and WhatsApp; 88.6% of search is brand keywords (users search the brand name after seeing ads).

Speculation:

  • India and Egypt account for a large share of traffic and WhatsApp appears among social sources, suggesting influencer or affiliate promotion aimed at the "make money with AI side hustles" crowd in those two countries (unverified).
  • Apps users generate carry no Wron branding when published to app stores, so in-product virality is weak.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Our data: 51,576 checkout visits last month, about 23x the 4–6 months ago average (2,267).
  • Only about 12 Trustpilot reviews, mostly negative, with refund disputes; retention may be weak (speculation).
  • User numbers and revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • "Took off on paid ads and influencers" is triangulated from the zero-exposure baseline in the 2026-06 third-party report, Trustpilot users' self-reports from three months ago onward, and our traffic curve; medium credibility. The specific influencer or ad has not been found.
  • Founders and legal entity remain completely blank.
  • Second-pass check: takeoff method changed from "Not found" to "Paid ads" and growth engine from "Not found" to "Paid ads" (based on Trustpilot users mentioning TikTok ads, Facebook videos and influencers); added the zero-exposure baseline from the 2026-06 CGEN report and the $25/month pricing at that time.
