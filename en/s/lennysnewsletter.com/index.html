Basics

  • Product: a paid newsletter on Substack (product, growth, career, leaning AI in recent years), plus Lenny's Podcast, a private Slack community (30,000+ members), Lenny's Jobs (with TrueUp), in-person Meetups (Luma) and the Product Pass software perks bundle.
  • Who made it: Lenny Rachitsky, 7 years at Airbnb (growth, product lead), angel investor. As of 2025-03 there were no full-time employees; editing/proofreading/design/podcast production/community lead/assistant/engineer were all contractors or part-time (the "1,000,000" post).
  • Launch date: WHOIS domain created 2020-02-26; the earliest post date in the Substack archive is 2019-06-14 (old posts migrated from Medium), and the first issue "This Week #0" is 2019-09-12; Wayback captures the standalone domain from 2020-11.
  • Pricing: first paid tier in 2020-04 at $15/month, $150/year (33% off for the first 48 hours); now $20/month, $200/year; Insider $400/year (Folio review, 2026-04).
  • Traffic (profile file): roughly 4,018 visits in 2026-09 (this data source clearly underestimates, since most reading happens in the inbox); search 49%, social from LinkedIn, X and YouTube.

Where the first users came from (author's own account): the first 100 were Twitter followers from the Airbnb article; 100 -> 1,000 came from guest posts on First Round Review and Andrew Chen's blog; 1,000 -> 10,000 was nine months of weekly writing + Twitter summaries + word of mouth.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Content quality -> readers forward it / word of mouth; the author calls this the main driver (the "1,000,000" post).
  • Substack's recommendation network: 9,000+ other newsletters (2023) recommend him; the hockey-stick inflection coincided with the feature's launch, and he estimates he would be at roughly 500K without it.
  • The newsletter spun up the podcast, podcast revenue overtook the newsletter, and the podcast feeds subscriptions in return.
  • The Product Pass perks bundle did not accelerate free sign-ups but clearly lifted paid conversion.
  • Paid ads, SEO, referral rewards and BD all "didn't work" (author's words).

Speculation:

  • Q&A from the paid Slack community is compiled into public "Community Wisdom" posts, forming a content-supply loop.
  • Company expensing + a real need for career development make $200/year an easy sell to PMs.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2023-04: about 18,000 paid, about $2M/year in subscription revenue (Folio citing reports, unverified).
  • 2025-03: 1M subscribers, podcast revenue larger than the newsletter; 2026: 1.2M+ subscribers, 30,000+ in Slack.
  • The team is entirely contractors, so nearly all profit goes to the individual.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The main evidence is the author's own milestone posts (500,000, 1,000,000, the paid announcement, issue #0), so high confidence.
  • The Fast Company piece returned 403, summary only; the specific month Substack's recommendations feature launched is from memory.
  • The profile file's 4,018 monthly visits is a serious underestimate (the newsletter is mostly read in the inbox).
