Basics

  • Product: two lines. (1) Main business: D Story Productions Limited, Hong Kong, corporate storytelling and presentation design, data visualization, video, training; says it has served 50+ Asia-Pacific brands (Standard Chartered, Nike, Bupa, Manulife and others), and the team has lectured at Oxford, Imperial and HKUST. (2) Templates: dstory.co/templates lists about 52 pop-culture-themed PPT templates, sold/distributed through Gumroad (page button "Get Templates on Gumroad"), plus a presentation design course (beacons.ai summary).
  • Price: not found (the Gumroad page was not fetched); the flagship Genshin Impact template has 1.7K ratings and Spotify Wrapped has 456 ratings.
  • Team: the site only says "small team", and a client testimonial mentions Barney leading the team; the founder's name is unverified.
  • Users: traffic is US 62%, Philippines 13%, Indonesia 10% - template users are English-speaking and Southeast Asian students/young people, completely different from the clients of the Hong Kong consulting business.

Where the first users came from: TikTok and Instagram short videos - each video shows a pop-culture-themed PPT and directs viewers to "download from the link in the profile". No earlier launch post or Reddit/PH launch was found, and the specific numbers for the first users were not found.
Where new users come from: still short videos (riding trends like new K-pop groups, new games and new movie releases to make templates) + Pinterest (the main social traffic) + searches for themed long-tail terms like "dstory templates ppt" and "template ppt my melody".

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Content is the product: each video is a template showcase, with the comments/profile link pointing to the Gumroad download; Buzzlytics records an average of about 250K views and a 15% engagement rate.
  • Trend-riding topic selection: K-pop group comebacks, new games, the Barbie movie and Apple Vision Pro were all turned into templates.
  • Search + Pinterest: the profile shows search at 48%, with Pinterest the main social source.
  • Speculation: free templates ("Get the free ILLIT template") exchange for emails/follows, with paid templates and courses monetizing; students use the templates in class and classmates who see them may ask about them, but there is no evidence. The template line also gives brand exposure to the consulting main business (the site claims content has reached 130 million people).

Revenue and scale clues

  • A single Gumroad item has at most 1.7K ratings, suggesting downloads of at least tens of thousands (speculation). Buzzlytics estimates the price of one sponsored video at $10.9K-16.3K. Actual revenue was not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Social follower counts and template page content are highly credible; the 2023 takeoff year is inferred from the template page's first capture; the TikTok account's creation date and the first viral video were not found; the founder's name, template prices and revenue are all missing.
  • New site, written from scratch.
