Overview

Racecut (racecut.app): Browser tool turning any song into an AI-voiced marble-race video for TikTok, Shorts and Reels. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Racecut to a checkout payment page — #364 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 12,708 (estimated from global rank). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: search 48%, direct 36%, referrals/email/paid 10%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows branded free output, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 74%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $1 first month; $24.99/mo after.
