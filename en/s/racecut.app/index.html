Basics

  • Product: makes "AI-voiced marble race" videos in the browser: upload a song, name the marbles and assign AI voices, it automatically arranges the race by beat / chorus and exports a 1080p, 60fps vertical MP4, which can be scheduled to TikTok, Shorts and Reels; the whole site supports Spanish.
  • Pricing: Plus is $1 for the first month, then $6.99/month (15 videos a month, up to 4 voices); Unlimited is $5 for the first month, then $24.99/month (unlimited, 8 voices); paid versions have no watermark.
  • Claims: the marketing page says "5,000+ creators" and "As seen all over TikTok."
  • Team: footer says "© 2026 RaceCut," contact hello@racecut.app, has a Discord; founder not found.
  • Social: Instagram @racecut.app (3,001 followers / 72 posts at the time of the search snippet, 6,472 followers when fetched on 2026-10-25), TikTok @racecut.app, YouTube Shorts.
  • Our data: Mexico 67.4%, Argentina 32.6%; search 48.3% (brand keywords 74.3%, non-brand such as marble race ia), direct 35.6%, referral 9.7%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Format-driven: the product does only one format already popular on TikTok, and the marketing page says outright "Make the marble race videos you keep seeing."
  • Creator-led selling: dancoversia's videos repeatedly say "made with @racecut.app, link in bio," with a Spanish how-to tutorial; the official account's posts are co-posted with him.
  • Brand keyword search is the main entry (brand keywords 74% in our data), showing users see a video and then search the name to arrive.
  • The low $1/$5 first month lowers the barrier to pay.

Speculation:

  • dancoversia may be the founder's own account or a partner creator on a revenue share (many co-posts), not verified.
  • Free/trial exports carry a watermark, so videos users post carry the racecut brand, forming a loop of "watch video -> search the brand -> make your own -> post again"; the profile record mentions "non-unlimited plans carry a watermark," while the site now only says paid versions have no watermark, and the free tier details were not found.
  • The market is only Mexico and Argentina, and the surge depends on the heat of this format on Spanish-language TikTok, so traffic risk is high once the format fades.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 5,000+ creators. No revenue data. Assuming a 5-10% paying rate and an average of $10/month, monthly revenue would be about $2.5-5K (pure speculation, order of magnitude only).
  • Instagram followers roughly doubled within a month (3,001 -> 6,472).

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site, written from scratch. The timeline relies on RDAP, search snippets and the official site; the original text of TikTok/Instagram posts could not be fetched and is marked "summary only."
  • Most of the search results were irrelevant (CVEs, CapCut alternatives, etc.).
  • Gaps: the founder's identity, dancoversia's relationship to the product, the exact launch date, the free-tier watermark rules, revenue.
