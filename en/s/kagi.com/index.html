Overview

Kagi (kagi.com): Subscription-funded ad-free search engine with AI assistant and browser. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,691 visits went from Kagi to a checkout payment page — #144 of the 607 sites we track. That is 350% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,933.

Total website visits that month were about 1,431,215 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 96%, search 3%, social 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 96%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: $5/mo Starter (300 searches); $10/mo Professional; $25/mo Ultimate; annual -1.
