Basics

  • Product: research and "packaging" tool for YouTube creators: finds outlier videos that vastly outperform their channel average among tens of millions of videos, tracks by niche/competitor, generates topics, titles and AI thumbnails; web + free Chrome extension + API/MCP.
  • Founders: Richard Do (co-founder/CEO, former MrBeast content strategist; his agency is now 1of10's strategy division, hosts the 1of10 Podcast); Riad El Otmani (co-founder/CTO, built the first version, formerly at Microsoft and Thales Alenia Space, ran his own YouTube channel of about 40K subscribers). Sources: https://1of10.com/about and a third-party wiki.
  • Pricing: Free $0 (60 credits) / Basic $19/month / Pro $89/month / Ultra $4,999/year (compiled by OutlierKit 2026-09). In early 2024 there was no free tier and $50–$350 had to be prepaid (Reddit user complaints), with a $1 first-month promo.
  • Domain: 1of10.com registered 2014-04-28 (old domain, a redirect/blank page for years before); first product page on Wayback 2024-01-29 ("1of10 Finder", with leftover French "Installer sur chrome", suggesting the CTO has a French-speaking background).
  • Traffic: United States 41%, India 7%, Canada 6%; direct 68%, search 21% (brand 80%), social 11% (YouTube, X, Instagram).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder as the brand: Richard Do's X posts, podcast and Skool community keep pushing the "outlier methodology", and the tool is that methodology made concrete.
  • Affiliate commissions: Rewardful 30% commission, with YouTubers and podcasters (/affiliate/podcast landing page) promoting via links; Reddit users repeatedly mention "YouTuber recommendations" and "affiliate ads".
  • Sponsoring creator-economy media: sponsored tweets on the official Colin & Samir X account (before 2024-02).
  • Free Chrome extension + free tier: shows the outlier multiplier directly on YouTube pages, funneling to paid.
  • Agency business: client case studies from the strategy division feed product and marketing.

Speculation:

  • Social traffic 11% (YouTube/X/Instagram) and direct 68% mainly reflect brand awareness driven by creator content, not built-in product sharing (the output is thumbnails/titles, which carry no branding).
