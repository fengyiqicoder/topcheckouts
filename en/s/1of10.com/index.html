Overview

1of10 (1of10.com): AI SaaS for YouTube creators that finds outlier videos and generates ideas, titles and thumbnails. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from 1of10 to a checkout payment page — #285 of the 607 sites we track. That is 55% up from the April–June monthly average of 8,309.

Total website visits that month were about 126,814 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 10.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 68%, search 21%, social 11%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows branded free output, referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 80%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
