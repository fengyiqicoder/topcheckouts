Overview

Zanima (zanima.me): Slovenian news and commentary site with analyses, opinion pieces and video. Category on TopCheckouts: Media.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Zanima to a checkout payment page — #420 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 233,281 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 3.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 68%, search 24%, social 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 10%, with queries like “tina gaber”, “rončević”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
