Basics

  • Product: a daily Slovenian-language news and commentary site (current affairs, analysis, opinion, "news with commentary", video), some articles paywalled; the YouTube channel @zanimame_slo carries the weekly podcast "Tedenski safari" and election livestreams.
  • Who built it: Zanima.me d.o.o. (Brezovica pri Ljubljani), founder and director Igor Vovk (media strategist), responsible editor Rok Čakš, video editor Mirko Mayer (veteran TV journalist), reporters Peter Merše, Andreja Barat and others; guest columnists include Žiga Turk (former minister, from memory, unverified). The 2024 placeholder page was signed "Zavod Iskreni". Rok Čakš was previously an editor at the right-wing weekly Reporter (from memory, unverified).
  • Pricing: subscription from €69/year; during the pre-sale there was a "Gold supporter €250/year" tier.
  • Domain: the.me domain was registered 2007-09-24 (WHOIS), and the 2010-2018 snapshots belong to a previous holder; the 2024-12 snapshot is a GoDaddy site-builder placeholder, "Razvijamo nekaj navdihujočega" ("we're developing something inspiring").
  • Traffic (profile data): Slovenia 97.7%; direct 67.7%, search 24.2% (brand keywords 82.2%, non-brand are newsmaker names); referrals from rtvslo.si 7.5%, skyscrapercity.com 7.4%, nova24tv.si 3.4%; social 4.8% (X, YouTube, Facebook).

Where the first users came from: the founding journalists' existing readers/viewers, plus a YouTube podcast head start of 8 months (evidence: the podcast started in 2024-09, the website launched in 2025-05, and the gap was funded by pre-sale subscriptions).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Podcast/video first: a weekly YouTube podcast + famous guests + election livestreams.
  • Reader subscription pre-sale: recruited paying supporters before launch.
  • Longtime readers return directly (direct 67.7%).
  • Citations from other media and forums: referrals from rtvslo.si (the national broadcaster), nova24tv.si (a right-wing TV channel) and the skyscrapercity forum.

Speculation:

  • Political news hooks (elections, politicians) bring non-brand search and social sharing; mutual citation with the right-wing media circle such as Nova24TV.
  • Slovenia has about 2.1 million people, so the market ceiling is very low and growth mainly comes from deepening the existing political audience.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscription from €69/year; subscriber count not published. With 11.5k YouTube subscribers, paying subscribers are presumably within the low thousands (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. The search query "zanima me" is a common phrase meaning "I want to know" in the South Slavic languages, and the reddit results were all irrelevant; evidence comes from the official About page, Slovenian Wikipedia, the Wayback placeholder and pre-sale pages, and YouTube about data.
  • Subscriber count, revenue and funding source (whether there is a party or capital backer) were not found; the journalists' previous employers are partly from memory and unverified.
