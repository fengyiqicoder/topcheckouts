Luis Monge Malo had been writing a web design/SEO/marketing blog at mongemalo.es since 2008 (then as founder of Clever Consulting). In 2018 he moved to mongemalo.com and rewrote it as a "sales" blog (as sales director at Entradium, running sales training for companies). Around 2020–2021 he cut the blog down to a mailing list with "just one subscription box", going from one email a week to one a day, using provocative, sex-tinged headlines ("cuando hablo de ventas hablo de ligar") to drive traffic to the email from YouTube / Facebook (trucosdeventas) / LinkedIn, then selling a roughly €450 sales course in every email. Basics

  • Product: a Spanish-language daily sales/marketing email (free subscription) that sells courses inside the emails, such as the "objeciones" (objection handling) course: 4.5 hours of video + a 68-page manual + 3 months of Q&A, €450 including tax (https://mongemalo.com/curso-objeciones/).
  • Founder: Luis Monge Malo, Spanish, describes himself as "Ingeniero que ama las ventas"; around 2013 he was founder of Clever Consulting (web design/development/online marketing); in 2019 the site said "Director comercial de entradium" and he ran sales training for companies (Wayback).
  • Scale: the About page claims "ya se han apuntado 15 000 y pico personas" and says he "started 12 years ago" (https://mongemalo.com/acerca-de-mi/).
  • Domain: mongemalo.com registered 2012-05-11 (RDAP); in 2018-08 Wayback shows a 301 to mongemalo.es; the earliest Wayback for mongemalo.es is 2008-09.
  • Traffic (profile): Spain 59%, Mexico 7%; direct 44.5%, search 41.7%, email 8.3%; brand keywords 77.8%; social from YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn.

Where the first users came from: the evidence points to the Spanish-language marketing and web professionals who followed his blog at mongemalo.es since 2008; when the whole blog moved to.com in 2018 it inherited that audience, plus contacts from his time as Entradium sales director and corporate trainer (speculation). No external coverage, Reddit posts or launch posts were found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free daily email → high-priced course sold inside the email (the €450 course page) → the email subjects themselves become social content material.
  • Social traffic: the YouTube channel bio reads "Vendo cantidades exageradas de cualquier cosa desde playas exóticas. Cuento cómo en mongemalo.com"; the Facebook page "Cada día un consejo de ventas"; LinkedIn carries the same tagline.
  • Search: old blog posts (such as provocative ones like "los 10 mandamientos del satanismo") still bring non-brand search.

Speculation:

  • Provocative, edgy headlines generate shares and discussion; this is the Spanish-language "personal brand daily email" model, and growth mainly depends on the author's continuous output rather than the product spreading itself.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 15,000+ subscribers (About page).
  • Single course €450; the course page says the author "sold over €1 million in a 20-minute conversation" (marketing copy, unverified).
  • No revenue figures or team size seen; speculated to be a one-person business.

Confidence and gaps

  • The site is written from scratch. The evolution (.es design blog →.com sales blog → weekly email → daily provocative email) is supported by year-by-year Wayback snapshots, so credibility is medium-high.
  • Gaps: no external interviews or direct statement on where the first subscribers came from; the search results are almost entirely unrelated; the subscriber growth curve and course sales were not found. Facebook/YouTube follower counts were not verified.
