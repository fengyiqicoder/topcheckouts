Overview

Voicedub (voicedub.ai): Browser-based AI studio that swaps song vocals for AI voices, with voice cloning, duets and free vocal remover. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Voicedub to a checkout payment page — #400 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 78,216 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 11.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 57%, direct 39%, AI assistants 4%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 62%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
