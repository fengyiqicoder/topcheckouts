Overview

Prompts (prompts.chat): Free open-source community platform for sharing, discovering and collecting AI prompts, skills and workflows. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 7,868 visits went from Prompts to a checkout payment page — #488 of the 607 sites we track. That is 32% up from the April–June monthly average of 5,976.

Total website visits that month were about 148,085 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 5.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 63%, referrals/email/paid 28%, search 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows open source, public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 69%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: free (GitHub Sponsors link, Gumroad book price not shown).
