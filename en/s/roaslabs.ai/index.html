Basics

  • Products:
  • Platform: agency ad accounts (whitelisted Meta, Google, TikTok accounts) + a control panel (top-ups, monitoring; the sample dashboard shows 337 active accounts and 131 banned accounts).
  • ROAS AI: account scoring and action recommendations, said to be trained on "$600M+ of ad-spend data".
  • ROAS Academy: media-buying playbook course for €47, portal academy.roaslabs.ai.
  • ROAS Agency: fully managed ad buying (onepager).
  • Pricing: account service not public, by application, "no long-term contract, cancel anytime"; course €47.
  • Self-reported figures (unverified and inconsistent): About page says 5,000+ advertisers, $600M+ managed spend, 12+ years' experience; the onepager says 5,000+ members, $900M+ spend, 150+ countries; claims official Meta/TikTok/Google partner status (badges with no verifiable links). The three testimonials on the onepager (Sophie van den Berg, etc.) have names and companies that cannot be verified and look templated.
  • Team: Amsterdam address; no founder named on the site. Telegram contact t.me/vincentvandenb shows the name Vincent van den Brink; X @roaslabsai is "Ed @ ROASLabs.ai", bio "Co-Founder of an AI-powered advertising infrastructure", registered 2026-03-30, 3 tweets, 0 followers.

  • Domain: registered 2026-01-30 (RDAP); first Wayback 2026-04-02.
  • Traffic (profile): 4,824 visits in 2026-09, +75.5% MoM; US 54.7%, Netherlands 16.9%, Italy 12.4%; direct 66.4%, referral 25.2%; brand keywords 66.4% (roaslabs, roas labs portal), plus intent terms like agency ad account meta.

Where the first users came from: no public launch post, press or Product Hunt record; social media has almost no followers (X 0, Instagram 194), so the first clients can only have come from the founders' existing relationships in the media-buyer/e-commerce scene and private communities (Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, Facebook groups); the profile indeed shows Discord and Facebook as the social sources. "12+ years' experience" implies the team previously ran an ad agency or account business; the specific predecessor was not found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Private-community closing: the site only offers an application form, Calendly, Telegram and WhatsApp → one-on-one sales.
  • Low-price front end: €47 course + AI diagnosis tool → filters out high-spend buyers → converts them into agency-account clients (the course page also carries a Calendly booking link).
  • SEO posts and "meta/google/tiktok agency ad accounts" landing pages capture intent terms.

Speculation:

  • The agency-account business takes a cut of top-ups (industry norm 2–6%, from memory, unverified); the more clients spend the more revenue, and existing clients referring new ones is the main growth path.
  • Recommendations and kickbacks in media-buyer Discord/Telegram groups are the main channel (common in the industry, but no evidence on this site).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No credible revenue data. The site's figures (5,000+ clients, $600M–900M spend) are badly mismatched with its near-zero social presence; they may be repackaged from the team's past experience or industry data.
  • Sample dashboard: $20,289 total spend, 337 active accounts (illustration).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were all irrelevant (health, SQL tools, etc.); this report relies on the site, sitemap, RDAP, Wayback, fxtwitter and Telegram/Instagram metadata.
  • Self-reported figures and testimonials have low credibility; "agency ad accounts" sit in a grey area of platform policy (helping banned or high-risk buyers get around bans), which is worth noting.
  • Gaps: founders' real backgrounds and predecessor business; Dutch KvK registration; fee model; which specific communities the clients come from.
