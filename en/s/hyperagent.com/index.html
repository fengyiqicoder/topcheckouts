Basics

  • Product: an AI Agent platform you can "hire", producing websites, slide decks, documents, videos and dashboards; each session gets its own VM (filesystem, browser, shell); Skills (reusable workflows), Rubrics (LLM grading); Slack/Telegram/webhook/scheduled triggers; Workspaces for team sharing; connects to a ChatGPT subscription; iOS app (2026-09).
  • Company: the Airtable team (TikTok bio "By the team at Airtable"; led by Howie Liu); the Miraheze wiki records it as Hyperagent, Inc. (West Hollywood, California). On 2026-08-04 Bending Spoons announced a $1.285B acquisition of Airtable, completed in 2026-09.
  • Domain: hyperagent.com is an old domain registered in 2005; the first snapshot of the new site is 2026-02-20.
  • Pricing: usage-based; a free tier (low-cost models); paid monthly budgets of $20–$10,000, with higher tiers giving more bonus ($100→$115 … $10,000→$14,500).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Cold start relied on the parent company: the Airtable brand and Howie Liu's personal exposure (CNBC, Substack, podcasts); the PH/HN launches got a lukewarm response.
  • Giving away credits to acquire users: $1,000 for signing up/referring; Founding 500 gives $20K each; a $100K contest. Users post many referral links to earn credits (about 70% of Reddit comments are referral links, the author estimates).
  • Creator partnerships: giving influencers compute credits to pass on to followers (Ms. Anti Work, Valerie Chapman, Marina Mogilko, Greg Isenberg demos).
  • Paid ads: paid traffic about 39% (Similarweb profile).
  • Catching competitor buzz: OpenClaw migration guide, search terms including openclaw/manus.
  • Spread within teams: Workspaces share Agents.
  • Speculation:
  • Websites/slide decks produced by the Agent carry branded outbound links (unverified).
  • The traffic decline after 2026-08 may be related to cutting credit giveaways/ad spend after the acquisition.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public Hyperagent revenue or user count. Checkout visits averaged 100K+ per month in April–July, peaked at 177K in July, and fell to 26K in September.
  • High acquisition cost: nominal credits of $1,000 per new user (actual consumption far lower) + paid ads.
  • Airtable overall: 450K+ organizations, valued at $1.285B.

Confidence and gaps

  • The credit giveaways and creator partnerships are corroborated by multiple sources (dev.to, agentical.blog, RevenueBrew); "70% of comments are referral links" is a blogger's estimate.
  • The specific start date of the referral program and the scale of paid ad spend were Not found; what happens to Hyperagent after the acquisition was Not found.
  • Second follow-up check: added the 2026-01-29 Superagent PH launch, Howie Liu's CNBC/Substack buildup, the $1,000 referral credits and the Founding 500 $20K credits, and the creator credit-giveaway partnerships; growth engine changed from "Paid ads" to "Referral rewards or affiliates" (paid ads remain the second-largest source).
