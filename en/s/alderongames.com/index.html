Overview

Alderongames (alderongames.com): Path of Titans, a dinosaur MMO. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 47,278 visits went from Alderongames to a checkout payment page — #45 of the 607 sites we track. That is 1% down from the April–June monthly average of 47,617.

Total website visits that month were about 211,903 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 22.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 57%, referrals/email/paid 28%, search 15%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows multiplayer rooms, referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 63%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
