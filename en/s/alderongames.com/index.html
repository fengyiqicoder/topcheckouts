Basics

  • Product: Path of Titans, a survival MMO where you play as dinosaurs, up to 200 players per server, 30+ dinosaurs and hundreds of unlockable skins, with mod support. Cross-play across Windows/macOS/Linux, PS4/5, Xbox, Switch, iOS and Android. Priced at USD 30 / EUR 30 (Founder's Pack on the official site).
  • Company: Alderon Games Pty Ltd, an indie studio in Victoria, Australia, founded 2016-05-26. Founder and project lead Matthew Cassells, co-founders Rebecca Slater (art) and Connor S. Parks (tech). Team size is not public; a press release says "almost all members started out as mod authors".
  • Funding: no venture capital found. The 2019 Indiegogo crowdfunding (flexible goal) raised about $63.9K against a $24.4K goal. Another account says close to AUD 100K, and the wiki gives 1,990 backers and $64,710.
  • Launch: the alderongames.com domain was registered 2011-08-19, and the earliest Wayback snapshot is 2013-08-26 (the Breaking Point era). Path of Titans was announced in 2019-01, backers got early access at the end of 2019, early access and a demo were offered on the PC self-built launcher in 2020, it reached consoles and Switch in July–August 2022 (Game Preview / Founder's Pack), and the mobile version launched the same year.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral program: generate a referral code in the in-game Refer A Friend menu, or have the friend enter your Alderon ID at checkout. After the friend buys, both get the tier-1 skin; the referrer unlocks skins like Suchomimus and Pachy tier by tier for 1–6 referrals, and also gets a cave decoration statue. Only new players who bought the game less than 24 hours ago can be referred, and if the friend gets a refund both rewards are taken back.
  • Playing together on the same server gives a boost: doing quests with the referred friend earns a Growth Buff, and both get a passive Friend Buff (only for the same diet). This directly rewards "bringing friends to play".
  • Full cross-play, including low-barrier devices such as phones: classmates can join the same server on any device.
  • Mods and community servers: the community can run its own servers and make mods and maps, and each community server pulls in its own Discord group.

Speculation:

  • The main organic traffic comes from dinosaur survival and roleplay videos on YouTube (kid and teen audiences); our social source data also puts YouTube first, but no specific big creator or viral video was found, unverified.
  • Direct sales on the official site (PC and the account system run through the alderongames.com store), so checkout traffic holds steady at about 47K visits a month; the growth phase has passed and it is in a steady state.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Priced at $30; Google Play Android downloads 5M+ (whether the mobile version has a free entry or demo or requires purchase was not verified).
  • The company has not published revenue or player counts. The Edgegap case study page calls it "a massive MMO success story" but gives no numbers.
  • Speculation: if paying players across console, PC and mobile number in the hundreds of thousands to a million, cumulative revenue would be on the order of tens of millions of dollars; pure speculation.

Confidence and gaps

  • The company background and crowdfunding figures are backed by press releases and media, so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: the exact mobile launch date, when Refer-A-Friend went live, which YouTube/TikTok creators drove the volume, and cumulative sales were all not found. Reddit and Wayback page contents could not be scraped directly.
