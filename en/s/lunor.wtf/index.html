Lunor (lunor.wtf): Online store selling premium Roblox executors, scripts and external tools with instant digital delivery. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Lunor to a checkout payment page — #429 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.
