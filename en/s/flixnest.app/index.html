FlixNest began (2025-09) as an Android/TV streaming client built by Hungarian developer ThSándor (compatible with Stremio add-ons, Trakt, Real-Debrid, Torrentio). In 2026 it pivoted to selling "Flix-Streams": one account bundling Stremio source add-ons, live sports/IPTV (M3U/Xtream), Kodi add-ons, Emby/Jellyfin and a VPN, with one-time payments from €8/month. Acquisition rides entirely on the existing Stremio/Kodi/IPTV "free streaming" scene: the free add-on (free.flixnest.app) circulates in Stremio add-on communities and on Reddit, Firestick/Kodi tutorial sites like TROYPOINT publish install guides, and Discord plus r/flixnest are used to upgrade free users to paid. Essentially resale of aggregated pirated streams.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a Flix-Streams account: Stremio manifest (movie/TV/anime sources), live and sports catalogs, Kodi add-on, IPTV links (M3U/Xtream/VOD/EPG), Emby/Jellyfin login, WireGuard VPN. Claims not to host content, only to handle "configuration, routing and account access." The free tier only scrapes public sites such as HDHub, Dailymotion and DLStreams; the paid tier advertises 80K+ live channels, 190K movies and 43K TV series. Also a FlixNest Android/TV app and a web player at player.flixnest.app.
  • Pricing: one-time, no renewal: 1 month €8, 3 months €15, 6 months €27, 1 year €31.50 (was €45); payment via VexaRack or Paymento crypto (30% off). As of 2026-06, TROYPOINT recorded the payment method as Ko-fi sponsorship unlocks.
  • Who built it: GitHub account Thsandorh, Google Play developer "ThSándor," package name hu.kinetik.streamapp (the.hu prefix points to Hungary); the site has /ncore/configure (nCore is a Hungarian private tracker), further supporting a Hungarian background. Individual or team not found.
  • Launch date: domain registered 2025-07-27 (Spaceship); first Wayback capture 2025-09-29 (Android app landing page); GitHub releases 1.3.7/1.3.8 on 10-28 and 11-03 (presumably 2025).
  • Current numbers: Android app 10,000+ downloads (AppBrain summary); Discord, r/flixnest.
  • Traffic (profile): direct 62.3%, social 28.3% (Reddit, Discord), referrals 5.7%; search is brand keywords (flixnest, flix-streams reddit); United States 36%, United Kingdom 22%, India 13%; two months ago 1,300 → last month 3,147.

Where the first users came from: the Stremio/Kodi/Firestick scene. Evidence: TROYPOINT (one of the largest Firestick/Kodi tutorial sites) published an install guide; a Greek site, techvortex.eu, listed it in a 2026-05 "best Stremio add-ons" roundup that appears in search results (summary only); social traffic comes from Reddit and Discord. The source of the first users in the app phase not found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free-tier hook: the free add-on at free.flixnest.app (unstable sources) → upsell to the paid "Vault" sources and live TV.
  • Community operations: Discord (paid users get roles synced automatically) + r/flixnest.
  • Distribution via third-party tutorial sites (TROYPOINT) and Stremio add-on roundup articles.

Speculation:

  • A Stremio add-on is itself a shareable link, so recommendations in Reddit communities like r/StremioAddons are the main source of new users (Speculation; original Reddit posts unverified).
  • One-time payments plus a crypto discount sidestep the risk of subscription payment channels being shut down.
  • High legal risk (pirated live streams/IPTV); could be taken down or forced to change domains at any time.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue. About 3K visits last month; a few hundred paying users × about €10/month would put monthly revenue in the low thousands of euros (our estimate).
  • Android app 10,000+ downloads.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Useful evidence: GitHub releases, the TROYPOINT guide, the official site and free-tier page, Wayback snapshots (2025-09, 2025-12), RDAP. AppBrain returned 403; Reddit could not be opened.
  • The developer's identity is known only at the username level; paying user count and revenue not found.
  • Note: the service resells third-party pirated streaming/IPTV sources; this report only documents the growth path.
