Basics

  • Product: study-abroad and employment consulting in Italian (also in English/Spanish/French): CV "stress test", mock interviews, entrance exam simulations, CV/cover letter/LinkedIn edits, consulting with industry experts; a free guide "Guida" for current students and graduates in political science, international relations and foreign languages; also "Guida alle ONG Vol.1".
  • Pricing: first call free, paid consulting prices not public.
  • Team: Fabrizio Barbon Di Marco (founder, from a small town, two master's degrees in international relations / political science and diplomacy, one from the College of Europe; about ten years of international development and humanitarian aid experience in the Middle East, Africa and the Caribbean, and sales and marketing work in Germany and the UK); Clara Lefrançois (Business Customer Manager, with field experience in Africa, the Middle East and Haiti, and UN human rights work in Mexico and Chad). Source: https://carriereinternazionali.it/

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Lead funnel: free 30-page career paths guide (name + phone + email) → free call → paid customized consulting.
  • Showing word of mouth: actively invites customers to write Trustpilot reviews (14, all 5 stars).

Speculation:

  • The visit surge in 2026-09 + direct visits at 50% + Instagram as the social source looks more like Instagram/Meta ads or a piece of content taking off and sending people to the guide page; no matching post or ad evidence was found.
  • The search term "prioloweb" suggests a web building/marketing agency is involved, which may also be doing SEO/ads.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public data. A two-person team doing one-on-one consulting, so the scale is very small; the 14 Trustpilot reviews can serve as a lower-bound reference for the number of paying customers.

Confidence and gaps
