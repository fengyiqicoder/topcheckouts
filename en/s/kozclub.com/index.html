Kozclub (kozclub.com): Group lunches for 4–6 strangers, €11.99 each, 30K sign-ups. Category on TopCheckouts: Consumer services.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,442 visits went from Kozclub to a checkout payment page — #477 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 3,019 (estimated from global rank). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).
