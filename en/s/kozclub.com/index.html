Basics

  • Product: group lunches for professionals. Flow: short questionnaire (role, situation, what you want, personality energy) → pick city and date → D-72h AI matching with human review → D-48h restaurant and tablemates' names announced. Two tables are booked at the same restaurant to guard against no-shows; no-show rate <3% (site FAQ).
  • Company: KOZ club SAS, share capital €1,000, 1 rue d'Algérie, Lyon, founded 2025-07-01 (French company registry API: president Victor Linder, born 1992; managing director Aris Favia Zingarelli, born 1991); site tagline "Fait à Lyon, mangé partout".
  • Pricing: €11.99 per lunch seat, no subscription (from 2026-07); €9 per lunch from 2025-08 to 2026-04.
  • Launch date: domain registered 2025-05-08 (RDAP; 2008–2010 snapshots belong to a previous owner); first Wayback snapshot 2025-07-12; site says "11 months in" (2026-10), implying the first lunches around 2025-11 (or counted from preparation; uncertain).
  • Traffic (profile): France 100%; direct 38%, search 47% (brand keywords 75%), referrals 9%, social only LinkedIn; 2026-09 visits 8,442, +48% month over month.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid acquisition infrastructure: the homepage carries a Google Ads conversion tag (AW-17360833489) and a Meta Pixel (fbq); all signup buttons point to a Fillout form with a canal=lp parameter for channel attribution, the classic "landing page + paid traffic" setup.
  • Word of mouth/brand search: brand keywords are 75% of search and direct is 38%; the profile judges that most new users search the name after attendees tell colleagues; homepage testimonials list names, titles and cities (Nice, Lausanne, Annecy, Grenoble).
  • City expansion: every new city adds a batch of local users who can sign up; 17 cities by 2026-07.

Speculation:

  • The first users were probably the two founders' Lyon network + LinkedIn posts (the only social source is LinkedIn), but no founder posts were found.
  • The jump from 10k to 30k signups (2026-07 → 10) may correspond to increased ad spend or media exposure; no specific event found.
  • Getting matched with good tablemates → come again + bring colleagues is a natural repurchase and referral loop.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported 30k signups; homepage shows "+147 this week". If about 150 paid seats a week × €11.99, roughly €7,000 a month (speculation; "+147 this week" may not be paid seats).
  • Share capital only €1,000, headcount not declared (NN); an early-stage small team.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Company, founders and launch date come from the French company registry API and raw Wayback snapshots; credible. Signup numbers etc. are all self-reported by the site.
  • Gaps: exact date and city of the first lunches, press coverage and founders' LinkedIn posts all not found; search results entirely irrelevant (CVEs, health sites). The profile's "new users = Viral" coexists with the paid-pixel evidence on the page; takeoff set to "Paid ads" based on page evidence, medium confidence.
