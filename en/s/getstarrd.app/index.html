Basics

  • Product: AI studio that turns photos of you (or your pet) into cinematic short videos, portraits, and songs based on trending templates; built on models such as Seedance and Kling Motion Control, "Starrd writes the prompt". iOS app "Starrd - AI Video Generator" + web version.
  • Pricing: one-time credit packs, $4.99–$49.99; videos from 100 credits (official pricing page, App Store in-app purchases).
  • Developer: App Store seller Ian Brillantes, copyright holder Take 220 Productions LLC (site footer "© 2026 Take 220 Productions LLC").
  • Launch date: domain registered 2026-04-02 (RDAP); App Store version 1.0 released April 9 (page shows no year; given the domain date it should be 2026); first Wayback snapshot 2026-04-03.
  • Scale: App Store 4.7 stars, 504 ratings (2026-10).
  • SEO assets: about 94 single-template pages, 18 template category pages, 34 "vs / alternative" comparison pages (e.g. "Mischief alternative"), and multiple trend tutorial blog posts.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: one template page + one tutorial blog post + competitor comparison pages per trend; in our data the non-brand search queries are trend names (including Spanish), and search is the largest source.
  • Competitor article citation: Kavel, a competitor in the same space, named and recommended Starrd in its Hotel Lobby comparison article, bringing referral traffic.
  • Product output gets posted to TikTok (the Hotel Lobby craze saw "thousands of versions within days", reported by The Tab, cited via Kavel).

Speculation:

  • The first users most likely came from the 2026-06 AI prank video craze: people asked "what app is this" in the comments, then searched "AI prank video generator". The blog title is literally "The App Everyone's Asking About", but there is no user data to back this up.
  • Generated videos don't necessarily carry a watermark or branding, so "branded output" only partly holds; classified as Mixed.
  • Brand keywords make up 59% of search, indicating a base of returning users or word-of-mouth awareness.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 504 App Store ratings; using typical rating rates, downloads are roughly in the tens of thousands (speculation).
  • One-time credit packs $4.99–$49.99, about $5 per video. Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch date, developer, pricing, and SEO structure all have first-hand sources; credible.
  • No Product Hunt, Reddit, or founder interviews found; official TikTok account and breakout videos not found.
  • Gaps: downloads and revenue, whether TikTok ads are being run, which specific template drove the first spike.
