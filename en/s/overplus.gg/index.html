Overview

Overplus (overplus.gg): Paid third-party Dota 2 add-on unlocking cosmetic items, Dota Plus-style visuals and an AI hero/item advisor. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Overplus to a checkout payment page — #436 of the 607 sites we track. That is 144% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,517.

Total website visits that month were about 65,142 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 13.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 47%, direct 45%, referrals/email/paid 8%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 28%, with queries like “оверплюс”, “скинченджер дота 2”, “овер плюс”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
