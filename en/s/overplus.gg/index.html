Basics

  • Product: Overplus, a third-party Dota 2 plugin. Shows all cosmetics locally (skins, terrain, weather, river, couriers, emblems etc.), with Dota Plus visual features (excluding build suggestions), Meta Tracker (lane/hero win rate and pick rate), Shop Builds (replaces suggested builds with stats-based ones), and a draft-phase analyzer.
  • Pricing: sold by number of days (page shows both ₽ and $; the exact pricing page requires login), 7-day full-feature free trial, 20% off the first order; a 2022 YouTube tutorial title says "$6/month". Software is bound to the PC's hardware ID. Sources: https://overplus.gg/en; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rf-iLHxNpPs
  • Team: not disclosed; site is mostly Russian (also English and Chinese at /cn); users Russia 63.3%, Belarus 8.6%, Germany 8.1%.
  • Launch: first Wayback snapshot 2022-10-20 (Nuxt frontend, redirects to /en); no RDAP data for the.gg domain.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral-code affiliates: 152 ref links and 73 distinct referral codes on Wayback, continuing from the second month after launch through 2026; YouTube creators put referral links in video descriptions with "enter XX at signup for 14 days".
  • Free trial: 7-day full-feature trial + 20% off the first order.
  • Search: 46.6%, non-brand about 28% (скинченджер дота 2); Russian brand keyword оверплюс.
  • Referrals: promokodi.net (promo-code site, 5.5%), yandex.ru, pikabu.ru; social from YouTube and Telegram.
  • Controversy exposure: the public boycott by pro players (2024-01) and Valve's ban wave (2025-12) were both covered by media.

Speculation:

  • The first users came via referral codes from Russian-speaking Dota streamers and YouTube/Telegram tutorial makers (the codes look like streamer IDs and appeared in bulk right at launch), i.e. streamer spark + affiliate-driven (commission rate not found).
  • Ban news brings short-term search interest but may hurt retention long-term (videos like "IS OVERPLUS SAFE" show users worry about bans).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Price was about $6/month in 2022; now sold by the day.
  • User count and revenue not found. Existing users returning directly to top up days account for 45.4%, indicating a stable base of repeat payers.
