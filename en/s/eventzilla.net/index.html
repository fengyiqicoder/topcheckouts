Basics

  • Product: event management SaaS covering an event website + registration/ticketing + agenda/call for speakers + check-in and badge printing + reports; free events are free, paid events are charged a per-order fee; supports 135+ currencies and 4 languages, with regional sites for the US/France/Germany/Spain/Australia/Canada/India/UK.
  • Team: third-party sources name the founder/CEO as Muthu Kumar, and others list Asir Paulraj as a co-founder; HQ is said to be either Dublin, Ohio or around McLean, Virginia, both from secondhand sources (the site has no about information). Latka estimates 11 employees (2023); no funding record, bootstrapped.
  • Pricing evolution: around 2018–2022 Basic was $1.25/ticket and PRO 1.5% + $0.99 (capped at $4.99), versus up to $24 per ticket at Eventbrite (Skift); in 2026 Basic is $1.50/order, Pro 1.9% + $1.50, Plus 2.9% + $1.50, and Scale billed annually.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Organizer acquisition: a content blog (since 2010, including SEO articles like "make your event go viral"), software directory reviews (Capterra/G2/TrustRadius, about 770), paid reviews in industry media (Skift 2018), an AppSumo lifetime deal (2022), and industry landing pages (e.g. /solutions/product-launches/).
  • Branded output: event pages are hosted on events.eventzilla.net (Reddit has a dedicated domain aggregation page), and attendees meet the brand during ticket purchase. Channel profile: direct 45.6%, referrals 40.8% (from registration links on organizers' sites), email 8.2%.
  • Speculation: other organizers among attendees become organizers themselves (a weak loop); the first 1,000 organizers most likely came from SEO + blog + directories in 2009–2012, specifics Not found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Latka (third-party estimate): annual revenue about $1.2M (2023-12), about 10,000 customers, and about $1.1M in 2018 — basically flat over many years.
  • September checkout visits were 17K, strongly seasonal, and the high share of New Zealand suggests heavy influence from a single large organizer.

Confidence and gaps

  • The 2009 launch has triple evidence from WHOIS + Wayback + Skift; the founders and revenue are all secondhand.
  • No founder interview or launch event was found; the "first users" can still only be speculated.
  • Second follow-up check: added the 2018 Skift paid review (20+ countries, price comparison against Eventbrite), the 2022–2023 AppSumo lifetime deal, the G2/Capterra/TrustRadius review counts, and the lead on large organizers in New Zealand; classification unchanged.
