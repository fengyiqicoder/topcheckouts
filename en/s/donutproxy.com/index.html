Donut Proxy is a small proxy IP seller that appeared in the 2021 sneaker bot boom, selling specifically to US resellers so they can place multi-account orders during limited releases such as Yeezy Supply and Nike. Customer acquisition in this scene happens almost entirely in Discord cook groups, in "successful checkout" posts on X and in YouTube bot tutorials: proxy sellers post "our success in two Yeezy Supply drops" and word of mouth spreads in the groups. So 97.6% of its traffic is from the US, social sources are in order Discord, YouTube and Reddit, and search is almost all brand keywords. It is a vertical small business attached to the sneaker-botting scene, with no SEO and no self-spreading product.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Community word of mouth: social traffic sources are Discord > YouTube > Reddit; successful drop results are shown off on X.
  • Repeat purchases by existing customers: direct visits are 47%, and proxy data is consumed per GB or per day, so customers must renew repeatedly.

Speculation:

  • The common practice in the botting scene is to do co-branded discount codes with cook groups, in-group sponsorships, and affiliate payouts to tutorial YouTubers; Donut very likely does the same, but no evidence of its own partnerships was found.
  • The business scale fluctuates with sneaker drop hype; after Yeezy production stopped in 2023, sneaker botting cooled overall, and it may be shifting toward broader "automation" customers (the official site title now includes Automation).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Prices, customer count and revenue were not found. Similar small proxy sellers in the industry typically see monthly revenue in the tens of thousands of dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch (no earlier report). Search results were almost entirely irrelevant (DonutSMP, Donut Media, free proxy listicles), X returns 402 and cannot be opened, and the official site's prices and team information are client-rendered and could not be captured.
  • Low evidence level: only the domain registration, Wayback and the channel profile are fairly solid; the scene's playbook is structural speculation.
