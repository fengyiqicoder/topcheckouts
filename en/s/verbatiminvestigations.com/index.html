Verbatim is a newly founded non-profit undercover investigation project by investigative journalists Lawrence Carter and Louis Wilson. It registered the domain in 2026-07 and on 2026-09-03 published, all at once, "Dirty Money", a year-long undercover investigation inside Reform UK (video + full text + downloads): it revealed that a Reform adviser had a US family office pay for JL Partners polling that ran in the Telegraph and the Times, and caught Farage himself on tape saying "all above board". The revelations were widely reshared by UK media, Facebook news pages, X and YouTube (hidden-camera clips carrying the Verbatim name), putting a brand-new site at the center of British political conversation overnight and triggering a Metropolitan Police investigation. The site runs on event traffic + donations and has no ongoing user acquisition mechanism.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: an investigative journalism site; the core is currently a single undercover investigation (video, text version, document downloads) + About + a donation entry point.
  • Who made it: founders Lawrence Carter and Louis Wilson (About page: "a new not-for-profit investigative project" focused on high-level political and corporate influence). The About page lists the team's past work: the ExxonMobil undercover sting (reported by the NYT on 2021-06-30), Project 2025, Gulf oil states, Russia's shadow fleet. The two previously did the ExxonMobil undercover reporting at Unearthed, Greenpeace's investigative journalism unit (from memory, unverified).
  • Launch date: WHOIS created 2026-07-08; earliest Wayback capture 2026-09-03 18:37 (publication day).
