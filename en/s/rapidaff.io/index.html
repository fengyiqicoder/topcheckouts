PureSoft Labs OÜ, a small studio in Tallinn, Estonia (indie developer Adrian Weber), found Rewardful/FirstPromoter too expensive and heavy while building its own SaaS. It registered the domain in late 2024 and in early 2025 built a "lightweight affiliate back office that connects to Stripe in minutes", from $25/month. It launched on Product Hunt (only 9 votes), got into the Stripe partner directory, and built "vs Rewardful / Tolt / FirstPromoter" comparison pages to capture search. The traffic that actually arrives comes from the product's built-in distribution: every customer has a <customer name>.rapidaff.io affiliate portal (marked "Powered by Rapid Affiliates"), customers' own sites point their "Affiliates" link here, and affiliates searching "some product affiliate" also land on rapidaff.io, which is why referral traffic is 45.9%.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: SaaS affiliate program management: guided program setup, paste a tracking script (rapidaff.io/scripts/ra.js), connect Stripe to detect payments, real-time conversion dashboard, branded affiliate portal, and PayPal/Wise bulk payout formats.
  • Pricing: Starter $25/month (billed annually, 1 program, unlimited affiliates), Growth $50/month (3 programs, email marketing), Pro $90/month (10 programs); 14-day free trial.
  • Who built it: the footer says "© PureSoft Labs OÜ 2025… Made with ❤️ in the European Union"; the Stripe partner directory lists Tallinn, Estonia. On Product Hunt the maker is Adrian Weber, who calls himself a "solo independent builder", with Hamza Afzal Butt listed as a team member. The About page's origin story: "We wanted to add an affiliate program to our own SaaS and found existing tools too expensive or hard to use for indie developers, so we built one ourselves." Social accounts: x.com/puresoftlabs, LinkedIn puresoftlabs, Facebook (created in 2025).
  • Launch date:.io WHOIS registered 2024-12-19 (Spaceship); Wayback's first record is 2024-12-20 (308), with the first normal snapshot on 2025-03-10. Product Hunt shows "Launched in 2025".
  • Traffic (profile data): US 92.2%, Poland 7.9%; referral 45.9%, direct 38.8%, search 9.2% (such as "aismutwriter affiliate", "rapid app affiliate").

Clues: a homepage testimonial says "installed Rapid Affiliates when their beta went live", so the earliest customers were presumably the founder's own and nearby indie developers' SaaS products; the Product Hunt launch got only 9 votes and brought very little traffic; the Stripe partner directory and comparison pages are ongoing entry points (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Each customer's affiliate portal lives on a <customer>.rapidaff.io subdomain, marked "Powered by Rapid Affiliates", so the more customers there are, the more affiliates visit the rapidaff.io domain (referral 45.9%, with search terms of the form "some customer + affiliate").
  • Comparison pages capture "Rewardful alternative"-type searches; exposure in the Stripe partner directory.

Speculation:

  • Many affiliates are themselves SaaS founders/indie developers, and seeing "Powered by" may convert them into customers. This is a branded-output loop, but there is no conversion data.
  • Most of the traffic in the profile is actually customers' affiliates, not potential paying customers of Rapid Affiliates, so the real number of paying customers may be very small.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Homepage says "Trusted by 1 clients"; Product Hunt shows only 33 followers and 9 votes. At $25-90/month, monthly revenue is roughly a few hundred to one or two thousand dollars (speculation, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results were almost all irrelevant (RapidAPI, assorted alternatives rankings); evidence comes from the official site, Product Hunt, the Stripe directory, Wayback, WHOIS and customer portal pages.
  • The exact Product Hunt launch date, customer count and MRR were not found; "Adrian Weber" is the name on Product Hunt and has not been checked against the business registration of PureSoft Labs OÜ.
