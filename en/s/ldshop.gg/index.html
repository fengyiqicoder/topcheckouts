Overview

Ldshop (ldshop.gg): Online store for game top-ups, game keys, gift cards and subscriptions. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Ldshop to a checkout payment page — #470 of the 607 sites we track. That is 83% down from the April–June monthly average of 49,700.

Total website visits that month were about 759,380 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.1 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 75%, direct 22%, referrals/email/paid 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 60%, with queries like “wuwa codes”, “aniimo tier list”, “genshin banners”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: discounts only e.g. 43%OFF, no absolute prices.
