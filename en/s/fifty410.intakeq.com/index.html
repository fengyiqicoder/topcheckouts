This subdomain is not a standalone product but the patient portal and intake forms hosted on IntakeQ for the Texas online weight-loss telehealth brand Fifty 410 (fifty410.com, founded in 2023, Dallas/Prosper TX). Fifty 410 caught the 2022–2025 semaglutide/tirzepatide shortage and the window in which the FDA allowed pharmacies to compound them, entering with "cash-pay compounded GLP-1 online prescriptions at $133–199/month". It registered the domain in 2023-10, launched in 2023-11, opened Facebook/Instagram/TikTok in 2024, and built up 6,500+ reviews at 4.8 on Trustpilot by actively inviting reviews, claiming to have served 200K+ patients. The portal subdomain's traffic is almost all existing patients coming back to fill out forms for refills (direct 80%, email 15%); it does not acquire customers itself.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: the Fifty 410 patient portal (/portal) and enrollment intake form hosted on IntakeQ; the "Get Started" button on the Fifty 410 site jumps straight to an intakeq.com form, and the footer "Patient Portal" link goes to fifty410.intakeq.com/portal.
  • Parent brand: Fifty 410, "a telehealth platform connecting patients with licensed doctors; it does not compound drugs itself, they are compounded by independent compounding pharmacies (503B)". Offering: compounded semaglutide/tirzepatide GLP-1 plans from $199/month (tirzepatide $133/month, $399 for 3 months; high dose $183/month), with hair regrowth, Sermorelin, NAD+ and more coming soon.
  • Company: LinkedIn shows founded in 2023, headquartered in Dallas, Texas, 11–50 employees (12 on record, hiring a CMO); Trustpilot address 1630 W Prosper Trail #620, Prosper, TX. Founder not public.
  • Self-reported scale: the site says "Trusted by over 200K patients"; Trustpilot 4.8 / 6,567 reviews (3,677 in the last 12 months, the company actively invites reviews); Instagram @fifty.410 about 45K followers, 82 posts.
  • Domain: fifty410.com registered 2023-10-12 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2023-11-01; Facebook page ID 61565081587604 (in the range of new-style page IDs created in 2024, speculation).
  • Traffic (subdomain): United States 58%; direct 80.5%, email 14.7%, social 2.9% (Facebook); peak of 13,950 two months ago, 3,316 last month

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The portal subdomain itself is a retention tool: existing patients must come back to fill out the intake form for every refill/renewal, hence direct visits 80% and email 15%.
  • Actively inviting Trustpilot reviews produces a large number of good reviews (4.8 / 6,500+), used as social proof.
  • Low cash-pay prices (from $133/month) + 3-month plans lock in repeat purchases.

Speculation:

  • New customers mainly come from Meta paid ads and Instagram content (45K followers); only Facebook shows up in the social traffic.
  • The decline after the two months ago peak may be related to tighter regulation of compounded GLP-1 or a change of pharmacy (a Trustpilot review mentions "couldn't find out the new pharmacy's credentials").

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 200K+ patients; at $133–199/month, even 10K active patients would mean revenue in the millions of dollars per month, a pure estimate, not verified.
  • Team of 11–50, hiring a CMO.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Note: the profile in profile/out describes this subdomain as the patient login page of "Club Compound", which does not match the evidence that the fifty410.com footer links directly to fifty410.intakeq.com/portal; this report changes it to Fifty 410 (second-pass check: the parent brand attribution was changed).
  • Gaps: the founder, launch channel and ad spend scale were not found; the weightlossrankings review and the 2023 Wayback snapshots were not read due to rate limiting (429); more than half the Google results were unrelated (CVEs, photo galleries).
