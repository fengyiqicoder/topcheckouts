Growth loop

Evidence:

  • TikTok is the only social source (profile: social 100% TikTok); the site turns viral TikTok videos into a homepage "Viral" wall and calls itself the #1 trending app on TikTok; the Instagram hashtag page shows many Reels promoting it.
  • The name rides the Gen Z slang "the ick"; brand search is 87% of search traffic, meaning viewers search the name after watching short videos.
  • After going viral, copycat sites and apps proliferated (theick.me, thelck.io, same-name apps on the App Store); the site has a dedicated "only these are official" page.
  • SEO defense from 2026-09: competitor comparison pages, long-tail blog posts like "how to see who your boyfriend recently followed", multilingual pages for Latin America/Europe.

Speculation:

  • The videos are most likely bulk-placed paid/affiliate UGC creators (the "check your boyfriend's follows" skit template is a common playbook for this kind of app), but no clear evidence of creator partnerships or an affiliate program was found.
  • The high share of Brazil, Peru and Latin America may relate to Portuguese/Spanish TikTok creator placements.
  • The -44% month-over-month drop last month web traffic may signal fading TikTok buzz.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Google Play 1M+ downloads, 192k ratings (a high rating count usually means in-app rating prompts).
  • Subscription $9.99/week or $34.99/year; no public revenue figures. If 1–2% of downloads convert to paid, annual revenue could be in the hundreds of thousands to a million dollars (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Evidence on product/pricing/developer/Android scale is solid (site, Google Play citations).
  • Gaps: iOS launch and delisting dates, the team behind Vaste Company, and specific viral video links and view counts all not found; search results were mostly irrelevant pages (Kik, itch.io, Omegle alternatives) and copycat sites.
  • Takeoff set as "Creator or press spark" (TikTok videos), but whether it was organic UGC or paid creator placements could not be distinguished.
