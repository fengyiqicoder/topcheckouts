Overview

Tally (tally.so): Free no-code online form builder with unlimited forms and submissions, hosted in the EU. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 16,988 visits went from Tally to a checkout payment page — #201 of the 607 sites we track. That is 3% up from the April–June monthly average of 16,418.

Total website visits that month were about 4,774,603 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 0.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 62%, social 29%, referrals 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)
