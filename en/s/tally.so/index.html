Basics

  • Product: no-code online forms (surveys, sign-ups, lead collection), document-style editing, with the free plan having no limit on forms or submissions; Pro about $24-29/month, Business $74/month; data hosted in the EU; has Tally AI and an MCP server.
  • Founders: Marie Martens (marketing background, Belgian) and Filip Minev (software engineer, Bulgarian, who had started several companies before); the two are life partners. The starting point was quitting to do freelance work, losing half their clients, then switching to no-code tools (mentioned in the Year 1 blog post, details not read).
  • Company: Belgium, fully bootstrapped, a team of 10 in 2026; Filip was the only developer until 2023.
  • Funding: none.

First 1,000 users: friends and family → cold emails (picking a few hundred creators, indie developers and founders from PH and Twitter lists) → TweetDeck monitoring of tweets discussing similar products, and answering "what form tools are there?" questions on Reddit/Quora/Indie Hackers → maker communities such as 500 Makers and Slack groups (all backed by the founders' blog posts). Source of new users: early on PH and Twitter, then for five years mainly the "Made with Tally" badge + word of mouth, and from 2025 AI assistant recommendations are number one (43%).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Badge loop: free forms carry the "Made with Tally" badge → people filling them out see it and sign up → about 1-2% eventually upgrade to paid. Unlimited free use means more forms get sent out, and more badge exposure.
  • Two PH launches (the 2021 launch and the 2023 2.0) both brought clear jumps.
  • Build in public: the blog keeps publishing MRR, making it a topic in circles like Indie Hackers.
  • AI recommendations: built an /ai-info page and an MCP server; in 2026, 43% of new users came from AI assistants.
  • Affiliate program (since 2021-06), startup and NGO discounts.
  • Speculation: the Notion user community (document-style editing + Notion integration) was an important early spillover source; Similarweb's 28.7% social comes from tutorials and recommendations on LinkedIn/YouTube.

Revenue and scale clues

  • MRR: $64 (2020-10) → $5K (2021-10) → $100K (2024-02) → $338K (2025-10) → $501K (2026-09), ARR $6M; 2.5 million users; 10 people, zero outside funding (data published on the founders' blog).
