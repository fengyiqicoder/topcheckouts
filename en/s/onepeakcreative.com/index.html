Three friends in Kelowna, BC, Canada (Meg Jackson, Glen Jackson, Conar Fair) first ran a video agency making ads for brands (they say it hit seven figures in three years). In 2022 they launched a new TikTok account and ran an "Honest Ads" series ("if ads were honest"), going from 0 to 25K followers and 10M views in two weeks. The hit was both content and proof for their pitch: "we know how to make million-view videos." At the end of 2022 they packaged the method into the Find Your Peak creator course, selling it through their own TikTok/Instagram (335K IG followers), a free guide to collect emails, a weekly free live masterclass, paid ads and Kajabi affiliate links. In 2026 they added Format Finder, a $97/month AI tool, to turn one-time course buyers into subscribers. In 2025 the main domain switched from the agency site to the course site, claiming 160,000+ students.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The hit as an ad: Honest Ads got tens of millions of views in two weeks and went straight into the course pitch ("this is how we grew ourselves").
  • Funnel: free guide / 6 Steps to Viral collects emails -> weekly free live masterclass -> sell courses -> then sell the Format Finder subscription; on Trustpilot some say they bought after seeing "a ton of ads", and others are returning students buying again.
  • Affiliates: Kajabi affiliate links appear in large numbers from 2025, with students and partner creators promoting; co-promotion with Nina Zadeh in 2026.
  • Student cases: the homepage lists students' video views (cooking 17M, fitness 2.1M, etc.) as social proof.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 students probably came from their own TikTok/Instagram followers (the following from Honest Ads plus 335K IG followers), amplified by paid ads after 2023 (many of the "160K students" are buyers of the $58 low-price courses).
  • The agency track record with brands like Doritos and Expedia adds trust to the courses, and also lets corporate clients such as an Amazon social media team buy training.
  • Brand keywords at 90% and social at only 1% suggest most traffic first saw them on IG or in ads, then searched the name or clicked an email link (clicks from inside social platforms may be counted as direct).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 161,454 creators have used it (2026-04); LinkedIn says 110K+ students.
  • If 160K students averaged $60-100 per order, cumulative course revenue would be about $10M-$16M (pure speculation; "students" may include free users).
  • Format Finder is $97/month; subscriber count not public.
  • In 2022 they claimed the agency business was already seven figures (CAD or USD not stated).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there is no earlier report in notes. Search results were mostly irrelevant generic articles, so evidence came mainly from Wayback (2021-2026 snapshots of the main site and course subsite, CDX paths), RDAP, LinkedIn and Trustpilot.
  • Founders, the path from agency to courses, Honest Ads numbers (self-reported) and student count (self-reported) are medium-high credibility; the exact Honest Ads publish date was not found (speculated to be the second half of 2022).
  • Gaps: scale of paid ad spend, actual course sales, Format Finder subscriber count; on Reddit r/Tiktokhelp someone asked "has anyone bought this course", only the snippet was seen.
