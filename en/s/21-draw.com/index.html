Basics

  • Product: online art education platform, video course subscription (71–75+ courses, 1,940+ lessons) + self-published drawing instruction books (print/ebook; books.21-draw.com is a Shopify bookstore).
  • Company: 21 Draw (Sweden; LinkedIn HQ Knivsta, Uppsala, with a production center in Stockholm).
  • Founders: Renco Smeding (co-founder/director, former MD), Darko Perunicic (co-founder/CEO), per LinkedIn and The Hub.
  • Size/funding: The Hub lists 1–10 people, Pre-seed, 75k users (elsewhere they claim 70k community members, 40k books sold, 10k Kickstarter backers, $632k total crowdfunded).
  • Name origin: the initial budget only covered 21 artists; so many wanted to join that they kept adding more.
  • Domain: registered 2014-01-20; earliest Wayback snapshot 2014-03-02 (crowdfunding teaser page, copyright 2013).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Multi-author credits: every book/course involves multiple well-known industry artists, who are themselves distribution nodes (names like Steve Rude and Tom Bancroft are used as endorsements).
  • Crowdfunding → email list → next crowdfunding: three consecutive Kickstarter/Indiegogo campaigns, roughly 10k backers in total.
  • Free entry points: free courses (Gesture Drawing, Learn to draw in 21 days preview) + free sample-chapter PDF in exchange for an email ("Grab the first 35 pages of our bestselling book").
  • Completion certificates can be shared to portfolios/social media (official site; matches the known share_result mechanism).

Speculation:

  • Social sources Facebook/YouTube/Instagram are most likely driven mainly by paid ads + instructors' own accounts (21 Draw has long run deep-discount countdown promos, a typical FB ad funnel), not pure organic spread.
  • The 10x checkout visits in 2026-09: possibly a new course/big sale, or a checkout migration to Stripe that caused a spike in checkout referrals; no external event evidence found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Roughly $632k crowdfunded in total (self-reported) across three campaigns: $127k / $200k+ / $150k.
  • 70–75k community members/users, 40k books sold (self-reported); top single course 157.5k students (including free).
  • Pricing: annual list price about $240 (listed as NT$7,628), 75% off promo about $60; ebooks $29, 8-ebook bundle $119.
  • Team of 1–10, Pre-seed. Revenue not disclosed.

Confidence and gaps

  • The 2014 crowdfunding origin path is well-evidenced (Wayback + Amazing Stories + Kicktraq).
  • Founder information comes from LinkedIn / The Hub; no founder interview found.
  • Gaps: cause of the 2026-09 jump not found; course subscription revenue and share of paid ads unknown. Note it already had about 2.5k visits over the past 4–6 months, so this is a second surge for a mature site rather than a new-site takeoff.
