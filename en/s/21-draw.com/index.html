Overview

21-draw (21-draw.com): Online art-education platform selling video courses and learn-to-draw books by professional artists. Category on TopCheckouts: Courses & content.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 27,480 visits went from 21-draw to a checkout payment page — #95 of the 607 sites we track. That is 981% up from the April–June monthly average of 2,543.

Total website visits that month were about 181,168 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 15.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 51%, direct 32%, social 10%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 63%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
