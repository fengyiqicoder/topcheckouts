Basics

  • Product: a practice site for the Hazard Perception Test (HPT) of the NSW, Australia driver licence, with 30+ touchscreen realistic scenarios, instant feedback, "finish in 30 minutes"; includes a link to the official HPT handbook PDF, a How to Pass guide and a blog. The first 5 scenarios are free with no signup, and $5 one-time unlocks all 32 (Stripe checkout). Source: https://hptpracticensw.com/ and a brandfetch summary.
  • Tech: an Astro static site + PWA (manifest, service worker), GA4 (G-DXNJKT60ZY), build-id 20260925135543, so it was still being iterated at the end of last month
  • Who runs it: not found. The site only says "© 2026 HPT Training" and disclaims any affiliation with Transport for NSW/Service NSW; there is no personal About information or social accounts.
  • Launch timing: domain registered 2024-12-30 (RDAP); first Wayback capture 2025-02-24, when it was already a working free practice site.
  • Traffic (profile data): search 80.8%, of which non-brand 48.5% (nsw hazard perception test practice, hazard perception test practice nsw); direct 14.4%, email 2.9%, social from Reddit; countries Australia 86.7%, United States 12.1%, India 1.2%.

Where the first users came from: search. The domain is the keyword, and the launch title read "Free … HPT Practice | RMS/Service NSW Preparation", leading with "free + no signup", which caught people searching "hpt practice nsw" before the test; non-brand search is about half. There is Reddit in the social traffic (profile data), and the founder probably replied with recommendations in subreddits like r/sydney and r/NSWdrivers (speculation; the Reddit pages could not be opened, not verified). No trace of Product Hunt, press or video.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: exact-match domain + title keywords; non-brand search 48.5%.
  • Free trial hook: the first 5 questions are free with no signup, lowering the barrier to entry, then monetized with a small $5 one-time charge.
  • Content widens the long tail: blogs on adjacent topics like driving lesson costs and supervised hours were added in 2026-09.
  • A small amount of Reddit social traffic and 2.9% email traffic (possibly paying users returning from login/receipt emails).

Speculation:

  • The "10,000+ learners" line on the 2025 homepage may be marketing exaggeration, and it was later removed, so it can't be taken as a real user count.
  • Learner drivers leave after the test, so there is no retention or word-of-mouth loop, and growth depends entirely on search rankings; free users may churn after charging began, but revenue has started.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue. At $5 one-time, estimated from the monthly visits search brings and common paid conversion rates, monthly revenue is probably in the tens to hundreds of Australian dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Apart from brandfetch, all Google/Bing results were unrelated; the evidence comes from the site, robots/sitemap, source code, RDAP and multiple Wayback snapshots, so the timeline is highly credible.
  • The founder and team were not found at all; the Reddit referral posts could not be opened and verified; actual visits and number of paying users are unknown.
