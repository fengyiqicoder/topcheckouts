Basics

  • Product: AI UGC growth engine. Enter a website and it automatically researches the niche, writes hooks and scripts, generates AI avatar videos, posts them to TikTok/Instagram from real devices, and tracks performance; it also sells "pre-warmed accounts" ($100/month each) and a fully managed service (from $7,500/month).
  • Pricing: free 300 credits; Hobby $16/month; Pro $100/month; custom enterprise plan (official site).
  • Team (about page): Eric Owusu Sekyere (co-founder and CEO, X handle @thechidwi), Karol Zdebel (co-founder and CTO), Victor Batirow (Founding Systems Analyst), 3 people total, San Francisco; the site says "built by engineers from Google and DoorDash." No funding mentioned.
  • Launch date: domain registered 2025-06-05 (RDAP); the about page says the first line of code was written 2025-06; first Wayback snapshot 2025-07; public launch 2026-09.
  • Note: the "Pamba Delivery app" Instagram in search results, the Pamba (pediatrics) app on Google Play, and SaaSBrowser's "appointment scheduling Pamba App" are unrelated products with the same name.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Service first, product second: during the agency phase they were their own users, building up case-study numbers like "500 million views, 24¢ CPM" that went straight into the homepage headline and ad copy.
  • Paid ads: about 39% in our data, one of the main sources of new users.
  • X: the 9.2% social traffic comes mainly from X; the launch post got about 300k views, and the CEO also posts demos on X.
  • Content marketing: a burst of data-analysis blog posts in 2026-07 (the 400M-views retrospective), well suited to spreading on X/LinkedIn.

Speculation:

  • The first paying users very likely came from converting agency-phase clients and the founder circle on X (the target customer, "startups that want to acquire customers through short video", overlaps heavily with the X startup crowd). No direct evidence.
  • The product itself builds TikTok accounts for clients, and the videos carry no Pamba branding, so the output does not spread the brand.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Agency phase self-reported as "six-figure" revenue (about page; unclear whether annual or cumulative).
  • The SaaS has been publicly live for just over a month; revenue not found. The top fully managed tier is $7,500/month, so a handful of large clients could carry revenue.
  • 110 of their own accounts, 6,678 videos, 500M+ views.

Confidence and gaps

  • Team, timeline, and their own account data all come from the site's about page and blog, i.e. self-reported, medium credibility.
  • None of the X posts would load (402), only search summaries; no Product Hunt, HN, or Reddit launch records found.
  • Gaps: funding, number of paying users, whether the launch post was promoted, agency client list.
