Basics

  • Product: upload exported chats from WhatsApp / iMessage / Instagram / Messenger, and the AI character "Brandon" writes a verdict on each person in the chat (romance, friend group, family and other scenarios). The first report can be previewed for free, the rest are paid per report, with payments through Stripe. The /setup page source contains a €7.99 price string and $1–$22 price strings; the exact tiers are not verified. iMessage requires installing the Apple Silicon Mac client.
  • Company: GumGum (gumgum.app), which calls itself "a product lab in Paris run by two brothers, Matthias and Robin" (official site /about). LinkedIn lists 2–10 employees, headquartered in Paris, and the only visible employee is Matthias Bauswein. Note it is not the same company as the US ad-tech firm GumGum (gumgum.com).
  • Funding: Not found.
  • Sister products from the same lab: Moka Score (mokascore.com, registered 2026-09-16, scores a single relationship), plus a new group-chat sharing app marked "Soon".
  • Launch time: domain registered 2026-05-13 (WHOIS), earliest Wayback snapshot 2026-08-22, and /about says "Brandon launched in 2026". In our data, average checkout visits over the past 4–6 months were 0 and about 60K last month, so the actual launch and takeoff are inferred to be 2–3 months ago. In 2024 gumgum.app was only a parked page redirecting to /lander, with no sign of an earlier product.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Reports are designed to be screenshotted and sent back to the group chat. FAQ wording: "Verdicts are designed to be screenshotted and sent to group chats". The homepage "Wall of Love" shows group reactions and encourages users to DM reaction screenshots to the official Instagram.
  • Paid UGC engine: GumGum Creators pays a base fee per video (settled every Monday) plus bonuses for hits, priced by country and language, and applications come in by Instagram DM. The FAQ claims the reaction videos "get tens of millions of views every month".
  • Multilingual site (de/es/fr/he/it/pt/tr) and multilingual reports, which makes it easy to spread across creator markets in different countries.

Speculation:

  • The loop is roughly: A uploads a group chat → posts the report screenshot into the group → B and C get called out and get curious, then upload their own groups or private chats → the free first preview lowers the paywall to the minimum.
  • The top country is India and the main social source is YouTube, which may relate to low-cost-region creator placements (YouTube Shorts / Reels). Not verified.
  • The official Instagram has only about 6,074 followers and 0 posts (scraped 2026-10), which suggests traffic comes mainly from creator accounts rather than the official account.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported total of 240–250K reports, with about 50K per week recently.
  • Paid per report: the first report has a free preview, the price is suspected to be around €7.99, with several tiers, not verified. If paid conversion is 10–20%, monthly revenue would be on the order of several hundred thousand dollars (pure speculation).
  • Team of 2–10 people, no funding seen.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch date and main channels rest mainly on the company's own statements plus the domain registration date, so credibility is medium.
  • Gaps: the founders' full names are only inferred from LinkedIn (Bauswein); no links to specific viral videos, no creator list, and no launch post were found; the reason for the high India share is unknown; Reddit could not be searched.
