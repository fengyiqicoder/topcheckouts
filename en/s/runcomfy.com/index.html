In late 2023 ComfyUI (the node-based workflow tool for Stable Diffusion) blew up in the AI video/image scene, but setting up a local environment, filling in missing nodes and configuring a GPU were the biggest pain points (screens full of "red errors"). A small 2–10 person team in New York registered runcomfy.com and opened a Discord in 2023-12, then launched in 2024-01 as "ComfyUI in the cloud, working workflows guaranteed, no red errors": one-click workflow templates with nodes and models preinstalled (AnimateDiff, MagicAnimate and other hot video techniques) + a Resolver that auto-fills dependencies + GPUs rented by the hour. It then pushed "content as the entry point" to the extreme: a ComfyUI node encyclopedia launched in 2024-07 (now 18K node pages), workflows and tutorials translated into 10+ languages, Playground pages for each vendor's new models added in 2025 (about 740 pages per language), with a page ready the moment a new model drops, capturing long-tail searches like "comfyui workflows" and "power lora loader"; plus an API/Serverless offering and an affiliate program (Tolt).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Programmatic SEO: 18K node pages + workflow pages + model Playground pages x 10+ languages; non-brand keywords include node-name long tail like "power lora loader".
  • Templates as the entry point: every hot workflow (AnimateDiff, Flux, Wan etc.) gets a one-click run page with downloadable JSON.
  • Affiliate program: pages load the Tolt affiliate tracking script.
  • Community: Discord of 7,185 handles technical support.
  • AI search referrals already about 1.5% (ChatGPT, Gemini).

Speculation:

  • Publishing Playground/workflow pages the day a new model is released, to grab "<new model> comfyui workflow" searches, is the main ongoing acquisition method.
  • The "Viral (sharing workflow outputs)" classification lacks evidence; the generated outputs themselves carry no branding.

Revenue and scale clues
