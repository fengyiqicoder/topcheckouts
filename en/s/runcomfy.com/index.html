Overview

Runcomfy (runcomfy.com): Cloud-hosted ComfyUI platform with no-setup GPUs, ready workflow templates and serverless API. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Runcomfy to a checkout payment page — #287 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 111,915 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 11.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 54%, direct 42%, social 4%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 85%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $19.99/mo Pro; $239.90/yr Pro; GPU $0.99-$9.59/hr pay-as-you-go.
