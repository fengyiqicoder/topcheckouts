Overview

Kickbot (kickbot.com): Cloud chatbot for Kick streamers with commands, TTS, alerts, clips and widgets. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 34,384 visits went from Kickbot to a checkout payment page — #71 of the 607 sites we track. That is 47% up from the April–June monthly average of 23,422.

Total website visits that month were about 122,122 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 28.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 74%, search 17%, social 8%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 82%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Free to use (no tiers listed).
