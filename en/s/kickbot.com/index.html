Kick (a Twitch competitor launched 2022-12) had almost no native tools or open API early on. In May 2023, before xQc signed with Kick (2023-06) and set the platform on fire, KickBot launched Kick-specific chat overlays, follow/sub alerts and VOD downloads with one-click Discord login, and became the default third-party tool for Kick streamers. From there it scaled passively on the growth of Kick itself, claiming 150K+ streamers. Both its monetization and its spread live inside streamers' broadcasts: the clip links generated by!clip, the alert overlays and the tipping page (kickbot.com/tips/streamername, 6% platform cut) are seen and used by every streamer's viewers, so the checkout page visits are in fact mostly viewers tipping.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: cloud tools for Kick streamers (no download), including a chat bot with custom commands, timed messages, moderation, 35+ OBS overlay widgets, follow/sub alerts, 3,000+ AI TTS voices,!clip clipping, VOD download and editing, tipping, and a Stream Deck plugin (Elgato Marketplace). Free for streamers.
  • Monetization: the tipping page collects via Stripe Connect, with KickBot taking a 6% platform fee (plus Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30), paid out automatically on the 15th of each month. The site says cumulative tips exceed $900K, which at 6% implies about $54K+ in cumulative platform revenue. Sister product VoxBot (vox.bot, also under KickBot, Inc.) does cross-platform (Twitch/YouTube/Kick/TikTok/Rumble/Pump.fun, etc.) TTS plus tipping, with three tiers: Free / Premium $9.99 / Pro $24.99 per month.
  • Company: KickBot, Inc. Tracxn lists it as San Francisco, founded 2023, unfunded. Founders and team not found.
  • Launch: the kickbot.com domain was registered in 2013 (an old domain, bought later). The product first lived on kickbot.app: a Wayback snapshot from 2023-05-17 shows "The Best Bot for Kick — Widgets. Alerts. Downloads. Login with Discord". Decoding the snowflake, the official Discord server was created on 2023-05-11. kickbot.gg had snapshots by 2023-06. It moved to kickbot.com around May–August 2024.

First 1,000 users: no launch post found. Most likely from the early Kick streamer community and Discord (Discord login, Discord support server), plus the wave of streamers who moved to Kick in June 2023. KickBot grabbed the position of "the first usable bot and overlays on Kick".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Streamers put KickBot's overlays and alerts into their stream layout and the KickBot bot account runs commands in chat, so viewers and other streamers naturally see it in other people's streams.

-!clip generates shareable clip links, and there is a Clips Gallery.

  • The tipping page kickbot.com/tips/streamername is linked by streamers in their bio and chat, and viewers come in to pay (the card statement shows "KICKBOT TIP - username"), so checkout page visits grow with both streamer and viewer counts.
  • The official Kick channel, YouTube, X and Discord are community touchpoints; it is also listed on the Elgato Stream Deck marketplace.

Speculation:

  • Word of mouth between streamers ("what alert overlay are you using?"); new streamers going live on Kick search "kick bot" and find kickbot.com (exact-match domain, hence #2 in organic search).
  • Checkout visits rose from about 23K over the past 4–6 months to 34K last month, possibly tied to Kick's own growth in 2026 or promotion of the tipping and TTS features; unverified.
  • "public_profile_leaderboard" probably corresponds to the public tipping page and clip gallery; no evidence of a leaderboard was found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 150K+ streamers (the docs say 100K+, G2 says 90K+; figures are inconsistent); cumulative tips $900K+, which at a 6% cut is about $54K+ in cumulative platform fees.
  • VoxBot subscriptions $9.99 and $24.99 per month; whether KickBot itself has paid tiers not found.
  • Tracxn records no funding.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch date has two first-hand pieces of evidence (Wayback and the Discord snowflake), so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: founder identity, whether kickbot.co is the same team, the exact date of the move to.com, direct causality with the xQc migration wave, and the specific YouTube videos that drove traffic were all not found. Reddit scraping was blocked.
