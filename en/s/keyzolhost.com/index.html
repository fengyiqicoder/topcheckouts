Basics

  • Surface product: a shared-hosting reseller site built on WHMCS, Starter $1.99/month, Pro $2.99/month, Business $5.99/month (NVMe, DirectAdmin, free SSL), also selling domains (.com $12.16).
  • Entity: Keyzol Technologies LLC, 5931 Greenville Ave #1169, Dallas, TX 75206 (a commercial mailbox address); WHOIS registrant state TX, country US, registrar Cloudflare.
  • Related sites:
  • keyzol.com (registered 2023-12-05): sells Windows/Office/Visio/Project/SQL Server activation keys and "Office 365 E3 accounts", "Genuine Software Keys for Cheapest Price", with a reseller program (Become a Reseller) and WhatsApp price alerts; the footer also says "Keyzol Technologies LLC".
  • keyzol.uk (registered 2024-08-07): "Keyzol IT Related Professional Services", selling scattered $10-150 IT freelance services (UI design, installing Nextcloud, writing bash scripts), UK phone number +44 7462051831.
  • Team/country: not found; a US LLC + a UK number + multiple languages suggests an individual seller from South Asia or elsewhere running through a US shell company (speculation).

Growth loop

  • Evidence: keyzol.com has a Reseller program (the entry exists, but the detail page returns 404) and a WhatsApp price-alert channel (contact number +1 307, a Wyoming area code); the footer payment badges are Stripe/Visa/Mastercard/Discover; there are no TikTok/YouTube/Facebook/Telegram account links, which suggests the main site does not acquire customers through social content. At least three Keyzol sites share the same LLC.
  • Industry background (summary only): a Reddit r/PcBuild post mentions that "cheap Windows activation keys are all over TikTok and YouTube"; this kind of key shop usually acquires customers through influencer endorsements, promo links in YouTube video descriptions, and searches for "cheap windows key"; but no promotion traces of Keyzol itself were found. https://www.reddit.com/r/PcBuild/comments/1b7iy6j/is_there_actually_a_good_site_that_has_windows/
  • Speculation: key buyers arrive from keyzol.com (and resellers/ads/searches for "cheap windows key") and at checkout are sent to the WHMCS billing page on keyzolhost.com to pay via Stripe and similar - so keyzolhost's "checkout visits" reflect the order volume of the keyzol key business, not hosting customers. The switch from keyzol.uk to keyzolhost.com in 2026-07 may be a change of front site after the previous payment account was flagged by risk control (speculation, the redirect was not directly observed).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Hosting prices $1.99-5.99/month; key store prices were not recorded one by one.
  • If 20K checkout-page visits a month correspond to real orders, at a $5-30 order value the monthly volume would be roughly tens of thousands of dollars (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • There is first-hand page evidence that the three sites share one entity; the "front site for payments" theory is strong speculation based on "a zero-content hosting site + disproportionate checkout traffic + the previous checkout domain dropping to zero"; the redirect chain was not verified with an actual order (no order was placed, to avoid creating a transaction).
  • Operator identity, payment channel and real revenue were not found.
  • Second-pass check: 5 Google queries for keyzolhost/keyzol returned no relevant results (no Reddit posts, no Product Hunt, no media or videos); only keyzol.com's social and payment information was added, and the conclusion is unchanged. Where the first users and new users come from is still not found; "Piggyback on a platform" (that is, piggybacking on the keyzol.com key store) remains speculation.
