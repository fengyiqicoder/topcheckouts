Occasions: birthdays, weddings, parents, children, memorials. No prices on the site. Payment: Visa/MC/Amex/CB/PayPal/Apple Pay/Google Pay/Bancontact.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid social is the main engine: Meta/TikTok/Reddit pixels are all installed; organic search is only 2% (sitedata); in our channel data Mail is #1 and Paid Social #2.
  • Ad creative formula: fictional "singer persona" (French Instagram ad "VOTRE INTERPRÈTE: Antoine, 46 ans - Montpellier", Italian "Marco Bellini") + AI-generated family scene images + emotional hook copy ("she cried at the first note"). https://www.instagram.com/reel/DblFkYkI1e2/ (summary only)
  • Branded output: the deliverable is a private votremelodie.com preview page, and users post the preview page and song to Facebook/Threads (Facebook posts by Didier Morel and Agnes Hogie both carry VOTREMELODIE.COM link cards), so recipients and their friends all see the brand.
  • High Trustpilot ratings as trust signal (2,592 Trustpilot reviews); Similarweb shows 2.42% of outbound traffic from trustpilot.com going to votremelodie.com, so the Trustpilot page itself drives traffic.
  • Cloning into new languages: /it/ + an Italian Facebook page.

Speculation:

  • First users: no "origin story"; almost certainly bought directly through Facebook/Instagram ads during the May Mother's Day window, with no PH/Reddit/press launch.
  • Classic Chinese/Hong Kong cross-border DTC playbook: the same template cloned across languages, possibly with sister sites (similar yourownmelody, giftyourmelody, etc.; connection unconfirmed).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Prices not public; reviews mention "a bit expensive" and a 25 € download license. Similar services usually charge 40–100 € per song (speculation).
  • last month visits to Stripe 221K (sitedata) / 292K in our data; at a 5–10% checkout conversion that is roughly 10–30K orders a month, i.e. monthly revenue on the order of a million euros (speculation, very wide margin of error).
  • 2,592 Trustpilot reviews, site claims "2,000+ customers" (this slogan does not match the review count; the customer count should be well above 2,000).

Confidence and gaps

  • Entity, tech stack, AI images, Trustpilot and sitedata figures are all directly verifiable first-hand, credible.
  • Second follow-up check: added the Reddit pixel, sitedata's last month #3 entertainment ranking and 2% organic search, the fictional singer persona ad creatives, the Italian market expansion, evidence of users resharing preview pages, and the fact that all Trustpilot reviews are tagged "unprompted"; the "5-year anniversary" claim was checked and does not appear on the homepage.
  • Still missing: ad volume and start date in the Meta Ad Library; the founding team; whether the songs are AI-generated (not officially acknowledged); still no evidence for the "covered by Le Monde etc." claim.
