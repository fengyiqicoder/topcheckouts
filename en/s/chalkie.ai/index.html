Basics

  • Product: AI-generated lesson slides, unit sequences, worksheets, interactive classroom activities and rubrics aligned to curriculum standards (Common Core, NGSS, UK National Curriculum, TEKS, etc.); supports 40 languages and 100+ countries; has a schools edition (with DPA) and Live Activities.
  • Company: Chalkie AI Ltd (UK); LinkedIn says founded 2024, 2-10 employee bracket but shows 14 staff (Chris Moorst, Mark Hughes, Smilyan Pavlov, Ye Myat Min, Phil Daneshyar, etc., with members in the UK and Singapore). Phil Daneshyar posts job openings on LinkedIn (head of teacher education, first growth marketer), presumably a co-founder, unverified.
  • Launch: domain registered 2024-07-09 (RDAP); first Wayback capture 2024-08-06, titled "Chalkie - AI Lesson Generator For Teachers", "Designed with teachers for teachers"; Trustpilot profile claimed 2024-10.
  • Pricing: free tier (5 resources every 7 days); Pro $6.65/month or $79.80/year; Max $12.99/month (profile).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Branded output: teacher-generated lessons and worksheets go straight into the classroom; homepage showcases "best lessons of the week"; public lesson/worksheet library (profile).
  • Teacher community word of mouth: teachers discuss it unprompted in Facebook groups (Australia, homeschooling); Facebook page has 46k followers; TikTok account @chalkie.teachers posts short videos with a "teacher persona"; YouTube channel runs tutorials and weekly webinars.
  • Institutional/regional portals: the Spanish Castilla-La Mancha education portal brings nearly 5% of referrals, and Chile is the second-largest country, suggesting recommendations within Spanish-language education systems.
  • SEO: from 2026, lots of "competitor alternative/template" articles intercept searches for Twinkl, TPT and MagicSchool.
  • Started selling to schools in the second half of 2026 (trade events, schools edition, DPA).
  • Speculation:
  • First users most likely came from UK teachers the team knew ("designed with teachers") and teacher Facebook groups; no PH/HN launch found.
  • The surge in Chile/Spain may be from a recommendation by an education authority or training course, or forwarding in Spanish-speaking teacher WhatsApp groups (WhatsApp appears among social sources in our data).

Revenue and scale clues

  • ARR: 2025-10 $1M → 2026-05 $5M (or £5M), per ARR Club, source not stated.
  • Teacher count: 2025-09 50,000 → 2026-01 250,000 → 2026-10 1M+ (website/LinkedIn).
  • Team of about 14; no funding information seen (PitchBook page could not be opened).

Confidence and gaps
