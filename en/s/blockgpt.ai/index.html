On 2025-05-18 an anonymous small team bought (re-registered) blockgpt.ai, which had belonged to a Web3 "decentralized AI" project, and built a tool where you "enter text or upload an image and get a Minecraft building schematic (.schem/.litematic) in 30-60 seconds", billing itself as "the first AI tool for generating Minecraft builds". In January 2026 it launched a burst of blog tutorials and a public gallery; every building a user generates becomes a searchable /build/<name> page (1,000+ already indexed by Wayback), which together with generic terms like "minecraft build generator / ai minecraft builder" brings non-brand search to about half of traffic. It then converts visitors with YouTube/TikTok showing generated results, a ChatGPT plugin, 5 free previews a day and a $1 first-build trial, and has expanded to a visual mod editor at mods.blockgpt.ai.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: an AI Minecraft build generator: text/images generate structures, exported as.schem/.litematic/.schematic/.nbt, supporting Java and Bedrock; a community gallery (about 15,000 builds free to download, profile); a ChatGPT plugin (https://blockgpt.ai/support); and mods.blockgpt.ai, a drag-and-drop mod editor.
  • Team: not public; blog posts are signed "BlockGPT Team"; has a Discord (https://discord.gg/SWEecHnkUe).
  • Pricing: 5 free previews per day; first build $1; subscriptions Basic $9/month (8 generations), Standard $19/month (20), Pro $49/month (60), annual billing saves 17% (https://withai.top/tool/blockgpt, profile).
  • Domain: current registration is 2025-05-18 (RDAP/whois); the 2023 snapshots are a different project ("BlockGPT - Connecting the AI world to blockchains", Web3), unrelated to this product. The same-named aiblockgpt.com (AI literacy education) and X accounts @blockgpt / @BlockGPT_AI are also not this product.
  • Traffic (profile): United States 23%, United Kingdom 6.8%, India 5.7%; search 83.5% (about 50% non-brand: minecraft build generator, minecraft ai generator, ai minecraft builder and building long-tail), direct 12%, social is only YouTube.

Where the first users came from: no launch post was found. The evidence points to search (few competitors for terms like "minecraft ai build generator") plus a YouTube channel (bio: "first ever AI to Minecraft Build generator"). Specific numbers were not found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • User-generated content becomes SEO pages: each generated build gets a /build/ page, Wayback has 1,000+ indexed, the profile says the gallery has 15,000 builds, and non-brand search is about 50%.
  • Holding generic terms: the title reads "AI Minecraft Build Generator | Text to Schematic AI".
  • Free previews + a $1 first order lower the trial barrier; the ChatGPT plugin adds another entry point.

Speculation:

  • Short Minecraft build videos ("AI generates a statue/castle in one click") are naturally watchable on YouTube Shorts/TikTok and are a source of brand awareness, though social is a small share of traffic.
  • When players show generated builds on servers or in videos they mention the tool's name, giving some word of mouth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue or user numbers. The gallery has about 15,000 builds (profile).
  • Subscriptions are $9-49/month with few generations, so per-generation cost is high, suggesting it relies on a small number of heavy players paying (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The domain change of hands, the January 2026 content rollout and the scale of /build/ pages have RDAP/Wayback/blog evidence.
  • Gaps: the real launch date, the team, YouTube/TikTok data (pages could not be fetched) and revenue were not found; search results were full of unrelated same-name projects (the Web3 BlockGPT, aiblockgpt.com).
