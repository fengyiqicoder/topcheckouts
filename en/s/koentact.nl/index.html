Basics

  • Product: a Dutch language school in central Amsterdam with evening/morning/intensive/conversation/online classes, private lessons and corporate courses; evening course €525, intensive course €975, online evening course €420 (profile).
  • Founder: Koen Gijzel, who studied French and Dutch linguistics in Amsterdam and Paris (about page). The team page lists 4 staff, plus several teachers.
  • Location: Elandsgracht 70 (venue for the Wednesday language night); open day at Da Costastraat 36.
  • Reputation: 520 Google reviews; AICexpats (an expat services platform) has a product page.
  • Current traffic (profile): Netherlands 94%; direct 44%, paid 41.9%, search 8.4%; non-brand keywords dutch courses amsterdam, intensive course dutch, taalthuis (a competitor's name).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • First students: word of mouth among French students → corporate demand from Air France KLM employees (the about page calls it "the best recommendation").
  • Community acquisition: ALC holds weekly language nights in Koentact's classroom, with 18,700 Meetup members; newly arrived expats come for free socializing first and naturally encounter the school's courses, while graduates stay in the circle.
  • Open days + free trial lesson conversion.
  • Paid: 41.9% of current traffic comes from paid search, bidding on "dutch courses amsterdam" and the competitor term taalthuis.

Speculation:

  • Meetup's own recommendations (new users searching "language exchange Amsterdam") keep bringing people to ALC, probably the main free channel in the early days.
  • 520 Google reviews support local search and map conversion.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Course price €320–975; student numbers and revenue: Not found.
  • A venue, 15 volunteers, 4 staff plus several teachers: a small local offline school.

Confidence and gaps

  • The founding story comes from the school's own website, with no third-party coverage.
  • No data on annual student numbers, revenue, or ALC conversion rate.
  • Takeoff year is counted from the founding of ALC (2010); in reality it was an offline business that started up gradually over 2009–2010, with no single takeoff event.

How did Koentact get started?

Koen Gijzel, a linguistics graduate who originally taught French, saw word of mouth among his students reach employees of Air France KLM, who came to him to learn Dutch; in 2009-09 he opened a Dutch language school starting from there.
