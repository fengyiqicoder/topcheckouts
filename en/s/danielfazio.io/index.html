Basics

  • Product: 1-hour one-on-one acquisition consulting (reviewing the acquisition process, building a financial model, advice on 10 areas), for owners earning $5K–$300K per month; no refunds; the page says 150+ people have bought. A similar consultation on Gumroad (coldemailwizard.gumroad.com) is priced at $1,700 with 77 sales.
  • Who is behind it: Daniel Fazio, Florida State University, now living in Tampa. His early X brand was "Cold Email Wizard" (140K+ followers); TikTok 17K followers; YouTube channel @danielfazio. His ventures: the Cold Email Mastery course; Client Ascension (co-founded with Andre Haykal Jr., Dan Crowley and Christian Bonnier, Tampa); ListKit (B2B lead data SaaS, co-founded with Andre Haykal Jr.); Olympia (a high-end mastermind for agencies earning $30K+/month); the AI Business Challenge 4-day live class. No outside funding seen.
  • Launch date: WHOIS created 2023-05-26.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First clients: free work in exchange for case studies (the Instagram agency period).
  • First audience: Twitter cold email tips → agency owners/freelancers follow → course buyers.
  • Social proof loop: free consulting in exchange for students tweeting their results → landing page screenshots → new buyers.
  • Email list (50K+) + Loom videos close the deals; the same customer is upsold layer by layer (course → Client Ascension → Olympia → ListKit).
  • New customers at this stage: YouTube long videos + description links, after which users search "daniel fazio" to enter the site.
  • Speculation:
  • The 2026-09 surge may come from YouTube descriptions/email blasts pushing 1-on-1 consulting in a concentrated way, or from upsells after the AI Business Challenge live class.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported combined monthly revenue of about $1.2M (Client Ascension >$400K/month, ListKit >$800K/month); the four co-founders together claim "$30M+ in revenue". All self-reported, unverified.
  • Consulting: Gumroad $1,700, 77 sales; the site says 150+ people have bought.
  • Client Ascension 2,000+ purchases, 1,000+ students.
