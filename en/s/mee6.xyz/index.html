Overview

Mee6 (mee6.xyz): Discord bot, visible to the whole server once added; long-running. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Mee6 to a checkout payment page — #458 of the 607 sites we track. That is 30% down from the April–June monthly average of 12,217.

Total website visits that month were about 315,465 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 2.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 76%, search 22%, messaging 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows built-in sharing (medium), and direct plus brand search is about 98%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
