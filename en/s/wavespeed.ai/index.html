Overview

Wavespeed (wavespeed.ai): Hosted platform giving developers and creators access to 1,000+ AI image/video/audio/3D/LLM models via web, API, CLI/MCP. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 189,112 visits went from Wavespeed to a checkout payment page — #4 of the 607 sites we track. That is 18% down from the April–June monthly average of 231,544.

Total website visits that month were about 559,027 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 33.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 79%, search 21%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 8%, with queries like “video watermark remover”, “seedance 2.0”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
