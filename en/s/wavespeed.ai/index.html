Basics

  • Product: hosted inference platform for multimodal generation (image / video / audio / 3D / LLM), claiming 1000+ models, one API, low latency, no cold starts. Usage-based billing.
  • Company: WaveSpeedAI; the about page says "founded in 2025 by CEO Zeyi Cheng, headquartered in Singapore" (address 3 Phillip Street, Singapore); Preqin lists Hangzhou, so the team is likely Chinese with a Singapore entity.
  • Founder: Zeyi Cheng (成泽毅), author of the open-source libraries stable-fast / ParaAttention / Comfy-WaveSpeed, previously led inference engineering at a Silicon Valley AI infrastructure company (reports say he improved that company's image generation inference performance 10x). Co-founder and CTO Yangbing Li (English name David Li), formerly at Meicai and DP Technology.
  • Funding: announced a multi-million-dollar angel round on 2025-04-15 (36Kr / PingWest), investors not disclosed. No Series A found.
  • Domain registered: 2025-01-16 (GoDaddy WHOIS); earliest Wayback snapshot 2025-01-19.

Growth loop

Evidence

  • Open source → hosted: the homepages of 3 high-star repos all point to wavespeed.ai, so ComfyUI users see the brand when installing the nodes.
  • New-model capture: every hot model (Wan, Hunyuan, Seedance, Seedream, Nano Banana) gets listed along with blog / HN / X posts, and multilingual blog posts catch long-tail "X model API" searches (Organic Search is the second-largest channel, consistent with this).
  • Affiliate commission: 10% commission, capped at $150 per customer, tracked via Tolt, aimed at YouTubers, tutorial authors, newsletters and developers (YouTube leads social traffic, consistent with this). https://wavespeed.ai/static/affiliate
  • Also a "Be a Creator" creator program (details Not found).
  • B2B integrations: the about page lists Freepik, Novita AI, MiniMax, Draw Things and others as "trusted by" partners, whose downstream users indirectly use it.

Speculation

  • Developers write the WaveSpeed API into their own open-source projects, ComfyUI nodes and tutorial code, and the next developer copying it becomes a new user ("code samples as distribution").
  • Public model pages / example galleries have some UGC showcase quality (the known mechanism tag is public_profile_leaderboard, but no specific public leaderboard was found).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site claims 1M+ users and 1000+ models.
  • No public MRR; Latka has no revenue data either.
  • Funding: 2025-04 angel round, several million dollars.
  • Checkout visits averaged about 230K in April–June and about 190K in September, high but declining, possibly as new-model hype rotates.

Confidence and gaps

  • Founder background, open-source repo dates, domain dates and funding are all hard evidence.
  • Gaps: Reddit is blocked by WebFetch, so the date and traction of the Comfy-WaveSpeed launch posts on r/StableDiffusion / r/comfyui were not obtained; no founder account of where the first paying users came from; the 1M user figure is the company's own claim.
