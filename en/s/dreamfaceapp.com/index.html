Overview

Dreamfaceapp (dreamfaceapp.com): AI generator app for avatar videos, AI videos and AI images on web, iOS and Android. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Dreamfaceapp to a checkout payment page — #197 of the 607 sites we track. That is 38% down from the April–June monthly average of 27,950.

Total website visits that month were about 133,581 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 12.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 91%, referrals/email/paid 8%, search 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 92%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
