Basics

  • Product: AI that animates photos (singing, talking, dancing), plus digital-human talking videos, TTS and voice cloning, pet singing, and AI image generation. Available on iOS, Android and Web, supporting 30+ languages.
  • Company: the App Store developer is New Port LLC, the site footer says © NewPort LLC, and the support email is dreamface@newportai.com. The G2 profile shows headquarters in Redwood City, USA. The App Store Chinese title is "DreamFace: AI 数字人口播视频一键生成-宠物唱歌", and there is a lot of discussion in Chinese communities, so it is probably an overseas-expansion project by a Chinese or Chinese-diaspora team (unconfirmed).
  • Founder and funding: not found. The "team interview" on the official blog in 2025-11 is also signed only "Dreamface Team", with no founder revealed.
  • Note: the "DreamFace" in the ShanghaiTech and Deemos Technology SIGGRAPH 2023 paper (text-to-3D digital human) is a different project of the same name and is unrelated to this app.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • The App Store copy emphasizes "daily viral templates" and a dance feature "made for viral dance videos on TikTok and Instagram"; the product itself is designed around "make a short video and post it".
  • There is an invite code system: creators post AI pet videos on Instagram with an invite code and a cloudf.dreamfaceapp.com link (the 2026-04 Russian creator case).
  • Official content centers on "recreate a viral video" (pets doing trending dances, babies rapping), and user output goes straight into material for short-video platforms.
  • Free users can generate about 10 videos each but can only download standard quality (from a competitor YouCam's blog, needs rechecking); paying removes the limits, possibly including the watermark.
  • Speculation:
  • Videos from the free tier carry a watermark or app mark and spread on TikTok, Kwai and Reels, forming a "branded output" flow back to the product.
  • Brazil is the main market: this fits Brazilian users' habit of forwarding funny videos on WhatsApp and Kwai, and the forwarding chain brings new users back.
  • The Chinese self-media promo articles are paid volume placements, and it is also doing ASO and ad buying (the typical playbook of an overseas-expansion tool app).

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site claims "Loved by Millions Users Worldwide"; the 2025-11 official interview claims 20M+ users and 200K+ subscriptions (company figures).
  • It once had a one-time or long-term subscription of about 200 RMB (Zhihu). No public MRR or download count (Sensor Tower and similar data not checked).

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch time (first half of 2022) is supported by WHOIS and the App ID, so it is credible.
  • The team, funding and source of the first users were all not found. Which specific viral video drove it up was also not found; the "short-video template spread" claim is mainly inferred from product design.
  • Second-pass check: added the official interview (product started from Live Photo, lip-sync was the breakthrough, 20M users / 200K subscriptions) and the evidence of a Russian creator promoting with an invite code; the first users and the specific viral event are still not found, and the founder is still not public.
