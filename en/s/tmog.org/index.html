Basics

  • Product: TMOG, a free cross-platform task manager (Windows C++/Win32, macOS Swift/AppKit, Linux C++/Qt 6) with 12 views; TMOG Pro adds Flight Recorder (record-and-replay telemetry), benchmarks, disk space, iPhone remote monitoring (Mac) and more.
  • Author: Dave W. Plummer, former Microsoft engineer (author of Task Manager and the Windows port of Space Cadet Pinball), YouTube channel Dave's Garage; operating entity Plummers' Software LLC (US). No funding.
  • Baggage: his early company SoftwareOnline was sued by the Washington State Attorney General over products such as a "registry cleaner" and settled (HN comments dig this up repeatedly).
  • Domain WHOIS registered 2026-07-29 (the author says the.com was taken so he used.org; Pro is sold at tmog.store / store.tmog.org). Wayback was unavailable this time.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder brand + press chain: at least two Tom's Hardware pieces, Windows Central, Engadget and OMG! Ubuntu all hang their headlines on "the father of Task Manager".
  • The author discusses the development process on his YouTube channel (HN comment: "I've seen Dave discussing the process on the YT channel").
  • Free → $39.95 one-time license (3 computers) / $99.99 nine-computer pack. https://tmog.org/pro.html

Speculation:

  • Thailand as the third-largest traffic source (5.85%) comes from coverage by Thai tech outlet droidsans.com (5.22% referral, see our profile); Germany's linuxnews.de likewise — tech-press re-reporting carries the author's brand into non-English markets.
  • Videos on Dave's Garage, with its million-plus subscribers, are the main traffic source (Direct is the top channel), and "vibe coding a 107-page spec" itself became a secondary talking point in AI-coding circles.
  • No obvious viral mechanism inside the product; distribution relies almost entirely on the author's persona, i.e. a "streamer sells it" pattern.
