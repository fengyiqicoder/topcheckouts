  • Content creators: YouTube "make money with CPA" tutorials (2023-12), TikTok creators carrying go.nodemaven.com tracking links (2026).
  • User referrals: a successful referral gives 50% bonus traffic (https://nodemaven.com/referral-program/); a user in an About page testimonial says a friend recommended it.
  • Ecosystem integrations: antidetect browsers such as Multilogin, Dolphin Anty, AdsPower, GoLogin (listed in the AffTank review).
  • SEO: landing pages made in bulk by platform (Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, OpenAI, Grass…), country and city, plus free detection tools; in our profile non-brand search is about 36% (including chatgpt plus free, прокси тг, happ vpn).
  • Russian-language market: supports Russian bank cards and YooMoney, referrals include vc.ru, and Russia is the second-largest traffic source.
  • Speculation:
  • Kenya first: locally, the "make money online / CPA / remote task account" crowd watches YouTube/TikTok tutorials and buys proxies, and links carrying commissions spread in WhatsApp/Telegram groups, i.e. "creator affiliate" rolling in a particular community. There is only one piece of indirect evidence, the CPA tutorial video; no direct evidence for Kenyan creators was found.
  • Main sources of new users = commission-driven content creators + affiliate review sites + SEO long tail, all three depending on high commissions.
