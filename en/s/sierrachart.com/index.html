Basics

  • Product: Windows desktop financial charting and trading software (futures/stocks), known for low latency, order flow (footprint charts, Market Depth, MBO), Volume/TPO Profile, and ACSIL C++ custom studies; it runs its own Denali Exchange Data Feed and Teton order routing, and can also connect to Rithmic, CQG, IB and others; it launched the open protocol DTCProtocol.org.
  • Company: the early entity was New Zealand's Sierra Pacific Software Ltd (NZ company number 3054245, "trading as www.sierrachart.com"), dissolved in 2021-08; it now presents itself through a registered agent address in Sheridan, Wyoming, with operating contact Teton IT Management Services (president Anthony Sarafa) (compiled from Elite Trader posts, partly forum speculation). The About Us page says "since 1996", "done by engineers around the world", and it runs its own market data, order routing and web servers.
  • Founder: a 2015 Optimus Futures interview calls the founder "Anthony"; an Elite Trader post gives the full name Anthony Sarafa (former president of Sierra Pacific Software). A 2026 YouTube video title mentions "Eric Kruger", which could not be verified (from the summary only, possibly the video author's mistake).
  • Pricing: Base Standard $26/month, Base Advanced $36, Integrated Standard $36, Integrated Advanced $46, Integrated Advanced MBO $56; prepay for 1/3/6/12 months; 21-day trial; the company explicitly does not sell perpetual licenses.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Acquisition channels by its own account: support from brokers and clearing firms, trading educators, and referrals from existing users (About Us); no marketing (Optimus interview).
  • Broker distribution: brokers such as Ironbeam push Sierra Chart + Teton to their clients as their own platform; trading community owners direct members to open accounts at brokers and connect Sierra.
  • Creator supply: the Custom Studies store, paid Chartbooks/templates (r/SierraChart "are paid Chartbooks worth it"), and TikTok traders selling templates.
  • Community: the official Support Board forum + r/SierraChart + a user-built Discord. Channel profile: direct 57%, search 42% (brand keywords 98.7%).
  • Speculation: the first users came from trading newsgroups/forums in the late 1990s and the compatibility lists of data vendors (such as DTN and eSignal); order-flow trading education (after 2015) drove a new wave of users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • "Thousands of users" in 2015. Price $26–56/month, plus Denali exchange fees. Estimating from about 17K monthly checkout visits, paying accounts may be in the tens of thousands (pure speculation).
