In 2024 they first attracted users with a "free forever + Chrome extension to save ads" competitor ad library (positioned against Foreplay), then sold lifetime licenses on AppSumo in 2024-04 to land a first few hundred paying brands and agencies plus cash flow. Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Launch platform: the 2024-04 AppSumo lifetime deal (sold out) brought early users and cash flow in one go.
  • Free acquisition: "free forever, no credit card" in 2024, with the competitor ad library + Chrome extension that users reach for whenever they save an ad while browsing the Facebook ad library.
  • Media-buying KOLs and buyer word of mouth: homepage testimonials come from DTC media-buying KOLs on X; media buyers promote Ad Launcher on X unprompted (28K views); media buyers on LinkedIn share "bulk uploading 50+ ads a day". Our data shows brand keywords at 67.9%, meaning most people hear about it and then search the name.
  • Free migration service: actively poaching users from competitors.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 users most likely consisted of AppSumo buyers plus free ad-library users (already 500+ brands by 2024-03).
  • Users share analysis snapshots and creative feedback links with clients and colleagues (mentioned in our profile), creating A-brings-B spread between agencies and brands; no direct evidence to quantify it.
  • Malaysia accounts for 12% of traffic, possibly a local agency team using it at scale; reason unknown.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 5,000+ brands and agencies (self-reported); paid tiers $150-$499/month. If 5-10% pay at an average of $200/month, ARR would be about $600K-$1.2M (pure speculation).
  • AppSumo lifetime deal sold out with 18 reviews, a small-to-mid-sized AppSumo launch.
  • Team of about 19 (LinkedIn).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: no earlier report in the notes. Search results were polluted by same-named companies (adnova-ai.com, adnova.digital, Singapore's ADNOVA AI PTE) and generic AI ad tool roundups; evidence comes mainly from Wayback homepages over the years, AppSumo, LinkedIn, Trustpilot and X posts.
  • The launch timeline and user-count trend have first-hand Wayback evidence, credible.
  • Gaps: founder interview; AppSumo units sold and price; whether Andrew Faris is a paid endorser or investor is unknown; revenue unknown.
