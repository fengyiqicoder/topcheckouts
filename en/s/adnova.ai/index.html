Overview

Adnova (adnova.ai): AI platform for ad creative analytics, inspiration research and bulk Meta ad launching for brands/agencies. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 10,969 visits went from Adnova to a checkout payment page — #317 of the 607 sites we track. That is 127% up from the April–June monthly average of 4,842.

Total website visits that month were about 42,280 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 25.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 68%, referrals 24%, social 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 68%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $1K-$10K/mo (Creative Analytics); $1K-$10K/mo (Inspirations); $10 per competit.
