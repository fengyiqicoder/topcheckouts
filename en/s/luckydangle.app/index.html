The Show HN on launch day, 2026-08-12, got only 3 points and went nowhere; the real spark was Instagram Reels. Basics

  • Product: a small app for Mac (menu bar, macOS 14+) and Windows (tray); the charm uses rope physics and everything except the charm is click-through; 11+ charms each with a "small ritual" plus any emoji/photo; supports the luckydangle://bless command (can be hooked into deploy scripts or git hooks to "bless" them).
  • Price: Lucky $7.77 / €7.77 / ₹777 (the press page gives the India price as ₹201, the homepage says ₹777, inconsistent); Extra Lucky $11.11 / ₹1,111 (same app, the extra is counted as support); pay once, no subscription or account, UPI available in India.
  • Who runs it: Karthik Mahadevan, company Karthik Mahadevan B.V. (Amsterdam, Netherlands), one-person team, bootstrapped (TrustMRR); X @karthikio with about 1,209 followers, bio "ai / smartglasses / accessibility"; Instagram @karthik.mahadeva with about 3,028 followers. Inspired by the protective charms he saw outside South Indian shops growing up.
  • Launch timing: domain registered 2026-08-07; Mac 1.0 on 2026-08-12; Windows 1.0.0 on 2026-08-26; Mac App Store on 2026-09-23 (1.7).
  • Traffic (profile data): search 50.3% (brand keywords 87.6%: lucky dangle, lucky dangle for mac), direct 37.3%, referral 7.5%; mainly Indian users.

Where the first users came from: the HN launch got 3 points and the author's X has only about 1,200 followers, so the launch brought almost no traffic. The first wave of users at scale came from Indian Instagram Reels in early 2026-09: the small account @dusktillus got a reel past 1.9M views, then tech creators followed; the "post a video for a refund" incentive got buyers to film it themselves.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: the charm shows up in screenshots, screen recordings and screen shares, and the site makes this an explicit selling point ("It shows up in screenshots and screen shares. When someone asks, you have a story to tell").
  • Refund for posting a video: the site says "Post your own and you can earn your money back"; the @dusktillus caption confirms "we got the refund".
  • The site displays user reels as social proof; the press page offers assets and gives the app free to media.
  • India localization: Indian protective-charm theme + rupee pricing + UPI payment.

Speculation:

  • Revenue has fallen back from its peak since 2026-09 (total $6.5K, about $400 in the last 30 days), which suggests a long tail after one burst of reels, with no sustained acquisition mechanism.
  • The open-source clones show it spread widely among Indian developers, but they also divert paying users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • TrustMRR (Stripe verified): total $6,532, $395–411 in the last 30 days, no subscriptions. At $7.77 that is roughly 800+ paid orders (more if the cheaper India tier is common, estimate).
  • The breakout reel has 1.9M+ views and 107K likes.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The useful search results were the official site, press page, notes, the author's personal pages, Show HN, TrustMRR, GitHub clones and YouTube; the "review OR alternative" group was entirely unrelated (dating apps). The HN API, Instagram public meta and fxtwitter were also used to fill in numbers.
  • Among the reel posters, only the order of @dusktillus on 09-08 is confirmed; the date and views of the @technikindia reel were not obtained.
  • The India price is inconsistent between ₹201 and ₹777, and which is current was not verified.
