Basics

  • Product: Assetto Corsa multiplayer platform and content packs: 60+ free cars, 30+ free maps, 100+ free servers (city/highway traffic), individual and crew leaderboards, racing seasons (Series 6/6.7), custom servers; a one-click Windows launcher.
  • Pricing: Tier 0 free (public servers); Public+ $2.42/month ($29/year); Midnight $5.58/month ($67/year); Underground $6.67/month ($80/year).
  • Team: the About page names only the founder "Chris", who started from a personal hobby project; the footer states it is not affiliated with Assetto Corsa's official Kunos. It also runs a FiveM roleplay server, "No Hesi RP" (2.0 released 2023-09).
  • Partner brands: Adro (body kits), Moza Racing (sim equipment, affiliate link), Valvetronic (exhaust).
  • Scale: the site claims "over 1 million drivers" and 200K+ active members on the homepage (100K+ in 2024); TikTok @nohesigg 268K followers, 6.7M likes; YouTube @NoHesiGG 81.8K subscribers, 23.68M total views, 207 videos (channel registered 2018-10-29).
  • Domain: nohesi.gg registered 2022-07-06 (WHOIS), first Wayback capture 2024-03-30.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Short-video content: the official account says its content has "billions of views"; TikTok 268K followers, YouTube 80K subscribers; players and creators post weaving runs and new car reviews.
  • Creator revenue share: the Rewardful affiliate program plus creators putting VIP links in video descriptions.
  • Lower barrier to entry: the site lists cheap Assetto Corsa purchase links (Steam, G2A $6.90, Instant Gaming $4.49, with affiliate commission), and the one-click launcher replaces the old dozen-step install tutorial.
  • Community retention: leaderboards, seasons, crew rankings, Discord; in our data direct visits are 54.9% and brand keywords 88.7%, with Discord and YouTube as the social sources.

Speculation:

  • The first players came from the existing Assetto Corsa modding circle and Discord (AC itself has a huge mod community), and TikTok weaving videos then broke out to young car fans.
  • Every high-score video carries No Hesi's HUD, map and leaderboard, so viewers know where to go play, and the output carries the brand by nature.
  • "No Hesi" is already a term in street racing culture (there is also a rap meme of the same name on TikTok), so the name comes with built-in search volume.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 1M+ registered drivers (self-reported), 200K+ active members.
  • VIP annual fees $29–$80; at a rough 1–3% paying rate, annual revenue could be in the range of hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars (speculation, no data).
  • Our data: United States 35.9%, Germany 7.2%, Canada 5.6%.

Confidence and gaps

  • Prices, self-reported scale, social data and domain dates are credible; about the founder we only know the name Chris.
  • The launch search group returned only irrelevant vulnerability advisories, Reddit could not be opened, and the About page has no dates.
  • Gaps: Chris's full name and background, the first viral video, the specific milestones when it took off in 2022–2023, revenue.
