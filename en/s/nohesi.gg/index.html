Overview

Nohesi (nohesi.gg): Driving-game content platform for Assetto Corsa with original cars, highway maps, custom servers and cut-up competitions. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Nohesi to a checkout payment page — #279 of the 607 sites we track. That is 64% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 201,754 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 6.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 55%, search 34%, social 7%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows multiplayer rooms, public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 89%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Free (Tier 0 Public, 1.5 server hours/day); $2.42/mo or $29/yr (Tier 1 Public+).
