Basics

  • Product: an AI video editing agent. Paste a YouTube link or upload a long video, and it automatically picks highlights, cuts them to vertical, adds captions, and schedules posts to TikTok, Instagram, X, LinkedIn and Facebook. Later added conversational editing ("describe the edit you want"), a REST API, MCP connectors (Claude, Cursor, Codex, etc.), and landing pages such as Twitch clipping.
  • Target users: clippers, creators, agencies, and brands that make long videos.
  • Pricing: Starter $19.99/month, Creator $49.99/month, Pro $99.99/month; no free tier listed on the site.
  • Founder: Vadim Strizheus (X @VadimStrizheus), 19, joined a marketing startup around age 17, quit at 18 to build the product; first spent about 4 months as a "Lovable prompt engineer," then had Claude teach him to code, and now mainly uses Codex. Tech stack Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, PostHog, DataFast, with AWS for video rendering.
  • Our data: United States 35.4%, India 16.8%, Brazil 9.0%; search 65.4% (brand keywords 91.4%), direct 25.9%, social 5.2% (X).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder content drives it: X first, then Instagram, both with the founder himself on camera or posting; new customers see the content and then search the brand name to arrive (brand keywords 91.4% in our data, social referral only 5.2%, showing that traffic from content platforms mostly comes back in the form of "searching the name").
  • Copying formats: spend 45 minutes a day analyzing competitors' viral videos -> copy the format -> swap in his own product; "ride one format until saturated and then switch to the next."
  • ManyChat auto-DMs a link on comment keywords, turning Instagram engagement into visits.
  • The Starter Story interview and its re-shares (2026-10) brought a wave of startup-circle exposure around "19, can't code, $10K a month."
  • Developer channel: API + MCP connectors and a Claude page, hanging onto the AI agent wave.

Speculation:

  • Clippers are themselves people who post content on social platforms, and clips made with Vugola carry no branding, so the product itself spreads weakly and relies mainly on the founder's content.
  • The X bio "Coming soon to bnb" and the Orynth project page may involve exposure in crypto communities / tokens, not verified.
  • The site's 95,000 creators figure may be cumulative signups or include free trials; the basis is unclear.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported MRR about $11,200 (2026-10, Starter Story), about 400 paying users, about 10K signups, conversion about 4%; about $2-3K/month in the X stage. All founder self-reported, not independently verified.
  • The site claims 95,000+ creators.

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site, written from scratch. The main evidence is biggo's retelling of the Starter Story interview and an Instagram re-share post, all founder-reported data; the Wayback first-capture time and the RDAP registration date are credible.
  • Gaps: the official launch date (whether there was a Product Hunt launch was not found), the specific posts the first users came from, the respective contributions of the X stage and the Instagram stage; Vadim's X posts returned 402 on WebFetch, so only snippets could be used.
