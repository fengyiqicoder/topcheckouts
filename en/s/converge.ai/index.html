Basics

  • Product: Enter Pro (AI development Agent, from idea to deployable app with database, auth, hosting, payments), Framia Pro (AI marketing/video assets), Converge Work (team + Agent workspace), Combos (AI collaboration to make playable games); subdomains enter.converge.ai, work.converge.ai, combos.converge.ai.
  • Who built it: Converge AI (site lists no legal entity or address). On Product Hunt, Enter Pro's maker is Eric Zhang, with Zhongsheng Ji and Rohan Chaubey (a well-known hunter-for-hire on PH, from memory, unverified) on the launch team. Team background and location not found; PH describes "Enter Pro, the builder layer of Converge AI".
  • Pricing: $20/mo (first month $16 for new users), 100 credits = $1.07 (profile data).
  • Domain: converge.ai registered 2017-12, 2016-2025 snapshots belong to other owners; transferred 2025-12-04 (RDAP transfer), new site snapshots from 2026-08. enter.pro is an aged domain from 2007, transferred 2025-09-09, snapshots from 2026-01-16 read "Enter | Chat to build websites & apps". X account @ConvergeAI_X registered 2026-05-08, 2,259 followers.
  • Traffic (profile data): Brazil 31.6%, United States 11.9%, India 6.8%; paid 47.6%, direct 46.3%; paid terms are competitor names "lovable", "base44", "heygen"; referrals from devhunt.org 6.7%, producthunt.com 6.2%, enter.pro 4.1%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Buying competitor keywords: Google paid search on lovable, base44, heygen.
  • Launch platforms: Product Hunt #2 of the day (6.2% referral), DevHunt (6.7% referral).
  • Offline conference sponsorship: SuperAI Singapore.
  • Cross-promotion between sub-products: enter.pro -> converge.ai referral 4%.

Speculation:

  • Brazil at 31.6% indicates ad spend scaled in low-cost regions.
  • User cases (e.g. BeABA) and the blog do content marketing, but the volume is still small.
  • The team behind it is well funded (bought two aged premium domains, sponsored a conference, buys ads across the board), looks like it has funding or is incubated by a larger company; unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public figures; X 2,259 followers, PH 868 followers, organic word of mouth is very small. Subscription $20/mo.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were mixed with many same-named companies (Convergence.ai acquired by Salesforce, the OpenAI Converge accelerator, converge-ai.io, etc.) unrelated to this site; evidence from the site's About/Newsroom, Product Hunt, RDAP, Wayback, fxtwitter.
