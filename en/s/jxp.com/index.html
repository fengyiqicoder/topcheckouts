A Chinese team (which had already built a Discord called "Seedance Funs" in 2025-07) took over the three-letter old domain jxp.com from Chinese domain investors in autumn 2025 (in 2023-2024 it was still on a "domain for sale" page). It launched an English AI video aggregator in 2025-10 and registered the Hong Kong company JXP COM LIMITED in 2025-11. The playbook is "add every new model as soon as it ships": Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Nano Banana Pro, Wan 2.5/2.6, Kling 3.0, and especially a bet on ByteDance's Seedance (Seedance 2.0 in 2026-02, then 2.5). Each model gets its own landing page, targeting new-model searches like "seedance 2.0 / seedance 2.5 free trial". Combined with credit-based referral rewards, a free-trial page, paid-style intro videos from a crypto YouTuber and iOS/Android apps, the short three-letter domain's credibility turns search traffic into credit-pack revenue.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI video/image generation aggregator; one credit account covers Seedance, Veo, Sora, Kling, Wan, Hailuo, Vidu, LTX, Seedream, Nano Banana, GPT Image and more, plus lip-sync and AI dance tools; web + iOS (listed 2026-05-14) + Android Beta (package name com.jxp.dance).
  • Who built it: footer says "JXP COM LIMITED" (Hong Kong, business registration no. 79212711, incorporated 2025-11-20; source https://www.tempb.com/companies/jxp-com-limited/ and Qichacha, summary only); iOS developer name "jxp com corp". Founder not found.
  • Pricing: $10 / 100 credits, $30 / 330 credits, $99 / 1,211 credits (one-time), and the homepage also lists monthly plans at the same price points; 7-day refund policy (profile data; https://www.jxp.com/refund).
  • Referral program: share a personal link, and when the referred user pays you get credits based on their payment amount, manually reviewed and credited within 24 hours (https://www.jxp.com/referral).
  • Domain: jxp.com registered 2001-08-05 (RDAP); the Wayback snapshots from 2023-06 and 2024-09 are both Chinese "domain for sale" pages ("jxp.com 进项票 机械盘 教学片…"); the AI video site first appears 2025-10-10.
  • Traffic (profile data): US 28.5%, Brazil 3%, India 2.7%; direct 44.8%, search 41.4% (non-brand about 24.8%, mainly "seedance 2.5", "seedance 2.0", "seedance 2.5 free trial"), social 8.3% (YouTube, Reddit); referral ahrefs.com 4.16%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Capturing new models: the homepage title and landing pages follow new Seedance versions (1.0 -> 2.0 -> 2.5 and so on), one page per model (194 pages in the sitemap), with non-brand search around 25%.
  • Referral rewards: the referred user pays and the referrer gets credits proportionally.
  • Free-trial pages (/seedance/free, "AI Video Generator for Free By Referral") capture "free" long-tail terms.
  • Multi-platform: iOS, Android app; Japanese and Korean versions.

Speculation:

  • The short three-letter.com domain raises trust and direct visits (direct traffic 44.8%).
  • The 4% referral from ahrefs.com may come from Ahrefs keyword/traffic tool pages, suggesting the team does heavy SEO research (speculation).
  • The crypto YouTuber video is a form of cheap paid promotion.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Credit packs $10-$99; no public user count or revenue. The Discord has only 5 members and the iOS app has 1 rating, so the community is tiny and this is mainly tool-style one-time-payment traffic.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results were mostly irrelevant; the evidence is mainly Wayback homepage history, RDAP, the Discord/iTunes APIs, the official referral page and company registry summaries.
  • Gaps: team identity, price paid for the domain, the specific traffic gained from the Seedance 2.0 window, and revenue were not found.
