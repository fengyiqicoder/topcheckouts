Basics

  • Product: a Hebrew-language Southeast Asia travel community site. Country guides (Thailand, Chiang Mai, Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos, the Philippines, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Japan, India), entrances to 300+ WhatsApp groups split by country and a "main group" that summarizes the other groups' information every day, member attraction discounts, visa services, "כרטיס פיקטיבי" (fake/virtual return flight tickets, for entry checks), travel insurance, party listings, a newsletter.
  • Operator: the homepage "insurance team" names Shlomi Yehuda (59, CEO and owner of the insurance brokerage A.S Insure, a second-generation insurance agent) and his daughter Sharon Yehuda Rosenman (30, third-generation insurance agent), "working with all insurance companies, no added agent fee." The community's original founder was not found and may be someone else (speculation).
  • Community size: Instagram @mizrah.co.il has 24K followers; Facebook group "StayUpTodate" (member count would not open); the site says "thousands of Israelis use our WhatsApp groups every day."
  • Traffic (our data): direct 45.4%, search 39.4%, paid 9.1%; non-brand keywords about 39.4% (מזרח, מוצילר מזרח, קמבודיה), brand keywords 45.4%. Israel 100%.
  • Domain: Wayback shows that in 2012-2014 this domain was a forum about Islam and Middle East current affairs, unrelated to the current site; ISOC-IL whois shows the current holder from 2024-05-02 (possibly a transfer or re-registration date).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Group network: WhatsApp groups grew from 150+ (2023-12) to 300+ (2026-01), split by country and city, with a main group summarizing the other groups daily; every page on the site pushes "join a group."
  • Search: non-brand keywords are 39.4% ("מזרח", "Cambodia", "backpackers East"), with guide pages catching people doing homework before departure.
  • Monetization hangs off the community: insurance (the operator is himself an insurance broker), attraction discounts, visas, fake tickets; paid traffic of 9.1% shows they also buy ads.
  • Instagram at 24K followers and a Facebook group serve as second entrances.

Speculation:

  • The first users came from the Facebook groups around 2015: Israeli backpackers searching for groups before departure and helping each other locally, spread by word of mouth; the site was only added after the community grew large.
  • Every year a new batch of Israelis finishing military service sets off, so the community naturally gets fresh blood; everyone in a group forwards the group link to friends traveling with them.
  • The community may have been taken over by, or partnered with, the insurance brokerage, trading community traffic for insurance policies (speculation, no evidence of a deal seen).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Main revenue is speculated to be travel insurance commissions (long-haul backpacking insurance policies are relatively pricey in Israel), attraction discount commissions, and visa and fake ticket service fees; amounts not public.
  • Early on it also accepted donations (the 2023 homepage had a 20-200 shekel donation button).
