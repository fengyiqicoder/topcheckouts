Basics

  • Product: cross-device bookmark manager (collections, tags, highlights, full-text search, broken/duplicate link checks, permanent copies, publicly shared collections, team collaboration; recently added the AI assistant Stella and MCP). Browser extensions (Chrome/Firefox/Safari/Edge) + web + macOS/Windows/Linux/iOS/Android clients.
  • Price: free plan + Pro subscription (about 20% cheaper billed yearly than monthly; see the site for exact prices).
  • Author: Rustem Mussabekov, independent developer. The 2023 About page says: "a computer science degree, nine years of UI/UX design experience… Raindrop.io is my main job and only source of income… that income is enough to keep me independent, with no funding" (https://web.archive.org/web/20230601/https://help.raindrop.io/about). Nationality/location is Kazakhstan from memory (unverified).
  • Domain: RDAP has no event data (.io); first Wayback snapshot 2013-10-27, a Russian-language beta page ("ßeta © 2013 Raindrop.io / О проекте / Расширение для браузера / Клиент для iPhone"). GitHub organization raindropio was created 2014-10-20.
  • Scale: Chrome extension 500,000 users, 787 ratings at 4.2 (fetched 2026-10, the store page says "13 years in a business").
  • Note: the "Raindrop raised $50M / Series A" in the search results (raindrop.ai, AI agent monitoring) and raindrop.com (a procurement platform) are different companies with the same name and unrelated to this site.

The Russian-language launch channel was a Habr post from memory (unverified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Launch platform: listed on PH five times, #1 of the day in 2014 and #1 of the week in 2020; each major release returns to PH for another boost.
  • Public collections carry the brand: since 2014 there has been "Create public collections, share bookmarks with the world / subscribe to collections", and shared collection pages carry the raindrop.io domain (branded output).
  • Alternative-seeking traffic: users look for alternatives when Pocket, Delicious and similar shut down or change; the profile shows alternativeto.net at 5.38% of referrals, plus workona.com and asana.com integration pages.
  • Every platform + extension stores: Chrome/Edge/Firefox/Safari extensions and Google Play and the App Store are themselves discovery entry points; Chrome has 500K users and a "Featured" badge.
  • Community: the r/raindropio subreddit and a public feature-voting board (better.raindrop.io), with the developer replying in person.

Speculation:

  • Pocket's 2025 shutdown (from memory, unverified) may have brought a wave of migration.
  • Direct visits of 96.6% and search terms that are all brand keywords show new customers now come mainly from word of mouth and comparison articles, not SEO long tail.

Revenue and scale clues

  • In 2023 the author said Raindrop.io is his only source of income and the income is enough to stay independent with no funding; maintained by one person.
  • Chrome 500K users + clients on multiple platforms; 17,000 users and 1 million bookmarks as of 2014-06.
  • No public MRR; by a rough estimate from 500K+ extension users, a single-digit-percent paid rate and a Pro annual fee of a few dozen dollars, annual revenue may be in the hundreds of thousands of dollars (speculation, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • The site is written from scratch. The 2013 Russian beta, the 2014 PH launch and the 17,000 users, the PH rankings over the years, and the one-person development statement all have original page evidence, so it is fairly credible.
  • Gaps: the original Russian-language launch posts (Habr and others) were not found; Reddit posts could not be opened so only summaries were used; no paid user count or revenue; the author's country is unverified. News about the same-named AI company Raindrop's funding got mixed into the search results and has been excluded.
