Overview

Raindrop (raindrop.io): Cross-device bookmark manager for saving, organizing and searching links, articles and files. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,573 visits went from Raindrop to a checkout payment page — #542 of the 607 sites we track. That is 7% down from the April–June monthly average of 4,921.

Total website visits that month were about 1,218,971 (Semrush figure rescaled to Similarweb). Roughly 0.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 97%, search 3%, AI assistants 0%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows open source, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 100%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: from $0; yearly ~20% cheaper than monthly.
