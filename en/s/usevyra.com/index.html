Basics

  • Product: AI video editor; upload your own footage, describe the effect you want in conversation, and it automatically makes a rough cut, syncs cuts to the beat, adds captions and motion graphics (code-generated titles/lower thirds), and can reference a style; web + iOS (listed 2026-10-01) + MCP (callable directly from Claude/ChatGPT/Cursor).
  • Founders: Sulan Zhang (Brown University visual arts + CS, took a leave after sophomore year; painter and Instagram creator, X bio says creator 65k+); Caleb Pong (former freelance editor, 7 years of editing experience). Team of 2, San Francisco, YC F26, partner Aaron Epstein - sources https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vyra, https://www.usevyra.com/about
  • Pricing: free to start; MCP Starter $24/month, MCP Pro $65/month; Vyra AI Starter $54/month, Pro $129/month.
  • Domain: usevyra.com registered 2025-08-21 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2026-03-19.
  • Traffic: India 36%, US 31%, Indonesia 5.7%; direct 43.7%, search 41.8% (brand keywords 68.7%), referral 8.7%; social sources X, LinkedIn, Instagram.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder as creator: Sulan edits her own Reels with Vyra, so the hit videos are themselves product demos, ending with a prompt to comment for the link.
  • Paid UGC: the site's "Create for us" recruits users to shoot Vyra promo shorts with their own footage and pays them; the founders and promoters use /go/<name> tracking links.
  • SEO landing pages: the sitemap has 255 URLs (/tools/tiktok-video-editor, /compare/vyra-vs-adobe-premiere, /alternatives, etc.), and search traffic is 41.8%.
  • Riding the AI Agent ecosystem: MCP access for Claude/ChatGPT/Cursor, with /claude, /chatgpt and /cursor landing pages.
  • Third-party review endorsement: ranked first in a Buffer roundup (self-reported).

Speculation:

  • YC backing + the MCP demo on X bring early adopters from the developer/AI circle.
  • The "Made with Vyra" showcase + user-published videos may bring a little word of mouth, but whether exported videos carry a watermark was not found.

Confidence and gaps
