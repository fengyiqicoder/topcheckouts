Overview

Ticktick (ticktick.com): To-do list, calendar and productivity app with habit tracking, Pomodoro timer and cross-device sync. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Ticktick to a checkout payment page — #296 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 2,004,325 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 0.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 76%, search 18%, social 4%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 94%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Free US$0; Premium US$49.99/Year (less than US$4.17/month).
