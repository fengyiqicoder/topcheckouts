Basics

  • Product: customercontrolpanel.de is netcup's Customer Control Panel (CCP), used to manage contracts, invoices, payments and upgrades; there is also an SCP (Server Control Panel). The domain does not look like netcup and is often mistaken for phishing: a false positive on the RPiList blocklist (2022), and in 2023 a phishing clone appeared at customercontrolpanel-netcup.co.in.
  • Company: netcup GmbH, Karlsruhe, Germany. Founded by Felix Preuß as a sole proprietorship, converted to a GmbH in 2008, merged into the Anexia group in 2016. Current managing directors Alexander Windbichler and Oliver Werner.
  • Scale (per the website's 2025 figures): 300K+ customers, 400K+ hosting products, 550K+ domains, 100+ employees.
  • Product line: Webhosting, VPS (x86/ARM64), VPS Lite, Root Server, Managed Private Server, domains; a US data center in Manassas, Virginia added in 2024.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Award endorsements: repeated HOSTtest public-vote "Hosting Provider of the Year" and Vogel IT awards.
  • Users write tutorials on their own: Chinese VPS communities (Linux.do, NodeSeek) have user-written registration, tax-exemption and promotion-calendar guides plus plenty of discussion.
  • Price-announcement design: existing contracts keep their price, and same-generation upgrades inside the CCP keep the old price too, locking in existing customers and nudging them to upgrade before the increase.
  • Channel data: this site is 74.9% direct, 23.2% referral (mostly from the main netcup site), 0.2% search; it does no acquisition at all.
  • Speculation:
  • The first users came from the German webmaster scene of the early 2000s; too long ago, not found.
  • netcup's main acquisition engine has always been limited-time holiday coupon codes (Easter, Black Friday, etc.) spreading through cheap-VPS communities like LowEndTalk, mydealz, Reddit r/selfhosted, Linux.do and NodeSeek.
  • The 1–3 months ago peak comes from upgrades and buying ahead of the price increase, plus an influx of new users from the Chinese scene.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 300K+ customers, 100+ employees (website). Revenue not public; the Anexia group does not break it out.

Confidence and gaps

  • This is a payment back office, not a product site; the growth analysis is about the parent company netcup.
  • The original Linux.do and NodeSeek threads would not open (403), so only search summaries were used; the size and effective dates of the price increases come mainly from HN titles and forum summaries.
  • The causal link between the 2026 peak and the price increases is speculation.
  • Second-pass recheck: added the Chinese VPS communities (Linux.do 2025-06 tutorial, NodeSeek 2025–2026 discussions) as a new-user source, split 2026 into two rounds of price increases (March for existing contracts via the CCP price-adjustment page, last month for new orders), and added the issue of the domain being mistaken for phishing.
