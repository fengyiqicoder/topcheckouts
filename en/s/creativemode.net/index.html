Basics

  • Product: A no-code AI Minecraft mod/add-on generator (Java + Bedrock) that can generate mobs, items, blocks, recipes, structures, skins or whole mods; includes community Explore, Leaderboard, Best Mods, ModJams and the CreativeMode multiplayer server.
  • Team: blog author Wilson S. (wilson@creativemode.net, says he studied computer science at MIT and learned programming through ComputerCraft), presumed co-founder; company name, country (likely the US) and funding not found.
  • Pricing: 300 free coins per week; Pro $19.99/month (7,500 coins, about 75 mods, includes a free hosted multiplayer server); Pro Ultra $39.99/month (18,000 coins). Also an affiliate program (terms not public) and friend-invite rewards.
  • Social: YouTube @CreativeModeMods (channel description "Join the 700,000+ players"), TikTok, Discord (joining gives 3 free mods).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Short videos from the official accounts acquire users: a player says they found it from a YouTube Short; the team runs YouTube and TikTok, and the Wall of Love also has users writing "saw it on TikTok".
  • User output is public: Explore (Staff Picks, Hot Java Mods, Hot Bedrock Addons), leaderboards, ModJam contests; user-made mods become the content supply, and first-time users post to forums to show off as soon as they make one (like the "squid rocket launcher").
  • Invite rewards: inviter and invitee each get 500 coins (Pro inviters get 1,000); Pro also has creation-streak and download-milestone rewards.
  • The official multiplayer server + Pro includes a hosted server: "I want my friends to play my mod" leads to pulling friends onto the server.
  • Joining Discord gives 3 free mods; an affiliate program.
  • Search 54.3%, of which non-brand is about 21.7% ("minecraft skins", "minecraft mods"); a Minecraft Wiki tutorial page holds a spot; paid search is among the channels.
  • Speculation:
  • Kid users show off the mods they made on TikTok/YouTube, and the videos naturally carry the message "made with CreativeMode".
  • The summer peak may involve a Minecraft YouTuber collaboration; who it was was not found.
  • Most payers are parents; the gift guide and Education Edition target parents and teachers.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 700–750k+ players, 637k mods (self-reported on the site and YouTube).
  • Pro $19.99/month; third-party estimate of MRR about €24k (Starte.ai, low credibility).
  • Our data: August checkout visits 50,648, April to June averaged 27,948.
