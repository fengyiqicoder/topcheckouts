Overview

Creativemode (creativemode.net): No-code AI tool for making Minecraft mods with a public marketplace, leaderboard and multiplayer servers. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 19,226 visits went from Creativemode to a checkout payment page — #141 of the 607 sites we track. That is 31% down from the April–June monthly average of 27,948.

Total website visits that month were about 88,291 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 21.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 54%, direct 46%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows multiplayer rooms, public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 78%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
