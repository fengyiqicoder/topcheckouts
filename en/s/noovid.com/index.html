Basics

  • Product: Brazilian UGC video marketplace. Brands create a project → pick creators → receive and review videos → push in one click to Meta/TikTok ad accounts and get data back; there is also a managed-service version (/gerenciado), an agency version, and free ad-size/preview tools. The site already supports pt-BR, pt-PT, en, es, fr, de, it and nl.
  • Company: In 2026 the site footer was changed to "Noovid LLC, Newark, Delaware, USA" (a US entity, presumably set up for expanding into Europe and hiring overseas staff). The Brazilian entity is NOOVID NEGOCIOS DIGITAIS LTDA, CNPJ 54.059.885/0001-23, founded 2024-02-26, Brasília DF, share capital R$10,000, micro-enterprise; the sole shareholder and manager is Lucemilton Francelino Bezerra Junior (BrasilAPI registration data). Funding: Not found.
  • Pricing: brands pay from R$169 per video, the platform is free to join with no monthly fee; creators sign up for free and get paid for each approved video.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Two-sided network: on the creator side, "no follower requirement, free sign-up, get paid per video" lowers the supply barrier; on the brand side, fixed price with rights included solves the pain of DM haggling, no-shows and copyright (site copy).
  • Embedded in the ad workflow: after review, one click publishes to Meta/TikTok and ROAS data flows back, so brand repurchases happen inside the platform.
  • Content/SEO: the profile data shows search at 38.7% and non-brand keywords around 23% (ugc creator, ugc, ugc meaning); these terms mainly attract people who want to become UGC creators, i.e. the supply side. Landing pages by use case (such as /brands/ugc-for-tiktok-ads) attract the brand side; the blog (Meta partnership ads, Black Friday UGC, CONAR influencer rules) and free tool pages (ad resizer, ad preview, ad checker) hold search positions.

Speculation:

  • First users (supply side): the first snapshot in 2024-04 targeted creators only, with copy like "no need to be an influencer to earn money and free products". Presumably the first creators came from Brazilian Instagram/TikTok "earn money filming videos at home" side-hustle content and "ugc creator" searches; the official TikTok @noovid has only about 1,276 followers (summary only), so its own accounts are not the main source.
  • First brands: presumably the founder's direct sales in the Brazilian e-commerce and ad community plus email outreach (email is 7.7% of the channel mix); no evidence.
  • Creators themselves share "I earn from orders on Noovid" on TikTok/Instagram, which creates self-spreading on the supply side.
  • The brand side relies mainly on ads and sales (the channel mix includes Mail, which suggests email marketing/outreach) and on SEO tool pages.
  • The September surge may correspond to brands buying UGC creative in bulk ahead of Black Friday.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site: 10,000+ creators, 7,000+ active brands (self-reported).
  • Price from R$169 per video; the platform's commission rate is not public.
  • Share capital is only R$10k, with a single shareholder.

Confidence and gaps

  • The founder's background, funding and the source of the first brands are still Not found; the search results contain no founder interviews, PH/HN posts or media coverage, and Reddit search results are all about the similar-sounding nasal spray Enovid.
  • Second follow-up check: added the Delaware LLC entity in the US, the 2026 Europe expansion, the growth lead job post (showing the growth team is just starting), evidence that non-brand search terms skew to the supply side, and the very small official TikTok following. Takeoff method is still set to "Not found"; the leaning guess is that the supply side was recruited first through search and social content about earning money from UGC, with the brand side coming from sales and ads.
  • The "checkout page" may include both brand payment pages and creator payout pages, so the meaning of the visit counts is uncertain.
