Overview

Noovid (noovid.com): Brazilian platform connecting brands with UGC creators for fixed-price videos with usage rights. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 47,889 visits went from Noovid to a checkout payment page — #41 of the 607 sites we track. That is 957% up from the April–June monthly average of 4,531.

Total website visits that month were about 64,576 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 48%, search 39%, email 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 23%, with queries like “ugc creator”, “ugc”, “ugc meaning”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
