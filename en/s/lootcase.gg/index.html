LootCase.GG is an online Pokémon physical-card pack opening site relaunched under an old unboxing-site brand: lootcase.gg was originally a 2017 Dota 2/CS:GO skin case-opening/betting site (it shut down in 2017-06 when Valve banned trading accounts, community post "Lootcase.gg shuts down"). The domain was re-registered in 2024-11, a Discord was set up the same week, and in December it launched as "Cards You Love, Service You Trust!", swapping the CS:GO case-opening mechanics (pay to open digital packs, unboxing battles, leaderboards, cash-out for balance, daily free Lootbucks) for Pokémon/TCG physical cards that it promises to ship. Acquisition follows the old unboxing-site playbook too: small streamers record "opening packs on LootCase" videos on YouTube/Twitch/TikTok with an invite code, and a full affiliate/referral code system on the site pays commissions, combined with Discord giveaways and Gleam sweepstakes. Direct traffic of 79%, a bounce rate of 18% and 13.6 pages per visit show a heavily replayed, gambling-style product.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Affiliate/referral codes: the front-end code contains complete affiliate code, referral_code, Affiliate Bonus, affiliate_balance and referral commission statistics; search terms include "lootcase promo codes".
  • Content creators: small YouTube/Twitch/TikTok streamers record pack-opening videos (unboxing content naturally carries the brand).
  • Retention mechanics: daily free Lootbucks, chat room, leaderboards, unboxing battles, cash-out for balance (mentioned in reviews).
  • Gleam giveaway tracking script + Discord giveaways.

Speculation:

  • As with CS:GO case-opening sites, growth mainly comes from paying streamers a share + deposit bonuses rather than organic word of mouth; the visible individual videos have very low view counts, suggesting a "long tail of many small streamers" rather than a one-time spike.
  • The 2025–2026 Pokémon TCG boom (new sets such as Prismatic Evolutions) is a tailwind.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Global rank about 79,000 (the highest among the 10 sites); bounce rate 18%, 13.6 pages per visit, indicating frequent replay.
  • Discord about 3.7k members; Trustpilot 37 reviews.
  • Stripe checkout visits about 4.7k in 08 and about 12.9k in 09 (tiered estimate); other top-up methods unknown. Revenue not public.

Confidence and gaps
