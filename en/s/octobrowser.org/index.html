Basics

  • Product: an anti-detect (fingerprint) browser; each profile simulates the fingerprint of one real device, used for multi-account operations. Supports Windows/macOS/Linux, with iOS/Android newly launched, plus an API, profile transfer and team collaboration. Release cadence: 2.9 (2026-01), 3.0 with a new interface (2026-07), 3.1 mobile/UDP proxies (2026-09).
  • Company: BLACKBIRD SECURE BROWSING LTD., London. A comparison article by competitor Gologin says it was "launched by a Belarusian team in 2020" (competitor source, unattributed); no individual founder is public.
  • Users: Gologin says it is "especially popular with Russian-speaking affiliate marketers, media buyers and crypto teams", later expanding to e-commerce, ticket grabbing, bonus hunting and scraping. Our channel data shows the country split as United States 55.5%, Ukraine 16.5%, Russia 16.3%.
  • Pricing: Lite €10/month (3 profiles), Starter €29, Base €79 (100 profiles), Team €169, Advanced €329 (1000+); 3/6/12-month prepayment gives -10%/-20%/-30%; no free version, and the promo code TRIAL gives a 2-day trial.
  • Referral program: 15% of every payment from a referred user for one year.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral program: 15% revenue share for one year, and the company encourages promoting it in communities, websites, blogs and contextual ads (official /referral page). The referrers in the profile data are mostly coupon-code sites and procpa.media (a CPA media outlet).
  • Ecosystem swap discounts: the partner section on the site gives Keitaro (tracker) users 20% off, 3SNET (CPA network) gives 5%, and CyberYozh (proxies and SMS verification) gives 10% off. Upstream and downstream tools in the arbitrage workflow send traffic to each other.
  • Team spillover: a testimonial reads "We farm ad accounts for affiliate marketing and always recommend Octo to clients because profile transfer is easier". Account-farming studios deliver the accounts together with the Octo profiles to clients.
  • Community operations: the Telegram community is mainly Russian-language; a Trustpilot review mentions that being active in the Telegram group earns subscription discounts.
  • Industry conferences and awards: Best Anti-Detect Browser at MAC 2024 and Affiliate Space 2025.
  • Content and SEO: the official blog blog.octobrowser.net has many long posts on how to make money on TikTok/ad platforms and how to farm accounts; the official Reddit account has posted experiment-style content since 2022.

Speculation:

  • The first roughly 1,000 users came from Russian-language arbitrage forums and Telegram channels, which is the circle the team was already in. This is based on the Belarusian team background, the Russian-language communities and Russian gambling platform cases, but no launch post was found.
  • The new.org domain is used to split off the English/US market or to get around bans: US traffic is 55% in the profile data and brand keyword traffic is 62%.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing runs from €10 to €329+/month; user counts and revenue are not public. Trustpilot has 80 reviews (4.4 rating), G2 has 4.8 (66 reviews).
  • The secondary domain octobrowser.org alone had 47K checkout visits last month, so the main.net domain should be larger (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: added "launched by a Belarusian team in 2020" (from competitor Gologin, unverified), the upstream/downstream tool swap discounts, the Telegram activity-for-discount point, and the start of Reddit content marketing (2022-05). Takeoff method stays "Existing audience".
  • The launch post and the specific source of the first users are still not found. Original Reddit posts could not be scraped, only summaries; the search results contain no founder interviews, and Russian-language media was not searched.
