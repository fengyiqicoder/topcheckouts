Overview

Octobrowser (octobrowser.org): Antidetect browser for running many accounts with separate fingerprints. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 47,278 visits went from Octobrowser to a checkout payment page — #44 of the 607 sites we track. That is 37% up from the April–June monthly average of 34,567.

Total website visits that month were about 15,786 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 62%, referrals/email/paid 34%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 62%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Lite €10/mo, €9/mo billed 3mo, €8/mo billed 6mo, €7/mo billed annually etc..
