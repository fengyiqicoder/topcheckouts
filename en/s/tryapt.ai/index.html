Growth loop

  • Evidence: the only CTA on the homepage and every SEO landing page is 'Start quiz'; lots of long-tail quiz pages (/workplace-personality-test, /ai-career-test, /ai-job-search, /blog/best-ai-tools-job-seekers-2025) are staking out search rankings. In the channel data Paid Social ranks second.
  • Evidence (second-pass check): (1) a review site that earns commissions holds the top spot for the search 'apt ai reviews' (the MyPassion article); (2) on TikTok, 'whatisXdoing' lifestyle accounts with a consistent naming template post #tryapt seeding content; (3) the paywall uses pressure-style conversion design such as a countdown, a preselected annual plan, and 'Join 50 million+ people'.
  • Speculation: the main driver is Meta/TikTok quiz ads ('what career fits you' style hooks), and the UGC-style video testimonials on the homepage look a lot like reused ad creative; the product itself has no obvious A-brings-B mechanism (results come with no share card), so spread relies on paid acquisition rather than word of mouth. The Trustpilot 3.5 rating and many refund complaints also fit the profile of a paid-acquisition-funnel subscription product.

Revenue and scale clues

  • User count claims: 100K+ (Crunchbase/Trustpilot blurbs) -> 700K+ -> 1M+ -> 2.5M+ (homepage 2026-10), all self-reported.
  • Subscription about $19.99/month; Trustpilot 3.5/5 (78 -> 96 reviews).
  • Pricing: $119.88 annual, $59.97 quarterly, $29.99 monthly (2026-05); early on it was a $29.99 one-time payment (2024-02).
  • No public MRR found. Rough math: if 2% of 2.5M quiz takers pay an average of $80, cumulative revenue is about $4M (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Founder, team size, and funding were not found; the launch channel (the first 1,000 users) was not found, and there is no Product Hunt / Reddit launch record (the Reddit API was blocked, so only indirect searches were possible).
  • Second-pass check: added the two earliest public traces from the early period (a spontaneous endorsement on LinkedIn in 2024-01 and a Reddit scam-suspicion post in 2024-02, the latter showing it was a $29.99 one-time payment back then), the pricing change from one-time to subscription, the TikTok UGC account matrix, and the commission-earning review site holding the search spot. There is still no direct evidence of where the first 1,000 users came from, and the founder is still not found. The classification is unchanged.
  • The 'Paid ads' call rests mainly on our channel data (Paid Social in the top three) plus the product form, so the strength of the inference is medium (speculation).
