Overview

Shophunter (shophunter.io): Research tool estimating Shopify store revenue and tracking Facebook ads to find winning products. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 38,682 visits went from Shophunter to a checkout payment page — #60 of the 607 sites we track. That is 40% up from the April–June monthly average of 27,533.

Total website visits that month were about 17,413 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 79%, search 21%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 8%, with queries like “winning hunter”, “winninghunter”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
