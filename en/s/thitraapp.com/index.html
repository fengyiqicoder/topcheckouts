From last month he began buying paid traffic to a single article, "Whop Review 2026", whose "Try Now" link redirects with a Whop referral code (whop.com/?code=NEWLJFE); visits jumped to over 4,000 within a month. Basics

  • Product: marketing/SaaS tool review site (AI tools, LinkedIn automation, Shopify, WordPress plugins, AppSumo, Whop, etc.), with many banner ads (SiteGround with afcode, ShareASale with afftrack), and articles carry no dates (https://thitraapp.com/).
  • Operator: footer says "owned and operated by: Tran Ao", address Thu Dau Mot, Vietnam (Binh Duong province); there is no About page.
  • Key page: https://thitraapp.com/product-review/whop-review-2026/, whose CTA points to https://thitraapp.com/getwhop/, which uses JS to redirect to https://whop.com/?code=NEWLJFE (tested 2026-10-25).
  • The home page's "100K+ Reviewed Products / 50K+ Happy Clients / 3.5M Feedbacks" are template placeholder numbers that already existed at the first 2023 capture, alongside "Lorem ipsum" placeholder articles on the same page, so they cannot be used as evidence of scale.
  • Domain: registered 2023-04-14 per RDAP; first Wayback capture 2023-04-26.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,291 visits in September (only 36 in August); paid 55.0%, direct 33.8%, email 6.7%; the only search term is whop; no country data.

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: the 2026-09 traffic is mainly paid traffic (55%) + direct visits; search is the single term whop.
  • Speculation: the ad target is the Whop review page, and users who click the CTA sign up for Whop with the referral code; the 6.7% email may come from an ad platform or promotional emails. Before that, from 2023 to 2026-08, there were essentially no users.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Review page → redirect to Whop with referral code (code=NEWLJFE); other pages carry SiteGround and ShareASale affiliate banners.

Speculation:

  • Paid traffic → Whop signup → referral income; if ROI is positive he keeps spending; it is not a self-sustaining loop, and when the spending stops, the traffic stops.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue sources: Whop referrals + other affiliate ads. Whop's commission policy for referrals is, from memory, a share of the referred user's platform revenue (from memory, not verified).
  • Scale: about 4k monthly visits, half of them bought; no direct evidence of revenue level.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration time, owner identity and the Whop referral-code redirect all have primary evidence; the "bought traffic" judgment rests on the profile's 55% paid traffic and the two months ago-to-last month jump, so credibility is medium.
  • Gaps: search results were all unrelated subdomain-scan pages, with no external coverage; the ad channel (Google Ads / Meta / native ads) was not found; the text of the "Affiliate Disclosure" page was not read.
