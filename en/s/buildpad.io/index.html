After two failed projects in a row, the Swedish Heikka brothers (based in Budapest) started in 2024 by posting a survey in founder subreddits to validate the 'AI co-founder' idea. On 2024-08-19 they posted the MVP back to the same subreddits and replied to 50 posts a day on X, reaching their first 100 users in two weeks; about a month later they finished #4 of the day on Product Hunt (roughly 500–591 votes), passed 1,000 users within a week, and got their first paying customer that night. From there the two brothers kept posting '$X MRR / N users' build-in-public stories on HN, Reddit, X and LinkedIn, turning the founders' own growth story into acquisition content; they launched on PH a second time in 2025-04 (435 votes, #4) and from 2025 began paying to sponsor AI-tool creators. Basics

  • Product: Buildpad, an "AI co-founder": breaks startup goals into phases (validate → build → launch), dispatches Agents to Reddit, the App Store and elsewhere for demand and competitor research, and keeps documents and research on a single canvas.
  • Team: David Heikka (born 1997, former professional poker player, previously co-founded a SaaS and ran marketing) and Felix Heikka (born 1999, former Swedish Army NCO), Swedes working in Budapest, Hungary; self-funded, no outside funding.
  • Pricing: Pro $39/month (200 credits), Max $85/month (500 credits); another tier seen at Pro $59 / Max $125.
  • Launch: domain WHOIS registered 2024-07-16, first commit 2024-07-07, MVP launched 2024-08-19 (official blog).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • First 100 users: the survey and MVP posts in target subreddits, plus 50 replies a day on X (per the founders).
  • First 1,000 users and first revenue: the Product Hunt launch (#4). The second PH launch in 2025-04 brought another wave.
  • Founder content: the two brothers keep posting build-in-public stories on HN (at least 15 posts), Reddit, X, LinkedIn, IndieHackers and Medium, and get re-covered by Starter Story, WeAreFounders, Medium and others, forming a "startup story carries the link" loop.
  • Paid creator sponsorships (planned from mid-2025): AI-tool, indie-dev and newsletter creators.
  • Search: the profile shows search at 40.9%, with the non-brand keyword "ai cofounder" at about 16%; the site has /blog review and comparison articles.

Speculation:

  • The product itself has no viral mechanism (no evidence of sharing or invite rewards).
  • In 2026 India and Bangladesh became top-3 countries, and with Instagram referrals, this presumably comes from sponsored South Asian startup creators or a rising ranking for the "ai cofounder" keyword (unverified).
  • The site's "100k+ founders" is 5x the 20k of 2025-10, implying another growth spurt in 2026; the cause was not found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue: $970 in the first 17 days → $2,700 MRR, about 150 paying customers (2025-02) → $5K MRR (2025-05) → $6K MRR (2025-06) → about $7.3K/month (2025-07 Medium headline).
  • Users: 100 (2024-09) → 1,600 (2024-11) → 5,000 (2025-03) → 7,000+ (blog) → 20,000 (2025-10) → site claims 100,000+ (2026-10).
  • Pricing $39 / $85 per month.

Confidence and gaps

  • The early timeline comes from multiple first-person accounts by the founders, rich in detail but all self-reported; the PH launch date is given inconsistently (9-30 vs November), and the vote count also differs (500+ vs 591).
  • The cause of 2026 growth and the source of Indian traffic not found; the Medium body and the arcadiastudio article could not be opened.
  • Second-pass check: added the exact MVP launch date (2024-08-19), the second PH launch data (2025-04-23, 435 votes, #4), the 2025 MRR curve ($5K → $6K → about $7.3K), and the paid creator sponsorship marketing plan; the old conclusion was not overturned.
