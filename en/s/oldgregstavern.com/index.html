Basics

  • Product: Old Greg's Tavern, a text-based fantasy tabletop RPG with an AI Game Master (D&D-like, own simplified rules, disclaims any WotC affiliation): solo or up to 6 per party, asynchronous turns, pause and resume anytime; NPCs remember your actions; community can build and publish worlds; AI-generated avatars and music. Web + iOS + Android, 18+ only.
  • Operator: Old Greg's Tavern, LLC (registered in Wyoming, registered agent address in Sheridan, WY, a common US remote-registration setup). The official newsletter (beehiiv), TikTok and Instagram are all signed by the fictional persona "Old Greg"; founder, team size and funding still not found.
  • Pricing: web Adventurer $5 one-time for 5 credits/50 turns; Hero $15/month for 200 turns; Legend $25/month for 450 turns; iOS also has $9.99–49.99 credit packs. The host pays, invited friends play free at the same table.
  • Community: Discord is the main base (linking Discord gives 30 turns), plus r/OldGregsTavern, TikTok @oldgregstavern (about 6K followers), Instagram @oldgregstavern, and a weekly beehiiv update email.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Invites: the host pays, up to 5 friends join the party free (confirmed by the official site, App Store and the Arcanum review); one paying player brings a whole table.
  • Short video: the official TikTok has posted AI DM story clips continuously since the first week after launch, a few hundred to a thousand-plus likes each; a single Instagram Reel with 180K likes is the biggest exposure point found so far.
  • Paid ads: the site runs Google Ads (AW-18025044777), Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel and Reddit Pixel at once (privacy policy states the Reddit Pixel is for "conversion tracking and audience building for Reddit ads"), consistent with Display in our channel data.
  • Retention: weekly newsletter updates, Discord party-finding, turns for linking Discord; a daily free dungeon pulls people back.
  • Brand spillover: competitors like Friends & Fables and Mythx write comparison/alternatives articles, showing the brand keyword has search volume (our data: brand keywords are 95.9% of search).
  • Speculation:
  • First users: Reddit/Meta/TikTok ads from launch + its own story shorts, rather than community posts (no launch record on Product Hunt or HN; Reddit was blocked so specific posts could not be verified).
  • The Instagram Reel has 180K likes but only 337 comments, a skewed ratio; it may be an ad creative boosted via Meta, with organic and paid sharing the same asset.
  • Small YouTubers' unprompted playthrough videos are word-of-mouth spillover at modest scale.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Official claim of "over 285,000 players (mid-2026)", relayed by a third-party review, unverified.
  • iOS about 5K ratings (4.8). 30K checkout visits three months ago; at a $5–25 ticket size, monthly revenue may be in the hundred-thousand-dollar range (speculation).
  • No public MRR or founder interviews found.

Confidence and gaps
