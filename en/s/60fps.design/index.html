Overview

60fps (60fps.design): Curated library of UI/UX animation and interaction design examples from top apps. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 3,737 visits went from 60fps to a checkout payment page — #576 of the 607 sites we track. That is 729% up from the April–June monthly average of 451.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 49%, search 38%, social 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)
