A local Thai team (บริษัท ลองเล่น เอไอ จำกัด; "ลองเล่น" means "try playing") registered the domain in April 2025 and launched a beta in June, building a "Thai Character.AI". Users create their own AI characters and fantasy worlds, and the tokens spent when others chat with a character are shared with its creator, so creators have a reason to repost their characters to Facebook and other social platforms. On the strength of this UGC supply (the sitemap already has about 4,500 characters and 4,500 worlds) and Thailand's anime and romance role-play crowd, social makes up four-tenths of traffic. It monetizes with small token top-ups via PromptPay and TrueMoney (from ฿19), and in October 2026 it added an Android app (50k+ downloads).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a mainly Thai AI character chat/role-play platform with three sections, Characters, Worlds and Creators; has daily/weekly/all-time popularity rankings; the 2026-10 update added voice calls, a Token feature, a voice system and a comment section. The site supports 9 languages including English, Vietnamese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Lao and Burmese.
  • Who built it: the terms of service say "ภายใต้การควบคุมและดูแลของ บริษัท ลองเล่น เอไอ จำกัด" (LongLen AI Co., Ltd., a Thai company). Founder name not found. The terms include Thailand-specific clauses such as no offending the royal family and no pornographic content.
  • Pricing: token top-up packs from 300 tokens for ฿19 up to 58,000 tokens for ฿1,699 (large packs give a 100%+ bonus), each message costs 10 tokens at minimum; payment by PromptPay, TrueMoney and credit card; there are also monthly/yearly subscriptions (not shown on the pricing page). https://www.longlenai.com/th/topup
  • App: Google Play "LongLenAI", developer LONGLEN AI, 50K+ downloads, 4.6 rating from 182 reviews, with ads and in-app purchases; APKPure's earliest version 1.7.0 is dated 2026-10-02.
  • Launch date: registered 2025-04-22 per Verisign RDAP (Cloudflare); first Wayback snapshot 2025-06-20, titled "ลองเล่นเอไอ (Beta)", already with a Community link.
  • Traffic (profile data): Thailand 98.8%; social 42.7%, direct 43.0%, search 8.5% (brand keywords 48.2%).

Presumably the first users came from anime, novel (like the ReadAWrite/Joylada web-fiction scene) and role-play groups on Thai Facebook, brought in by early creators posting their character links in the groups (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator supply: anyone can create characters/worlds, the site has a creator ranking and an earnings statistics page (/statistic?view=earning), and the profile records that creators earn tokens by the number of messages others send.
  • Popularity rankings (daily/weekly/all-time) give new characters exposure.
  • Social is 42.7%, and the official Facebook/Instagram accounts keep posting updates and calls to creators.

Speculation:

  • Creators post character links to Facebook and TikTok to earn tokens, bringing new users -> new users top up tokens to chat -> creator earnings rise -> more people create. This is a variant of branded output, but the evidence is only the earnings page and the social share.
  • Thai localization + local payments (small PromptPay/TrueMoney top-ups) are the moat against Character.AI.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Top-up minimum ฿19, each message from 10 tokens; Google Play 50k+ downloads; about 4,500 public characters. No revenue figures. Monthly revenue is presumably in the hundreds of thousands of baht (speculation, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Apart from the official site, Facebook, APKPure and Instagram, search results were basically irrelevant. The Facebook page body text could not be fetched, so only the summary was used.
  • The founding team, funding, the specific takeoff event and the source of the first users were not found, so the takeoff mode is recorded as "Not found".
