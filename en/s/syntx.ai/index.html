Basics

  • Product: An AI aggregation subscription: 90–100+ models for images, video, text and audio (GPT, Claude, Nano Banana, Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Suno, etc.), web version + Telegram bot @syntxaibot; also Trends templates, Academy courses (academy.syntx.ai), a knowledge base (docs.syntx.ai), and Contests ("coming soon").
  • Billing: FREE (5 tokens + 5 LLM requests); Pro about $14.45/month, VIP about $35.10, Elite about $52, Ultra Elite about $100 (compiled by third-party traksource in 2026-06); 15% off yearly; tokens roll over within the subscription period.
  • Entity: SYNTX INTELLIGENCE – FZCO, Dubai Digital Park, registered 2025-10-21 (https://syntx.ai/public-offer). Founder, team, funding: not found.
  • Risk signals: otzyvmarketing.ru says the site is blocked by Roskomnadzor for Russian IPs (only reachable via the bot or non-Russian IPs); user rating 2.7/5, complaining about price hikes and hard-to-cancel auto-renewal; Trustpilot 3.0/5 (14 reviews).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: at launch in 2023-08, self-promotion on Reddit AI boards + Russian TikTok pitching "MJ/DALL-E/GPT-4 in one place inside Telegram"; the product lives in Telegram, so Russian users need no VPN or foreign card.
  • Model-hype landing pages: every new model gets its own page (/models/seedance, /image/banana), and a new model release = a new acquisition window.
  • Creator marketing: paying freelancers to make tutorial Reels (the Contra case), running the @syntx_creators account to aggregate creator works, free Academy courses, and Contests collected via the contact email; overseas creators' works all carry the @syntx_global tag.
  • Russian-language referrals come from AI news sites and self-media: grokhub.ru, dzen.ru, musicsuno.ru (channel profile).
  • Speculation:
  • The review sites' promo links (syntx.ai/welcome/…) hint at an affiliate/commission arrangement, but no official commission description was seen.
  • The usual Russian-market play (buying ad slots in Telegram channels) was not found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Telegram bot has about 468k monthly active users.
  • Prices are about $14–100/month across four tiers; the number of paying users and revenue were not found.
  • Geography: Russia 69%, Germany 6%, Belarus 3%; in 2026 content from India and English-speaking creators began appearing, and globalization has only just started.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch date (the 2023-08-11 Reddit launch post, right after domain registration) has summary-level evidence; the Reddit originals could not be opened.
  • The founder, funding and number of paying users are still not found; Russian media (vc.ru, Habr) have no ready links.
  • Second-pass check: launch time changed from "inferred 2024" to 2023-08 (takeoff is still recorded as 2024, since the web version and scale came after 2024); growth engine changed from "not found" to "creators or community" (evidence of paid creator outsourcing), pure product-driven spread set to "no"; added pricing tiers, the Russia block and reputation risks.
