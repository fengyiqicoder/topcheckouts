Basics

  • Product: a free PSD mockup directory site. FAQ: "Free Mockup operates as a curated directory… carefully select and showcase the best free mockups from talented designers and agencies worldwide… a small portion… are created by our own team" (https://www.free-mockup.com/faq/). License page: copyright of the assets belongs to the original authors, and the self-made ones are commercially usable.
  • Extra tools: Image Converter, PSD to JPG, Figma to PNG, AI Image Upscaler, Mockup Generator and more.
  • Monetization: Placeit ads on the homepage ("Now 15% Off"); the 2018 About page carried Envato Elements ("Unlimited downloads, from $16.50/month") and a "Premium Mockups" entry; an "Advertising" section on the homepage.
  • Team: not public, the site is signed "The Free Mockup Team"; the domain is registered through Cloudflare with WHOIS hidden.
  • Domain: RDAP registered 2016-12-26; first Wayback snapshot 2018-02-03 (already a finished site, with the tagline "The biggest source for Free photorealistic Mockups online!").
  • Traffic profile (profile): India 12%, Indonesia 7.7%, Philippines 5.5%; search 33.7%, direct 63.7%; search terms free mockup, mockup smartphone, app icon logo mockup; social from Pinterest, X, YouTube.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Scale of aggregated content: about 780 mockup entries listed over 2017–2018, with categories covering dozens of category terms.
  • Social distribution accounts: Behance, X, Facebook, Pinterest, Instagram, Newsletter; the profile shows Pinterest as one of the social sources.
  • Domain as a generic term: brand keywords are 90.7% because "free mockup" is both the site name and the category term.

Speculation:

  • Designer supply side: original authors (Graphic Burger, Mockup World types) release free mockups, and this site aggregates them and links back to the authors, who are happy to be included. That is a weak version of "creator supply", but no evidence of a submission entry was seen.
  • The 2026 tool pages were added to capture tool long-tail terms like "psd to jpg" and "image upscaler".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue comes from affiliates (Envato Elements, Placeit) and display ads; no public figures.
  • Small scale: last month visits of 4,524 (1,945 two months ago) per the profile.

Confidence and gaps

  • The site is written from scratch. The search results were entirely unrelated game sites (poki, crazygames and the like) with no usable external links; this report relies entirely on the site's FAQ/License, sitemaps, Wayback and RDAP.
  • Gaps: the operator's identity, the takeoff event, the source of the first users and revenue are all Not found; "takeoff method: long-tail search" is inferred from the site type and the sitemap cadence.
