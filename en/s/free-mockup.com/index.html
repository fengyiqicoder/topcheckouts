Overview

Free-mockup (free-mockup.com): Free downloadable PSD mockup templates for designs, logos, packaging and UI. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,524 visits went from Free-mockup to a checkout payment page — #543 of the 607 sites we track. That is 32% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,427.

Total website visits that month were about 91,208 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 5.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 64%, search 34%, referrals/email/paid 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)
