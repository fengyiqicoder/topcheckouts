Basics

  • Product: YouTube Millionaire System, teaching how to build a faceless "brand channel" (documentary and explainer-style long videos), with 6 weekly modules + 30+ hours of lessons + one-on-one support + community + templates; one-time $997 (page lists an "original price $5,997"), with optional ongoing coaching at $997/month after 30 days; checkout goes through Whop (whop.com/magnates, 522 members joined, 28 reviews at 4.9).
  • Founder: John (signed as John F. on the site), says he studied business at university at a $50K tuition cost, "knew nothing about YouTube or making videos" before starting the channel, and only reached 1,000 subscribers in the first year; says he has 0 full-time employees and uses outsourced writers and editors (hired scriptwriters on Reddit r/HireaWriter in 2023, summary only). Nationality unverified; the UK is the top traffic country (29.7%), so he is presumed British.
  • Channel: YouTube @MagnatesMedia, created 2018-12-18; as of 2026-10-24 about 1.86M subscribers, 194M views, 255 videos; in the last 28 days 440K views and zero subscriber growth (vidIQ).
  • Traffic (our records): direct 45.2%, search 39.7% (brand keywords 77%: magnatesmedia, magnates media), referrals 9%; the only social source is YouTube. Countries: United Kingdom 29.7%, Brazil 17%, India 15.9%.
  • Domain: registered 2020-06-19 on Namecheap (RDAP).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Channel traffic: video descriptions link magnatesmedia.com (visible in the 2023-06 video description); 77% of the site's search traffic is brand keywords and the only social source is YouTube, showing new customers basically watch the channel first and then search.
  • Build a waitlist before selling: from 2023-07 to 2024-06 the site only collected emails, so when sales opened there was already a group of interested people (waitlist size not public).
  • Student cases feed back: the sales page uses a student's channel (450K subscribers) to prove the system is replicable, and the "faceless" and "brand channel" pitches are also pushed on X (summary only).

Speculation:

  • The first students most likely came from waitlist emails + voice-overs on the channel, reaching 350+ students in the first year of sales.
  • The course's "success" depends on the channel's own fame; with subscriptions flat in 2026, new-customer sources for the course will narrow too, so in 2026 it added $997/month coaching and "investing in student channels" to raise order value.
  • Pirate sites (gfxfather and others) are circulating download packs of the course, showing the course has some name recognition in the "YouTube automation" scene.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Students: 350+ (2025-01) → 650+ (2026-02); at $997 that is about $650K cumulative (rough, discounts or installments possible); 522 joined on the Whop page.
  • Channel: claims over $1M in cumulative YouTube ad revenue plus sponsorships; vidIQ estimates current monthly ad revenue at only about $2,350 (model estimate, likely low).
  • Annual course revenue estimated in the $300K-$500K range (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there was no old report in the notes. Search results were mostly unrelated generic articles; the work relied mainly on Wayback snapshots (2021, 2023, 2024, 2025), RDAP, vidIQ, the official site and the Whop page for evidence.
  • The channel creation date, the period from waitlist to sale, and the student counts are credible; "on sale in July 2024" is inferred from Wayback page-size changes, and no formal launch announcement was seen.
  • The Reddit hiring post was seen only as a summary; the founder's full name, nationality and sales in the launch month were all Not found.
