myinnercise.com is not a new site started from scratch but the membership app domain of the motivation/mindset training brand NeuroGym (founder John Assaraf, a featured guest in "The Secret" and a New York Times bestselling author). For years NeuroGym built up a large student base through Assaraf's personal brand + free online classes/challenges + high-priced courses. It registered myinnercise.com in 2020-10, launched the web app at the end of 2021, and listed on iOS in 2023-07, moving old students and high-priced course users into the $97/month Platinum membership; new customers come in almost entirely by searching parent-brand terms like "neurogym" (52.6% search traffic in the record).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: Innercise® mindset/brain training membership: 500+ guided visualizations, meditations, affirmations and "brain training" classes; the app has an AI coach, a member community and expert Q&A (Google Play description summary). Claims "10 years of brain science research" behind it, with recommending experts including Oxford's Dr. Sarah McKay and others.
  • Pricing: Platinum Membership $97/month (profile record); a free trial is offered.
  • Company: NeuroGym LLC (US); founder John Assaraf.
  • Scale: self-reports 100,000+ members (official blurb reposted by mergeek); Google Play 10K+ downloads (updated 2026-06-02); the iOS version "NeuroGym® - Brain Training" has 1,220 ratings, 4.85 rating, and the App Store records a release on 2023-07-24 (iTunes API).
  • Subdomains: app. (landing page), web. (login/sign-up, page branding now renamed NeuroGym), mkt. (marketing), dev.
  • Traffic (profile record): search 52.6% ("neuro gym", "neurogym"), direct 29.9%, referral 10.5%; US 92%, India 8%.
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2020-10-05; first Wayback snapshot 2021-12-18 (React single-page app); the root domain redirects with a 301 from 2024-08 (presumably to the web./app. subdomains or myneurogym.com).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Parent brand sends traffic: search traffic is almost entirely the "neurogym" brand term, and the login page is already directly named NeuroGym (profile record + web.myinnercise.com).
  • Founder personal-brand endorsement: landing pages/app blurbs repeatedly use "New York Times bestselling author, Brain Whisperer John Assaraf" and endorsements from experts at top universities (mergeek).
  • Community and AI coach: an in-app member community, sharing achievements and expert Q&A improve retention (Google Play description summary).

Speculation:

  • NeuroGym's main acquisition is Assaraf's free online classes/challenges + a Facebook/YouTube ad funnel, then upselling high-priced courses and the $97/month membership (a typical playbook for similar motivational training, from memory, unverified).
  • The appearance of checkout visits in 2026-09 is a change of checkout system, not a new takeoff.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reports 100,000+ members (may include free/historical students). If 2,000–5,000 people pay $97/month, monthly revenue would be $200,000–500,000 (Speculation, wide range).
  • iOS 1,220 ratings + Android 10K+ downloads means app installs themselves are not large; the main paying base may be on the web.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Both the myneurogym.com and myinnercise.com homepages were blocked by Cloudflare (403/JS rendering), so the product and price come mainly from the profile record, app stores and reposted blurbs.
  • Gaps: NeuroGym's founding date, Assaraf interviews, the actual member count and ad spend all Not found in original text; "The Secret" and the 2011 date are inferred from memory/summaries.
