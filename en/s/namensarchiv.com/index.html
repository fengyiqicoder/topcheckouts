Basics

  • Product: German-language surname origin lookup. Free, no sign-up to get results (word root, name type, regional distribution, historical map); paid: PDF certificate €3.90, a personal surname video delivered within 48 hours €9.90, video + certificate €12.90, gift edition €12.90 (with a message, printable card and redemption code), plus voluntary donations. An English version exists.
  • Method: an AI system does an automatic first analysis based on standard onomastics literature (methodik page) before presenting the result.
  • Author: Jan Ozeir, Berlin, a small individual operator (§19 UStG Kleinunternehmer, small business exemption); his publishing project "Deutsche Chroniken" has posted history/onomastics/local history short videos since 2025, self-described as "about 86,000 people reached". Sources: https://namensarchiv.com/ueber-uns.html; https://namensarchiv.com/impressum.html
  • Scale: homepage "190,000+ lookups", "2,147 names today", "TikTok 100,000+ followers".
  • Traffic (profile record): direct 57.4%, search 31.9% (brand keywords 89.4%), referral 6.4%; Germany 98.3%, Austria 1.7%.
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2026-08-25; no Wayback archive (the Archive was briefly offline during the query, and a retry found no record); Gridinsoft/Scamadviser both label it "very new".

First 1,000 users: from the creator's own German-language history/surname short-video audience (the About page states plainly that "many messages asking about the meaning of their own names led to this website"), a direct migration of an existing fan circle.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Content → tool: every surname video tells the story of one surname and directs viewers to "look up your own surname" at namensarchiv.com (URL in the TikTok bio, About page).
  • Sharing the file: "Akte teilen" (share file) copies a link in one click, and lookup results can be shared (homepage).
  • Giftified monetization: the personal video is made into a gift (redemption code + printable card), suited to giving family members for birthdays/holidays (/geschenk).

Speculation:

  • Result pages get shared into family WhatsApp groups, and relatives then look up their own/their spouse's surname, "multiple lookups per family"; direct visits at 57% fit this.
  • "Do my surname" comments are themselves TikTok engagement signals that boost video distribution, forming a content ↔ tool loop.
  • The paid videos are most likely AI semi-automated (48-hour delivery), with low marginal cost.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 190,000+ lookups (about 2 months); at 1–2% buying and an €8–10 order, cumulative revenue is about €15,000–40,000 (Speculation).
  • The homepage donation counter is 0, so donations are not a source of revenue.
  • September checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The founder, origin story and launch timing are all supported by the site's self-reports + WHOIS, fairly high credibility.
  • Gaps: the TikTok page could not be fetched, so single viral video view counts and the specific date of the surge were not found; the exact date the site launched (between late August and September) is not pinned to a day.
