Basics

  • Product: a proxy IP service with residential ($3/GB and up), static ISP ($0.5/IP/day and up), unlimited residential ($230/day and up), 4G/5G mobile and datacenter proxies; listed uses are cross-border e-commerce multi-store setups (Amazon/Shopee/eBay/Lazada), data scraping, QA, sneaker botting and AI training data (site homepage, profile file).
  • Who built it: the site has no team information; the page title and meta are in Chinese ("high-speed, stable proxy network", "built for cross-border e-commerce, data scraping, QA/risk control and automation"), so probably a Chinese team (speculation). Trustpilot lists the country as the US, with only the email Support@ipfist.com.
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2025-07-18; earliest Wayback 2026-01-31 (earliest visible Trustpilot review 2026-01-03), so the formal launch was probably between late 2025 and 2026-01 (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Display ad buys account for 40.8% (profile file).
  • The FIST20 promo code, referral program, partner program and Discord community (site, profile file).
  • Direct traffic is 44.4%: proxies are a pay-as-you-go consumable, so existing customers come back to top up (profile file).
  • Trustpilot shows 55 almost all five-star reviews in a short window, used to build trust (Trustpilot).

Speculation:

  • Referral/partner commissions get purchase-bot circles, tool sellers and gray-market tool marketplaces to distribute it, a common playbook in the proxy IP industry.
  • The reviews on Trustpilot are clustered and almost all five-star, so there may be an incentive for invited reviews (speculation).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Usage-based pricing: residential $3/GB, unlimited residential $230/day, etc.; no revenue or customer count numbers.
  • The site has only 42 URLs and Trustpilot has 55 reviews, so scale is probably below a few tens of thousands of visits a month (speculation).

Confidence and gaps
