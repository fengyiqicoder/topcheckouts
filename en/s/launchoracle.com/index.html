Basics

  • Product: a pre-launch marketing platform for crowdfunding: builds warm-up landing pages for project creators, captures email leads, offers an optional $1 reservation deposit (paid directly via Stripe), shows traffic sources/conversion, runs audience research and ad tests, plus an "Explore campaigns" browsing page.
  • Operator: the terms say "Launch Oracle is a product developed by Pulling Power Media" and are governed by South African law (https://www.launchoracle.com/terms-of-service, updated 2026-04-02). Pulling Power Media is a full-service digital agency in Cape Town (social media, paid ads, animation, websites); in 2021-2022 its site called itself "fast growing", and its portfolio includes Ghostfire Gaming's Questo crowdfunding creative and Sensible Object social media (Wayback 2022-02). Founder name Not found.
  • Pricing: not public (the pricing page is empty; the homepage is a waitlist / Get early access).
  • Domain: launchoracle.com registered 2025-05-20 (RDAP); Wayback has an old unrelated snapshot from 2021. pullingpowermedia.com was registered 2017-04-29. DNS for both domains changed on 2026-04-10, same owner.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Client ads bring traffic: warm-up page URLs are launchoracle.com/<publisher>/<project>, and publishers' Facebook, Instagram and YouTube ads and posts all point to them (our data: paid social 52.1%, organic social 30.6%, search terms mostly specific project names).
  • Warm-up pages show up in BGG listings, YouTube/Bilibili video descriptions and Bluesky posts, and players see the launchoracle.com domain every time.
  • The homepage uses well-known board game projects as endorsement (Plants vs. Zombies, Dice Throne, Clash Royale, The Witcher, Eragon, etc.) to pull in the next publisher.
  • Migration of the agency's existing clients: Ghostfire Games is both an old Pulling Power client and on the Launch Oracle homepage project list.

Speculation:

  • The first customers were board game crowdfunding clients Pulling Power was already serving; Launch Oracle was probably an internal tool used when running their ads, later productized (the "productized service" wording in 2025-07 supports this).
  • The board game publisher circle is small and publishers can see each other's warm-up pages, so peer word of mouth is the main source of new customers.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The homepage claims it has "helped products launch and raise millions of dollars"; a single project page can reach 3,000+ leads.
  • The pricing model is not public, possibly an agency service fee plus a platform fee; amounts Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The Pulling Power Media relationship comes from the terms text itself, credible; "migration of agency clients" is inferred from the overlap between the portfolio and the project list.
  • Search results were mostly unrelated rocket-launch/Oracle articles; BGG and Facebook could not be opened, so only summaries were used.
  • Gaps: founder name; pricing; the first client project to launch and when; number of paying clients.
