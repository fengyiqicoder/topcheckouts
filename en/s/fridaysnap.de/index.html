Basics

  • Product: wedding QR-code shared album (web app, guests don't install an app), unlimited photos and videos, bulk download, access permissions, photo games, folders, a Live slideshow screen, a digital guest book, prints/albums; photo booth rental from €189 newly added. Supports 25+ languages.
  • Who built it: FridaySnap, Frankfurt (Platz der Einheit 2), founders Daniel (customer contact) + Dennis (head of tech), surnames not public. https://fridaysnap.com/de/about-us/
  • Launch timing: fridaysnap.de and fridaysnap.com were both registered on 2024-09-03 (RDAP; Wayback that day still shows a hosting provider placeholder page); by 2024-10-04 it was a complete sales page. There are also multi-country sites such as fridaysnap.at and fridaysnap.co.uk.
  • Pricing: one-time €39.99 (crossed-out price €85.99; €49.99 in 2025), no monthly fee; a "early bird 50%" or "Last Minute 15%" discount is displayed year-round; the TikTok bio gives a 5% discount code tiktok5.
  • Channel profile: Germany 90.9%, Austria 9.1%; direct 56.5%, paid 22.5%, instant messaging 20.9%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Every wedding has dozens to over a hundred guests scanning in to the FridaySnap page to upload photos (product mechanic), so guests = product exposure.
  • Instant messaging traffic is 20.9%: couples send the album link to guests via WhatsApp (channel profile).
  • Exposure through wedding directories/fairs (the Bekannt aus list) and Pinterest (Trustpilot reviews).

Speculation:

  • Engaged couples/bridesmaids among the guests see it for the first time at a wedding and search the brand directly when planning their own, which fits the 56.5% direct traffic.
  • Year-round discount countdowns + Google/Meta ads are the main paid acquisition methods.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 10,000+ weddings and 400,000+ users (2026-03); 4,300 weddings in 2024-10. At €40–50 per wedding, cumulative revenue is on the order of €400,000–500,000 (Speculation, excluding photo booths and prints).
  • Checkout visits: 0 over the past 4–6 months, 4,712 two months ago, 12,894 last month These numbers are identical to several sites in the same batch, suspected to be estimated lower-bound/placeholder values, and should not be read as real changes.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, first report. Launch timing, founders' first names, pricing and claimed scale have evidence from the official site and Wayback.
  • Gaps: founders' full names, whether there was a predecessor brand before 2024-09 (4,300 weddings one month after launch is suspicious), ad campaign details and real revenue all Not found; the takeoff method "paid ads" rests on the 22.5% paid traffic in the channel profile and year-round promotions, which is an inference.
