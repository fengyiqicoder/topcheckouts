Basics

  • Product: a PPV live streaming platform for events. Each event is ticketed separately (e.g. 1VS1 Fighting II €14.99; tiers include early-bird, standard and replay), supports Stripe, Montonio, MobilePay and bank transfer, with an anti-piracy policy, geo-restrictions and casting; the interface is in English, Finnish and Swedish.
  • Tech: WordPress + Next.js, hosted on Cloudflare, Google Workspace email (osintintel report); social accounts are Instagram @broadcastingplatforms / @joinlivearena. The logo alt text on broadcastingplatforms.com reads "Dream Tribe Logo", possibly the parent company's old or affiliated brand (unverified). | https://osintintel.com/report/livearena.io
  • Operator: the terms name Broadcasting Commercial Operations OÜ (an Estonian company, trading as "Broadcasting Platforms", broadcastingplatforms.com, domain registered 2020-06-30), which sells white-label live streaming / creator monetization solutions; its site claims a cumulative 12M+ unique views, 180M+ live-stream minutes, $7.6M in transactions and $5.1M paid out to creators, and quotes an "early investor" (indicating angel investment, amount not found). LinkedIn: broadcasting-platforms; Instagram: @joinlivearena.
  • Content partners: ICE CAGE (editions 4–9), 1VS1 Fighting, Voimaa on! Fight Night (Lappeenranta, Finland), and the Nordic League 2026 finals (Berlin). The main audience is in Finland.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: checkout visits are perfectly synchronized with event dates (16–34K in months with an event, close to 0 in months without), showing traffic comes from organizers funneling fans, not from the platform's own retention or word of mouth. The platform has "event announcement" push notifications (opt-in) and organization_invitation (organizer onboarding), i.e. a B2B2C model: each additional event organizer signed = one more stream of traffic.
  • Speculation: fighters / influencer fighters promote their own bouts on Instagram and TikTok with the stream ticket link, which is the main acquisition action; cross-promotion across events (people who watched ICE CAGE get pushes for 1VS1 and Voimaa on!).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ticket prices €7.5–16.99 (page price fields). 34K checkout visits in September; at a 20–30% conversion that would be about 7–10K PPV tickets per event, roughly €100–150K in revenue (pure speculation).
  • The parent company claims $7.6M in cumulative transactions (including other platforms).

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass check: livearena.ai (event ticketing and venue operations SaaS, an innova17 case study) and livearena.com in the search results are unrelated same-name companies and have been excluded. The parent company's site still lists no founders or customer names and does not mention LiveArena. No origin story was added this round; only confirmed that "first users = ICE CAGE viewers, funneled via YouTube / Instagram event promotion". Classification unchanged.
  • The correspondence between the fight calendar and traffic is first-hand evidence and highly credible; who organizes ICE CAGE, whether there are influencer fighters, and Broadcasting Platforms' founders and funding were not found (WebSearch quota exhausted, no other search used to work around it).
