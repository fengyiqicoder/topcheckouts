This is not a product that acquires customers by itself, but a payment portal using a WHMCS template, held under a shell-like Bulgarian company (NGDigital LTD), selling plans of unclear nature by "30/180/365 days, 1/2/4 TB". 95.8% of new customers come from referrals, over half are Japanese, and Japanese users who saw "GATORKEY.NET 利用国 BGR" on their credit card statements did not recognize it and went online to ask. These clues all point to it being the payment checkout for some file-hosting premium membership (highly suspected to be a file host like Rapidgator that sells by TB of traffic). Its traffic depends entirely on the upstream site, and it has no growth story of its own.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product (2025-02 Wayback): WHMCS customer portal, with items "Cloud Hosting plan 30/180/365" (only says "30 Days Storage") and "VPN Netherlands"; the prices captured in the profile are 30 days 1 TB €16.99, 2 TB €33.00, 4 TB €45.00, priced in euros. No product description.
  • Entity: terms (updated 2024-02-14) name NGDigital LTD, registration number 207573588, Bulgaria, Sofia 1000, 59 Vitosha Blvd., 6-17; the terms were adapted from a generic hosting-provider template (document language tag RU) and do not match what is actually sold.
  • Current state (2026-10-25): the page shows "ionCube Loader not installed" and cannot be accessed, but the title Bing indexed is still "Copyright © 2026 Gatorkey.net", and the knowledge base has payment-related articles such as "My card was declined" and "How Do I Update My Payment Method".
  • Same name, unrelated: YouTube @Gatorkey ("Gator Keys") and the GTA puzzle creator gatorkeys on Reddit are both unrelated to this site.
  • Funding, team: Not found.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • The site itself has no marketing content, social accounts or referral program.
  • Traffic structure: referral 95.8%, direct 3.1%, search 0.6%; the search terms are all things like "gatorkey.net forgot password", "abo removal", "support mail", which existing customers use to find support or cancel (profile).
  • Users do not recognize this merchant name: in 2024-08 a Japanese user went online to ask about it as a suspicious charge.
  • Speculation:
  • The first users and new users all come from an upstream site, redirected to gatorkey.net to pay at upstream checkout. The upstream is most likely a premium membership of a file host: tiered by days + TB of traffic (30 days 1/2/4 TB), many Japanese users, "gator" in the merchant name, the Chiebukuro sidebar full of Rapidgator renewal questions, and Bing also links it to Rapidgator posts on r/Piracy. From memory, unverified: Rapidgator premium is also sold by 30 days + TB traffic quota, priced in euros.
  • Using a generic product name + a Bulgarian entity + mismatched terms fits the pattern of a "high-risk business collecting payments through a clean merchant account".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Price: 30 days €16.99–45.00 (profile); the amount on the Japanese statement is 16.690 in foreign currency units.
  • Scale: monthly checkout visits of 30K–40K. Assuming an order value of €17–45 and 10–20% payment conversion, monthly gross revenue could be on the order of €50K–300K (pure estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: takeoff year changed from "Not found" to 2024 (site live in 2024-02, Japanese users already charged by 2024-08); added the charge evidence from Japan's Chiebukuro and the specific speculation of "suspected payment collection for a file host such as Rapidgator"; added the TB-tier pricing.
  • Gaps: there is no direct evidence that the upstream is Rapidgator (rapidgator.net cannot be fetched, scamdoc 403). Suggest checking gatorkey.net's referring domains against our referral source data to confirm.
