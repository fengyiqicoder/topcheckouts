Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator video description slot: every new Jesper video has "Sign up for Scalios here" plus an exclusive discount code in the description, and the viewers are exactly the sellers doing Shopify dropshipping.
  • Community endorsement: the site's testimonial section carries the Freedom Achievers name and its Trustpilot badge, moving the trust of the course community onto the SaaS.
  • Brand keyword search at 85%: users hear the name elsewhere first (videos, community) and then search for it, rather than arriving through generic keywords.

Speculation:

  • Freedom Achievers students are asked or recommended to use Scalios in the course, and are the first paying users.
  • The high share from France and Germany may be European students in Jesper's community, or the local network of the Dutch team.
  • Very small scale: videos get a few thousand views and the community has 353 people, so the user base is probably a few hundred to one or two thousand.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue data. The upstream traffic pool: YouTube about 11K subscribers with a few thousand views per video, and a paid Skool community of 353 people.
  • Pricing is not public, so no estimate is possible.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The tie between Scalios and Jesper Hensgens / Freedom Achievers has first-hand evidence (the original text of the video descriptions, the Trustpilot badge on the site) and is credible; the equity relationship between them is unclear.
  • Most search results were generic AI tool lists, with no Reddit, Product Hunt or media coverage.
  • Gaps: the founders' identity, pricing, paying user count, whether it is listed on the Shopify App Store.
