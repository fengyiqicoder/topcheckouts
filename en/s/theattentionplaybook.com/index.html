Growth loop

Evidence:

  • First users = the author's own X followers: the launch tweet got 350k views, and every subsequent tip/viral-breakdown tweet carries the presale link; the only social source is X.
  • Step-up pricing + a "N sold" counter create urgency; buyers post purchases on X and the author retweets with thanks, forming small-scale social proof.
  • Testimonials come from indie developers in the author's X circle (founders of Postiz, Once, etc.).

Speculation:

  • The course doubles as content marketing for SuperX: students learning X growth may also buy SuperX, and SuperX users are potential course buyers.
  • The promised "community" may be a hook for future repurchases/renewals.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site shows 225 sold; the presale price stepped up to $249 from a lower start, starting price not found. If it started around $149, rough presale revenue is $40–50k (speculation).
  • The author's main business SuperX has $22k MRR (TrustMRR-verified, via IndieMakerList).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Launch tweet metrics come from the fxtwitter API and are credible; the 225 sales figure is the site's counter (self-reported).
  • Gaps: starting price of the step-up ladder unknown; no post-launch data (delivery only on 10-31); no sign of affiliate distribution.
