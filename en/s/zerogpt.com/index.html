After ChatGPT launched on 2022-11-30, GPTZero by Princeton student Edward Tian went viral around New Year 2023. Three days later (2023-01-05) someone registered zerogpt.com, a name almost its reverse, and within two weeks launched a free, no-signup detection box whose title was stuffed with "Chat GPT / Open AI / AI essay detector for teacher", catching a lot of "gpt zero / zero gpt / chatgpt detector" searches and confused traffic. After that, teachers and students kept coming back to run checks, and the "is it accurate?" debates on Reddit kept generating brand exposure. It then filled out the site with tools such as an AI humanizer rewriter, plagiarism check and translation, plus versions in 5 languages, and got the generic term "ai detector" to rank too; in 2026 it made the a16z top 50 generative AI websites (#39).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI content detection (text, images, video) + a writing toolkit (AI rewriting/humanizing, plagiarism check, grammar, summarizer, translator, citation generator, chatbot, etc.) (profile file, LinkedIn).
  • Who built it: the operating entity is Olive Works LLC (Casper, Wyoming), "doing business as ZeroGPT" (site Terms, version of 2026-10-07); CEO Rawad Baroud (2026-01 press release, relayed by theairankings); LinkedIn says founded in 2022, 11-50 people. It is an unrelated company from GPTZero (gptzero.me), and the similar names are often confused.
  • Pricing: free tier is $0 forever; Personal/Business subscriptions, 40% off with annual billing (profile file).
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2023-01-05; the earliest Wayback, 2023-01-18, is already a working detection page, "ZeroGPT - Chat GPT, Open AI and AI text detector Free Tool", claiming "analyzed 10 million texts, 98%+ accuracy" and "Millions of users trust ZeroGPT".
  • Current traffic (profile file): United States 41.9%, Philippines 6.7%, India 5.5%; organic search 60.4% (brand keywords 53.9%: zerogpt, zero gpt; about 36% non-brand: ai detector, ai checker, detector de ia); direct 29.7%; referrals quillbot.com 4.5%, toolify.ai 4.4%, ucm.es (Complutense University of Madrid) 4.3%; paid search 5.9%.

Where the first users came from: direct evidence is thin, but the timing is very clear: registered on day 3 after GPTZero went viral, live in week 2, with page title and description written entirely around "chat gpt detector / open ai detector / ai essay detector for teacher" search terms. The first users were probably teachers and students searching "gpt zero" and "chatgpt detector" in January 2023, some of them confused traffic that took it for GPTZero (speculation, based on the name and timeline).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: organic search 60.4%, ranking for both the brand term and the generic term "ai detector" (profile file).
  • Free, no-signup detection, and returning users come back repeatedly (direct 29.7%) (profile file, Wayback).
  • Referrals from AI tool directories such as QuillBot and toolify.ai, and from university sites (profile file).

Speculation:

  • The "is it accurate?" controversy is itself the spread: Reddit, academic papers and media reviews repeatedly name ZeroGPT, pushing brand-term search volume ever higher.
  • Detection → pushing the paid "AI humanizer rewriter" tool, so the same students are customers at both ends.
  • Multilingual versions (French/German/Spanish/Italian/Portuguese) catch terms like "detector de ia" in non-English countries.

Revenue and scale clues

  • #39 in the a16z top 50 generative AI websites (by monthly unique visitors, reposted by the CEO).
  • Subscription-based, with exact prices not shown on the pricing page; revenue figures not found. LinkedIn says 11-50 people.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were mostly accuracy review articles; the evidence comes from the Wayback 2023-01 homepage, RDAP, the site's Terms (operating company), LinkedIn, the theairankings roundup and the profile file. Reddit was seen only through summaries.
  • The founding team's background, early traffic data and whether it deliberately rode the GPTZero name have no primary evidence; "confused traffic" is an inference from the registration timing and naming.
