start.innovatingwithai.com is just the subscriber resource-pickup page for the "Innovating with AI" newsletter (guides, case studies and toolkits are all delivered via email links) and does not acquire users itself. The parent site was registered and launched in 2023-02, right after the ChatGPT boom, by serial entrepreneur Rob Howard (CEO of the development agency HDC, previously behind MasterWP, Understrap and others). It trades lead magnets like a "free No-Code AI Toolkit" for email addresses, claimed 45,000 subscribers by 2023-10 and 175,000 in 2026, and converts subscribers into paid programs such as the AI Consultancy Project, the AI Executive System and the AI Founder Accelerator (claims 2,000+ students trained since 2023).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a free weekly AI newsletter + paid programs (The AI Consultancy Project, The AI Executive System, The AI Founder Accelerator), Private AI, and a "find a consultant" directory; the start subdomain is the subscriber resource download entry, with the page stating "nothing to browse" and all content going through email.
  • Who made it: Innovating with AI, LLC; founder Rob Howard (about 20 years in tech, sold a 1M-user cloud storage startup in 2009, CEO of development agency HDC with clients including Harvard, MIT and the World Bank); course development by Nyasha Green (LinkedIn Learning instructor).
  • Launch date: innovatingwithai.com WHOIS created 2023-02-05, first Wayback capture 2023-02-22; the start subdomain has no Wayback record, and the profile file says it newly appeared in 2026-09.
  • Tech stack: main site on WordPress, email via Kit (ConvertKit) (homepage source).
  • Traffic (profile file, start subdomain): roughly 3,920 visits in 2026-09; direct 46%, referrals 40%, email 8%, no search terms; United States 76%, United Kingdom 17%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free resources (No-Code AI Toolkit, guides, case studies) exchanged for email -> weekly newsletter -> paid programs (homepage structure, start subdomain positioning).
  • Homepage displays media logos from Fortune, Forbes, CNET, Barron's, Entrepreneur and others as endorsements.

Speculation:

  • AI newsletters at the 175,000-subscriber scale commonly use SparkLoop/Kit recommendation networks or paid subscriber acquisition, but no evidence was found in this research, so the growth engine is marked "Not found".
  • The start subdomain's 40% referral traffic comes from the main site and partner links, meaning it is existing subscribers picking up resources, not new user acquisition.

Confidence and gaps
