Dr. Nicolai Klemke of Berlin (a hobby musician for 12 years) became fascinated with Stable Diffusion animation in late 2022, registered the domain in 2022-12 and put up a waitlist in 2023-01 for a "text-to-animation" tool. Early 2023 happened to be a window with few AI video tools, and listings in AI directories and newsletters such as There's An AI For That and Product Hunt (featured twice, 2023-02 and 2023-08) brought the first users. He then found that most paying users were independent musicians who wanted visuals for their songs, so in H2 2023 he pivoted to "audio-reactive AI music videos", a "DAW for video", and has focused only on music videos since. Growth afterwards came from an affiliate program paying 20% for 9 months that gets music YouTubers/coaches to promote it, a blog plus free tool pages like mp3 to mp4 that pick up long-tail search, and community showcase pages. After a year solo he started hiring in late 2023; now a 17-person team, claiming hundreds of thousands of users, with no funding raised at any point.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI music video platform: upload a song and generate audio-reactive animated videos driven by beat/frequency bands, lyric videos, lip-sync videos and short clips, with frame-by-frame timeline editing; Autopilot for one-click videos; community discovery page at play.neuralframes.com/discover.
  • Founder: Dr. Nicolai Klemke (personal site klemken.com), founded late 2022; Neural Frames GmbH (Berlin, Germany). 17-person team, self-funded (Substack interview https://aimusicnewsletter.substack.com/p/from-song-to-screen-inside-neural).
  • Pricing (profile): Neural Knight $26/month, Neural Ninja $66/month, Neural Nirvana $199/month (discount for annual billing); free trial credits available. Affiliate program: 20% commission on referred paying users' payments for the first 9 months (https://affiliates.neuralframes.com/).
  • Domain: registered 2022-12-06 (RDAP; 2011 snapshots belong to a previous owner); first new snapshot 2023-01-04 (waitlist page).
  • Traffic (profile): United States 26%, Germany 7%, Brazil 4%; search 49% (non-brand about 20%: video to audio converter, mp3 to mp4), direct 46%; AI referrals 4.7% (openart.ai, ChatGPT); referrals wondershare.jp/.tw.

Growth loop

  • AI directories and newsletters: TAAFT, futurepedia, aiworldtools, AI Tool Report etc.

Speculation:

  • Musicians post generated videos to YouTube/Spotify Canvas/short-video platforms and "what did you make this with" comments bring word of mouth; but finished videos generally carry no watermark, so the spread is limited.
