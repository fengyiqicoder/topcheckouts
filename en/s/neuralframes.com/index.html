Overview

Neuralframes (neuralframes.com): AI music video platform turning uploaded songs into audio-reactive videos for musicians. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Neuralframes to a checkout payment page — #447 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 122,578 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 7.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 49%, direct 46%, AI assistants 5%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 20%, with queries like “video to audio converter”, “mp3 to mp4”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: $26/mo Neural Knight; $312/yr Neural Knight; $66/mo Neural Ninja; $792/yr Neu.
