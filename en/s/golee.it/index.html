A Milan sports-tech startup that built amateur football club management software at the end of 2018. Its real takeoff came in 2020 during the pandemic, when it landed partnerships with regional committees of the Italian football federation (FIGC/LND): the Lombardy, Piedmont, Friuli, and Calabria committees handed out Golee's basic version 'free forever' to clubs in their jurisdictions, and customers grew from 200 to 1,400 within a year. After that, each club pulled dozens to hundreds of families/players into the App to fill in registrations, sign documents, and pay membership fees ('the organization brings the people'), and partnerships with individual sports federations, a Facebook group of 8,000+ clubs, and SEO took it to 100+ sports and 20,000 clubs. The last month surge in checkout visits is the seasonal peak of concentrated registration and payment at the start of the new Italian season (early last month).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a management SaaS for amateur/professional sports clubs (ASD/SSD): online member/player registration, reminders for document and medical certificate expiry, fee collection and receipts, document signing, schedule communication, club websites, a coach App, and a shop; parents/members pay and sign via browser or App.
  • Company: Gle Holding / Golee, Milan, Italy, an innovative startup.
  • Founding team: Tommaso Guerra (CEO), Felice Biancardi (president / cofounder), Stiven Muccioli (CMO), Daniele De Pandis/Roselli (names differ across sources); CTO Luca Giovenzana; advisors Clarence Seedorf (former AC Milan star) and Leonardo Limatola (former Fiorentina CFO). 18 people in 2020, 20 full-time in 2022.
  • Funding: €850K in 2019 (Maider and others); direct investment from Qatar Sportstech (Qatar Foundation); 2020-11 BacktoWork equity crowdfunding targeting €500K (about €1.3M cumulative including grants and tax credits); 2022-02 targeting €800K (SeedBlink €300K + 7 Investment, the Moratti family office, CDP national venture fund €500K); there are also reports of a €2M capital increase at Gle Holding (date not found). A new round in 2024-2026 was not found.
  • Pricing: a time-unlimited free basic version + paid modules (exact prices not found).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The organization brings the people: once a club is set up, it uses 'share one link' to let members/parents register online (the site feature 'Raccogli le anagrafiche dei tuoi tesserati condividendo un semplice link'), and parents then pay and sign via browser/App -> each B2B customer brings dozens to hundreds of consumer users (in 2022, 2,500 clubs = about 250K users).
  • Distribution through parent bodies: FIGC regional committees (2020) -> individual sports federations (FICK and FIJLKAM in 2026) push Golee as a recommended/free tool down to member clubs.
  • Community: a private Golee Facebook group of 8,000+ club secretaries/presidents helping each other.
  • Permanently free basic version -> paid module upgrades.

Speculation:

  • Parents/coaches belong to several clubs at once (a child playing two sports, coaches working across clubs) and recommend Golee to another club; WhatsApp ranks high among social sources, matching clubs posting registration/payment links in parent groups.
  • Italy's 2023 sports reform (RASD registration, labor rules) raised the compliance burden and pushed digitization; no direct Golee data was found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2022 revenue target of €500K (actual not disclosed); club count: 2020-06 180 -> end of 2020 1,400 -> 2021-06 1,650 -> 2022-02 2,500 -> 2022-06 3,000+ -> 2025-06 16,000+ -> 2026 20,000+ (mostly on the free version, paid share unknown).
  • Users: 250K unique users in 2022.

Confidence and gaps

  • The 2020 free distribution by FIGC committees = the takeoff point, with strong evidence (multiple Calcio e Finanza articles + Forbes Italia + Il Giorno).
  • Gaps: recent revenue, number of paying clubs, funding after 2023, the specific date of the Serie C partnership; the relationship between the 2016-2018 'golee!' youth football community and the current company.
