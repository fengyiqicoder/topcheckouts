Overview

Remoterocketship (remoterocketship.com): Paid remote job board that scans company career pages for new remote listings. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 55,874 visits went from Remoterocketship to a checkout payment page — #35 of the 607 sites we track. That is 85% up from the April–June monthly average of 30,217.

Total website visits that month were about 605,701 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 9.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 48%, search 46%, AI assistants 4%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 48%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: $29/mo (US); $59/3 months (US); $99/yr (US).
