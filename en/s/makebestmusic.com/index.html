In mid-2024 Suno/Udio made "AI songwriting" explode, but they only had English sites and a high subscription barrier. A Chinese team with a Hong Kong entity (App Store developer Century Network (Hong Kong) Limited) registered the domain in 2024-06 and launched an "AI music production suite" (songwriting + covers + stem splitting + mixing) in July. In the first months it submitted the site to dozens of AI tool directories such as toolify, TAAFT and Fazier to get its first users and backlinks, then bought competitor and generic terms like "suno", "suno.com", "udio" and "ai music" directly on Google/Bing to intercept traffic (paid search is about 1/3 of traffic), used multilingual pages (Japanese, Italian etc.) and mini-tool pages like stem splitting/vocal removal to pick up organic search, and added an iOS app in 2026. A Japanese blogger pointed out that the underlying generation appeared to call Suno's backend (download URLs pointed to cdn1.suno.ai).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Where the first users came from: the evidence points to AI tool directory submissions (inbound links with parameters from dozens of directories) and Google ads from 2024-08; no founder social posts, Product Hunt or press launch were found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Buying competitor terms: Google/Bing ads on suno, suno.com, udio, ai music, lyrics generator, ai music cover etc.; paid search is about 33% of traffic.
  • AI directory blitz: submitted to dozens of directories in H2 2024.
  • Tool pages and multilingual pages picking up the long tail: voice remover, stem splitting, sound effects etc.
  • Community showcase pages (/shared-music/) are crawlable, and track links carry the brand domain.

Speculation:

  • Users posting generated songs to short-video platforms generally do not carry the site's brand, so showcase pages contribute little; growth relies mainly on the "paid ads — free credits — subscription" funnel.
  • Italy's unusual 11% share may come from local targeted ads or a local community.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No revenue or funding disclosure found.
  • Buying Suno/Udio competitor terms for several years running suggests positive ad ROI (speculation).
  • About 43 Trustpilot reviews (French-page summary); the app has only 18 ratings, so the app side is small.

Confidence and gaps

  • New entry, written from scratch. Launch date, directory submissions, competitor-term ads, Hong Kong entity and the Suno-backend controversy are backed by RDAP/Wayback/App Store/note evidence.
  • Gaps: team identity, user count and revenue all not found; the Suno-backend claim comes from a single blog's observation and is unverified.
