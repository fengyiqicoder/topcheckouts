Basics

  • Product: AI video generation producing 5-second/10-second 9:16 vertical clips, 1–4 per batch; suited to faceless niches like sports highlights, space documentaries, kids' animation, finance explainers and UGC ads; also a Vixal Clipping Tool (uses Claude; paste a YouTube link to auto-cut shorts, no watermark). Underlying models: homepage says "powered by Sora 2"; a review lists Kling 2.5 Turbo, Kling 3.0 Pro, Veo 3.1 Fast, PixVerse V6.
  • Pricing (2026-07 review): Starter $19/month for 250 credits (about 16 five-second clips), Creator $39/month for 400 credits (incl. commercial license), Pro $89/month for 800 credits; a 5-second clip costs about 15 credits. No-card trial.
  • Team: Ethereal Media (Trustpilot company blurb "built by Ethereal Media", address 1630 Quail Way, Crystal Lake, IL 60014); founder unnamed. The two YouTube shorts "Vixal.app is crazy/insane" were posted by the Griffin Buehler channel (whether he is the founder is unverified).
  • Social and platforms: Instagram @vixal.app 184 followers, 0 posts; Whop store 596 members; homepage claims "39,000+ creators" (the July review cited 1,150+ creators and a 4.8 rating).
  • Domain: registered 2026-06-22 (RDAP); Wayback has a single snapshot from 2026-08-10.
  • Traffic (profile): July 14,218 → August 8,485 → September 5,037; US 50.9%, Indonesia 12.2%, UK 11.2%; search 44.9% (brand keywords 86.2%), direct 41.3%; no visible non-brand search, referral or social sources.

Where the first users came from: no Product Hunt, Reddit or press launch on record. Evidence points to the faceless-YouTube-income circle: the product copy, quiz-funnel questions and Trustpilot blurb ("built for creators who want to earn from faceless YouTube channels") all target this group; the review article says searches spiked in July. Which specific ad/video lit the fuse was not found; a Trustpilot user mentions the product being "based of ads".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • "Faceless income" narrative → quiz funnel → no-card trial → subscription (/start page).
  • Talking-head shorts "Vixal.app is crazy/insane" (the title is the brand name, prompting searches); brand keywords are 86.2% of search.

Speculation:

  • The main driver is Meta/TikTok paid ads (the quiz funnel is a typical ad landing-page format; Trustpilot "based of ads"); once ads stopped or decayed, traffic fell 65% from July to September.
  • User-generated Shorts carry no watermark or brand, so the product itself has no viral mechanism.
  • The 12% Indonesian traffic may come from local faceless-income tutorial circles.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Homepage claims 39,000+ creators (mostly trial sign-ups); Whop 596 members; 7 Trustpilot reviews.
  • Subscriptions $19–89/month; with a few hundred paying users, monthly revenue would be on the order of $10k (pure speculation, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Company info comes from the Trustpilot company page, YouTube video dates and view counts from page metadata, prices from the July review; credibility medium. The "paid ads" call rests mainly on the funnel shape and user reviews, so it is an inference.
  • All three search groups ("launch", "alternative", "tiktok") returned nothing relevant.
  • Gaps: Ethereal Media's background and founder; actual Meta Ad Library records; whether Griffin Buehler is a team member; the specific source of the July peak.
