Basics

  • Product: AI problem solving for math (plus physics, chemistry, accounting, statistics), accepting text/photo/PDF; step-by-step explanations, AI-generated animated explanation videos with voiceover, practice problems, quizzes, graphing; iOS/Android app (PocketMath). A few free uses per day, unlimited with a subscription.
  • Founders: Nour Gajial and Yanni Kouloumbis, both Cornell Engineering CS undergrads, class of 2026 (the Show HN poster yannigk is Yanni). They were working on LLM-related projects before ChatGPT.
  • Support: Cornell Life Changing Labs, Beck Fellowship, Canaan Fellowship, Blackstone LaunchPad at Cornell. Funding amount not found.
  • Note: mathgpt.ai (a separate classroom-focused company) and mathgpt.today are not the same company.

First 1,000 users: manual recommendations in math Discord servers (Evidence) + the founder's TikTok (Evidence). New users afterwards come mainly from "math gpt" brand-keyword search (Similarweb brand keywords 85.8%) and the app.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: founder's on-camera TikTok demos → students share (60K+ shares); recommendations in student Discord groups; generated explanation videos have shareable links; calculator hub pages, multi-subject sub-tools and the blog build SEO; Similarweb shows social traffic is mainly YouTube, Discord and Reddit.
  • Speculation: word of mouth among students during homework season; demand for "ChatGPT for math" spills over to the exact-match domain "math gpt"; paid small-creator recommendations.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2024-07: 385K registered accounts, 3.5M+ problems; 2024-11: 2M+ users, 800M tokens per day; the 2026 homepage still says 2M+.
  • Free daily quota + paid subscription; prices and revenue not found.
  • Our data: 17,192 checkout visits in September, April–June average 20,083, summer dip in July–August (12,741 / 9,217), a classic school-term curve.
