Overview

Math-gpt (math-gpt.org): AI math solver and homework helper with step-by-step solutions, video explanations and practice quizzes. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Math-gpt to a checkout payment page — #193 of the 607 sites we track. That is 14% down from the April–June monthly average of 20,083.

Total website visits that month were about 784,166 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 2.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 64%, search 36%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 14%, with queries like “math solver”, “math ai”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
