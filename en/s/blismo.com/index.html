Overview

Blismo (blismo.com): AI booking and business management SaaS for barbershops, salons and spas. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Blismo to a checkout payment page — #268 of the 607 sites we track. That is 267% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,517.

Total website visits that month were about 23,564 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 54.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 47%, referrals/email/paid 37%, direct 10%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 47%, with queries like “barbershop providence”, “nelson barber shop sunnyside”, “hidden barber”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
