Lee Lewis, a digital forensics examiner in UK law enforcement, wrote scripts in his spare time in 2012 to automate online searches, in 2013 built a Python script that checks a username across 600+ sites, and in 2014 turned it into the free website usersearch.org, one of the earliest username lookup sites. It rode the long-tail search demand for "which sites/dating apps does this person have accounts on" for nearly a decade, earning mostly from Google ads. In 2020 he tested paid membership with usersearch.io, and in 2023-12 rewrote it as the paid OSINT platform usersearch.ai, pushing the old traffic into subscriptions with "Upgrade on UserSearch.ai" across every page of the free site, continually plugging in third-party data sources (OSINT Industries, Pipl, IntelX, etc.) and expanding toward law enforcement/security professionals.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: people/account OSINT search by username, email, phone number, image, crypto address, domain, court records and more, covering 3,000+ platforms; everyday users check on "a partner / someone met online", the professional tier targets law enforcement, security, journalism and anti-fraud. Also SargeBot, Forensic Capture, the OneQuery API and an affiliate program.
  • Founder: Lee Lewis (founder / director / original developer), formerly in digital forensics at several UK police forces, the National Crime Agency (NCA) and HMRC; usersearch.org was a side project from 2015 to 2022.
  • Team: the first dev team joined in 2023; Dheeraj Yadav became Head of Marketing in 2024-10; 7 roles hired at once in 2024-11; Mark Bentley joined as domain expert in 2026-06.
  • Location: Trustpilot lists Anguilla; the founder's background is in the UK, and he became a founding member of a UK OSINT association in 2024-05. No funding announcements.
  • Brand/domains: usersearch.org (free site, 2014) → usersearch.io (paid alpha, 2020-11) → usersearch.ai (Beta 1.0, 2023-12; login page titled "UserSearch 2.0") → usersearch.com (a 2003 domain, acquisition announced 2025-01, launched as the new homepage 2025-08-05; OSINT.wiki acquired at the same time).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • For nearly a decade the free site usersearch.org captured the "username search / dating site search" long tail, monetized with Google Ads; in 2021 it reported fast growth in US searches.
  • "Upgrade on UserSearch.ai" placed throughout the free site's result pages and popups, a freemium funnel; in 2024 the free username module was expanded to 3,000 sites to keep the hook strong.
  • Acquiring category-keyword domains like usersearch.com and OSINT.wiki, plus the free DorkSearch tool and a Reddit OSINT community, to occupy OSINT search and community entry points.
  • Brand keywords are 92.2% of usersearch.ai search traffic, direct 68.7% → the paid site's new users come almost entirely from its own free site and brand awareness; search terms include "cancel usersearch membership".
  • Professional side: joining a UK OSINT association, hiring via OSINT-Jobs, targeting the law enforcement/security circle.
  • Speculation:
  • "Check whether your partner is on dating apps" content spreads on TikTok/Reddit, bringing social traffic (4.7%). No user-brings-user step.

Revenue and scale clues

  • usersearch.org/.com claims 500K+ monthly active users and "15+ years of service".
  • In 2025-01 it reported year-over-year growth in paid and free subscriptions (no numbers); the team went from a one-person side project to continuous hiring in 2024 (7 roles), suggesting annual revenue can support a team of ten or more.
  • Our checkout visits: 17,192 in September. MRR and prices not found (usersearch.ai help page returns 403).
