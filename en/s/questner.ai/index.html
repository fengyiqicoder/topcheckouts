Basics

  • Product: a browser-based AI Game Master text adventure game; players can use the Game Designer AI to create and publish their own scenarios to the public library, which shows play counts and ratings; has memory, cards, images and multi-model switching. English only for now, no native app.
  • Company: Softimization Technology OÜ, Rotermanni tn 6, Tallinn, Estonia (https://questner.ai/about). The team calls itself a "small team", and Discord members include ViktorOfNorthmarch (Viktor) and Ola (Oksana). Funding: Not found.
  • Launch: WHOIS registered 2026-01-12; About says "We started building Questner in early 2026"; Wayback 2026-01-28 and 01-31 are 308/307 redirects, and from 2026-04-27 it is 200.
  • Scale: About says "More than 50,000 people play here now"; play counts of popular community scenarios: In Character 13,679, London Life: Realism 12,074, Custom Adventure 9,204, Concrete Jungle 8,319.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Creator supply: players can create and publicly publish scenarios, About says "most of the games on the platform are built by our amazing community"; the public leaderboard shows play counts and ratings.
  • Community operation: its own subreddit r/QuestnerAI, Discord and a Facebook group, where the founders reply in person.
  • SEO: comparison articles that intercept competitor terms like "AI Dungeon alternative".
  • Paid ads: in our channel data Paid Social at 6.4% ranks in the top three; a player says they tried it only after seeing an ad on Reddit (https://kitsonlow.dev/2026/05/08/i-tried-questner-ai-part-1/). Social sources include Facebook, YouTube and Reddit.
  • Speculation:
  • British street-themed scenarios (London Life, Concrete Jungle) spread easily on TikTok/Reels or get used as ad creative, attracting non-traditional RPG players.
  • Scenario authors post their scenario links in communities to bring people to play (creators bringing players); no clear revenue-share incentive was seen on the platform.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: $9.99 (€8.99)/month for 2,000 messages, $99.99/year for 24,000 messages (as captured in the old report); a 2026-10 competitor article says 3,000 messages/month and 36,000/year, with a $9.99 add-on pack of 3,000 messages; new users get 40 free messages + a 7-day trial (one trial per card); message packs can be bought as add-ons.
  • 50,000+ players (About self-report). Roughly estimated from our last month 73K checkout page visits, paying users are probably a few thousand, and MRR in the tens of thousands of dollars (pure estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • Company entity, launch date and pricing come from the site, credible.
  • Reddit pages still would not open, so the content of the r/Solo_Roleplaying thread was not seen; official TikTok/Instagram accounts exist but view data was not retrieved.
  • Second follow-up check: the takeoff method went from "Paid ads (low credibility)" to evidence-backed — a player's blog states they saw its ad on Reddit before 2026-05; added the Toolify/Tranco rankings and the 2026-10 plan adjustment. No new team information.
