Berlin startup Passionfroot (CEO Jen Phan) first put up a Typedream waitlist page in 2021, "Meet Passionfroot", pitching creators a personal "storefront": sponsorship rates, latest stats, booking and payments in one link, replacing media kits and email back-and-forth. In three months ago Insight Partners led a $15M Series A; revenue grew 13x over the past year, and at least $10M was paid out to creators over 18 months. Basics

  • Product: B2B brand x creator sponsorship platform. Creator side: storefront, order intake, scheduling, payments, reporting; brand side: creator gallery/matching, an AI agent "Zest" (give it a goal and it generates ICP, platforms, budget, copy and a creator shortlist), global payout wallet. workspace.passionfroot.me is the login dashboard.
  • Pricing: free for creators; 15% take on platform-brokered deals (payment fees included), brands pay an extra 2% per transaction (our profile, from the pricing page).
  • Company: Berlin, about 15 people, opening New York and Sao Paulo offices; CEO and co-founder Jen Phan relocated to New York. Other co-founders not named by TechCrunch; not found.
  • Funding: over $21M in total; a 2024 round (amount not found); 2026-07 Series A of $15M led by Insight Partners, with Creandum, Supernode Global and s16vc participating (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/passionfroot-raises-15m-to-expand-its-b2b-creator-marketplace-to-the-us/).
  • Domain: RDAP lookup for passionfroot.me returned nothing; passionfroot.xyz had a waitlist page in 2021-09; first Wayback capture of passionfroot.me is 2023-02; the workspace subdomain first appears 2023-03.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Storefront links carry the brand: creators post workspace.passionfroot.me/partners/... links on Instagram, in newsletters and elsewhere (search results include an Instagram reel with this link), and brands who click through must register on the platform.
  • Top-creator endorsements: the 2024 homepage lists Ali Abdaal, Superhuman, Milk Road and others as trust signals for other creators and brands.
  • Content marketing to attract creators: blog, podcast, weekly newsletter, free templates (2023 homepage).
  • Email re-engagement: deal requests and payment notifications pull users back to the dashboard (our data: email 15.1%, direct 79.9%; referrals include substack.com).
  • Founder-led brand sales: the CEO's booking link routes straight to brand signup.

Speculation:

  • The first batch of creators came from the founders' connections in the newsletter / creator-economy scene (e.g. indie creator communities like Arvid Kahl's); no launch post was found.
  • From 2024 the shift from "creator tool" to "brand matchmaking + commission" drove the revenue surge, mostly from AI/SaaS companies' B2B creator budgets.

Revenue and scale clues

  • At least $10M paid to creators over 18 months; at a 15% + 2% take, platform revenue over the same period would be on the order of $1.7M (estimate, unverified).
  • Revenue up 13x in one year (per the CEO).
  • Over $21M raised in total; 15 people.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Early product form comes from raw Wayback snapshots; funding and scale from TechCrunch, credible.
  • Most search results are unrelated to the site; no founder interview or launch post was found.
  • Gaps: other co-founders; the 2022 seed / 2024 round amounts; where the first creators specifically came from; creator and brand counts.
