Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Cook group channel: drop tutorial/intel groups write it into the must-have setup list for new members and hand out exclusive discount codes (the CCN Botting case).
  • Discord closed loop: logging in to the dashboard requires Discord; our data shows social 3.5% all from Discord, direct 77%, brand keywords 91% (proxy haus, haus proxies).
  • Resellers: the dashboard is positioned for "high-volume resellers", letting second-tier proxy sellers buy in bulk and resell to their own groups.
  • Discount code: HAUS30 for 30% off on the homepage.

Speculation:

  • The first users came from the founder's existing contacts in the drop community (other proxy sellers or cook groups), which is how a Discord of 6,500 people was built in 5 months; no founder or predecessor information was found.
  • The Pokémon card drop craze (Pokémon Center ranks near the top of the tuned-retailer list) is a tailwind for 2026 demand.
