Basics

  • Product: a viral video library / swipe file for TikTok Shop affiliate creators, screening well-selling videos and products every day so creators can 'copy and shoot'. Paid subscription.
  • The X account @thedailyvirals_ describes itself as 'the swipe file for TikTok Shop affiliates'; the pinned post claims 'one account earned $1 million in commission posting TikTok Shop videos' (self-reported, unverified).
  • Team / founder / country / funding: not found. The official site is blocked by Cloudflare, so the about and terms pages could not be read. The main market is the US.
  • Domain: RDAP registration date 2024-12-16. In 2016 and in 2023-2024 the domain was an unrelated news blog called 'DailyVirals' (the Wayback 2024-04 snapshot shows 2023 sports / tech news), which means the current team re-registered or took over the domain in 2024-12.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: a large number of TikTok videos promote it with creators' referral codes (search snippets mention 'use code X for one month free' and 'comment to get a free month'), i.e. it is driven by affiliate / referral commissions; some users comment that this style of promotion is 'scammy', which indirectly confirms how dense the promotion is.
  • Evidence (second-pass check): the #thedailyvirals hashtag has 1,004 posts; content like 'my video ranks #1 on Daily Virals' shows that the creators it features are natural spreaders (branded output: get featured, show off the ranking, followers go check it out).
  • Speculation: users = TikTok creators = natural promoters. A uses it to find viral products, shoots selling videos and earns commission, then shoots a 'what tools I use' video, and B signs up with A's code. 'Selling creators recommending selling tools' is a closed loop, and it overlaps completely with Daily Virals' own content theme.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: no specific figure found (official site blocked).
  • Monthly checkout visits are about 30-40K, among the top tier of TikTok Shop creator tools.
  • No public MRR or user count found.
