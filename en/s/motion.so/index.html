Basics

  • Product: Motion, a video Agent from Mosaic. Give it assets, text, a reference video, a product link or an X post, and it plans the storyboard, generates each shot, adds music and cuts the finished video (launch videos, explainers, logo animations, overlay motion graphics, etc.). It can edit parts without redoing everything; it has an API and MCP, and can export to Premiere, After Effects, DaVinci and Remotion.
  • Company: Mosaic AI Labs, Inc., calls itself an "Applied AI Product Lab", YC W25; announced a $4.3M raise on 2026-04-07 (YC, Mayfield, Elevation Capital, News Corp). Source: https://mosaic.so/; https://www.explainx.ai/blog/motion-so-url-launch-video-agent-august-2026
  • Team: Product Hunt shows founder Adish Jain wrote the first comment, Shiv Trivedi is a maker, and Garry Tan is on the launch team; the explainx article calls the CEO "Jay". No other background found.
  • Pricing: Flex $5-6 one-time for 200 credits; Pro $29/month (1,000-1,250 credits); Max $99/month (5,000 credits); Studio/Enterprise custom (premium launch videos with a human director). Source: profile file; https://nicobabierra.com/blog/motion-review
  • Domain: in 2020 motion.so was a parked page (redirecting to ww12.motion.so), the 2025 snapshot is an empty page, and by the 2026-04-01 snapshot it is already Mosaic's Motion ("Skip the timeline, create graphics instantly"). Unrelated to the calendar app Motion (usemotion.com).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: the launch video and the "Made with Motion" showcase are all made by Motion itself; launch videos customers make with Motion get posted on X/LinkedIn and double as demos.
  • YC circle: Motion Studio made launch videos for 20+ YC companies; Garry Tan was on the PH launch team and publicly praised Mosaic.
  • Credits for reposts: at GA, a LinkedIn "repost + comment MOTION for 1,000 credits" campaign.
  • Affiliate commission: 25% on Pro/Max subscriptions for 6 months, 90-day cookie, paid ads prohibited; an editor has already written an affiliate review.
  • Agent ecosystem distribution: API + MCP, pitched for plugging into Claude and Codex; search terms include "motion mcp".
  • Traffic: direct 47.3%, search 39.5% (86.8% brand keywords), social 7.9% (X, YouTube, Instagram); United States 16.7%, India 12.7%, France 7.2%.

Speculation:

  • The first users were mainly YC companies and the startup circle on X, who happen to need product launch videos; this is a migration of Mosaic's existing audience (evidence: the number of YC clients and Garry Tan's endorsement, but no first-1,000-users account).
  • The Mosaic site's "100K+ users" may include users of the video editing product that preceded Motion (unconfirmed).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Raised $4.3M (2026-04).
  • The Mosaic site says "Trusted by 100,000+ users".
  • @motion_so has about 13,979 followers (2026-10); single posts get 3k-12k views.
  • Revenue not found; pricing runs from $5 one-time to $99/month.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The product, funding and timeline come from the Mosaic site, X posts (read through the fxtwitter API), the Product Hunt page and third-party blogs, so they are fairly reliable.
  • Gaps: the exact Product Hunt launch date (the page shows both "5 months ago" and a link to the 2026-10-24 leaderboard, so there may have been two launches), the founders' backgrounds, the specific source of the first users, and revenue figures were not found.
