Basics

  • Product: free YouTube subtitle/transcript extraction, translatable into 100+ languages, 6 download formats; YouTube summaries, subtitle download, podcast transcription, API (/api, /get-api-key) and MCP service (About page says it is listed in the official MCP registry). Interface in 11 languages (English/Simplified Chinese/Traditional Chinese/Japanese/Korean/Spanish/French/Portuguese/Vietnamese/Thai/Arabic).
  • Team: About page only says "a small independent team", unsigned. FAQ states it is "the same team" as SubKun (subkun.com, a YouTube real-time bilingual subtitles + AI summary extension).
  • Developer clues: the Chrome extension promoted on the homepage, "YouTube Transcript, Summary, AI Chatbot - SubKun", lists store developer XiaoLong Zhang, email will@itoytoy.com, address in Nanaimo, BC, Canada. Presumably a Chinese indie developer (inferred from the name).
  • Domain: youtube-transcript.ai registered 2025-07-19 (Cloudflare registrar, details hidden); subkun.com registered 2026-02-19 (this domain had an old archive from 2007, so it is a re-registration).
  • Pricing: the site itself is "completely free, no sign-up, no limits"; the footer has "Manage subscription"; monetization is via SubKun Pro (unlimited use, transcription of videos without subtitles, downloads) and the API.
  • Our data: countries US 18.57%, India 11.23%, France 7.91%; channels search 49.2%, direct 38.1%, AI assistants 7.6%; search terms are youtube transcript, youtube transcript generator and the French transcription video youtube en texte gratuit.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: search is 49.2% in our channel data, and the search terms are the generic head terms youtube transcript / generator plus French long-tail; exact-match domain + 11-language hreflang + a batch of "youtube transcript xxx" long-tail landing pages (visible in the sitemap).
  • AI assistant referrals 7.6% (our data); the site built a dedicated MCP service and API page, and the About page says the MCP is in the official registry.
  • Site → extension funnel: homepage and FAQ push the SubKun Chrome extension, with links tagged utm_source=youtube-transcript&utm_campaign=hero; the extension has 7,000 users.
  • Occasional spontaneous creator recommendations: the Ethiopian creator's video brought 229 sessions (per the team's own blog).
  • Search results show this space is extremely crowded (yttranscript.ai, transcriptmagic, notegpt, smry, wave.co and dozens of similar free tools); no launch post, Product Hunt or Reddit promotion post was seen.

Speculation:

  • The first users most likely came directly from Google/Bing searches for "youtube transcript": exact-match.ai domain + free with no sign-up, starting to rank a few months after launch. No direct evidence.
  • Direct traffic at 38.1% is high; presumably students and creators coming back repeatedly (bookmarks) plus redirects from the extension.
  • r/YouTubeTranscript is a subreddit the team created itself (footer link), possibly to occupy Reddit search results; size not found (Reddit could not be opened).

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site itself is free; revenue comes from SubKun Pro subscriptions and the API, amounts not found.
  • SubKun extension: 7,000 users, 298 ratings (2026-08). About page claims "1,818 real user reviews, 4.7/5".
  • That 229 sessions in 4 days from a creator was treated as worth a blog post suggests the site's overall traffic in 2026 is still small (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch timing (registered 2025-07, first capture 2025-10), the same-team relationship, extension developer details and our channel data all have first-hand sources; credible.
  • The given search results are almost all similar competitors, with no coverage, interviews or launch posts about this site; the growth path is inferred mainly from the website, sitemap, RDAP, Chrome Web Store and our channel data.
  • Gaps: whether the founder is XiaoLong Zhang (can only confirm he is the SubKun extension developer), the specific source of the first users, revenue, and the size of r/YouTubeTranscript (neither Reddit nor the Wayback body could be opened).
