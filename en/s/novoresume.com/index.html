Overview

Novoresume (novoresume.com): Online resume builder with ATS-friendly templates, AI writing help and paid Pro upgrade. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Novoresume to a checkout payment page — #388 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 247,082 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 3.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 60%, search 36%, AI assistants 4%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 7%, with queries like “flowcv”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
