A few Dutch founders (co-founder Koen previously ran Google Ads for clients' Shopify stores, CEO Glenn has built several SaaS products, technical partner Lyubomir) could not work out which ad spend was actually profitable when running their own Google Ads stores. They registered the domain in 2024-03 with a "launching soon" email capture page, built for almost two years, and on 2026-01-10 announced "Pythago is now live" on Instagram, aimed specifically at "dropshippers who use Google Ads" to see true ROAS and profit per product. The first users came from acquaintances in the Dutch dropshipping scene (people who commented on the launch post later left positive Trustpilot reviews); after that Koen worked full-time on agencies, communities, influencers and e-commerce coaches, funnelling traffic through coaches/YouTube/WhatsApp groups with referral links carrying a?red= parameter (e.g. terryecom); 78% of traffic is in the Netherlands.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: connects Shopify + Google Ads to calculate true profit after costs/fees/ad spend and 7/14/30-day ROAS by product, country and campaign, with the option to delist losing products directly; also product research and competitor monitoring.
  • Founders (https://pythago.io/about): Koen (co-founder, in charge of partnerships: agencies, communities, influencers, coaches, also runs product and support; previously ran Google Ads for clients' Shopify stores), Glenn (CEO, has built several SaaS products), Lyubomir (technical partner). First names only, no surnames. Support WhatsApp is a +31 Dutch number.
  • Pricing: 1 store $59/month; 2 stores $49/store/month; 3+ stores $39/store/month; no free tier, no explicit trial.
  • Domain: registered 2024-03-03 (whois); first Wayback snapshot 2024-04-16 ("Launching Soon" email capture page).
  • Traffic (profile): Netherlands 78%, United States 15%, United Kingdom 5%; direct 83%, social 12.5% (YouTube, WhatsApp), search 2.5% all brand keywords. Instagram @pythago.io about 3,700 followers, only 9 posts.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Partnership distribution: the about page states that one co-founder works full-time on agencies, communities, influencers and coaches; the site has?red= referral parameters (terryecom etc.).
  • Social traffic 12.5% from YouTube and WhatsApp (profile), consistent with how Dutch e-commerce coaches distribute via YouTube + WhatsApp groups.

Speculation:

  • The Dutch dropshipping coach scene is very concentrated; one coach's recommendation can bring a batch of students; referral codes may carry commissions.
  • Tiered multi-store pricing encourages agencies to connect all their clients' stores.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue/user figures. Only 5 Trustpilot reviews, 9 Instagram posts.
  • At $39–59/store/month, scale is likely tens to a few hundred stores (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New entry, written from scratch. Domain date, placeholder page, official launch date, founder roles and referral codes are backed by whois/Wayback/Instagram/site evidence.
  • Gaps: founders' full names, who terryecom is, and paying user count all not found; search results were mostly irrelevant (Python etc.).
