Overview

Pythago (pythago.io): Google Ads profit-tracking SaaS showing real profit per product/campaign for Shopify stores after COGS, fees and ad spen. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Pythago to a checkout payment page — #446 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 6,309 (estimated from global rank). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 83%, social 12%, search 2%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from social platforms: organic social is about 12%, led by YouTube › WhatsApp Webapp.

Pricing: $59/mo (1 store); $49/store/mo (2 stores); $39/store/mo (3+ stores).
