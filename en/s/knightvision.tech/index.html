Basics

  • Product: AI ad studio - UGC video/product video generation, ad images, competitor ad analysis (paste a Facebook Ad Library/TikTok link to get an A-F score, hook breakdown and a UGC script), subtitles, upscaling, API. The main site has moved to knightvision.ai.
  • Price: Pro $39.99/month (3,000 credits), 15% off for 3 months, 25% off for 6 months (profile); payments through Whop.
  • Founder: Alexander Knight (TrustMRR); YouTube channel name "Alex Knight", with Instagram knightmediaaa in the video descriptions. Unrelated to the marketing firm of the same name knightvision.co (Julia Knight), the Canadian video company knightvision.ca, or the chess-cheating app KnightVision that shut down in 2023.
  • Users: e-commerce brands, agencies, media buyers; traffic is Germany 67% (unusually high, possibly brought by some German community/agency, cause not found) and US 15%.

Where the first users came from: the founder's own short-video audience - TikTok @knightmediaa (30.8K followers, marketing/AI/SaaS content), Instagram, and YouTube tutorials; the content is "how to make UGC ads with AI", with his own tool pushed along the way. On X he says "one post did pretty well", and he is active in the Whop seller community (summary only).
Where new users come from: still mainly the founder's content (brand keyword search 86%, direct 44%) + exposure on revenue leaderboards like TrustMRR + SEO landing pages (lots of "AI TikTok video ads" and "AI product video generator" pages on knightvision.ai) + API integrations.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: founder tutorial videos → free trial → credit subscription; public revenue (TrustMRR) brings exposure; many feature/scenario SEO pages; API docs aimed at AI coding users.
  • Speculation: affiliate/referral volume inside the Whop ecosystem (it collects payments through Whop, and Whop sellers often give promoters a cut); the high German traffic may come from some German-language e-commerce / media-buying community; 30-day revenue of $35K is far above MRR of $8K, indicating a lot is one-time credit packs or Whop channel revenue.

Revenue and scale clues

  • TrustMRR (verified via the Whop API): cumulative $164K, last 30 days $35K, MRR $8,055, 202 subscriptions, about 326 users. Build Radar estimates monthly revenue of $20K+ with a 70% margin (third-party estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • Revenue and founding time come from TrustMRR verified data, so credibility is high; the founder's identity is matched via the YouTube channel name + the TrustMRR name, credibility medium-high; the original X post could not be opened and the content of the "viral post" is unknown; the reason for the high share of German traffic was not found.
