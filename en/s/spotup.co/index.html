Overview

Spotup (spotup.co): Sells a one-time A$30 key that upgrades an existing Spotify account to Premium with guided setup. Category on TopCheckouts: Consumer services.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Spotup to a checkout payment page — #237 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 7,382 (estimated from global rank). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 63%, search 28%, social 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from social platforms: organic social is about 6%.
