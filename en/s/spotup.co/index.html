Basics

  • Product: Spotify Premium "lifetime" activation codes (SPTU-xxxx-xxxx), upgrading on the user's original account by following a guide while keeping playlists; "lifetime" is limited to "replacements provided while SpotUp remains in operation", with the terms stating further conditions; moving to a new account is required when necessary.
  • Pricing: from A$30 one-time; the page compares "Spotify Individual A$15.99/month, price verified 2026-09".
  • Operator: company name not public; Trustpilot lists the US, software sales; after-sales via Discord tickets.
  • Back-office structure (visible in front-end code): admin has customers/orders/acquisition/discounts/referrals/inventory/stock-queue/creators/resellers modules, plus a /reseller dealer panel (can manage customers and activation codes on their behalf) and a Partners page, indicating three channels: referral rebates, creator promotion and resale. Source: the front-end JS bundle of https://spotup.co/.
  • Traffic (profile record): direct 62.6%, search 28.0% (brand keywords 90.6%), social 5.6%; Australia 96.1%, UK 3.9%.
  • Domain: WHOIS created 2026-01-31 (GoDaddy; Wayback in 2017 and 2021 shows traces of a previous owner, unrelated to the current site); Wayback has a snapshot of the current site's JS resources on 2026-09-12.
  • Note: Instagram @spotup.co ("SPOTUP || STARTUP || BUSINESS", 50 followers) looks like a same-named startup content account; it is unclear whether it is the same entity, so it was not relied on.

First users: the only direct evidence is a Trustpilot review mentioning "saw it on TikTok"; combined with Australia 96% + brand terms 90% + direct visits 63%, the first users probably came from "money-saving tips" videos on Australian TikTok (possibly creators paid or on rebates from the site), who then searched the brand name directly to buy.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The back office has referrals, creators, resellers, discounts and acquisition (attribution) modules (front-end code).
  • TikTok brought at least some users (Trustpilot review).
  • Discord serves as the after-sales community, replacing failed codes (merchant replies on Trustpilot).

Speculation:

  • Creators/referred users earn a rebate by posting short videos of "get Spotify Premium with one payment", and viewers privately forward them to friends; money-saving topics naturally suit word of mouth, but as a grey-market product it cannot run regular ads and can only rely on rebate-based distribution.
  • Australia-only may be because some regional loophole / family plan resale channel is only available in that region (Speculation).

Revenue and scale clues

  • last month checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate); at 20–30% closing and A$30 per unit, monthly revenue would be about A$80,000–120,000 (Speculation, the upper bound is optimistic).
  • 22 Trustpilot reviews, not a large scale.
  • Risk: depends on Spotify not banning accounts; negative reviews already mention "Premium taken back" and "oversold stock", so the business's sustainability is in doubt.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The product, price and launch timing (mid-2026) are well evidenced; the operating entity is unclear.
  • Gaps: specific TikTok videos and view counts Not found (no relevant entries in search results); the rebate rate is unknown (the Partners page content comes through an API and wasn't rendered); Reddit search results were useless.
