Overview

Juicysms (juicysms.com): Pay-per-use temporary phone number rental for receiving SMS verification codes, with API. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Juicysms to a checkout payment page — #182 of the 607 sites we track. That is 119% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 54%, search 44%, referrals 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)
