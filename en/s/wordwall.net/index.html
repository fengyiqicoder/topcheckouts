Overview

Wordwall (wordwall.net): Web tool for teachers to build interactive classroom games and printable activities. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 34,384 visits went from Wordwall to a checkout payment page — #69 of the 607 sites we track. That is 124% up from the April–June monthly average of 15,317.

Total website visits that month were about 17,782,408 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 53%, search 47%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 81%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
