Overview

Compilatio (compilatio.net): French SaaS suite for plagiarism detection, AI-text detection and AI-assisted grading for schools and students. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 34,384 visits went from Compilatio to a checkout payment page — #76 of the 607 sites we track. That is 34% down from the April–June monthly average of 51,967.
