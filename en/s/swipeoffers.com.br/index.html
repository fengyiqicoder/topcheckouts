Overview

Swipeoffers.com (swipeoffers.com.br): Portuguese subscription swipe-file database of validated ad offers, hooks, copy and creatives with spy extension. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 9,199 visits went from Swipeoffers.com to a checkout payment page — #333 of the 607 sites we track. That is 54% down from the April–June monthly average of 20,158.

Total website visits that month were about 10,373 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: direct 81%, search 14%, social 3%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, and direct plus brand search is about 95%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
