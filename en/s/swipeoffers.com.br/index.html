Basics

  • Product: a Portuguese ad swipe library, updated Monday to Saturday with about 14 "verified" offers a day (creatives, transcribed VSLs, funnels), filterable by niche and funnel type, with saved lists and team sharing; claims it can "crack any cloaker"; the Chrome extension Swipe Offers Spy analyzes the Facebook Ad Library and transcribes videos.
  • Pricing: free tier (one offer a day, affiliate program open); Swipe Offers R$97/month; Swipe + SPY R$147/month; the "Dia D" promotion has an annual fee of R$697 paid at once or 12 x R$72 (original price 12 x R$147 = R$1,764/year); 7-day refund (14 days on the promotion page).
  • Team: domain registrant Henrique Trevizan, technical contact "Swipe Offers Ltda"; blog posts are signed Henrique Trevizan, who calls himself the founder of Swipe Offers and says he "reached seven figures in revenue".
  • Scale: Chrome extension 1,000 users, 8 ratings at 5.0 (v1.6.8, updated 2026-07-09); Instagram @swipe.offers about 6,400 followers (verified); Facebook page 39 followers.
  • Traffic (our records): direct 81%, search 14.2% (brand keywords 95.3%: swipe offers, swipe offers login), social 2.8%; referrals almost entirely from netlify.app (13.2%, presumably the member area or landing pages hosted on Netlify). Brazil 99.3%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Affiliate commissions: all three price tiers (including the free tier) say "affiliate program open", so users can sign up and promote for commissions; the commission rate is not stated on the page.
  • Launch events: a WhatsApp VIP group + a one-day annual-plan discount (Dia D) + guest livestreams capturing WhatsApp leads is the standard launch playbook of the Brazilian infoproduct scene, turning monthly payers into annual payers.
  • The product ships "new stock every day": 14 new offers a day, so users form a habit of logging in daily, with direct visits at 81%.
  • From 2026-09 it added an SEO blog and began working on non-brand search.

Speculation:

  • The first users came from the founder's contacts in Brazil's direct-response scene (copywriting, media-buyer communities, WhatsApp groups); recommendations inside the circle + affiliate commissions are the main way of acquiring users, hence brand keywords at 95%.
  • The 13% netlify.app referrals may be a member back-end or promotional landing pages that send users back to the main site.
  • There are many similar competitors (MineraROI's Minera Ads, Copycat Ads, etc.), and Swipe Offers differentiates through "human curation + cracking cloakers".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Extension 1,000 users (only part of the SPY-tier users); Instagram 6,400 followers.
  • If there are 500-1,500 paying users x about R$100/month, monthly revenue would be about R$50K-R$150K (pure speculation).
  • The founder claims "seven-figure revenue" (the sum of his personal businesses, not necessarily this product).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there was no old report in the notes. Search results were almost all unrelated (spy extension roundups, TikTok scam articles); the work relied mainly on the registro.br RDAP, Wayback, the site's sitemap and WordPress API, the Chrome store and Instagram for evidence.
  • The founder's identity (RDAP + blog byline) is credible; the launch month can only be inferred from the domain registration (2025-06) and the image path (2025-09).
  • Gaps: paying-user count, affiliate commission rate, which community the first users came from, what page the netlify.app referrals actually are.
