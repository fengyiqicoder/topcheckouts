Built in 2022 by two Italians based in London as a "Fantacalcio (Italian fantasy football) auction assistant". The core is a **free online league auction room**: an admin sends one link and the whole league of 8-12 people has to join, so every single auction pulls an entire friend group into the product. On top of that it partners with Fantacalcio media and influencers such as SOS Fanta, Il Profeta and Carmyspecial (who put their "tier tables and recommended prices" into FantaLab and sell with discount codes). Growth bursts every year in the 1–2 months ago Serie A auction season, and payment is a Premium/Ultra subscription.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a Fantacalcio auction preparation and execution tool (web plus app). Features include an online auction room (invite by link, timer, Classic and Mantra rules, live view of opponents' squads), solo mode (Premium), influencer tiers and recommended prices, Italy-wide average auction prices, and various stats. New for the 2026/27 season: built-in video calls and recording, auction highlight videos, Magic Asta (a 20-minute draft with strangers), Ninja mode, auction rankings (Ultra), reactive prices. Also a FantaLab Manager management game (over 120K signups last season).
  • Company: FANTALAB LTD, UK Companies House number 14175206, founded 2022-06-15. Directors are Federico Allegro (CEO, Italian, Techstars London 2021 cohort) and Luca Soldera (CTO, Italian, engineering background from the University of Padua). Headquartered in London, RocketReach estimates about 11 people. Funding Not found; the only thing confirmed is that Allegro took part in Techstars London 2021 (the project then was Deary, not FantaLab).
  • Note: it only shares a name with "Fantalab srls" (the FantaMaster team) founded in 2017; there is no evidence they are the same company.
  • Domain: fantalab.it registered on 2022-05-14. The 2018 Wayback snapshots belong to a previous holder.
  • App Store: iOS app id6450352620, listed 2023-06-28, 4356 ratings, average 4.64 (Italy store, 2026-10).

Where the first 1,000 users came from: no direct evidence found. Speculation: the 2022/23 season (Aug-Sep 2022), through leagues in the founders' circle, plus the spread of auction-room links where "one league uses it and the whole league joins". After that, volume came from partnerships with Fantacalcio YouTube and media creators (YouTube has many videos like "FantaLab Tutorial / La Guida completa a Fantalab 24-25").

Growth loop

Evidence

  • Auction room link: an admin creates an auction for free and sends the link to the league's WhatsApp or Telegram group, and all teammates click the link to join (SOS Fanta 2025-09-04). This matches WhatsApp and Telegram ranking high among the site's social channels. One auction is 8-12 new users.
  • The paywall sits on the admin: video calls and highlights "only need the league admin to have Premium" (SOS Fanta 2026-08-26), so one person pays and the whole league benefits, which also makes it easier to win the admin of the next league.
  • Auction highlight videos and recording downloads: output that can be shared as video content (new in 2026, spread effect still to be verified).
  • Creator distribution: SOS Fanta, Il Profeta (115K YouTube subscribers) and Carmyspecial (89K IG followers) put their tier tables on FantaLab and drive traffic with discount codes (SOS Fanta, Vermilion).
  • Media endorsement: the line "the most used auction tool in Italy" is repeated by SOS Fanta every year.

Speculation

  • Data such as Italy-wide average auction prices and auction rankings become more accurate as user numbers grow, forming a data network effect.
  • Magic Asta, which sets up games with strangers, is used to recruit people who do not have a fixed league.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Model: online auctions are free; Premium (solo mode, video calls, highlights and more) and Ultra (auction rankings and more) are paid, with specific prices Not found (the site needs JS rendering to be seen).
  • Scale: claims about 100K players (2026-08), Manager had over 120K signups last season, 4356 iOS ratings.
  • MRR: Not found.

Confidence and gaps
