Freebiesbug is a WordPress blog opened in September 2012 by Italian designer Pasquale Vitiello, who hand-picked one "free PSD / UI kit / mockup / font / HTML template" every day and wrote a post for it, catching the 2012–2015 wave of Dribbble and free PSD resources. Each asset gets its own keyword-rich page ("xxx free PSD mockup"), and over a dozen years the site has accumulated 2,180+ posts, capturing long-tail searches like "free fonts, free mockup, figma ui kit" (non-brand search is still the biggest source today at 42.6%); in April 2016 it hit #1 of the day on Product Hunt (385 votes). Monetization has always been light: a single BuySellAds slot plus private sponsorship, and it funnels traffic to the author's own sister sites such as Cruip (Tailwind templates), SaaS Landing Page and Magdeleine.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a free design-resource directory/blog, with categories for fonts, PSD, Figma, Sketch, HTML templates, icons, mockups, free stock photos and landing page inspiration; 219 listing pages and 2,180+ resources; the assets are mostly third-party designers' work, and the site only curates and links/hosts.
  • Who runs it: the maker on the Product Hunt product page is Pasquale Vitiello (the Launch Team also includes Kevin William David); the site itself is not signed. The home-page sponsor slot is Cruip (Tailwind CSS templates, link carries?ref=fbbg), and the footer promotes SaaS Landing Page ("A project by Davide Pacilio") and Magdeleine. Cruip was co-founded by Pasquale Vitiello and Davide Pacilio (from memory, not verified).
  • Launch timing: domain registered 2012-09-03; first Wayback snapshot 2012-09-15, titled "Freebiesbug – Freebies Alerts for Web Designers", already with several PSDs posted that day (login form, Facebook page mockup, tooltip, Instagram UI) and a "Report a freebie" submission entry.
  • Monetization: the /advertise page offers a BuySellAds 290×218 banner (shown site-wide, fixed price) and private sponsorship; on PH the author says he "sells 2 to 3 ad slots". There is no paid tier for users.
  • Traffic (profile file): non-brand search 42.6% (free fonts, open sans round, emojis pack ios 15 figma), direct 35%, social 7% (Pinterest, YouTube); US 10%, India 7.9%, Brazil 5.3%. A third-party estimator claims 3.3M monthly visits (updowntoday, low credibility).

Where the first users came from: there is no author interview. From the first version's shape, they were designers in the 2012 Dribbble/PSD free-resource circle: the site reposted free PSDs published by designers on Dribbble and elsewhere with credit, and featured designers and "report a freebie" submitters would reshare; plus Twitter @freebiesbug, Facebook, Pinterest and a Feedburner RSS subscription. The exact number of first users and when volume began were not found; the takeoff year of 2013 is inferred from the PH comment.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • One page per asset; long-tail keyword pages accumulated to 2,180+, and non-brand search is the top source.
  • The submission entry (Report a freebie) lets designers send in content on their own; featured authors have a motive to reshare.
  • Being #1 of the day on Product Hunt in 2016 brought a wave of exposure.
  • Distribution through Pinterest, Twitter, Facebook and RSS.

Speculation:

  • Designers cite Freebiesbug links as the "asset source" in tutorials and roundup articles, creating backlinks that further consolidate search rankings (the profile file shows referrals from sakshamux.com, grafmag.pl, etc.).
  • Sister sites funnel traffic to each other (Freebiesbug → Cruip / SaaS Landing Page / Magdeleine), turning free traffic into Cruip template sales.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ad slots + sponsorship; the author says 2–3 ad slots; specific revenue was not found.
  • What matters more to the author may be funneling traffic to Cruip's paid templates (speculation).
  • Traffic scale: a third-party estimate is millions of monthly visits (low credibility); the profile file shows it is still mainly search-driven.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. The only useful search results were the Product Hunt page and WHOIS; the rest comes from the first Wayback snapshot, the home-page source (sponsor/sister-site links), the /advertise page and the SaaS Landing Page footer.
  • There is no founder interview; the 2012–2015 growth process, revenue and team were not found; the link to Cruip's founders is marked as from memory and unverified.
