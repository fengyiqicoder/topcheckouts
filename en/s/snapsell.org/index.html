A small company in Baar, Switzerland (early on signed as PUBLIbusiness24 AG, with the app developer being FABS Media GmbH) launched an app in 2024-06 that lets you "upload photos/videos/PDFs → generate a paid link → fans pay by card and download", stating from the start that it is for "selling to your fans on Telegram and X". It needs no store and has no platform-rule review, so creators in the German-speaking world selling content on X/Telegram (a considerable share of them adult content creators) use it to post snapsell.org/buy/xxx links. Basics

  • Product: creator monetization app (iOS/Android + web app app.snapsell.org). Upload content, set a price and generate a snapsell.org/buy/<id> link; buyers pay with a bank card/Apple Pay and download directly without installing an app; digital KYC; payouts on the 1st and 15th of each month; free with no monthly fee, commission rate not found.
  • Company: in 2024 the site footer read "PUBLIbusiness24 AG, Rosenweg 3, 6340 Baar, Switzerland"; App Store developer FABS Media GmbH; a search snippet says "Operated by FABS Media GmbH, Wollerau, Switzerland". Founder not found.
  • Launch: snapsell.org was currently registered 2024-05-10 (RDAP; the 2017-2019 Wayback snapshots belong to a previous domain holder); iOS app listed 2024-06-02 with 91 ratings, 4.5 stars, rated 17+ (App Store lookup API).
  • Traffic: Germany 86%, US 14%; direct 70.6%, social 22% (Telegram Webapp, X), referral 4.4%; almost no non-brand search.

Where the first users came from: judging by positioning and channels, they were individual creators in the German-speaking world who already had followers on Telegram and X and needed a simple way to get paid for pictures/videos (a Trustpilot 5-star reviewer says they sell content across platforms for "models"). No founder statement or launch post found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The seller link is the ad: every piece of content generates a snapsell.org/buy/ link, which sellers post on X and Telegram (22% social in the traffic profile comes from Telegram Webapp and X).
  • Zero barrier for buyers: no app install, no registration, pay by card and download, lowering the friction of link conversion.

Speculation:

  • Friendlier to categories restricted on mainstream platforms such as adult content, with word of mouth spreading among those sellers (reviews like "GAMECHANGER APP").
  • Buyers who see "selling content is this easy" turn into sellers, forming a branded-output loop.
  • Negative reviews about payout delays (2025) may have limited word of mouth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Commission rate not found; iOS has 91 ratings; Trustpilot has 22 reviews (3.1 rating, 36% one-star, mainly complaining about payout delays).
  • 86% of traffic comes from Germany.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Launch date, legal entity and early positioning have RDAP, App Store and Wayback evidence.
  • The live site was blocked by a CloudFront 403, so the How it works page could not be read; X links in the search results were seen only as snippets.
  • Gaps: commission, number of sellers, GMV, founder, and whether referral rewards were ever offered were all not found; the "share of adult content" is only inferred from X accounts in search snippets.
