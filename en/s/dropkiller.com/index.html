A Spanish-language "Dropi product-research tool" built in 2024 by a team in Medellín, Colombia (DropKiller SAS; the publicly visible partner is Juan Vargas). Dropi is a cash-on-delivery dropshipping platform in Latin America; Dropkiller scrapes sales/inventory from Dropi's country catalogs to tell sellers "what's selling," and uses a free Chrome extension (which overlays data directly on Dropi, Shopify stores and the Meta Ad Library) as the entry point. The founder says "not a single customer in the first 4 months, and we lost $30,000 over 3 months selling to the wrong audience"; they changed the sales pitch and took off thanks to a "from $0 to $5K" video. After that they used affiliate commissions (FirstPromoter referral links + lifetime discount codes) to get Latin American dropshipping educators/YouTubers promoting it, backed by a free "Dropkiller University" course, Discord, and Whop bundles. TrustMRR: MRR $60,179 in 2026-08, 2,702 paid subscriptions, $452k cumulative revenue.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: Spanish-language e-commerce/dropshipping product research and ad intelligence: product sales and inventory tracking on Dropi/Aliclick and similar platforms, TikTok/Meta ad spying (AdKiller), Shopify store analytics (Store Radar), AI store builder, MCP interface; the free Chrome extension has about 20k users and 4.6 stars (saasdistribution data).
  • Who built it: DropKiller SAS (Medellín); one co-founder is Juan Vargas (YouTube/IG/TikTok @jeivi_f; the dropkiller.clips account says "all videos at youtube.com/@jeivi_f"). In a conference talk he says "the idea wasn't mine"; the team's origins involve partners in Ecuador and Cali, with past equity-split and legal disputes. Other founders' names not found.
  • Launch date: domain registered 2024-06-14; Wayback shows the site live on 2024-09-18 as "La Herramienta #1 para Dominar Dropi" plus the extension; Stripe start date recorded by TrustMRR is 2024-10-30.
  • Pricing: from $13.25/month (annual billing equivalent), paid tiers from $18.99/month; Whop Starter $19/month.
  • Channel profile: Colombia 50%, Venezuela 16%, Ecuador 15%; direct 66.6%, search 25.1% (brand keywords 91.6%); referrals dropi.mx 7.8%, trustmrr.com 5.9%, dropi.cl 1.6%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Piggybacking on an upstream platform: the extension works on Dropi pages, so Dropi users install the extension -> sign up -> pay; dropi.mx/cl referrals about 9%.
  • Affiliate commissions: referral links carry?fpr= (FirstPromoter) plus a referrer-specific "lifetime 15% discount code" (Juan Vargas video descriptions), turning Latin American dropshipping creators into salespeople.
  • Educational content: free "Dropkiller University," YouTube tutorial playlists, Discord, TikTok clips account @dropkiller.clips.
  • Whop marketplace + ScalBoost bundle (743 members).
  • Used AI to pull 200 user testimonials out of 159 recorded lessons as marketing material (video description).

Speculation:

  • Users recommend it to each other in WhatsApp/Telegram seller groups (direct traffic 66.6%, brand keywords 91.6%).
  • Meta ad spend (the site has a Meta Pixel, per saasdistribution's assessment); share unknown.

Revenue and scale clues

  • TrustMRR (Stripe-verified): MRR $60,179 (2026-08), 2,702 paid subscriptions, $452,343 cumulative; founder's framing is "half a million dollars in revenue."
  • Extension about 20k users; Trustpilot about 50 reviews, 4 stars.
  • Checkout visits 4,712 two months ago, 12,894 last month These figures are identical across several sites in the same batch, so they look like a floor estimate or placeholder.
