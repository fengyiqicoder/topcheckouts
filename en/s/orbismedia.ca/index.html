Basics

  • Main business: Orbis Media, a "strategy-first" video marketing agency (strategy, scripting, filming, editing, ad placement), with three packages, The Message / The Engine / The Department, quoted via a Calendly call. "Based in Toronto, working anywhere".
  • Founder: the site and Studio reviews give only the first name Karim ("Karim and his team"), no surname; contact team@orbismedia.ca.
  • Client logos: Royal LePage, Sina AI, Aviron, Cookie Place, OK Tire, Vapi; case study Dr. Golf (Instagram has collaboration Reels for OK Tire Hyde Park, Gutter Guys Plus and Doctor Golf from 2025-07~11).
  • Side product Orbis Studio (studio.orbismedia.ca): a browser-based AI image/video/audio studio aggregating 30+ models (Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, MiniMax, Gemini Omni, Nano Banana 2.1, GPT Image 2.5, Seedream 5.0, Grok Imagine 2.0, Krea 3, ElevenLabs); storyboard planning, timeline editing, character-consistent Avatars, a node Canvas, and an Agent mode connected to Claude. Pay per use, balance never expires, refunds on failures; no signup bonus and no referral program.
  • Orbis Studio Desktop: a local version where you bring your own API key, one-time purchase, sold on Payhip for $49 (https://payhip.com/OrbisMedia).
  • Social: Instagram @orbismedia_ (verified, 14.2K followers, bio: "Creative Marketing / Advanced Ad Reach / AI-enhanced / Ontario, Canada", link studio.orbismedia.ca); YouTube @Orbis-media has only 10 subscribers and 4 videos; TikTok has no real account (@orbismedia has 411 followers, unclear whether it is the same company).
  • Funding: Not found, a self-funded small team.
  • Unrelated to the real-time video model "Orbis" from the company Visko (released 2026-09, $10M pre-seed); beware of the same-name confusion.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Channel: social traffic is 30.1%, all from Instagram; the Instagram bio links straight to studio.orbismedia.ca; the 7–8 month Reels posting cadence coincides with the two months ago spike.
  • Price-comparison acquisition: the landing page uses "the same Seedance 2.0 video for $8.50 vs Higgsfield's $11.18" as the selling point, and there is a dedicated Higgsfield comparison page.
  • The search term orbis studio ai shows that people search the name after watching the content.
  • The product has no referral/invite/watermark mechanism.
  • Speculation:
  • First users: the agency's existing Instagram followers and client circle (marketers who make short video), then spread through "finished AI video" Reels; the "Advanced Ad Reach" positioning suggests it may have run Meta ads on its own Reels.
  • The two months ago spike was possibly one or two Reels blowing up or paid promotion; there was no sustained engine afterward, so last month fell back.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Studio has no subscription and is topped up per use; the Desktop version is a $49 one-time purchase.
  • Checkout visits were ~80K two months ago and ~17K last month, a one-off spike that then fell back.
  • No public MRR or user count; the Studio page has only 4 five-star reviews.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: confirmed that Instagram @orbismedia_ (14.2K followers, verified) is the only social account of any scale, with the bio linking straight to Studio and the 7–8 month Reels cadence matching the two months ago spike; changed the growth engine from "Not found" to "Creators or community" (its own Instagram content); confirmed that Studio has no referral/bonus mechanism and no Product Hunt launch.
  • Still missing: which Reel blew up (Instagram does not show like counts), whether ads were run; the founder's full name; Payhip sales (scrape failed); the columbus.tools revenue estimate returned 403.
