This is not a startup product but the fundraising arm of Congregation Pirchei Shoshanim, a US Orthodox Jewish charity (501(c)(3), EIN 20-3330272): the domain was registered in 2021, and in 2024–2025 it was just a GoHighLevel donation landing page, with individual cases (an orphaned bride's wedding, serious illness, families of terror victims) raising money on the third-party crowdfunding platform The Chesed Fund. Between July and September 2026 the cases were migrated to a self-built platform (chesedprojects.org/<number>/donate, with?aff= referral codes, personal "Raise" fundraising pages, and optional fee coverage), after which every case is distributed via "live blog" appeal posts on Orthodox Jewish news sites such as The Yeshiva World (YWN), WhatsApp/SMS lists, and referral links from rabbis and volunteers. Traffic comes from the Orthodox Jewish community's existing media and personal networks; the website itself is merely the payment endpoint.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: case-by-case charity fundraising pages: one page per family in need (story + progress bar + donate), with the option to open a personal "Raise" page for a case to forward to friends and family; donors can cover processing fees; donor accounts via Google login; email/WhatsApp/SMS subscriptions for new cases.
  • Operator: Chesed Projects, "a division of Congregation Pirchei Shoshanim, a registered 501(c)(3)" (homepage); the 2024-12 Wayback page described it as "Another Project of Congregation Pirchei Shoshanim." No specific person in charge is named.
  • Pricing: donations, no fixed price; a common pitch is "donate $72 or more and the couple will pray for you on their wedding day."
  • Scale: 248 cases on the site (3 active, 245 closed); a single case typically raises a few thousand dollars (e.g. $3,895/117 donations, $3,376/119 donations, $1,842/48 donations).
  • Domain: WHOIS registration 2021-02-17; a blank "Coming Soon" page on Wayback in 2021-11; a GoHighLevel donation page from 2024-12; the current self-built case platform from 2026-09.
  • Traffic: search 52.5% (brand keywords 82.5%), direct 30%, referral 10.5%; United States 34.6% (data for other countries missing; Israel presumably among them).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Every new case = a new round of distribution: YWN live blog post + email/WhatsApp/SMS list push -> back to chesedprojects.org to donate.
  • Referral codes (?aff=) track donations brought in by each channel/person; case pages publicly show "Rabbi X brought in $X / N donations."
  • "Raise" personal fundraising pages: donors are encouraged to create their own page for a case and forward it to friends and family (P2P fundraising).
  • Religious incentive messaging (donate $72+ and the couple prays for you) lifts conversion.

Speculation:

  • The YWN live blog posts are quite possibly paid placements (YWN has an "Advertise" entry point but the posts carry no sponsored label; unverified).
  • Checkout visits appearing from 2026-07 line up with the move from The Chesed Fund back to self-hosted Stripe payments, i.e. a change in how traffic is counted rather than a surge in demand.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 248 cases; a single case usually raises $1k–$5k, occasionally $24k (the "9 children sleeping in one room" case raised $24,606 of $30,000 on The Chesed Fund, summary only).
  • Roughly 248 x a few thousand dollars puts cumulative donations below the million-dollar mark (speculation, unverified).
  • Stripe checkout visits about 8.5k in 07, about 4.7k in 08, about 12.9k in 09 (bucketed estimates). Global rank about 2.81 million.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The organizational affiliation (Pirchei Shoshanim 501(c)(3)) is stated on the homepage, and the migration timeline is supported by Wayback plus the change in YWN links; medium confidence.
  • Gaps: who at Pirchei Shoshanim runs it and why they built their own platform in 2026 not found; whether aff=2040 is YWN's channel code and whether the YWN posts are paid is unverified; total funds raised not found.
