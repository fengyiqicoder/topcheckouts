Basics

  • Product: AI Agent hosting cloud servers; each instance is its own Linux VPS with root access. Supports OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, Claude Code and Codex, and can connect to channels like Telegram. It describes itself as "an independent service, not affiliated with OpenClaw".
  • Pricing: Lite $29/month ($199 billed yearly), Pro $59/month ($399 yearly), Max $119/month, Ultra $239/month. The early GitHub README said Lite $19 / Pro $39 / Max $79, which shows prices were raised later. Tokens/credits must be bought separately on top of the subscription.
  • Who is behind it: The footer says "© 2023 Cubo World Inc.", governed by Delaware law. The site also hosts AI tools such as a passport photo maker, tattoo generator and LinkedIn headshots, so it looks like a matrix of AI tool / SEO sites. The 2026-02-05 press release is dated San Francisco with contact email jason@flot.ai, hinting at a link to the flot.ai team. The GitHub account LeoYeAI maintains all the MyClaw-branded repos. The founder's name is not public; no funding found.
  • Launch date: WHOIS created 2026-01-30. The earliest blog post is "OpenClaw Getting Started" on 2026-01-31. It entered the waitlist stage on 2026-02-05, and the formal launch was roughly mid-to-late 2026-02 (a YouTube review video already existed on 2-21).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Grabbing launch mindshare: registered the domain on the day OpenClaw got its name, issued a paid press release 6 days later, calling itself "world's first".
  • Paid search on big terms: brand keywords 0%, paid search 55.3%, organic search 38.6%.
  • SEO tutorial pages: from 2026-01-31 it keeps publishing OpenClaw tutorials, comparison pages and Skills pages.
  • GitHub skills collection repos for traffic: openclaw-master-skills has 2.2k stars, plus repos like guardian, backup and bench, all carrying "Powered by MyClaw.ai".
  • YouTube creator tutorials and reviews, Medium/Substack promotional posts, in a form consistent with paid partnerships.
  • Affiliate channel: impact.com appears in referral sources at 3.8%.
  • Speculation:
  • The YouTube and Medium reviews are most likely paid or affiliate partnerships.
  • There is no in-product loop where users bring in users; traffic rises and falls with the heat around OpenClaw/Hermes, essentially "piggybacking on upstream traffic plus paid ads".
  • Poor Trustpilot reputation suggests mediocre retention, so continuous ad spend is needed to refill new users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing is $29–$239/month, with extra revenue from token credits. The about page says "hundreds of early adopters" (early wording).
  • Revenue not found. The two months ago/last month numbers are exactly the same as esim.gg and ainative.studio, possibly tiered estimates from the data source.

Confidence and gaps

  • Evidence for the takeoff path is fairly strong: the domain registration date, first blog post and paid press release dates all line up.
  • The founder is still not public; the relationship between flot.ai and Cubo World Inc is speculation. No launch post on Reddit or Product Hunt was found.
  • Second-pass check: added the 2026-02-05 paid press release (waitlist, flot.ai contact email), GitHub skills collection repo traffic (2.2k stars), YouTube/Medium reviews, Trustpilot bad reviews and early pricing; the OpenClaw rename timeline now has a supporting link.
