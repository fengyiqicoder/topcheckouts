Basics

  • Product: a Minecraft build resource subscription platform. Each build has a step-by-step 3D guide (one shape per step, with blocks and quantities labeled), an interactive material list, and Java/Bedrock schematic and world file downloads; individual sales (Small/Medium/Large/Maps, about $7–13 inferred from "creators get 50%: $3.50/$4.50/$6.50") + All Access Pass monthly/annual subscription (amount not rendered on the page); also build requests (build-requests).
  • Scale: the homepage self-reports "5,000+ active members/subscribers" and 542 builds (another place says 898 unlocked); the creator page self-reports "200,000+ unique visitors per month".
  • Team: no public personal information; Trustpilot lists the company's country as France, contact support@heybuilds.com; tech stack Next.js. The TikTok page has a French interface.
  • Traffic (profile record): direct 52.5%, search 40.7% (brand keywords 85%), referral 6.9%; US 19.1%, France 11.4%, Germany 5.5%; social from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram.
  • Domain: WHOIS registered 2025-05-20; Wayback was a 404 on 2025-07-10, with the first 200 snapshot on 2025-09-25 (pure front-end SPA).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator supply + revenue share: creators submit exclusive builds and get a 50% share on individual sales, download rewards, and $5/month for affiliate subscriptions (creator-guideline / creator-information). The more supply, the more valuable the subscription.
  • Creators as distribution: the affiliate link gives every creator an incentive to promote Heybuilds in their own videos/descriptions (the mechanism is evidenced, the actual traffic brought is unknown).
  • Scheduled releases + countdown: the homepage has a countdown to upcoming builds and limited-time free builds, driving subscribers to revisit directly (homepage).

Speculation:

  • Social sources (YouTube/Instagram/TikTok) are mostly links in creators' build video descriptions; the 85% brand search share suggests users see the name in a video first and then search for it.
  • Non-brand terms in search are few (about 8%), so SEO is not the main driver.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 5,000+ subscribed members (self-reported). If the monthly fee is $5–8 (Speculation, price Not found), MRR would be about $25,000–40,000.
  • The creator affiliate pays $5/month per subscription, which implies the subscription price is at least above $5, possibly about $8–10/month (Speculation).
  • 2026-08/09 Stripe checkout visits about 13,000/month (estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The launch timing (around 2025-08) is well evidenced (RDAP + Wayback + Trustpilot).
  • Gaps: founders/team Not found; no original-post evidence for the launch channel and first users; TikTok/X/Instagram pages could not be fetched; the subscription price page was not rendered. The takeoff method is a Speculation.
