This is not a software product but the brand name that Greg Philippe, a Caribbean-born LinkedIn lead-generation consultant in Montreal, gave his own service. Since around 2020 he has written on LinkedIn about "how to book 3-5 meetings a week with LinkedIn" and "how to land high-ticket clients", building 22K followers. In September 2025 he registered presellos.com and used GoHighLevel to build three pages: a call-booking page ("Business Influencer Funnel", for agency owners and coaches), a $100 "Epic LinkedIn DM Script" lead-in product, and a 90-day "revenue production system" done-for-you service page for established B2B companies. His acquisition is the method he sells: 5 LinkedIn posts a week, 30 DMs a day, and pre-sell videos (VSL) to screen people, so about 80% of site traffic is direct, clicking in from LinkedIn posts and DMs.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder's LinkedIn content: 22K followers, with posts "not for likes, but to create buying intent", and a profile with just one call to action (book a call or DM).
  • DM outreach: 30 DMs a day, "202 meetings booked in 12 months".
  • Pre-sell video screening: prospects watch a VSL before getting on a call, and he says he doesn't do discovery calls.
  • Low-priced lead-in product: a $100 script course + GPT tool converts readers into buyers, who are then upsold to high-priced services.
  • A Skool community profile reading "Founder @ PreSellOS.com" provides passive exposure.

Speculation:

  • The first customers came from the LinkedIn followers and DM conversations he built up over the years; the website is just a closing landing page and does not do acquisition.
  • CAD settlement and Portuguese checkout fields suggest it was quickly assembled on a GoHighLevel template, and the business is very small.

Revenue and scale clues

  • He says he signs about 4 clients a month at $8k-$15k each (per the post, unverified; may refer to client contract value). If true, monthly revenue would be about $30K-$60K.
  • Sales of the $100 lead-in course are unknown.
  • "Trusted by 100+ B2B companies" is self-reported.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there was no old report in the notes. Of the 5 sets of search results only the LinkedIn post and the Skool profile page were useful; the rest were unrelated (pharmacies, new food products, Stack Overflow, etc.); the work relied mainly on the three official site pages, the LinkedIn post, RDAP and Wayback CDX.
  • Gaps: the exact date of the LinkedIn post and past viral posts; real customer count and revenue; why traffic shows "France 100%".
