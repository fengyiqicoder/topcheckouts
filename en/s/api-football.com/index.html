Overview

Api-football (api-football.com): Sports data API with a free tier and free embeddable score/fixture widgets; buyers are mostly solo developers; niche, low-cost data API. Category on TopCheckouts: Developer tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 25,788 visits went from Api-football to a checkout payment page — #108 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 23,600.

Total website visits that month were about 149,975 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 17.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 80%, search 10%, AI assistants 6%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows built-in sharing (medium), and direct plus brand search is about 90%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
