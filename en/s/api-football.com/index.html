Basics

  • Product: football REST API (live scores updated about every 15 seconds, fixtures, lineups, events, standings, player stats, odds, predictions), with free score widgets; the parent brand API-SPORTS also has basketball, F1, NBA, NFL and other APIs (api-sports.io).
  • Team: API-SPORTS Team, France; founder name Not found; no funding information seen (looks self-reliant).
  • Launch: WHOIS registration 2018-07-22 (OVH, registrant country FR); earliest Wayback snapshot 2018-11-11.
  • Pricing: free tier of 100 requests per day with limited endpoints; paid from $19/month. Covers 1,200+ competitions, with live scores, lineups, events, and no advanced data such as xG; the documentation is rated "Excellent". Third-party comparisons position it as "cheaper than Sportmonks (from €78/month) and broader in coverage than football-data.org (only 12 competitions)", recommended for prototypes and side projects (footyapps, 2026-02).
  • Easily confused same-name competitors: apifootball.com, apifootball.co and football-api.com are different companies and split traffic from each other when searching "api football" (the u/apifootball account on Reddit belongs to apifootball.com, not this site).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • RapidAPI marketplace distribution + #1 ranking, with developers subscribing directly in the marketplace.
  • Projects, tutorials, SDKs and courses developers build with it cite it repeatedly → new developers come in through search/tutorials (search ranking).
  • Free score widgets let site owners embed them directly (base44.com is in our referrals, and AI site-builder output is embedding them too).
  • GitHub has 35 public repos tagged api-football (score apps, fantasy, betting predictions), and this open-source code in turn becomes LLM training data and templates for latecomers.
  • Our data: ChatGPT referrals are about 5%, second only to search, as developers asking AI for a "free football API" get it recommended.
  • Speculation:
  • The widgets carry an API-Football link/badge (branded output), unverified.
  • When LLMs write football project code they recommend API-Football by default, forming a new Gen AI channel.
  • World Cup 2026 (3–4 months ago) brought a surge of prediction/betting-type projects.
  • Nigeria at 10.5% and Brazil at 11.7% suggest developers of betting and prediction sites are an important paying group (supported by the r/algobetting discussion).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public MRR/user count. Based on 25K checkout visits last month and a price range of $19-$300+/month, annual revenue may be in the millions of dollars (speculation, no evidence).
