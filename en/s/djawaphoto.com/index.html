Basics

  • Product: a photographer's personal photobook catalog site (WordPress + UX theme), 242 digital photobooks grouped by model; no prices on site, each detail page links to djawaphoto.gumroad.com for purchase (236 Gumroad links), with a few links to Korean creator platforms likey.me and ctee.kr.
  • Who made it: photographer Djawa (South Korea), a solo studio; social accounts X @DjawaPhoto, Instagram djawaphoto.official, Tumblr. The X bio says "all digital photobooks are sold only on my page" (Buhitter summary).
  • Launch date: WHOIS created 2024-08-20 (GoDaddy); first Wayback capture the same day, 2024-08-20; an X post on 2024-08-30, "More Previews and Purchase Link", points to the new site. The earliest work date on the site is 2020-12-25 ("Christmas Special 2020", presumably back-filled older work).
  • Publishing cadence (WP API work dates): 1 book in 2020, 5 in 2021, 25 in 2022, 57 in 2023, 50 in 2024, 56 in 2025, 48 so far in 2026. Has reached a 50th book with a collaborating model (the "50th book" with Mimmi).
  • Traffic (profile file): roughly 3,934 visits in 2026-09 (-36% month over month); South Korea 43%, United States 41%, Japan 7%; direct 57%, search 33% (brand keywords 76%, non-brand terms are model names); social from X, YouTube and Instagram.

Where the first users came from: presumably the photographer's existing photobook-scene followers on X/Instagram and each collaborating model's own fans; the site was only built in 2024 as a landing page for an existing audience. No direct evidence on the size of the first buyers.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Preview posted on X -> link to the detail page on the site -> purchase on Gumroad (X posts, site link structure).
  • Non-brand search is model names, meaning model fans search a name and land on the site (profile file).
  • Holiday promo codes (XMAS2025).

Speculation:

  • Every additional model signed adds a new fan entry point; "model = supply + channel".
  • Pirate sites (image-scraping aggregators appear in search results) bring some search and awareness in reverse but also siphon off paying customers.
  • Both traffic and X engagement are declining in 2026, possibly due to platform throttling of adult-leaning content.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 242 books x Gumroad sales; unit price and sales volume not obtained (Gumroad pages returned only titles).
  • Roughly 4,000 monthly visits and declining month over month; solo-studio scale.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Evidence comes from WHOIS, Wayback CDX, the sitemap and WP API (work dates, outbound link domains), and X post search summaries.
  • Content is adult-leaning photography (suggestive/swimsuit/cosplay); no sign of minors was found; as required, only text and metadata were read and no image content was opened.
  • The photographer's real identity, Gumroad prices and sales, and the starting channel before the books were not found; the takeoff year is inferred from publishing volume.
