Basics

  • Product: pay for parking by phone/web (street meters, parking lots), later extended to business parking management (Register your business, annual plans) and transit (Facebook account name hotspotparkingandtransit); merchants can pay for customers' parking and push ads to them (a CRM feature).
  • Company: HotSpot Parking Inc., Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada; founder and CEO Phillip Curley (started as a UNB civil engineering student).
  • Funding: graduated from the PropelICT and ACcelR8 accelerators, and received equity investment from BDC Capital and the New Brunswick Innovation Foundation (amounts not found). Team of a dozen or so (per CIRA profile).
  • United States: partnered with Passport of Charlotte and entered the US under the Hotspot Merchant Solutions brand.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Municipal contract = distribution: cities connect their meters to HotSpot and announce it on the meters and the official site (halifax.ca parking page), and drivers naturally download it to pay.
  • Free for the municipality, paid by drivers ($0.10 per use or $2/month or $20/year), lowering the city's procurement barrier and speeding up city signings.
  • Users from one city can use it directly when parking in another city, giving 40K+ cross-city users and strengthening the pitch for signing the next city.
  • Merchants paying for customers' parking plus ad CRM adds B2B revenue.
  • After merging into Arcadis, HotSpot is bundled with Arcadis's municipal parking consulting, enforcement and license plate recognition businesses, and clients have grown to 150+ North American institutions (per the site).
  • Referral traffic comes directly from partner institutions' websites: lambtonshores.ca, acadiau.ca, lassomption.qc.ca (profile data).

Speculation:

  • The Referrals channel mainly comes from backlinks on municipality websites and university parking pages.
  • There is no in-product mechanism for people to bring in others; growth relies on BD signing cities / universities / parking lots.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: drivers $0.10 per use or $2/month or $20/year (Halifax site figures); businesses have annual plans.
  • In 2018, 40K+ cross-city users and 100K+ parking sessions in Halifax's first month.
  • No public MRR; the early team was a dozen or so people with BDC and NBIF investment; it now belongs to Arcadis, with 150+ North American clients and millions of parking sessions a year.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: added two big cities coming on board, Barrie (2023) and Edmonton (2024-04-29, replacing MyEPark), and that HotSpot is now an Arcadis product with 150+ North American clients. The acquisition date was not found. Classification unchanged: the first users were the drivers in the Fredericton municipal pilot.
  • The origin and the Halifax launch have first-hand coverage, so credible.
  • Several entrevestor articles have no clear date and the fetch failed this time, so the Ontario/Passport timing is not pinned down.
