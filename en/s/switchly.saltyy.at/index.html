Basics

  • Product: an Android app that blocks apps, websites and certain sections inside apps (such as short-video pages) by profile, can be scheduled by time / Wi-Fi / Bluetooth, and is switched on and off with NFC, QR code and barcode tags; there are two spin-off versions, Switchly Lite and Switchly Launcher. Supports English and German.
  • Pricing: core free, no ads; the premium version is a one-time purchase; tag + offline premium €14.99, bundles €17.99 / €19.99 (from the profile record); the Play version uses Google Play billing, and the direct-install APK version uses Stripe or a redeem code.
  • Open source: gitlab.com/Saltyy/switchly-public (repo created 2026-01-31 with the 1.4.2 source released; as of 2026-10 it has 15 stars and 3 forks), plus switchly-lite-public.
  • Developer: Realm of Salt, owner Andi Schreiner, Vienna, GISA 39407450 (site Legal page). saltyy.at is his personal portfolio, which also has Windows tools, Minecraft plugins, Godot games, a Pokemon card price app and more; Wayback first captured it in 2020-11.
  • Google Play: 10K+ downloads, 4.7 rating, about 195 reviews, latest update 2026-09-13 (v2.3.1).
  • Our data: India 70.4%, Canada 16.4%, United States 13.2%; direct 78.2%, search 16.4%, referral 3.3%; non-brand search about 13%, with terms like foqos, foqos android, alternative to brick.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Competitor-alternative search: the comparison pages cover popular physical phone-detox products like Brick and Foqos, and the non-brand terms in our data are precisely foqos android and alternative to brick.
  • Recommendations in the phone-detox community: Jose Briones (Moving Offline, a creator making dumbphone / offline-life content) recommends it, then it is relayed on the Fairphone forum.
  • Organic supply from the 3D-printing community: people post matching NFC tag models on Printables and MakerWorld, with descriptions pointing straight to Switchly.
  • Open source + free + no ads, well suited to being recommended in privacy / open-source circles.
  • Organic distribution on Google Play: 10K+ downloads, 4.7 rating.

Speculation:

  • India at 70% of site traffic is unusual; it may be a wave from some Indian YouTube/Instagram video, or a small sample skewing the stats, with no evidence found.
  • Direct visits of 78% are mostly returning users coming from in-app links (download APK, buy tags, delete-account page).
  • The first 1,000 users are speculated to have come from Google Play searches for "app blocker / NFC" and recommendations in the phone-detox community, with no launch post as evidence.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Play 10K+ downloads, about 195 reviews. Revenue comes from the one-time premium version and physical tags (€14.99-19.99), with no public figures; a rough estimate of a few hundred orders puts annual revenue on the order of a few thousand euros (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site, written from scratch. The developer's identity (Legal page), Play data, the GitLab repo creation time and Wayback are all primary evidence, fairly high credibility.
  • Most of the search results were irrelevant (Changelly, CVEs, etc.); AppBrain, Printables and MakerWorld all returned 403, so only snippets could be used.
  • Gaps: the original video / date of Jose Briones's recommendation, the source of the Indian traffic, sales and revenue; whether there are Reddit posts (such as on r/digitalminimalism, r/dumbphones) was not found.
