Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid acquisition: paid traffic is 46%; the privacy policy collects ad click identifiers for conversion tracking.
  • Sharing and referral attribution: the policy lists "share links, referral attribution", indicating a share-link/invite mechanism.
  • Unlock payments: coin top-ups and subscriptions unlock hidden segments, the "hook then paywall" model of short-drama apps.

Speculation:

  • AI-generated content keeps supply cost extremely low, so clips can be produced in bulk per language/market and ad spend reallocated by country based on ROI.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No revenue data. The high share of paid traffic suggests a sizable ad budget (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. To avoid adult content, only the privacy policy (a non-content page) was read; video pages, X and TikTok were not opened. No evidence of child-related illegal content was found, but no content review was done either.
  • Gaps: operator, pricing and ad-channel details all not found. The security-vendor blacklist signals risk.
