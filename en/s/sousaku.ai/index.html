Overview

Sousaku (sousaku.ai): Japanese AI creation platform for images, videos, music, characters and short dramas with contests. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Sousaku to a checkout payment page — #157 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 60,087 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 28.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 72%, referrals 21%, search 4%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows branded free output, public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 74%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
