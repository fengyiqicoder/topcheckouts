Basics

  • Product: browser-based AI creation platform switching among dozens of models including GPT Image, Seedream, Nano Banana, Midjourney, Kling, Veo, WAN and Hotgen, producing images / video / music / characters / short dramas; has Agent short-drama production, template mini-apps (OC Maker, stickers, holiday effects), an API (platform.sousaku.ai), a mobile app since 2025-12, and an AI manga service Mangami in beta from 2026-10; has an NSFW mode (discussed by creators by 2026-06).
  • Operator: the seller of record under Japan's commerce law is "創作未来株式会社" (Jinnan, Shibuya, Tokyo); X bio says Tokyo, Japan. Third-party reviews call it "the official generative AI tool released by BytePlusJapan (ByteDance group)"; the 2025 Halloween Cup was co-hosted with BytePlus; aipicks checked in 2026-08 and found no public material showing a capital relationship with ByteDance. On 2025-09-30 a note author wrote a critical piece calling it "a 'made-in-Japan AI' built by the company that runs TikTok" (opinion piece; the data-flow claims lack evidence).
  • Pricing (tax included, subject to change): free tier 10 credits/month; Standard ¥1,036–1,480/month (¥1,480 in 2026-03, including 150 images + 50 videos); Premium and Ultimate tiers; credits at roughly ¥0.62–0.69 each; periodic discounts (30% off annual plans 2026-03-10 to 31).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Contest rules require "follow on X + join Discord + share with hashtag + submit a link to the work", so entries become ads on X; a cup roughly every 1–2 months.
  • CPP creator program (participates in judging cups) + note creators' PR tutorials carrying share_code → creators bring in new users with tracked codes.
  • Referrals 21.4% from 1link.jp, note.com etc. confirm note articles drive traffic; direct 71.5%, brand keywords 74.1%.
  • At launch, AI art creators posted work in a concentrated burst under #BytePlus (creator seeding).
  • Watermark-removal toggle (free output carries a watermark by default); NSFW mode generates "sousaku ai nsfw" searches.
  • Speculation:
  • The launch-period creator seeding was most likely organized by BytePlus/the platform (uniform hashtags, concentrated timing); no contracts disclosed.
  • ByteDance-family models may come at lower cost or earlier access, used as a selling point.
  • The July spike may relate to the NSFW mode spreading or a new model launch; no specific event found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Official X about 5,600 followers (2026-10), YouTube about 5,400 subscribers.
  • Our September checkout visits 17,192, Japan 91.5%. User count and MRR not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Medium-high. Operating entity, contests and PR articles with referral codes have first-hand evidence; the ByteDance relationship rests only on third-party accounts + co-hosted events.
  • Gaps: specific share_code commission rules, CPP program details, the trigger for the July spike.
