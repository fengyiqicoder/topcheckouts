Overview

Playlistpush (playlistpush.com): Music promotion service pitching artists' tracks to Spotify playlist curators and TikTok creators. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Playlistpush to a checkout payment page — #392 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 64,688 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 13.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 60%, search 37%, AI assistants 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 60%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: from $280 (Spotify campaign, one-time); from $350 (TikTok campaign, one-time).
