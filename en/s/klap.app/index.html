Overview

Klap (klap.app): AI tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Klap to a checkout payment page — #238 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 179,311 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 7.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 62%, search 28%, referrals/email/paid 10%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 79%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $29/mo.
