Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Industry sources → breaking news first → practitioner subscriptions: the 2017 about page clearly pitches "official sources + learn of program changes first", with a weekly newsletter to bring readers back.
  • Interviews and submissions: video interviews with industry figures and open calls for submissions/tips make practitioners both readers and content suppliers (interviewees share the pieces).
  • Chinese channel: the WeChat account "Investment Migration Insider" covers the agent circles of China, the largest source market for investment migration.
  • Media citations: Portugal, Grenada, Forbes and other coverage cite IMI data, bringing authoritative backlinks and search rankings.
  • Search: non-brand search is now about 30%, riding trending news keywords; AI assistants bring 7.6%.
  • In-person events + membership: each IMI Connect sells out its 125 seats, and IMI Pro members get event priority and mutual referrals (300+ members, 50+ countries).

Speculation:

  • The first readers most likely came from contacts the founder built in the investment migration / media circles before starting (Nesheim's earlier background is from memory, unverified: worked on content/research in the investment migration industry).
  • Since 2022 the US readership has been the largest, likely tied to consumer searches from the American "Plan B / second passport" boom.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue sources: IMI Pro €840/year, IMI Executive €10,000/year, event tickets and sponsorship, sponsored content (claims 750K+ monthly visits), job board.
  • Third-party traffic estimates differ widely (hypestat about 5.9K daily visits, updowntoday about 1,260 daily visits).
  • Team of 1–10 people. Specific revenue: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The founder's earlier background and the specific source of the first readers were not found in the source text, so "migration from an existing industry circle" is inferred only from the about page.
  • 750K monthly visits and 100% sold out are both the company's own marketing claims.
