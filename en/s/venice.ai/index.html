Basics

  • Product: A privacy-first, lightly censored AI platform with 200+ text/image/video/audio models. Prompts are encrypted on the client and conversations are stored locally on the user's device (local-first storage with RxDB). Also offers Pro subscription $18/month, Pro+ $68/month, a developer API, the VVV token (on Base, staked for inference credits) and the DIEM token (staking mints $1 of AI credit per day).
  • Who is behind it:
  • Founder and CEO Erik Voorhees.
  • Co-founder, president and CTO Jesse Proudman: after selling Blue Box he spent about three years as a VP at Betterment, built Venice in his spare time, then went full-time.
  • Other executives: VP of marketing Austin Virts, head of strategy Jonathan Shapiro, head of engineering Tim Shakarian.
  • Headcount grew from about 15 a year ago to about 45, split between San Francisco and Seattle, incorporated in Wyoming.
  • Funding: announced a $65M Series A on 2026-07-01 at a $1B valuation, the first outside money. Led by Dragonfly, with Coinbase Ventures, North Island, Archetype, Morgan Creek Digital and Liquid 2 participating.
  • Launch date: Public launch in May 2024 (first HN post 2024-05-10; the 1000startups report says "public launch May 2024"). The domain was registered in 2019, presumably bought later.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Founder narrative driven: the 1000startups report says "marketing driven substantially by founder narrative — Voorhees's public statements on X and in press".
  • Token incentives: VVV/DIEM act as both referral and retention mechanisms, "without traditional paid acquisition spend". About 8% of users pay in crypto, and over 40% of the VVV supply has been burned.
  • Controversy lifting awareness: "uncensored" brought negative hacker-forum coverage, which objectively reinforced the positioning.
  • Launch platform: #1 of the day on Product Hunt.
  • High share of brand search: direct visits 80.6%, and 91.9% of search is brand keywords.
  • Third-party referrals: sites like venice-aii.github.io and godmod3.ai (a jailbreak community) send traffic.
  • Speculation:
  • Users share "uncensored" outputs and jailbreak comparisons on X/Reddit, generating word of mouth.
  • Crypto KOLs promote the product while pushing VVV, and rising coin prices bring signup peaks (the 2026-09 VVV high coincided with the rise in checkout visits).
  • Many third-party reviews and comparison blogs capture long-tail terms like "uncensored ai".

Revenue and scale clues

  • ARR above $70M, profitable in 2026 Q1 (the CEO's figures, relayed by Yahoo/TechCrunch).
  • Users: 3M+ active (2026-04), 3.5M registered (2026-07), 4M+ (2026-10).
  • About 45 employees, $1B valuation, VVV market cap about $1.16B (2026-09).

Confidence and gaps

  • Funding, ARR and user counts come from the company and multiple media outlets, so credibility is high.
  • The first users came from Erik's X followers and the Morpheus/crypto communities: the Reddit post is only a summary, and Erik's launch tweet was not directly verified.
  • The VVV issue date conflicts: memory says 2025-01, the 1000startups report says 2026-01, unverified.
  • Second-pass check: added co-founder Jesse Proudman, ARR of $70M and profitability, headcount, #1 of the day on Product Hunt (2025-03-07), the hacker-forum popularity coverage (2025-05), the Morpheus community start-up lead, and the DIEM launch date.
