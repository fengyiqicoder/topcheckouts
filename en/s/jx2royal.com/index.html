Basics

  • Product: JX2 Royal, a wuxia MMORPG (Wudang, Shaolin, Emei, Kunlun and other sects), based on Kingsoft's JX Online 2 (known in Vietnam as Võ Lâm Truyền Kỳ 2); most likely an unauthorized private server (the site never mentions a Kingsoft/Seasun license; speculation).
  • Operator: Crownmark Games, site address Angel Place Level 17, 123 Pitt Street, Sydney (a common coworking/virtual office address, presumably a shell registration), game region Singapore, Google Cloud. crownmarkgames.com registered 2026-06-15 (Namecheap); jx2royal.com registered 2026-06-06 (Namecheap). Founder not found.
  • Target market: Cambodia (event times in UTC+7 Cambodia time; payments support Bakong/KHQR, QR scanning via ABA, ACLEDA, Wing and Canadia bank apps, plus Visa/MC/Amex/Discover cards).
  • Currency: Crown Coin; the Imperial Street Shop sells scrolls, mounts, bundles and power items.
  • No Wayback snapshots at all.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Streamers/ambassadors: the site lists 5 local streamers (Facebook: bun.san.58118, johnsmeyscreator, ravenblaze99, SaBayRaZer, Tochminutess; YouTube: @engbunsann3745, @johnsmeys, @razer.gaming, ravenblaze99), and accepts "streamer applications"; approved streamers get an active streamer profile and join later events.
  • Resellers (top-up agents): resellers buy Crown Coin inventory in bulk and top up players, with tiered discounts, turning offline/in-group top-up vendors into a ground sales force.
  • Multiplayer: sects, guilds (Clan Chambers), battlefields, tournaments, world chat; an MMO naturally pulls in friends/guilds (matches the known multiplayer_room mechanism).
  • Seasons: launch leveling races, bosses and monthly season updates create return points.

Speculation:

  • Most first players are veteran Cambodian JX2/Võ Lâm Truyền Kỳ players migrating in via Facebook and Telegram groups as "whole guilds moving house"; the continuous DDoS suggests competing private servers.
  • The last month checkout peak coincides with the 09-04 Mid-Autumn season and tournament preparation.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue or online player counts. Indirect clues: streamer teaser videos have only a few hundred views, suggesting a small circle; the fairly large DDoS attacks (15.4 Gbps) indirectly suggest it is worth targeting by competitors.
  • Pricing: no specific prices published (shop and Crown Coin top-ups).

Confidence and gaps

  • Timeline comes from the site's news and WHOIS, medium-high credibility.
  • Operator background is questionable: extremely recent registration, coworking building address, no founder information, no mention of a copyright license.
  • Gaps: online player count, Facebook group size (Facebook not opened), launch Facebook post, streamer partnership terms all not found. Khmer-language search results were empty.
