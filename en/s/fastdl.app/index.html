Basics

  • Product: a no-login Instagram download web tool; paste a link to download public videos, Reels, photos, stories, IGTV, carousels and audio; also an anonymous viewer, Instagram to MP4, an Android APK, an API and an MCP page.
  • Operator: the terms/privacy policy say "Company refers to iGram" and "Country refers to: Alaska, United States" (template-style fill-in). The actual team was not found. Footer "© 2020-2026 FastDl".
  • Pricing: free, monetized by ads (the privacy policy lists Google Ads remarketing); a malwaretips summary calls it "ad-supported, with misleading download buttons".
  • Self-reported scale: "Over 30 Million Users Worldwide" (https://fastdl.app/fastdl).
  • Domain: fastdl.app registered 2023-02-09 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2023-02-10. The related domain fastdl.cc was only registered on 2026-01-02 (the first search result, suspected to be an imitation or a backup site of the same team).
  • Social: Instagram @fastdl.app, TikTok @fastdl.app, YouTube @FastDLapp, X @FastDLapp, a Facebook group (site footer).
  • Traffic: India 26%, United States 16%, Indonesia 14%; direct 56%, non-brand search 43% (instagram video download, instagram downloader), referrals fdown.net 7.9%, snaptik.app 5.0%, snapinsta.to 4.9%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • A landing page matrix of many languages (20+) × many content types (Reels/Story/IGTV/Carousel/Photo/MP4) holds search positions.
  • Similar downloader sites refer traffic to each other (fdown.net, snaptik.app, snapinsta.to).
  • A competitor comparison page (fastdl-vs-snapinsta) intercepts competitors' brand keywords.
  • Repeat visitors: direct visits 56%; the APK and the f-d.app prefix shortcut (site links look like https://f-d.app/https://www.instagram.com/…) lower the barrier to repeat use.

Speculation:

  • The terms name iGram, so it may belong to the same team that runs several Instagram/TikTok downloader sites, using a network of sites to spread the risk of being blocked by Google or Instagram.
  • Downloaded videos carry no watermark and the output carries no brand, so spread mainly relies on users telling each other to "use fastdl".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ad revenue, not disclosed. Claims 30 million users.
  • Similar sites (snapinsta, snaptik) follow a high-traffic, low-ARPU model; no evidence for specific amounts.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The launch time (RDAP + Wayback) and operator name (the terms say iGram) have original evidence; how it took off is mostly speculation.
  • Gaps: the last two groups of Google results were empty; the malwaretips page returns 403 so only the summary was used; the 2023 Wayback page snapshots were not read (rate-limited); the team, country, and exact relationship to iGram/snapinsta were not found.
