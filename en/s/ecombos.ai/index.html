Basics

  • Product: an e-commerce AI creative studio — one product image generates studio shots, white-background images, posters, UGC digital-human videos, lip sync, person/scene swaps, Ad Decoder (breaking down competitors' Meta ads), an AI video factory, MCP (callable from Claude/ChatGPT), and more; claims 40+ models (Sora 2, Veo 3, Kling, Seedance, etc.), 22 languages and 4K export.
  • Tech: the front-end bundle has many lovable.dev references — generated and hosted by Lovable (front-end code scraped by us).
  • Team/company/country: Not found (WHOIS privacy protection; no company entity on the site; the support email in the code is still the placeholder support@example.com). The LinkedIn company page has 588 followers and Instagram 270 — very small social presence, showing traffic does not come from its own social followers.
  • Pricing: Starter $19 (100 credits), Pro $49 (300), Ultimate $99 (700 + 365 unlimited Nano Banana uses), Creator $199 (1,600), plus a $399 tier; annual billing is about 20% off ($180/$468/$948/$1,908/$3,828).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Affiliate commissions: an impact.com affiliate program + review sites/YouTube reviews carrying ref links (from 2026-06).
  • Paid search: the pages embed Google Ads conversion code (AW-18337966140); channel profile shows search at 49% and brand keywords at 84% (including "can you cancel an ecombos subscription").
  • X/LinkedIn content lead-ins: from last month, frequent long posts on AI ad methodology + "follow + RT + comment to get a DM" posts, using MCP connection to Claude as the hook; the front end maintains short links and UTM attribution for yt/ig/tt/li/x channels.
  • Speculation: the 2–3 months ago peak came from concentrated affiliate promotion and increased ad buying (the 12.7% Vietnam traffic matches ref=TUAN779, but this is not directly confirmed); the last month fall and the "how to cancel a subscription" searches suggest mediocre retention, sustained by continued ad buying. Generated assets carry no branding, so there is no branded-output loop.
