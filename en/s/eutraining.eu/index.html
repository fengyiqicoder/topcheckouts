Basics

  • Product: an online prep platform for EPSO (European Personnel Selection Office) exams (AD5/AD7/CAST/specialist posts, etc.): question bank and mock exams (self-described 55,000+ questions, 24 languages), written test grading, live classes/workshops, one-on-one coaching; the free parts include a 5-question demo, free registration, webinars explaining new recruitment notices, a free ebook, an EU Jobs listing and a salary calculator.
  • Team: founders András Baneth and László Zlatarov (childhood friends), founded in 2006; the footer company name is Arboreus; the About page lists about 18 staff (coaches, back office, support). Baneth is the author of "The Ultimate EU Test Book" and co-author of "The Ultimate EU Handbook" (2025). Source: https://eutraining.eu/about-us
  • Scale: self-reports 175,000–180,000+ registered users, 17M+ question uses, 64,000 Facebook followers; Instagram about 2,463 followers (search summary).
  • Pricing: free registration; AD5 packages €135/€275/€395/€695; single questions €0.17–0.21; written test grading €49 per submission (profile record).
  • Traffic (profile record): direct 49.4%, search 38.0% (brand keywords 72%), referral 7.6%; Belgium 21.7%, Italy 18.6%, Germany 11.1%.
  • Domain: earliest Wayback 2008-09-25 ("Online EU Training…under construction"); the 2011-01 snapshot is already a complete EPSO question bank + live training + jobs + forum + blog site.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Exam-season event driven: whenever EPSO publishes a new round of recruitment notices, EU Training writes a guide that day and hosts a free explainer webinar (YouTube/Facebook live), pulling notice-related search traffic and candidates into the site (YouTube channel description + 2011 snapshot blog).
  • Free → paid funnel: free registration, a 5-question demo, a free ebook → question bank packages by exam type and grading/coaching.
  • Book and site cross-promotion: the founder's prep book was "used by 100,000+ candidates", and the book and site share brand endorsement (About page).
  • Traffic structure: brand terms are 72% of search and nearly half of visits are direct, typical of an old brand with word of mouth and candidates recommending each other.

Speculation:

  • Candidates spread it by word of mouth in the EU institutions, the Blue Book trainee circle and university EU studies programs, and the brand-term searches mainly come from such recommendations.
  • EPSO's switch to computerized competency tests in 2010 (from memory, unverified) caused a sudden jump in demand for "online mock questions", the first window of volume growth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 180,000+ registered users, 56,000+ practice packs sold (homepage self-report). At an average order of €100–150, cumulative revenue would be in the millions of euros (Speculation).
  • Team of about 18, a long-term profitable small company (Speculation, no funding records seen).
  • 2026-09 Stripe checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The founding year, founders and user count come from the site's About page/homepage, credible.
  • Gaps: no interview original text on the first users and growth in 2006–2010; Reddit pages could not be opened; the book's publication year and sales rely on the site's "100,000+ candidates" wording and are not independently verified.
  • This site's "checkout visit jump" in 2026-09 should be understood as an old site switching to a new checkout system, not a takeoff event.
