Overview

Eutraining (eutraining.eu): EPSO exam preparation service with practice tests, workshops, coaching and free EU career guides. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Eutraining to a checkout payment page — #232 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 74,986 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 17.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 49%, search 38%, referrals/email/paid 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 15%, with queries like “eu jobs”, “field-related mcq”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
