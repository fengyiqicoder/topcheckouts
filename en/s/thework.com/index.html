Thework (thework.com): The Work of Byron Katie: self-inquiry practice site with worksheets, videos, podcast, live events and books. Category on TopCheckouts: Courses & content.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Thework to a checkout payment page — #440 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.
