Overview

Manta (manta.net): Web platform for reading manhwa, manga and novels with genre browsing and trending lists. Category on TopCheckouts: Media.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Manta to a checkout payment page — #283 of the 607 sites we track. That is 56% up from the April–June monthly average of 8,283.

Total website visits that month were about 547,669 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 2.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 75%, direct 24%, AI assistants 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 60%, with queries like “manhwa”, “manhwa online”, “bl”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
