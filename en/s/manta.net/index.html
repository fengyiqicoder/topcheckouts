Korean ebook/web-novel unicorn RIDI translated a decade's worth of its own manhwa and web-novel IP into English and launched the subscription app Manta in North America in 2020-11 ("Netflix for manhwa", unlimited reading from $3.99/month). It started with romance/BL hits that had already blown up in Korea under the parent company, such as Under the Oak Tree, hit #1 on the US Google Play free comics chart and 300K downloads within 3 months, 1M downloads at 6 months, then 5M (2022-04) → 10M (2023-03) → 15M (2024-06) → 16M+. Early on it was a pure app play, with manta.net just a landing page; in the last two years the web version opened up manhwa/manga/web-novel reading pages and feeds on search traffic for big terms like "manhwa", "manhwa online" and "bl" (search 75%, non-brand about 60%). In 2026-10 it also capitalized on the shutdown of Kakao-owned Tapas, launching a "move your bookshelf" program and an independent-creator platform to grab users.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: subscription reading platform for Korean webcomics (webtoon/manhwa), manga and web novels, iOS/Android apps + web; mainly romance, romance fantasy and BL.
  • Company: RIDI Corporation (Korea, founder and CEO Bae Ki-sik; Korea's first content unicorn). Manta appointed its first standalone CEO Travis Kim (ex-Amazon, LG) in 2023-10.
  • Pricing: $3.99/month at launch (KED 2021-03); third-party reviews now cite from $4.99/month; free chapters available, "Free Pass" restored on 2025-07-01.
  • Domain: manta.net registered 2001-12-10 (RDAP); in 2018 it was still a "premium domain for sale" page, and RIDI bought the old domain; the "Manta Comics" page first appeared 2020-11-19.
  • Current traffic: United States 24.6%, India 5.2%, Germany 4.4%; search 75% (non-brand about 60%: manhwa, manhwa online, bl, manhwa site), direct 24%, referrals include comick.dev.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Upstream IP supply: RIDI's decade of web-novel/comic IP localized quickly, with hits bringing new users (Under the Oak Tree 7M reads, made the NYT bestseller list).
  • Subscription + first chapters free: free preview first, subscribe once hooked (the company says subscribers read 4x as much as à-la-carte buyers).
  • IP spillover: Kindle, print books (Ize Press/Inklore), audio dramas and live-action series (Semantic Error) feed the brand.
  • Web SEO: 60% of current visits come from big non-brand terms like "manhwa" (traffic profile).
  • Poaching from a shutting-down competitor: the 2026-10 "move your bookshelf" migration program for Tapas users.

Speculation:

  • The app phase relied mainly on paid acquisition and store charts; web SEO only started after 2022 (Wayback shows the homepage mostly 307-redirecting to /en from 2023, exact launch date of the reading pages unverified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Downloads: 300K (2021-02) → 1M (2021-05) → 5M (2022-04) → 8M (2022-10) → 10M (2023-03) → 15M (2024-06) → site claims 16M+ in 4 years.
  • The site's About page says "2022 revenue $200 million, valuation $1.6 billion", which looks like the parent company RIDI's numbers rather than Manta's alone; claims to be "the comics platform with the second-most paying users in the US" and #1 in Google Play comics in 31 countries.
  • Pricing $3.99 → $4.99/month.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Launch date and download milestones have multi-source evidence from Korean media, Variety and ICv2, high credibility.
  • Gaps: specific early paid channels and spend, subscriber count, Manta's standalone revenue, and when the web reading pages opened and started capturing SEO traffic are all not found.
