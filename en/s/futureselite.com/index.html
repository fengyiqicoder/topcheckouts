Basics

  • Product: FuturesElite, a futures prop firm: paid Evaluation accounts and Instant funded accounts with no evaluation, up to $1.5M stacked capital, up to 100% profit split, no activation fee, 24-hour average payouts; gamified design: Elite Packs card draws (discounts, free resets, removal of the consistency rule), Skills & Quests leveling, tournaments ($5 entry). In-house trading back end, connected to Project X and Volumetrica. CFD sister brand fundedelite.com (registered in Saint Lucia).
  • Operator: Quantum SRL (Latina, Italy, Corso G. Matteotti 61, tax ID 03095010595).
  • Founders: Christian Habibi (co-founder and CEO, trading since 2016, previously ran a pet-supplies distribution chain in Italy), Artur S. Deshko (co-founder and COO, IT/blockchain background, trading since 2015); both entered prop trading in 2020 and decided to open their own prop firm in 2023. Claims a team of 25+. No funding seen.
  • Self-reported data: $9.5M+ cumulative payouts, each published on PayoutJunction; FundedElite separately reports $3.6M+ payouts and "50,000+ traders".

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: existing traders and partners of sister brand FundedElite (the About page states "launched with structure and industry partners already in place"), plus prop-firm comparison/review sites (PropFirmMatch, PropFirmBridge with utm-tracked links, Lune, MyPropGenius etc. all wrote reviews with discount codes).
  • Takeoff: the community partner program, exclusive codes like NQ/PJ/TT + commissions + a community profile page + a public payout leaderboard; community owners earn commissions, members get discounts, and the leaderboard sets communities competing.
  • Trading YouTuber review videos (discount codes are industry standard).
  • Social proof: public payout certificates, Discord result sharing, "real trader interviews" posted on X.
  • Repeat purchases: 10th account free in bundles, card packs, skill points, tournaments.
  • Speculation: most community owners are trading KOLs on YouTube/X/TikTok who use the codes on stream, which explains the high Direct share; the 2026-09 payout dispute and Trustpilot warning may slow later growth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Partner communities alone account for 31,654 traded accounts; at an estimated average evaluation fee of $50–150, evaluation revenue from the community channel is in the one-to-several-million-dollar range (speculation). Site-wide cumulative payouts $7.44M (PayoutJunction, as of 2026-09-14) to $9.5M+ (site's own claim).
