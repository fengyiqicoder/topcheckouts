Basics

  • Product: AI website builder (site in 30 seconds: template, images, copy) + CRM, invoicing, booking, review automation, AI blog, brand/logo generator, marketing Agent; in 2026 added Durable Money (payments, business cards, Money agent, limited to eligible US users).
  • Who built it: James Clift (founder, previously ran a web development agency), headquartered in Vancouver with a remote team. Investors: Spark Capital, Torch Capital, Dash Fund, South Park Commons, Infinity Ventures, Soma Capital (site About page).
  • Pricing: free to start; Starter $15/mo, Business $25/mo, Pro $45/mo (third-party pricing page); profile data lists a Launch plan at $25/mo.
  • Domain: durable.com registered in 1995; the 2023-06 snapshot was still a French environmental news site "Durable", and by the 2024-06 snapshot it was Durable AI, so the domain was presumably bought between H2 2023 and H1 2024 (speculation, based on Wayback). The main site before that was durable.co (Wayback from 2021-09).
  • Traffic (profile data): United States 37.6%, Georgia 10.4%, India 10%; direct 56.7%, search 32.5%, referral 6.5%; brand keywords 89.2%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: generated sites carry a "Built with Durable" badge (a "Built with Durable" section appears on the homepage in the 2024-03 snapshot).
  • Free tools and guide pages capture search: business name generator, payroll calculator, state startup guides, website templates, Google ad copy generator.
  • Product Hunt launch; many third-party review sites (G2, Trustpilot with 106 reviews, HostAdvice, Cybernews) keep publishing reviews.

Speculation:

  • Paid ads (YouTube/Facebook) became one of the main drivers later; 89% brand keywords in the profile shows return visits driven by brand awareness.
  • Georgia at 10% of traffic is anomalous, possibly outsourced/agency site builders or data noise.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site count went from "millions" (2023) to "10M+ sites" (2026); users "3 million+".
  • Funding: $14M in 2024-01. Revenue not disclosed.
