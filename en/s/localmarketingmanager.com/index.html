It is not a standalone startup product but a white-label, de-branded domain that Merchynt (a local SEO software company in Santa Monica, Los Angeles) prepared for its own AI Google Business Profile management tool, Paige. In February 2024 it bought this expired old domain (which people had used since 2011) and put the generic name "GBP Manager" on it, so that when over 1,000 agencies resell Paige to their own local business clients, those clients don't see the Merchynt name on login pages, reports or emails. Traffic comes from agencies' clients arriving via back-office links/emails (direct 67.9%, referral 24.1%), and growth depends entirely on Merchynt's agency distribution network.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: the homepage has only one line, "GBP Manager helps businesses grow by optimizing their local online presence", with contact email support@localmarketingmanager.com; the real product is at app.localmarketingmanager.com, whose page title is "Paige" and which loads Merchynt's support widget (support.merchynt.com), Merchynt's HubSpot, FirstPromoter affiliate tracking and a Zapier partner widget, and whose analytics script registers three domains: merchynt.com, app.localmarketingmanager.com and audit.app.localmarketingmanager.com.
  • Who built it: Merchynt (Santa Monica, CA, hello@merchynt.com). Merchynt describes itself as "13,000+ local businesses · 1,000+ agencies · 4.9/5 from 1,000+ reviews", with products Paige (AI that automatically manages Google Business Profile, Google Maps and AI search visibility), Sam (an AI sales assistant for agencies finding clients) and ProfilePro. The merchynt.com domain was registered 2019-02-03. The founder is Ali Husayni from memory (from memory, unverified).
  • Agency program: $100/month membership, including a white-label local SEO audit tool (50 reports a month with the agency's logo), "Paige emails sent from your own domain, so clients only see your brand", white-label weekly reports, wholesale-priced citation building and reputation management; there is also an affiliate program. https://www.merchynt.com/agencies
  • Domain history: Wayback 2011-2015 shows a local search marketing consultant site called "Local Marketing Manager" (cagora template), which became an empty WordPress site in 2016 and was taken over by a Chinese spam site in 2022; Verisign RDAP shows the current registration is 2024-02-19 (Amazon Registrar), i.e. it was bought again after expiring. From 2025-11 the Wayback snapshot title is "Local Marketing Manager"/"GBP Manager".
  • Traffic (profile data): US 100%; direct 67.9%, referral 24.1%, email 4.8%; search terms are specific business names and addresses (such as "sadkhin north williamsburg", "1725 rosencrans ave gardena") and "app.localmarketingmanager".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Agency distribution: Merchynt's $100/month agency program + white-label tools, with agencies reselling Paige under their own brand (merchynt.com/agencies).
  • Affiliate commissions: the app page loads the FirstPromoter tracking script, and Merchynt has an Affiliate Program page.
  • Traffic structure: direct + referral + email is over 97%, and the search terms are clients' business names, meaning visitors are almost all existing customers (profile data).

Speculation:

  • A free GBP audit (with the agency's logo) -> the agency uses it to win local businesses -> the business signs up for Paige -> logs in at the neutral domain localmarketingmanager.com. Growth speed equals the expansion speed of Merchynt's agency network.
  • Buying the expired old domain may have been about the generic name "Local Marketing Manager" and the old domain's reputation (speculation).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue is booked under Merchynt: 13,000+ businesses, 1,000+ agencies (Merchynt's own claims). $100/month agency membership x 1,000 is about $1.2 million a year in membership fees alone (rough calculation from the claimed numbers, speculation), plus Paige subscription fees. localmarketingmanager.com has no separate revenue data.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results were almost all irrelevant (CVE vulnerability databases, local SEO tool rankings). The key evidence is the page source of the app subdomain (Paige title + Merchynt scripts), so the relationship judgment is medium-to-high confidence; but Merchynt's official site never says "localmarketingmanager.com is our white-label domain", so it is inferred from source code.
  • The old record said "the official site's body text could not be fetched"; this time it was fetched: the homepage has only one sentence, and the real content is on the app subdomain.
  • Merchynt's founder, founding year and revenue are unverified.
