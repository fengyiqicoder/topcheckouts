Basics

  • Product: online fax send/receive (iPhone / Android / Windows / web at app.mfax.to), plus document scanning, PDF editing and signing, dedicated fax numbers, email-to-fax; enterprise tier has HIPAA/BAA.
  • Operator: Octagon Lab. App Store seller is OCTAGON LAB - FZCO (Dubai free zone); the 2024-07 press release names OctagonLab Limited (Hong Kong); Android package is com.octagonlab.faxme; Trustpilot profile claimed 2025-12 with a Dubai address. Founder not found, no funding info; a typical utility-app portfolio company.
  • Brand confusion (important):
  • The original "mFax" was a fax product from a different US company; in 2025 it merged with mSign and mDrive and was renamed Documo (documo.com/mfax page: "mFax Is Now Documo"). This site is unrelated but shares the name.
  • "mFax: Send & Receive Fax" (WhatsMe Limited / mfaxfree.com) is also unrelated.
  • The site's blog claims it has been "on iOS/Android since the mid-2010s", with "5 million users", "2 million faxes" and a "4.8 App Store rating". This does not match an app that only launched in 2024; likely borrows the old mFax brand's history or exaggerates (self-reported, unverified).
  • Pricing: web consumers get the first 7 days free, then $7.99/month; also $0.99 one-time, $9.99/week and $19.95/month tiers (mentioned by Trustpilot users); iOS in-app purchases are $7.99–9.99 weekly, $19.99–29.99 monthly, $139–249 yearly; enterprise is $3/seat + $4/100 pages, 20% off annual.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Paid search 49.8%, organic search 33.6%, brand keywords only 30.1%; search terms include competitor faxzero, "free fax online" and "can you fax things through email" → new users come mainly from bought keywords + non-brand SEO.
  • SEO content matrix (how-to-fax-online, receive-faxes-online, online-fax-api) + free tools (cover page generator, document converter) + an "mFax" brand explainer page.
  • Bulk submissions to small launch directories for backlinks.
  • Web version sends faxes with no download; $0.99 / 7-day free trial lowers the first-purchase barrier, then converts to auto-subscription (confirmed by Trustpilot complaints).
  • Multi-store distribution (iOS / Android / Windows).
  • Speculation:
  • Residual "mfax" brand searches after the Documo rename are being captured by this site (timing fits: old brand renamed in 2025, this site registered 2025-09); traffic migration not directly verified.
  • No user-brings-user step; most users have a one-off need, so volume is continuously refilled via ASO + paid keywords + SEO.

Revenue and scale clues

  • About 6K App Store ratings (4.7), about 1.02K Google Play reviews; 19 Trustpilot reviews (4 billing complaints).
  • Blog claims 5 million users and 2 million faxes (self-reported and inconsistent with the launch date; low credibility).
  • Our checkout visits: 17,192 last month; at $7.99–19.95/month pricing, estimated monthly revenue in the tens of thousands of dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Medium. Operating entity, pricing and brand confusion have hard evidence; self-reported user numbers have low credibility.
  • Gaps: founder, real revenue, ad budget; Reddit was blocked and not checked (Reddit threads in search results are generic fax discussions, none mention this site).
  • Second pass: (1) newly found that the original mFax was renamed Documo in 2025, and this site registered mfax.to in 2025-09 and adopted the "mFax" name, possibly capturing the vacated brand searches; (2) takeoff method changed from "Long-tail search" to "Paid ads + Long-tail search", growth engine changed from "Search ranking" to "Paid ads" (paid search is nearly half); (3) added the $0.99 → auto-subscription funnel and billing complaints reflected in 19 Trustpilot reviews; (4) added web pricing of $7.99/month and the blog's self-reported numbers (flagged as unreliable).
