Basics

  • Product: HuskyApp, a Portuguese no-code app generator aimed at Brazilian knowledge-product sellers (infoprodutores), integrating with 30+ platforms such as Hotmart, Kiwify and Eduzz (after a student buys a course on those platforms, app access is activated automatically, per search summaries), with templates, the "HuskyIA" AI assistant, and a 14-day free trial; two entry points, "seller login" and "student login" (admin.husky-app.com).
  • Team: the site names no company or founder; the contact WhatsApp number is +55 61 (the Brasília area code). Funding Not found.
  • Note: unrelated to the Brazilian cross-border payments company Husky (husky.io, founded 2016, acquired by Nomad in 2022); searches are full of same-name noise.
  • Domain registration: 2025-01-30 (WHOIS; ScamAdviser also shows a 2025-01 registration). Wayback was unavailable this time.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The site embeds a Google Ads conversion tag (AW-17076472644), showing it runs Google search/display ads to acquire sellers.
  • The product structure is "sellers build an app → students log in with the app"; the site self-reports 3,000 apps for 1.4 million end users, so each paying seller brings in roughly 470 students' worth of traffic on average.

Speculation:

  • Student-side traffic (including login links in emails) could explain why "Mail" ranks second and Direct first in our data: sellers send students welcome emails/WhatsApp messages carrying a husky-app link.
  • Seller-to-seller word of mouth: Brazil's knowledge-product circle depends heavily on WhatsApp groups and mentor systems, and once a mentor recommends it, people migrate in batches.
  • The main country for checkout page visits shows as the United States, possibly a statistical bias from Stripe settlement/proxy IPs; the real users should be in Brazil.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Site self-reports: 3,000+ apps, about 1.4 million users, 1,000+ reviews.
  • Pricing: the site has a "pricing" block but does not show amounts directly; 14-day free trial. MRR Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: no new evidence found. The search results were all same-name noise (dogs, Husky payments, clothing), and re-crawling the homepage only confirmed 3,000+ apps / 1.4 million users / 1,000+ Google reviews and the "build an app for free" entry; added Kiwify and Eduzz as integrated platforms from search summaries. The conclusion is unchanged.
  • Public information is very thin: founders, legal entity, launch channel and pricing are all Not found.
  • The same-name Husky (under Nomad) caused heavy interference and has been ruled out.
  • Most of the growth loop is Speculation; the reason for the three months ago traffic drop is unknown (possibly ad budget or Hotmart policy changes, unconfirmed).
