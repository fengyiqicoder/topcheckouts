Overview

Husky-app (husky-app.com): No-code platform for creators to turn courses or products into branded premium apps (Brazil). Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 65,716 visits went from Husky-app to a checkout payment page — #27 of the 607 sites we track. That is 58% down from the April–June monthly average of 155,464.
