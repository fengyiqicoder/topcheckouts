Basics

  • Product: SnapGen, official tagline "Free unlimited ai video generator". The sitemap has video generation, speech generation, dialogue generation, image generation (imagen), music generation, pricing and contact, 15 URLs in total, with no blog/SEO content pages.
  • Pricing (third-party yix.ai hands-on test, 2026-10-10): there is a $0 free tier; Veo Max is $20/month, and "unlimited" actually caps at 300 per day, 1,500 per week and 6,000 per month, with a maximum of 8 seconds per clip; when not logged in, Veo 3.1 Fast costs 4 credits per clip and Grok 8 credits. Our profile: Image Max from $10/month, Grok Super from $30/month, $10 = 2,000 credits.
  • Relationship with GeminiGen: in search results the title of docs.snapgen.ai is "GeminiGen AI - Advanced AI Generation Services", and the snippet describes GeminiGen's image, video, speech and dialogue generation services, which matches snapgen.ai's product line (video/speech/dialogue/imagen) exactly. Judgment: snapgen.ai is a re-skin/sister site on the same backend as GeminiGen (strong speculation; the WebFetch of geminigen.ai failed to connect this time, so it could not be verified directly).
  • Team: Not found. Clues: (1) the earliest SSL certificate was issued by TrustAsia (a Chinese CA); (2) subdomains include admin / app-api / app-eks-api, h5 / cdn-h5, ul / link, watermark and so on, the standard setup of an "App + H5 + Web" overseas tool site; (3) the Reddit account u/Snapgen_AI already existed on 2024-02-19 (summary only), and TikTok @snapgen.ai has about 1,635 followers with content tagged aifilter / avatar, which belongs to the early face-swap app era. Speculated to be an Asian team going overseas, unconfirmed.
  • Beware of same-name confusion: snapgenai.org, snapgenai.io, snapgen.org (an API gateway) and snapgens.com all belong to others; the "SnapGen AI" entry on getlatka corresponds to snapgenai.io (marked as China, founded 2026), not this site.
  • Main markets: India 21.57%, United States 11.22%, Germany 8.29% (our data).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Brand keywords are 76.8% of search traffic, and the non-brand keywords are mainly "geminigen ai" / "geminigen" (our channel data); together with docs.snapgen.ai hosting GeminiGen documentation → the traffic is strongly tied to the GeminiGen brand.
  • Direct visits 57.7%: returning users come back every day to use their quota (the monthly plan design with a 300-per-day cap encourages daily return visits).
  • There is a watermark subdomain, so free output may carry a watermark; there are also link / ul deep-link subdomains.
  • Third-party tutorial/review sites (yix.ai, asimplifiedhub) are writing about it, and the brand keyword is being grabbed by copycat sites → there is organic word-of-mouth spread.
  • Speculation:
  • The key to the takeoff is the "free/unlimited Veo 3.1" price hook, spreading through YouTube/X tutorials and word of mouth in markets such as India and Brazil (social sources include YouTube, X and Facebook, but specific posts are Not found).
  • It may share a user pool with GeminiGen or send traffic to it (GeminiGen users searching and finding snapgen, or the reverse).
  • There may also be a small amount of paid promotion (referrals include adtrackr.site 0.39%).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Our checkout page data: 21,235 in July, 26,823 in August, 17,192 in September (September declined).
  • At $10–30/month pricing, and judging from the checkout visit volume, monthly revenue is guessed to be in the range of tens of thousands to a few hundred thousand dollars (speculation, not verified).
  • There is no public revenue or user count claim; the "$1" on getlatka is a placeholder and belongs to another site, snapgenai.io.

Confidence and gaps

  • Medium-low. Hard evidence: the certificate, the sitemap, Product Hunt, yix.ai's hands-on pricing test, and the GeminiGen title on the docs subdomain.
  • Gaps: geminigen.ai could not be reached this time, so the sister-site relationship is not directly verified; the first users and the specific event behind the 2026-07 takeoff (which video/post) are Not found; Reddit, Wayback and TikTok could not be scraped.
