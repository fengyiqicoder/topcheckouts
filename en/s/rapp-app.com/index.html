Basics

  • Product: online practice for the Belgian driving license theory exam (A motorcycle / B car): full traffic-rule explanations, 1,500+ questions at GOCA difficulty, realistic mock exams of 50 questions at 20 seconds each, a wrong-answer 'study coach', and training videos for the risk perception test required in Flanders. A web app (app.rapp-app.com), with a marketing site built on Framer.
  • Team: Tars Van Der Vaerent, Daan Van Isterdael, Nick Van Isterdael (three tech consultants); it started from Tars's MBA thesis at the Vlerick Business School (market research + interviews with test takers/parents). https://www.rapp-app.com/over-rapp
  • Company: Viinovate (Belgian KBO number BE1003...), with payments going through an Irish third-party processor (Stripe). https://www.rapp-app.com/privacy
  • Funding: not found.
  • Pricing: free to start (the full theory text + a free PDF + 1 risk perception test), Premium one-time €9.99.
  • Social: TikTok @rapp.rijbewijs, Instagram @rapp_rijbewijs.
