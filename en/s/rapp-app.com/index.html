Overview

Rapp-app (rapp-app.com): Dutch-language online platform for practicing Belgian driving theory exams and risk-perception test. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 32,562 visits went from Rapp-app to a checkout payment page — #78 of the 607 sites we track. That is 912% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,217.

Total website visits that month were about 20,908 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: search 43%, direct 43%, referrals/email/paid 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 35%, with queries like “meilleur site pour permis théorique belgique ? avis”, “perceptle test”, “theorie examen belgie rijbewijs b”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: €9.99 per 2 weeks (14-day access).
