Overview

Careerhound (careerhound.io): Job-search tool surfacing listings posted directly on company career sites instead of LinkedIn/Indeed. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 31,658 visits went from Careerhound to a checkout payment page — #79 of the 607 sites we track. That is 732% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,803.

Total website visits that month were about 208,872 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 15.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 48%, search 39%, referrals/email/paid 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 48%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
