Basics

  • Product: RPBuddy, "The Ultimate AI RPG Sandbox". Build worlds on hex tiles, with AI generating buildings, interiors, NPCs, lore and illustrations; NPCs have memory, spread gossip and keep schedules; turn-based combat, an economy system and equipment; nearly 400 original music tracks; four genres x multiple art styles; a Memory Web relationship graph; a three-level mature content switch. 18+.
  • Pricing (2026-10): Traveler is free (50 image credits per month, 50 AI actions per day); Adventurer $10/month or $100/year; Hero $20/month or $200/year; credit packs 50/$5, 100/$8, 300/$20. No free tier at launch: 7-day trial, then $10/$20 (LinkedIn article, 2026-07-23); the free tier was added afterwards (the Arcanum review on 2026-10-19 already says "free to start").
  • Team: Jacob Garner (engineer, founder, hunted it himself on PH) and his wife (handles community and marketing, unnamed). The two work on it in their spare time, with no employees, outsourcing or funding (founder's LinkedIn article). Country not stated; 56% of traffic comes from the United States.
  • Scale: Discord server (created 2026-04-02) with about 208 members; 19 public worlds in the community hub sitemap (2026-10-25).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Cold start: the founder posts in person in Reddit AI RP and chatbot communities, which counts as an "existing circle", but was blocked by community rules and "no free tier" (founder's own account).
  • Main engine: targeted Reddit ads in D&D and gaming communities, using visuals to attract non-AI players, with ads paying for themselves (founder's own account + rdt_cid + paid social being 44% of traffic).
  • Product side: the community hub pre-loads worlds with story hooks so new players can start playing immediately; narrative structure was strengthened based on feedback from churned players (founder's own account); public world pages /w/<slug> are in the sitemap.
  • Supporting: the blog does long-tail SEO, robots.txt welcomes AI crawlers; free memberships go to TTRPG review blogs.

Speculation:

  • The September traffic surge corresponds to "adding the free tier + increasing Reddit ad spend"; the free tier lowered the conversion barrier after an ad click.

Revenue and scale clues

  • On 2026-07-23 MRR passed $1,000 (founder's own account), with the $20 tier the most popular → roughly 50–100 paying users.
  • September checkout page traffic was 39K; Discord has only 208 members and there are only 19 public worlds, so community buildup is weak and it relies mainly on ads.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch time (late 2026-04) is corroborated three ways by WHOIS, Discord and the founder's own account, so credible.
  • Second follow-up check: found the founder (Jacob Garner and his wife, two people with no funding), the source of the first users (self-posting on Reddit → blocked by community rules → switched to ads), and July MRR of $1K; corrected the claim "ran ads from launch" (it was self-posting first, then ads), and added that "there was no free tier at first and it was added later".
  • Gaps: the original Reddit post would not open (only the repost was read); MRR after September, ad spend and the exact date the free tier was added were all not found.
