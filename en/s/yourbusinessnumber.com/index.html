Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: no founder account of the first users was found. What can be confirmed is that early on it went through B2B partnerships: estate agent Winkworth (with a branch case study) and recruitment and restaurant companies, each bringing in a batch of staff numbers; aimed at individual practitioners (estate agents, personal trainers, tradespeople, beauty and hair).
  • Where new users come from: (1) paid search 47.9% (profile), the largest source; (2) SEO 36.1%, with non-brand terms like virtual number for whatsapp and blog terms such as 'how to know if someone blocked you on whatsapp', brand keywords only 16.8%; (3) referrals/affiliates: 'Refer Us To Earn £20', with affiliate links in the?via=sandra form (Rewardful-style) showing up in search results; (4) the Teams version spreads within the same business.
  • Speculation: the founders' celebrity family background may have helped them get early press exposure and funding, but no specific coverage was found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2024: 10K monthly paying users (Startups 100). At £5.99/month, ARR is about £720K (estimated).
  • Trustpilot 331 reviews, roughly 4.4-5 stars.
  • £400K pre-seed.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass check: added the founders (George Lineker, Sebastian Lewis), the £400K pre-seed, 10K paying users in 2024, the B2B partnership channel, and evidence of affiliate links, and found the new brand domain yournum.com.
