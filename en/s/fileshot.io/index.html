Basics

  • Product: browser-side AES-256-GCM encryption before upload, with the key kept in the URL fragment; expiry / download limits / burn after reading, WebRTC P2P transfer, Archive Builder, a personal inbox at yourname.fileshot.io, portfolio pages, an API, a paid file download marketplace (sellers keep 91%), desktop / Android / browser extension, encrypted chat, 22+ online tools (video download, PDF, compression, conversion, etc.; about 280 tool landing pages in the sitemap), and a Tor hidden service.
  • Pricing: Free 50GB total / 10GB per file; Pro $10/month; Creator $24/month; Professional $38/month (HIPAA/BAA/SLA); also weekly billing at $2.50–9.50; 50% off the first month with code UNEED50 (from the listing on the Uneed directory).
  • Team: GraySoft (graysoft.dev), indie developer Brendan Gray, a University of Maine alumnus, solo developer, who also builds projects such as guIDE, Vidzilla and Secrypt (cross-linked in the footer). HN accounts GraysoftDev / fileshotdev / fileshotio. GitHub: github.com/FileShot/FileShotZKE.
  • Funding: none, a self-hosted solo project.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Search: the channel profile shows search at 77.5% and non-brand about 77%, with the main terms being video downloader types; the sitemap has several hundred blog posts + about 280 tool pages, the classic "tool pages eat the long tail" play.
  • Directory backlinks: listed on 58 directory sites, which also gives SEO backlinks.
  • Branded output: the share link itself is a landing page, and the recipient must visit fileshot.io; the yourname.fileshot.io inbox/portfolio pages and paid file pages bring buyers onto the site.
  • Referral: affiliate program with 30%/40%/50% lifetime commission.
  • Launch platforms did not work: two Show HN posts totaled 5 points and dev.to had 0 comments, so the first users did not come from there.
  • Speculation:
  • First users: directory sites (especially Uneed, with its dedicated 50%-off code) + long-tail traffic from the early SEO blogs.
  • July peak: possibly a video download / large-file sharing tool page ranking on Google, or a popular resource distributed through a fileshot link; the sitemap lastmod only shows the batch update of tool pages in September, so the July event cannot be confirmed.
  • Users arriving for the video downloader (India, Indonesia, Brazil, the Philippines) have low willingness to pay, so checkout visits are high but conversion may be low.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public MRR/user count. Trustpilot has only 3 reviews (4.0).
  • Checkout visits were 17K in September; at $10–38/month subscriptions, monthly revenue is estimated at the scale of a few thousand dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: changed the takeoff method from "Launch platform" to "Long-tail search" and the growth engine from "Branded output" to "Search ranking", because the HN/dev.to launches produced almost nothing, while the sitemap shows hundreds of blog posts in 2026-02~04 and about 280 tool pages in 2026-09, and non-brand search is 77% in the channel profile; branded share links were downgraded to a secondary loop.
  • Still missing: the specific trigger of the July peak; Google ranking data; view counts of the Chinese TikTok tutorial (TikTok cannot be scraped).
