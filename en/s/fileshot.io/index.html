Overview

Fileshot (fileshot.io): Zero-knowledge encrypted file-sharing service with free and paid tiers plus creator marketplace. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Fileshot to a checkout payment page — #168 of the 607 sites we track. That is 455% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,100.

Total website visits that month were about 185,842 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 9.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 78%, direct 17%, referrals/email/paid 3%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 78%, with queries like “download video from link”, “video downloader”, “link downloader”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
