Basics

  • Product: artist website + music download store. Yacht House Summer Vol 1-3 ($15 each, $38 for all three), Vol 3 DJ Mix ($10), Goodie Bags Vol 1-8 ($15 each, compilations of unofficial remixes), singles like "Ain't Nobody (Party Pupils Remix)" $1.99, full catalog of 13 releases $125; states "all music can be listened to online for free," and what you pay for is the download (for DJs to play out). Merch is on Fourthwall (Logo T-shirt $20, Yacht Girl hoodie $50), plus a tour page, order lookup and Party Pupils Radio.
  • Site builder: footer says "Built by Gigamesh," with the contact link pointing to gigameshmusic.com; Gigamesh and Party Pupils collaborated on "Michael McDonald - I Keep Forgettin' (Gigamesh x Party Pupils Remix)" (2025-09).
  • Artist: Party Pupils, Facebook bio "Yacht House pioneer and DJ/producer"; Instagram about 249K followers, 705 posts; Facebook 85,053 followers; X about 6K followers; Apple Music artist ID 1158467787 (profile created around 2016). Real name and location not found this time.
  • Domain: partypupils.com registered 2016-08-06 (GoDaddy; there is an older archive from 2010 belonging to a previous holder).
  • Our data: United States 100%; direct 48.2%, search 35.7% (brand keywords 83.9%: party pupils tour and individual remix names), referral 9.6%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Social short videos drive traffic: every remix video (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram) has a description pointing to partypupils.com to download; Facebook shows 115K "talking about this."
  • Brand search: of the 35.7% search, brand keywords are 83.9%, and the searches are "party pupils + song name remix" and "party pupils tour," showing people hear a song and then come looking for the download.
  • Touring: the tour page is one search entry, and live shows give exposure to fans and fellow DJs.
  • Mutual help within the circle: Gigamesh built the site and collaborated on a remix.

Speculation:

  • A large share of buyers are probably other DJs (who need WAV downloads to play out), who play Party Pupils remixes in their sets and bring new listeners.
  • The self-coined category label "Yacht House" lets him claim a spot in the yacht rock nostalgia wave, and the fame of the original songs does the spreading for him.
  • The starting point was the remix scene on SoundCloud/YouTube around 2016, from memory, not verified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Prices: singles $1.99-3, compilations $10-15, full catalog $125; merch $20-50.
  • Followers: IG about 250K, FB about 85K; our data shows only US traffic.
  • Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The store launch time (2026-05), prices, site builder, funneling wording and follower counts all have primary sources.
  • The search results provided were only the artist's own social media and a few irrelevant items, with no interviews or media coverage.
  • Gaps: the artist's real name and background, the specific viral videos and dates that made Yacht House take off, download sales.
