Basics

  • Product: large file hosting and downloads (up to 100 GB per file, advertised "unlimited bandwidth"). Free downloads have a countdown (about 15 seconds per a third-party tutorial), ads, and a speed limit (Trustpilot users report 150-300 KB/s). Pro removes the speed limit, countdown and ads. The share dialog offers File URL, HTML code, forum BBCode, iFrame embed, Watch and Download (already present in the 2019 snapshot).
  • Operator: the site only signs "© 2026 UploadHaven", with no company name or address. Founder, team, country and funding were all Not found, and the Trustpilot page is unclaimed.
  • Relationship with SteamUnlocked: the launcher page on gogunlocked.com states "The SteamUnlocked Launcher is built by the same team behind GOG Unlocked … Powered by UploadHaven", and you can log in to UploadHaven Pro directly inside the launcher. There is no direct evidence of the same owner, but the relationship is clearly very close (Speculation: same team or deeply tied).
  • Domain: WHOIS creation date 2017-12-07 (Namecheap).

Where the first users came from: the inference backed by evidence is redirect traffic from game download sites. In 2018 only webmasters could sign up, SteamUnlocked and UploadHaven both started in 2018, and both the fandom wiki and third-party tutorials say SteamUnlocked downloads redirect to UploadHaven. The exact date it began hosting for SteamUnlocked was Not found.

Growth loop

Evidence

  • Upstream site referrals: users click download on SteamUnlocked or GOG Unlocked, get redirected to UploadHaven, go through the countdown, ads and speed limit, and some of them pay for Pro. That is why Referrals is one of the top three channels, and the "embed_badge" mechanism corresponds to the HTML, BBCode and iFrame download embeds.
  • Launcher lock-in: from 2026-07 the launcher has built-in Pro login, Pro-only parallel downloads and renewal prompts, converting download-site users straight into Pro subscribers. The timing matches the September rise in checkout visits (about 107K, versus an Apr-Jun average of about 66K).

Speculation

  • Regular users share large file links (mods, saves, modpacks in Discord, Reddit and YouTube descriptions), and every downloader sees the Pro pitch, forming weak self-spread.
  • It does not grow on product word of mouth: Trustpilot rating is 1.3, and it mainly relies on the lock-in effect of "the content lives elsewhere and the download has to go through here".

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: Pro $2.99 for 3 days, $8.99 per month (official site).
  • The site claims 1M+ files shared and 99.9% uptime.
  • Our data: September checkout visits about 107K versus an Apr-Jun average of about 66K, a second round of growth for an old site, not a new takeoff.
  • MRR and user count: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The operating entity is completely opaque. "Same team as SteamUnlocked" can only be inferred from the launcher page.
  • The business depends on pirated game download sites (SteamUnlocked is widely seen as distributing unlicensed games). What TopCheckouts can borrow is the monetization structure of "free but throttled plus paid acceleration", not a direction to copy outright.
  • The growth milestones of 2019-2025 (for example SteamUnlocked's traffic changes) were not broken down year by year.
