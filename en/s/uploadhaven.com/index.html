Overview

Uploadhaven (uploadhaven.com): File-sharing service for uploading, sharing and downloading large files with a paid Pro tier for faster ad-free download. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 107,450 visits went from Uploadhaven to a checkout payment page — #12 of the 607 sites we track. That is 64% up from the April–June monthly average of 65,617.

Total website visits that month were about 2,737,658 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 3.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: referrals/email/paid 77%, direct 23%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source: other — mostly referrals from other websites (about 77%).

Pricing: $2.99 one-time (3 days); $8.99/mo.
