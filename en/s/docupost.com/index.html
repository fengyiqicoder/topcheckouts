David Nelson of Iowa (Nelson Ventures, LLC, dba DocuPost) bought docupost.com from HugeDomains sometime after 2017 and, no later than 2021-05, launched a US mailing service where you upload a document online and it prints, stamps and mails it for you. It posted on Product Hunt once in 2021 (89 votes, #15 of the day, little impact). Real acquisition comes from buying high-intent search terms like "mail a letter online": paid search is 52%, the top channel. At the same time it wedged into small businesses with "no subscription, no minimum volume, from 85–95¢ per letter" pricing, integrated with law-firm and finance software such as Clio, FileVine, QuickBooks, HubSpot and Zapier, and built industry landing pages for law firms, property management, healthcare and credit repair; existing business customers come back directly to top up and mail (direct 30.7%). The site claims to serve 100K+ businesses and to have mailed millions of letters.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: DocuPost, sends physical mail online: letters, postcards, checks, certified mail, Priority Mail; upload via the web or send in bulk via API/integrations, prepaid account balance, pay per item.
  • Entity: Nelson Ventures, LLC dba DocuPost, LLC (Iowa LLC), headquartered in Johnston, Iowa (Crunchbase says Waterloo, Iowa); LinkedIn shows 11–50 employees. Source: https://www.thegeeksclub.com/platforms/docupost/
  • Team: David Nelson (CEO & founder, PH account @davidjn), Nicolas Ancel (head of business development), Henri Dura (advisor). Source: https://docupost.com/about/
  • Pricing: free account, pay per item; letters from 95¢ (85¢ in 2023), postcards from 99¢, checks from $2; Premium $9.95/month, Enterprise $99.95/month; certified mail $10.25, Priority Mail $19.
  • Domain: RDAP registration date 2012-10-27; a GoDaddy parking page in 2011, redirected to a HugeDomains sale page 2013–2017; Wayback first captured the DocuPost product page on 2021-05-19.
