Basics

  • Product: Data Career Launch Program, a data-analytics career-change coaching program (Excel, SQL, Tableau/Power BI, Python, data storytelling + unlimited 1:1 coaching + an "analytics internship" + interview coaching + lifetime support); the landing page asks for name, phone and email to watch the training video, then "Apply Now to See if You Qualify" to book a call; no price is listed (https://datacareerblueprint.com/).
  • Founder: Kedeisha Bryan, MBA (Emory Goizueta 2023–2025), 37k followers on LinkedIn, based around Miami; says that about three years ago she was working in a warehouse and delivering pizza at $8/hour, and now "leads a community of 80,000+" (the landing page's own wording, not verified) (https://www.linkedin.com/in/kedeishabryan).
  • Company: Data In Motion, LLC; the LinkedIn company page says founded in 2022, 27,522 followers, headquartered in Atlanta, and its website field already points to datacareerblueprint.com (https://www.linkedin.com/company/data-in-motion-llc).
  • Old site: datainmotion.co (in Wayback 2023-03 it was still a GoDaddy for-sale page; from 2024-07 it is a bootcamp site "Launch a New Career in Data Without Going Back to School", with For Schools / For Employers / Weekly Challenges sections).

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: the founder's personal brand: 37k LinkedIn followers, 27k company-page followers, the YouTube channel "Your Data Career Coach"; her posts say she has helped "50+", "over 1,000" and "thousands" of career changers respectively (summarized from LinkedIn pages). Search traffic to the landing page is almost entirely the founder's name.
  • Speculation: the first students came from her early LinkedIn career-change stories and weekly data challenges (datainmotion.co had a "Weekly Challenges" section in 2024), with YouTube and Facebook promotion added later (social sources include Facebook; whether it is paid advertising is not verified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Personal-story content → followers → free training video (leave phone and email) → apply for a call → high-priced program, a typical VSL funnel.
  • Student results become content again: the LinkedIn company page reshares student projects and job-start posts, and the landing page carries student stories and "graduates at Microsoft / Roku / Cigna".

Speculation:

  • Paid ads probably push the VSL (landing page format, Facebook source, "Apply before spots fill up" copy), but there is no direct evidence.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Price not public; comparable "1:1 coaching + job guarantee" data career-change programs typically cost several thousand dollars per sale (from memory, not verified).
  • Scale figures are inconsistent: the landing page says an "80k+ community", LinkedIn posts say "1,000+" and "thousands"; LinkedIn lists 35 associated employees (probably mostly students/coaches affiliating themselves).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration time, the old site's evolution, founder identity and social media scale have primary page evidence; credibility is medium.
  • Gaps: search results were almost all unrelated, and no interviews or podcasts were found; YouTube subscriber and view counts were not obtained; program price and student numbers are only self-reported; there is no direct evidence of Data In Motion's early acquisition process (the first students) in 2022–2024.
