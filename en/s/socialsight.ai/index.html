The domain started as a content site on "AI products seen through social listening" (the SocialSight Media blog in February 2025, with a "What Reddit Thinks" column). Two months later it became a directory for "finding AI tools by job role", and in mid-2025 it turned into a multi-model AI image/video generator. "200 free credits a day, no card required" keeps people coming back daily to claim them, and it then follows the launch pace of new models like Kling, Seedance and Nano Banana with Instagram/Facebook/X/Discord operations and promo code distribution. Today 77.5% of traffic is direct, which suggests a base of returning users claiming credits is carrying it; there is no direct evidence of where the first users came from.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: an AI image and video generation site for creators, aggregating third-party models such as Kling, Seedance and Nano Banana, with a character factory, motion imitation, background swap, 4K upscaling, logo/anime generators, preset templates and a Media Vault (profile file, 80 URLs in the site's sitemap).
  • Positioning: multiple models + one-click presets, 200 free credits every day (Wayback 2025-09 homepage meta: "Get 200 free credits every single day... No credit card required").
  • Pricing: a third-party article says the basic plan is about $15/month (oreateai article); coupon sites list STUDIO30 (30% off) and a Facebook post gave out YOURSHOT (20% off).
  • Who built it: the site's About page only says "we started by analyzing millions of conversations to figure out what people want from AI", with no names. The 2025-02 blog posts are signed David Ko. A Miraheze community wiki says the founder is Fayd Speare (graduated from the University of Queensland in mathematics and statistics, Australian background, company in New York), registered in 2025, unfunded; the wiki has no primary source, so low reliability. The Facebook page lists New York as its address. On Product Hunt it calls itself "a small team of passionate AI trend lovers".
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2025-02-17; earliest Wayback 2025-02-23.
  • Current traffic (profile file): United States 53%, Brazil 12%, Egypt 3.5%; direct 77.5%; search 16.1%, of which 93.6% is brand keywords; social 3.4% (Reddit, YouTube, Instagram); referral 3.0%. The Miraheze wiki cites Semrush: about 635K monthly visits in 2026-03 and about 1.29M in 2026-04.

Where the first users came from: no direct evidence was found. What is visible: (1) from 2025-02 it spent a few months on "what Reddit thinks of an AI product" SEO content, which may have built a first batch of search traffic (speculation); (2) the Product Hunt launch got little response (6 votes), so it was not the takeoff source; (3) after the generator launched, "200 free credits a day" drove acquisition and retention, with US and Brazil users the main base, and Brazil's 12% hints at Portuguese-language community or short-video spread (speculation).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Daily free credits (200/day, no card) → users come back daily to claim them → direct traffic is 77.5% and brand keywords are 93.6% of search (Wayback homepage, profile file).
  • Marketing that follows upstream new-model launches: an X teaser for the Kling 4.0 integration; aggregates Seedance, Nano Banana and others (X summary, profile).
  • Promo code distribution: STUDIO30 and YOURSHOT appear on coupon sites and in a Facebook post (search summaries).
  • Third-party "review/alternatives" SEO articles (creativetoolsai, goenhance, media.io, LinkedIn posts) mention it a lot, calling it the "best value on Reddit" (search summaries).

Speculation:

  • Many of the third-party reviews read like affiliate or cross-promotion content and may be placed advertorials or from an affiliate program; not verified.
  • No evidence was seen that user-generated images and videos carry a brand mark that sends traffic back, so the branded output loop does not hold or is very weak.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Monthly visits in 2026 are in the 600K-1.3M range (Semrush relayed through the wiki, self-contradictory, for order of magnitude only).
  • Basic subscription is about $15/month; no revenue figures seen. The wiki says it is unfunded and lives on subscriptions and API contracts (no source).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were mostly generic "best AI social media tools" lists; the useful sources were the site's About page, sitemap, the PH page, Wayback homepage snapshots, RDAP, the miraheze wiki and one third-party Reddit roundup article.
  • The positioning shift (content site → tool directory → generator) has Wayback evidence; the generator's exact launch date (2025-06) comes only from the wiki, and the Wayback snapshots for 2025-06 to 07 are empty JS shells with no readable content.
  • The founder's identity (blog author David Ko vs. the wiki's Fayd Speare) is not confirmed by any primary source.
  • The takeoff event, original Reddit posts and the specific channel for the first users were not found; the "Piggyback on a platform" classification is inferred from its playbook of following hot model launches.
