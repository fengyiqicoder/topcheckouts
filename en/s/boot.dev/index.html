Basics

  • Product: interactive in-browser backend courses (about 30 courses on Python, Go, SQL, Docker, Kubernetes, Git, etc.), gamified with XP/levels/guilds/leaderboards; course content is free to read, interactive exercises are paid (about $199/year, with purchasing-power discounts by region).
  • Founder: Lane Wagner (HN account wagslane), previously a backend engineering manager leading a team of 3 with total payroll about $200K/year; the trigger was difficulty hiring Go backend engineers in 2020, and another motive was helping his wife Breanna switch careers (old report). Podcast co-host Allan is a team member. Team is in the Salt Lake City, Utah area.
  • Instructors: ThePrimeagen (ex-Netflix) and others.
  • Funding: $330K angel round from a former CFO introduced by an ex-colleague, date not given (Starter Story interview, relayed via millionairecopy / aftermvp).

First users: SEO on the founder's personal blog (Evidence, founder verbatim "We eventually grew through my blog"), reaching about $2K/month. Main source of new users: YouTube (founder verbatim "To this day, we mostly attract users via YouTube"), including sponsored creators and their own channel/podcast, with paid ads layered on from 2024.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Sponsored-creator loop: ask existing users "whose videos do you watch" to find creators → sponsor plugs, and supply ready-made B-roll to lower the creator's cost of working with them → new users arrive (relayed by aftermvp).
  • Content acquisition: the freeCodeCamp long course, their own YouTube channel ("purely for growth, never made money from it"), the Backend Banter podcast, guest spots on other podcasts.
  • Freemium: course content free, interactive exercises paid; gamification (XP, guilds, leaderboards) boosts retention; Discord community and word of mouth.
  • Paid ads on YouTube/Google/Meta from 2024, with marketing about a third of revenue.
  • Speculation: affiliate program/discount codes drive volume; completion certificates get shared on LinkedIn.

Revenue and scale clues

  • MRR path: about $2K (blog era) → about $10K → about $30K → $441K (2024-11, IH) → close to $1M (2025-04, Starter Story).
  • 2024 revenue $5.7M, profit about $2.5M (second-hand); 2025 revenue about 2x 2024 (founder on HN).
  • Our data: 17,192 checkout visits in September, April–June average 20,083, 2–3 months ago down to 12,741 / 9,217.

Confidence and gaps

  • MRR and 2024 financials come from aftermvp/millionairecopy relaying a Starter Story video, original video not watched; the IH original returned 403, summary only.
  • Exact dates of the freeCodeCamp course and the angel round not verified.
  • Second pass: takeoff year changed from 2021 to 2022, takeoff changed from "Launch platform" to "Creator or press spark" (evidence shows the blog and HN only got to $2K/month, and the real takeoff came from YouTube creator sponsorships); pure product-led changed from "Mixed" to "No"; added the $330K angel round, the MRR ladder, 2024 financials and the YouTube sponsorship playbook.
