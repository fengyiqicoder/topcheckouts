A classic "get paid to upload" file locker (PPD locker). The domain is an old one registered in 2002 and was still a "domain for sale" page in 2017; the operator (now DUCKIER INTERACTIVE FZCO in Dubai plus Duckier AG in Switzerland) turned it into a file host around 2017-2019, and by 2020 the homepage read "Make Money Uploading Files, No Files Deletion, Unlimited Bandwidth". Growth does not come from the product spreading itself but from paying uploaders: publishers on forums and resource sites (game cracks, Japanese adult videos, link aggregators such as keeplinks, etc.) put files on ddownload and earn a share by download volume, while downloaders are pushed by speed limits and waiting to buy an Ultimate membership. On top of that, Real-Debrid and multi-host account sites (multihosters) list it as a supported host, which gives downloaders a channel. Official figures put users at 200K in 2020, 500K in 2021 and 1M in 2023; in 2025 it handled 847K DMCA requests, which indirectly shows pirated-content distribution is the main source of traffic.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: file-hosting locker, uploads generate download links; free downloads are speed-limited with waiting; paid Ultimate (no speed limit, no waiting, parallel downloads, 200GB of traffic per day). Our records: €3.33/month (€39.99 billed annually); multihoster listings show €10 for 1 month, €40 for 6 months, €50 for 12 months (possibly old or reseller prices).
  • Operator: headquartered at DUCKIER INTERACTIVE - FZCO (IFZA business park, Dubai), payment entity Duckier AG (Zug, Switzerland, UID CHE-341.824.767), with servers in the Netherlands, the US, the UAE and Hong Kong (https://ddownload.com/about). Trustpilot gives an address on Queen's Road Central 183, Hong Kong. Founder name Not found.
  • Domain: registered 2002-02-01 (RDAP); 2002-2017 it was other people's parking pages (a "The Leading Download Site on the Net" domain-for-sale page in 2013, still a for-sale page in 2017).
  • Current state: the whole site was redesigned in 2026, with homepage copy changing from "get paid to upload" to "secure cloud storage, GDPR compliant"; it also published a transparency report and an S3-compatible API.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Uploader revenue share: the homepage said "Make Money Uploading Files" for a long time (2020 Wayback); an uploader complaint on smartcustomer says "only got 25% instead of 100%" (summary only, https://www.smartcustomer.com/reviews/ddownload.com), showing a plan that pays a share by downloads/sales.
  • Resource-site links: our data shows 64% referrals, from keeplinks.org, jpupskirts.club, jgirl.co and similar; search terms are specific resource names (game version numbers, video catalog codes).
  • Downloader monetization: speed limits plus waiting push people to buy Ultimate; multi-host services such as Real-Debrid and mega-debrid, and reseller sites like multihosters, list it as a supported host, bringing paying downloaders.
  • Resellers: resellers are allowed to sell memberships (multihoster pages); the mediakit provides embeddable ad banners for promoters.

Speculation:

  • The first users were probably resource publishers migrating from other PPD lockers (for example similar services that shut down or cut payouts); no direct evidence.
  • Japan and Germany make up 70% of traffic, matching the publisher circles of Japanese adult videos and German-language resource sites (German warez boards).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Official: 200K registered users in 2020, 500K in 2021, 1M in 2023.
  • In 2025, 847K DMCA requests and 2.4M files scanned per day (official transparency report).
  • Revenue comes from Ultimate memberships; exact figures Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The timeline mainly comes from the official About page (official figures) and raw Wayback snapshots; the two disagree on "founded in 2017", so Wayback is used and the actual launch is put at 2018-2019.
  • Search results were almost all generic download-tool articles; the beermoneyforum post returned 403, and Reddit was used via summaries only.
  • Gaps: founder/team behind it; uploader payout rates and rules (the official site no longer mentions "making money"); member count and revenue.
