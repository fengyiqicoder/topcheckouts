Basics

  • Product: AI ad creative generation (images, UGC video, product animations) + an "AI CMO" that automatically tests creatives and adjusts budgets in the Meta account + automatic social posting; built-in assistant Atlas; also a landing page CRM, SEO article generation and more.
  • Who built it: the About page names founder and CEO "Maj", who spent over ten years in e-commerce brands and performance marketing in Europe; legal entity in the footer is Alchevo, Inc. (larven.ai/about, /help/what-is-larven). Team size not found; the LinkedIn company page has only 19 followers.
  • Pricing (profile data): free trial includes $50 in credits; $99.90/month, $399/month; half price with annual billing. The affiliate page says the average customer pays $190 per month.
  • Domain: larven.ai registered 2026-01-06 (RDAP, GoDaddy); first Wayback snapshot 2026-08-23.
  • Traffic (profile data): US 100%; paid ads 54.1%, direct 39.9%, no organic search; search terms include "larven ai cmo" and the competitor term "predis ai affiliate program".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid ads: paid traffic is 54.1% (profile data); Facebook posts write the ad copy in the voice of a user testimonial.
  • Affiliate commissions: 20% lifetime recurring commission, 30-day cookie, settled through Rewardful, $50 minimum payout (larven.ai/affiliate);?via= referral links already appear in Wayback.
  • Trust signals: proactive Trustpilot review invitations + larvenreviews.com, self-built or affiliated, aggregating 1,040 reviews.
  • Content/SEO starting up: the blog and help center have been updated heavily lately (sitemap 2026-10-23/24), but organic search traffic is currently 0.

Speculation:

  • Dogfooding: it runs its own ads using creatives generated by Larven, so the ads double as product demos.
  • The founder's network in the European e-commerce scene brought early seed customers (hinted at by the About page, no direct evidence).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Affiliate page: average customer pays $190 per month; the estimator shows "brands: 1300" (meaning unclear).
  • One reviewer relays that the homepage once claimed "12k satisfied clients" (secondhand, unverified).
  • A rough estimate of 1,300 paying brands x $190 gives about $250k monthly revenue (speculation, the source of the number is unreliable).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results were mostly irrelevant generic AI-tool articles; the main evidence comes from the official About/Help/Affiliate pages, Trustpilot, RDAP and Wayback CDX.
  • The founder is known only by the name "Maj", with no surname, LinkedIn or interview; the registration location of Alchevo, Inc. was not found.
  • The relationship between larvenreviews.com and Larven is not disclosed, so the credibility of the review count is questionable.
  • Gaps: launch date, ad spend and the real number of paying users.
