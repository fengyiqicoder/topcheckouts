In spring 2024, when "GPT was no longer the only player", two French founders (Luc Bouvattier, Martial Garchery) built a €10/month aggregator subscription for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, Grok plus image generation. They launched on Product Hunt on 2024-06-07 (about 190 votes, not a big hit), and after that relied mainly on paid sponsorships of French-language YouTube (24 channels, 37 videos, 16 million subscribers combined) and French TikTok/Instagram creators, selling "cheap, made in France, one subscription for all models" to mainstream users in France and Germany.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: multi-model aggregator chat subscription (text, images, voice, web search, custom agents called "Projets Mammouths"), a Teams version launched in 2025-01, and an OpenAI-compatible API launched in 2025-07.
  • Company: MAMMOUTH AI (SIREN 932983968, registered in Paris on 2024-09-03). Directors Luc Bouvattier (CEO) and Martial Garchery.
  • Funding: not found.
  • Pricing: Starter €10/month (€12 incl. tax), Standard €20, Expert €60, plus a Team plan.
  • Positioning: individual users and small businesses in France, and also working on institutional partnerships with schools, city governments and the like (Telecom Paris alumni materials).

First users: the PH launch (about 190 votes) + the self-created Reddit community, at a small scale (evidence exists but small volume). After that, new users came mainly from paid sponsorships of French-language YouTube and social creators (evidence); Similarweb shows new users are almost all brand keywords and direct visits, meaning people watch a video and then search the name.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: paid YouTube sponsorship is the main channel (digitiz statistics); paid promotion by TikTok/Instagram creators (labeled #Publicité); the PH launch; institutional partnerships (schools, city governments) appear in official materials; rapid release of new models as a continuing talking point.
  • Speculation: French review-site "Mammouth avis" articles hold positions on long-tail terms like "ChatGPT pas cher / alternative"; the Teams plan spreads bottom-up inside companies; German traffic (18%) may come from German-language YouTube sponsorships (unverified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public MRR or user count. The checkout page gets about 20-30K visits a month, suggesting tens of thousands of paying users and MRR in the hundreds of thousands of euros (speculation, no evidence).
  • Trustpilot French site has 197 reviews (search summary); the English site's rating is low.

Confidence and gaps

  • The company registration, directors and PH launch date are credible; the scale of YouTube sponsorship has only one source, digitiz, with no channel names or dates.
  • Second-pass check: found that the PH launch was 2024-06-07 (the old report said "reportedly 2024-10", now corrected), the early spelling Mamouth and the self-created Reddit community, the Teams/API launch dates, TikTok/Instagram paid promotion and the expansion into the English-speaking market; the takeoff method was changed from "New-platform window" to "Paid ads" (the PH launch was small, and the ramp came from paid YouTube sponsorships).
