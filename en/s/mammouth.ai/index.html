Overview

Mammouth (mammouth.ai): French subscription giving access to many text, image and video AI models in one app from €10/month. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Mammouth to a checkout payment page — #198 of the 607 sites we track. That is 38% down from the April–June monthly average of 27,950.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 83%, search 14%, social 4%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)
