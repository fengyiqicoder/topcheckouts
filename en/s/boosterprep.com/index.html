Overview

Boosterprep (boosterprep.com): Online exam-prep study tools for DAT, medical, optometry and grad-school admissions. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Boosterprep to a checkout payment page — #286 of the 607 sites we track. That is 46% up from the April–June monthly average of 8,818.

Total website visits that month were about 206,654 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 6.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 88%, search 10%, social 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 6%, with queries like “dat crusher”, “dat bootcamp”, “datcrusher”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
