Basics

  • Product: an AI music video generator: upload a song, or use a cover image + song idea, to generate a beat-synced MV (vertical and other formats) in a few minutes; it can also generate AI songs from text (profile file, App Store description).
  • Who built it: App Store developer TUNESPHERE SG PTE. LTD. (Singapore); the contact information on Trustpilot lists Anguilla (where.ai domains are based; possibly filled in casually). Team members not found.
  • Pricing (App Store in-app purchases): VIP weekly $4.99/$9.99, monthly $29.99, yearly $59.99; Super Pro weekly $29.99; credit packs 800/$9.99, 3,200/$29.99, 8,000/$59.99; "MV Proof Creation" $14.99. The web gives new users 10 credits (heybop summary: "enough to write a script, not enough to make a video"). A YouTube review title says "$60 a month".
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2025-03-19; Wayback 2025-03-20 homepage is a "Launching Soon" placeholder (built on GoDaddy, with email signup); by 2025-07 the Wayback homepage is "Sondo Ai - Create full songs, vocals, and lyrics with AI - free on web & app"; the earliest visible iOS app version is 2.0.2 (2025-11-25).
  • Current traffic (profile file): United States 30.9%, United Kingdom 14.7%, Brazil 13.5%; paid search 44.8%, direct 40.3%, organic search 9%; brand keywords 45.7%, with suno and suno ai among the search terms; the only referral is msn.com 1.5%; social is Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp. last month visits were 8,596, down 53% month over month.

Where the first users came from: no direct evidence. What is visible is paid search (including the competitor term suno) and Facebook ads; the first users were probably Suno users bought through ads and independent musicians wanting videos for their songs (speculation). The App Store's 1.7K ratings suggest the app side has some volume too, possibly from Apple Search Ads (speculation, not verified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid search at 44.8%, buying Suno competitor terms (profile file).
  • Ad creative stresses "make your MV right now", and users are drawn in by the ads (Trustpilot reviews).
  • High-ARPU monetization via weekly subscriptions + credit packs, with a low free allowance (10 credits) pushing people to pay (App Store, heybop summary).

Speculation:

  • Generated MVs may be posted by users to TikTok/YouTube Shorts, possibly bringing back a little traffic through branding, but no watermark or evidence was seen.
  • Refund/overcharge complaints and the halving of traffic last month suggest ad ROI is falling or the budget is being cut.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Web monthly visits are in the tens of thousands or lower (8,596 in 2026-09), and the app has 1.7K ratings; revenue figures not found.
  • Pricing is high (weekly subscriptions at $4.99-$29.99), a "high-priced weekly subscription + ad buying" model.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Most search results were generic Suno-related articles; the evidence comes from Wayback, RDAP, the App Store, Trustpilot and the profile file. The heybop review article would not open (connection dropped), so only its summary was used.
  • sondoai.com (which claims "10M+ creators") is a different domain, possibly a copycat or SEO site, and is not counted.
  • The team, funding, first-user source and ad spend scale were not found.
