Overview

Eden (eden.so): AI workspace for creators to research trending posts, plan/schedule content and sell courses, coaching and email lists (. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,783 visits went from Eden to a checkout payment page — #336 of the 607 sites we track. That is 6% down from the April–June monthly average of 9,381.

Total website visits that month were about 273,671 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 3.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 55%, social 34%, search 7%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows branded free output, embed badge, public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 59%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $9/mo (Personal); $29/mo (Starter); $5 intro offer (30-Day Creator Sprint); P.
