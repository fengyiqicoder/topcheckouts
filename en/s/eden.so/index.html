Basics

  • Product: an AI workspace for creators. Save and highlight any content (links, videos, files, auto-transcribed and tagged, searchable down to a frame in a video), research viral content (Outliers, creator research, ad library, claims to index 3M+ posts), the AI assistant Eve, drafting in your own voice, scheduling posts to X/Threads/YouTube Shorts/Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/Facebook/Substack; a store (courses, digital downloads, coaching, calendar, forms, email marketing, Instagram auto-DMs, custom AI); MCP connection to Claude/ChatGPT; Chrome extension, iOS/Android companion apps.
  • Pricing: Personal $9/month, Starter $29/month, Pro $79/month, Max $199/month, Agency Lite $299/month, Agency $999/month; "30-day creator sprint" first month $5; store takes a 5% cut (per a third-party wiki).
  • Team: Kortex team page lists Dan Koe (co-founder and CEO), Matthew Ao (co-founder and CPO), Arihant Bapna (CTO), plus 1 designer, 4 full-stack engineers and 1 support person; legal entity Agentic Era LLC (third-party wiki). Bootstrapped, no funding seen.
  • Traffic (our data): direct 54.6%, social 34% (YouTube, X, Instagram, mainly Dan Koe's own content), search 6.8% (non-brand keywords include the free tool instagram transcript generator); referrals from kortex.co 1.58% and AI tool directories. United States 38.4%, United Kingdom 10%, India 7.9%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder audience: Kortex's waitlist and newsletter all hang under Dan Koe's name (Synthesizer Sunday, a writing list with "46,529+ people joined"); social is 34% of our traffic, mostly from Dan Koe's YouTube/X/IG.
  • Existing users carried over: Kortex lifetime and paid members moved into Eden first, and kortex.co still has 1.58% referrals.
  • Startup diary: Matt & Ari document the launch publicly on YouTube, which is content in itself.
  • Affiliate: 20% lifetime commission, with higher rates negotiated for influential partners.
  • Branded output: quiz result pages and store pages that users build in Eden carry "Built on Eden".
  • Comparison pages / free tools for search: Eden vs Buffer and similar comparison pages, instagram transcript generator.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 users were almost certainly Dan Koe's paid readers and Kortex lifetime members; after the store was added in 2026, Dan Koe selling his own courses on Eden acts as a demo for fans, who follow by opening stores.
  • The store plus commissions give course-selling creators a reason to bring their students into Eden too, creating a "creator brings students" spread, but current evidence only goes as far as the "Built on Eden" badge.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Kortex-era waitlist + lifetime presales about $760K (third-party wiki, not verified); Dan Koe's post admits Kortex revenue was "unsustainable".
  • Eden user count and revenue are not public; the "$100M" in the Matt & Ari video title is a goal / gimmick, not valuation evidence.
  • Prices run from $9 to $999/month; the agency tiers suggest a move up-market to B2B (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there is no earlier report in notes. Search results were mostly unrelated things like the Switch emulator and Celestia chain with the same name; evidence came mainly from Kortex's official migration post, the Kortex team page, Wayback (kortex.co 2023-2026, eden.so 2021-2026), whois, YouTube oEmbed, and Eden's website and affiliate page.
  • The Kortex -> Eden migration timeline and team list are credible; the Kortex presale amount and Dan Koe's follower count come from a third-party wiki or memory and are marked as not verified.
  • Gaps: Eden paying user count, MRR, and the specific posts and numbers from Dan Koe promoting Eden himself.
