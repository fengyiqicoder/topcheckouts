A small company in Catalonia, Spain built a 'one-click PDF to flipbook' tool. In 2017 it started as a free, ad-free, watermark-free converter that fed on long-tail SEO. In 2020-12 it relaunched as a subscription and went on AppSumo to get its first paying users; in 2022 it plugged into Canva's 'Share -> More -> Heyzine' and became a built-in exit inside the Canva ecosystem. After that, every flipbook that gets shared/embedded (cdnc.heyzine.com links plus the Heyzine mark on the free reader) is an ad, and the claimed user count went from the 90K range in mid-2024 to 2.5 million in 2026.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: PDF/Canva design -> online flipbook, shareable by link, embeddable on web pages, downloadable as an offline version, with interactivity (video, links, forms), read analytics, lead-capture forms, and custom domains.
  • Company: Heyzine Flipbooks SL (tax ID B56474539), address Eliseu Meifren 4, 43850 Cambrils (Tarragona, Spain); the about page says 'based in Barcelona'. The site has a Catalan version.
  • Founder: the AppSumo page signs the Founder as Albert Balada (signature updated 2020-12 / 2021-01); no other public material corroborates this.
  • Funding: no funding information found (speculated to be bootstrapped).
  • Pricing (2026): Basic is free (5 books, no ads, no watermark, but the reader carries the Heyzine logo, and removing it requires paying for 'White branding'); Standard $59/year; Professional $108/year ($14 monthly); Premium $228/year ($29 monthly); plus packs bought by number of books.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Output is the share: each flipbook is a public link / embedded iframe (cdnc.heyzine.com links are directly cited on HN and elsewhere), and readers who open it see the Heyzine reader; the free plan carries the Heyzine logo, and removing the logo is a paid feature (branded_free_output / embed_badge). 2.2 billion page turns a month = a huge exposure surface.
  • Canva app: 'Share -> More -> Heyzine' inside Canva brings in the huge base of Canva users making brochures/catalogs/portfolios directly (a 2025 Capterra user says 'a link directly in the Canva panel is a must').
  • Affiliate program: 20% recurring commission, 60-day attribution.
  • SEO: multilingual (English, Spanish, Catalan, German, French, Italian, Portuguese) + how-to content (digital catalogs, travel brochures, interactive PDFs).

Speculation:

  • The social sources Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn correspond to users posting flipbook links on those platforms (brochures, menus, magazines, annual reports), and readers then become authors.
  • Teachers/schools, real estate, and small business catalogs are the main audiences (Capterra reviews, outilstice education-focused write-ups), not quantified.
  • The 2026 surge in checkout visits may relate to a tightened free quota / Canva traffic / AI search recommendations, not verified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2.5 million users (self-reported 2026), 18 million books, 23 million monthly visits.
  • Revenue not found. Rough estimate: with 1-2% paid conversion and an order value of ~$100/year, ARR would be in the $2.5M-5M range, pure speculation.

Confidence and gaps

  • The company entity, the launch/relaunch timing, and the Canva integration timing have fairly strong evidence.
  • Gaps: the founder's background, team size, revenue, the reason for the 2024-09 jump in user count, and the reason for the 2026 surge; some Wayback CDX requests were rate-limited.
