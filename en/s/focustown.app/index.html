Basics

  • Product: Focus Town: Study With Friends (also named Focustown: Pomodoro Timer in the App Store, id6758457625), iOS, Android (wesh.apps.focustown), Web (focustown.app/app). Real-time multiplayer studying + a gamified Pomodoro timer, pixel town, character outfits, seasonal quests; the site has multilingual versions (including Traditional Chinese).
  • Team: Second Round Inc, two founders (from Korea and France, friends), names not found; investors/observers on X also said they "don't know who made it". Funding not found.
  • Monetization: core is free; ads (watch ads to draw gold beans), Pro $6.99/month or $39.99/year, gold beans $3.99–9.99.
  • Scale: the site shows 9,464 people online in real time, 1,037 rooms, 134 countries; App Store 4.8 rating / 8,400 ratings (appshunter).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: from 2026-04 a UGC matrix of study creators (12–15 people, reusing trending hooks + product demos), with viewers asking "what app is this" in the comments and looking for study buddies (Jibran's post, DarkUGC, 36Kr); TikTok #focustown has 1,622 videos.
  • In-product invites: inviting a friend to focus together gives both double rewards; inviting 5 people unlocks a scene; outfits are seen by others in the room.
  • Channel profile: direct 64.7%, search 26.4% (91% brand keywords), social includes Facebook Messenger (private chats pulling classmates into a room).
  • A Reddit user said they were drawn in by the "focus to earn outfits" mechanic (relayed by 36Kr).
  • Speculation:
  • The creator matrix is most likely paid or barter partnerships (the same accounts post repeatedly with a uniform formula); DarkUGC did not confirm the partnership.
  • Students post screenshots of their focus summaries on social media for secondary spread.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 550k+ downloads (mid-2026); about 9.5k online on the web; 8,400 App Store ratings.
  • An Instagram aggregator page says "reported $45k monthly revenue" (footnote source not shown, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • The UGC takeoff path has double evidence from the X post + DarkUGC play counts; the founders' names, the original link to the first viral TikTok and whether the partnerships are paid are not found. "Focus Arena", focus.town and lofi.town are different products.
  • Second-pass check: added the specific takeoff mechanism (a UGC matrix of 12–15 creators from 2026-04, up to 5.85M plays for a single video), the 2026-05 X observer post, and the lead on about $45k monthly revenue; classification unchanged.
