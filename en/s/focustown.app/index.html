Overview

Focustown (focustown.app): Social study app where students join live virtual rooms to work alongside others worldwide. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Focustown to a checkout payment page — #148 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 940,369 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 1.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 65%, search 26%, social 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows multiplayer rooms, and direct plus brand search is about 91%, plus shares via Messenger. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
