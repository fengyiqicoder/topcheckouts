Basics

  • Product: travel blog + long gluten-free/celiac travel guides (free), paid multilingual celiac translation cards (two rounds of translation proofreading), hand-drawn food-map posters/tote bags/T-shirts ($22–42), the book "The Food Traveler's Handbook" (2012), Patreon membership, tips; past income included freelance writing, photo licensing, paid speaking, Saigon/Oaxaca food walking tours ("Jodi Eats", discontinued), and six years as a G Adventures brand ambassador.
  • Founder: Jodi Ettenberg, McGill Law graduate, about five years as a corporate lawyer in New York; diagnosed with celiac disease in 2001; developed a spinal cerebrospinal fluid leak after a lumbar puncture in 2017, now mostly bedridden, chairs the board of the Spinal CSF Leak Foundation, lives in Ottawa, Canada; also has a personal site jodiettenberg.com.
  • Launch: blog founded in 2008 (About page); legalnomads.blogspot.com later showed "This page has moved to a new address"; the legalnomads.com domain was registered 2009-05-15 (RDAP), Wayback from 2009 to 2010-04 still shows a Network Solutions parking page, and the blog first appears 2010-05-14 ("Proving That Even Lawyers Can Have Fun", Myanmar and Thailand content).
  • Traffic (profile): US 36%, Canada 9.5%, UK 5%; search 55%, direct 33.7%, referral 6.7%; non-brand keywords eat pray love, puerto escondido, egg coffee, belize; social: LinkedIn, Reddit.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Long guides → long-tail search (55% search, mostly non-brand keywords) → celiac readers → buy translation cards → recommend the guides and cards on Reddit/forums (the JapanTravelTips thread).
  • Press coverage and talks bring brand exposure and paid-speaking income.
  • Newsletter brings back existing readers (profile).

Speculation:

  • The celiac community is high-trust and high-repeat (one card per country visited), and word of mouth spreads among fellow patients.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 29,000+ translation cards (unit price not checked; from memory around $10, unverified).
  • Food-map merchandise $22–42; Patreon member count not checked.
  • Historical income: freelance writing, speaking, food tours, six-year G Adventures brand partnership.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Founding date, site migration date, press/speaking and translation-card sales all come from the About page and Wayback, so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: early traffic/subscriber numbers; press articles not opened one by one; Reddit threads summary only.
