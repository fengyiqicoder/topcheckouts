Basics

  • Product: link-in-bio tool that turns a liinks.co/name into a customizable mini-site (links, images, video, forms, Instagram sync, multiple tabs, folders, custom domains, QR codes, an AI Chat beta feature). Also a free side tool, mockups.liinks.co (AI-generated phone mockups, "from the team at Liinks"); in 2023-03 it launched "Liinks: Convert" on PH (AI-driven website-to-link-in-bio conversion).
  • Who built it: the Starter Story profile lists founder Charlie Clark (X @clarkcharlie03), solo founder; Trustpilot lists the company as US-registered, with reviews praising support from "Charlie." The only review on PH is also from Charlie Clark. Team size not found.
  • Launch date: first Wayback capture 2020-03-02, titled "Free landing page for your links, with super powers."; Product Hunt launch 2020-02-26. From 2021 the title changed to "Supercharge your link in bio," and in 2025 to "Centralize your online presence." RDAP returned no registration date for.co.
  • Pricing: Starter $5/month or $48/year; Pro $12/month or $120/year (sold in packs of 5 pages); no free tier, 14-day trial.
  • Current numbers: the site claims "10,000+ creators & businesses"; Trustpilot 82 reviews at 4.6 (profile claimed 2020-09); Instagram @liinks.co 2,755 followers.
  • Traffic (profile): paid social 48.7%, organic social 35.1% (Instagram/YouTube/X, mostly clicks on users' pages), direct 9.7%, referrals google.com 6.4%, bit.ly 3.8%; United States 33%, Germany 11%, Canada 7%.

Where the first users came from: the only evidence is the 2020-02 Product Hunt launch and the "free" positioning at the time; the PH numbers are small (23/171), so the launch was not a big hit. Later users came mainly from paid ads (Starter Story lists Google Ads and Instagram Ads; the profile shows paid social at 48.7%) and passive exposure from users' pages. The specific source of the first 1,000 users not found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: every paying user's page lives at liinks.co/name and sits in their social bio, where visitors click it (organic social 35%); pages carry a "Build yours at liinks.co" style prompt (official example page liinks.co/liinks).
  • Paid ads: paid social at 48.7% is the top traffic source; the Starter Story tool stack lists Google Ads and Instagram Ads.
  • Regular Product Hunt posts for new features/side products (2020, 2023, 2026) and the free tool mockups.liinks.co for backlinks and exposure.
  • Proactive Trustpilot review invitations; reviews often mention "switched from Linktree / Milkshake / Linkinbio."

Speculation:

  • The paywall keeps the user base small but revenue steady; $48–$120/year annual plans absorb the ad acquisition cost.
  • "Linktree alternative" searches bring some new users (the profile notes search terms containing linktree), but not a large share.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Starter Story estimates about $32K/month (2026, third-party AI aggregate, unverified).
  • The site claims "10,000+ creators & businesses." At $48–$120/year, 3,000–5,000 paying users would be roughly $15–40K/month, consistent in magnitude with the estimate above (our estimate).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Most Google results are irrelevant; the useful sources were the official site, the Product Hunt product page, Trustpilot and Starter Story. Reddit and X could not be opened; Wayback snapshots only captured page titles (single-page app).
  • Founder Charlie Clark comes only from Starter Story and circumstantial PH/Trustpilot evidence; background not found.
  • When it switched from free to paid-only, and where the first 1,000 users came from, not found.
