mde.tv is the own paid site of the comedy group Million Dollar Extreme (Sam Hyde, Nick Rochefort, Charls Carroll, founded 2009), and it runs on the fan base the group built over more than a decade, not on the site acquiring users itself. The group started on YouTube; in 2013 Hyde's fake TEDx talk went viral; in 2016-08 World Peace aired on Adult Swim and was cancelled the same year over "alt-right" controversy; in 2018-05 the YouTube channel was permanently banned and in 2018-09 the subreddit was banned. Once mainstream platforms pushed them out, fans could only pay at their own place: in 2018 mde.tv briefly expired and was up for sale, was later reclaimed, redirected in 2023–2024 to a Gumroad "MDE.TV High-Roller Paywall" membership, became a self-built site in early 2025 (tour tickets, presales for the new show Extreme Peace), and launched iOS/Android apps in 2026-04. New users come from Sam Hyde's various exposures: the Fishtank reality show, tours, fan-cut clips on X/YouTube; direct is 83.6%, essentially fans returning.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: MDE.tv, Million Dollar Extreme's video/audio/article site: old sketch collections, The Sam Stream Odyssey, The Sam Hyde Show, Extreme Peace (a new 6-episode 30-minute show), a music player, mini-games ("Gadgets"), articles (Screeds), a merch store, donations; a paid Producer area; billing at billing.mde.tv; also tv.mde.tv.
  • Entity: MDE.TV Inc. (App Store developer name).
  • People: Sam Hyde, Nick Rochefort, Charls Carroll; Erick Hayden joined for the new show.
  • Pricing: not found (monthly membership in the Gumroad era; a Reddit post says the first month may have been free, summary only).
  • Domain: RDAP registration 2011-01-21; MDE's official site 2011–2012; replaced by spam content 2014–2017; showed "domain may be for sale" in 2018-01; redirected to Gumroad from 2023-03; self-built site from 2025-03.
  • Users: United States 74.4%, Spain 6.1%, United Kingdom 4.8%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Fans returning: direct 83.6%; search 6.7% is almost all brand keywords (87.7%).
  • Built its own paid channel after platform bans: banned from YouTube (2018) and Reddit (2018), moved to Gumroad membership, then to a self-built site and apps.
  • Cross-promotion: Fishtank viewers discuss whether to subscribe to MDE.TV; tours and new-show presales sit on the mde.tv homepage.
  • Social 8.1% (YouTube, X, Instagram), email 1.6%.

Speculation:

  • New fans come mainly from Sam Hyde clips and memes on X/YouTube (the notoriety from long-running internet memes like "Sam Hyde is the shooter"), then pay at mde.tv; no direct data.
  • The site itself has no acquisition mechanism; this is "a creator with an audience moving fans to an owned platform".
