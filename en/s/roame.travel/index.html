Overview

Roame (roame.travel): Search tool for award flights and hotel stays using miles, transferable points and member hotel rates. Category on TopCheckouts: Consumer services.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Roame to a checkout payment page — #184 of the 607 sites we track. That is 46% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 225,034 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 7.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 78%, search 18%, referrals/email/paid 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 78%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: Friends of Roame $9.16/monthly; Friends of Roame $109.99 billed annually (原价 $155.88); Inner Circle $25.00/monthly etc..
