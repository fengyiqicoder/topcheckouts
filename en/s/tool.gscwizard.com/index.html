Growth loop

Evidence:

  • GEO ranking: lots of "best SEO MCP servers / best AI tools for GA4 / connect GSC to ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini" pages with its own product ranked first, cited by AI assistants as answers (GEO 40%).
  • Grabbing new AI platform entry points: ChatGPT App, Claude/Codex MCP, Muse connector, OpenAI Dots, an OpenClaw plugin and more; every time a new AI platform appears it builds a connector and a landing page.
  • Free tool pages (/tools/quick-wins, content-decay, cannibalization, etc.) route traffic to the 7-day trial.
  • Review platforms (G2, Trustpilot, OMR) build social proof, and some reviewers got an extended free trial (stated by one Trustpilot review).

Speculation:

  • The founder's influence in the SEO scene on LinkedIn was probably the main early distribution channel (his X account is almost inactive, and LinkedIn could not be read).
  • The self-reported user count grew from 2,800 to 5,750 within this month; if true, MCP/GEO converts efficiently, but how it is counted (whether trials are included) is unclear.

Revenue and scale clues

  • €17–349/month subscriptions; self-reported 5,750+ SEOs and 88,650+ sites (whether trials are included is unclear). At a 5% paid rate and a €39 average price, roughly €10K/month, a pure estimate, not verified.
  • No funding seen.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The Google results were entirely taken over by the band "Tool"; this report's evidence comes from the official site, sitemap, reviews page, RDAP, Wayback CDX and fxtwitter.
  • Gaps: the founder's LinkedIn posts (the most likely launch channel) could not be read; the Medium self-promotion article was not opened; the exact launch date is known only from the domain and Wayback dates; user counts are self-reported.
