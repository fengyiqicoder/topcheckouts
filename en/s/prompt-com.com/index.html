Overview

Prompt-com (prompt-com.com): Aniborn (formerly PromptCom): Japanese creator platform for posting AI illustrations, manga, anime and prompts with mone. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Prompt-com to a checkout payment page — #293 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 167,678 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 7.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 70%, referrals/email/paid 20%, search 6%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, public profiles / leaderboards, referral rewards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 74%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: points purchase (amounts not shown); 20% platform fee (15% for selected creator.
