Basics

  • Product: AI text humanizing rewriter (Hue V4 engine, Ultra Mode, 7 tones); a Chrome extension can rewrite inside any web input box and simulate typing; claims to support 100+ languages and to pass GPTZero, Turnitin, ZeroGPT, Originality.ai, Copyleaks, Scribbr and others.
  • Pricing: Basic $5.99/month, Pro $19.99/month, Max $39.99/month (chimpwrite says there is no free tier; the profile records a free trial).
  • Team/company: the site only credits "Hue Write ™" with no address; the Chrome Web Store developer is listed as a "non-trader", email support@huewrite.com; GitHub organization huewrite. Founder Not found (not the focus).
  • Predecessor: the site says "Previously Lucid Writer ™", and the Chrome extension description says "formerly called Lucidwriter".
  • User counts are inconsistent: the homepage in the Google snippet said "trusted by 1,553,000+ users", the current homepage says "50,000+ Users"; the Chrome extension has only 846 users and 3 ratings.
  • Current traffic (profile): US 40%, Germany 18.6%, Philippines 8.6%; direct 52%, search 35.9% (brand keywords 80.8%), referrals 7.2%; social sources YouTube and Facebook Messenger.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Programmatic SEO: the site has "bypass a given detector" pages like /zh/bypass/writer, including Chinese and other language versions, targeting the "bypass + detector name" long tail.
  • Review/affiliate site endorsements: detectiondrama clearly uses affiliate links; WorthEPenny has a coupon-code page.
  • Low-price anchor: review sites repeatedly mention $5.99/month as "cheapest in its class".

Speculation:

  • The first users came from migrating old users of the predecessor Lucid Writer plus long-tail search; the current 80% brand search suggests people saw the name in reviews and YouTube videos and then searched for it.
  • The high share of Germany and the Philippines may relate to the multilingual pages or to the Philippine ghostwriting / content outsourcing scene; unconfirmed.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscription $5.99–39.99/month; official user counts range from 50K to 1.55 million, low credibility; Chrome extension 846 users.
  • Revenue: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The predecessor Lucid Writer's domain, launch date and growth process were all not found, so "where the first users came from" can only be speculated.
  • No Reddit discussion, founder interview or Product Hunt launch in the search results.
  • The product is a gray-area use that helps students evade AI detection, and third-party tests found its effect mediocre.
