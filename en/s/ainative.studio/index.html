Basics

  • Product: OpenAI-compatible API connecting 65+ open-source models (Kimi K3 at $0.28/M input, GLM-4.6, MiniMax M2, DeepSeek V3.2), plus ZeroDB persistent memory, 69+ MCP tools, Cody CLI, AI Kit (React components), Agent Cloud, and the latest Intent-Casting Marketplace (based on the Beckn protocol). It also runs a sub-site, builder.ainative.studio, selling an "AI co-founder".
  • Pricing: Hobbyist $5/month, Pro $49, Business $149, Enterprise $999, "Cody Virtual CTO" $4,999/month; 3-day trial, 7-day refund. In 2026-06 the GitHub repo ainative-openai still said "free use of Llama 3.3 70B, free tier no credit card", which suggests there used to be a free tier that was later replaced by a trial plus $5.
  • Who is behind it: Toby Morning, founder and CEO. He was the first employee at IdeaMarket, incubated by Bill Gross's Idealab, and was a mentor at Techstars Tulsa in spring 2023. The team also includes Karsten Wade (head of developer relations, titled "Head of Vibe Coding"); the "CTO Cody Jackson" is actually an AI Agent. The company entity is AI Native Lab Inc; Santa Cruz Works reports it is in Santa Cruz, while LinkedIn says San Francisco and Tulsa. Funding: bootstrapped all along; started its first round in 2026-06 and has opened an investor deal room.
  • Launch date: GitHub organization created 2025-05-31; Santa Cruz Works says "started building AI infrastructure from September 2025", which can be read as a pivot to the API and memory platform in 2025-09.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Offline events: startup camps, hackathons, Speakeasy events; Cody handed out stickers at hackathons.
  • Community: Discord, WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn newsletter, AINative Live.
  • Open-source repo traffic: hundreds of public repos, such as ainative-openai and ainative-openai-python, pitching "free open-source models + drop-in OpenAI replacement".
  • Listed in third-party AI tool directories, such as withai.top.
  • Channel data: direct 62.5%, search 28.1% of which 90% are brand keywords ("ainative studio api key"), display ads 5.6%.
  • Speculation:
  • Users concentrated in India, Bangladesh and Nepal suggest the cheap open-source model API has word of mouth among South Asian developers. It may come from freebie posts like "free/cheap Kimi, GLM API" or YouTube tutorials, but no specific post was found.
  • Display ads may be a small-scale buy.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site claims 5,600+ developers and 99.9% uptime.
  • The team is 2 people plus AI Agents, bootstrapped, and only started raising in 2026-06, so revenue should be small.
  • In our table this site's August/September numbers are exactly the same as esim.gg and myclaw.ai (4,919 / 21,490), likely tiered estimates from the data source; real traffic may be much smaller.
