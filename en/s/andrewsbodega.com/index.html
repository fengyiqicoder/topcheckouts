  • Product: One Click Color Grade (DaVinci PowerGrade/LUT, color grade bundle $59), audio plugins/presets $17, Creator Blueprint course $79 (profile), each a one-time purchase. There is also a course site, creator-blueprints.com.

  • YouTube: 312K subscribers, 33 videos (channel page fetched 2026-10).
  • Traffic (profile): Brazil 56%, India 19%, US 16%; social 64.3% (mainly YouTube), direct 26.7%, search 5.3% (brand keywords).

Where the first users came from: viewers of his own YouTube channel. A breakout video in 2024-04 brought subscribers, the color grade style shown in the videos is itself the selling point, and the first line of the description sends them to the store. Note: the 2024-04 video description already contains an andrewsbodega.com link but the domain was only registered on 2024-05-22, so the description was edited later.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Video as ad: every video uses his own color grade, with the description "Colorgraded with my One Click Color Grade" + a link; tutorials such as "The power of SIMPLE Colorgrading" directly promote the product.
  • Free audio presets traded for email addresses (2024-10 description), then courses sold by email.
  • Brand sponsorship (Brick) is another source of income.

Speculation:

  • The high share of Brazil/India traffic may be viewers there buying low-price LUTs or being drawn by the affordable pricing, or may relate to how YouTube recommends in those regions (unverified).
  • The product itself has no spreading mechanism; growth depends entirely on the channel.

Revenue and scale clues

  • YouTube 312K subscribers; three videos over 1 million views.
  • One episode is entirely about earnings (2025-10, "How Much Money I made"); its content was not opened.
  • The NewsBreak advertorial's "five figures a month" and "10,000+ students" are unverified and low in credibility.

Confidence and gaps

  • The site is written from scratch. The subscriber timeline comes from the original YouTube video descriptions, so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: actual store sales; the specific numbers in the earnings video; why the Brazil share of traffic is so high.
