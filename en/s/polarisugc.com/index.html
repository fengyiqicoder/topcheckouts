Basics

  • Product: An e-commerce AI creative studio with features including AI UGC video ads from a product URL, AI actors, cartoon ads, auto captions, viral ad cloning and a free UGC script generator; can be connected through the Claude MCP (polarisugc.com/api/mcp); models include Seedance, Kling and Nano Banana.
  • Company: The footer copyright says "Maverick International Enterprises LLC"; founder, country, team size and funding were not found (the About/Careers links are # placeholders).
  • Social: X @PolarisUGC (account registered 2022-07-04, 197 followers, likely an old account renamed), Instagram @polarisugc (510 followers, 36 posts), YouTube @PolarisUGC.original (does tutorials like "How I make winning ads for Q4"). The official social accounts are all tiny.
  • Pricing: first 12 free, no card needed; Starter $84/month (or $99/quarter), Creator $169/month, Max $340/month, 30% off yearly (https://polarisugc.com/pricing); another version is $49/$98/$350 per month (1,500/5,000/17,500 credits).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Affiliate program: referred stores pay a 30% commission on every payment, "forever"; you don't need to be a paying user yourself; settled through Whop with a 30-day hold period (https://polarisugc.com/refer).
  • Promo links have appeared in the tutorial descriptions of third-party YouTubers (D Scalez, /r/PLR-... code with a limited-time 50% off).
  • The official accounts have very few followers, yet social traffic is 23.5%, meaning social traffic comes from other people's accounts.
  • The site claims "$750M+ in ad spend runs on Polaris" and "#1 e-commerce marketing tool", which is marketing talk.
  • Search is only 4.7%; the SEO blog hasn't kicked in yet.
  • Speculation:
  • Germany first, Philippines second: German-speaking e-commerce creators (such as Frank Wieler) bring volume, and the Philippines may be an agency/virtual-assistant crowd.
  • Generated ads run on TikTok/Meta without branding, so the output doesn't spread on its own; growth relies almost entirely on commissioned creators, so it rose fast and fell fast (37k two months ago, 20k last month).

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public MRR or user count; Trustpilot has only 2 reviews. From 20k–37k checkout visits over the past 1–2 months and a ticket size of $49–340/month, monthly revenue might be in the tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars (pure speculation).
