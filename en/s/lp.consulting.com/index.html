Basics

  • Product: The Side Hustle Challenge, "Make Your First $2,000 Online (Without Investing Thousands To Get Started)". Includes: a 45-day challenge (pick a digital product, build it, make the first sale, with a private peer group; top 20 win lifetime Monetise access), Digital Launchpad (10+ business-model courses, originally $444/year), AskIman AI ("10 years of my experience after generating $100M+ online, compressed into an AI"), and Russell's Bootcamp recordings.

Current Consulting.com homepage: "Established 2016 | 23,000+ Clients Served", products Monetise / UpLevel / Quantum / Mastermind. In 2025-07 the homepage read "New Ownership. New Era. Where the first users came from: Iman Gadzhi's existing audience, i.e. YouTube (the only social source), the email list (email 29%) and the WhatsApp waitlist group; plus roughly 11% paid ads. The high India/Pakistan share matches its "low-cost online side hustle" audience.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Launch funnel: content → waitlist group/email list → limited-time stepped price increases → low-ticket entry product → the contest prize Monetise (the high-ticket flagship course) as the upsell hook.
  • The contest mechanic (top 20 get Monetise free) motivates students to post results, which can be reused as case studies.

Speculation:

  • After each challenge, the "conversion results" get turned into YouTube/ad material and the next cohort opens, with word of mouth inside the student group bringing in new users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Unit price $145–$195+; no sales count. The Consulting.com homepage claims 23,000+ clients; the AskIman copy claims "generating $100M+ online" (self-reported).
  • The old consulting.com (pre-handover) claimed 20,000 active students, $690M in student revenue, and 3,758 reviews at 4.9 stars.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Landing-page copy, launch dates, price ladder and site structure all come from the original pages, so credibility is high; "Iman Gadzhi took over Consulting.com" is inferred from Wayback homepage text, with no official announcement link seen; "Ali" was not identified.
  • Gaps: sales volume, ad platforms, details of the ownership deal.
