Basics

  • Product: a free quality-of-life client for Minecraft Bedrock Edition (DLL injection + launcher), 140+ optional modules, HUD, FPS tools, replay mod, Lua scripting; supports Windows, Linux, Android, and a macOS launcher (Apple Silicon). The core DLL is open source (github.com/flarialmc/dll-oss, 218 stars).
  • Team: the site's team page lists 43 people (executives bari2d, raspberryepicly, wh8te, ashanki, oanshul; development 15, marketing 9, events 6, design 8, volunteer support 18). In 2025 the site credit read "Made by Ashank with help from ttf | In partnership with Sear Hosting"; in 2024 it read "Made with ♥️ by MBG1337". Copyright holder Flarial Consulting LLC.
  • Monetization: client is free; Flarial+ $3.99/month; capes $6.99–$7.99, nameplates etc. $3.99; ads on the website and the Android launcher; partners (such as Sear Hosting).
  • Domain: flarial.xyz registered 2024-08-02 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2024-10-01. The Discord server was created 2022-12-07 (derived from the guild ID); the GitHub organization flarialmc was created 2023-05-02 (the launcher and dll-oss repos were created the same day).
  • Traffic (profile file): US 26%, India 15%, Brazil 12.7%; search 47.5% (brand keywords 77.4%, non-brand such as "client minecraft bedrock"), direct 39.4%, referral 7.9%.

Where the first users came from: the Discord community came first (2022-12), and the early site's reviews were all taken from active Discord users, indicating the first users joined Discord by word of mouth in the Bedrock PvP player circle (evidence: site copy; how the first batch of people was drawn in was not found). After that, new users mainly came from Bedrock YouTubers' client reviews/tutorial videos (evidence, see above) and from searches for "minecraft bedrock client" (profile file).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • YouTuber reviews: several Bedrock creators made "best Bedrock client" videos with 45K-158K views; the homepage embeds these videos directly.
  • Discord community operations: 160K members, with 9 people in marketing, 6 in an "events squad" and 18 volunteer support staff on the team; the site's reviews, FAQ and support all go through Discord.
  • The official YouTube (@flarialclient) and TikTok (@flarialofficial) post release videos for each version, and the Android launch video got 110K views.
  • Platform expansion: Windows→Android→Linux/macOS, with each new platform being a launch event.
  • Open source + script marketplace: community contributors write modules and scripts.

Speculation:

  • Cosmetics such as capes and nameplates are seen by others on servers, which is itself a form of branded output (whether cosmetics are visible to non-Flarial users was not verified).
  • Teen players recommend it by word of mouth at school and among friends.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Discord about 160K members (2026-10).
  • Income comes from the $3.99/month subscription, $4-$8 cosmetics and ads; scale not found.
  • GitHub dll-oss has 218 stars and 78 forks.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The search results were almost entirely unrelated health/scam-checker pages, so evidence was filled in mainly from the site's about/team/changelog, the GitHub API, the Discord invite API, Wayback and YouTube page data.
  • Gaps: the founder's real name and founding story, the old domain/reason for the pause in updates between 2023 and 2024, download counts and paying user count were not found.
