Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Where new users come from: (1) Affiliate commissions: WHMCS referral links dmit.io/aff.php?aff=xxx are all over review blogs (vps234, idcspy, zhujiceping), batch GitHub repos and Google Sites; (2) Word of mouth in Chinese tech communities: in referrals linux.do is 3.27% and dmitea.com 2.82%, plus NodeSeek restock monitoring posts; (3) Brand keyword search: search is 20%, of which brand keywords are 89.3% (dmit, dmit官网), and direct is 73.4%, meaning most people hear about it in the community and come straight to the site; (4) Scarcity marketing: limited plans are only released on restock or Black Friday, and users rush to buy and resell them on their own.
  • Tailwind from new demand: in the AI era users need "clean US IPs to access services like OpenAI", and DMIT is a popular option (the LowEndTalk abuse warning thread is indirect evidence).

Speculation: The first users came from 2018 discussions of "return routes" in Chinese VPS forums such as hostloc; the launch post was not found. The batches of GitHub review repos appearing in 2026 are an SEO farm run by affiliate customers, not an official action.
