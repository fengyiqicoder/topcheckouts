A father and son (Wyoming attorney Mark Pierce and his serial-entrepreneur son Andrew Pierce) turned one department of the law firm into a website around 2015. They bought an exact-match keyword domain like "wyomingllcattorney.com". Andrew had only come to "write website copy and do marketing", but along the way he realized Wyoming LLCs (no state income tax, anonymous ownership, asset protection) were a great selling point. They went after searches like "wyoming llc", "registered agent" and "asset protection" with lots of SEO articles and YouTube videos, then converted with "attorney backing + 24-hour filing + no hidden fees". Later it split off from the law firm into a software platform and expanded to all 50 states (rebranded as llcattorney.com), entirely self-funded, claiming over 100,000 companies formed in total. A second wave of demand came from non-US residents forming US companies (Morocco, Spain, etc.).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.
