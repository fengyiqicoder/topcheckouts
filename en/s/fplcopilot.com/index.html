Basics

  • Product: free FPL toolkit: transfer solver/planner (up to 10 gameweeks ahead), expected points, chip strategy optimization, fixture difficulty, penalty takers, price changes, mini-league analysis, team rating, and the AI chat assistant Copilot.
  • Who built it: Spiros, who calls himself an "Engineer, data nerd" and ranks in the top 0.1% of FPL overall; the project grew out of his 2023 thesis at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) (fplcopilot.com/about). A personal project, no company seen.
  • Pricing: the About page says it is free; profile data says a paid subscription and trial exist but the price was not found (pricing page returns 404).
  • Domain: fplcopilot.com registered 2023-05-14 (RDAP, Namecheap); X account @fplcopilot registered the same day, 2023-05-14.
  • Traffic (profile data): UK 29%, US 11%, Netherlands 7%; search 44.9%, direct 41.3%, referral 8.3%; non-brand search about 36%, with terms "fpl ai", "rate my fpl team", "fpl team rater".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • X content: keeps posting predictions/solver results, 3,386 tweets and 6,165 followers.
  • Search ranking: tool pages map to terms like "rate my team", "chip strategy", "fpl planner" and "expected points"; a set of timely blog posts every gameweek; non-brand search about 36%.
  • Seasonal rhythm of return visits: direct traffic is 41%, and users come back every gameweek to adjust transfers.

Speculation:

  • The "season review / team rating" page after entering a Team ID suits screenshot sharing, which would bring social spread (a season-review page exists, but no sharing data was seen).
  • The name is close to the official "FPL Companion powered by Copilot" and may pick up some mistaken searches.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public user count or revenue; the About page says it is free. X 6,165 followers, Discord 399 members, YouTube 19 subscribers: a small personal project.
  • Profile data mentions a paid subscription, price not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Most search results were irrelevant speedtest/stackoverflow pages; the main evidence comes from the official About page, sitemap, Wayback CDX, RDAP and the X/Discord/YouTube APIs.
  • The founder is known only as Spiros; surname and interviews not found; The Athletic article could not be opened.
  • Gaps: the first viral X post, a Reddit launch, when the paid tier launched, and user scale.
