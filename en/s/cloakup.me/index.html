Overview

Cloakup (cloakup.me): Brazilian SaaS that filters/cloaks website traffic to block bots, spy tools, competitors and ad-platform moderators. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 3,241 visits went from Cloakup to a checkout payment page — #594 of the 607 sites we track. That is 41% up from the April–June monthly average of 2,295.

Total website visits that month were about 9,336 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 34.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 83%, social 13%, referrals/email/paid 3%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 83%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: R$247/mo Starter; R$497/mo Growth; R$897/mo Scale.
