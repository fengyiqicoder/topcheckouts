Basics

  • Product: real-time traffic filtering that detects Facebook/Instagram, Google, TikTok and Kwai review bots and human reviewers, crawlers, proxies/VPNs, ad spy tools, plus filtering by geolocation, device, source, URL parameters, browser language, UA, ISP and black/white lists; higher tiers include "Ferramentas black" (safe-page generator, image metadata stripping, text encryption, page cloning, unlocked 7 days after subscribing). Integrates via PHP/JS/WordPress.
  • Pricing: Starter R$247/month (20K requests, 1 domain), Growth R$497 (100K, 3 domains), Scale R$897 (500K, 10 domains); overage billed per thousand requests; enterprise by contacting support. 7-day free trial / 7-day refund.
  • Who built it: not found. No company name/CNPJ/address on the site; terms are in Portuguese, pointing to a Brazilian team.
  • Launch date: first Wayback capture 2022-07-23, invite-only at the time ("Solicitar convite"), already claiming "100M+ requests" and "filtering for 7-figure offers"; registration opened and plans published in 2023-11; /br /en /es trilingual site launched in 2024-05/06.
  • Social: Instagram @cloakup.me about 1,269 followers, 10 posts; YouTube @cloakup_; WhatsApp support.
  • Traffic (profile): Brazil 94%, United States 3.4%, Chile 2.6%; brand keywords (cloakup, cloaker) 82.6%, social 13%, referrals 2.6%.

Where the first users came from: an invite-only start in 2022, with invitations circulated only within Brazil's media-buyer scene (Speculation: through the founding team's existing media-buyer contacts/communities; no specific people found). From 2024 it spread through training/mentor partner pages and referral commissions. No Product Hunt or press coverage found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral commissions: referrers earn 5% of all payments from those they refer, for life.
  • Partner channel pages: imersao1x1, letsgrow and similar "exclusive offer + 7-day trial" pages, plus upsurge-sourced links, show distribution via media-buyer training and communities.
  • Own ad landing pages lp1/lp2 (Speculation: advertised to Brazilian media buyers).
  • Trilingual site extends into Latin America's Spanish-speaking markets (Chile and others make up a small share of traffic).

Speculation:

  • Brazilian direct-response/affiliate media buyers rely heavily on communities (WhatsApp/Telegram groups, mentor courses), so the tool spreads by word of mouth in those groups; brand keywords at 82.6% back up the "hear about it, then search the name" pattern.
  • The product becomes harder to drop the longer it is used (getting an ad account banned is costly), so renewals are steady.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue. The site claims "thousands" of users; 1,000 paying accounts × about R$400/month on average ≈ R$400K/month (about US$70K/month), purely our estimate.
  • Already claimed "100M+ requests" in 2022.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Search results were almost entirely irrelevant (only Instagram posts); evidence comes mainly from the official site and Wayback snapshots (2022-07, 2023-11, 2024 multilingual/landing pages).
  • Team, company entity, specific source of first users and revenue all not found.
  • Note: a cloaker's core purpose is to bypass ad-platform review (the site explicitly says "show platforms a fake page" and "Ferramentas black"), making it a gray/black-hat tool; this report only documents its growth path.
