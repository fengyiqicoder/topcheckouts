Growth loop

Evidence:

  • YouTube → website: social is 35.9% of traffic and mostly YouTube; video descriptions link to templates/articles (brand keyword "jeff su meeting notes template").
  • Website → newsletter → courses: the homepage and articles all carry signup boxes, a courses section was added from 2022, and the newsletter sends one AI tip a week to bring back existing readers.
  • Content topics follow trends: from job hunting (resumes, cover letters) → Google Workspace productivity → Notion → AI (NotebookLM, Gamma, Deep Research); each topic change captures a new wave of search and recommendation traffic.

Speculation:

  • The first audience came from YouTube's recommendation algorithm distributing "resume / job hunting / productivity tips" videos; the "ex-consultant + Googler" identity provides credibility. The channel was created in 2013 but the specific videos that took it off were Not found (from memory, unverified: videos on job hunting and remote work during the pandemic, around 2020–2021, lifted it).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Workspace Academy course $329–399 (one-time), corporate training from $22,000.
  • YouTube 1.93 million subscribers, 107 million views; newsletter reader counts are inconsistent (21,718 / 130K+).
  • Specific revenue: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The specific videos and dates behind the channel's takeoff, and when he left Google, have no direct evidence.
  • No founder interviews or podcasts in the search results; the origin story is mainly reconstructed from the about page and Wayback.
  • The 2013 jeffsu.org captures do not connect to the current 2021 registration, so they are judged to be from a previous owner.
