Basics

  • Product: a forum for sharing character cards and mods for Illusion games (Koikatsu, AI Girl/HS2) — an adult-game community.
  • Operations: admin kloeny233, identity not found. now.cc is a free Hong Kong subdomain service ("NOW shared domains", run since 2008 by the Hong Kong IT Alliance); backup domain kknow.cc.
  • Scale: the sharing section has 865 threads and 81K messages; the card-request thread has 74K replies and 42 million views; the mod-search thread about 1 million views; Telegram @kknowcc has 4,253 subscribers.
  • Monetization: on-site credits exist, but no paid membership or ads seen. Japan is about 45% of traffic.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Users in the card-request thread keep re-posting cards by popular authors (Hongcha, BT1, sixpluswan), creating a steady content supply.
  • Long-tail searches like "koikatsu card" bring newcomers; rentry.co referrals about 3.2%.
  • The Telegram channel and backup domain handle recall after outages.

Speculation:

  • With Japan at 45% of traffic, it may spread by word of mouth in Japanese player circles.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No paid membership or ads seen; the source of checkout-page traffic is unexplained.
  • Scale figures are in Basics (all on-site counts).

Confidence and gaps

  • The forum that hosted the original thread is never named explicitly; F95 is inferred from the replies.
  • The admin's identity and what the checkout page actually sells were not found.
