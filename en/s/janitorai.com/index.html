JanitorAI caught the 2023 "AI role-play" window created by the ChatGPT API: users create their own character cards, and the backend connects to a shared OpenAI endpoint, free and uncensored. It took in a large number of role-play players who found Character.AI's filters too strict (this migration is the common industry account, from memory, unverified), and third-party reviews say it had 1 million users in its first week. After OpenAI sent cease-and-desist letters and the shared proxies were shut down, it changed to "bring your own API key / connect your own proxy", with its in-house free model JLLM as a fallback. Hundreds of thousands of user-created character cards in the community, plus proxy and model tutorials on Reddit and Discord (for example Reddit was flooded when DeepSeek came out), formed a "creator supply → players → new creators" loop. The company (now called jani) has investment from Khosla Ventures, Sky9 and others, generates 13 trillion+ tokens a month on its own GPUs, and claims 2.5 million+ daily actives with over 100 minutes per person per day; it only launched its first paid subscription, janitor+ ($12.99/month), in 2026-06.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: an AI character chat platform (18+). Users create and share character cards and can choose a model: the official free JLLM, or connect their own proxy to OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and so on. It has Scripts (character "prompting" scripts), comment sections and creator profile pages; iOS/Android apps; and the official Reddit r/JanitorAI_Official and Discord.
  • Pricing: mostly free. On 2026-06-24 it launched janitor+ at $12.99/month (5x context, priority queue, regenerate with frontier models), with the company saying pricing is "roughly break-even". From 2026-07 there is transparent pricing for third-party model access (official blog).
  • Team and company: founder Jan Zoltkowski (a third-party review says he once won a HackerOne hackathon); JanitorAI Inc., San Francisco; the parent or current name is jani (jani.com, "training custom narrative models on our own GPUs"). Funding: a third party says $3 million (North Equity, Premise Ventures, Sky9); the jani site says it is backed by Khosla Ventures and Sky9 Capital.
  • Domain: earliest Wayback snapshot 2023-06-01, titled "Janitor AI - Wow such bots".
  • Users: US 38.5%, Philippines 4.3%, Russia 3.7%; direct 92.5%, brand keywords 99.7%; referrals from similar sites crushon.ai and saucepan.ai (channel profile).

The first 1 million users: from the 2023 AI role-play player community (users unhappy with Character.AI, and ChatGPT jailbreak and role-play communities, e.g. Reddit, Discord, TikTok), attracted by "free, uncensored, build your own characters" ("1 million in the first week" comes from a third party; the original source was not found, nor was a specific launch post).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator supply: hundreds of thousands to over a million user-created character cards, with creators having profile pages, followers and comments; character card links are shared in the community.
  • Community-made tutorials: lots of proxy and model tutorials on Reddit and Discord, even made in character card form (Gemini Proxy Guide, DeepSeek Guide); users write Greasy Fork scripts to extend features.
  • Every time a new model comes out (DeepSeek, Gemini), the community produces content around "how to connect it to Janitor", bringing a wave of new users and returning users.
  • Extremely high stickiness: direct visits 92.5%, over 100 minutes per person per day.

Speculation:

  • Traffic is traded with similar sites like crushon.ai and saucepan.ai, and users use several sites at once.
  • Many knock-off domains (janitorai.com.au, janitor-ai.io, us-janitorai.com, etc.) intercept its brand search traffic, which in turn shows how hot the brand is.
  • The 2026 mandatory ID verification and paid tier may cause some users to drift to alternative sites.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2.5 million+ daily actives (jani site), 15 million+ registered users (homepage claim, secondhand).
  • Largely free before 2026-06; janitor+ at $12.99/month is "roughly break-even". Revenue scale is unclear, with GPU costs mainly supported by venture capital.
  • Funding: a $3 million seed round (third-party claim), and later Khosla Ventures participated (jani site); round and amount not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch (no earlier report). The launch date (Wayback 2023-06) and scale (jani site) have fairly solid evidence; "1 million in the first week" and "OpenAI cease-and-desist" come from third-party reviews, and original reports were not found.
  • Conflicting accounts: aiangels says "used a shared proxy before 2023-02", but the first janitorai.com snapshot is 2023-06. Earlier it may have used another domain or a beta version; unverified.
  • Gaps: direct evidence of the Character.AI migration, the specific videos that drove TikTok growth, and the creation date and member count of the Reddit board (old.reddit could not be opened) were not obtained.
