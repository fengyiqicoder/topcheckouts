Overview

Revid (revid.ai): AI video creation SaaS that scripts, generates, edits and publishes short-form videos with viral-trend research. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Revid to a checkout payment page — #299 of the 607 sites we track. That is 5% down from the April–June monthly average of 13,612.

Total website visits that month were about 169,075 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 7.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 56%, direct 38%, social 5%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 72%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
