French serial entrepreneur Tibo (Thibault Louis-Lucas, co-founder of Tweet Hunter and Taplio, both sold to Lempire, and Product Hunt's Maker of the Year for 2022) acquired Typeframes, a text-to-animated-video tool built by an indie developer, in September 2023 and rebuilt it as Revid: "AI finds viral topics, writes the script, generates a vertical short video and publishes it automatically". On June 24, 2024 it launched on Product Hunt and took #2 of the day (963 votes). The first users came from Tibo's existing creator circle and "build in public" followers on X and Product Hunt. Long-term new users since then come from programmatic SEO: one page per tool/effect/template, with about 1,800 free tool pages, 1,430 "make" pages and 8,000+ category pages in the sitemap, capturing long-tail searches like "AI TikTok video generator" and "Reddit story video", plus Rewardful affiliate payouts and coupon sites. Basics

  • Product: AI short-video generation: creates vertical videos from a prompt, script, link, product page, PDF or music, with AI voiceover, subtitles, visuals and avatars, auto-publishing to TikTok, Reels and Shorts; has viral search and script generation; 150+ free tools (tools.revid.ai).
  • Pricing: free trial (no credit card required), paid from $39/month (2,000 credits), up to about $199/month.
  • Team: Tibo (Thibault Louis-Lucas), co-founder and CTO Nicolas Coutureau (formerly Jitter Video), and Product Hunt also lists Ayush Soni; about 4 people (wiki, 2026). Part of Tibo's TMAKER product portfolio (which also includes Outrank and others).
  • Predecessor: Typeframes, built by indie developer Lilian in late 2022, acquired by Tibo in 2023-09 (wiki, secondary source).
  • Domain: registered 2024-04-15 (RDAP); first Wayback capture 2024-04-16.
  • Funding: bootstrapped, no outside VC (wiki).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • The founder's existing circle: Tibo is Product Hunt's 2022 Maker of the Year and the founder of Tweet Hunter/Taplio, and the launch took #2 of the day.
  • Programmatic SEO: tens of thousands of tool, template and category pages, and a free tools site at tools.revid.ai; non-brand search accounts for about 22.6% of new users (our data).
  • Affiliate payouts + coupons: referrals include Rewardful (an affiliate platform) and an Indian coupon site.
  • The product itself publishes videos: users publish 30K+ videos a month to TikTok/IG/YouTube, but whether those videos carry a Revid watermark is unverified.
  • Third-party reviews: YouTube reviews, G2, Trustpilot, and all kinds of "alternatives" articles.

Speculation:

  • Tibo publicly shares Revid's revenue and growth on X (build in public), bringing the new product creators and indie developer users; the original X posts were not opened this time.
  • Videos from the free plan may carry a watermark, serving as branded output.

Revenue and scale clues

  • MRR about $18K (early 2025) and ARR about $440K (end-2025 estimate), both from a wiki's secondary compilation, with no first-hand source seen.
  • Self-reported 14,258+ users (this number has not been updated since 2025-06) and 100K+ videos rendered per month (2026-09).
  • Pricing $39–199/month.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The Product Hunt launch, the changes in homepage user counts and the sitemap scale have first-hand evidence; the founder's background, the Typeframes acquisition and revenue figures come from the miraheze wiki (secondary, marked as "estimate").
  • The search results included many look-alike/mirror sites (revid-ai.com, revid.me, revidai.org, revidai.pro), which are not the official site and were not used.
  • Gaps: the original text of Tibo's own revenue posts on X, the affiliate commission rate, the share of each channel.
