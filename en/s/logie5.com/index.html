Basics

  • Product: an AI assistant for the Amazon Influencer program: finds campaigns / Creator Connections, scores products, requests samples, AI-generates promo images, a brand DM inbox, store health monitoring, branded deep links and multi-platform tracking, auto-publishing to YouTube / Amazon CA·UK / Pinterest (https://logie5.com/).
  • Pricing (profile file): Silver $597 billed annually, Gold $997, Platinum $1,997; free tier "Logie Shield" (10 credits/month); 14-day trial; the current home-page offer is "30% off any paid tier + 14 days free, until 2026-11-30" (https://logie5.com/faq).
  • Parent company: Logie (logie.ai), founder Ehud Segev, a globally distributed team (mostly members in Indonesia and Kenya) (https://logie.ai/our-team/). The parent site was registered 2021-01-06 per RDAP; the first capture in 2021-05 was "LOGIE 'LIVE' Management & Monetization System Coming Soon"; in 2023 it positioned itself as a "predictive eCommerce marketing platform" and long showed "at capacity, join the waiting list"; in 2026 it says "Since 2021…", "creators earn 10–50% commission per order", and cites a community survey that "69% of creators earning $5K+/month use Logie" (Wayback 2023-01, 2025-01, 2026-05).
  • Referral mechanism: a page banner says "You're on someone's team, your launch price is credited to them, paid by Logie, not by you" (https://logie5.com/, /faq); the exact commission rate is not stated on the page.
  • Domain: logie5.com registered 2026-06-27 per RDAP; no Wayback captures.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,221 visits last month (newly appeared), US 80.7%; direct 42.1%, search 43.4% (brand keywords 85.5%: logie5, logie5 affiliate program); social source YouTube; there is also a Spanish version at /es/.

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: the Amazon affiliate creator base already on the Logie parent platform (claims to be the most-used third-party commission platform, with a community and weekly training); the home page says all 30 beta testers reported income growth of 12%–100%+; beta testers promote the launch offer in personal videos marked AD.
  • Speculation: email/community notices from the parent platform are the main driver (direct 42% + brand-keyword search), and the referral team mechanism has early users keep pulling in peers.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Referral teams: referred users' launch price is "credited to the referrer and paid by Logie", so the referrer gets a reward; the search term "logie5 affiliate program" appears.
  • Creator testimonials: the beta users are themselves affiliate creators who post promotional videos (marked AD).
  • Free tier + limited-time launch price create urgency.

Speculation:

  • The product's "auto-publish to YouTube/Pinterest with tracked links" feature does not carry the logie5 brand, so outward product-driven spread is weak; growth relies mainly on referral rewards and traffic from the parent platform.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Annual plans of $597–$1,997; with a few hundred paying users, annual revenue would be in the hundreds of thousands of dollars (speculation, not verified).
  • The only self-reported numbers: 30 beta testers; the parent platform's "69% of creators earning $5K+/month use it" (source survey not verified).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration date, parent company history, the referral mechanism and the beta videos have evidence; credibility is medium.
  • Gaps: dates and view counts of the Facebook/TikTok videos were not obtained; the referral commission rate, number of paying users and logie5's official launch date were not found; Ehud Segev's background was not looked up.
