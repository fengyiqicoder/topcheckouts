Overview

Logie5 (logie5.com): AI assistant for Amazon Influencers handling campaign discovery, brand inbox, content, publishing and link tracking. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,221 visits went from Logie5 to a checkout payment page — #556 of the 607 sites we track. That is 1523% up from the April–June monthly average of 260.

Total website visits that month were about 2,834 (estimated from global rank). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: search 43%, direct 42%, referrals/email/paid 9%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 86%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Silver $49.75/mo intro (billed annually $597), regular $59; Gold $83.08/mo intro (billed annually $997), regular $99 etc..
