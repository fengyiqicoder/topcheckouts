This is a physical sauna bathhouse in Singapore's CBD, not an internet product. It sits in the basement of Singapore Land Tower next to Raffles Place MRT, right in front of hundreds of thousands of financial-district office workers. Acquisition rests on three things: ranking for local searches like "sauna singapore", "spa singapore" and "ice bath singapore" and appearing in media lists (Straits Times, Women's Weekly, GetGo and other "best saunas in Singapore" roundups); converting first-timers into members with a $99 five-visit or $75 two-week unlimited off-peak intro package; and partnering with nearby office buildings, gyms, running clubs and radio stations (The Executive Centre, Braze, Singapore Runners Club, MONEY FM 89.3, etc.) for group traffic. The differentiator is a "competition-grade Aufguss sauna + an Aufguss school co-founded with world champions", which is what gets it onto media lists. Basics

  • Product: 95°C dry sauna, 85°C Aufguss sauna (built to Aufguss.it specs with Estonian sauna wood), 42°C magnesium hot pool, 6-8°C cold plunge, two ice showers; sister venue House Longevity upstairs offers hyperbaric oxygen and red light. Pricing (SGD): intro pass $99 (5 visits + 1 red-light session, within 31 days); two-week off-peak trial $75; single 2-hour visit $55; also visit packs and monthly memberships. Hours Mon-Sat 7:00-21:30, Sun 10:00-18:00.
  • Address: 50 Raffles Place #B1-01, Singapore Land Tower, 1 minute from Raffles Place MRT.
  • Team: the site names no founder or company entity; the Aufguss school was co-founded with world championship medalists Riccardo Marzi and Enrico Ganassin.
  • Domain: registered 2025-05-22 at GoDaddy (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2025-07-13 (site already up); in 2026-05 the site runs on Wix.
  • Social: Instagram @saunabathhouse about 2,333 followers; a WhatsApp channel posts daily Aufguss session times.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Local search: non-brand terms sauna singapore, spa singapore and dry sauna spa singapore bring new users; brand keywords at 65.6% are returning customers.
  • Media lists: the GetGo list, Straits Times, Women's Weekly awards; competition among similar Singapore venues (The Ice Bath Club, The Plunge Club, REVA, etc.) is fierce, so list exposure matters.
  • First-visit conversion: two intro packages, $99 for five visits and $75 for two weeks of unlimited off-peak, explicitly limited to first-timers.
  • Corporate and community partnerships: office-building coworking spaces, companies (Braze), gyms, running clubs, a badminton academy and radio stations are listed as partners, which is B2B group traffic.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 customers most likely came from office workers in the same and nearby buildings walking past, Google Maps "sauna near me", Instagram, and referrals from partner gyms/running clubs; no direct evidence.
  • Radio partnerships (MONEY FM, ONE FM, Kiss92) may be brand exposure via resource swaps.
  • The "performance" feel of Aufguss sessions lends itself to customers shooting short videos for Instagram/TikTok, giving some word of mouth, but social volume is small (IG 2,333 followers).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Single location; $55 per visit, $75-$99 intro packages. Revenue and member count not public.
  • Our data: Singapore 90.3%; search 46.9%, direct 37.5%.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: no earlier report in the notes. Search results were almost entirely irrelevant home-sauna roundups and StackOverflow pages, and the Reddit batch was empty; evidence comes mainly from the site's homepage and About page, RDAP, Wayback, the GetGo list and Instagram.
  • Gaps: exact opening date, founder and company entity, the original Straits Times article and its date, membership size; the body of the 2025-07 snapshot could not be read.
