Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Programmatic SEO for itself: 66 free tool pages, 44 comparison/alternative pages, 40 industry pages, 8 CMS platform pages, all launched in bulk from 2026-05 on.
  • Affiliate commissions: 30% lifetime recurring, 60-day cookie; the page claims 100+ active promoters, $8,000+ paid out in total, $3,000+ per month.
  • Promotional conversion: a permanent "Get 50% off" on the homepage, $288/year framed as $0.79/day, 14-day trial, refund guarantee.
  • Branded output (weak): each article comes with X/LinkedIn/Reddit/Facebook posts that may expose the product when users share them (no evidence of brand watermarks).

Speculation:

  • Competitor-review YouTube/blog affiliates are the main source of new users (videos like "GrandRanker vs Rocketito" exist, though that one promotes the competitor).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Affiliate page: 500+ active paying customers at $79-200/mo -> rough monthly revenue $40K-100K (speculation; the figures come from a promotional page and may be inflated).
  • $3,000+ paid in affiliate commissions monthly; at 30%, affiliate-driven revenue is about $10K/mo.
  • The homepage's "8,022+ Founders" may include free trial signups.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch.
