A French-language Shopify dropshipping tool made in September 2022 by French e-commerce entrepreneur Thomas Salic and others. It started as "monitor competitor Shopify stores' sales + copy winning products to your own store in one click" ("Espionner, Copier, Chiffrer": Spy, Copy, Quantify), pulling in French beginner sellers with a 3-day free trial and the LANCEMENT30 code (30% off the first month). It later added AI one-click whole-site generation and an ad library; the preview images of generated stores carry the text "GÉNÉRÉ PAR COPYFY EN 1 CLIC", and it adds an affiliate program, weekly live coaching and blog posts reviewing French e-commerce training courses, to draw in the French-speaking world's "beginners doing dropshipping". Basics

  • Product: a French e-commerce SaaS: find winning products, track competitor stores' sales and themes, view the ad library, generate a Shopify store with AI in one click (you can paste an AliExpress/Amazon/Shopify link), and "Clone ad" to copy winning ad creative; has a Chrome extension and MCP interface docs (profile file, site, X summary, docs.copyfy.io).
  • Pricing: Starter €49/month, Growth €89/month, Pro €129/month (longer-period discounts) (profile file); in 2022 it was a 3-day free Découverte plan, then €49.99/month, and Pro €69.99/month (Wayback).
  • Who built it: co-founder Thomas Salic, who says he has been doing e-commerce and SaaS since 2017 with over €15 million in cumulative revenue, and has YouTube (@thomas.builders) and X (@tom_builders) (site author page). The Chrome extension developer is registered as INNOVIFY LLC (San Diego address, US); Trustpilot lists the country as France. Other co-founders not found.
  • Launch: WHOIS registration 2022-09-21 (OVH); the earliest Wayback, 2022-10-29, is already a full product page and price table.
  • Current traffic (profile file): France 24.2%, United States 5.9%, Spain 3.8%; only direct 55.5% and search 44.5%, brand keywords 73.3% (copify, copyfy), about 27% non-brand (biblioteca de anuncios facebook, trendtrack, etc.); social from YouTube, TikTok.

Supporting evidence: (1) the founder has been in business in the French e-commerce scene since 2017 and has a personal YouTube, so the first users may have been his acquaintance network in the French-language dropshipping scene (course students, YouTube viewers) (speculation); (2) in 2022-2023 new users were drawn in with a free trial and the LANCEMENT30 launch promo code (Wayback).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: store preview images generated by AI carry "GÉNÉRÉ PAR COPYFY EN 1 CLIC" (profile file).
  • An affiliate program (profile file); weekly live coaching for retention (profile file).
  • Content SEO: French/Spanish blog posts on the Facebook ad library, product research guides and reviews of French e-commerce training courses, intercepting people who "want to learn dropshipping" (site blog, search summaries).

Speculation:

  • French-language dropshipping training courses/YouTubers recommend product research tools and take affiliate commissions, the most common distribution method in this industry; Copyfy's blog specifically reviews these courses, suggesting it is competing for or borrowing traffic from these course providers.
  • Brand keywords are 73%, meaning most people heard about it elsewhere (videos, courses, communities) and then searched for it, rather than discovering it through search.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscriptions at €49-129/month; Trustpilot has 143 reviews and the Chrome extension has about 1,000 users, so paying users are probably in the thousands (speculation).
  • The founder's claim of "+15M€ revenus générés depuis 2017" (over €15M in revenue generated since 2017) is the total across his personal projects, not Copyfy's revenue alone.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were polluted by same-name terms such as "Copify" (another UK copywriting company), copy trading and plagiarism-check tools; the evidence comes from the Wayback 2022/2023 homepages, WHOIS, the site's author page, the Chrome Web Store, Trustpilot and the profile file.
  • The takeoff event, the first-user channel, partners and revenue were not found; the size of Thomas Salic's YouTube was not verified.
