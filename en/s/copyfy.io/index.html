Overview

Copyfy (copyfy.io): French SaaS that finds trending products, spies on competitor shops and ads, and generates Shopify stores with AI. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Copyfy to a checkout payment page — #461 of the 607 sites we track. That is 45% down from the April–June monthly average of 15,733.

Total website visits that month were about 43,517 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 19.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 56%, search 44%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows embed badge, and direct plus brand search is about 73%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
