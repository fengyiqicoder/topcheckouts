In February 2023, after ChatGPT blew up and GPTZero took off, a self-taught indie developer (Jozef Gherman, Miami) grabbed stealthgpt.ai and built a tool that "rewrites AI text until it can't be detected", sold to students afraid of being caught by Turnitin/GPTZero and to SEO writers. In its first few months it launched two "pay people to bring people" mechanisms: TikTok/YouTube/Instagram creators posting videos with #stealthgpt were paid $1 per 1,000 views (up to $500 per video), plus an affiliate program paying 10% for life. On top of that came the surge in "bypass AI detection / undetectable AI" search demand; by its AppSumo launch in November 2023 it self-reported 2,000 paying customers and $38,000 in monthly recurring revenue. Since then it has kept releasing new models as detectors upgrade (in September 2026 "Super", claiming to break Pangram), and keeps going with review articles, the affiliate program, paid search and low-priced student trials ($1 for three days).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI "humanizer" rewriting (Humanizer) + Stealth Writer + an AI detector + an essay writer, watermark removal, a Chrome extension, an iOS app, an API (docs.stealthgpt.ai), and MCP integration for Claude/Cursor (Leap review, 2026-10).
  • Who built it: founder and CEO Jozef Gherman, who describes himself on AppSumo as a "self-taught engineer and 2010s crypto enthusiast", inspired by the GPTZero viral video in 2023 to build StealthGPT; the company is XYZ AI, Inc., incorporated in Delaware with an office in Miami; in 2023-11 the team was 3 developers + 2 customer support + 2 marketing, self-funded with a little money from friends and family (AppSumo page). Originality.ai, citing Crunchbase: founded in 2023, with a pre-seed round in 2024.
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2023-02-03; Wayback first captured the homepage on 2023-02-13.
  • Early form (Wayback 2023-06-05): the page is marked "BETA", with Blog, API and Creators links; 7-day free trial, "refund if detected"; annual plans Essential $47.99 / Pro $95.99 / Exclusive $249.99, with copy saying "great for SEO specialists, blog editors and creators".
  • Current pricing: new users get a 3-day $1 trial, students pay $0.99 for the first month, then a subscription (profile file); Pro/Max have weekly word quotas of 12,500 / 50,000 (Leap review).
  • Traffic (profile file): United States 42.5%, Nigeria 7%, India 5.2%; organic search 43.5%, direct 42.4%, brand keywords 68.5%; non-brand about 17% (stealth writer, undetectable ai, claude watermark detector); paid search 8.5%; social from Reddit, YouTube, X; has an affiliate program.

Where the first users came from: the founder has not said directly. The existing evidence points two ways: (1) timing: the early-2023 ChatGPT homework wave plus GPTZero going viral made "bypass AI detection" a suddenly huge search demand, and stealthgpt.ai was among the first products to grab that term; (2) acquisition tactics with evidence: by 2023-06 (about 4 months after launch) there was already a pay-per-view creator program - anyone posting StealthGPT videos on TikTok/YouTube/Instagram got $1 per thousand views, up to $500 per video, limited to education/ChatGPT/AI topics, which amounts to buying student-oriented short-video exposure in bulk at a very low CPM - alongside a 10% lifetime-share affiliate program. Exactly who the first 1,000 users were was not found, but "search + pay-per-view UGC short video + affiliates" are verifiable early channels.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creators paid per view ($1 per 1,000, from 2023-06) + 10% lifetime affiliate share turn TikTok/YouTube student-oriented creators into a distribution channel (Wayback /creators).
  • Demand renews itself through the "detectors vs. rewriters" arms race: every time Turnitin/GPTZero/Pangram upgrades, StealthGPT ships a new model and shouts on X that it "broke through" (2026-09 Super).
  • Search: organic search 43.5%, with non-brand terms like undetectable ai and stealth writer; many "StealthGPT review/alternatives" articles treat it as the industry benchmark, which in turn sends volume to the brand term.
  • Paid search 8.5% + affiliate program + low-priced trials ($1/3 days, students $0.99) (profile file).
  • The AppSumo lifetime deal (2023-11) brought one-time cash and reviews.

Speculation:

  • It claimed "hundreds of thousands of users" in 2024-06 versus 2,000 paying customers in 2023-11, suggesting a low free-trial-to-paid conversion rate (speculation).
  • Word of mouth among students ("use this to pass Turnitin") is probably the main reason brand keywords are 68.5%, but there is no direct evidence.
  • The higher shares of Nigeria and India may come from ghostwriting/content-farm writer groups.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2023-11: 2,000 customers, $38,000 MRR (self-reported on AppSumo, currency not stated; assuming USD, about $460K annualized).
  • Team of 7 (2023-11), pre-seed in 2024.
  • 2024-06 homepage claims "hundreds of thousands of users" (including free).
  • Current revenue not found; annual subscription pricing $48-$250 (2023), billed by weekly word quota (2026).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The "launch" and "tiktok" search groups were almost all unrelated generic articles; the main evidence comes from the AppSumo founder post and FAQ, the Wayback 2023-06 homepage, RDAP, and Crunchbase as relayed by Originality.ai.
  • Reddit (including old.reddit and.json) would not open; after Wayback rate-limiting, a retry succeeded and read the 2023-06 /creators page and the 2024-06 homepage.
  • There is no data on how many users the creator program actually brought; the growth engine is classified as "Referral rewards or affiliates" based on "affiliates + the pay-per-view creator program".
  • The revenue figures are founder self-reports and were not independently verified.
