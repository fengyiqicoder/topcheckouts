Basics

  • Product (now): AI & SaaS leaderboard + AI news newsletter; the higher a product's bid, the higher it ranks, with rank #1 starting at $10 (profile data records the current highest bid as $73 and leaderboard bids of $10–$72); has Live Stats, Rules, Dashboard and subscription pages. Tool detail pages (such as /product/klap) are kept.
  • Product (2023 to mid-2026): a WordPress AI tools directory, "Browse 100+ AI tools in 50+ Categories" (2023-06), later adding Featured, Deals, Shop, Pricing, Advertise, "20,000+ Free Prompts", a homepage "Sponsor: AI Universe" ad slot and a "Join 1000+ Subscribers" newsletter (2025-01).
  • Who built it: founder Zulkar Naim (LinkedIn in Bangladesh, "AI & SaaS Founder"); his personal site zulkarnaim.com says "Join 46,000+ readers of The Saturday Letter"; his X account @zulkarnaimx has about 25,412 followers.
  • Launch date:.io WHOIS registered 2023-06-15 (Namecheap); first Wayback snapshot 2023-06-17. The leaderboard redesign came after 2026-09-03 (that day's snapshot is still the old directory) and was live by early October.
  • Traffic (profile data): India 36%, US 27.8%, UK 11.9%; direct 66%, social 25.5%; mixed search terms (jev, candy ai, janitor ai), brand keywords 66%. Hypestat estimates about 890 unique visitors a day and about 27,000 visits a month (summary only).

Where the first users came from: the first visitors in 2023 presumably came from AI tool name searches (the directory has one page per tool, so searches like "klap ai" or "candy ai" land here) and links from other AI tool directories (speculation, no backlink data found). The first paying product owners for the 2026 leaderboard came from the founder's own X and newsletter readers (evidence: he posted X posts with links in a row at launch; the newsletter's 46k readers is the founder's own claim).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Traffic from the founder's personal channels: X posts, the #buildinpublic topic, a personal newsletter.
  • The bid-ranked leaderboard is public, and product owners outbid each other for rank ("Compete in public").
  • Social sources 25.5%, direct 66% (profile data).

Speculation:

  • Indie developers who make the board will repost "I'm ranked #X on OpenPedia" to their own X, bringing in more developers (a weak version of branded output).
  • In the 2023-2025 directory stage, income came mainly from SEO long tail and affiliate/featured spots; after Google demoted AI directory sites in 2024-2025, traffic fell, which prompted the 2026 pivot (speculation, traffic change not verified).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Leaderboard bids $10-73 per slot; the directory stage had paid featured spots, ad slots and a Shop. Hypestat estimates about 27,000 monthly visits. Monthly revenue is presumed to be a few hundred to a few thousand dollars (speculation, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Search results included the founder's LinkedIn, X posts, personal site and hypestat; the rest were irrelevant. The X posts were read in full, with view counts, via the fxtwitter API.
  • Whether the founder runs it alone, the real traffic and revenue of the directory stage, and the current number of paying products on the leaderboard were not found.
