Overview

Extensionpay (extensionpay.com): Open-source API letting developers accept subscription and one-time payments in browser extensions. Category on TopCheckouts: Developer tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Extensionpay to a checkout payment page — #291 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 126,312 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 10.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 70%, social 22%, search 4%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows open source, and direct plus brand search is about 75%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: 5% per transaction, no monthly fee.
