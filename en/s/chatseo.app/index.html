Where the first 1,000 users came from: (1) Paul Vengeons's existing French SEO audience (YouTube, Substack, LinkedIn, consulting clients); (2) the pre-launch email list (landing page + LinkedIn "comment for the playbook" posts + Reddit story posts); (3) the three beta waves, about 350 people in total, converting to paid. About 3,500 signups were reached within roughly 6 weeks of launch (yesoperator).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder personal brands: each of the two posts 3 playbook posts a week on LinkedIn (comment to get the resource), weekly YouTube uploads (FR/EN); YouTube converts best.
  • Outreach: 50 personalized LinkedIn DMs a day via PhantomBuster, 10–25% reply rate, leading with a free mini audit.
  • Newsletter: 3–4 emails a week, automated with Customer.io.
  • Free mini-tools: 2–4 free mini SEO tools a week as lead magnets (the "similarweb alternative free" search term comes from these).
  • Long-tail SEO: about 2 FR/EN articles a week; 2,500+ brand searches a month.
  • Affiliate program (one €600 payout so far); tuning paywall placement/free quota produced revenue jumps.
  • Zero ads (first 8 months); TrustMRR shows no Meta ads.

Speculation:

  • The jump to $41k monthly revenue in 2026-09 overlaps with exposure from "public revenue stories" on Indie Hackers / MilestoneWins etc., forming a second loop of "public MRR -> spreads in founder circles -> new users."
