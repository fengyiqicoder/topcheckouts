This is not a startup product but the in-house ticketing subdomain of a physical zoo with more than 60 years of history. The zoo opened in the 1960s as Broxbourne Zoo, was bought by the Sampson family in 1984 and renamed Paradise Wildlife Park; from 2020 the CBBC children's show One Zoo Three was filmed on site (starring the three Whitnall brothers), bringing national recognition; in 2022-04 it announced the rename to Hertfordshire Zoo, completed the rebrand at its 40th anniversary in 2024, and the same year (earliest Wayback 2024-03) launched bookings.hertfordshirezoo.com to sell day tickets, annual passes, animal experiences, adoptions and gift vouchers. Traffic comes from the migration of old customers and the old brand (searches for old names like "broxbourne zoo"), redirects from the main site (referrals 41.4%), searches for "hertfordshire zoo tickets", plus 640K Facebook followers and short animal videos on TikTok; a 10% discount for buying online pushes people to this subdomain.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Main-site funnel: every buy-ticket, adopt and change-date button on the main site points to the bookings subdomain; referrals account for 41.4%.
  • Price nudge: 10% off day tickets bought online.
  • Search: 43.9%, with terms like hertfordshire zoo tickets, broxbourne zoo (old name), plus animal long-tail terms such as where to buy capybara uk.
  • Social and TV: 640K Facebook followers; regular animal and seasonal-event content on TikTok/Instagram; the CBBC show has been filmed at the zoo since 2020.
  • Return visits: annual pass renewals and date changes; direct 8.8%.

Speculation:

  • The takeoff was not an online event but the old zoo's offline footfall plus post-2020 TV and social exposure, migrated wholesale to this subdomain during the 2024 rebrand and new ticketing system.
  • Seasonal events (Halloween, Christmas, dinosaur night sessions) are the main hook for new users (supported by the official event calendar, but no traffic breakdown).
