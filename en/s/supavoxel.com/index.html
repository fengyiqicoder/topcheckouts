This is the standard 'AI tool site for global markets' playbook. The domain was registered in January 2026, built on a Next.js SaaS template and wired to an image-to-3D API ('Generator 2.0'); it launched in 22 languages on day one, pitching 'free, no login, 3 models a day' to capture big search terms like 'ai 3d generator free', 'image to 3d' and '图像生成3d模型'. At the same time it rolled out a ring of satellite sites: hy-3d.net (Hunyuan 3D) and accurig.pro (AccuRIG auto-rigging), which ride on big companies' open-source model and tool names, plus free mini-tool sites like 3daimaker.com and stl-viewer.org, all funneling back to supavoxel via utm-tagged links in footers and 'review' blog posts. It also uploaded 94 linked sample models to Sketchfab, posts generation samples on Instagram and X, and layers on about 10% paid traffic. The free quota drives return visits; monetization is a $19–99/month subscription.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI image-to-3D (GLB export), 'Convert Any Image to 3D In Seconds'; 3 free a day without login; has a 3D-printing page, a Tools subdirectory, an Android app (com.supavoxel.app, on APKPure since 2026-09), and the Ultra tier includes API access.
  • Pricing: Starter $19/month ($9 first month), Pro $29/month, Ultra $99/month (channel profile); the pricing page benchmarks against Meshy ('switch from Meshy Studio and save $60 a month for the same quota').
  • Team: the terms and privacy pages only list an email, with no company entity or founder information. Judging by the satellite-site network, multilingual template and brand-name piggybacking, it looks like the tool-site playbook of a Chinese or Asian indie developer (speculation, unverified).
  • Domain: registered 2026-01-09 (Squarespace Domains, RDAP); earliest Wayback 2026-02-07, by which point it already had 22 languages, 'Generator 2.0' and a sample gallery. The sitemap has 89 URLs, about 4 pages per language (home / pricing / 3d-printing / tools).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Multilingual SEO: landing pages in 22 languages; non-brand search makes up about a third of search ('ai 3d generator free', '3d model generator from photo', 'image to 3d', '图像生成3d模型').
  • Satellite and brand-piggybacking sites: hy-3d.net (Hunyuan 3D), accurig.pro (AccuRIG), 3daimaker.com and stl-viewer.org funnel traffic via footers and advertorials, provable through utm parameters.
  • Content platforms: 94 linked models on Sketchfab, generation samples on Instagram and X, plus newsjacking (Winter Olympics athlete figurines).
  • Paid traffic 9.9% (channel profile).
  • 3 free a day: direct is 49.6% as users return to use the free quota.

Speculation:

  • The satellite sites themselves capture brand long-tail like 'Hunyuan 3D' and 'AccuRIG' and pass it to the main site — a variant of piggybacking on a platform.
  • The G2 page and 'review' advertorials are meant to rank for searches like 'supavoxel review' and 'alternative'.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscriptions at $19/$29/$99, with a $9 first-month promotion. No public user or revenue figures; the homepage counter is a template placeholder (shows 0).
  • Channel profile: United States 18.2%, India 11.3%, Kazakhstan 5.6%.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written fresh from scratch (no earlier report). The launch date, multilingual SEO and satellite-site funnels all have first-hand evidence (RDAP, Wayback, utm links in each site's source).
  • Gaps: operator identity, the underlying model used and revenue were not found; the X and G2 pages would not load. That the satellite sites share an operator with this site is only inferred from cross-links and bylines.
