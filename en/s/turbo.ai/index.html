Overview

Turbo (turbo.ai): Study app that turns lectures, PDFs, videos and notes into quizzes, flashcards, notes and podcasts. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 98,854 visits went from Turbo to a checkout payment page — #15 of the 607 sites we track. That is 131% up from the April–June monthly average of 42,850.

Total website visits that month were about 781,719 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 12.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 68%, search 32%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 68%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
