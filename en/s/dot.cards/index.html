Overview

Dot (dot.cards): Digital business card; every tap shows the other person a branded page. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Dot to a checkout payment page — #408 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 639,039 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 51%, direct 38%, social 11%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows built-in sharing (medium), and direct plus brand search is about 58%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
