Overview

Mailmeteor (mailmeteor.com): Mail merge and email outreach tool for Gmail, Outlook and Google Sheets/Docs. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 11,338 visits went from Mailmeteor to a checkout payment page — #315 of the 607 sites we track. That is 157% up from the April–June monthly average of 4,418.

Total website visits that month were about 709,921 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 68%, search 31%, social 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 25%, with queries like “email checker”, “behind the email”, “gmail generator”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: Hobby $0; Starter $5.99/mo per user; Premium $17.99/mo; Professional $35.99/m.
