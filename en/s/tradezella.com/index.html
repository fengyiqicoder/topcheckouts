Basics

  • Product: a trading journal + analytics SaaS (automatic broker sync, review replay, backtesting, prop firm rule tracking, AI feedback), covering stocks/options/futures/forex/crypto.
  • Founder: Umar Ashraf, a US day trader and trading educator (who also owns Ashraf Capital and Stock Market Lab); third-party sources say the company is in Miami, while the official llms document only says 'United States'.
  • Team: remote-first; early development was outsourced and later brought back in-house (meobserver 2024). Headcount not found.
  • Funding: self-described as bootstrapped and profitable (meobserver 2024 / Fast Company 2024).
  • Pricing (2026): Essential $35/month ($26 billed annually), Pro $59 ($44 annually), Ultra $99 ($74 annually); no free plan, no trial. Early on (2024) the starting price was $24/month.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Affiliate program (/partners): aimed at trading YouTubers/TikTokers/X creators, signal-calling teachers, and Discord group owners; 20% for 6 months -> 30% for 12 months -> 10% lifetime, 60-day cookie, with a 20% discount coupon for the audience. This lines up with its social sources YouTube/X/TikTok.
  • Mentor Mode / Spaces: mentors and students share accounts two ways, invited by email or share link, and both sides need a paid subscription -> one mentor pulls in a whole cohort of students (the concrete form of group_invite/multiplayer_room).
  • '150+ trader communities integrated', a Discord community, daily livestreams, a bootcamp, and monthly challenges (community retention + acquisition).
  • Prop Firm Sync (200+ prop firms): embeds into the workflow of prop firm traders, a high-growth group.
  • The founder's own content (YouTube 750K+) keeps sending traffic.

Speculation:

  • Users post screenshots of TradeZella's P&L calendar / stats on X/TikTok (share_result), forming free exposure in the 'trade-screenshot' style; no official share card data was found.
  • The prop firm wave (FTMO/Topstep/Apex booming in 2022-2024) was an external push for a second leg of growth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported profitable and bootstrapped (2024).
  • Users: 50K+ -> 100K+ active traders (official 2026, inconsistent figures). Rough math: if 50K paying x ~$40/month = about $24M ARR, pure speculation; no public MRR/ARR found.
  • No free plan -> a high share of visits go to the checkout page.

Confidence and gaps

  • The launch time is fairly credible (waitlist snapshots + two media reports agreeing on early 2022).
  • Gaps: the specific launch video/post, early user-count milestones, team size, and revenue figures were all not found; the Reddit API was blocked. The Fast Company original returned 403 and could not be read directly, so its content comes from secondhand accounts. Hauteliving and the like are advertorials, used only as a reference for follower counts.
