Overview

Tradezella (tradezella.com): AI trading journal and analytics with auto-sync, backtesting, replay and prop-firm rule tracking. Category on TopCheckouts: Trading & finance.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 34,384 visits went from Tradezella to a checkout payment page — #75 of the 607 sites we track. That is 28% down from the April–June monthly average of 47,944.

Total website visits that month were about 567,594 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 6.1 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 77%, search 20%, social 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows group invites, multiplayer rooms, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 93%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $35/mo (Essential); $59/mo (Pro); $99/mo (Ultra); $315/yr; $531/yr; $891/yr.
