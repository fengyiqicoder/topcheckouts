Basics

  • Product: Elite ISP / PK1 ISP (retail-optimized) proxies at $2.50/each/month, full subnet $350; residential proxies $5/GB (bandwidth never expires, 172 countries, 50 million+ IPs); aimed at retail drops, scraping, and browser automation. The 2025 site's first users most likely also came from the same drop community (Pokémon cards/retail drops) and Discord recruiting, but there is no direct evidence that the 2021 brand and the 2025 site are run by the same people (speculation, based on the same name and the same "retail ISP" positioning).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Drop-community success posts: after copping, botters thank the @bot, @proxy provider, and @cook group on X at the same time, so the proxy provider's name gets exposed along with them (many examples in 2021).
  • Discord operations: every entry point on the site leads to Discord; the homepage "Our Wins" shows community results.
  • Invite-only and restocks create scarcity (2021: "follow for an invite draw", "5 invites with the restock").

Speculation:

  • The 2025–2026 retail drop crazes for Pokémon cards, Labubu, etc. brought new demand; users share success screenshots in Discord, attracting more people.
  • Non-brand search queries are competitor names, meaning users comparison-shop and migrate between similar proxy providers.

Revenue and scale clues

  • ISP $2.50/each/month, subnet $350, residential $5/GB; Discord about 2,850 members. No revenue data seen.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Site and Discord scale have first-hand data; the 2021 sneaker-scene evidence comes from X tweets (read via fxtwitter).
  • Search results were almost entirely unrelated proxy recommendation articles and CVE pages, not used.
  • Gaps: whether the 2021 brand and the 2025 site are the same team, operator identity, and specific acquisition channels (whether it partners with cook groups/bots) are all unverified.
