Instead of going head-on in San Francisco/New York, it opened first in cities like Cape Town, Johannesburg, Lisbon and Tallinn, where the startup scene is small and short on organizers, and brings in friends through member-hosted events. It bought the old domain and built the site in May 2026, listed on iOS last month, and by this month had 4,000+ members in 8 cities, including 1,014 in Cape Town alone and 47 member-organized events in 90 days. Basics

  • Product: founder social network (app + web). Free version: discovery in your own city, DMs, joining/starting meetups; PRO: AutoPilot (automatic weekly matching), cross-city connections, unlimited messages and "Onfound Intelligence" search, Communities, more flexible event hosting. Prices are only shown inside the app.
  • Cities: Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, London, Bangkok, Lisbon, Porto, Tallinn (8 active cities, others queued to open).
  • Scale: 4,000+ members, 75+ industries (self-reported as of 2026-10); Cape Town 1,014 people (83–86 new in the last 30 days), 47 member-organized events in 90 days; the homepage lists 18 events from 10/23–11/15.
  • B2B revenue: Founder Perks listing $150/city or $600/all cities per year, pinned $300/$1,200; featured partnership from $750/month (3 months); listed partners include Xero, Deel, Notion, Kit, Gusto, ClickUp, Netlify. Source: https://onfound.com/partner/
  • Team: Koa Browne (founder, "over ten years of startup experience"); company Onfound Inc.; the App Store seller is Deep Movement Collective LLC; LinkedIn shows 2–10 people, actually 2, founded in 2025.
  • Traffic (profile record): search 43.0% (brand keywords 85.7%), direct 42.6%, referral 8.6%; South Africa 69.6%, Estonia 30.4%; social from Facebook.
  • Domain: onfound.com has been registered since 2012 (previous owner), Wayback in 2025-07 still shows it listed for sale on HugeDomains; domain info changed on 2026-05-19 (presumably completing the purchase/transfer); the WordPress author sitemap is dated 2026-05-26.

Judging from 69.6% South African traffic and 1,014 people in Cape Town alone, the first users almost certainly came from the Cape Town startup circle (the founder's local network + word of mouth from Cape Town hike/dinner type in-person events); this is a Speculation, and Koa Browne's location and background are unverified.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Member-organized events: starting a meetup is free, and Onfound handles sign-up and reminders; 47 in 90 days in Cape Town (city page).
  • City density: an open-city/queue mechanism, opening only once enough locals are present (FAQ: "waitlist means the local community is not yet open").
  • Two-sided monetization: member PRO subscriptions + selling perk slots/partnerships to SaaS vendors (partner page).
  • SEO start: from this month it began writing long-tail articles such as "how to find a cofounder" (sitemap).

Speculation:

  • In-person events bring people in: people who attend a hike/dinner bring startup friends along, and friends must register in the app to sign up, "invite to use".
  • The 30% Estonia traffic comes from the Tallinn startup scene (Estonia's entrepreneur community is tight-knit), possibly the second city opened through a local organizer.
  • The Facebook social source is probably event links forwarded in local founder groups.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 4,000+ members; if 3–5% pay for PRO (price unknown, assume $10–20/month), monthly revenue would be about $1,200–4,000 (Speculation).
  • B2B perk slots are priced at $150–1,200/year, partnerships from $750/month, with 7 brands on the list (unclear whether paid or affiliate).
  • last month checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate, high compared to a 4,000-member scale, possibly including repeated visits to the in-app upgrade page).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The launch timing (site built 2026-05, iOS in 09) is well evidenced; the member count is self-reported on the site.
  • Gaps: the founder's background, why the first city was Cape Town, and the launch channel original text all Not found; PRO price unknown; for LinkedIn only the company page summary was obtained.
