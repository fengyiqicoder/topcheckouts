Overview

Payhip (payhip.com): Hosted ecommerce platform for selling digital downloads, courses, memberships and physical products. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 31,079 visits went from Payhip to a checkout payment page — #81 of the 607 sites we track. That is 1% down from the April–June monthly average of 31,322.

Total website visits that month were about 3,196,564 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 70%, social 13%, search 10%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows embed badge, public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 78%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: Free Forever $0/mo + 5% fee; $29/mo + 2% fee (Plus); $99/mo no fee (Pro).
