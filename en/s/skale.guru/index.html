Basics

  • Product: Skale (Skale Collective), an ad-buying coaching membership for DTC brands, with the tagline "scale ads to $100K+/day". Contents: a 60-minute private teardown on joining, weekly livestreams by the three mentors, a weekly member hot seat (queued by join order, "if you join in November you'll be placed later"), blueprint materials, the operators' private network and vendor discounts, and the option to pitch the mentors' fund (incubation/investment). Monthly billing with no contract, refundable within 30 days or before the first hot seat. Signup goes through a Whop application page.
  • Mentors:
  • Nick Shackelford: over ten years of paid media, handled hits like fidget spinners and magnetic lashes, says he has run ads for 248+ DTC brands; partner at Konstant Kreative, Structured Agency, GeekEx, Lucyd and BRĒZ; advisor to Motion, Northbeam, Triple Whale and MarketerHire.
  • Jordan Menard: co-founder of Instant Hydration (says it reached $100M+, with its first $100K day 105 days after launch) and of Motif Digital; about 13k followers on X.
  • Jason Applebaum: serial entrepreneur and investor, with several eight- and nine-figure brands.
  • Self-reported on the site: $1B+ in combined sales for the three, 103 reviews at 5.0 (from paid 1-on-1 calls, not from members). No funding information.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Source of the first users: the mentors' X launch post (Menard's post got 70k views), with only 5 early-bird spots; trust endorsement comes from reviews by 1-on-1 paying clients.
  • Scarcity/queue design: hot seats are queued by join order, so the earlier you join the sooner you get broken down; the early-bird sellout and price-rise messaging create urgency.
  • Channel profile: the only social source seen is X, and search at 49.9% is mostly brand keywords, i.e. people search the names after seeing a mentor's post.
  • Speculation:
  • Nick Shackelford himself (with a larger following) probably promoted on X/podcasts at the same time, but no specific posts were found.
  • Members themselves have no acquisition mechanism; "pitch the mentors' fund" lets members bring good businesses in, which is a deal source for the mentors rather than an acquisition loop.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Priced at $5,000/month (first 5 at $2,500 for life); designed at a scale of "about a dozen people per room".
  • If the first round is 5 early-birds + 10–20 at full price, monthly revenue is about $60k–$110k (pure speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • The sale date (2026-09-17 X post) and early-bird rules have first-hand evidence; the member count and Nick's promo posts were not found.
  • Search results are mostly the same-named SKALE blockchain and Skale video/SEO agencies, unrelated to this site.
  • Second-pass check: added the launch event (2026-09-17 Menard X post, 70k views) and the real meaning of "sold out in 5 minutes" (only 5 early-bird spots); corrected the second mentor's introduction and filled in the member benefits.
