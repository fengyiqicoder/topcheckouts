Basics

  • Product: Juno, an AI employee aimed first at marketing/GTM teams and later at service businesses. Has a cloud computer, browser, file space, coding and site building, scheduled tasks (Routines), memory and email; connects to Reddit, LinkedIn, TikTok, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Search Console, Semrush and more; ships preset Playbooks (ChatGPT visibility tracking, LinkedIn lead finding, ad variants, brand mention monitoring, SEO articles, etc.).
  • Pricing: Free $0 (1,000 credits/week), Grow $69/month (30,000 credits), higher tier $299/month (channel profile + https://getjuno.com/pricing).
  • Company: Reword Limited trading as Juno (footer of the site's Creators page), contact hey@getjuno.com. Reword is an AI writing tool (reword.com: 'AI Writer For People-First Articles', Cowriter, Search Ring, etc.). Founder names not found; the YouTube tutorials are presented by 'Cal' (identity unverified).
  • Domain: getjuno.com registered 2017-10-27 (GoDaddy, RDAP, with a change record on 2026-04-19). In 2021 it was a teaser page for a different startup, 'Juno open-banking payments'; 2022–2024 an unconfigured WP Engine page; 2025-03 to 2026-03 a parked redirect (/lander); the current product first appeared 2026-04-19.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free tier plus credit system, low sign-up friction (one-click Google/Microsoft sign-up).
  • Creator partnerships: the /creators page recruits 'creators with an audience in marketing'; terms not disclosed.
  • Programmatic landing pages: one page per connector (Reddit MCP, TikTok MCP, …) plus audience pages and a Playbook library, capturing '[x] MCP' long-tail beyond brand keywords.
  • YouTube demo videos.

Speculation:

  • The 28.8% referral share may come from affiliate/creator links or redirects from the old Reword site; unverified.
  • Australia's 45.8% traffic share may mean the team or early customers are in Australia, or that Facebook ads run there; unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported: '5,000+ brands' in 2026-05, '5,249+ teams' in 2026-07, '2,000+ service businesses' in 2026-10 (a narrower definition).
  • Priced at $0 / $69 / $299 per month. Paid conversion unknown, revenue not disclosed.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written fresh from scratch (no earlier report). Evidence for the domain history, launch date and positioning changes is hard (multiple Wayback snapshots + RDAP).
  • Gaps: founders, whether the Reword-to-Juno user migration actually happened, referral sources and the reason for the Australian traffic were not found. Search results are swamped by namesake Junos (employee benefits, ISP, weddings, voice app); the scaleupcollective piece on 'Juno's first customers' is about a different employee-benefits company, Juno (withjuno.com), unrelated to this site.
