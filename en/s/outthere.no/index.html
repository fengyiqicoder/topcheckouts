Basics

  • Product: an offline dating service for university students and young people in Norway.
  • Blind Date: match results sent by SMS every Thursday, the platform suggests a time and place, 199 NOK, students 99 NOK; refund if no match within a month.
  • Gruppedate: gender-balanced group dates of 4–6 people, bring up to two friends, with friend codes ensuring you land in the same group.
  • Event tickets: e.g. "friend pitch night", where each person gets 3 minutes to pitch a single friend; also on-site matching at music festivals.
  • Matching: rule-based scoring + Gemini AI evaluation. Tech stack Lovable/Supabase/Vercel/Twilio. Payments via Stripe and Vipps.
  • Team / company: initially Out There Studentbedrift (a student company under the Ungt Entreprenørskap system, founded 2025-08-26, Trondheim); registered as OUT THERE AS on 2026-01-26 (org 937082355, share capital 30k NOK). CEO and chair Isabella Kjos-Hanssen, board members Ingeborg Kjelvik, Charlotte Christiansen.
  • Funding: the site embeds an "Investor pitch" page saying "Vi søker kapital for å eie Trondheim — og deretter Norge" (seeking capital to own Trondheim first, then Norway). No completed funding found.
  • Scale: Instagram @outthere.no about 727 followers (2026-10), bio lists Trondheim / Bergen / Oslo.

Growth loop

Evidence (from the site's frontend copy)

  • Friend codes: "share this code with a friend to go on a group date together"; you can enter one or two friends' codes at signup to form a two- or three-person date.
  • SMS invites: you can "send a friend a Blind Date SMS invite" and the friend signs up through the link, corresponding to the /inviterer path; you can also "recommend a friend" and the platform will reach out to that friend.
  • Pitch nights: attendees go on stage to pitch their single friends; both the pitched person and the audience buy tickets. One user brings at least one new user, plus audience.
  • A group chat is created on the day of a group date, phone numbers are exchanged in person, and the meetup experience itself becomes word-of-mouth material. User review: "Fikk høre konseptet fra en kompis" (heard about the concept from a buddy).
  • Channel partnerships: student organizations (Start Bergen), revenue share with bar venues ("we bring bars customers in the off-season and take a cut"), music festivals.

Speculation

  • Match SMS sent to everyone every Thursday creates a recurring talking point; students ask each other "did you get a match this week".
  • The September traffic peak corresponds to freshers' week (fadderuke) and multi-city expansion; sustainability remains to be seen.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Prices: Blind Date 199 NOK / students 99 NOK; group dates and events ticketed separately; plus venue revenue share.
  • About 26k checkout visits in September (near 0 in April–June). At a 5–10% conversion rate, monthly ticket volume would be roughly in the thousands (rough estimate, no evidence).
  • Company just registered, share capital 30k NOK, looking for investors. Under 1,000 IG followers, still very small.

Confidence and gaps

  • Company registration, domain and product mechanics (frontend copy) are all hard evidence.
  • The origin story is only the site's own account; no media coverage found. Festival name, date of the first event and actual user numbers were not found. Searches of Norwegian media (Adresseavisen, Under Dusken, NRK Trøndelag) all came back empty.
