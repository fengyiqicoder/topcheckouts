In late 2014, Hamburg designer André V. built a link directory that listed only free PSD mockups cleared for commercial use. On 2015-06-11 he cold-started with a press release, collecting emails with a "free summer mockup pack + tutorial ebook"; on 2015-07-10 it launched on Product Hunt, where Facebook designer Justin McKissick and others retweeted "EXACTLY what I've been looking for", producing the first wave of exposure in the design world. For the next ten years it held a vast long tail of "free xxx mockup" searches, plus designers submitting their own work for exposure, Reddit design communities recommending it repeatedly, and a 31k-person email list, steadily capturing designer traffic and redirecting it to paid mockups from sellers on Envato, LS Graphics and Gumroad, earning affiliate commissions and ad revenue.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: A directory of links to free Photoshop mockups; it doesn't host the files itself, and the download button jumps to the original author's page. Has Advertising, Sell Mockups, Submit Mockup and Deals & Coupons sections; email signup gives a pack of 12 high-resolution PSD mockups ("Join 31,000+ designers").
  • Operator: André V., Hamburg, Germany (legal page), no company name seen; an individual or small team, no funding seen.
  • Business model: ad slots, sponsorships, and affiliate links to paid mockups (8 envato links on the homepage, envato.market/c/ promo links, ls.graphics, and referral links with a via=mw parameter). It shows up in checkout referral sources precisely because it sends traffic to the paid mockup pages of sellers on Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy and the like.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Designers and mockup authors can "Submit Mockup" to get free exposure; the directory keeps expanding, creating more long-tail search pages.
  • As a synonym for "free mockups", it is included in all sorts of design resource lists and repeatedly named in Reddit recommendation threads, bringing backlinks and brand searches.
  • An email list of 31k+ (a mockup pack in exchange for an email), used for re-engagement and promoting paid mockups.
  • Affiliate and ad monetization: promo links to Envato, LS Graphics and others.
  • Speculation:
  • After being accepted, authors say on their own social media or Behance that they were "featured on Mockup World", bringing traffic back.
  • Users search for a specific item (e.g. "free t-shirt mockup") and land on the site, download a free one, and click a paid promoted one along the way, creating affiliate revenue.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue or user count; email list 31,000+. Revenue comes from ad slots, affiliate commissions (Envato and others), and paid promotion from sellers (speculation).
  • Our data: last month checkout visits 21,047, six months ago to four months ago averaged 24,370.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass check: added the 2015-06-11 press release (cold start by collecting emails with a free mockup pack), the exact Product Hunt launch date 2015-07-10 and the 2019-12-19 2.0 relaunch, the Facebook designer's retweet on launch day, Reddit word of mouth and the 31k email list. Takeoff method changed from "long-tail search" to "launch platform", because the first wave of users really did come from the PH launch, and search was the long-term engine afterwards.
