In 2023 the prop-firm space (pay for an evaluation, pass, then trade simulated capital for a profit split) was red hot, and GFT came in as a latecomer with a "cheap + fast" pitch: perpetual discounts (50% off codes like FIRSTGFT for new users), $1 starter accounts, payouts within 2 days or a $1,000 penalty, and instant-funded accounts. It then gave affiliates up to 20% recurring commission plus milestone prizes like a Rolex or MacBook, so a crowd of trading KOLs, coupon sites and review sites recruited for it on YouTube, X, WhatsApp and in emerging markets (India, Nigeria, Kenya). Evaluation fees are one-time revenue and the vast majority of buyers never get a payout (about 3.7% of buyers do), which is what funds the high commissions and permanent discounts.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: simulated forex/CFD/gold evaluations with 1-Step, 2-Step, 3-Step and Instant Funding account types; platforms include MT5 (own broker), cTrader, TradeLocker, Match-Trader and others; also a Goat Stride sub-product, trading competitions and a points-reward system.
  • Company: the trade name belongs to Hong Kong's Wishes Tower International Limited, and simulated trading services are provided by Saint Lucia's Goat Funded LTD (site footer, affiliate page disclaimer). Unregulated.
  • Founder: propnavi names the founder as Edoardo Dalla Torre; Velotrade and proptradingarea give no name. Single source, unverified.
  • Domain: registered 2023-01-18 at GoDaddy (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2023-04-01.
  • Scale (official transparency dashboard, 2026-10-21): 1.2M registrations, 594,997 people who bought at least one paid evaluation, 28,859 active funded accounts, $26.16M in cumulative payouts to 22,279 people.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Affiliate commissions: the official affiliate page offers 20% on the first order, then 10-15% recurring, up to 20%; revenue milestones earn a gaming chair, AirPods, a MacBook, a Rolex (total value $20,950), and referral-count bonuses of $50-$1,000; claims 5,000+ affiliates. Search results are full of review sites and coupon sites (lunefi, velotrade, etc.) writing reviews with affiliate links or discount codes.
  • Permanent discounts and a low entry bar: codes like FIRSTGFT 50% off and GFT40 stay valid long-term; the $1-for-a-$1,000-simulated-account offer is described by reviewers as "low-cost acquisition"; the homepage lists the $100K account at $263 (list price $438).
  • Fast payout promise: rewards within 24 hours / 2 business days or a $1,000 penalty, used as a trust selling point.
  • Community and competitions: Discord, X, YouTube, Instagram; free trading competitions and a points-reward system (related financemagnates coverage).
  • The transparency dashboard and third-party payout trackers (Prop Firm Match, Payout Junction) serve as trust backing, offsetting the hidden Trustpilot negatives.

Speculation:

  • The first users most likely came from KOL promo codes in trading YouTube/Telegram/WhatsApp groups, plus the overall 2023 prop-firm boom (from memory, unverified: in 2023-08 the US CFTC sued MyForexFunds, which shut down, leaving many traders looking for alternatives just as GFT launched).
  • The India/Nigeria/Kenya-heavy traffic mix suggests emerging-market KOLs and WhatsApp group forwarding are the main acquisition route; payout certificate screenshots (with QR verification) get shown off in communities, acting as branded output.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 594,997 paying buyers; evaluation fees range from $1 to several hundred dollars, so rough cumulative evaluation revenue is in the tens of millions of dollars (speculation, assuming $100+ per person).
  • Cumulative payouts: official $26.16M (plus $3.17M early payouts not counted); independent trackers about $11.2-11.9M (mid-2026). 4th on Payout Junction's 30-day leaderboard ($1.59M).
  • Trustpilot: 4,328 reviews but the score is hidden.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: no earlier report in the notes. Launch timing (domain 2023-01, Wayback 2023-04, reviews say 2023-05) is corroborated by three sources, credible; proptradingarea's "operating since 2022-05" contradicts the domain registration date and is not used.
  • Scale figures come from the official dashboard, relayed by financemagnates but unaudited.
  • Gaps: founder (only propnavi mentions Edoardo Dalla Torre); no first-hand evidence on where the first 1,000 users specifically came from; Reddit threads did not appear directly in search results and were not opened.
