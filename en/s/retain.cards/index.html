Retain is an AI flashcard app that started in the German-speaking world: upload PDF slides to get cards automatically, with an adaptive spaced-repetition algorithm scheduling reviews, pitched as "20% more efficient than Anki" and aimed at medical students and exam candidates who must memorize a lot (the homepage shows the crests of TUM and LMU in Munich). The domain was registered in April 2024 and an English-German version launched in May 2024. Its growth engine is the "Creator Program": it recruits several hundred amateur creators, Retain sets up new TikTok/Instagram/YouTube study accounts for them and pays a fixed fee per post, with bonuses of up to €1,000 for hits. It says these accounts get over 10 million views a week and over 500 million in total. French was added in 2025-10, and France quickly became tied with Germany as the largest market. This "pay-per-post account matrix" is in essence buying traffic, just with the money paid to amateur content; that is why its traffic is almost all direct visits and brand search (people watch a video and then search retain cards).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: an AI flashcard and exam preparation tool. After uploading PDF/PNG/JPEG, AI generates cards (supports math formulas and images); adaptive spaced repetition (claims to be modeled on 220 million learning records); study plans generated from the exam date and target level; also AI podcasts, mock exams, Voice Mode and Anki import. Available on Web, iOS and Android (Google Play package name cards.retain.app.production). Interface languages are English, German, French, plus Polish, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Turkish and Hindi.
  • Pricing: free to use, paid prices not found. There are also school and business editions (Schools/Businesses entries on the homepage since 2025-05).
  • Team: not found (the official site has no Impressum page; legal and imprint both return 404). The channel profile and the TUM/LMU crests suggest it is most likely a Munich student team (speculation). Note that retaincards.com ("developed by a medical student") and tryretain.com are other products with similar names, not this one.
  • Domain: registered 2024-04-23 (RDAP); earliest Wayback snapshot 2024-05-19.
  • Users: Germany 25.5%, France 24.3%, Switzerland 13.8%; direct 71.3%, brand search 28.7% ("retain cards", "retain cards gratuit", "metodo retain") (channel profile).

The first users: German (Munich) university students, especially medical students, who originally used Anki (speculation, based on the homepage's Anki-comparison pitch, the TUM/LMU crests and German-first approach). The growth from 100K to 1M happened in step with the creator account matrix, and the French market was opened by French study accounts after French was added in 2025-10 (speculation, based on "gratuit" appearing in the brand keywords).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator matrix: a fixed fee per post plus hit bonuses (up to €1,000); Retain sets up new accounts for creators with no follower requirement, managed over WhatsApp; covers TikTok, Instagram and YouTube; 500 million+ total views. The official TikTok account calls itself the "main account" (934 followers), implying many sub-accounts.
  • Localized expansion: German first, then French, then 9 languages; the brand keywords include French "gratuit" and Italian "metodo retain".
  • Shared decks: users can share decks by link and others clone them in one click (channel profile).

Speculation:

  • Students share decks with each other, and classmates in the same course pull each other in, creating small-scale viral spread.
  • It took German medical students as seed users, then used the account matrix to replicate to high-exam-pressure markets like France and Switzerland.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 1M+ students (2026-10), about 10x growth in a year and a half.
  • The Creator Program pays fixed monthly fees to several hundred creators, suggesting a marketing budget of tens of thousands of euros a month or more (speculation); there may be funding, but none was found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch (no earlier report). The growth curve relies on Wayback snapshots of figures the official site reports about itself, so it is self-reported data, but the time series is consistent; the creator program mechanism is evidenced by official pages.
  • Gaps: the founders, funding, paid prices and launch channel were not found; many same-name products in the search results (retaincards.com, tryretain.com) caused interference.
