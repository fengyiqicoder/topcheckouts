Overview

Awwwards (awwwards.com): Web design awards and inspiration platform with courses, a professional directory and template market. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 3,344 visits went from Awwwards to a checkout payment page — #587 of the 607 sites we track. That is 2% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,292.
