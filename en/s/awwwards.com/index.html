Overview

Awwwards (awwwards.com): Web design awards and inspiration platform with courses, a professional directory and template market. Category on TopCheckouts: Community.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 3,344 visits went from Awwwards to a checkout payment page — #587 of the 607 sites we track. That is 2% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,292.

Total website visits that month were about 1,698,509 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 61%, search 36%, AI assistants 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 14%, with queries like “free fonts”, “portfolio website”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: Market templates from 29-49 USD one-time.
