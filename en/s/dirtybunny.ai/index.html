Basics

  • Product: AI influencer generation studio, a fixed persona → unlimited photo/video generation; includes BunnyFlow motion transfer, image editing, first-frame-to-video, and scene swapping. Credit packs $3/50, $5/100, $10/250, $20/500, $70/2,250, credits never expire, subscription optional.
  • Operator: Dirtybunny LLC (a Florida limited liability company), terms last updated 2026-08-21; founder not public. Tech stack Next.js, hosted on AWS.
  • Scale: the homepage self-reports both "Join 7,000+ creators" and "42,000+ creators onboard" (inconsistent figures), and "139,000+ images generated".
  • Traffic (profile record): search 54.9% (brand keywords 88.7%), direct 33.8%, AI assistants 6.8%; almost all US.
  • Domain: WHOIS (whois.nic.ai) created 2026-07-11 (NameCheap, registrant country shows Iceland/privacy protection), updated 2026-08-11; no Wayback archive.

First users: judging from the Instagram summaries, they came from the "AI influencer / faceless account money-making" side hustle circle, where bloggers show AI girl accounts in Reels and give the tool name (summary only, whether it was a paid partnership could not be verified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Side hustle content brings traffic: at least two Instagram posts name dirtybunny.ai in the caption, one clearly on the "AI influencers make money" side hustle topic (summary).
  • Low-barrier pay-as-you-go: credit packs from $3 that don't expire, suited to impulse trials (homepage).

Speculation:

  • The side hustle bloggers are mostly affiliates / paid promotions (common practice for similar AI influencer tools), but no affiliate program is seen on the site, unverified.
  • The AI influencer accounts users produce carry no watermark/brand themselves, so "branded output" spread is weak; brand-term searches at 88.7% show people mostly search after others mention it verbally or in videos.
  • 6.8% comes from AI assistants (profile record), possibly ChatGPT and the like recommending it for "AI influencer generator" type questions.

Revenue and scale clues

  • last month checkout visits about 12,900 (estimate). At 5–10% conversion and a $10–20 order, monthly revenue would be about $6,000–26,000 (Speculation).
  • Self-reports 139,000 images generated; at roughly 5–10 credits per image, that is about 700,000–1.4 million credits consumed ≈ $10,000–30,000 in cumulative top-ups (Speculation, rough calculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Registration time, operating entity and pricing are credible.
  • Gaps: founder unknown; Reddit search results were useless; the Instagram original posts could not be opened, only summaries; whether it pays for ads or affiliate promotion Not found. Both the takeoff method and growth engine are Speculation.
