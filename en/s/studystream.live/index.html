Overview

Studystream (studystream.live): Web and mobile platform where people join live online focus rooms to work or study alongside strangers. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Studystream to a checkout payment page — #282 of the 607 sites we track. That is 64% up from the April–June monthly average of 7,867.

Total website visits that month were about 455,876 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 2.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 67%, search 25%, referrals 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows multiplayer rooms, and direct plus brand search is about 92%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
