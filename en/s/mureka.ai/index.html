Overview

Mureka (mureka.ai): AI music platform generating songs, instrumentals and vocals from text prompts. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 25,179 visits went from Mureka to a checkout payment page — #114 of the 607 sites we track. That is 2726% up from the April–June monthly average of 891.
