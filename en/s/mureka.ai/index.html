For the first two years it kept up buzz with "fast iteration on in-house models + a Product Hunt launch and Suno benchmark comparison for every release" and accumulated nearly 10 million registered users, but paid conversion was very low; in mid-2026 it was repositioned as an "AI-native music platform" (create + listen + social sharing), while buying "suno / udio" competitor keywords and social ads at scale and seeding coverage in the shopping-guide sections of Variety and Rolling Stone, and checkout visits rose about 28x from 2026-06 to 09.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: generates a whole song from text/lyrics with controllable genre/mood/instruments, reference audio, stem separation, MIDI, an editor, AI vocals (TTS/cloning), and MV Director to generate music videos; in 2026 added personalized radio and community remixes; developer API (platform.mureka.ai); 10 languages.
  • Company: Skywork AI (registered in Singapore) / Kunlun Tech (listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange, founded by Zhou Yahui in 2008; announced going all in on AGI in 2023). Mureka's revenue is folded into the parent company's financial reports (Miraheze wiki). LinkedIn: founded 2024, 51–200 employees.
  • Launch: SkyMusic 2024-04; mureka.ai domain registered 2024-06-06; earliest Wayback 2024-07-11; PH launch 2024-07-25; the wiki says the Mureka-branded consumer product officially launched in 2024-08.
  • Pricing: free trial; Basic $8/month ($96/year); Pro $24/month ($288/year); in-site virtual currency Gold; most recent price change 2026-09.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • 2024–2025 cold start: PH launch + relaunching on PH/HN with each model release, benchmarking against Suno; YouTube AI tool tutorial creators promoting it on their own or with links (tutorial videos with bit.ly referral links).
  • 2026 takeoff: paid search intercepting the Suno/Udio competitor keywords + social ads (paid about 41%).
  • Exposure in mainstream media shopping-guide sections (Variety, Rolling Stone, around 2026-08).
  • Song share pages (song-detail?is_from_share=1), community remixes and radio: works carry the brand and bring traffic back.
  • In-site contests (Gold rewards) stimulate UGC.
  • Speculation:
  • Backed by Kunlun Tech (with overseas traffic businesses like Opera), it has ample ad budget, and increased buying after the mid-2026 repositioning.
  • Non-English markets such as Japan and Brazil are served by multilingual landing pages + local SEO articles (note.com introductions).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Nearly 10 million registered users (LinkedIn 2025-07; repeated by the wiki in 2026-08).
  • Revenue is not disclosed separately and is folded into Kunlun Tech's reports. Checkout visits only reached the 25K level in 2026-09, indicating a low payment rate.

Confidence and gaps

  • The parent company attribution is stated explicitly by the Miraheze wiki (not first-hand, but consistent with the old report's recollection), medium-high credibility.
  • The 2026 ad budget and whether the shopping-section coverage is a paid partnership are both speculation; the Variety/Rolling Stone originals could not be opened (paywall redirects).
  • Second follow-up check: confirmed the parent company as Kunlun Tech / Skywork AI, the predecessor SkyMusic (2024-04), nearly 10 million users, the full model iteration timeline, and the 2026-06 platform repositioning; takeoff method kept as "Paid ads", with added evidence of competitor keyword buying and media shopping sections.
