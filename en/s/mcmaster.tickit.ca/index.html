Basics

  • Nature: MSU's official ticketing page (event listings, a face-value-only resale marketplace, e-ticket transfer/recovery); support email eventspromo@msu.mcmaster.ca (https://mcmaster.tickit.ca/help).
  • Platform: Tickit (tickit.ca), "Proudly independent since 2008", operated by Villager Events Inc.; clients are mostly BC music festivals (Vancouver Island MusicFest, Bass Coast, etc.) (https://www.tickit.ca/).
  • Mechanics: e-tickets live on a private order page; they can be shared, transferred, resold to a specific person or listed on the marketplace, and resale is capped at face value (anti-scalping).
  • Current status: on 2026-10-25 the homepage read "There are no tickets for sale at the moment"; the organizer link on marketplace event 36620 points to mcmasterhomecoming.ca (https://mcmaster.tickit.ca/marketplace/36620), showing that 2026 Homecoming tickets were sold here.
  • Subdomain: first Wayback capture of mcmaster.tickit.ca on 2021-05-17; the tickit.ca main domain has Wayback records since 2012.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,178 visits in September (newly appeared), 97% Canada; search 56.7%, almost all non-brand event keywords (mac hoco, mcmaster hoco, swae lee tickets mcmaster); direct 32.5%, email 6.5% (purchase confirmation emails).

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: MSU's officially designated channel + students searching event names + return visits from confirmation emails.
  • Speculation: the student union posts ticket links on Instagram and in campus event promotion; no independent acquisition needed.

Growth loop

Evidence: no self-sustaining growth loop; traffic follows the student union's event calendar entirely (Homecoming and orientation week each year).
Speculation: at the platform level Tickit expands through B2B word of mouth ("one student union uses it, so other student unions/festivals do too"), but this research found no other university clients of Tickit.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ticket prices not found; Tickit charges a per-ticket service fee ("flexible", specifics not public). MSU represents roughly 27,000 undergraduates (https://msumcmaster.ca/), which is the ceiling on potential buyers.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The site's nature, the platform operator and the resale mechanics are backed by evidence; "last month traffic came from Homecoming ticket sales" rests on the profile's search terms and the marketplace page pointing to mcmasterhomecoming.ca, so medium-high confidence.
  • Gaps: fetching mcmasterhomecoming.ca failed (connection closed), so the lineup, ticket prices, on-sale date and number sold were not obtained; whether Swae Lee was the 2026 Homecoming guest comes only from search terms and is unverified. This site is not suitable as a commercial success case study.
