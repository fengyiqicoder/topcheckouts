Basics

  • Product: Parker, an 'AI creative strategist/creative director'. Connects to the Meta ad account to analyze past performance, automatically researches TikTok trends, Reddit, competitor ads and customer reviews, and outputs ad angles, hooks, scripts, briefs and an idea library; integrates with Slack; 'Autonomous Mode' scans TikTok overnight and pushes results; has Parker MCP, open-source agent skills and a desktop app (parker-desktop-releases).
  • Pricing: Starter $299/month (Meta spend ≤$100k/month), Growth $499 (≤$500k), Scale $699 (≤$2M), custom for agencies/enterprise; unlimited usage and seats, with a dedicated Slack channel.
  • Team: co-founders Alex Cooper (former founder of the Adcrate agency), Maneesh Apte, Jimmy Slagle (per Alex Cooper's LinkedIn post). GitHub org real-simple-labs (display name heyparker.ai). Website designed by Ashwin Pradeep Kumar.
  • Domain: heyparker.ai registered 2025-05-01 (Namecheap, RDAP); earliest Wayback 2026-01-07.

First 100 users: existing clients of Alex Cooper's agency (Adcrate) and his DTC network. Evidence: 7 brand logos were already on the homepage before launch, the LinkedIn launch post claimed 100+ brands, and the site testimonials include Adcrate's own creative strategists.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder personal brand: Alex Cooper posts on LinkedIn (20k followers) using 'comment the keyword to get the resource' engagement plays; YouTube demos drive traffic back to the agency.
  • Free content for leads: open-source course materials, the '1,000 winning ads' library, quarterly creative reports (reports.heyparker.ai), open-source agent skills and MCP. These also explain why github.com appears in referrals and 'parker mcp' in brand searches.
  • Paid ads: paid is 23.2% in the channel profile.
  • Design word of mouth: the site was featured by Peek Paper and onepagelove, and designers wrote unprompted posts about it on LinkedIn.

Speculation:

  • Agency clients → SaaS cross-sell: the brands the agency served naturally became the first paying customers.
  • A premium subscription with a dedicated Slack channel is closer to 'SaaS as a service' — retention relies on hands-on support, not product-led spread.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 100+ brands (2026-01) → 'hundreds of brands with over $1B in annual ad spend' (2026-10).
  • Priced at $299–699/month. At a few hundred paying brands, ARR is roughly $1M–3M (speculation, unverified).
  • The founder's previous agency did $3M/year with 45 people. No funding information found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written fresh from scratch (no earlier report). Evidence for launch timing, the launch post and the first customers is fairly hard (Wayback + original LinkedIn post + careers page).
  • Gaps: no primary data on which moment 'went viral' refers to — possibly just that LinkedIn post; paid-ad channels, any Product Hunt launch and funding were not found. Search results are flooded with unrelated 'Parker pen anti-AI ad' namesake content.
