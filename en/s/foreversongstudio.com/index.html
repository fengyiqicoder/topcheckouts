Registered at the end of 2025, and from January 2026 it ran emotional short videos on Facebook in the style of "My husband never cries, but this song made him cry", selling a gift of "a custom song written from your memories", aimed at middle-aged American women. The funnel is "2-minute form → free preview emailed within 24 hours → pay to unlock if satisfied", which is why email makes up 51% of our traffic data (the preview email pulls people back to pay). Paying customers share their song page on Facebook (the page preview reads "Make your own at Forever Song Studio"), and friends and family who see it come make their own, creating branded secondary spread. In 2026-09 the brand was renamed Songs by Sophie, with the old domain 301-redirecting to it, and Trustpilot has accumulated about 2,000 reviews at 4.9.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: custom song gifts (partner, children, parents, birthdays, weddings, pets, memorials); the team writes lyrics and produces "radio-quality" songs that can be downloaded and revised; free preview first, no card required. Suspected AI generation combined with human lyric writing (not verified).
  • Price: not shown on the site; a Trustpilot review says "$100 well spent".
  • Current status: foreversongstudio.com now 301-redirects to songsbysophie.com ("Songs by Sophie", contact sophie@songsbysophie.com, US).
  • Reputation: Trustpilot (songsbysophie.com) about 2,030 reviews, 4.9 rating, 96% five-star; profile claimed and on a paid plan, actively invites reviews; the profile notes other Trustpilot pages have been merged in (probably including the old brand, speculation).
  • Competitors: ForeverSongs.com (16K Trustpilot reviews), Songfinch, My Forever Songs, etc.; this site's name is very close to the top competitor ForeverSongs, so some brand-keyword traffic may come from confusion (speculation).
  • Our data: mainly US; email 51%, direct 32%, paid ads 10.2%, social Facebook; brand keywords such as forever songs and custom song gift about 32%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Acquiring customers with emotional Facebook videos: the official page and Reels are all "make them cry" emotional creative with links attached; paid ads are 10%, and the social source is Facebook.
  • Free preview + email return: the site states "free preview emailed within 24 hours", and our data shows 51% email traffic, meaning conversion mostly happens after email clicks.
  • Branded output: each song has its own page, and when customers share it on Facebook it shows "Make your own at Forever Song Studio"; this kind of share is visible on several ordinary users' profiles.
  • Review asset: actively invites reviews, with about 2,000 Trustpilot reviews at 4.9 shown on the homepage as social proof.

Speculation:

  • The first 1,000 users came from Facebook/Instagram ads in January-February 2026 (around Valentine's Day); no raw ad library data found (the aurapesca advertiser directory returned 403).
  • The rename to Songs by Sophie may be to avoid a trademark/brand conflict with ForeverSongs.com and to build trust with a "real person Sophie" persona; not verified.
  • Songs are most likely generated by an AI music model and then edited by hand, which is low cost and suited to buying ads at scale.

Revenue and scale clues

  • About 2,000 Trustpilot reviews (mostly from 2026-07 to October); at a 10-20% review rate that implies 10-20K orders; at about $100 per order, cumulative revenue would be about $1-2 million (pure speculation, order of magnitude only).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Domain registration, Wayback, the redirect and Trustpilot data are first-hand evidence; the Facebook posts come only from search snippets.
  • Search results are mostly similar competitors (ForeverSongs, My Forever Songs) and unrelated content.
  • Gaps: operator identity, exact price, ad spend, reason for the rename.
