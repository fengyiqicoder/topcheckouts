Not an independent small site that grew from zero through self-spreading: its parent company FlowGPT (a prompt / small AI app sharing community launched in 2023-01, which built up millions of users and creators on "jailbreak prompts + AI girlfriend/boyfriend characters" content just as ChatGPT took off) pulled the most profitable part of the community, "uncensored character companion chat", out into the Emochi app in 2024, and migrated FlowGPT's creators and character library over as content supply. In 2025–2026 it then used large-scale mobile paid ads (mainly AppLovin) plus user-built character UGC to reach 2M DAU and $45M ARR. Basics

  • Product: companion chat / role-play platform with user-built AI characters (mostly anime/fantasy characters, marketed as "unrestricted / lightly censored"), app + web.
  • Company: Kaon AI (formerly FlowGPT Co.), California, USA (started in Berkeley, R&D center now in San Mateo). Products: FlowGPT, Emochi, Tickle, Branches.
  • Founders: Jay Dang (UC Berkeley CS dropout, CEO), Lifan Wang (former Amazon engineering manager, Berkeley MBA, COO); CTO Alex Xi.
  • Funding: early investment from DCM (2023); $10M Pre-A on 2024-02-26 (led by Goodwater, DCM following); about $60M Series B on 2026-07-08 (B Capital, Redpoint Ace, Goodwater, DCM; reported by Variety, valuation "hundreds of millions of dollars", Caplight estimates $400M).
  • Domain: emochi.com registered 2014-04-27 (GoDaddy, an old domain with other people's content since 2004); Wayback shows the first Emochi product page on 2024-05-23 ("FlowGPT's App - Emochi, is now available! Be among the first 1000 users to download Emochi, the uncensored chatbot app").
  • iOS App ID 6476081894 (developer FlowGPT Co., the ID range corresponds to creation around early 2024); Android package name com.flow.mobile (reuses FlowGPT's mobile package, 10M+ downloads).

"The first 1000 users": Emochi's own first users came from traffic redirected from FlowGPT's existing millions of users and creators (the landing page literally says "FlowGPT's App"), which is a migration of an existing audience; FlowGPT's own first users came from prompt-sharing communities such as Discord in early 2023 (the Haas report only mentions Discord interviews; the specific launch post was Not found).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • UGC supply loop: users/creators build characters → others chat with those characters → creators get tips/incentives (FlowGPT already had tips; the Emochi homepage has a campaign to "refer creators to join and earn rewards together").
  • Parent site traffic: FlowGPT community → Emochi app (stated explicitly on the landing page).
  • Paid ads: programmatic mobile ads such as AppLovin/AdMob/Moloco are one of the main sources of new users (Insightrackr), which contradicts the initial assumption of "no ad spend".
  • Search: Semrush estimates about 92.5% of US keyword traffic is brand keywords, showing web visits are mostly known users returning or app users switching to the web.

Speculation:

  • Character share links and public profiles/leaderboards (a known mechanism, public_profile_leaderboard) let creators post their characters to Discord/TikTok/YouTube to drive traffic; "uncensored" spreads by word of mouth in the Character.AI refugee community on Reddit (no specific posts found, Reddit API blocked).
  • Traffic to the web Stripe payment page may come from users who moved to web subscriptions after the iOS app was pulled (iOS removal in 2026-10 is close in time to the September data, so this is speculation).

Revenue and scale clues

  • $45M ARR, 2M+ DAU, 150 minutes per day on average (company-reported, 2026-07).
  • "6M+ users worldwide" (subscription page marketing copy); Google Play 10M+ downloads, rating about 4.4–4.6.
  • Pricing: Free $0 (with ads) / Plus $9.99 / Ultra $16.99 (includes 2000 Mochi tokens).
  • Semrush web visits: 2026-07 13.7M → 08 18.4M → 09 23.1M (third-party estimate, retold by aiidelist, not verified by logging in).
  • Median user age 23–25, the US is the largest market.

Confidence and gaps

  • Company, funding and ARR come from press releases and Variety retellings, medium-high credibility; Appfigures/Insightrackr data are secondhand retellings.
  • Gaps: the exact date of Emochi's official launch and the launch-channel posts (Reddit/Discord) were Not found; the specific rules of the referral rewards were Not found; "released in 2025-09" conflicts with the app already being listed in 2024, possibly a repositioning/redesign.
  • Judgment: this is a venture-backed company product with large-scale paid ads, not a "pure self-spreading" sample; its reference value lies mainly in the step of "existing community audience → splitting out a vertical high-paying scenario".
