Overview

Emochi (emochi.com): Free platform for chatting and roleplaying with user-created AI characters. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 30,086 visits went from Emochi to a checkout payment page — #90 of the 607 sites we track. That is 13% up from the April–June monthly average of 26,700.

Total website visits that month were about 13,476,051 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 81%, search 18%, social 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 98%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
