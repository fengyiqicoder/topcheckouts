Basics

  • Product: the documentary series "Reality Revolt — See In The Dark," 11 episodes (The Game, The Narrative, Transhumanism, Satanic Rituals, Mind Control, Predictive Programming, Feminism, Gender Confusion, Transvestigation, Ancient Knowledge, The Code), about 6 hours; a one-time payment for lifetime viewing. There is also a subscription "Reality Shelter" members area (Le Carnet + a private discussion group) and a "Founding Member" early-bird plan. The site has English/French/Spanish/German/Portuguese/Arabic language switching.
  • Seller: REALITY FILMS LLC, registered in Wyoming (Sheridan registered agent address), contact@realityrevolt.com; terms updated 2026-06. Payment via Stripe or USDT/Bitcoin. Prices are shown in euros, but the specific amounts are not displayed on the page.
  • Creator: Dimitri R, social media name Dimitri Oswald; Instagram @reality.revolt has about 2.66M-3M followers (HypeAuditor 2026-10 says 2,663,518, ranked 2369th among global IG influencers), Facebook has 3,000,722 followers; X @reality_revolt_; YouTube @seeinthedark. Content is bilingual in French and English.
  • Domain: RDAP shows registered 2025-07-29 (Squarespace), but Wayback has captures from 2018-08 and 2024-11 and the footer says "© 2024-2026," so it is speculated to have been in use in 2024, with 2025-07 possibly a transfer or re-registration.
  • Our data: United States 50.84%, France 25.26%, Australia 12.42%; paid social 48.5%, direct 35.3%, organic social 9.7% (X, Facebook, YouTube), search terms are basically brand terms like reality revolt and dimitri revolt.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Traffic from owned followers: IG, FB, X and YouTube Shorts content all end with "Watch my docu Series realityrevolt.com"; a single Reel can reach 600K likes.
  • Paid promotion: paid social at 48.5% in our data is the top source (speculated to be ads on hit Reels/FB videos pointing to the site).
  • Riding news events: uses hot topics like Charlie Kirk and celebrity political clashes for "it's actually scripted" interpretations to attract new followers.
  • Brand search: the search terms are all the creator's name and site name, showing people watch a video and then come to search.

Speculation:

  • The first paying users came from the French-language followers already built in 2024-2025 (France is 25%), and English content then expanded to the US and Australia.
  • Conspiracy content naturally provokes controversial comments, and the comment volume (19K) boosts algorithmic distribution, forming a loop of "video blows up -> followers grow -> ad spend converts."
  • Crypto payment is to cope with payment channel risk (the content is sensitive).

Revenue and scale clues

  • One-time purchase + subscription membership, priced in euros, prices not public.
  • About 3M followers (FB 3,000,722; IG 2.66M-3M).
  • Revenue and number of buyers not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The seller company, product form, payment methods, follower counts and the funneling wording all have primary sources; social posts are mostly search snippets (IG/X/FB would not open).
  • The domain registration date contradicts the earlier archive, so the starting year is counted from the footer's 2024.
  • Gaps: the creator's real identity, when he started making videos, prices, number of paying users, ad budget.
