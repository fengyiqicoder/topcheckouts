Basics

  • Product: AI TikTok video generation, primarily in French (also EN/ES/DE interfaces): generates a script from a link or topic, AI voiceover, auto-editing, pitched as "faceless" and "monetizable" TikTok content; includes small tools like "Caption Mask"; also sells "TikTok accounts with monetization already enabled" (250€, member code CORTIA35 takes 35€ off).
  • Who built it: operating entity CORTIA SAS, share capital 1,000€, address 47 rue Vivienne, Paris (a shared registered address), RCS Paris 107 174 419, incorporated 2026-07-03; president Florian Matthieu Jouel (born 2000), managing director Tasnim Bennacer (born 2000). Sources: the site's CGV (text inside the JS bundle) + French company registry API (recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr). The YouTube channel "Flojo | TikTok Money" (@tikproelite) has repeatedly posted "Le site que j'utilise chaque jour pour faire mes TikToks: cortia.io"; Flojo is Florian Jouel (names match, speculation).
  • Pricing (site CGV): subscriptions TIK 17€/mo (250 credits), PRO 37€/mo (600), ELITE 77€/mo (1,500), roughly 15% off annually; credit packs from 100 credits for 15€ to 1,000 credits for 100€.
  • Domain: cortia.io registered 2025-09-08 (WHOIS, Hostinger); no Wayback snapshots (pure front-end SPA).
  • Traffic (profile data): France 95.6%, Turkey 4.4%; direct 62.2%, search 28.4% (all brand keywords "cortia", "cortia tiktok"), referral 5.7%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder-led content selling: Flojo posts daily "earnings screenshot + tutorial" shorts that end by pointing to cortia.io; Cortia's own TikTok/Instagram post the same kind of content.
  • Affiliates: the page integrates FirstPromoter; affiliate page copy promises 30% lifetime recurring commission (currently marked "coming soon" in the code).
  • Upsell: sells monetization-enabled TikTok accounts at a member price, raising average order value.

Speculation:

  • Viral videos students make with Cortia become the next round of "earnings screenshot" material, closing a "tutorial -> tool -> student case -> tutorial" loop.
  • Once affiliates open, the model gets replicated to other French-language "make money on TikTok" creators.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public user numbers. Skool community 132 members; YouTube 9.1K subscribers; official Instagram only 21 followers.
  • Price points 17-77€/mo, plus account sales at 250€ each. Scale is most likely in the hundreds of paying users (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were mostly irrelevant pages (cortia.dev, courtyard.io, etc.); main evidence comes from the CGV/pricing/affiliate text in the site's JS bundle, the French company registry API, WHOIS, YouTube RSS, and the Skool page.
  • Flojo being Florian Jouel is inferred from the matching name and the channel naming the site; he has not been seen stating it himself.
  • Gaps: number of paying users, exact launch date (whether there were users before the SAS was formed), official TikTok follower count (not captured from the page).
