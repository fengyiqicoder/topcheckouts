Overview

Ausar (ausar.ai): AI content studio generating video, images and audio from multiple third-party models with one credit balance. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 13,433 visits went from Ausar to a checkout payment page — #212 of the 607 sites we track. That is 11094% up from the April–June monthly average of 120.

Total website visits that month were about 41,952 (estimated from global rank). Roughly 32.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 43%, direct 43%, referrals/email/paid 9%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 86%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $9/month (billed yearly $108/yr); $15/month (Starter，billed yearly $180/yr).
