Overview

Gimkit (gimkit.com): Teacher starts a game, the whole class joins with a code; ~7 people, no funding. Category on TopCheckouts: Education & exams.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Gimkit to a checkout payment page — #453 of the 607 sites we track. That is 10% down from the April–June monthly average of 9,544.

Total website visits that month were about 4,598,480 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 56%, search 36%, referrals/email/paid 8%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows built-in sharing (strong), and direct plus brand search is about 92%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
