Overview

Rekonise (rekonise.com): Link tool that locks content behind social actions (follow, subscribe, join Discord) to grow audiences. Category on TopCheckouts: Creator tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 14,556 visits went from Rekonise to a checkout payment page — #209 of the 607 sites we track. That is 78% up from the April–June monthly average of 8,157.

Total website visits that month were about 1,115,574 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 1.3 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 60%, social 31%, referrals/email/paid 5%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 60%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $0 Free forever; $9.99/week Pro; $29.99/mo Pro; $149 one-time Lifetime.
