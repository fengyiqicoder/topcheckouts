Overview

Xivmodarchive (xivmodarchive.com): Searchable community archive of 98K+ user-submitted Final Fantasy XIV mods. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,524 visits went from Xivmodarchive to a checkout payment page — #546 of the 607 sites we track. That is 57% down from the April–June monthly average of 10,615.

Total website visits that month were about 531,305 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 0.9 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 85%, search 13%, social 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 93%, plus shares via Discord. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
