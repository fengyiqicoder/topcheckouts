FF14 mods used to be scattered across the "mod release" channels of Discord servers such as TexTools and could not be searched. In October 2018 developer Sel built a Discord bot that automatically pulls mod posts made by authors in those channels into a website, with Discord login and filters by race/gender/type; it launched with 2,297 mods. From then on authors only had to post in Discord and the site filled itself (3k in 2019-01 → 18k in 2021-01 → 60k in 2024-01 → 98k in 2026-10). Whenever the player community or Reddit asked "where do I find FF14 mods", people pointed to it, and it became one of the two big FF14 mod libraries alongside Nexus. It is sustained by Patreon subscriptions (about 1,860 members).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: FF14 player mod index/archive (outfits, hairstyles, body types, animations, UI, etc.), Discord login; adult (NSFW) mods are visible only when logged in. This investigation only looked at the home page, tools page, ToS and Wayback home-page text; no mod content was opened.
  • Mechanism: the home page says "This site is automatically curated via a Discord bot… If you want the bot to record mod releases in your server, you must contact Sel… All mod releases must be posted by their original authors only". It also offers a Discord bot (!xma search) and a "Mod Post Formatter" to help authors keep post formats consistent.
  • Relationships: the tools page lists the TexTools website and the "Official TexTools Discord" first, and automatically syncs the TexTools Discord #tools_releases channel (https://www.xivmodarchive.com/tools). Sel's own relationship with the TexTools team is, from memory, that he is one of the TexTools maintainers (not verified).
  • Operator: the ToS says "operated by Out Of Nothing Designs LLC", last updated 2019-12-20 (https://www.xivmodarchive.com/tos.txt).
  • Revenue: Patreon (https://www.patreon.com/xivmodarchive), creator Sel, 1,863 members, from $1/month; subscribers' mods are pinned in "New and Updated from our Patreon Subscribers" on the home page and there is a "sponsored" filter.
  • Domain: registered 2018-10-01 per RDAP; first Wayback capture 2018-10-20.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): US 50%, UK 8.4%, Germany 6%; direct 85.2%, search 12.8%; referrals trello, patreon, nexusmods.

Where the first users came from: the evidence points to the Discord mod-release channels. It launched on day one with 2,297 mods, which means the existing posts from an existing Discord community were ingested in one go, and players learned about it through a Discord announcement (the home page says "There will be an announcement in discord").

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Automated creator supply: authors post mods in the formatted style in partner Discord servers → the bot ingests them automatically → site content grows → more players come looking → more authors are willing to post in the format.
  • Bot in servers: any server can add the XMA bot to use!xma search, embedding the site in other Discords.
  • Community word of mouth: in "where to find mods" threads on Reddit r/ffxiv and r/ffxivdiscussion it is routinely recommended, on par with Nexus.
  • Patreon pinning: paying authors' new releases appear on the home page, giving authors a reason to subscribe.

Speculation:

  • From 2023 the Penumbra/Mare plugin ecosystem lowered the barrier to modding and sped up mod counts; but the profile shows recent visits falling (-56.8% from two months ago to last month), cause not investigated.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Patreon: 1,863 members, from $1; at an average of $3–5/month, monthly revenue would be about $5k–9k (speculation; paying-member count and tier distribution not verified).
  • Mod library: 98k entries.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Launch time, bot mechanism, mod-count curve, operator and Patreon scale all have primary page evidence, so credibility is fairly high.
  • Gaps: Sel's real identity and relationship to TexTools not verified; the original Reddit posts would not open, so only summaries were used; the text of the first Discord announcement was not found.
  • Content note: the site hosts adult NSFW mods (visible only when logged in); no mod pages were opened this time; no illegal-content leads that would require stopping the investigation were found.
