Basics

  • Product: OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible unified LLM gateway; one key and one balance to call GPT, Claude, Gemini, Kimi, Grok, Qwen, DeepSeek and more; model catalog sorted by the Artificial Analysis intelligence index; side-by-side Playground comparison (launched 2026-10-20); Connect CLI configures Claude Code/Codex/Cursor/opencode with one command; TH Orchestra auto-selects models.
  • Company/team: Token Harbor PTE. LTD. (Singapore), two people: William Song (PH Maker, @wsongth) and Erika (PH Hunter, @erika_th) (MakerStack, Product Hunt). Reddit founder post titled "started as a side project 5 months [ago]".
  • Launch: domain registered 2026-04-17 (RDAP, GoDaddy privacy protection); PH launch 2026-08-06; first Wayback capture 2026-08-10; free-tier gateway launched 2026-09-16.
  • Pricing: prepaid per token, claims upstream list prices with no markup; new accounts get $5 welcome credit, no card; $2 back per invited user who activates; first top-up match and spend milestones unlock free models; free tier deepseek-v4.1-flash:free and mimo-v2.6-flash:free, 7-day rolling quota, 60 requests/minute; our data also records Agent Pass $0.99 first month then $1.99/month, Office $9.99/month.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Launch: Product Hunt 2026-08-06, the only recorded formal launch channel (MakerStack: "Product Hunt launch is the only channel named").
  • Referral cashback: $2 for each invited user who activates, $5 for new accounts with no card; this is exactly why the bulk sign-up script appeared.
  • Free model tier: from September the "free DeepSeek" offer was reposted repeatedly by X creators, awesome free lists, and Chinese/German/Russian freebie and review sites, becoming the main entry point for new users; social sources are X, YouTube, Reddit, Threads.
  • Listed on third-party checker sites (BazaarLink, ModelVerify), entering the API relay price-comparison scene.
  • Speculation:
  • The high Indonesia/India share suggests the free credits spread among Southeast and South Asian developer groups (Threads, Telegram, YouTube tutorials).
  • The cheap Agent Pass converts free-tier users into small subscriptions, with scale coming from volume.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public user numbers or revenue.
  • About 90 PH votes and 134 followers, a very small scale; the real volume comes from reposts of the free tier.
  • Revenue model: prepaid usage + cheap subscription; free-tier costs are covered by upstream free models (DeepSeek's and MiMo's free promotional tiers), with data retained after user consent.

Confidence and gaps

  • Team, company and PH launch date are corroborated by MakerStack and the PH page; the PH page's daily ranking link points to 10-24, contradicting MakerStack's 8-06; the same-day MakerStack review is taken as authoritative.
  • The original Reddit founder post and the viral X post could not be opened, only summaries; repost volume unclear.
  • "The free tier drove the main growth" is inferred from the timeline and traffic countries; no official figures.
