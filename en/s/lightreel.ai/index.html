Basics

  • Product: an AI agent that searches thousands of TikToks / Reels in real time (about 10K new per day) to answer questions like "which app hooks are working this week", "find skincare creators in LA with 20–80K followers" or "why did my video only get 200 views". Also offers virality prediction, alerts, team sharing, Meta ad library analysis (claims to have analyzed 3M+ Meta ads), an API, an MCP connector, and an iMessage version ("runs your UGC from iMessage"). Publishes a daily "top UGC video of the day" page (lightreel.ai/top-video) that doubles as SEO content.
  • Company: Lightreel LLC (terms updated 2026-09-15); LinkedIn says founded 2026, headquartered in Dallas, Texas, 2–10 people.
  • Founder: Jibran Khalil (UT Austin 2019–2022; previously built iOS apps such as Peeko and Scan To Sheets), X @jibran_05. Funding not found; Frontrun.vc records 17 tracked investors following @lightreelai since 2026-06-30 (8,731 X followers as of 2026-10-19), showing VCs have taken notice, but there is no public funding.
  • Self-reported: used by 4,000+ brands and agencies, with customer cases totaling "1B+ / 600M+ / 700M+ views".

Growth loop

  • Evidence:

1. Back-to-back launch videos on X: every new feature gets an "Introducing the first AI that…" short video, three times on 7-23, 8-10 and 9-17, the last with 1.34M views, each one pushing its visibility in the X growth community up another notch.
2. Customers (app founders, UGC agencies) publicly show "X million views from finding creators / hooks with Lightreel", and Lightreel reposts to amplify.
3. Listed in tool directories such as creatorstoolbox.com; in Similarweb referrals creatorstoolbox is 6.46% and viralbench.ai 8.91%.
4. The API + MCP connector get it embedded in other people's AI workflows, riding the buzz of the Claude ecosystem.
5. A fellowship cohort for community building.
6. The daily public "top UGC video of the day" page brings search traffic.

  • Speculation: the UGC scene is itself a community that "posts growth methodology as content", and operators mention the tool while posting methodology, which amounts to free distribution; "A major competitor shut down" (Queue's testimonial) suggests it also caught users flowing out after a competitor folded; there are YouTube review videos like "I hired a content strategist for $200/month" that are most likely about it (the $199 price matches) but were not opened to confirm.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: Pro $199/month, 3-day free trial, 7-day refund.
  • Self-reported 4,000+ brands / agencies (including trials, unverified). If there were 1,000 paying users, MRR would be about $200K (pure speculation).
  • Monthly checkout visits grew from 11K to 34–39K; 8,731 X followers.

Confidence and gaps

  • Medium-high. Founder, pricing, customer circle and the dates and view counts of the three X launches all have evidence (the original X posts are unreadable; search summaries and launchvideo's third-party stats were used). Funding and real paying-user numbers still not found.
