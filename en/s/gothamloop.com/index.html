In October 2024, xAI co-founder Greg Yang tweeted that interview cheaters told him the Chinese site 1point3acres (一亩三分地) was "the most comprehensive compendium of interview questions" (2.4M views), which for the first time made the English-speaking world broadly aware that 1point3acres had up-to-date real interview questions. In November 2025 someone registered gothamloop.com and used a screenshot of that tweet as the home-page selling point, building an "English version of 1point3acres": interview reports from 1point3acres, Blind and private Discords are translated, organized and split into pages by company, sold at $199/month or a "today only" $329 lifetime. From March 2026 Blind posts named it as an "English version of 1point3acres / cheating site"; visits peaked in July and then declined. Basics

  • Product: a "leaked" software engineer interview question bank organized by company (the 2026-10 home page claims 168+ companies, e.g. Amazon 87 questions, Google 84, OpenAI 77, Uber 90, Jane Street 26), with a demo subsite demo.gothamloop.com and an app at app.gothamloop.com; each company has its own SEO page (e.g. /waymo: "57 verified questions from the last 14 days").
  • Claimed question sources: the home page says "Monitor gated communities… 1Point3Acres, Blind, and private Discord groups"; the 2026-01 version said a monthly drawing gives away over $10,000 in Amazon gift cards to encourage candidates to share interview questions, and the 2026-07 version changed to "we pay recent hires… to tell us what they were asked".
  • Price: $199/month; lifetime $800 struck through → $329 (permanently shown as "Today only / only 20 spots left / countdown 3h45m23s"; the countdown number is identical in the January, April, July and October snapshots, so it is a fixed sales pitch); paid through Stripe, with a link carrying the MMM2026 coupon code.
  • Self-reported figures: 2026-07 "3,800+ SWEs hired, average TC +$122K, rating 4.93"; 2026-10 "5,450+ engineers hired… 4.93/5 from 1,286 reviews" (all self-reported, not verified).
  • Team: not public, contact email team@gothamloop.com; has a "Piracy Bounty Program".
  • Domain: registered 2025-11-04 per RDAP; first Wayback capture 2026-01-18.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): US 94.9%; 15,188 visits in July then declining (4,522 in September, -52.6% month over month); direct 65.5%, search 25.9% (brand keywords 91.4%); social 5.2% from Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn.

Where the first users came from: no direct evidence. All that can be confirmed is: (1) the whole positioning and home-page copy borrow directly from Greg Yang's viral tweet and 1point3acres' name recognition in the English-speaking world; (2) from 2026-03 the Blind job-seeker community lists it among "cheating sites"; (3) the profile shows social sources Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Riding upstream: 1point3acres + the Greg Yang tweet serve as trust endorsement, quoted in full on the home page.
  • Company-page SEO: one "X software engineering interview questions" page per company, already indexed by search engines.
  • Conversion copy: a perpetual "today only" lifetime price, self-reported hire counts and TC increases.
  • Question-source incentives: gift-card drawing / paying for questions, getting new hires to supply them.

Speculation:

  • Main acquisition probably comes from "where to find the latest interview reports" discussions on Blind and Reddit job-search subreddits (including soft promotion for competitors or itself); the high share of brand-keyword search suggests users saw the name in a community and then searched for it.
  • The decline after July may be due to competitors (PracHub, onsites.fyi, InterviewDB) taking share or negative community reviews ("few questions, scraped"); unconfirmed.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No reliable revenue data. If the self-reported "5,450+ engineers hired" each bought at least once at $329, sales would be in the millions of dollars, but this is a marketing number with low credibility.
  • Monthly visits on the profile basis: 4,500–15,000.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Registration time, the evolution of pricing and copy, and the Blind mention have primary page evidence.
  • Gaps: operator identity, launch channel and real number of paying customers were not found; the Greg Yang tweet was not opened directly (only seen as this site's screenshot quote); Reddit discussions did not appear in search results (the "reddit" search results were all unrelated CVE pages).
  • Risk note: the product sells "leaked interview questions", a gray area involving breach of interview confidentiality agreements and company intellectual property; the countdown and "Next 20 customers only" are fixed sales copy.
