Basics

  • Product: paste a YouTube link → choose start and end times → download an MP4/WEBM/MP3/AAC clip; no login, no ads, no watermark, works on mobile. Pro: up to 8K, priority processing, 3 parallel jobs, 5 clips per batch, multi-language audio tracks.
  • Pricing: Free (240p, some pages say 360p); Pro $4.99/month; Lifetime $29.99 one-time ("Special Offer"). No referral/affiliate program.
  • Team: unsigned, only support@trimtube.io; the blog has an unsigned "Founder's Note": "we didn't set out to start a company, but as users fed up with existing tools", naming dissatisfaction with YT Cutter's pop-ups and untrustworthiness. WHOIS privacy protection (Spaceship registrar; Gridinsoft shows the registration country as Iceland, while the old report recorded US), servers on DigitalOcean in New Jersey. Founder Not found.
  • Funding: no sign.
  • Note: trimtube.vercel.app and looptube.vercel.app are same-name/similar small tools by others, with no known relationship to this site.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • First users: Google search — an SEO comparison post existed on the second day after launch, followed by bulk tool pages, use-case pages and competitor-alternative posts; the channel profile shows search at 76.7% and non-brand about 77%, with main terms youtube video download without watermark / 4k / long video download.
  • No community launch, social media account or referral program (Gridinsoft also notes no social media information).
  • Return visits: direct traffic 17.4%, users come straight back when they need it.
  • Speculation:
  • The two months ago jump is the result of Google's "sandbox period" for new sites ending and rankings rising overall (about 8–9 months for a new domain).
  • When older downloader sites such as y2mate are blocked or unstable, replacement search traffic spills over; the clips it produces carry no watermark, so there is almost no in-product spread.

Revenue and scale clues

  • $4.99/month, $29.99 lifetime. 1–2 months ago checkout visits were about 17–18K per month, roughly a few thousand dollars in monthly revenue (speculation).
  • No public user count.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: confirmed the launch time (an SEO comparison post already existed on 2025-12-01) and that the launch was search, not any community platform; added the anonymous "Founder's Note" (targeting YT Cutter) and the use-case page matrix; corrected the WHOIS country to Iceland (Gridinsoft), host DigitalOcean. Classification unchanged.
  • Still missing: the operator's identity, specific keyword ranking history, and the direct cause of the two months ago jump.
  • Risk: YouTube download tools are heavily affected by platform anti-scraping and copyright complaints, so sustainability is in doubt.
