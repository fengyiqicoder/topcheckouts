Basics

  • Product: all-in-one software for appointment-based small businesses: custom booking page, embeddable booking widget, payments and deposits, CRM, marketing, scheduling, analytics, AI assistant, free iOS app; 200 industry templates (2024). Supports English, Dutch, and Spanish.
  • Pricing: 30-day free trial (no card required), from $9.99/month (official site); when Vev+ launched in 2024-02 it was $8.99/month or $89.99/year. Trustpilot complaints mention the platform charging fees on the full service price.
  • Team: CEO and co-founder Michel Elings (former senior software manager at Apple); the team comes from Prss (an iPad magazine publishing tool acquired by Apple in 2014). About page: "While at Apple, we saw the importance of giving everyone access to the best tools."
  • Funding: €5 million in 2023-02 from Notion Capital and Point Nine Capital.
  • Headquarters: Utrecht, Netherlands.
  • Launch date: the vev.co domain was registered 2010-07-20 (WHOIS, presumably an old domain bought later); Wayback 2016–2022 shows only a placeholder page (titled simply "vev.co"), redirecting to the real site from 2023-02; they interviewed 350+ merchants before launch (press release).
  • Scale: nearly 20,000 merchants by 2024-02 (Europe and Latin America), hundreds of thousands of bookings/orders processed (press release); the site now says "thousands of entrepreneurs".
  • Users: Netherlands about 20%, United States about 15%, United Kingdom about 13% (our profile).
  • Note: VEV (vev.com, Jolt Capital investment 2026-09), Vevor, and Vevo in search results are unrelated companies.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Press spark: the 2023-02 funding news plus the "ex-Apple team" story, covered simultaneously by TechEU, Business Insider, and FD, served as the public launch moment.
  • Branded merchant pages: merchants send their vev booking/order page to customers (our profile: mainly direct and Instagram social), so end customers see Vev when booking.
  • Merchant stories and "Vev Awards" for community building, with content feeding back into Instagram.
  • SEO: English "free booking system" listicles and Spanish long-tail queries.

Speculation:

  • First 1,000 merchants: possibly from the 350+ merchants interviewed pre-launch and the local Dutch small business scene, with the free tier lowering the barrier; Latin American (Spanish-speaking) users may come from nail and hair circles recommending each other on Instagram. No direct evidence.
  • Free shop setup → customers see the booking page → peer merchants ask "what are you using" → sign up: a classic branded-output loop, but the embedded booking widget carries no Vev branding, which weakens the loop, hence Mixed.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Nearly 20,000 merchants by 2024-02; Vev+ at $8.99–9.99/month, plus transaction fees.
  • Raised €5 million (2023); no later funding found.
  • Revenue not found; assuming 5%–10% of merchants pay, annual subscription revenue would be in the hundreds of thousands of dollars (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Team, funding, launch date, and the 2024 merchant count all have first-hand sources in press releases and TechEU; credible.
  • Business Insider and X wouldn't load; the Trustpilot sample is only 5 reviews and doesn't represent overall reputation.
  • Gaps: where the Latin American growth came from, merchant count and paid conversion after 2024, whether there is paid advertising.
