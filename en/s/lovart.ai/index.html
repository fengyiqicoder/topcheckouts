Basics

  • Product: an AI design Agent + infinite canvas (Figma-like but web-only); type one sentence to generate posters, brand VI, menus, product images, and so on, and keep editing on the canvas; by 2026 it is at 'Lovart 2.0' (Skills, Pinterest assets, Clipper extension, delegated tasks).
  • Team: incubated by LiblibAI (哩布哩布, founded 2023-05), CEO Chen Mian (陈冕), formerly head of global commercialization for ByteDance's Jianying/CapCut. Claims a San Francisco headquarters externally (TC Brand Studio sponsored article). About half of users are in China (36Kr quoting an employee).
  • Funding: LiblibAI claims the highest total funding among China's AI image startups (amount undisclosed); Lovart's own funding was not found (the sponsored article says 'backed by several well-known investors').
  • Pricing: free quota + credit subscriptions (monthly/annual/team). https://www.lovart.ai/pricing

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Invite-code scarcity creates social currency (existing users hand codes to friends or resell them), a complete copy of the Manus playbook (36Kr).
  • Users showing off their work sets it off: the coffee-brand VI case got 1M+ views (TC sponsored article).
  • A Discord community catches the traffic (100K in 5 days).
  • Lots of third-party 'fake official site' sites: lovart.pro (calls itself the 'official website', with a 'Lovart Promo Code' page, assorted directory badges, and UTM links), lovart.me, lovart.fyi, lovart.art, and others. Some of the traffic from users searching the brand is intercepted by these sites and then sent back; in Similarweb referrals, lovart.io accounts for 3.49%, sonicourses.com for 2.96%, and saaspo.com for 4.38%. Such sites most likely drive traffic through affiliate commissions or promo codes (speculation; no commission terms seen).
  • Speculation:
  • LiblibAI's existing AI art community of tens of millions, plus tutorials from AI design creators on China's Xiaohongshu/Bilibili, is an ongoing source of acquisition (China being the main country supports this).
  • Design drafts are naturally suited to sharing as images, and 'made with Lovart' became a content tag, forming a weak branded-output loop.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 100K waitlist in 5 days of beta; claimed 800K users at the official launch; 300K visits in the first month.
  • ARR and paying user count not found.
  • From April to September 2026, checkout traffic fell from an average of 44K to 27-34K, so the buzz is past its peak.

Confidence and gaps

  • The main sources are 36Kr (editorial content, fairly credible) plus the TC Brand Studio sponsored article and press releases (company framing).
  • Second-pass check: all 5 sets of search results this time were the official site and imitation sites, with no new interviews or coverage. Re-read the original 36Kr article, confirmed the launch details (2025-05-13, 20K queued on day one, invite codes resold for 500 yuan, Discord 100K in 5 days, 300K visits in the first month), and added two founding details: 'was already planning an Agent before Manus launched' and 'three weeks -> one month sprint'. 36Kr does not mention Xiaohongshu or KOL acquisition, so the 'Xiaohongshu spread' in the old report remains speculation. Also found the brand-keyword imitation / promo-code site cluster (lovart.pro /.me /.fyi /.art). The classification is unchanged.
  • This round's WebSearch quota ran out, so Lovart's ARR, separate funding, and Xiaohongshu spread data in China could not be checked.
