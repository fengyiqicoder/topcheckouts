Basics

  • Product: National Fund For Women Foundation, 501(c)(3), EIN 39-2230375, Washington, DC. Membership $1.25/month or $15/year, entering monthly microgrant drawings ($100–5,000), brand discounts (e.g. 20% off Hoopsy in 2026-10) and the "Zero Dollar Store"; Founding Member $100/year, including sponsorship of 5 other women's memberships; those who can't pay can join a waitlist for sponsorship. Limited to US citizens or permanent residents aged 18+.
  • Founder: Rebecca Lee Funk (President & Founder), Yale master's, former development economist; founded the feminist T-shirt brand The Outrage in 2016-10, went viral after the 2016 election with a campaign donating to Planned Parenthood "in the name of Trump/Pence", collaborated with Naturalizer in 2020.
  • Team: at least a Marketing and Growth Director "Kathleen" (SOW document); outsourced social media content contractor with a budget of $1,500–2,000/month.
  • Funding: nonprofit, no external funding seen; goal is to become a financially independent permanent institution by 2028.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Social media is the main battleground: high-frequency Reels on IG / FB / TikTok, with ManyChat comment-to-DM funnels and paid boosts (SOW); IG reached about 36K followers in half a year.
  • Our channel data: display ads 5.6%; direct 62.7% (in-app jumps from social apps are often counted as direct).
  • $100 Founding Member = own membership + sponsoring 5 women; a free waitlist collects people waiting for sponsorship.
  • "micro grants" non-brand search about 9%; listings on grant aggregators bring search traffic.
  • Brand discount partners (Hoopsy etc.) cross-promote in partnership posts.
  • Speculation:
  • The first members came from the founder's existing The Outrage audience and email list + early paid boosts; the last month jump may be scaled-up boosts or a Reel going viral, no specific post found.
  • Grant recipients sharing their stories drive reposts.
