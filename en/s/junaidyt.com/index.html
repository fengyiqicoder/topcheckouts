Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Direct-response funnel: VSL video, countdown, scarce spots, fake original price, sign-up pop-ups and a refund promise, one page to the bottom with a single "Join Now" button.
  • Localized payments: mobile wallets + bank transfer + screenshot upload lower the payment barrier for Pakistani users.
  • Community delivery: Facebook group + WhatsApp group + weekly livestream, with student results ("a new channel got 10 hours of watch time in a week") used as testimonials on the page.
  • Search: in our data the non-brand keyword "18 days formula youtube" is about 18.5% and brand keywords 66%, showing people see him on social media and then search his name and the course name.
  • Price cut for volume: after PKR 5,800 → 3,900, self-reported students rose from 4,700 to 8,550 in the two and a half months from 2026-02 to 2026-04.

Speculation:

  • Volume mainly comes from Facebook/Instagram paid ads: Facebook is the first option in the sign-up form's source list, the funnel shape is a typical Meta ad landing page, and the Pakistani online-education scene commonly does this. No ad library evidence was obtained.
  • The first 1,000 students (about 4 months, 2024-11 to 2025-02) probably came from followers of his personal YouTube/Facebook accounts plus small ad tests.
  • The student count staying around 8,550 for a long time after 2026-04 may mean the page wasn't updated, or that growth slowed.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Self-reported 8,555+ students. At an average of PKR 4,500, cumulative revenue would be about PKR 38M, roughly $130-140K (pure speculation; the self-reported student count may be padded).
  • New students from 2026-02 to 04: about 3,850 x PKR 3,900 ≈ PKR 15M (about $53K), corresponding to about $20K/month (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there was no old report in the notes. Of the search results only the official site, checkout page, refund page and Facebook page were useful; the rest were scam-checker sites and unrelated sites; the work relied mainly on the official site, checkout page, Wayback snapshots from 2025-02/2026-02/2026-04, and RDAP.
  • The timeline, prices and self-reported student counts have snapshot evidence; the acquisition channel is inferred from the funnel shape and the sign-up form.
  • Gaps: Junaid's YouTube/Instagram/TikTok accounts and follower counts; whether he is running ads in the Meta Ad Library; the real student count.
