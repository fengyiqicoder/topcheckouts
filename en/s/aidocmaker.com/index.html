The domain was registered in August 2023, catching the window of the ChatGPT plugin store (autumn 2023) and the GPT Store (opened 2024-01-10) with a plugin and a set of GPTs that "generate PDF/Word/PPT/spreadsheets from one sentence inside ChatGPT". Every generated file is delivered through an aidocmaker.com download link (link paths are coded by channel: a1/m1/e1/s1… and g1/g2/g3… which appeared two days after the GPT Store opened), so the huge number of users inside ChatGPT were routed to its own domain. After the GPT Store hype faded, it turned the same "free, no sign-up, one-click documents" into a standalone website, with the homepage going after core keywords like "ai pdf maker" and "ai document generator", then expanding inner pages for PPT, spreadsheets, voice and more by keyword group, becoming a subscription site driven mainly by direct visits + search.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI document generator - enter a topic/text to generate reports, PDFs, Word files, PPTs, spreadsheets and voice, with file attachments, 50+ languages, free download without sign-up; also an AI Chat page (title says "Powered by Gemini").
  • Who makes it: footer "© 2026 AI Doc Maker · Based in California", with no founder or company entity listed; support uses Intercom. On payment provider DigitalPurse's "successful merchants" list AiDocMaker ranks #21, right next to AI Black Magic (#20), and the two may share an operator (speculation).
  • Pricing: Starter is free forever (100 credits/month); Standard and Premium are annual subscriptions, with a 7-day trial on paid plans (profile file).
  • Endorsements: homepage badge "Featured on OpenAI's ChatGPT Plugin Store and GPT Store"; media logos Wired, PC Guide, Geeky Gadgets, Beebom, etc.
  • Domain: aidocmaker.com registered 2023-08-22 (RDAP); the earliest Wayback capture is a 2023-09-05 download link /a2/file, with the first homepage snapshot on 2023-10-19, titled "Doc Maker - Document Creation Powered by ChatGPT".
  • Traffic (profile file): India 21%, US 13%, Nigeria 6%; direct 58.2%, search 36.4% (non-brand about 14.6%: ai pdf maker / text to pdf ai / ai pdf generator), AI 5.4% (ChatGPT 3.35%).

Where the first users came from: the ChatGPT plugin store (the site says it was listed on the Plugin Store and GPT Store). After the plugin/GPT generated a document in ChatGPT it gave an aidocmaker.com download link, and users who clicked the link arrived at the website - from 2023-09 Wayback captured many such channel-coded file links. The "Doc Maker" plugin ranked fairly high in the 2023 ChatGPT plugin store (from memory, not verified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Branded output: every generated document is a download link on aidocmaker.com, and when users forward the link to classmates/colleagues it sends traffic to the site (Wayback has indexed 800+ such file links).
  • Platform distribution: catching two windows, ChatGPT Plugin Store → GPT Store; the site still carries the "Featured on GPT Store" badge, and 3.35% of traffic still comes from ChatGPT.
  • Search ranking: the homepage title and content target the core keywords "AI document generator / AI PDF maker", then standalone tool pages cover PPT, spreadsheet, voice and other keyword groups (confirmed by the title of Gefei's case).
  • No-sign-up free download lowers the barrier, and payment converts to subscriptions via the credit cap.

Speculation:

  • The letters in the link paths (a/m/e/s/p/g) correspond to different distribution channels or different GPTs, used to track the performance of each channel.
  • Students in India and Nigeria make up a high share of users, with word of mouth spreading among student groups.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ranks 21st on DigitalPurse's successful merchants list (specific amounts not disclosed).
  • Subscription + free credits; paid prices not shown.
  • The site only says "trusted by thousands of users worldwide", with no specific user count.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Most search results were unrelated; channels were inferred mainly from the site, RDAP and Wayback CDX URL patterns.
  • "a/g codes correspond to plugins/GPTs" is inferred from timing (the g series appeared two days after the GPT Store opened) and has not been officially confirmed.
  • Gaps: founder identity, the specific names and conversation volume of the plugin/GPTs, the body of Gefei's case, and revenue figures were not found.
