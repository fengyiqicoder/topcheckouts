Basics

  • Product: a Dendreo-hosted extranet for students/instructors/corporate clients ("Accès à l'Extranet"); students view courses and materials, instructors manage courses, corporate clients follow up on employee training; the interface is available in French, English, Dutch, Spanish and Polish.
  • School: École Lyonnaise de Plantes Médicinales et Savoirs Naturels (ELPM), address 13 rue Alsace-Lorraine, 69001 Lyon. "Fondée il y a plus de 40 ans par Patrice de Bonneval au sein de son herboristerie lyonnaise"; now led by two professional herbalists, Patrice de Bonneval and Gilles Corjon, plus a director (directrice). https://ecoledeplantesmedicinales.com/qui-sommes-nous/
  • Courses: the flagship is the 3-year "Parcours Herbaliste" (in person in Lyon-Dardilly, in person at the southwest branch in Montégut, or online e-learning), plus "Vente-conseil nutrition & produits naturels", short courses, practical workshops and free webinars (e.g. "Le fenouil, ange ou démon"). It holds Qualiopi certification (France's quality certification for vocational training, required to be reimbursed with public funds).
  • Pricing: priced per course (PDF price list), installments available; vocational training is VAT-exempt and can be funded by third parties such as OPCO, employers and France Travail; enrollment fee of €20–80 per year. https://ecoledeplantesmedicinales.com/tarifs/
  • Traffic mix (profile record): search 69.5%, direct 22.8%, brand keywords 92.4%, non-brand search 0, no social; essentially students searching the school's name or clicking in from email to log in.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Alumni network: the school runs a "Réseau d'Herbalistes", a directory of current students and graduates who have opened practices, shown publicly, which creates word of mouth and referrals.
  • Offline events: student markets, public talks and free webinars to attract new students.
  • Industry endorsement: founding member of the federation and Qualiopi certified, so students can pay tuition with OPCO/employer funds.
  • The official homepage asks students to leave Google reviews.

Speculation:

  • New students mainly come from searches for "école herboristerie Lyon", word of mouth, and online courses extending the reach nationwide; the extranet is just a tool for students after enrollment.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No student numbers or revenue data; the official site's "people trained" counter shows 0 (not loaded).
  • Revenue comes from tuition for the 3-year course, short courses and practical workshops, partly funded by third parties.

Confidence and gaps

  • The classification mainly describes the school itself; the extranet as a login portal has no independent growth story, and the takeoff method is loosely classed as "Friends and local circle" (a herbal shop owner starting inside his own shop and recruiting by word of mouth).
  • This is a new site, written from scratch this time; the search results were all irrelevant, and the evidence comes from the extranet page, the school's official site (Qui sommes-nous, Tarifs, Parcours Herbaliste) and RDAP/Wayback.
