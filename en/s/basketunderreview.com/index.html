Basics

  • Product: independent college basketball (NCAA, men's + women's) outlet: articles (team previews, game analysis, transfer analysis, Bracketology, Bubble Watch), daily newsletter (plus a women's edition), 4 weekly shows (The Basket Under Review Podcast / STATS by Will & Jim / Scout Team / Weekend Under Review), paid Discord, app.basketunderreview.com (score data + CBB Grid, Wardle and Five-Out mini-games, in beta 2026-09).
  • Platform: Ghost ("Published with Ghost"); about 1,300 articles in total (sitemap-posts.xml, 2026-10).
  • Team: 30+ people on the authors page; core bylines Eamonn Brennan, 3MW (Jim Root, Will Warren), Kevin Sweeney, Eric Fawcett, Brian Rauf, Matthew Winick, Lukas Harkins, Trilly Donovan, Skim Milkey (women's) and others; "more than a dozen freelance writers." The site does not name a founder/owner.
  • Background (from memory, unverified): Eamonn Brennan previously covered college basketball at ESPN and The Athletic; Three Man Weave is a college basketball analysis podcast/site run for years by Jim Root and others; Kevin Sweeney previously wrote college basketball for Sports Illustrated and others.
  • Pricing: free content plus at least 1 free article per week; ad-free $2.99/month or $29.99/year; membership (includes Discord) $10/month or $100/year; also an affiliate program (apply via X DM).
  • Domain: WHOIS registration 2025-05-30; earliest Wayback snapshot 2025-07-15.
  • Traffic: direct 61%, search 29% (brand keywords 84%), social 5.8% (X); countries United States 50%, Spain 48% (the Spanish share is anomalous, possibly data noise or bot traffic).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Free podcasts (YouTube + podcast platforms) + free daily newsletter as the top of the funnel; paywall + Discord as the monetization layer.
  • The paid Discord sells "insider" material: coaching-change/recruiting intel (Trilly Donovan, Skim Milkey), 3MW betting picks, daily prediction contests and Top 25 votes.
  • Affiliate program: earn a cut for referring subscribers (https://www.basketunderreview.com/affiliates/).
  • Institutional partnership: weekly content with Belmont University, which the school's official channels repost.
  • Mini-game app (CBB Grid, Wardle); "basket under review cbb grid" already appears in search terms.

Speculation:

  • Strong seasonality: the November tip-off and March Madness are subscription peaks; the 2026-08/09 rise in checkout visits corresponds to pre-season renewals/presales.
  • Multi-author "content collective" model: each author reposts their own pieces on X, effectively several smaller accounts funneling traffic together.
