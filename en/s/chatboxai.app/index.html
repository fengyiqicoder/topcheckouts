Overview

Chatboxai (chatboxai.app): Cross-platform AI client app giving access to many AI models and APIs through one interface. Category on TopCheckouts: AI platform / API.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 21,490 visits went from Chatboxai to a checkout payment page — #136 of the 607 sites we track. That is 12% down from the April–June monthly average of 24,530.

Total website visits that month were about 254,812 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 8.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 72%, search 26%, AI assistants 2%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source is unclear: mostly direct visits (about 72%) with no visible invite or sharing mechanics, so new users likely come from word of mouth, private channels or off-site communities.
