Basics

  • Product: generates personalized songs from the story the user tells (multiple genres), with a free 60-second preview and a paid unlock for the full MP3 + lyrics; also photo karaoke videos and AI music videos. Occasions: birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, Valentine's Day, memorials.
  • Who built it: RedRoad AI LLC (contact page); Trustpilot lists it as a UK media company, and the page was claimed in 2026-01. Founder not found. Profile data shows people searching "redroad ai".
  • Pricing: from £9.99 per song (strikethrough price £19.99, "limited-time offer"), one-time payment, no subscription.
  • Domain: registered 2025-12-26 per Verisign RDAP; first Wayback snapshot 2026-01-10.
  • Traffic (profile data): US 44.5%, France 39.1%, UK 8.2%; direct 49.8%, referral 37.6%, paid 7.5%; search terms include "redroad ai" and "ai lyrics generator"; social is only Facebook.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Affiliate commissions: 20% for regular promoters, 25% for influencers, personal link melodybolt.com/yourname, withdrawable via Wise/bank transfer from £25 (https://www.melodybolt.com/affiliate); matches the profile's 37.6% referral traffic.
  • Branded output: users use the songs in Facebook videos and credit "Generated by melodybolt.com".
  • Try before paying: reviewers mention "being able to listen before buying" as a reason to try it.
  • Trust signals: proactively invites Trustpilot reviews and replies to 100% of negative reviews.
  • About 100 gift-occasion blog posts (SEO groundwork, though the profile shows very little non-brand search volume).

Speculation:

  • The gift nature creates second-order spread: "the recipient hears it, then wants to give one too". The profile classes new users as "viral" with nearly half of visits direct, but there is no specific data.
  • Holidays (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Christmas) are likely sales peaks.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public figures. Trustpilot has 81 reviews (in 10 months); at a typical 1-3% review rate, paid orders would be roughly in the thousands (speculation); average order about £10-£20.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch for a new site. Apart from the official site, Trustpilot, Facebook and YouTube, all search results were irrelevant; evidence comes from the official contact/affiliate pages, Trustpilot, RDAP, Wayback and the sitemap.
  • The takeoff event, the source of the French traffic and the team background were not found; the claim that affiliate commissions drive volume is inferred from the referral share and the affiliate page, and which promoters are involved was not found.
