In early 2025 it switched to "monthly-fee agency ad accounts" ($299–$4,999/month, 0% spend fee, unlimited replacements), and through 2025–2026 upgraded again into a self-described "compliant" Meta/TikTok whitelisted agency account service plus spend cashback and offline community events.   • Pricing: per a third-party roundup (marked as 2025-04 data) Bronze $299/month, Gold $599, Diamond $799, Platinum $2,499 (bring your own card + cashback), Mythic $4,999; Google Platinum $1,499 (sold out). Some plans are sold on Whop.
  • Who built it: co-founder Jan van Dijk (LinkedIn: Dutch background, Leiden University of Applied Sciences 2011–2015, company registered in Wyoming, USA; claims "$300M+/yr in ad spend"). Other co-founders not found. Trustpilot support staff names include Carmela, Daniel, Kristine, Olivia and Andrei.
  • Launch date: first Wayback capture 2024-03-29 ("Coming Soon"); full site in 2024-08.
  • Current numbers: Trustpilot 76 reviews at 4.6 (all within the past 12 months); homepage "$80M+", community "$500m+".
  • Traffic (profile): direct 72.4%, search 20.7% (brand keywords 93%: adrevival, adrevival pricing), paid search 4.1%; United States 62%, United Kingdom 10%, Germany 6%; Jul 23,611 → Sep 3,172 (-81%).

Where the first users came from: Discord. In 2024 every purchase path ran through Discord, and the customers were affiliate/e-commerce media buyers banned by Meta (the homepage at the time also carried promo links for anti-detect browsers and proxies). Which specific circle they came from not found; Speculation: word of mouth in affiliate-marketing/e-commerce Discord and Telegram circles plus the "top affiliates" endorsements.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Community operations: Discord is the single entry point for purchases, support and community; a "Leaderboard"; global offline community events; homepage cites "$500m+ community spend."
  • Scene endorsements: homepage "Vouched by the world's top affiliates."
  • The founder keeps posting spend numbers on LinkedIn ($30M/month, $180M per half-year).
  • Cashback hook: up to 2.3% spend cashback, more attractive the more you spend, locking in large clients.
  • Some plans listed on the Whop marketplace.

Speculation:

  • The product morphed from "selling black-hat accounts" into "compliant agency accounts," and the customer base moved up from small media buyers to brands/agencies spending millions per month, multiplying value per client.
  • The sharp drop in site traffic in September may reflect deals closing mainly in Discord/private community, or a business shift; unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Ad spend: $180M in H1 2026, record month $42M (founder's own LinkedIn claims).
  • Revenue model: monthly fees of $299–$4,999 plus payments/cashback spread; 158 clients spend over $100K per month. At an average fee of $1,000–2,000 and a few hundred clients, monthly revenue would be in the hundreds of thousands of dollars (our estimate, unverified).

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Useful search results were the founder's LinkedIn page, Trustpilot and a third-party pricing analysis (hiddenaio, itself affiliate content with contradictory numbers); the key business evolution comes from the Wayback snapshots of 2024-08 and 2025-01.
  • Other founders, company entity details and the specific source of first customers not found. All numbers are self-reported.
  • Note: the 2024 "buy BMs/aged pages + anti-detect browser" business was a gray-area operation for evading platform bans; this report only documents the growth path.
