This is not a startup product but the official website of the students' union at Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU, formerly Ryerson University). The union was founded in 1967 (originally the Ryerson Students' Union, RSU, old site rsuonline.ca); all full-time students are automatically members and pay the fee with tuition. After the university renamed itself in 2022, the union became TMSU, registered yourtmsu.ca on 2022-09-02 and launched in late September. Users come institutionally: new-student onboarding, links from the university site (pages like Wellbeing Central on torontomu.ca), Instagram (@yourtmsu, 24k followers) posting events, plus students landing via non-brand searches like "tmu clubs" and "greenshield toronto metropolitan university" (health and dental insurance).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Organization: Toronto Metropolitan Students' Union (TMSU), representing 38,000+ full-time undergraduates (site; Wikipedia says 45,000+); all full-time students must join and pay the fee (Wikipedia).
  • Site functions: club/student-group directory and funding, event sign-up (e.g. Terry Fox Run), health and dental insurance (Green Shield plan) opt-in/opt-out, food bank, 24/7 legal hotline and legal advice, printing services, eight equity service centres, election voting.
  • Fees: the site charges nothing; the union fee is collected with tuition, amount not found.
  • Predecessor and domain: in the RSU era it used rsuonline.ca (Wayback from 2006), a domain now taken over by an unrelated "Student Futures" career-training site. yourtmsu.ca registered 2022-09-02 (RDAP), first Wayback 2022-09-25.
  • Social: Instagram @yourtmsu (24k followers, 686 posts, search snippet), LinkedIn tmu-students-union.
  • Traffic (profile): 4,824 visits in 2026-09; Canada 86.1%, US 13.9%; search 59.5% (non-brand about 48%: tmu clubs, greenshield toronto metropolitan university, student clubs); direct 30.4%; social from Instagram, Reddit.

Where the first users came from: there was no "cold start"; students are members automatically, and the site is the portal that took over RSU's existing services after the rename. It launched right at the fall 2022 start of term, with new-student onboarding, university website links and the union's Instagram directing students to it.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Institutional distribution: mandatory membership + university website (Wellbeing Central and Student Groups pages on torontomu.ca) linking to union services.
  • Seasonal demand: insurance opt-outs, club recruitment and elections all require students to use the site → non-brand searches (tmu clubs, greenshield…).
  • Instagram with 24k followers posts events and service information.

Speculation:

  • Traffic follows the academic calendar (peaks at the September start of term, opt-out deadlines and spring elections).
  • Each year's incoming students automatically replenish the user pool, with no acquisition spend needed.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue comes from the (mandatory) union fee, amount not found; at 38,000 students paying tens to a hundred-plus Canadian dollars a year, annual fees would be in the millions of CAD (pure estimate, unverified).
  • Scale: 38,000+ members, Instagram 24k followers, about 4,800 monthly site visits.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Institutional background comes from Wikipedia and the site, domain dates from RDAP/Wayback, so credibility is high.
  • Most search results were irrelevant (CVE databases, dictionaries, etc.).
  • Gaps: exact date and resolution of the rename; fee amount and annual budget; when the old site rsuonline.ca was retired (the domain has changed hands).
