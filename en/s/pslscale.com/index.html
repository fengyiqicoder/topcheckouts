Basics

  • Product: upload a front-facing photo → 0–8 PSL score (PSL comes from the attractiveness scale on the PUAhate/SlutHate/Lookism forums, looksmaxxing community slang) + seven tier labels + 8-dimension explanation + improvement tips. Spin-off tools: True Adam/True Eve, Face Report, Camera PSL, face symmetry, face shape detection, golden ratio, Hunter Eyes, AI hairstyle change, PSL Rating Compare (two photos scored side by side, radar chart). Free in-browser face detection.
  • Pricing: credit-based; a regular analysis costs 10 credits, Face Report 200 credits, hairstyle change 4 credits; new users get 10 credits; guests pay $3 for a single full report.
  • Team: the site is unsigned (© 2025 pslscale.com). The HN launcher czmilo (registered 2025-03, about: "indie maker") has submitted only his own keyword sites: soraapp.org, soravideodownloader.com, curateclick.com, a2aprotocol.ai, openclawagent.net, randomtopicgenerator.net, decisionapi.org — a classic "chase hot keywords and build sites" matrix player. Nationality Not found (the site matrix includes Chinese-language pages, speculated to be a Chinese developer).
  • Funding: none.
  • Competition: many sites fight for the same term — pslscale.net, psl-scale.org, pslscore.com and pslscaleme.com all published "PSL guide" blog posts in 2026, showing this is a keyword-site melee.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • External event: the PSL slang broke out of forums into the mainstream in March–May 2026 (Know Your Meme, media coverage, Omoggle + Twitch streamers); the site itself took no part in those events and passively caught the search demand.
  • Search ranking: the channel profile shows search at 67.5% and non-brand about 40%, with the main terms psl scale / psl rating / psl score being jargon themselves; an exact-match domain is an advantage.
  • Launch platforms did not work: no traction on HN, 1 vote and #236 on PH.
  • Sharing: results can generate a share card (photo can be hidden) with a QR pointing to the homepage; PSL Rating Compare naturally needs two people to compare, catering to Omoggle-style "mog duels".
  • Speculation:
  • First users: from 2025-12 to early 2026 they came from organic search for "psl scale", at very small volume; real volume came after the Omoggle craze.
  • "I tested my PSL score" videos under the TikTok #pslscale tag lead the curious to search; the sharp drop to 414 two months ago may be because the search ranking was pushed down by same-term sites, or a switch of payment method.

Revenue and scale clues

  • $3 single report / credit packs. last month checkout visits were 17K, roughly a few thousand dollars in monthly revenue (speculation).
  • No public data.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second follow-up check: the takeoff year is pinned to 2026, and the takeoff method was settled from "Long-tail search + External event tailwind" to "External event tailwind" — the concrete event chain was filled in: 2026-03-06 Know Your Meme, 04-09 media coverage, 05-05 Twitch ban lifted + Omoggle going viral (xQc, Jynxzi); also added the PH launch with 1 vote at #236 and the same-term competitor sites.
