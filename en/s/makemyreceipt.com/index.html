Basics

  • Product: online receipt generator; pick a template, fill in items, export PNG/PDF/JPEG; no sign-up. The homepage says "150+", "100+" and "197 templates" in different places (inconsistent), and the About page says 195+. Also offers free mini-tools such as a sales tax calculator, tip calculator and barcode generator.
  • Team: the About page describes "a tiny team designs the templates, writes the code, and answers the support emails", "operated from the European Union"; no company name, founder or address given.
  • Launch: domain registered 2025-12-01 (RDAP, Namecheap); About page says "online since late 2025"; earliest sitemap lastmod is 2025-12-12 (/blog/irs-receipt-requirements); first Wayback capture 2026-05-12.
  • Pricing: free to create and export (watermarked); Pro $4.50/week or $9/month (page shows "50% off list price"), removes watermark and allows saving (profile record).
  • Page structure (217 URLs in sitemap): 170 /template/ pages (about 75 are brand names), 16 blog posts (how-to-make-a-receipt, costco-receipt-codes, receipt-vs-invoice, etc.), 15 /reports/ statistics pages (tipping, grocery spending, return statistics, etc.), 16 static pages.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Search ranking: one page per brand/industry template ("Walmart receipt", "StockX receipt", etc.), plus blog and statistics report pages; search accounts for 71.9% of traffic (our data), driven by receipt maker, receipt generator, make a receipt, fake receipt generator.
  • Monetization: free exports carry a watermark; removing it requires a Pro subscription.
  • Almost zero outside discussion: across the 5 given search sets, nothing on Reddit, Product Hunt, press or review articles mentions it, apart from the site's own /builder page.
  • Speculation:
  • First users: most likely came from Google indexing the long-tail template pages after launch; no launch platform (no PH/HN/Reddit launch post found).
  • Watermarked receipts being shared and screenshots forwarded (our data notes "receipts shared onward bring some traffic back") is a minor "branded output" supplement.
  • The 78.6% brand keyword share may be because "make my receipt" is itself a generic phrase; search terms overlap with the brand, so it is not all genuine brand awareness.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site only says "thousands of people every month".
  • Pricing of $4.5/week and $9/month, with the weekly plan as the main option, points to a "one-off urgent need" model; no revenue figures at all.
  • Speculation: monthly visits in the tens of thousands to low hundreds of thousands (our checkout tracking picked it up); revenue probably in the low thousands of dollars per month, unverified.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch timing (registered 2025-12, online by year end) is corroborated by RDAP, the About page and the sitemap; credible.
  • Team identity and revenue not found at all; the given search results were mostly unrelated "receipt scanning app" articles, with no first-hand founding story.
  • "First users came from long-tail search" is inferred from traffic and page structure, with no direct evidence.
  • The product is in a gray area: brand receipt templates can be used to forge expense or return proofs, and the search terms include fake receipt generator, so there is a risk of action by Google or payment providers.
