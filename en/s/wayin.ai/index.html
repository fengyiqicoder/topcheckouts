Overview

Wayin (wayin.ai): WayinVideo AI platform that clips, summarizes, transcribes, translates and captions long videos. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 25,977 visits went from Wayin to a checkout payment page — #98 of the 607 sites we track. That is 124% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,610.

Total website visits that month were about 596,299 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 4.4 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 50%, direct 46%, social 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 40%, with queries like “instagram transcript”, “youtube transcript generator”, “facebook video transcript”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
