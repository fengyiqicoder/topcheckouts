A local fitness studio in Lublin opened by two working coaches, Radosław Hoduń and Sandra Hoduń, under the "Quality Power Club sports foundation" name. When the club moved to its new location at Wojciechowska 55/6 in October 2022 they registered power-club.pl. Customers came from the coaches' own personal-training clients and local word of mouth; the website's job is to catch local searches like "siłownia lublin" (gym Lublin). An interesting second acquisition line: they regularly invite nationally known coaches (Paweł Jurczyński, Maciej Przepiórkiewicz, StrongFirst kettlebell, etc.) to run coach-training courses, which pulls fellow trainers searching for those coaches' names onto the site. The homepage then sends people to Instagram to keep in touch.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Local search: landing pages for Lublin gym, pole dance, Aerial Hoop, etc.; search keyword "siłownia lublin".
  • Expert training courses: nationally known coaches are invited to teach, and fellow trainers searching for those coaches' names land on the site (Paweł Jurczyński and others show up in the search keywords).
  • Instagram: the homepage pushes follows; service reels are posted.

Speculation:

  • The 10% display-ad share may be local campaigns (Meta/Google Display).
  • Member retention relies on the personal-training relationship and cross-selling multiple services (training + nutrition + physiotherapy).
