Basics

  • Product: online Pokémon card (and "hobby" category) oripa. Top up coins (1 coin = 1 yen) to pull packs, in limited-count and unlimited varieties, labeled with a "minimum guarantee"; cards you win can be shipped or exchanged back into coins. Later added slot-machine and baccarat-style "game oripa" (such as "ハイトレファーム") and publishes weekly "return rates". Payment methods: credit card, convenience store, bank transfer, Amazon Pay, Merpay, Paidy.
  • Operator: 株式会社FRED, representative director 高山俊輝, PMO Ichigaya 5F, 2-19 Rokubancho, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; secondhand dealer license Tokyo Public Safety Commission No. 301002521237; describes itself as "a small team". Funding not found.
  • Channels: X @HighRoller_TCG, YouTube @HighRollerToreca, an official LINE account (redirected via sub.cash-savvies.com, presumably an agency or affiliate-marketing service provider), the iOS app "High Roller Toreca" (id6762183268, developer FRED, KK), and a BASE online shop highroller.base.shop (sells physical card packs during live streams, opens them on camera and ships them; the shop lists 株式会社FRED's secondhand dealer information).

Growth loop

  • Evidence:

1. The homepage "winning reports" button posts a tweet in one click, pre-filled with "#ハイトレ当選報告 @HighRoller_TCG", getting winners to post screenshots of high-value cards on X (branded output).
2. Up to 90%-off coupons for adding the LINE account, pulling traffic into a private community.
3. The tokutaisei system: high-rank players from other platforms start at a high rank here, used to poach heavy players.
4. The page loads Mercari's eagletag conversion code (tag.eisa.mercari.com), showing it runs ads on Mercari.
5. iOS app launched 2026-07, retaining users with daily login coin rewards.
6. The BASE shop opens physical packs on live stream, effectively using live streams to draw people in.
7. Weekly return rates are published (about 97–98% for the low tiers of ハイトレファーム), trading transparency for player trust.

  • Speculation: collaborates with card-game YouTubers / streamers on live oripa openings (an industry convention), but no specific partners were found; WebFetch of X posts returned 402 and could not be read.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Return-rate page (week of 2026-10-18 to 10-25): the single game "ハイトレファーム" alone had about 94K plays. A rough "tier × count" estimate gives about 420M coins wagered in one week (≈ 420M yen in volume), but most of it is returned as coins or cards (return rate about 98%), so it cannot be read as revenue. It does show plenty of heavy players: the 300K-coin tier had 183 plays in a week and the 1M-coin tier had 11.
  • Limited-pack sales: for example, a 40,000-coin pack with 2,000 slots had sold 1,145, about 45.8M yen in volume (extrapolated from page figures, for reference only).
  • App: 25k+ downloads (mwm.ai estimate).
  • MRR and registered user count: not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Company entity, growth tactics, app and return-rate data are all first-hand evidence; credibility medium-high.
