Basics

  • Product: Young Voices' Teacher Centre & Parent Music Room: teachers log in to get sheet music, backing tracks, choreography videos and teacher guides; parents register a "YV Parent account" with an email and a 6-digit verification code, set up a practice account for their child, and buy audience tickets ("Audience tickets are now on sale through the Parent Music Room"). https://youngvoices.co.uk/parents-and-carers
  • Who built it: Young Voices (UK) Ltd, company number 03830893, registered in Cardiff; there is also a charity, the Young Voices Foundation. In 2026 it says "30 years", i.e. starting around 1996 (Speculation). Founder Not found.
  • Scale: 200,000+ (about page) / 300,000+ (Teacher FAQ) children take part each year, up to 9,000 children per show, with venues including large arenas such as London's O2.
  • Launch timing: yvmusicroom.com registered 2023-08-10 (RDAP), earliest Wayback 2023-08-16, and by 2023-12 it was already a "Teacher login / Parent login" entry page. Similar features before that were on the musicroom.youngvoices.co.uk subdomain (search results).
  • Pricing: school annual fee £120 per school (not per head, includes sheet music, online learning portal and a performance place); on the parent side they pay for audience tickets and merchandise, prices Not found.
  • Channel profile: direct 69.5%, referral 25.2% (Semrush says 60% comes from youngvoices.co.uk), email 2.9%; search term young voices parent account.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • School subscribes → teacher gets the parent registration link → sends it to the class's parents ("Ask your Choir Leader for a link to set up your account") → parents register so the child can practise + buy audience tickets. Each school averages 30 children, and behind each child are 1–2 ticket-buying parents.
  • School websites and parent notices write the yvmusicroom link into the process (two examples: Muxton and Acrefair).

Speculation:

  • After watching the show live, parents recommend it to other schools/teachers, bringing new school subscriptions; but school sign-up goes through youngvoices.co.uk, not this site.
  • Checkout seasonality: parent registration and ticketing for the next year open in autumn, and drop to zero after the winter and spring show season ends.

Revenue and scale clues

  • School subscription £120 per school; 300,000 children a year ÷ 30 per school ≈ over ten thousand school subscriptions (Speculation, order of magnitude).
  • The main revenue should come from parents' audience tickets and merchandise (prices Not found).
  • Checkout visits 0 three months ago → 4,712 two months ago → 12,894 last month Note: this set of two months ago/last month numbers is identical to several sites in the same batch (fridaysnap.de, petrasvilla.com, pepsup.com, etc.), suspected to be estimated lower-bound/placeholder values, so the seasonal reading is only a Speculation.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, first report. The nature of the site (a portal the parent brand built itself), the £120 school subscription, and parents getting a link from teachers to buy tickets all have evidence from the official site.
  • Gaps: Young Voices' founder, the exact founding year (Companies House could not be opened) and audience ticket prices Not found; "around 1996" is back-calculated from "30 years".
