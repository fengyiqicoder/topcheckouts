Pawchive is a Kemono-style "public archive of paid content": users import creators' paid posts from Patreon, Pixiv Fanbox, Discord and similar platforms, and anyone can browse for free (a lot of adult content; essentially a piracy mirror). It launched around June 2026 in a window when Kemono and similar sites were unstable (main domain pawchive.st, with pawchive.pw registered on 06-23 as a backup). It runs on a UGC supply loop (importers contribute content → more content means stronger search and word of mouth), plus a Telegram channel (36K subscribers), a subreddit and word of mouth on adult forums; about two months after launch it had imported 20K+ creators and claimed to be close to Similarweb's global top 200. No paid acquisition at all: entirely migrated Kemono users plus brand-keyword return visits.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: archive/mirror of creators' paid content; search creators, browse posts, favorites; an "importer" lets subscribed users import content using their own session (the Fanbox importer was once disabled due to Pixiv detection).
  • Who built it: anonymous, not found.
  • Launch date: pawchive.st first captured by Wayback 2026-06-13; pawchive.pw registered 2026-06-23 and first captured the same day; pawchive.org registered 2026-07-03, pawchive.net 2026-06-29 (same operator unverified). pawchive.lat (registered 2026-09-14) and pawchive.blog (2026-10-21) are explainer-style SEO sites, presumably third parties riding the brand keyword.
  • Pricing: free to browse, no charges seen; no donation announcements on the Telegram channel.
  • Traffic (profile): direct 76.3%, search 17.8% (brand keywords 94%), referrals pawchive.st 4.6%, rule34.xxx 3.3%, kemono.cr 2.5%, social from Reddit, YouTube and X; United States 27%, South Korea 5%, Japan 4%.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator supply (UGC): users import paid content → the archive grows (20K+ creators in 2 months) → more browsers arrive, some of whom become importers.
  • Community notifications: Telegram channel with 36K subscribers and polls with 17K participants; Reddit r/pawchive.
  • Multi-domain backups:.st /.pw (and likely.org/.net), steering existing users to another domain when one goes down or gets blocked.

Speculation:

  • Brand keywords at 94% and direct at 76% mean it runs almost entirely on word of mouth and bookmarks; the keyword-riding SEO sites (.lat/.blog) are third parties feeding on its brand traffic.
  • Risk: countermeasures from rights holders/platforms (Fanbox deletions, importer detection, ISP blocks) will keep interrupting growth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No charges or donations seen; revenue not found (possibly ads, unverified).
  • Scale: claims to be close to Similarweb's global top 200 (around 2026-08, self-reported); Telegram 36K subscribers.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Given the nature of the content (an archive of pirated adult content), this investigation did not open the pawchive site itself or the adult forum threads, relying only on the Telegram announcement channel, RDAP, Wayback CDX and search summaries. No evidence of child sexual content was directly observed during the investigation, but archive sites of this kind carry that risk and no content-level check was performed.
  • Operator, revenue and the specific Kemono event that triggered the migration all not found.
