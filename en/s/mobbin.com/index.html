Basics

LinkedIn says founded in 2018, 11-50 employees, with 86 associated employees shown on the page and 21K followers. Founder Jiho Lim (HN account jiholim; the site says "Mobbin started in 2018 as Jiho's Dropbox folder of screenshots"). Co-founders and funding Not found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • First users: the March 2018 PH Beta and July HN post got almost no response; only the November PH #1 of the day (about 890 votes) brought the product to the designer community. So the first sizable batch of users came from Product Hunt's designer audience (exact user count Not found).
  • The 2024 PH #1 was the second clear growth spike.
  • Design KOL endorsements: third-party directories quote recommendations from a Shopify UX manager and a Spotify senior designer ("one of my main tools for finding flows").
  • New user sources (our channel data): direct 57.5%, search 31.9% (89.4% brand keywords), social 6.4% (YouTube, X, Reddit); referrals from design resource directories like unsection.com and designeer.xyz, and Threads. The high share of brand keywords indicates word of mouth and recommendations rather than long-tail search.
  • Ongoing content operations: "this app's X flow" breakdown posts on LinkedIn and Dribbble, each carrying Mobbin screenshots outward (branded output).

Speculation:

  • Designers recommend Mobbin in the Figma community, on X, in design newsletters and courses; it is a regular on "essential designer resources" lists.
  • Screenshot pages and app pages are indexed by search engines, capturing long-tail terms like "<app name> onboarding / paywall UI". But with brand keywords at 89%, the long-tail contribution may be smaller than the earlier report estimated.
  • Individual users bring it into their companies and upgrade to the Team plan.
  • MCP lets AI coding/design Agents call it directly, possibly driving a new round of paid conversions. The timing fits, but there is no causal evidence.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing: Pro about $10–15/month, Team $16/person/month, Finance+ $399/month.
  • Team: LinkedIn 11-50 bracket, 86 associated employees, 21K followers. A team this size usually corresponds to ARR in the millions of dollars (speculation).
  • MRR, paying user count: Not found.
  • Our data: 146K checkout visits in September.

Confidence and gaps

  • Both the launch and the second growth spike are backed by first-hand PH and HN pages, high confidence.
  • Second follow-up check: all 5 sets of search results this time were official pages and tool directories, with no interviews, Reddit or press coverage; the PH page has no maker first comment; about, careers and Medium all returned 403, and Wayback was rate-limited. The only additions are the LinkedIn team size, details of the PH Beta and HN cold-start failures, design KOL endorsements, and the brand keyword share in channel data. The growth engine was narrowed from "Search ranking + Creator supply" to just "Search ranking", with a note that the brand keyword share is very high and the long-tail contribution may be overestimated.
  • Gaps: founder interviews, the specific source of the first 1,000 users between Beta and v1, co-founders, funding, revenue.

How did Mobbin get started?

Founder Jiho Lim turned his Dropbox folder of app screenshots into a filterable design reference library. In 2018 he tested it on PH and HN with a lukewarm response; in November that year it relaunched on Product Hunt, took #1 of the day and week, and became a standard bookmark for UI/UX designers. Hundreds of thousands of screenshots then built a content moat and search footprint under a "browse free + subscribe to unlock" model.
