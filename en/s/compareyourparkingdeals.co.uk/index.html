Basics

  • Product: compares and books Park & Ride and Meet & Greet parking at ten UK airports (Heathrow, Gatwick, Manchester, Stansted, Luton, Birmingham, Liverpool, Bristol, Glasgow, plus Dublin), claiming up to 60% cheaper than on-airport prices (the homepage elsewhere says 70%), from £4.50/day, "Never Beaten on Price"; offers newsletter discounts, loyalty points, and promo codes (SAVE25, etc.).
  • Company: the site lists no company name, registration number, or address, only the phone number 03333661422; Trustpilot marks it as UK-based, profile claimed 2025-01. Nominet verified the registrant's identity on 2025-02-18.
  • Team: not found. In our traffic data Pakistan is about 18% and India about 10%, and Trustpilot complaints mention "difficulty communicating with the driver who collects the car", so there may be a South Asian team or outsourcing (speculation).
  • Launch date: domain registered 2024-01-01 (Nominet WHOIS, GoDaddy); first Wayback snapshot 2024-01-03.
  • Reputation: Trustpilot 2.2/5, 110 reviews, 64% one-star; main complaints are 60–90 minute waits for the car, unresponsive support, refund difficulties, and extra charges; the company does not reply to negative reviews.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid search: about 44% in our data, the largest source.
  • SEO landing pages: pages per airport and per terminal (e.g. "Save 70% on Heathrow Airport Parking Terminal 3") capturing generic parking queries.
  • Coupon site distribution: several coupon sites list its discount codes.
  • Newsletter and loyalty points for repeat bookings.

Speculation:

  • The first orders presumably came from Google paid search (airport parking is a classic high-intent category where you can buy traffic directly); there is no evidence of any community or press spread.
  • The name is highly similar to established comparison sites and may pick up some brand-confusion traffic.
  • Coupon sites most likely earn commission through an affiliate network (Awin, etc.); unverified.

Revenue and scale clues

  • About 105 Trustpilot reviews in a year (mostly negative); using the airport parking industry's rough rate of "one review per few hundred orders", annual orders are in the tens of thousands (speculation).
  • Each parking booking is typically £40–£150; commission or markup rate unknown. Revenue not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch date (Nominet, Wayback), channel structure, and Trustpilot reputation are credible.
  • No founder, press, or Reddit information in search results; the site's terms page returns 404, so the corporate entity can't be identified.
  • Gaps: operating company and team, whether it runs its own car parks, affiliate commission, order volume and revenue.
