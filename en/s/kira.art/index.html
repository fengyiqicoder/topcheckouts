Kira is an overseas AI image product incubated by ILLA Cloud (Beijing Aila Yunke; the team has early Douyin/TikTok R&D and Kuaishou incubation backgrounds), presented to the outside world as a Singapore entity. The domain was registered in June 2025, and it started as an "AI photo-editing agent" (background replacement, inpainting, upscaling); it launched on Product Hunt in August 2025 and placed #2 of the day, but that only brought a wave of early-adopter tech users. The real pivot was Kira 4.0 in March 2026: changing the product from a "photo editing tool" into a template entertainment app that "turns friends' photos into AI videos, soundtracks and parodies" (you in GTA, NBA stars, viral dances, motion control). It then scaled with a Douyin-style playbook: creator.kira.art pays ordinary people cash per post to publish 3-5 second hook videos on TikTok, $10-200 per video on a tiered scale by views; it runs weekly themed campaigns (#fall calendar, #vs26 runway show, World Cup) with credits as rewards; and inviting one friend gives 50 credits. The works generated carry the #kiraart tag and flow back to social platforms.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: template-based AI image and video generation plus a community. In-house or wrapped models in three tiers, Lite / Nova / Ultra; features include motion control (photos that dance), video editing and extension, music generation, and many one-click templates ("Sun-Kissed Tan", "3D Disney avatar", "You in GTA 5"). It has a Gallery, a Timeline community, @mentions and usernames (version 5.9), and a Discord.
  • Pricing: free signup gives 50 credits; there are Pro / Max subscriptions (video generation requires Pro or above), specific prices not found; inviting one person gives 50 credits.
  • Team: incubated by ILLA Cloud; members named on the pages include Chen Longbo (formerly head of front-end for an incubated business at Kuaishou) and Wu Xiaosong (formerly main-business-line designer at Douyin); the developer entity is Beijing Aila Yunke Technology Co., Ltd. (source: the Gongke AI-agent directory site). A third-party page calls it a "Singapore app".
  • Domain: kira.art registered 2025-06-04 (RDAP), earliest Wayback snapshot 2025-06-26 ("AI AGENT FOR CREATIVITY", photo-editing feature list).
  • Users: US 17%, Brazil 10%, South Korea 8.5%; direct traffic 67%, brand keywords 92% (channel profile).

The first 1,000 users: early adopters from the August 2025 Product Hunt launch and AI tool directory sites (toolcentral, bestofai and others). The volume took off in 2026, after turning into entertainment templates, through paid TikTok UGC (speculation, based on the scale design of the creator program and ad-buying-style requirements like "new accounts must be warmed up for 5-7 days first").

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid UGC matrix: pays ordinary people cash per post to publish TikTok hook videos, with more paid the higher the views, requiring a campaign link in the comments and the #kiraart tag. In essence this is buying traffic, but spent on amateur content.
  • Themed challenges use credits as rewards (up to a month of free Pro), across TikTok, Instagram and X.
  • Inviting one person gives 50 credits (new users in the channel profile are recorded as viral).
  • Output is naturally shareable: "turn your friends into content", and friends who are put into a video will reshare it; the on-site community with hashtags lets works accumulate.
  • Three Product Hunt launches, used for exposure at version milestones.

Speculation:

  • The high share of Korean and Brazilian traffic is probably tied to localized topics such as K-pop dance and football templates.
  • Referrals from sites like thefappeningblog and dcinside suggest borderline content or organic community spread.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Price not found; Product Hunt has 1.6K followers.
  • The paid creator program pays up to $200 per video, which shows a sustained marketing budget, backed by ILLA Cloud's funding (from memory ILLA Cloud has raised from Sequoia China and others, unverified).
  • A safety-score site gives it 34/100 and mentions that some vendors have blacklisted it, possibly related to the ad landing pages.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch (no earlier report). The evidence for the product pivot and the ad mechanism is fairly solid (Wayback, Product Hunt, creator pages).
  • Gaps: user count, revenue, paid prices and the growth curve after 2026-03 have no primary data; a 2025-12 snapshot shows "AI Photo editor / Photoshop alternative, coming soon", which may be another direction they tried, unverified.
