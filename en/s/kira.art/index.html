Overview

Kira (kira.art): AI image and video creation tool with one-click templates like body retouch and background fixes. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Kira to a checkout payment page — #265 of the 607 sites we track. That is 316% up from the April–June monthly average of 3,100.

Total website visits that month were about 518,225 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 2.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 67%, search 25%, referrals/email/paid 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 92%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
