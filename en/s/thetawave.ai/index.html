Basics

  • Product: AI notes (real-time lecture transcription → structured notes), PDF/Word/YouTube to notes, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, podcast conversion; Web + iOS/Android; multilingual (Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Korean, Thai, Spanish, etc.). Free tier + Pro $118.80/year.
  • Company: ThetaWave AI Inc., headquartered in Beijing per Forbes.
  • Founders: Li Wenxuan (CEO/CTO, physics olympiad gold medalist, interned in Tencent's recommendation algorithm team at 17, dropped out of UC Berkeley CS); Zhong Ziqiu, Elena Zhong (COO/CMO, dropped out of NYU Stern, sold study notes online in high school with over RMB 100K in sales, previously did growth for early AI teams). The whole team was born after 2000. Named to Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2026.
  • Funding: 2024-05 MiraclePlus seed round; 2026-01 Pre-A of several million dollars (led by BAI Capital and Hillhouse Ventures, MBA Fund participating, MiraclePlus adding); over $7M cumulative (Forbes).

New user sources: the student creator matrix's short videos (Evidence) + Korean brand-keyword search (Similarweb).

Growth loop

  • Evidence: paid student UGC creator matrix (TikTok / Instagram Reels), paying per post + view bonuses + leaderboard, with video templates and 1:1 coaching; content formats like "professor loses it" and "24 hours before the exam" skits; hundreds of millions of impressions per month. The official line is "no large-scale paid ads, the product is the content engine", with students sharing generated note cards for spread. Self-reported retention of 80%+ in month two and 65%+ in month three.
  • Speculation: localizing first in Chinese- and Korean-speaking student markets like Taiwan/Malaysia/Korea, where competition is lighter than in the West; exam-season word of mouth.

Revenue and scale clues

  • ARR passed $1M in H2 2025 (media reports, company figures); nearly a million users worldwide; 150+ countries.
  • Our data: 4–6 months ago average 20,500, July 0, two months ago 414, last month 17,192; very strong summer seasonality, or possibly a payment-channel switch over the past 2–3 months (Speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Growth and ARR figures are the company's own claims in funding news; the Zhihu original returned 403, a republished copy was used.
  • The "90% organic traffic" claim conflicts with the paid creator program: creator fees are essentially paid acquisition, so it is classified as Paid ads + Creator supply.
  • Second pass: added founders (Li Wenxuan, Zhong Ziqiu, Beijing, post-2000 dropouts), funding (MiraclePlus seed, Hillhouse/BAI Pre-A, $7M+ cumulative), ARR past $1M; the old report's "founders and country not found" is overturned; takeoff year changed from 2024 to 2025 (both the paid launch and the $1M ARR happened in 2025).
