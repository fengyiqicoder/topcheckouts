Basics

  • Product: a managed SEO service. After analyzing the site it automatically writes and publishes 30+ articles a month (WordPress plugin, Shopify and other integrations), builds 10+ backlinks a month through the customer swap network, and targets rankings in Google and AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity); WordPress.org has an official plugin, "GetAutoSEO AI Tool".
  • Founders (https://getautoseo.com/about): Peter Zaborszky (co-founder, runs product; serial entrepreneur and angel investor, built ProPrivacy.com and exited about three years ago); Dr. Mihaly Kertesz (co-founder, runs operations; veterinarian by training, ran Artisan Marketing for five years, attended Miami Ad School). Both names look Hungarian (speculation).
  • Pricing: $1 for a 3-day trial; the affiliate page uses a $149/month plan as its example; there are multi-site plans.
  • Domain: registered 2023-09-18 (RDAP; the 2018 snapshots belong to a previous owner); first product snapshot 2024-02.
  • Traffic (profile): United States 39%, United Kingdom 15%, New Zealand 9%; search 44% (85% brand keywords: autoseo, autoseo login), direct 41%, display ads 8.8%; new customers mostly see ads on Facebook, YouTube and Instagram and then search the name.

Where the first users came from: the source during the 2024 plugin stage was not found (possibly the WordPress plugin directory and the founders' SEO/affiliate marketing circle, speculation); after the 2025 pivot, new customers come mainly from paid social ads (profile: they see it on Facebook/YouTube/Instagram and then search the brand name) plus the free 3-article hook.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid ads: display ads are 8.8%; social ads drive brand searches; ChatGPT ads in 2026.
  • Free hooks: enter a URL for 3 free articles + a content plan; $1 trial.
  • Affiliate commission: 20% x 12 months, or converted into backlinks.
  • Backlink swap network: customers swap backlinks with each other, so more customers means a bigger backlink pool ("backlink exchange").

Speculation:

  • The founders came from affiliate review sites and understand performance ads and SEO, hence the play of "buy traffic + cheap trial + auto-renewal"; early Trustpilot complaints cluster around being charged after the trial, suggesting conversion relies on auto-renewal.
  • The blog itself is also a product demo (auto-generated SEO articles), but non-brand search is only about 15%, so SEO is not the main source.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 2,479+ businesses (site button); another place on the About page says 500+, so the figures are inconsistent.
  • If about 1,000 customers pay $100-150/month, monthly revenue would be about $100-150K (speculation, not verified).
  • No funding information seen.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The founders, domain timing, the pivot from 2024 plugin to 2025 managed service, the ChatGPT ads and the affiliate terms have site/Wayback/RDAP/sprites evidence.
  • Gaps: the exact month of the pivot, ad spend scale and paying user count were not found; search results contained no founder interviews or podcasts.
