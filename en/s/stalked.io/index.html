A Madrid company called Agencia Sisu Talent, S.L. (the name means a talent/creator agency) registered stalked.io six months ago and built a Spanish-language weekly subscription tool (€3.99-7.99/week) that lets you "peek at who any public IG account recently followed + get email alerts for new follows". It then took off through the site's built-in "creator affiliate program": female TikTok/IG creators get an exclusive link and film videos like "how I found out who he is secretly following"; users who subscribe through the link get a special price, and creators earn **recurring revenue share** on subscribed users (the site has a creator onboarding flow where they enter an IBAN and sign a contract). A user testimonial on the site reads "I found out about Stalked from a girl on TikTok". Traffic is concentrated in Spanish-speaking countries such as Peru, Colombia and Spain, and brand keywords are 87% of search, which suggests users search the site name after watching a video.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: enter any public Instagram username → see the people it recently followed and its follow history in chronological order, with an email alert when there is a new follow ("alert in under 4 hours", "100% anonymous", "no IG login needed"); free signup shows partial results, a subscription unlocks the rest.
  • Pricing (site terms): Standard plan €3.99/week (VAT included), auto-renewing, +€3.99/week for each additional monitored account; the landing page also shows tiers such as "Plan Semanal 7,99€/sem", a monthly "most popular" plan and "one-time payment · 7 days"; affiliate links get a special price; no refunds once activated; payments via Stripe.
  • Entity: Agencia Sisu Talent, S.L., Madrid commercial register Tomo 45490 / Folio 193 / Hoja M-800135; contact email stalked.io.info@gmail.com.
  • Language: mainly Spanish, with English and French translations (the site's i18n has EN/FR text).
  • Self-description: ">12,000 active users", "Trending en redes sociales".
  • Domain: WHOIS created 2026-04-15 (Porkbun); no Wayback captures. Instagram @stalked.io has about 200 followers and only one video, from 2026-06-07 - the official account is barely run, so traffic does not come from its own accounts.
  • Tech: Next.js (Turbopack) deployed on a Vercel-style platform (dpl_ deployment ID).
  • Traffic (profile): direct 48.4%, search 38.7% (brand keywords 87%: stalked io, stalkea; also "instagram stalker", "stalk ig"), referral 7.7%; Peru 25%, Colombia 21%, Spain 18%, followed by Chile and Mexico.
  • Similar competitors: stalkedapp.com (English, same name), theick.io, stalkyapp.com.

Where the first users came from: promo videos by Spanish-language TikTok/IG creators (evidence: the on-site testimonial "I found out about Stalked from a girl on TikTok", the creator affiliate terms and onboarding flow; the official IG account is almost blank, showing it does not rely on its own accounts). The operating entity is named like a "talent agency", so it presumably already holds a pool of creator resources it can assign to promotion directly (speculation).

Where new users come from: ongoing creator-affiliate videos → viewers search the brand keywords "stalked io / stalkea" to reach the site (brand keywords 87%) or type it in directly (48%); a small amount comes from demand-term searches like "instagram stalker".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Creator commissions: recurring commission is paid on referred users who stay subscribed, so the more creators and videos, the more new users.
  • Discount hook: users coming through a creator link get a lower subscription price, which raises video conversion.
  • Emotional demand: the "suspecting a partner/ex" hook (testimonial: "I doubted for months, and this app helped me confirm everything") is naturally suited to short video.

Speculation:

  • Weekly subscription + auto-renewal gives each user enough LTV to pay a recurring commission.
  • Peru and Colombia having a higher share than Spain suggests most partner creators are in Latin America; the Spanish entity handles payments and compliance.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 12,000+ active users; a conservative estimate at €3.99/week, if half pay, is about €100k+/month - pure speculation, unverified.
  • No public revenue.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The product, pricing, operating entity and affiliate program all come from the original terms text in the on-site front-end code, so confidence is high; "took off through TikTok creators" is backed by both user testimonials and the affiliate mechanism, but no specific viral video link was found.
  • Search results were almost all irrelevant (speedtest, generic PH guides, stalker news); no interviews or press coverage.
  • Gaps: the specific partner creators and videos, commission rate, background of Agencia Sisu Talent and its other projects, exact launch date.
