Overview

Kaze (kaze.ai): Online AI image/video generator and editor with watermark removal and AI headshots. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Kaze to a checkout payment page — #454 of the 607 sites we track. That is 22% down from the April–June monthly average of 10,967.

Total website visits that month were about 406,643 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 2.1 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 62%, direct 38%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 37%, with queries like “png maker”, “bald filter”, “remove logo from photo”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: credit tiers 200/500/1200 per month (no prices shown).
