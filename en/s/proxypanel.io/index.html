Basics

  • Product: US mobile proxies, HTTP / SOCKS5, REST API, IP rotation forced via URL, unlimited traffic, covering 244 US locations (Verizon / AT&T / T-Mobile).
  • Pricing (recorded by competitor HProxy on 2026-09-27): $3 / 3 hours, $10 / 24 hours, $30 / 7 days, $100 / 30 days; monthly saves 22% versus weekly; no refunds once sold, and payments in crypto are never refunded. Payment methods are cards (Stripe) and USDT / TRX / BTC / LTC.
  • Price evolution: 2021-02 was 8 cities at $175/month (HProxy citing Wayback) → $100/30 days in 2026.
  • Company: the pages are unsigned; the terms of service refer to an unnamed operator, one liability clause contains "PROXYTOWER" (suspected template leftover or a related entity); governing law is stated as "the operator's state". Telegram @proxypanelio is the only official channel.
  • Related brand: phoneproxies.com (has shared a certificate with proxypanel since 2025-10), speculated to be a second brand of the same operator.
  • Same-name confusion: the Telegram "ProxyPanel开发公告" (t.me/s/proxypanel) is a Chinese open-source proxy panel project, and TikTok @proxypanel_official is a Free Fire cheat panel; neither is related to this site.

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Direct visits are 97% and brand keywords are 98.8% of search → new users almost entirely come by typing the URL after knowing the name, i.e. circle word of mouth / private community.
  • There are user threads on BHW, and the 13 Trustpilot reviews come from Taiwan, Romania, Ukraine, Nigeria, Australia, Kenya, the US and other places → buyers are global MMO multi-account individuals.
  • Telegram is the only support channel and the channel for large-order inquiries; the $3 three-hour trial lowers the barrier.
  • Social sources include YouTube, Facebook and Telegram (our channel data).
  • Speculation:
  • The 2026 volume concentrated in Vietnam (85%) and Kenya (14%) is most likely local MMO studios/agents recommending it in bulk in Facebook groups and Telegram groups (running TikTok US shops, warming up US-region accounts), possibly including resellers; the site does not mention an affiliate program.
  • The product itself has no sharing mechanism, and word of mouth also carries a lot of negatives (61% of Trustpilot reviews are one star); repeat purchase relies on price and the scarcity of real US device IPs.
