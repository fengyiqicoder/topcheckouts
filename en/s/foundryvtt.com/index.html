Overview

Foundryvtt (foundryvtt.com): Self-hosted browser-based virtual tabletop for running tabletop RPGs online. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Foundryvtt to a checkout payment page — #456 of the 607 sites we track. That is 27% down from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 213,256 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 4.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 59%, direct 31%, social 6%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)
