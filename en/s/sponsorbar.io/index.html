Overview

Sponsorbar (sponsorbar.io): Mac menu bar app that shows paid ads in empty menu bar space and shares revenue with users. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 4,851 visits went from Sponsorbar to a checkout payment page — #536 of the 607 sites we track. That is 13760% up from the April–June monthly average of 35.

Total website visits that month were about 20,179 (estimated from global rank). Roughly 24.0 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 45%, social 41%, search 8%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from social platforms: organic social is about 41%, led by X (Twitter) › YouTube.

Pricing: free for users; $15 minimum advertiser spend; $2.00 per 1,000 one-minute impre.
