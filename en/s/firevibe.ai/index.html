Basics

  • Product: generates websites, mobile apps, SaaS, dashboards, and internal tools from natural language, bundling frontend, backend, database, auth, and hosting; users can plug in their own Stripe, Supabase, OpenAI, etc. keys. Paid plans publish to a firevibe.dev subdomain, Pro supports custom domains and source code export. There is also an "AI Mobile App Designer" at app.firevibe.ai (produces app design mockups, exports React/SwiftUI/Flutter) and a Dribbble account showcasing designs.
  • Pricing: free 200 credits; Starter $21/month; Pro $42/month; custom enterprise plan (official site).
  • Company: CB Insights lists it as founded in 2026, registered address Middletown, Delaware (a common registered-agent address); founders, funding, and headcount not listed. The team is very likely Turkish (speculation, based on the Turkish-language site, Turkish traffic, and Turkish creator promotion; no direct evidence).
  • Launch date: domain registered 2026-03-12 (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2026-04-02.
  • Self-reported on the site: about 2 million projects to date, about 100k new projects per week, sites built with it get 30 million visits per month (Turkish page; the English page's numbers don't match their labels, low credibility).
  • Has an affiliate program (Turkish footer "Ortaklık programı").

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid ads: our channel data shows paid ads at 43.6%, the largest source.
  • Localization: full Turkish-language site (/tr), Turkey accounts for 87% of traffic, Turkish non-brand search keywords present.
  • Creator short videos: Turkish Instagram creators post tutorial-style Reels on "building an app/website with zero experience" that name firevibe.ai (summary only).
  • Affiliate program: footer has an affiliate entry point, so a commission channel for creators is already in place.

Speculation:

  • The first users most likely came from Turkish paid ads and creator partnerships (creator videos may be paid or affiliate-commissioned; no disclosure visible).
  • User-generated sites live on firevibe.dev subdomains, which may bring a little brand exposure, but there is no evidence this is a major source.
  • The "AI Mobile App Designer" (app.firevibe.ai) and the Dribbble account suggest it may have started with app design mockup generation and later expanded into a full-stack generator.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Subscriptions at $21/$42 per month; the site claims about 2 million projects to date (unverified, high for a product barely six months old).
  • Revenue not found. Paid ads above 40% of traffic suggest enough paid conversion to cover ad costs (speculation).

Confidence and gaps

  • Launch date (RDAP, Wayback), pricing, Turkey as the main market, and the paid-ads traffic structure are credible.
  • Search results show almost no founder interviews, Product Hunt, Reddit, or press coverage; Instagram and YouTube detail pages wouldn't load, so creator promotion is summary only.
  • Gaps: founding team, whether there was a Product Hunt launch, whether creator partnerships were paid, real user numbers and revenue.
