Overview

Ssemble (ssemble.com): AI tool that clips long videos into shorts and auto-schedules them to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels, with clip rewards program. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 21,677 visits went from Ssemble to a checkout payment page — #119 of the 607 sites we track. That is 41% up from the April–June monthly average of 15,394.

Total website visits that month were about 189,060 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 11.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 58%, search 42%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows public profiles / leaderboards, and direct plus brand search is about 92%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $6/mo Pro ($72/yr); $12/mo Expert ($144/yr); $24/mo Business ($288/yr); $40/m.
