Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder personal brand: the only social entry on the site is Karlo's personal X; the profile's social source is also only X.
  • Two-sided matching: brands post requests in the community and students take the jobs; "learn it and get jobs" is the enrollment pitch, and reviews of students landing jobs are on the homepage.
  • Brand search: 84.7% of search is brand keywords, meaning most people saw it elsewhere (X, YouTube, word of mouth) and then searched.

Speculation:

  • The first students came from Karlo's personal-story content on X ("from $30 editor to agency") and the circle around his agency; the brands came from his agency's existing clients.
  • The high share of French and UK users may relate to word of mouth among European creators in the student base.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 200+ users × $97/month is roughly $20K a month (speculation, paying share unknown), plus $997/month brands.
  • Revenue: Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • The search results contain almost no third-party information (no Reddit, reviews or interviews); the whole origin story comes from the site's own account.
  • Karlo's full name, X follower count, YouTube subscriber count and launch channel were all not retrieved (X needs paid access, the YouTube page returned no content).
  • ecomtalent.io has no RDAP record, so the launch date is taken as Wayback 2025-03.
