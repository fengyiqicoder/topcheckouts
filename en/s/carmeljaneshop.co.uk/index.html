Basics

  • Product: an online photo pickup and printing shop for schools / nurseries / dance schools / graduation photos; parents log in with a code issued by the school. Orders are processed in 5–7 working days and mailed by Royal Mail second class. The parent site carmeljane.co.uk also has a school portal (managing bookings, SIMS photo import, class seating plans, GDPR/DBS policies).
  • Price (parent side): multi-image print sets from £9.50, single prints from £8.50, mixing across siblings is allowed, and there is a sibling discount.
  • Company: Carmel Jane Photography Ltd, Unit S5, Stubbers Farm, Blackmore, Ingatestone, Essex CM4 0NY; founder Carmel Jane (the about page says "My name is Carmel Jane"). Covers Essex, London, Suffolk, Hertfordshire, Kent and the Midlands. No funding seen; a traditional private small business.
  • Ordering systems: at least some events used the German school photography SaaS GotPhoto (carmeljane.gotphoto.co.uk, page asset version numbers correspond to about 2018-07); carmeljaneshop.co.uk is a different white-label school photography e-commerce system (`NTLF_CONFIG`, `is_prophoto`).
  • Reputation: Trustpilot has 17 reviews with a 2.4 rating (2024-05 ~ 2026-10), complaining about poor paper quality, excessive retouching and high prices; the company replied "we only make money if parents buy photos".

Growth loop

  • Evidence:
  • Pitch for signing schools: a commission-matching guarantee ("match the commission the school received from its previous supplier") + zero admin + a modern style; a St Benedicts (Colchester) testimonial explicitly says they switched from a long-time supplier, and New Hall School reports a "record number of orders" — school testimonials are used to sign the next school.
  • Parent side: schools send codes and pre-registration requirements, and the system emails notices when the photos go online (channel profile: email 26%, direct 65%); parents are just passive end buyers, and there is no acquisition loop.
  • The business extends from dance schools, graduation photos, sports days and school shows, and multiple shoots per school raise revenue per school.
  • Speculation: word-of-mouth recommendations among head teachers and between multi-academy trusts; attendance at trade shows like the Schools & Academies Show.

Revenue and scale clues
