Overview

Cleanup (cleanup.pictures): Web AI tool removing unwanted objects, people, text and defects from photos. Category on TopCheckouts: AI creation.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Cleanup to a checkout payment page — #301 of the 607 sites we track. That is 18% down from the April–June monthly average of 15,733.

Total website visits that month were about 403,140 (Semrush figure rescaled to Similarweb). Roughly 3.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 64%, search 32%, AI assistants 3%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 13%, with queries like “object remover”, “remove text from image”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: Free $0 (720p); Pro $5/mo or $36/yr; ClipDrop Pro from 11; API usage-based.
