Basics

  • Product: a music tech news and review site (news, reviews, deals, interviews, trade show coverage, giveaways), leaning toward hardware synths and Eurorack but also covering software and iOS.
  • Author: the About page is written in the first person by one person, who has lived in Vienna for several years; the YouTube bio says "based in Luxembourg and Vienna". Credited as "Synth Anatomy", author slug "tom", full name not found.
  • Monetization: the "big red buttons" in articles are affiliate and partner links (the site's own words: "uses affiliation & partner programs… to finance a part of the activity"); an "Advertise with us" ads page; a Support page (recorded as Patreon from $1/month in the profile, the Support page content was not read this time).
  • Domain: synthanatomy.com registered 2015-03-12 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2015-04-05.
  • Social: YouTube @SynthAnatomy joined 2014-07-07, about 37.5K subscribers, 1,577 videos, 10.13M total views; X @synthanatomy registered 2014-10-08, about 6,579 followers; Facebook about 29,411 followers; official subreddit r/Synthanatomy.
  • Traffic: United States 21%, Germany 9%, France 7%; direct 49%, non-brand search 29% (new product names such as movy, m-vave fm-1, donner sample boy), referrals rekkerd.org 6.2%, kvraudio.com 5.6%, elektronauts.com 1.6%; social 7% (Facebook, YouTube, Reddit). A third-party estimate puts it at about 110K monthly unique visitors (updowntoday, low credibility).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Daily new-product news → long-tail searches on new product names (non-brand search 29%).
  • Sites in the same circle link to each other: rekkerd, KVR and Elektronauts together make up about 13% of referral traffic.
  • YouTube demo videos and articles embed each other, with Facebook / X / subreddit distributing in sync.
  • Affiliate buttons + vendor ads + giveaways, with vendors sending in stories and prizes when they launch new products.

Speculation:

  • Vendors treat Synth Anatomy's long demo videos as the "sound reference" for new products, and the new-product release seasons (NAMM, Superbooth) bring traffic peaks.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue not disclosed; three lines: affiliate, ads and reader support.
  • Scale: YouTube 37.5K subscribers, 10M+ views; Facebook 29K followers; X 6,600 followers.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The founding approach (YouTube first, website later), timing and location come from the site's About page, RDAP, Wayback CDX, and YouTube and X data, so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: the author's full name was not found; the Support page (whether it is Patreon) was not read this time, so the profile's claim is carried over; after the Google search the three groups of results were basically unrelated (speed test sites, etc.).
