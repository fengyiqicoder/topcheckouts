Basics

  • Product: an unofficial helper tool for EA SPORTS FC Ultimate Team, with features including AI auto-solving SBCs, generating the optimal squad from your club, bulk pack opening, Gauntlet squads, player locking, transfer profit display and FUT Gallery collection planning. Available as a Chrome extension (injected into the EA FC Web App) and iOS/Android apps.
  • Company: FUTGenie Ltd (App Store seller name; extension language English (UK), presumably registered in the UK). Founder names and funding not found.
  • Pricing: Free £0 (5 solves per day, with ads; v1.2.1 on 2025-10-29 allowed 10 per day); Standard £6.49/mo + VAT, save 42% on annual; the website uses Stripe, the apps use in-app purchases.
  • Scale: the site claims 760,000+ active users; an official tweet on 2026-07-31 said "join 410,000 people"; Chrome extension 200K users, 4.6 rating; iOS app released 2025-12-21.
  • Social: X @FUTGenieGG (registered 2025-09-14, 10.3K followers), Discord, TikTok (official account and the #futgenie hashtag).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Streamer endorsements: the site's "Trusted by top FUT creators" section lists Danny Aarons, NepentheZ, MattHDGamer, La4Awale and Fut Sheriff.
  • Each creator has a dedicated landing page at futgenie.gg/p/<streamer name>, placed in video descriptions to track the traffic each streamer brings (whether commissions are paid is not stated on the page).
  • Controversy marketing: turning "EA staff use it too, no bans" and the EA developer's public criticism into talking points; the two tweets total about 1.67M views.
  • Free daily quota, converting to £6.49/mo once used up.
  • Lots of FC 27 tutorial/guide articles on the site (posts/), targeting search terms for new features like "FUT Gallery".

Speculation:

  • Each year's new EA release and each week's new SBC events bring seasonal return traffic; two months ago peak, last month drop as the game changes over.
  • EA's terms ban automation and there is ban risk, so spread happens mainly inside player communities.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claimed active users: 410K in 2026-07, 760K in 2026-10; Chrome extension 200K.
  • 57,737 checkout visits two months ago, the highest in this batch. Paid price £6.49/mo; no public MRR.
