GLOBA K.K., a Kyoto company founded in May 2015, opened MAIKOYA in Osaka in 2017, a six-story "maiko heritage house" offering tea ceremony, kimono and maiko performance experiences to foreign tourists. It got its first tourists through reviews and "Travelers' Choice" awards on OTAs like Tripadvisor (the 2018 site title was "Best Activities and Tours in Japan 2018 - Chosen by Travelers Reviews"). It then opened branches in Kyoto and Tokyo plus samurai/ninja museums, while writing 762 geisha/samurai/tea ceremony explainer articles translated into 16 languages, grabbing search with culture terms like "geisha, seppuku, ninja" and steering tourists from OTAs to book directly on its own site.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

2. Channelswhere new users come from and what the traffic looks like

New-user source: SEO (Non-brand search). Half of new customers come from non-brand search (about 49.6%), with culture explainer terms like geiko, geisha, seppuku and ninja, drawn in by the site's explainer articles; brand keywords are 37.8% and paid search 7.6%. Referrals come from travel content sites such as globa.info and redhairtravel.com (about 1% each); social includes Reddit, Facebook and YouTube. Tourists mostly book once, and direct visits at 37.8% are mostly people coming back to order after browsing.

Basics

  • Product: the MAIKOYA site, for booking kimono tea ceremonies, maiko (geisha) performances and interaction, kimono rental, calligraphy/origami and other experiences in Kyoto/Tokyo, plus tickets to the sister brand SAMURAI NINJA MUSEUM (Kyoto, Tokyo Asakusa) (site, profile file).
  • Who built it: GLOBA K.K. (GLOBA株式会社), Nakagyo Ward, Kyoto; established 2015-05-11; Representative Director 塚本 亘 (Ko Tsukamoto) (site About page).
  • Awards: MAIKOYA "Best Luxury Tea Ceremony Experience in Japan, 2026"; SAMURAI NINJA MUSEUM TOKYO won Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice 2025 (About page).
  • Launch: RDAP registration 2017-04-03; the earliest Wayback, 2017-05-28, shows "MAIKOYA OSAKA - Maiko Heritage House" (a six-story traditional building in Osaka: garden, sakura cafe, kimono, Sengoku/Heian/Edo themes, cuisine, a golden tea room), and it had already started blogging (2017-05 "Tokyo's Geisha Districts", "How to Make Sushi Rolls").
  • Current traffic (profile file): United States 32.8%, Australia 5.1%, Japan 5.0%; non-brand search about 49.6% (geiko, geisha, seppuku, ninja, etc.), brand keywords 37.8%, paid search 7.6%; referrals globa.info, redhairtravel.com; social Reddit, Facebook, YouTube.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Culture explainer SEO: 762 English articles x 16 languages, with non-brand search making up about half of new customers (sitemap, profile file).
  • OTA reviews and award endorsements (Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice, "Best Activities 2018") placed prominently on the site (Wayback, About page).
  • A travel agency partnership entry, Agencies (Wayback 2019).

Speculation:

  • Tourists leave reviews on Tripadvisor and Reddit after the experience → higher OTA ranking → more bookings, a typical flywheel for physical experience stores.
  • The number of physical stores (Osaka → multiple Kyoto stores → Tokyo) is the real scale limit, and the website is just an acquisition entry; there should have been a gap during the pandemic (2020-2022), not found.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Multiple physical stores plus museums, charged per experience; prices not found.
  • The company has a 10-year history and is a small-to-mid-sized tourism experience operator; revenue figures not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Search results were mostly unrelated (rocket launches, same-name singers, etc.); the evidence comes from the site's About page (company information), the sitemap, Wayback 2017/2019 homepages, RDAP and the profile file. The Tripadvisor forum returned 403, and Reddit was seen only through summaries.
  • The founding story (why a Kyoto company opened first in Osaka), the first tourist channel, pandemic impact and revenue were not found.
