Its predecessor was "WildCard (野卡)" (bewildcard.com): in March 2023, right after GPT-4 launched and when users in China could not pay for ChatGPT Plus, it sold overseas virtual credit cards that could be issued "in one minute, no ID needed, RMB supported", and became well known in China's AI circles by riding the demand for paying for ChatGPT. In mid-2024 it stopped issuing cards because of tighter regulation, and in October 2024 switched to "subscribing on behalf of users" (the user pays with WeChat Pay/Alipay and the platform opens ChatGPT Plus/Claude Pro for you); in September-October 2025 it was formally renamed WildAI on the new domain bewild.ai, with "existing users just log in" and balances usable toward subscriptions. So the first users of bewild.ai were WildCard's long-accumulated existing users (the site claims 200K+ users served); new users come mainly from the many "how to subscribe to ChatGPT/Claude" tutorials on Zhihu, Juejin, GitHub gists and personal blogs that carry invite codes (such as AIG, ACCPAY), written by promoters who earn invite rebates.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: a subscription-on-behalf service. Users pay with WeChat Pay/Alipay and upload account authorization info (Session), and the platform sets up or renews standalone ChatGPT Plus/Pro, Claude Pro/Max, Gemini and X Premium accounts for them; refunds on failure; no VPN provided.
  • Price: promotional articles list ChatGPT Plus at about $25.99 for 1 month, $46.99 for 2 months, $66.99 for 3 months (https://github.com/sjaqxv/BeWild, 2026-01); the official homepage does not show prices.
  • Operator: Skyfall Tech Limited (stated on the official site, a Hong Kong company); founder Not found.
  • Domain: bewild.ai registered 2025-09-28 (RDAP); the old site bewildcard.com was registered 2023-03-16 and is still online (title "WildCard | 一分钟注册, 轻松订阅海外线上服务", i.e. register in one minute and easily subscribe to overseas online services).
  • User scale: the official site says "over 200K users served".

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Existing-user migration: at the rename, "existing users just log in" and balances can be applied to subscriptions; direct visits 67% and brand keywords 70% (wildai, bewild) show many users came back remembering the WildCard brand.
  • Invite-code promotion: Zhihu, Juejin, GitHub gists/repos, personal blogs and wildai.help carry many articles with invite codes (AIG, ACCPAY) and?code= links; the official site has a "Rewards and Promotion" policy page.
  • Referrals: atatech.org, payment-related onerway.com and Zhihu (our data).
  • Must-have search: users in China searching "how to subscribe to ChatGPT Plus / Claude Pro" run into these promotional articles.

Speculation:

  • WildCard's 2023 takeoff relied on the must-have demand to pay for ChatGPT Plus plus recommendations from tech bloggers/WeChat accounts; no original 2023 coverage was found.
  • The invite rebate rate is not public; presumably it is cash back or balance credit based on order amount.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The official site says "over 200K users served".
  • At about $26/month for Plus (official price $20), gross profit per order is roughly $4-6 (estimate).
  • Revenue amount Not found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The WildCard → WildAI timeline cross-checks raw Wayback snapshots against three articles (Juejin, Zhihu, Toutiao); the shutdown month (2024-07) roughly matches the Wayback 301 period (from 2024-05), but only summaries of the Zhihu/Toutiao pieces were seen.
  • Promotional articles mostly carry invite codes, so their objectivity is limited.
  • Gaps: team identity; the exact reason for the 2024 shutdown and the refund outcome; invite rebate rate; number of paying users.
