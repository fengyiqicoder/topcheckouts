Growth loop

Evidence:

  • App store editorial picks (the three badges the site itself displays) = platform distribution.
  • The checkout page's top channel is Display (our data), showing that current growth relies on paid ads.
  • Meta ads are the main engine (hundreds running in 2025-08). The play is templated hooks: one winning hook has its words swapped in bulk across topics from the content library, each topic aimed at a niche audience, and it can also test whether there is demand for topics not yet built. The same creative is made in static, video and UGC formats to delay creative fatigue.
  • The subscription price is high: the main complaint on Reddit and Trustpilot is price, which fits the model of "paid acquisition + a high-priced annual subscription to pay it back".

Speculation:

  • The founder is a veteran of mobile game user acquisition (Pocket Gems/Episode started with paid ads + in-app purchases), and Imprint reuses that playbook; Meta is confirmed, no evidence was found for TikTok.
  • The first users (2019–2022) most likely came from Meta ads plus app store editorial picks. The tone of 2023 Reddit posts like "has anyone used it" is of people asking after seeing the ads.
  • Illustrated cards are naturally suited to screenshot sharing on Instagram/TikTok, and the official IG/FB/Twitter post illustrated content as content marketing, but no in-product sharing reward mechanism was seen.
  • After the US App Store allowed external link payments in 2025, many subscription apps moved to web checkout, and the appearance of the checkout page in July/September is consistent with that.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Sensor Tower: about $300K of iOS revenue in a single month around 2025-08 (quoted by GrowthKit); adding Android and web checkout, annualized revenue may be around $5M (speculation).
  • 58.7k App Store ratings + Play 1M+ downloads make it a mid-to-large subscription education app; specific revenue and funding were not found.
  • Subscription: monthly + yearly auto-renewing (price not obtained).
