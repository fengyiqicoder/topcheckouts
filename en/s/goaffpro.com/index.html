Overview

Goaffpro (goaffpro.com): Affiliate marketing SaaS letting ecommerce merchants run affiliate and multi-level referral programs. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Goaffpro to a checkout payment page — #261 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.
