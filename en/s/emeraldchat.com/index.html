A small anonymous social and random chat site launched in 2016 that repositioned around 2018, calling itself "The Best Omegle Alternative" (the title literally says "It's time to ditch Omegle") to capture searches like "omegle alternative". It then caught two external events: the boom in random video chat during the 2020 pandemic, and the spillover of traffic after Omegle shut down on 2023-11-08, becoming one of the "Omegle successors". After that it retains users through search plus direct visits and earns money with Gold/Platinum subscriptions (filtering by gender and country, buying karma and so on). Its growth comes from riding external events and search terms; the in-product viral loop is weak.One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: anonymous one-on-one text and video random chat, plus interest matching, group chat channels, friends, a feed and a karma rating system, with AI plus human moderation. Free to use; a paid subscription unlocks gender, country and language filters and karma-related features.
  • Pricing: App Store in-app purchases show Gold $9.99, Platinum $19.99, Increase Karma $7.99 (billing period not stated); AlternativeTo says about $3.89–4/month (older). Australia's eSafety says "Emerald gold" is a monthly subscription that includes priority matching and filtering by gender and karma.
  • Company: Connected Software, Inc., registered address 600 N Broad St Ste 5 #1198, Middletown, DE (a typical Delaware registered-agent address; the actual team location is unknown). The only publicly named executive is CMO Mike Volkin (TechBullion interview, 2024-06). Founder not found. The Android package name is com.cookie.emerald, and "cookie" may be an early developer's codename (speculation).
  • Funding: Not found.
  • Launch: domain registered 2015-04-01; earliest Wayback snapshot 2016-08-22, when the title was "Welcome to Emerald! The Place to meet new people", with interest matching and random matching. The iOS app (id6449822829) was listed around 2023 (inferred from the App ID range).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • SEO and riding positioning: since 2018 the page title has been "Best Omegle Alternative", and after Omegle shut down it was picked up by many roundup articles (ExpressVPN, ForestVPN, CyberGhost and others).
  • Social retention features: friends, a feed, persistent DM history and group chat channels let users build relationships on the site, which is why the direct-visit share is high.
  • The karma and rating system: forms an identity and reputation layer on the site and is also a paid feature.

Speculation:

  • YouTube ranks first among social sources, possibly from "messing around on a random chat site" videos (shot on Emerald after Omegle closed), but no specific video was found. The Discord source may be users pulling people they met in group chat into a Discord server, which is outflow in the reverse direction.
  • The known mechanism "multiplayer_room" corresponds to group chat channels, which have limited acquisition effect and mainly serve retention.

Revenue and scale clues

  • The site only says "Thousands" online and "180+ Countries", with no public MRR or user count.
  • Subscription prices are above. Based on our checkout page visits of 40–55K per month, monthly revenue is probably in the tens of thousands to around a hundred thousand dollars; pure speculation.

Confidence and gaps

  • The positioning change is attested by Wayback snapshots, so credibility is high.
  • Gaps: the founder's identity, the team's country, the specific data brought by the two external events, and the specific content behind the YouTube traffic were all not found. Reddit scraping was blocked.
