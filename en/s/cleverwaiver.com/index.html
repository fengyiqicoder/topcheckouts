Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Paid ads: in 2019 the page already had Google Ads signup conversion tracking, showing early reliance on paid search to bring in merchant signups.
  • Free plan + low entry price: free under 20 waivers a month lowers the trial barrier for small merchants.
  • Branded output: the signing page is at app.cleverwaiver.com so every consumer who signs sees the brand, and the referral sources are all signing links on customers' websites (the 84% direct traffic is mostly these signers).
  • SEO: recovered through SEO after the rename; the blog publishes template-type long-tail content; holds spots in software directories such as SourceForge.

Speculation:

  • Word of mouth between merchants (peer owners seeing it after their guests sign at a skydiving center or trampoline park) probably plays some role; no direct evidence.
  • Early self-promotion on Reddit/X likely had limited effect.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Pricing $9.99–499.99/month; "thousands of companies". Revenue: Not found.
  • Both bad reviews on topbusinesssoftware complain of being charged after cancelling and unresponsive support, suggesting some paying users but disputed service quality.

Confidence and gaps

  • The page only says "this February" for the rename year; combined with cleverwaiver.com being registered in 2019-01 and waiverdigital redirecting from 2018-10, it is inferred to be around 2019-02.
  • "Founded in 2012" does not match the domain only going live in 2018; the company entity may be older or the claim exaggerated.
  • Founder, first customers and customer count are all Not found; the takeoff year cannot be determined.
