Basics

  • Product: subscription streaming for audiobooks + ebooks + book summaries + meditation/children's stories, on web and iOS/Android apps (App Store listing 2021-10-15, seller Voxa Software S.R.L, 3,105 ratings at 4.6 in the Romanian store).
  • Who built it: CEO Dan Vidrașcu (Moldovan-born, runs the family publisher Editura Litera with his brother Marin, previously CEO of elefant.ro for four years). App package name com.wolfpackdigital.voxa (developed by Bucharest outsourcing studio Wolfpack Digital, speculation based on the package name).
  • Pricing: 48 RON/month at launch, 15-day free trial (Wayback 2021-12).
  • Domain: Wayback has snapshots from 2014 (previous owner); Voxa pages from 2021-09-25.
  • Traffic (profile data): Romania 76%, Moldova 9%, United States 4.5%; direct 48%, search 32%, referral 19.6% (elefant.ro 4.8%, vinted.ro 4.4%, elefant.md 2.5%); social from YouTube, Facebook, TikTok. Instagram 31K followers, TikTok 5,953 followers.

Where the first users came from: not stated directly in the coverage. Available clues: (1) both content and funding came from Editura Litera, and the publisher plus elefant.ro bookstore channel (the founder's former employer) remains the largest referral source; (2) the Romanian diaspora mattered a lot among early users, with Germany, the UK, Moldova, and Italy among the top countries by user count (NewMoney); (3) the SeedBlink crowdfunding itself targeted the local investor community and doubled as exposure. Speculation: the first users came mainly from publisher/bookstore reader funnels and Romanian diaspora Facebook groups.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Content moat: in-house studios mass-produce exclusive Romanian audiobooks (2,000+ titles in 2023, target 5,000-6,000).
  • Paid promotion: Romania's first TV ad for audiobooks; 15-day free trial converting to subscription.
  • Channel referrals: elefant.ro / elefant.md / vinted.ro referral traffic totals about 12%.
  • Cross-border replication: the same model extended to Moldova, Italy, Spain, Latin America, Hungary, the Balkans.

Speculation:

  • Publisher-platform synergy: Litera's new releases go up on Voxa simultaneously, and bookstore pages funnel readers to Voxa previews.
  • Social content (Instagram 1,934 posts, TikTok) with book lists / summary shorts bringing in new users.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 2022 revenue about 4M lei (800K+ euros), 2023 projected 12M lei (about 2.5M euros) (NewMoney).
  • 2025-10: ARR 5.5M euros, 1M+ registered users (ZF).
  • Parent company Editura Litera had about 20M euros revenue in 2022.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. Main evidence from two articles, NewMoney and ZF (originals opened); profit.ro returned 403 so only the summary was used; Reddit could not be opened.
  • The seed round amount differs between the two articles (300K vs 550K euros), possibly different accounting.
  • Exact year of the TV ad and the source of the first users lack direct evidence.
