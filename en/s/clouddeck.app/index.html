Overview

Clouddeck (clouddeck.app): Cloud gaming subscription streaming a full Steam-compatible PC with 25,000+ games to your own devices. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Clouddeck to a checkout payment page — #378 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 36,430 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 23.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 59%, search 25%, referrals/email/paid 17%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New-user source: other — mostly referrals from other websites (about 17%).

Pricing: €19.99/mo billed every 6 months; 1-month option, price not shown.
