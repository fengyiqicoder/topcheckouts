Basics

  • Product: cloud gaming PC subscription. 4 cores / 8 threads, RTX 3060 or RX 6600-class GPU, 16GB RAM, 250GB SSD (upgradable to 1TB), streams up to 1440p60, 6 hours per session, unlimited monthly hours, no queue; compatible with 25,000+ games that run on Linux/Proton. Servers in Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Charlotte, Detroit, and San Jose; sign-ups open only to Europe, the US, and Canada.
  • Pricing: $19.99/month, or $14.99/month equivalent on the 6-month plan (Show HN); the site shows €19.99/month billed semi-annually, Launch plan; refundable if used under 4 hours, no free trial.
  • Founder: Sven Frese (HN account sfrese, calls himself "the founder of CloudDeck" in the Show HN), also the blog author; the terms are governed by Dutch law, so the team may be in the Netherlands (speculation).
  • Launch date: domain registered 2023-08-19 (RDAP); first Wayback snapshot 2024-01-05, normal pages from 2024-02; the Reddit post "CloudDeck - just popped up on my radar" is from around 2024-06 (post ID 1dm0rm3, summary only).
  • Community: Discord and Twitter in the site footer.
  • Users: United States about 17%, India about 14%, France about 10% (our profile).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Existing circles: discovery post in Reddit's cloud gaming subreddit; the founder posted a Show HN (low score, not a major source).
  • Search ranking: official "Shadow PC alternative" comparison page; non-brand searches are "<game> + mac / geforce now", capturing the "this game won't run on my device" need.
  • Referrals: third-party cloud gaming news and game list sites (cloudbase.gg, clouddosage, comparison sites) deliver 16.8% referral traffic.

Speculation:

  • The first users may have come from the Steam Deck / Bazzite / Linux gaming crowd (the product is essentially Bazzite in the cloud), plus players who spilled over after GeForce NOW added its 100-hour cap in 2025. No direct evidence.
  • The Discord community handles retention and word of mouth.
  • The 58.6% direct traffic is mainly existing users returning daily to stream.

Revenue and scale clues

  • $14.99–19.99/month; GPU cloud hosting costs are high, so gross margin is limited.
  • User count and revenue not found; with sign-ups limited to Europe and North America and 5 data centers, scale is guessed at a few thousand paying users (no evidence).

Confidence and gaps

  • Founder, product form, pricing, and launch date (inferred from domain, Wayback, and Reddit post ID) are credible.
  • Neither Reddit nor clouddosage would load; only summaries were used.
  • Gaps: corporate entity, paying user count, which post brought the first users in 2024, whether YouTube reviews drove traffic.
