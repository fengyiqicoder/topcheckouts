Where the first users came from: evidenced, they are his own YouTube viewers. The video description two days after the domain registration already linked on-site pages (giveaway, templates), and after that every long video and clip video carries a thebrettway.com link (with the utm_source=clips parameter).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Video → free resources: one experiment video a month, with the description linking playbooks/templates/giveaway/free course to bring viewers to the site.
  • Clip account distribution: clip videos use utm_source=clips to send people to the same free "one-person company" course page.
  • Affiliate monetization: the on-site templates and the "Start on Whop" buttons are all Whop affiliate links.
  • Name searches: search traffic is mainly viewers searching his name.

Speculation:

  • The giveaway (giving away a whole business) and the public Leaderboard are probably used to collect emails/motivate viewers to participate, feeding back into the next video; the original giveaway rules were not seen.
  • The site itself has no independent acquisition ability, and visits rise and fall with video views.

Revenue and scale clues

  • SponsorRadar estimates the channel's monthly revenue at $1.0K–2.4K (a low third-party estimate), with a sponsorship rate of $1.9K–3.8K; the site claims "$5M+ Made online" (unverified).
  • The site gets about 7K visits a month (2026-09) and is just the landing page for his content business.

Confidence and gaps

  • A new site written from scratch. The domain registration time, YouTube data and the video description traffic-driving links all have direct evidence.
  • Gaps: the exact relationship between Brett and Whop (on X he reposts Whop company announcements, which may be a long-term partnership or employment, unverified); the number of people in the giveaway and the community. The rest of the search results were irrelevant.
