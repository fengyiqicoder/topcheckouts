Basics

  • Product: free audio tools and materials, including Sample Packs (4 pages), Max for Live devices (8 pages: Spectrowalk, Loop Paradox, Shatter, Glitch Machine, Granular Hand Sampler, etc.), Pure Data patches (usable as plugins via plugdata), Experimental Tools (Aural Mirror, Spectrowalk, Pointillisme, LOOM, Shatter) and "Musical Mirror"; every page says "download freely, but please support my music on Bandcamp or subscribe on YouTube / Spotify", and there is an email list for new tools (https://www.remodevicocomposer.eu/maxforlive/).
  • LOOM: a single-HTML time-stretch instrument that runs in the browser (for pads/drones/textures), works offline, with an M4L version for Ableton 12 + Max 9; pay-what-you-want on Gumroad with a suggested price of €35, 64 reviews, 4.9 rating (https://www.remodevicocomposer.eu/maxforlive/loom/, https://remodevico.gumroad.com/l/loom).
  • Gumroad store: 125 products, 1,346 verified reviews, 5-star rating (search summary, https://remodevico.gumroad.com/).
  • Author: Remo De Vico (born 1986, from Cosenza), studied literature/music at La Sapienza in Rome and electronic music composition at the Santa Cecilia Conservatory; composes for theatre and film (site Biography; https://remodevico.bandcamp.com/).
  • YouTube @remodevico: opened 2015-07-22, 9.28K subscribers, 817 videos, 2.35M total views (channel About fetched 2026-10-25); recent videos are tool demos such as "Windows 98 Sound Recorder Is Now a Sampler Looper for Ableton Live" and "Stop Slicing Samples. Shatter Them in Ableton". He also uploads devices (Sound Recorder, Aamonoise, etc.) to the free maxforlive.com library.
  • Domain:.eu has no RDAP dates; first Wayback capture 2021-12-18.
  • Traffic profile (profile file): 4,131 visits in September (down from a 13,458 peak three months ago); Russia 35%, Poland 20%, Netherlands 18%; social 44.4% (YouTube, Facebook), direct 40.9%; search terms cluster on loom audio plugin, loom fx, loom download.

Where the first users came from:

  • Evidence: the author's own YouTube channel (built since 2015, 9,000+ subscribers) + Facebook/Instagram demo shorts + the free maxforlive.com library, all stating "download: www.remodevicocomposer.eu" directly.
  • Speculation: the LOOM wave was ignited by outside producers' YouTube videos or Russian/Polish "free plugin" download sites reposting it (the country mix does not match the author's Italian base, and the search terms include "download"), but no specific video or post was found.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Build a new free mini-tool every week -> shoot a demo video for YouTube/FB/IG -> viewers come to the site to download -> subscribe to the newsletter/YouTube -> the next tool reaches them again.
  • Pay-what-you-want on Gumroad, with accumulated reviews (1,346) building trust.

Speculation:

  • Free plugins naturally get reposted by "free plugin roundup" videos and forums, and occasionally one blows up (LOOM) and brings a traffic spike; the tool names carry no author branding, so after reposts users search the tool name directly.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Revenue sources: Gumroad pay-what-you-want (most people probably pay 0), Bandcamp music sales, commissioned scoring work; no public figures.
  • Scale: YouTube 9.28K subscribers, 2.35M total views; Gumroad 125 products, 1,346 reviews; LOOM alone has 64 reviews.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The site's evolution, the author's background, YouTube scale, and LOOM pricing and reviews are backed by evidence; medium confidence.
  • Gaps: LOOM's release date and the outside video/download site that pushed it were not found; per-video YouTube view counts were not parsed; the maxforlive.com page returned 429 so download counts were not read.
