Overview

Usekonvert (usekonvert.com): Konvert (formerly Staticflow), SaaS for researching, planning and producing high-performing ads with AI ad generation. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 8,596 visits went from Usekonvert to a checkout payment page — #380 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 14,094 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Checkout visits are close to or above estimated site visits — site traffic is probably underestimated, or many checkout links live off-site (apps, communities, email).

Largest September traffic sources: search 51%, direct 32%, referrals/email/paid 10%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows shareable results, and direct plus brand search is about 73%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: $49/mo or $24/mo yearly ($288/yr) (Starter); $79/mo or $39/mo yearly ($468/yr) .
