Basics

  • Product: static ad template library (4,500+ templates, 70+ added weekly), Meta Ad Library saving and competitor tracking (Explore, Scout), AI canvas image generation, shared boards, Chrome extension "Konvert Meta Ad Library Extension" (about 940 users), MCP.
  • Pricing: Starter $49/month ($24/month equivalent billed annually); higher tier $79/month ($39/month equivalent billed annually); no free trial now, satisfaction guarantee (official site). In the 2024 Staticflow era it was "Free forever, No card required".
  • Company: site footer "© 2026 Staticflow Pty Ltd" (Pty Ltd is an Australian company form); the FAQ says "Staticflow is now Konvert... Same team. Same product. Bigger ambition." Founders not found.
  • Launch date: staticflow.io registered 2024-06-17 (WHOIS), Wayback snapshots from 2024-07, full homepage by 2024-09 (title "Staticflow: Create static ads that win", then 700+ templates, 50 added weekly); usekonvert.com registered 2025-11-07 (RDAP), first Wayback snapshot 2026-03-10; staticflow.io now 301-redirects to usekonvert.com.
  • Users: United States about 13%, Australia about 10%, Israel about 10% (our profile), very spread out.
  • Note: the Mike Nelson (Four15 Digital) LinkedIn in search results has no direct connection to this site; "converter" articles are all unrelated format conversion tools.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Affiliate commissions: "Affiliate program" has been in the main nav since the 2024 Staticflow homepage, and the site still has an affiliate entry point.
  • Free tier for acquisition: "free forever, no card required" in 2024 lowered the trial barrier for media buyers; switched to paid-only in 2026.
  • Shared boards and collections: users can create shared boards to send to their team or clients (our profile notes this brings some spread).
  • Mostly brand search: 72.6% of search traffic is brand keywords.

Speculation:

  • The first users most likely came from the DTC/Meta media buyer crowd: DTC Twitter on X, Facebook ads groups, e-commerce agencies, and creators making "ad creative" content recommending it for affiliate commission. No specific posts or creators found.
  • Templates themselves come from top ads in the Meta Ad Library, so content supply is cheap, and "new every week" gives a reason to keep coming back.
  • The elevated Israel and Australia traffic may relate to where the founding team is based or to early affiliate promoters.

Revenue and scale clues

  • Claims 10k+ users and 3,000+ companies (including the free-user era); paid plans $24–79/month.
  • Revenue not found; with a few hundred to a thousand paying accounts, monthly revenue would be in the tens of thousands of dollars (speculation, no evidence).

Confidence and gaps

  • The rebrand history, corporate entity, and launch date have first-hand evidence from the site, Wayback, and WHOIS; credible.
  • Search results are almost entirely unrelated "converter" articles; no founder interviews, Product Hunt, or Reddit discussion; the ABN lookup wouldn't connect.
  • Gaps: who the founders are, where the first users came from, affiliate commission rate, paying user count.
