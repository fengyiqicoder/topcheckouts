Overview

Spediamo (spediamo.it): Italian parcel-shipping service booking courier pickups via Poste Italiane, BRT and others, no subscription. Category on TopCheckouts: Consumer services.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 12,894 visits went from Spediamo to a checkout payment page — #290 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 11,800.

Total website visits that month were about 41,916 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 30.8 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 82%, search 17%, email 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows referral rewards, and direct plus brand search is about 99%. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.

Pricing: €3.97 headline; €4.84 per shipment 0-2kg drop-off; €6.78 per shipment 0-2kg ho.
