He registered appkittie.com in November 2025 and in January 2026 launched a lightweight app-intelligence tool for indie developers (revenue/download estimates, winning ads, viral videos, ASO, onboarding flows). Customer acquisition is the same content playbook with a different product: on X and LinkedIn he posts breakdowns like "an indie developer's app hit $100K a month, here's how", with every screenshot taken from appkittie (for example the Nomadtable post with 743 reactions), and readers who want to see other apps' data click through; a 30% commission affiliate program (Rewardful) gets other app-growth creators to post appkittie screenshots too. Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder content carries the brand: breakdown posts use appkittie screenshots with the source credited, on both X and LinkedIn; our data shows organic social around 37%, with sources X > Reddit > LinkedIn.
  • Affiliate commissions: a Rewardful program with 30% commission.
  • AI tool directories: referrals from aitoolhub.net, toolfolio.com and others, plus the Toolify listing.
  • Open-sourcing aso-mcp-skills builds exposure in the developer community.

Speculation:

  • The first users were the founder's indie-developer followers on X (about a year of content built up before the product launched); the original launch post was not found.
  • Facebook roundup posts like "Mobile app inspiration websites" and other people's "copy a winning app" tutorials may also be sending it traffic.
