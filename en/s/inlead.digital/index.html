In the second half of 2024, three partners from the state of São Paulo turned the "quiz funnel" into a drag-and-drop SaaS and sold it to Brazil's info-product and direct-response ad scene (Hotmart, and media buyers running VSL for courses and health products), with the pitch "stop depending on VSL, Typebot and sales pages". Media buyers send ad traffic straight to quiz pages hosted under the inlead.digital domain, which then redirect to the checkout page. So inlead's traffic and "checkout referrals" are really a by-product of its customers' ad spend: the more customers spend, the bigger the platform gets, and it also wins customers through endorsements from top media buyers and instructors in the scene. Another key amplifier is Brazil's "winning ad spy" sites (Swipe Offers and others): media buyers browse ads that are scaling, click through, and the landing page is a quiz under the inlead domain, so peers discover the tool by following the trail (Swipe Offers is inlead's largest referrer at 10.5%).One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Customer quiz pages are hosted on inlead.digital sub-paths (such as /comunidade-nova/, /do-zero-a-edicao-viral/, /sdd-storys-do-dailtinho/, /lure-digital-global/; these customer pages show up directly in search results), so end users and peer media buyers can both see the inlead domain: branded output.
  • Ad-spy site referrals: in the profile data the top referrer is swipeoffers.com.br (10.5%), verified as a Brazilian database of "winning ads currently running" plus a SPY plugin for affiliates, info-product sellers and media buyers; third is kirvano.com (5.63%, a Brazilian checkout). This shows many visits are peers scouting customers' funnels.
  • Its own social accounts are tiny: Instagram 623 followers, TikTok 25 followers (summary only), so acquisition does not rely on owned accounts.
  • Endorsements from scene opinion leaders plus attending industry events such as Hotmart FIRE, breaking into Brazil's digital marketing / course-selling ecosystem.
  • Self-reported user numbers: 100+ new subscriptions per day, 12K active subscriptions.

Speculation:

  • First users: the three partners are themselves in the digital marketing scene of São José do Rio Preto, so the first customers were presumably themselves and acquaintances in their media buyer circle, spreading further through testimonials (unconfirmed).
  • There may be affiliate/referral commissions (common for Brazilian SaaS), but no evidence was found.
  • The high "checkout visits" in our data come from customer funnels redirecting to checkouts such as Hotmart/Kiwify/Stripe, not from inlead's own subscription payments.

Revenue and scale clues

  • If 12K active subscriptions × an average of about R$150/month, monthly revenue is about R$1.8 million (about $300K, speculation based on the self-reported subscription count).
  • Checkout visits over the past 1–6 months are among the highest of any site in this batch, peaking at 104K three months ago

Confidence and gaps

  • The founders' background, the source of the first users and the launch channel are still Not found; the search results contain no founder interviews, PH or media coverage.
  • Second follow-up check: verified that the top referrer swipeoffers.com.br is a winning-ad spy site, strengthening the growth loop of "customer funnels carry the inlead domain, peers discover the tool by scouting" (upgraded from speculation to evidence); added the YouTube creator collaboration video, the 2026-10 MCP plugin, and the very small own social accounts.
