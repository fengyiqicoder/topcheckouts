Overview

Inlead (inlead.digital): Brazilian SaaS for building interactive sales funnels and quizzes with a drag-and-drop editor. Category on TopCheckouts: Marketing & e-commerce.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 47,580 visits went from Inlead to a checkout payment page — #42 of the 607 sites we track. That is 41% down from the April–June monthly average of 81,195.

Total website visits that month were about 334,335 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by Semrush monthly trend). Roughly 14.2 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 69%, social 25%, search 5%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from social platforms: organic social is about 25%, led by Facebook › Instagram › YouTube.

Pricing: R$97/mo BASIC; R$197/mo PRO; R$297/mo ELITE.
