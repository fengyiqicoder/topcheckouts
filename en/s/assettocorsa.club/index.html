Basics

  • Product: free AC car/track mod download site ("only tested, high-quality mods") + Content Manager download and Full-version key sales (Stripe / itch.io / Boosty); also funnels users to the Patreon/Boosty preview subscription for Custom Shaders Patch (CSP).
  • Team: "AC Club team", contact emails acclubteam@gmail.com / assettocorsaclub@gmail.com; started as a Russian-language site (2015 title "только качественные моды машин и трасс"), closely tied to Russian developer x4fab (author of CM and CSP, GitHub gro-ove/actools). The site owner's identity was not found.
  • Country: originated in the Russian-speaking scene (Speculation: Russia); current traffic is mostly from the United States.
  • Funding: none (community site).

First users: the Russian-speaking AC player community in 2015 (the site had a forum, the OSRW forum and racing events). Going global came from demand for the CM download.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: AC has no Steam Workshop, so mods can only be downloaded from third-party sites, and fake mod sites often carry malware. Its identity as the "official CM download page" makes players treat it as a trusted source (Steam discussion verbatim: "authors of the content manager"), which is its moat against similar sites like assettoworld and assettocorsamods.io. CM is a must-have tool for AC mod players, and nearly every "how to install AC mods / drift servers" tutorial (YouTube, racinggames.gg, drifted.com, simracingsetup) points to this page → continuous search and backlink traffic; CM's built-in "auto-download missing content" feature for online servers locks users into the CM ecosystem.
  • Speculation: AC online drift/street servers (Shutoko etc.) require CM+CSP to join, so players who bring friends onto a server make them install CM — an indirect "invite to use" loop; the aging game's constant discounts and YouTube drift videos bring in new players.

Revenue and scale clues

  • CM Full key: from $2 (itch minimum price); 215 ratings on the itch page.
  • Our data: 17,192 checkout visits last month, 4–6 months ago average 15,733, three months ago–August 17–18K, stable — a classic evergreen long tail.
  • Owner's income not public; CSP revenue goes through x4fab's Patreon (current patron count not checked).

Confidence and gaps

  • Owner identity and revenue-share arrangement with x4fab not found; the "official" label comes from third-party guides.
  • Reddit API access was denied, so community opinions of the site were not verified.
  • Takeoff = 2015 Russian mod scene + hosting CM from 2016; evidence from Wayback, fairly high confidence.
  • Second pass: added evidence on where English-speaking users came from. Around 2018 Reddit players migrated from RaceDepartment; in 2020 a Steam Community guide made the site the standard mod-install workflow; in 2021 new players looking for the Shutoko mod found it via search; in 2023 a Steam discussion listed it as trusted because of its "CM authors' site" identity. Conclusion unchanged, but the path "Existing audience (Russian scene) → Search ranking + Steam/Reddit tutorial distribution (English scene)" is now solidly supported. Owner identity still not found.
