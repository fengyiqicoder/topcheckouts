Overview

Assettocorsa (assettocorsa.club): Free site distributing community-made car and track mods for the Assetto Corsa racing simulator. Category on TopCheckouts: Games.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 17,192 visits went from Assettocorsa to a checkout payment page — #187 of the 607 sites we track. That is 9% up from the April–June monthly average of 15,733.

Total website visits that month were about 160,555 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 10.7 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 70%, direct 23%, social 4%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 28%, with queries like “content manager”, “content manager download”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.
