Basics

  • Product: futures day-trading indicators (Maverick AI Pro, Multi-X, Radar, Trender, Bear Bull, Auto Gold Levels, Pulse, Zones, ORB, etc.), sold in bundles of 2–11; Gamma Pro pushes SPX/NDX/SPY/Mag 7 gamma levels plus a morning and evening report every day before 7:30.
  • Pricing (official site): Monthly Starter $97/month; Core Bundle $499, Ultimate Edge $699, Ultimate Zones $949 one-time; PowerPack $149; Gamma Pro $697/year; individual indicators listed at $397–$997. The page has a countdown saying "Ultimate Edge will go up to $999".
  • Who runs it: the disclaimer names Quant Flux LLC, no personal name; support at Support@MaverickIndicators.com.
  • Launch timing: domain registered 2025-04-24 (RDAP); the Whop seller avatar upload path shows 2025-05-10; earliest YouTube video 2025-06-15 and first Wayback snapshot the same day, 2025-06-15; the walkthrough video "Maverick AI Pro Trading Indicator (Tutorial)" was published 2025-07-20 (1,671 views).
  • Self-description: the site says "Tools used by 1,000+ traders" and 4.9 stars on Whop; the Whop page shows 1,166 joined and 140 reviews.
  • Traffic (profile data): only 55 visits in 2026-08, 3,731 last month; direct 46.8%, social 39.9% all from YouTube, search 8% brand keyword only; aimed at US futures day traders and people taking prop firm evaluations.
  • There is also maverickindicators.live (a live-stream page, registered around 2026-04). The third-party site gregforex.com sells "Maverick Indicators NT8" (won't open, suspected resale/cracked copies, not verified).

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Its own YouTube channel posts a daily screen recording of "winning a trade with my indicators / passing a prop evaluation", with video descriptions sending viewers to the site and Whop; 100% of the site's social traffic comes from YouTube.
  • Promotion tactics: countdowns, "Last Chance" price increases, "7 free indicators worth $4,500+", and a first-week discount on the new Gamma Pro for existing users.
  • The site has a Google Ads conversion tag (AW-17386082291), which suggests Google/YouTube ads are running.
  • 140 Whop reviews at 4.9 serve as social proof.

Speculation:

  • The 2026-09 traffic surge may come from YouTube ad spend or one video being recommended, together with the Gamma Pro launch; the specific cause was not found.
  • The prop firm evaluation boom (lots of retail traders buying $50K evaluation accounts) is an external tailwind.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 1,166 members joined Whop; at an average order of $97–$949, cumulative revenue could be in the hundreds of thousands of dollars (pure estimate, not verified).
  • Gamma Pro at $697/year is new recurring revenue.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site, written from scratch. The useful Google results were the official site, the YouTube channel/videos, Whop, gregforex and NexusFi (the last two return 403 and won't open); the rest were unrelated "best TradingView indicators" articles. YouTube RSS, RDAP and Wayback CDX were also used to fill in the timeline.
  • The operator's identity, the Whop store opening date (only inferred from an image path) and the direct cause of the 2026-09 traffic surge were not found.
  • The "1,000+ traders" and winning-trade claims typical of trading indicator products are not verified; the page itself also says "results not typical".
