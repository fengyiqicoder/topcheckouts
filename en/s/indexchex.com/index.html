Basics

  • Product: IndexChex, backlink index submission (standard 24 hours / instant 5 minutes / 1–30 day drip) + bulk Google index checking (10,000 URLs per batch) + backlink monitoring; REST API (v1, plus a SpeedyIndex-compatible v2); homepage says "Get Indexed Faster on Google and ChatGPT".
  • Founder/owner: Jacky Chou (X @indexsy, jackychou.com "Founder of Indexsy", a Canadian SEO entrepreneur; nationality from memory, unverified). jackychou.com lists IndexChex 4th among his "products ranked by revenue" (after BrowserBlast, LocalRank.so, CTR Software), alongside Looker, Token Broker, Trackings.ai and others. The official about page carries no name.
  • Pricing: credit packs $21.99/5,000, $54.99/25,000, $164.99/100,000, $1,399.99/1,000,000; monthly plans from $19.99; standard submission/check costs 1 credit/URL, instant submission 60 credits/URL.
  • Customers: our data shows India 29.95%, Taiwan 28.58%, Switzerland 18.13%; homepage claims 16,199 SEOs and 30.64 million links submitted.
