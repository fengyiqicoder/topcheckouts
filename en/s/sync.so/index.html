Two researchers at IIIT Hyderabad open-sourced Wav2Lip in 2020 (13.3K GitHub stars, covered by The Verge), building developer mindshare. On 2023-07-16, ex-Microsoft Prady Modukuru stitched that open model into a little tool that "takes a YouTube link and translates the video into another language with matching lip-sync" and posted a demo of "Lex Fridman and Zuckerberg discussing Musk in Hindi", which drew 3.54M views and 14.6K likes. That viral post became the starting point for him to co-found sync. (originally synclabs.so) with the Wav2Lip authors. They then opened a real-time lip-sync API in 2023-10, joined YC W24, raised a $5.5M seed led by GV, and grew through "funneling traffic from the open-source repo README + embedding the API into upstream tools like Kapwing, Sieve, Premiere, ComfyUI and ChatGPT."One-line growth story, compiled by us from public sources; speculation is marked separately below.

Basics

  • Product: AI lip-sync / visual dubbing platform, API plus web Studio; models lipsync-1.9 → lipsync-2 (2025-01) → lipsync-2-pro (2025-08) → sync-3 (2026-04, 16B parameters, which PH says is 32x the previous generation's 400M), react-1; integrations with ElevenLabs voices, Premiere/DaVinci/ComfyUI plugins, and a ChatGPT plugin.
  • Company: Synchronicity Labs (sync.), 1700 Montgomery St, San Francisco, YC W24, founded 2023, 13 people (YC page).
  • Founders: Prady Modukuru (CEO, formerly Microsoft AI safety product, described on the YC page as "the youngest product lead in the org"); Prajwal K R (Chief Scientist, Oxford PhD under Andrew Zisserman, Wav2Lip author); Rudrabha Mukhopadhyay (CTO, IIIT Hyderabad PhD, first author of Wav2Lip); Pavan Reddy (sales/ops/finance, IIT Madras, two VC-backed startups).
  • Funding: $5.5M seed on 2024-08-16, led by GV; investors on the about page: Rebel Fund, Y Combinator, Lobster Capital, Google Ventures, Creator Ventures.
  • Pricing: Hobbyist $5/mo, Creator $19/mo, Growth $49/mo, Scale $249/mo, plus $0.04–0.05 per second; free tier capped at 20 seconds. Also a Startup Program and an invite-only Creator Partner Program that grants credits.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder's viral post as the launchpad: one celebrity video-translation demo tweet got 3.54M views, and the output itself (celebrities speaking a foreign language) is the spreadable material.
  • The open-source repo README sends visitors of a 13K-star repo to the commercial version.
  • The API is embedded in platforms like Kapwing and Sieve, plus Premiere/DaVinci/ComfyUI/ChatGPT plugins: other people's user bases become distribution channels.
  • Startup Program / Creator Partner Program give credits to attract developers and creators.
  • Blog SEO posts ("AI lip sync tools", celebrity lip-sync memes, etc.).
  • Traffic mix: direct 73.8%, search 22.1% (95.8% brand keywords), referral workatastartup.com 6% — new users come mainly through brand awareness rather than long-tail search.
  • Speculation:
  • Multilingual/meme videos users make with sync. spread on social media and bring in new creators (output has no visible watermark, so this is topical spread).
  • Neither the Show HN nor the PH launch gained traction (5 points, #11), so launch platforms are not a major source.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 13-person team; $5.5M seed; per-second billing. YC page says "thousands of developers". No public ARR or user count found.

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass recheck: found that Prady's 2023-07-16 viral post (3.54M views) is the "mega-viral app" the YC page refers to, so the takeoff method changed from "Launch platform" to "Creator or press spark" (sparked by the founder's own tweet); the Show HN only got 5 points, proving launch platforms were not the source; added the PH sync-3 launch at #11 and the Creator Partner program.
  • Sign-up conversion numbers after the viral post and whether there was a Series A were not found; the date of the domain switch from synclabs.so to sync.so is unverified.
