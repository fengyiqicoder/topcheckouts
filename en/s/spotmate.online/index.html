Overview

Spotmate (spotmate.online): Free web tool converting Spotify tracks into downloadable MP3 files. Category on TopCheckouts: Utility.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 7,475 visits went from Spotmate to a checkout payment page — #491 of the 607 sites we track. In April–June it was still below the visible threshold, so it is a newcomer.

Total website visits that month were about 1,197,792 (Semrush figure rescaled to Similarweb). Roughly 0.6 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: search 51%, direct 47%, referrals/email/paid 1%. (Source breakdown from Semrush, desktop only.)

New users mostly come from search: non-brand search is about 51%, with queries like “spotify downloader”, “spotify to mp3”, “spotify to mp3 player”. People find it by searching for a need, not the brand name.

Pricing: no public price found on the site — it may appear after login or inside the app.
