Where the first users came from: search. The domain itself was the model name (seedance2), launched before the model's release to hold the spot, then fed on "seedance 2.0 / free" keywords after launch; plus backlinks bought via adsy.com to lift rankings (referral evidence). No trace of a Product Hunt, Reddit or social media launch.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Search ranking: exact-match domain plus a dedicated page for each new version (2.0 → 2.5) as soon as it ships; non-brand search is 56%.
  • Buying backlinks: adsy.com (a backlink marketplace) shows up in referral sources.
  • Free-quota hook: one free video on sign-in and a "Seedance 2.0 Free" entry point, then monetized through subscriptions and credit packs.
  • Multi-model aggregation: new models (Kling 4.0, Veo 3.1, etc.) are listed as soon as they appear, paired with seasonal templates (Halloween) for promotions.

Speculation:

  • The move to the neutral brand seevio.ai was very likely forced by trademark risk around the model name (seedance2.ai went 404 last month and the domain was transferred at the end of the month); the migration lost the exact-match domain's rankings, and traffic fell about 82% from two months ago to last month
  • The affiliates page shows an attempt to add a referral channel; no data on its results.

Revenue and scale clues

  • No public revenue. With about 3K visits last month and subscriptions from $14.9, monthly revenue is most likely in the low thousands of dollars (Speculation); seedance2.ai was bigger before the migration, but no data found.

Confidence and gaps

  • New site written from scratch. Most search results were irrelevant; useful evidence comes from the site's migration notice, third-party directories, RDAP and Wayback snapshots (seedance2.ai 2025-11/2026-02/2026-07).
  • Team, location and revenue all not found; "Seedance 2.0 released in early 2026" is from memory, unverified; the reason for the domain change is Speculation.
