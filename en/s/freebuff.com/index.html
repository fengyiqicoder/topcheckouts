Basics

  • Product: Freebuff, a free coding Agent, including a CLI (`npm i -g freebuff`), desktop app, Freebuff Web / App Builder (renamed from Vly), research chat and mobile. 9 built-in sub-Agents; models are DeepSeek v4, MiMo 2.5 Pro, GLM 5.2, Minimax M3, with Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite for file search; users can also plug in their own Claude Code or ChatGPT subscription. Only 25+ countries get the full models; other regions are limited to Flash models and 6 one-hour sessions per day. Monetization is text-only sponsored ads inserted between CLI turns (cloud, hosting, dev infrastructure); the ad-free paid version is Codebuff Pro, aimed at organizations.
  • Team: Codebuff (formerly Manicode, renamed after a trademark dispute), YC F24, co-founders James Grugett and Brandon Chen; Freebuff Web lead Victor Cheng (formerly Vly). Funding about $1.5M, investors include YC and Pioneer Fund (from the wiki, no primary citation).
  • The domain freebuff.com is an old domain registered in 2000, presumably purchased; early Reddit posts used freebuff.ai.

Growth loop

  • Evidence: migration of existing user pools. Existing Vly users were auto-merged into Freebuff Web; Codebuff's open source project and the YC circle bring referral traffic (vly.ai 6.3% and ycombinator.com 4.5% in our data).
  • Evidence: programmatic SEO. Dozens of "free alternative to X" and "switched and saved $N" articles, built to intercept searches for paid tools like Claude Code, Cursor and Lovable. However, brand keywords make up 89% of current search traffic, so the non-brand traffic from these articles is limited.
  • Evidence: "completely free" is the strongest spreading hook. AI-tool YouTubers and Redditors make content on their own, and the community built an OpenCode Provider integration unprompted.
  • Evidence: `?ref=` referral links that users post on Reddit themselves (reward details not found).
  • Evidence: near-identical posts across multiple subreddits on the same day, signs of marketing-driven posting.
  • Evidence: an emerging two-sided flywheel. The user count ("500k developers") is sold directly as a selling point to advertisers (Freebuff Ads).
  • Speculation: word of mouth among students or freelancers in India, Indonesia and Latin America (official blog's India case study, rising share of non-English feedback, India at 23% in our data).

Revenue and scale clues

  • Users: about 230k developers in 2026-08 (wiki), self-claimed 500k developers in 2026-09 (PH tagline); peak npm monthly downloads about 106k; 1.9K PH followers.
  • Revenue: ads (text sponsorship slots, self-serve from 2026-09) and Codebuff Pro. Amounts not found.
  • Funding about $1.5M (wiki).

Confidence and gaps

  • Second-pass additions: the Vly merger, the 2026-02-12 official blog launch, the 2026-08-14 PH launch (#3 of the day), the 2026-09-22 Freebuff Ads launch, the 230k and 500k user figures, and the dozens of programmatic SEO articles.
