Overview

Resend (resend.com): Email API for developers sending transactional and marketing email, with inbound email, CLI and SDKs. Category on TopCheckouts: Developer tools.

In September 2026, Similarweb estimates about 10,076 visits went from Resend to a checkout payment page — #324 of the 607 sites we track. That is 1249% up from the April–June monthly average of 747.

Total website visits that month were about 2,175,419 (Similarweb 3-month total, split by month-over-month change). Roughly 0.5 of every 100 visits reached the checkout page.

Largest September traffic sources: direct 67%, search 25%, referrals/email/paid 5%. (Top channel measured; others estimated by rank.)

New users mostly come from the product spreading itself: the site shows open source, and direct plus brand search is about 92%, plus shares via WhatsApp. Most new users are brought in by existing ones.
