Basics

  • Product: an email API for developers that sends transactional and marketing email (Broadcasts), plus inbound email (Inbound), Automations, templates, a CLI, multi-language SDKs and an MCP Server; the companion open-source project is React Email. Pricing (our records): free tier with 3,000 emails per month; Pro $20/month (50K emails), $35/month (100K emails); Scale from $90/month.
  • Founders: Zeno Rocha (CEO, Brazilian, lives in San Francisco, previously head of developer experience at WorkOS; the WorkOS part is from memory, unverified) and Bu Kinoshita (CTO). YC W23.
  • Team: 6 people in 2024-04 → 21 in 2025-04 → 28 in 2025-12 → 43 in 2026-06; the YC page says 45.
  • Funding: $3M seed in 2023-04; $18M Series A on 2024-12-04, led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Guillermo Rauch (Vercel CEO), Michael Grinich (WorkOS CEO), YC and others.
  • Former names: zeebbu.com → klotty.com → after joining YC it bought resend.com for $25K.

Growth loop

Evidence:

  • Founder's personal influence sparks it: the launch tweet got nearly 1M views and the waitlist reached 6,338 people in 7 weeks.
  • Open source first: React Email was released a month before Resend, and its GitHub stars and npm downloads keep growing (12K stars in 2024-04 → 17K stars in 2025-12); the company itself says open source lets people see "the tip of the iceberg".
  • Integrations and developer community: a Vercel integration, Lovable has a Resend connector, and the Supabase Auth docs list Resend as an available SMTP service (our referrals from supabase.com are 5.22%); it runs the Resend Forward launch week, 9 developer conferences a year, and published 45 videos.
  • Brand keywords: our records show brand keywords at 91.8% and direct at 67.2%, with essentially no non-brand search traffic, so it comes from word of mouth and integrations.
  • Free tier by volume: 3,000 emails per month free lowers the trial barrier, and as sending volume grows users upgrade naturally.

Speculation:

  • The 4x user growth and 7x npm download growth in 2025 most likely come from AI coding tools (Cursor, Claude Code, v0, Lovable, Bolt) defaulting to Resend for email when generating projects; the official blog doesn't say so directly, but its 2026 strategy shift toward "Agent experience" (Markdown docs, MCP, CLI) is consistent with this.
  • The first 1,000 users: mainly Zeno's X/LinkedIn followers and React Email users, developers picked from the waitlist.

Revenue and scale clues

  • 1,000 paying customers in 2023-12; revenue up 5x in 2025; profitable since 2025.
  • Tens of millions of emails sent per day (2025-12).
  • With an $18M Series A, typical valuation multiples suggest ARR in the several-million-dollar range at the time (speculation).
  • 3M users (2026-06), team of 43-45.

Confidence and gaps

  • Written from scratch: there was no old report in the notes. The "launch" and "review" search sets returned completely unrelated health sites and CVE vulnerability pages, so the work relied mainly on Resend's official blog (milestones, waitlist retrospective, Series A announcement) and the YC page.
  • The timeline and user counts come from the company, credible; the reason for 2025 growth is speculation.
  • Gaps: exact ARR; Product Hunt launch ranking; the share of users brought by AI coding tools.
